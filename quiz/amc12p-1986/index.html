不大于 \(x\) 的最大整数与不小于 \(x\) 的最小整数之和为 \(5\)。\(x\) 的解集是

English（选项同上方）

The sum of the greatest integer less than or equal to \(x\) and the least integer greater than or equal to \(x\) is \(5.\) The solution set for \(x\) is

若 \(x\) 是整数，则 \(\lfloor x\rfloor+\lceil x\rceil=2x\)，不可能等于 \(5\)。若 \(x\) 不是整数，则 \[ \lfloor x\rfloor+\lceil x\rceil=2\lfloor x\rfloor+1\text{。} \] 它等于 \(5\) 的充要条件是 \(\lfloor x\rfloor=2\)，所以 \(2\lt x\lt3\)。

Thus the correct answer is E.

English（选项同上方）

The product \[ \begin{aligned} &\left(1-\frac1{2^2}\right) \left(1-\frac1{3^2}\right)\\ &\quad{}\cdots \left(1-\frac1{9^2}\right) \left(1-\frac1{10^2}\right) \end{aligned} \] equals

Thus the correct answer is D.

17单选

English（选项同上方）

A drawer in a darkened room contains \(100\) red socks, \(80\) green socks, \(60\) blue socks and \(40\) black socks. A youngster selects socks one at a time from the drawer but is unable to see the color of the socks drawn. What is the smallest number of socks that must be selected to guarantee that the selection contains at least \(10\) pairs? (A pair of socks is two socks of the same color. No sock may be counted in more than one pair.)

取出 \(23\) 只袜子时，数量为奇数的颜色种数也必须为奇数，所以至多为 \(3\)。因此，至多有 \(3\) 只袜子未配对，余下至少 \(20\) 只袜子可组成 \(10\) 双。但是，取出 \(22\) 只还不够：若四种颜色的数量分别为 \(7,5,5,5\)，则只能组成 \(3+2+2+2=9\) 双。因此，最小数量为 \(23\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

With \(23\) selected socks, the number of colors having an odd count must itself be odd, so it is at most \(3.\) Thus at most \(3\) socks are unpaired, leaving at least \(20\) socks in \(10\) pairs. But \(22\) socks do not suffice: color counts \(7,5,5,5\) produce only \(3+2+2+2=9\) pairs. Therefore the minimum is \(23.\)

Thus the correct answer is B.
