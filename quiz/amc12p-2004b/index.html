1单选

上周每次篮球训练时，Jenny 罚中的球数都是前一次训练罚中球数的两倍。她第五次训练罚中 \(48\) 球。她第一次训练罚中了多少球？

English（选项同上方）

At each basketball practice last week, Jenny made twice as many free throws as she made at the previous practice. At her fifth practice she made \(48\) free throws. How many free throws did she make at the first practice?

每次训练罚中球数都是前一次的两倍，所以倒着推要除以二。第五次是 \(48\)，前几次分别是 \(24\)、\(12\)、\(6\)、\(3\) 球。

因此正确答案是 A。

English

Each practice she made twice the previous, so going backward we halve. From the fifth practice at \(48,\) the earlier practices had \(24,\) \(12,\) \(6,\) and \(3\) free throws.

Thus, the correct answer is A.

2单选

在表达式 \(c \cdot a^b - d\) 中，\(a\)，\(b\)，\(c\) 和 \(d\) 的值是 \(0\)，\(1\)，\(2\) 和 \(3\)，但顺序不一定如此。这个表达式的最大可能值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

In the expression \(c \cdot a^b - d,\) the values of \(a,\) \(b,\) \(c,\) and \(d\) are \(0,\) \(1,\) \(2,\) and \(3,\) although not necessarily in that order. What is the maximum possible value of the result?

为了使结果最大，取 \(d = 0\)。此时 \(a, b, c\) 分别取 \(1, 2, 3\) 中的不同值；当 \(c = 1\) 且 \(a^b = 3^2 = 9\) 时，\(c \cdot a^b\) 最大。这给出 \(1 \cdot 9 - 0 = 9\)，比 \(2^3 = 8\) 以及其他分配都大。

因此正确答案是 D。

English

To maximize, set \(d = 0.\) With \(a, b, c\) taking \(1, 2, 3,\) the term \(c \cdot a^b\) is largest when \(c = 1\) and \(a^b = 3^2 = 9.\) This gives \(1 \cdot 9 - 0 = 9,\) which beats \(2^3 = 8\) and the other assignments.

Thus, the correct answer is D.

3单选

若 \(x\) 和 \(y\) 是正整数，且 \(2^x 3^y = 1296\)，则 \(x + y\) 的值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

If \(x\) and \(y\) are positive integers for which \(2^x 3^y = 1296,\) what is the value of \(x + y?\)

分解得 \(1296 = 6^4 = 2^4 \cdot 3^4\)。比较指数可得 \(x = 4\)、\(y = 4\)，所以 \(x + y = 8\)。

因此正确答案是 A。

English

Factoring, \(1296 = 6^4 = 2^4 \cdot 3^4.\) Matching exponents gives \(x = 4\) and \(y = 4,\) so \(x + y = 8.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

4单选

从满足 \(10 \le x \le 99\) 的整数中选一个整数 \(x\)。若所有选择等可能，\(x\) 的至少一个数字是 \(7\) 的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

An integer \(x,\) with \(10 \le x \le 99,\) is to be chosen. If all choices are equally likely, what is the probability that at least one digit of \(x\) is a \(7?\)

从 \(10\) 到 \(99\) 共有 \(90\) 个整数。个位是 \(7\) 的有十个，十位是 \(7\) 的有九个。由于 \(77\) 被重复计算，所以至少有一个 \(7\) 的数共有 \(10 + 9 - 1 = 18\) 个。概率为 \(\dfrac{18}{90} = \dfrac{1}{5}\)。

因此正确答案是 B。

English

There are \(90\) integers from \(10\) to \(99.\) Ten have a units digit \(7,\) and nine have a tens digit \(7.\) Since \(77\) is counted twice, there are \(10 + 9 - 1 = 18\) with at least one \(7.\) The probability is \(\dfrac{18}{90} = \dfrac{1}{5}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

5单选

Isabella 从美国去加拿大旅行，带了 \(d\) 美元。在边境她把钱全部兑换，每 \(7\) 美元可换 \(10\) 加元。花掉 \(60\) 加元后，她还剩 \(d\) 加元。\(d\) 的各位数字之和是多少？

English（选项同上方）

On a trip from the United States to Canada, Isabella took \(d\) U.S. dollars. At the border she exchanged them all, receiving \(10\) Canadian dollars for every \(7\) U.S. dollars. After spending \(60\) Canadian dollars, she had \(d\) Canadian dollars left. What is the sum of the digits of \(d?\)

兑换后得到 \(\dfrac{10d}{7}\) 加元。花掉 \(60\) 后，她有 \(\dfrac{10d}{7} - 60 = d\)。因此 \(\dfrac{3d}{7} = 60\)，所以 \(d = 140\)，数字和为 \(1 + 4 + 0 = 5\)。

因此正确答案是 A。

English

Exchanging gives \(\dfrac{10d}{7}\) Canadian dollars. After spending \(60,\) she has \(\dfrac{10d}{7} - 60 = d.\) Then \(\dfrac{3d}{7} = 60,\) so \(d = 140.\) The sum of its digits is \(1 + 4 + 0 = 5.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

6单选

明尼阿波利斯-圣保罗国际机场位于圣保罗市中心西南 \(8\) 英里处，且位于明尼阿波利斯市中心东南 \(10\) 英里处。下列哪一个最接近圣保罗市中心和明尼阿波利斯市中心之间的英里数？

English（选项同上方）

Minneapolis-St. Paul International Airport is \(8\) miles southwest of downtown St. Paul and \(10\) miles southeast of downtown Minneapolis. Which of the following is closest to the number of miles between downtown St. Paul and downtown Minneapolis?

西南和东南方向互相垂直，所以机场位于一个直角三角形的直角顶点，两条直角边长为 \(8\) 和 \(10\)。两个市中心之间的距离为 \(\sqrt{10^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{164} \approx 12.8\)，最接近 \(13\)。

因此正确答案是 A。

English

Southwest and southeast are perpendicular, so the airport sits at the right angle of a right triangle with legs \(8\) and \(10.\) The distance between downtowns is \(\sqrt{10^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{164} \approx 12.8,\) closest to \(13.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

7单选

一个正方形边长为 \(10\)，一个圆以该正方形的一个顶点为圆心，半径为 \(10\)。正方形和圆所围成区域的并集面积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A square has sides of length \(10,\) and a circle centered at one of its vertices has radius \(10.\) What is the area of the union of the regions enclosed by the square and the circle?

正方形面积为 \(100\)，圆面积为 \(100\pi\)。二者重叠部分是位于正方形内的四分之一圆，面积为 \(25\pi\)。并集面积为 \(100 + 100\pi - 25\pi = 100 + 75\pi\)。

因此正确答案是 B。

English

The square has area \(100\) and the circle has area \(100\pi.\) Their overlap is the quarter of the circle lying inside the square, with area \(25\pi.\) The union is \(100 + 100\pi - 25\pi = 100 + 75\pi.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

一位杂货商摆放罐头，最上面一排有一个罐头，每下面一排都比上一排多两个罐头。若这个陈列共有 \(100\) 个罐头，它有多少排？

English（选项同上方）

A grocer makes a display of cans in which the top row has one can and each lower row has two more cans than the row above it. If the display contains \(100\) cans, how many rows does it contain?

各排罐头数为 \(1, 3, 5, \ldots, (2n - 1)\)，前 \(n\) 个奇数之和为 \(n^2\)。令 \(n^2 = 100\)，得 \(n = 10\)。

因此正确答案是 D。

English

The rows contain \(1, 3, 5, \ldots, (2n - 1)\) cans, and the sum of the first \(n\) odd numbers is \(n^2.\) Setting \(n^2 = 100\) gives \(n = 10.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

9单选

点 \((-3, 2)\) 绕原点顺时针旋转 \(90^\circ\) 得到点 \(B\)。然后点 \(B\) 关于直线 \(y = x\) 反射得到点 \(C\)。\(C\) 的坐标是什么？

English（选项同上方）

The point \((-3, 2)\) is rotated \(90^\circ\) clockwise around the origin to point \(B.\) Point \(B\) is then reflected in the line \(y = x\) to point \(C.\) What are the coordinates of \(C?\)

将 \((-3, 2)\) 顺时针旋转 \(90^\circ\)，按 \((x, y) \to (y, -x)\)，得到 \(B = (2, 3)\)。关于 \(y = x\) 反射会交换坐标，所以 \(C = (3, 2)\)。

Rotating \((-3, 2)\) by \(90^\circ\) clockwise sends \((x, y) \to (y, -x),\) giving \(B = (2, 3).\) Reflecting in \(y = x\) swaps coordinates, giving \(C = (3, 2).\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

10单选

圆环是两个同心圆之间的区域。图中的同心圆半径分别为 \(b\) 和 \(c\)，其中 \(b \gt c\)。令 \(\overline{OX}\) 为大圆的一条半径，\(\overline{XZ}\) 在 \(Z\) 处与小圆相切，\(\overline{OY}\) 是经过 \(Z\) 的大圆半径。令 \(a = XZ\)、\(d = YZ\)、\(e = XY\)。这个圆环的面积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

An annulus is the region between two concentric circles. The concentric circles in the figure have radii \(b\) and \(c,\) with \(b \gt c.\) Let \(\overline{OX}\) be a radius of the larger circle, let \(\overline{XZ}\) be tangent to the smaller circle at \(Z,\) and let \(\overline{OY}\) be the radius of the larger circle that contains \(Z.\) Let \(a = XZ,\) \(d = YZ,\) and \(e = XY.\) What is the area of the annulus?

圆环面积为 \(\pi b^2 - \pi c^2\)。因为 \(\overline{XZ}\) 在 \(Z\) 处与小圆相切，所以它垂直于半径 \(\overline{OZ}\)，于是 \(\triangle OZX\) 在 \(Z\) 处为直角。由此 \(b^2 = c^2 + a^2\)，所以 \(b^2 - c^2 = a^2\)，面积为 \(\pi a^2\)。

因此正确答案是 A。

English

The annulus area is \(\pi b^2 - \pi c^2.\) Because \(\overline{XZ}\) is tangent to the smaller circle at \(Z,\) it is perpendicular to radius \(\overline{OZ},\) so \(\triangle OZX\) is right-angled at \(Z.\) Then \(b^2 = c^2 + a^2,\) giving \(b^2 - c^2 = a^2.\) The area is \(\pi a^2.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

11单选

代数课上的所有学生都参加了一次满分 \(100\) 分的考试。五名学生得了 \(100\) 分，每名学生至少得 \(60\) 分，平均分为 \(76\)。这个班最少可能有多少名学生？

English（选项同上方）

All the students in an algebra class took a \(100\)-point test. Five students scored \(100,\) each student scored at least \(60,\) and the mean score was \(76.\) What is the smallest possible number of students in the class?

每个 \(100\) 分比平均分 \(76\) 高 \(24\) 分，所以五个学生共高出 \(120\) 分。这些必须由低于平均分的分数抵消，而每个剩余学生最多低 \(76 - 60 = 16\) 分。因此至少需要 \(\dfrac{120}{16} = 7.5\)，即 \(8\) 名额外学生，总数为 \(13\)。五个 \(100\) 分和八个 \(61\) 分可以达到这一情况。

因此正确答案是 D。

English

Each score of \(100\) is \(24\) above the mean, so the five contribute \(120\) points above \(76.\) These must be balanced by points below the mean, and each remaining student is at most \(76 - 60 = 16\) below. So at least \(\dfrac{120}{16} = 7.5,\) hence \(8\) more students are needed, for a total of \(13.\) Five \(100\)s and eight \(61\)s achieve this.

Thus, the correct answer is D.

12单选

在数列 \(2001\)，\(2002\)，\(2003\)，\(\ldots\) 中，从第四项起，每一项等于前两项之和减去前一项。例如第四项为 \(2001 + 2002 - 2003 = 2000\)。这个数列的第 \(2004\) 项是多少？

English（选项同上方）

In the sequence \(2001,\) \(2002,\) \(2003,\) \(\ldots,\) each term after the third is found by subtracting the previous term from the sum of the two terms that precede that term. For example, the fourth term is \(2001 + 2002 - 2003 = 2000.\) What is the \(2004\)th term in this sequence?

递推给出 \(2001\)、\(2002\)、\(2003\)、\(2000\)、\(2005\)、\(1998, \ldots\)。偶数项为 \(2002, 2000, 1998, \ldots\)，每次减少 \(2\)。

更确切地说，用归纳法可以验证 \(a_{2k} = 2004 - 2k\) 且 \(a_{2k+1} = 2001 + 2k\)。因此 \[ \begin{aligned} a_{2004} &= a_{2\cdot1002} \\ &= 2004 - 2(1002) = 0 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因此正确答案是 C。

English

The rule gives \(2001,\) \(2002,\) \(2003,\) \(2000,\) \(2005,\) \(1998, \ldots\) The even-indexed terms are \(2002, 2000, 1998, \ldots,\) decreasing by \(2.\)

More precisely, the recurrence verifies inductively that \(a_{2k} = 2004 - 2k\) and \(a_{2k+1} = 2001 + 2k.\) Therefore \[ \begin{aligned} a_{2004} &= a_{2\cdot1002} \\ &= 2004 - 2(1002) = 0. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is C.

13单选

若 \(f(x) = ax + b\)，且 \(f^{-1}(x) = bx + a\)，其中 \(a\) 和 \(b\) 为实数，则 \(a + b\) 的值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

If \(f(x) = ax + b\) and \(f^{-1}(x) = bx + a\) with \(a\) and \(b\) real, what is the value of \(a + b?\)

因为 \(f(f^{-1}(x)) = x\)，所以 \(a(bx + a) + b = x\)。比较系数得 \(ab = 1\) 和 \(a^2 + b = 0\)。于是 \(b = \frac{1}{a}\)，且 \(a^2 + \frac{1}{a} = 0\)，所以 \(a^3 = -1\) 得到 \(a = -1\)、\(b = -1\)。因此 \(a + b = -2\)。

因此正确答案是 A。

English

Since \(f(f^{-1}(x)) = x,\) we have \(a(bx + a) + b = x.\) Matching terms gives \(ab = 1\) and \(a^2 + b = 0.\) Then \(b = \frac{1}{a}\) and \(a^2 + \frac{1}{a} = 0,\) so \(a^3 = -1,\) giving \(a = -1\) and \(b = -1.\) Thus \(a + b = -2.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

14单选

在 \(\triangle ABC\) 中，\(AB = 13\)、\(AC = 5\)、\(BC = 12\)。点 \(M\) 和 \(N\) 分别在 \(\overline{AC}\) 和 \(\overline{BC}\) 上，且 \(CM = CN = 4\)。点 \(J\) 和 \(K\) 在 \(\overline{AB}\) 上，使得 \(\overline{MJ}\) 和 \(\overline{NK}\) 都垂直于 \(\overline{AB}\)。五边形 \(CMJKN\) 的面积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

In \(\triangle ABC,\) \(AB = 13,\) \(AC = 5\) and \(BC = 12.\) Points \(M\) and \(N\) lie on \(\overline{AC}\) and \(\overline{BC},\) respectively, with \(CM = CN = 4.\) Points \(J\) and \(K\) are on \(\overline{AB}\) so that \(\overline{MJ}\) and \(\overline{NK}\) are perpendicular to \(\overline{AB}.\) What is the area of pentagon \(CMJKN?\)

因为 \(5^2 + 12^2 = 13^2\)，\(\triangle ABC\) 在 \(C\) 处为直角，面积为 \(\tfrac12 (5)(12) = 30\)。小直角三角形 \(\triangle AMJ\) 和 \(\triangle NBK\) 都与 \(\triangle ABC\) 相似，斜边分别为 \(AM = 5 - 4 = 1\) 和 \(BN = 12 - 4 = 8\)。它们的面积为 \(\left(\dfrac{1}{13}\right)^2 (30)\) 和 \(\left(\dfrac{8}{13}\right)^2 (30)\)。

五边形是剩余部分：\[ \begin{gathered} \left(1 - \dfrac{1}{169} - \dfrac{64}{169}\right)(30) \\ {}= \dfrac{104}{169}(30) = \dfrac{240}{13} \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

因此正确答案是 D。

English

Since \(5^2 + 12^2 = 13^2,\) \(\triangle ABC\) is right-angled at \(C\) with area \(\tfrac12 (5)(12) = 30.\) The small right triangles \(\triangle AMJ\) and \(\triangle NBK\) are each similar to \(\triangle ABC,\) with hypotenuses \(AM = 5 - 4 = 1\) and \(BN = 12 - 4 = 8.\) Their areas are \(\left(\dfrac{1}{13}\right)^2 (30)\) and \(\left(\dfrac{8}{13}\right)^2 (30).\)

The pentagon is what remains: \[ \begin{gathered} \left(1 - \dfrac{1}{169} - \dfrac{64}{169}\right)(30) \\ {}= \dfrac{104}{169}(30) = \dfrac{240}{13}. \end{gathered} \]

Thus, the correct answer is D.

15单选

Jack 年龄的两个数字与 Bill 年龄的两个数字相同，但顺序相反。五年后，Jack 的年龄将是 Bill 那时年龄的两倍。他们现在年龄的差是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The two digits in Jack’s age are the same as the digits in Bill’s age, but in reverse order. In five years Jack will be twice as old as Bill will be then. What is the difference in their current ages?

设 Jack 为 \(10x + y\)，Bill 为 \(10y + x\)，则 \(10x + y + 5 = 2(10y + x + 5)\)，所以 \(8x = 19y + 5\)。检验数字，只有 \(y = 1, x = 3\) 可行，因此 Jack 是 \(31\) 岁，Bill 是 \(13\) 岁。差为 \(31 - 13 = 18\)。

因此正确答案是 B。

English

Let Jack be \(10x + y\) and Bill be \(10y + x.\) Then \(10x + y + 5 = 2(10y + x + 5),\) so \(8x = 19y + 5.\) Testing digits, only \(y = 1, x = 3\) works, so Jack is \(31\) and Bill is \(13.\) The difference is \(31 - 13 = 18.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

16单选

函数 \(f\) 定义为 \(f(z) = i\overline{z}\)，其中 \(i = \sqrt{-1}\)，\(\overline{z}\) 是 \(z\) 的复共轭。有多少个 \(z\) 同时满足 \(|z| = 5\) 和 \(f(z) = z\)？

English（选项同上方）

A function \(f\) is defined by \(f(z) = i\overline{z},\) where \(i = \sqrt{-1}\) and \(\overline{z}\) is the complex conjugate of \(z.\) How many values of \(z\) satisfy both \(|z| = 5\) and \(f(z) = z?\)

写 \(z = x + iy\) 则 \(f(z) = i(x - iy) = y + ix\)。令 \(f(z) = z\) 得 \(y = x\)，这是一条过原点的直线。条件 \(|z| = 5\) 是一个圆，过圆心的直线与圆相交于 \(2\) 个点。

因此正确答案是 C。

English

Writing \(z = x + iy,\) we get \(f(z) = i(x - iy) = y + ix.\) Setting \(f(z) = z\) gives \(y = x,\) which is a line through the origin. The condition \(|z| = 5\) is a circle, and a line through the center meets the circle in \(2\) points.

Thus, the correct answer is C.

17单选

对某些实数 \(a\) 和 \(b\)，方程 \[8x^3 + 4ax^2 + 2bx + a = 0\] 有三个不同的正根。若这些根的以 \(2\) 为底的对数之和为 \(5\)，则 \(a\) 的值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

For some real numbers \(a\) and \(b,\) the equation \[8x^3 + 4ax^2 + 2bx + a = 0\] has three distinct positive roots. If the sum of the base-\(2\) logarithms of the roots is \(5,\) what is the value of \(a?\)

以 \(2\) 为底的对数之和为 \(\log_2(r_1 r_2 r_3) = 5\)，所以 \(r_1 r_2 r_3 = 2^5 = 32\)。由 Vieta 公式，方程 \(8x^3 + 4ax^2 + 2bx + a\) 的根的乘积为 \(-\dfrac{a}{8}\)。因此 \(-\dfrac{a}{8} = 32\) 得到 \(a = -256\)。

因此正确答案是 A。

English

The sum of the base-\(2\) logarithms is \(\log_2(r_1 r_2 r_3) = 5,\) so \(r_1 r_2 r_3 = 2^5 = 32.\) By Vieta’s formulas on \(8x^3 + 4ax^2 + 2bx + a,\) the product of the roots is \(-\dfrac{a}{8}.\) Thus \(-\dfrac{a}{8} = 32,\) giving \(a = -256.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

18单选

点 \(A\) 和 \(B\) 在抛物线 \(y = 4x^2 + 7x - 1\) 上，且原点是 \(\overline{AB}\) 的中点。\(AB\) 的长度是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Points \(A\) and \(B\) are on the parabola \(y = 4x^2 + 7x - 1,\) and the origin is the midpoint of \(\overline{AB}.\) What is the length of \(AB?\)

设 \(B = (a, b)\)、\(A = (-a, -b)\)。则 \(4a^2 + 7a - 1 = b\)，且 \(4a^2 - 7a - 1 = -b\)。相减得 \(14a = 2b\)，所以 \(b = 7a\)。再由 \(4a^2 + 7a - 1 = 7a\) 得 \(a^2 = \dfrac14\)，且 \(b^2 = 49a^2 = \dfrac{49}{4}\)。因此 \(AB = 2\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}\) \(= 2\sqrt{\dfrac{50}{4}} = 5\sqrt{2}\)。

因此正确答案是 E。

English

Let \(B = (a, b)\) and \(A = (-a, -b).\) Then \(4a^2 + 7a - 1 = b\) and \(4a^2 - 7a - 1 = -b.\) Subtracting gives \(14a = 2b,\) so \(b = 7a.\) Then \(4a^2 + 7a - 1 = 7a\) gives \(a^2 = \dfrac14,\) and \(b^2 = 49a^2 = \dfrac{49}{4}.\) So \(AB = 2\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}\) \(= 2\sqrt{\dfrac{50}{4}} = 5\sqrt{2}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

19单选

一个截锥有水平底面，两个底面半径分别为 \(18\) 和 \(2\)。一个球与截锥的上底面、下底面和侧面都相切。这个球的半径是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A truncated cone has horizontal bases with radii \(18\) and \(2.\) A sphere is tangent to the top, bottom, and lateral surface of the truncated cone. What is the radius of the sphere?

轴截面是梯形 \(ABCD\)，平行边长为 \(2\) 和 \(18\)，且有一个内切圆（球的大圆）。从 \(B\) 和 \(C\) 引出的切线段长度相等，所以斜边 \(BC = 18 + 2 = 20\)。从 \(C\) 向下底作垂线，得到一个水平直角边为 \(18 - 2 = 16\) 的直角三角形，所以高为 \(\sqrt{20^2 - 16^2} = 12\)。球的半径是高的一半，即 \(6\)。

因此正确答案是 A。

English

The axial cross-section is a trapezoid \(ABCD\) with parallel sides \(2\) and \(18\) and an inscribed circle (a great circle of the sphere). By equal tangent lengths from \(B\) and \(C,\) the slant side \(BC = 18 + 2 = 20.\) Dropping a perpendicular from \(C\) to the bottom base gives a right triangle with horizontal leg \(18 - 2 = 16,\) so the height is \(\sqrt{20^2 - 16^2} = 12.\) The sphere’s radius is half the height, \(6.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

20单选

一个立方体的每个面都独立地以概率 \(\tfrac12\) 涂成红色或蓝色。这个涂色立方体能被放在水平面上，使得四个竖直面颜色全相同的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Each face of a cube is painted either red or blue, each with probability \(\tfrac12.\) The color of each face is determined independently. What is the probability that the painted cube can be placed on a horizontal surface so that the four vertical faces are all the same color?

共有 \(2^6 = 64\) 种涂色。存在合适摆放方式的情况包括：六个面全同色（\(2\) 种），恰好五个面同色（ \(2 \cdot 6 = 12\) 种），或四个面为一种颜色且另外两个面为另一种颜色并位于相对面（ \(2 \cdot 3 = 6\) 种）。共有 \(2 + 12 + 6 = 20\) 种有利涂色，所以概率为 \(\dfrac{20}{64} = \dfrac{5}{16}\)。

因此正确答案是 B。

English

There are \(2^6 = 64\) colorings. A suitable orientation exists when all six faces are one color (\(2\) ways), exactly five faces are one color (\(2 \cdot 6 = 12\) ways), or four faces are one color with the other color on a pair of opposite faces (\(2 \cdot 3 = 6\) ways). That is \(2 + 12 + 6 = 20\) favorable colorings, so the probability is \(\dfrac{20}{64} = \dfrac{5}{16}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

21单选

方程 \(2x^2 + xy + 3y^2\) \(- 11x - 20y + 40 = 0\) 的图像是在 \(xy\) 平面第一象限内的一个椭圆。令 \(a\) 和 \(b\) 分别为椭圆上所有点 \((x, y)\) 的 \(\dfrac{y}{x}\) 的最大值和最小值。\(a + b\) 的值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The graph of \(2x^2 + xy + 3y^2\) \(- 11x - 20y + 40 = 0\) is an ellipse in the first quadrant of the \(xy\)-plane. Let \(a\) and \(b\) be the maximum and minimum values of \(\dfrac{y}{x}\) over all points \((x, y)\) on the ellipse. What is the value of \(a + b?\)

斜率 \(a\) 和 \(b\) 是使直线 \(y = mx\) 与椭圆恰好交于一点的 \(m\) 值。代入得 \[ \begin{aligned} &(3m^2 + m + 2)x^2 \\ &\quad {}- (20m + 11)x + 40 = 0\text{。} \end{aligned} \] 令判别式为零，得到 \(-80m^2 + 280m - 199 = 0\)。由 Vieta 公式，\(a + b = \dfrac{280}{80} = \dfrac{7}{2}\)。

因此正确答案是 C。

English

The slopes \(a\) and \(b\) are the values of \(m\) for which \(y = mx\) meets the ellipse in exactly one point. Substituting gives \[ \begin{aligned} &(3m^2 + m + 2)x^2 \\ &\quad {}- (20m + 11)x + 40 = 0. \end{aligned} \] Setting its discriminant to zero yields \(-80m^2 + 280m - 199 = 0.\) By Vieta’s formulas, \(a + b = \dfrac{280}{80} = \dfrac{7}{2}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

22单选

方阵 \[\begin{array}{|c|c|c|} \hline 50 & b & c \\ \hline d & e & f \\ \hline g & h & 2 \\ \hline \end{array}\] 是一个乘法幻方。也就是说，每一行、每一列和每条对角线上的数的乘积都相同。若所有项都是正整数，\(g\) 的可能值之和是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The square \[\begin{array}{|c|c|c|} \hline 50 & b & c \\ \hline d & e & f \\ \hline g & h & 2 \\ \hline \end{array}\] is a multiplicative magic square. That is, the product of the numbers in each row, column, and diagonal is the same. If all the entries are positive integers, what is the sum of the possible values of \(g?\)

由各行、列、对角线乘积相等，可把每一项都写成关于 \(b\) 的式子：\(h = \dfrac{100}{b}\)、\(g = \dfrac{100}{c}\)、\(f = \dfrac{100}{d}\)。比较行和列得 \(c = \dfrac{20}{b}\)、\(d = \dfrac{4}{b}\)，因此 \(g = 5b\)，且 \(e = 10\)。

所有项都是正整数当且仅当 \(b = 1, 2\)，或 \(4\)，对应 \(g = 5, 10, 20\)。它们的和为 \(35\)。

因此正确答案是 C。

English

From the equal row, column, and diagonal products, every entry can be written in terms of \(b:\) \(h = \dfrac{100}{b},\) \(g = \dfrac{100}{c},\) \(f = \dfrac{100}{d}.\) Comparing rows and columns gives \(c = \dfrac{20}{b}\) and \(d = \dfrac{4}{b},\) hence \(g = 5b\) and \(e = 10.\)

All entries are positive integers exactly when \(b = 1, 2,\) or \(4,\) giving \(g = 5, 10, 20.\) Their sum is \(35.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

23单选

多项式 \(x^3 - 2004x^2 + mx + n\) 的系数为整数，并且有三个不同的正零点。其中恰好一个零点是整数，且它等于另外两个零点之和。\(n\) 可能有多少个值？

English（选项同上方）

The polynomial \(x^3 - 2004x^2 + mx + n\) has integer coefficients and three distinct positive zeros. Exactly one of these is an integer, and it is the sum of the other two. How many values of \(n\) are possible?

设整数零点为 \(a\)。另外两个零点是无理共轭数 \(\dfrac{a}{2} \pm r\)，它们的和 \(a\) 等于整数零点。由 \(x^2\) 项系数的 Vieta 公式，\(a + a = 2004\)，所以 \(a = 1002\)，共轭对为 \(501 \pm r\)。

系数为整数当且仅当 \(r^2\) 是正整数；零点为正且互不相同当 \(1 \le r^2 \le 501^2 - 1 = 251{,}000\)。由于 \(r\) 不能是整数，排除 \(500\) 个平方数 \(r^2 = 1^2, \ldots, 500^2\)，剩下 \(251{,}000 - 500 = 250{,}500\) 个 \(n\) 的值。

因此正确答案是 C。

English

Let the integer zero be \(a.\) The other two zeros are irrational conjugates \(\dfrac{a}{2} \pm r,\) whose sum \(a\) equals the integer zero. Vieta’s formula on the \(x^2\) coefficient gives \(a + a = 2004,\) so \(a = 1002\) and the conjugate pair is \(501 \pm r.\)

The coefficients are integers exactly when \(r^2\) is a positive integer, and the zeros are positive and distinct when \(1 \le r^2 \le 501^2 - 1 = 251{,}000.\) Since \(r\) cannot be an integer, we exclude the \(500\) perfect-square values \(r^2 = 1^2, \ldots, 500^2,\) leaving \(251{,}000 - 500 = 250{,}500\) values of \(n.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

24单选

在 \(\triangle ABC\) 中，\(AB = BC\)，且 \(\overline{BD}\) 是高。点 \(E\) 在 \(\overline{AC}\) 的延长线上，且 \(BE = 10\)。\(\tan \angle CBE\)、\(\tan \angle DBE\)、\(\tan \angle ABE\) 的值构成等比数列，而 \(\cot \angle DBE\)、\(\cot \angle CBE\)、\(\cot \angle DBC\) 的值构成等差数列。\(\triangle ABC\) 的面积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

In \(\triangle ABC,\) \(AB = BC,\) and \(\overline{BD}\) is an altitude. Point \(E\) is on the extension of \(\overline{AC}\) such that \(BE = 10.\) The values of \(\tan \angle CBE,\) \(\tan \angle DBE,\) and \(\tan \angle ABE\) form a geometric progression, and the values of \(\cot \angle DBE,\) \(\cot \angle CBE,\) \(\cot \angle DBC\) form an arithmetic progression. What is the area of \(\triangle ABC?\)

令 \(\angle DBE = \alpha\)、\(\angle DBC = \beta\)。由于 \(\overline{BD}\) 是等腰三角形的高，\(\angle CBE = \alpha - \beta\)，且 \(\angle ABE = \alpha + \beta\)。等比数列给出 \(\tan(\alpha - \beta)\tan(\alpha + \beta) = \tan^2\alpha\)，化简为 \(\tan^2\beta(\tan^4\alpha - 1) = 0\)，所以 \(\tan\alpha = 1\)，\(\alpha = 45^\circ\)。

设 \(DC = a\)、\(BD = b\)，等差数列 \(\cot\angle DBE\)、\(\cot\angle CBE\)、\(\cot\angle DBC\) 变为 \(1, \dfrac{b + a}{b - a}, \dfrac{b}{a}\)，从而推出 \(b = 3a\)。又 \(BE = 10\)，且 \(\angle DBE = 45^\circ\)，所以 \(b = \dfrac{BE}{\sqrt2} = 5\sqrt2\)，于是 \(a = \dfrac{5\sqrt2}{3}\)。

\(\triangle ABC\) 的面积为 \(\tfrac12 (AC)(BD) = ab\) \(= 5\sqrt2 \cdot \dfrac{5\sqrt2}{3}\) \(= \dfrac{50}{3}\)。

因此正确答案是 B。

English

Let \(\angle DBE = \alpha\) and \(\angle DBC = \beta.\) Since \(\overline{BD}\) is the altitude of the isosceles triangle, \(\angle CBE = \alpha - \beta\) and \(\angle ABE = \alpha + \beta.\) The geometric progression gives \(\tan(\alpha - \beta)\tan(\alpha + \beta) = \tan^2\alpha,\) which simplifies to \(\tan^2\beta(\tan^4\alpha - 1) = 0,\) so \(\tan\alpha = 1\) and \(\alpha = 45^\circ.\)

Writing \(DC = a\) and \(BD = b,\) the arithmetic progression \(\cot\angle DBE,\) \(\cot\angle CBE,\) \(\cot\angle DBC\) becomes \(1, \dfrac{b + a}{b - a}, \dfrac{b}{a},\) forcing \(b = 3a.\) With \(BE = 10\) and \(\angle DBE = 45^\circ,\) we get \(b = \dfrac{BE}{\sqrt2} = 5\sqrt2,\) so \(a = \dfrac{5\sqrt2}{3}.\)

The area of \(\triangle ABC\) is \(\tfrac12 (AC)(BD) = ab\) \(= 5\sqrt2 \cdot \dfrac{5\sqrt2}{3}\) \(= \dfrac{50}{3}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

25单选

已知 \(2^{2004}\) 是一个 \(604\) 位数，且首位数字是 \(1\)，集合 \(S = \{2^0, 2^1, 2^2, \ldots, 2^{2003}\}\) 中有多少个元素的首位数字是 \(4\)？

English（选项同上方）

Given that \(2^{2004}\) is a \(604\)-digit number whose first digit is \(1,\) how many elements of the set \(S = \{2^0, 2^1, 2^2, \ldots, 2^{2003}\}\) have a first digit of \(4?\)

具有任意固定位数的最小 \(2\) 的幂位于 \(10^k\) 和 \(2\cdot10^k\) 之间，所以首位数字为 \(1\)。因为 \(2^{2004}\) 是以 \(1\) 开头的 \(604\) 位数，它是位数为 \(604\) 的第一个幂。因此集合 \(S\) 对从 \(1\) 到 \(603\) 的每一种位数恰含一个首位为 \(1\) 的数，共 \(603\) 个。

在每个首位为 \(1\) 的幂之后，下一个幂的首位为 \(2\) 或 \(3\)，再下一个幂的首位为 \(4, 5, 6\)，或 \(7\)。所以有 \(603\) 个元素首位为 \(2\) 或 \(3\)，另有 \(603\) 个元素首位从 \(4\) 到 \(7\)，剩余 \(2004 - 3(603) = 195\) 个元素首位为 \(8\) 或 \(9\)。

最后，把首位为 \(8\) 或 \(9\) 的幂除以二，会得到前一个首位为 \(4\) 的幂；把任意首位为 \(4\) 的幂乘以二正好是逆操作。这是一个双射，所以首位数字为 \(4\) 的元素有 \(195\) 个。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The smallest power of \(2\) with any given digit-count lies between \(10^k\) and \(2\cdot10^k,\) so it has leading digit \(1.\) Because \(2^{2004}\) is a \(604\)-digit number beginning with \(1,\) it is the first \(604\)-digit power. Thus the powers in \(S\) contain exactly one leading-\(1\) number for each digit-count from \(1\) through \(603,\) or \(603\) in all.

After each leading-\(1\) power, the next power leads with \(2\) or \(3,\) and the following power leads with \(4, 5, 6,\) or \(7.\) Hence \(603\) elements lead with \(2\) or \(3,\) another \(603\) lead with \(4\) through \(7,\) and the remaining \(2004 - 3(603) = 195\) lead with \(8\) or \(9.\)

Finally, halving a power that leads with \(8\) or \(9\) produces the preceding power with leading digit \(4,\) and doubling any leading-\(4\) power reverses this. This is a bijection, so there are \(195\) elements with first digit \(4.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.
