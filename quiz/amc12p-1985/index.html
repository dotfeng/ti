English（选项同上方）

Let \(a,\) \(a',\) \(b,\) \(b'\) be real numbers with \(a\) and \(a'\) nonzero. The solution to \(ax+b=0\) is less than the solution to \(a'x+b'=0\) if and only if

任意一个圆与函数 \(y=\sin x\) 的图像的交点数可以是

English（选项同上方）

An arbitrary circle can intersect the graph of \(y=\sin x\) in

取一个在原点与 \(x\) 轴相切、圆心位于坐标轴上方很高处的圆。随着半径增大，在任意长的区间内，它的下弧都保持为正且可以任意接近 \(x\) 轴。在 \(y=\sin x\) 的每个正弧上，正弦曲线在两端为 \(0\)，而在中间远高于这段圆弧，从而产生两个交点。因此，选取足够大的半径便可得到超过 \(16\) 个交点。

Choosing a sufficiently large radius therefore produces more than \(16\) intersections.

Thus the correct answer is E.

English（选项同上方）

English（选项同上方）

Exactly three of the interior angles of a convex polygon are obtuse. What is the maximum number of sides of such a polygon?

对于一个 \(n\) 边形，三个钝角之和小于 \(3\cdot180^\circ\)，其余 \(n-3\) 个角之和至多为 \((n-3)90^\circ\)。因此 \[ \begin{aligned} (n-2)180^\circ &\lt3(180^\circ)\\ &\quad{}+(n-3)90^\circ\text{，} \end{aligned} \] 化简得 \(n\lt7\)。六边形可以达到，例如其内角为 \(150^\circ\)、\(150^\circ\)、\(150^\circ\)、\(90^\circ\)、\(90^\circ\)、\(90^\circ\)。所以最大边数为 \(6\)。

因此正确答案是 C。

English

For an \(n\)-gon, the three obtuse angles have total less than \(3\cdot180^\circ,\) while the other \(n-3\) angles have total at most \((n-3)90^\circ.\) Hence \[ \begin{aligned} (n-2)180^\circ &\lt3(180^\circ)\\ &\quad{}+(n-3)90^\circ, \end{aligned} \] which simplifies to \(n\lt7.\) Six sides are attainable, for example with interior angles \(150^\circ,\) \(150^\circ,\) \(150^\circ,\) \(90^\circ,\) \(90^\circ,\) and \(90^\circ.\) Thus the maximum is \(6.\)

Therefore the correct answer is C.

18单选

English（选项同上方）

Thus the correct answer is D.
