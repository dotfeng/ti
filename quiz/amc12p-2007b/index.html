1单选

Isabella 的房子有 \(3\) 间卧室。每间卧室长 \(12\) 英尺、宽 \(10\) 英尺、高 \(8\) 英尺。Isabella 需要粉刷所有卧室的墙壁。每间卧室中门和窗占 \(60\) 平方英尺，这些部分不粉刷。需要粉刷多少平方英尺的墙壁？

English（选项同上方）

Isabella’s house has \(3\) bedrooms. Each bedroom is \(12\) feet long, \(10\) feet wide, and \(8\) feet high. Isabella must paint the walls of all the bedrooms. Doorways and windows, which will not be painted, occupy \(60\) square feet in each bedroom. How many square feet of walls must be painted?

每间卧室地面周长为 \(2(12+10)=44\) 英尺。

所以一间卧室的墙面粉刷面积为 \(44\cdot8-60=352-60=292\) 平方英尺。三间卧室共需粉刷 \(3\cdot292=876\) 平方英尺。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The perimeter of each bedroom floor is \(2(12+10)=44\) feet.

So the wall area in one bedroom is \(44\cdot8-60=352-60=292\) square feet. Across all three bedrooms, Isabella paints \(3\cdot292=876\) square feet.

Thus, the correct answer is E.

2单选

一名大学生开小型车回家过周末，路程 \(120\) 英里，平均每加仑行驶 \(30\) 英里。返程时他开父母的 SUV，平均每加仑只能行驶 \(20\) 英里。往返全程平均每加仑行驶多少英里？

English（选项同上方）

A college student drove his compact car \(120\) miles home for the weekend and averaged \(30\) miles per gallon. On the return trip the student drove his parents’ SUV and averaged only \(20\) miles per gallon. What was the average gas mileage, in miles per gallon, for the round trip?

去程用 \(\frac{120}{30}=4\) 加仑，返程用 \(\frac{120}{20}=6\) 加仑。

往返共 \(240\) 英里，用油 \(10\) 加仑，所以平均每加仑行驶的英里数为 \[ \dfrac{240}{10}=24\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The trip home uses \(\frac{120}{30}=4\) gallons, and the return trip uses \(\frac{120}{20}=6\) gallons.

The round trip covers \(240\) miles on \(10\) gallons, so the average is \[ \dfrac{240}{10}=24. \]

Thus, the correct answer is B.

3单选

点 \(O\) 是 \(\triangle ABC\) 外接圆的圆心，如图所示，\(\angle BOC=120^\circ\)，\(\angle AOB=140^\circ\)，求 \(\angle ABC\) 的度数。

English（选项同上方）

The point \(O\) is the center of the circle circumscribed about \(\triangle ABC,\) with \(\angle BOC=120^\circ\) and \(\angle AOB=140^\circ,\) as shown. What is the degree measure of \(\angle ABC?\)

点 \(O\) 周围角和为 \(360^\circ\)，所以 \[ \begin{aligned} \angle AOC&=360^\circ-140^\circ-120^\circ \\ &=100^\circ \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

由圆周角定理，\(\angle ABC\) 与圆心角 \(\angle AOC\) 截同一弧 \(AC\)，所以 \[ \angle ABC=\tfrac12\angle AOC=50^\circ\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The angles around \(O\) sum to \(360^\circ,\) so \[ \begin{aligned} \angle AOC&=360^\circ-140^\circ-120^\circ \\ &=100^\circ. \end{aligned} \]

By the inscribed angle theorem, \(\angle ABC\) subtends the same arc \(AC\) as the central angle \(\angle AOC,\) so \[ \angle ABC=\tfrac12\angle AOC=50^\circ. \]

Thus, the correct answer is D.

At Frank’s Fruit Market, \(3\) bananas cost as much as \(2\) apples, and \(6\) apples cost as much as \(4\) oranges. How many oranges cost as much as \(18\) bananas?

因为 \(3\) 根香蕉价格等于 \(2\) 个苹果，\(18\) 根香蕉价格等于 \(12\) 个苹果。

又因为 \(6\) 个苹果价格等于 \(4\) 个橙子，\(12\) 个苹果价格等于 \(8\) 个橙子，所以 \(18\) 根香蕉等价于 \(8\) 个橙子。

English

Since \(3\) bananas cost as much as \(2\) apples, \(18\) bananas cost as much as \(12\) apples.

Since \(6\) apples cost as much as \(4\) oranges, \(12\) apples cost as much as \(8\) oranges. Therefore \(18\) bananas cost as much as \(8\) oranges.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

5单选

\(2007\) 年 AMC \(12\) 的计分方式为：每答对一题得 \(6\) 分，答错得 \(0\) 分，空题得 \(1.5\) 分。Sarah 看过 \(25\) 道题后，决定尝试前 \(22\) 题，最后 \(3\) 题留空。为了至少得到 \(100\) 分，她在前 \(22\) 题中至少要答对多少题？

English（选项同上方）

The \(2007\) AMC \(12\) contests will be scored by awarding \(6\) points for each correct response, \(0\) points for each incorrect response, and \(1.5\) points for each problem left unanswered. After looking over the \(25\) problems, Sarah has decided to attempt the first \(22\) and leave the last \(3\) unanswered. How many of the first \(22\) problems must she solve correctly in order to score at least \(100\) points?

三道空题给 \(3\cdot1.5=4.5\) 分。

所以 Sarah 需要从答对题中得到至少 \(100-4.5=95.5\) 分。因为 \[ 15\lt\dfrac{95.5}{6}\lt16\text{，} \] 她至少要答对 \(16\) 题，此时得分为 \(16\cdot6+4.5=100.5\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The three unanswered problems give \(3\cdot1.5=4.5\) points.

So Sarah needs at least \(100-4.5=95.5\) points from correct answers. Since \[ 15\lt\dfrac{95.5}{6}\lt16, \] she must solve at least \(16\) problems correctly, which would give her \(16\cdot6+4.5=100.5\) points.

Thus, the correct answer is D.

6单选

三角形 \(ABC\) 的边长为 \(AB=5\)、\(BC=6\)、\(AC=7\)。两只虫子同时从 \(A\) 出发，沿三角形边界以相同速度朝相反方向爬行。它们在点 \(D\) 相遇。求 \(BD\)。

English（选项同上方）

Triangle \(ABC\) has side lengths \(AB=5,\) \(BC=6,\) and \(AC=7.\) Two bugs start simultaneously from \(A\) and crawl along the sides of the triangle in opposite directions at the same speed. They meet at point \(D.\) What is \(BD?\)

周长为 \(5+6+7=18\)，因此每只虫子在相遇前走了 \(9\) 个长度单位。

沿 \(A\to B\to C\) 方向的虫子到达 \(D\) 时走了 \(AB+BD=9\)，该点在 \(BC\) 上，且 \(AB=5\)，所以 \(BD=4\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The perimeter is \(5+6+7=18,\) so each bug crawls \(9\) before they meet.

The bug going \(A\to B\to C\) reaches \(D\) on side \(BC,\) having traveled \(AB+BD=9.\) Since \(AB=5,\) we get \(BD=4.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

7单选

凸五边形 \(ABCDE\) 的所有边长相等，且 \(\angle A=\angle B=90^\circ\)。求 \(\angle E\) 的度数。

English（选项同上方）

All sides of the convex pentagon \(ABCDE\) are of equal length, and \(\angle A=\angle B=90^\circ.\) What is the degree measure of \(\angle E?\)

因为 \(AB=BC=EA\) 且 \(\angle A=\angle B=90^\circ\)，四边形 \(ABCE\) 是正方形，所以 \(\angle AEC=90^\circ\)，且 \(EC\) 等于公共边长。

又 \(CD=DE=EC\)，所以 \(\triangle CDE\) 是等边三角形，\(\angle CED=60^\circ\)。因此 \[ \begin{aligned} \angle E&=\angle AEC+\angle CED \\ &=90^\circ+60^\circ \\ &=150^\circ \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Because \(AB=BC=EA\) and \(\angle A=\angle B=90^\circ,\) quadrilateral \(ABCE\) is a square, so \(\angle AEC=90^\circ\) and \(EC\) equals the common side length.

Then \(CD=DE=EC,\) so \(\triangle CDE\) is equilateral and \(\angle CED=60^\circ.\) Therefore \[ \begin{aligned} \angle E&=\angle AEC+\angle CED \\ &=90^\circ+60^\circ \\ &=150^\circ. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is E.

8单选

Tom 的年龄为 \(T\) 岁，也等于他三个孩子年龄之和。\(N\) 年前他的年龄是当时三个孩子年龄之和的两倍。求 \(\frac{T}{N}\)。

English（选项同上方）

Tom’s age is \(T\) years, which is also the sum of the ages of his three children. His age \(N\) years ago was twice the sum of their ages then. What is \(\frac{T}{N}?\)

\(N\) 年前 Tom 年龄为 \(T-N\)。三个孩子每人少 \(N\) 岁，所以他们当时总年龄为 \(T-3N\)。

条件给出 \[ T-N=2(T-3N)\text{，}\] 因而 \(5N=T\)，所以 \(\frac{T}{N}=5\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Tom’s age \(N\) years ago was \(T-N.\) His three children were each \(N\) years younger, so their ages then totaled \(T-3N.\)

The condition gives \[ T-N=2(T-3N), \] so \(5N=T\) and \(\frac{T}{N}=5.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

10单选

一些男孩和女孩洗车，为去中国的班级旅行筹款。起初，女生占全组的 \(40\%\)。不久后两名女生离开，两名男生加入，此时女生占全组的 \(30\%\)。起初组里有多少名女生？

English（选项同上方）

Some boys and girls are having a car wash to raise money for a class trip to China. Initially \(40\%\) of the group are girls. Shortly thereafter two girls leave and two boys arrive, and then \(30\%\) of the group are girls. How many girls were initially in the group?

两名女生离开、两名男生加入，总人数不变，女生比例从 \(40\%\) 降到 \(30\%\)。

下降的 \(10\%\) 就对应这两名女生，所以全组有 \(20\) 人，起初女生人数为 \(20\) 的 \(40\%\)，即 \(8\) 人。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Since two girls leave while two boys arrive, the total group size is unchanged. The drop from \(40\%\) to \(30\%\) girls corresponds to the two girls who left.

So those two girls are \(10\%\) of the group, meaning the group has \(20\) people. The initial number of girls was \(40\%\) of \(20,\) or \(8.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

11单选

四边形 \(ABCD\) 的角满足 \(\angle A=2\angle B=3\angle C=4\angle D\)。将 \(\angle A\) 的度数四舍五入到最接近的整数，结果是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The angles of quadrilateral \(ABCD\) satisfy \(\angle A=2\angle B=3\angle C=4\angle D.\) What is the degree measure of \(\angle A,\) rounded to the nearest whole number?

令 \(x=\angle A\)，则 \(\angle B=\tfrac x2\)、\(\angle C=\tfrac x3\)、\(\angle D=\tfrac x4\)。

角和给出 \[ x+\dfrac x2+\dfrac x3+\dfrac x4=\dfrac{25x}{12}=360\text{，} \] 所以 \(x=\dfrac{12\cdot360}{25}=172.8\approx173\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Let \(x=\angle A.\) Then \(\angle B=\tfrac x2,\) \(\angle C=\tfrac x3,\) and \(\angle D=\tfrac x4.\)

The angle sum gives \[ x+\dfrac x2+\dfrac x3+\dfrac x4=\dfrac{25x}{12}=360, \] so \(x=\dfrac{12\cdot360}{25}=172.8\approx173.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

12单选

一位老师给一个班考试，班上 \(10\%\) 是三年级学生，\(90\%\) 是四年级学生。全班平均分为 \(84\)。所有三年级学生得分相同，四年级学生平均分为 \(83\)。每个三年级学生得了多少分？

English（选项同上方）

A teacher gave a test to a class in which \(10\%\) of the students are juniors and \(90\%\) are seniors. The average score on the test was \(84.\) The juniors all received the same score, and the average score of the seniors was \(83.\) What score did each of the juniors receive on the test?

取班上有 \(10\) 名学生，则有 \(1\) 名三年级和 \(9\) 名四年级。

全班总分为 \(10\cdot84=840\)，四年级学生贡献 \(9\cdot83=747\)。所以那名三年级学生的得分为 \[ 840-747=93\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Take a class of \(10\) students, so there is \(1\) junior and \(9\) seniors.

The total of all scores is \(10\cdot84=840,\) and the seniors contribute \(9\cdot83=747.\) So the junior scored \[ 840-747=93. \]

Thus, the correct answer is C.

13单选

一个交通灯不断重复以下周期：绿灯 \(30\) 秒，然后黄灯 \(3\) 秒，然后红灯 \(30\) 秒。Leah 随机选择一个三秒钟的时间段观察交通灯。在她观察期间灯变色的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A traffic light runs repeatedly through the following cycle: green for \(30\) seconds, then yellow for \(3\) seconds, and then red for \(30\) seconds. Leah picks a random three-second time interval to watch the light. What is the probability that the color changes while she is watching?

周期长为 \(30+3+30=63\) 秒，每周期有三次变色。

Leah 看到变色当且仅当她的三秒观察区间在某次变色前的 \(3\) 秒内开始。共有 \(3\cdot3=9\) 秒的有利开始时间，在 \(63\) 秒周期中概率为 \[ \dfrac{9}{63}=\dfrac17\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The cycle length is \(30+3+30=63\) seconds, with three color changes per cycle.

Leah sees a change exactly when her three-second interval starts within the \(3\) seconds before a switch. That gives \(3\cdot3=9\) favorable seconds out of \(63,\) a probability of \[ \dfrac{9}{63}=\dfrac17. \]

Thus, the correct answer is D.

14单选

点 \(P\) 在等边 \(\triangle ABC\) 内。点 \(Q\)、\(R\)、\(S\) 分别是从 \(P\) 到 \(\overline{AB}\)、\(\overline{BC}\)、\(\overline{CA}\) 的垂足。已知 \(PQ=1\)、\(PR=2\)、\(PS=3\)，求 \(AB\)。

English（选项同上方）

Point \(P\) is inside equilateral \(\triangle ABC.\) Points \(Q,\) \(R,\) and \(S\) are the feet of the perpendiculars from \(P\) to \(\overline{AB},\) \(\overline{BC},\) and \(\overline{CA},\) respectively. Given that \(PQ=1,\) \(PR=2,\) and \(PS=3,\) what is \(AB?\)

设 \(s=AB\)。连接 \(P\) 与各顶点，把三角形分成 \(\triangle PAB\)、\(\triangle PBC\)、\(\triangle PCA\)，它们的面积分别为 \(\tfrac{s}{2}\)、\(s\)、\(\tfrac{3s}{2}\)。

这些面积总和为 \(3s\)，必须等于等边三角形面积 \(\tfrac{\sqrt3}{4}s^2\)。因此 \[ 3s=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}s^2\text{，} \] 得 \(s=\dfrac{12}{\sqrt3}=4\sqrt3\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Let \(s=AB.\) Joining \(P\) to the vertices splits the triangle into \(\triangle PAB,\) \(\triangle PBC,\) and \(\triangle PCA,\) with areas \(\tfrac{s}{2},\) \(s,\) and \(\tfrac{3s}{2}.\)

Their total is \(3s,\) which must equal the area \(\tfrac{\sqrt3}{4}s^2\) of the equilateral triangle. So \[ 3s=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}s^2, \] giving \(s=\dfrac{12}{\sqrt3}=4\sqrt3.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

15单选

等比级数 \(a+ar+ar^2+\cdots\) 的和为 \(7\)，其中含 \(r\) 的奇次幂的项之和为 \(3\)。求 \(a+r\)。

English（选项同上方）

The geometric series \(a+ar+ar^2+\cdots\) has a sum of \(7,\) and the terms involving odd powers of \(r\) have a sum of \(3.\) What is \(a+r?\)

奇次幂项为 \(ar+ar^3+\cdots\) \(=r(a+ar^2+\cdots)\)，即偶次幂项之和的 \(r\) 倍。偶次幂项之和为 \(7-3=4\)。

因此 \(3=4r\)，得 \(r=\tfrac34\)。又 \(a=7(1-r)=\tfrac74\)，\[ a+r=\dfrac74+\dfrac34=\dfrac52\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The odd-power terms are \(ar+ar^3+\cdots\) \(=r(a+ar^2+\cdots),\) that is, \(r\) times the even-power terms. The even-power terms sum to \(7-3=4.\)

So \(3=4r,\) giving \(r=\tfrac34.\) Then \(a=7(1-r)=\tfrac74,\) and \[ a+r=\dfrac74+\dfrac34=\dfrac52. \]

Thus, the correct answer is E.

16单选

正四面体的每个面涂成红、白、蓝三色之一。若两个涂色的全等四面体可以通过旋转使外观完全相同，则认为这两种涂色不可区分。共有多少种可区分的涂色？

English（选项同上方）

Each face of a regular tetrahedron is painted either red, white, or blue. Two colorings are considered indistinguishable if two congruent tetrahedra with those colorings can be rotated so that their appearances are identical. How many distinguishable colorings are possible?

四面体旋转群有 \(12\) 个元素：恒等、\(8\) 个绕顶点-对面轴的 \(3\) 阶旋转，以及 \(3\) 个绕对边中点轴的 \(2\) 阶旋转。

恒等固定全部 \(3^4=81\) 种涂色。每个顶点旋转固定一个面并轮换另外三个面，所以固定 \(3^2=9\) 种涂色；同样地，每个边旋转交换两对面，也固定 \(3^2=9\) 种。

由 Burnside 引理，可区分涂色数为 \[ \dfrac{81+8\cdot9+3\cdot9}{12}=\dfrac{180}{12}=15\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The rotation group of the tetrahedron has \(12\) elements: the identity, \(8\) rotations of order \(3\) about a vertex-face axis, and \(3\) rotations of order \(2\) about an edge-midpoint axis.

The identity fixes all \(3^4=81\) colorings. Each vertex rotation fixes one face and cycles the other three, so it fixes \(3^2=9\) colorings; likewise each edge rotation swaps two pairs of faces and fixes \(3^2=9.\)

By Burnside’s lemma the number of distinguishable colorings is \[ \dfrac{81+8\cdot9+3\cdot9}{12}=\dfrac{180}{12}=15. \]

Thus, the correct answer is A.

17单选

若 \(a\) 是非零整数，\(b\) 是正数，且 \(ab^2=\log_{10}b\) 则集合 \(\{0,1,a,b,\frac{1}{b}\}\) 的中位数是什么？

English（选项同上方）

If \(a\) is a nonzero integer and \(b\) is a positive number such that \(ab^2=\log_{10}b,\) what is the median of the set \(\{0,1,a,b,\frac{1}{b}\}?\)

对所有 \(b\gt0\) 都有 \(b\lt10^b\)，所以 \(\log_{10}b\lt b\)。若 \(b=1\)，则 \(a=0\)，不符合条件。若 \(b\gt1\)，则 \(0\lt\dfrac{\log_{10}b}{b^2}\lt1\)，因此 \(a\) 不可能是非零整数。

所以 \(0\lt b\lt1\)，从而 \(\log_{10}b\lt0\)，且 \(a=\dfrac{\log_{10}b}{b^2}\lt0\)。排序为 \(a\lt0\lt b\lt1\lt\dfrac1b\)，所以中位数是 \(b\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

For \(b\gt0,\) we have \(b\lt10^b,\) hence \(\log_{10}b\lt b.\) If \(b=1,\) then \(a=0.\) If \(b\gt1,\) then \(0\lt\dfrac{\log_{10}b}{b^2}\lt1,\) so \(a\) could not be an integer.

Hence \(0\lt b\lt1,\) so \(\log_{10}b\lt0\) and \(a=\dfrac{\log_{10}b}{b^2}\lt0.\) Thus \(a\lt0\lt b\lt1\lt\dfrac1b,\) and the middle value of the sorted set is \(b.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

18单选

设 \(a\)、\(b\)、\(c\) 是数字，且 \(a\ne0\)。三位整数 \(\overline{abc}\) 位于某个正整数平方与下一个更大正整数平方之间三分之一处。整数 \(\overline{acb}\) 位于同两个平方之间三分之二处。求 \(a+b+c\)。

English（选项同上方）

Let \(a,\) \(b,\) and \(c\) be digits with \(a\ne0.\) The three-digit integer \(\overline{abc}\) lies one third of the way from the square of a positive integer to the square of the next larger integer. The integer \(\overline{acb}\) lies two thirds of the way between the same two squares. What is \(a+b+c?\)

设较小平方数为 \(N^2\)，较大平方数为 \((N+1)^2\)，两者间隔为 \(2N+1\)。则 \[ \overline{abc}=N^2+\dfrac{2N+1}{3}\text{，}\]\[ \overline{acb}=N^2+\dfrac{2(2N+1)}{3}\text{。}\]

两式相减，\(\overline{acb}-\overline{abc}=9(c-b)\) \(=\dfrac{2N+1}{3}\)，所以 \(27(c-b)=2N+1\)。因为 \(\overline{acb}\) 在区间中位置更靠后，所以 \(c-b\) 为正；又因为右边是奇数，所以 \(c-b\) 是奇数。若 \(c-b\ge3\)，则 \(N\ge40\)，且 \(N^2\) 不再是三位数。

因此 \(c-b=1\)，得到 \(N=13\)。从 \(13^2=169\) 到 \(14^2=196\) 的三分之一与三分之二处分别为 \(178\) 和 \(187\)，所以 \(a+b+c=1+7+8=16\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Let the smaller square be \(N^2,\) so the larger is \((N+1)^2\) and the gap is \(2N+1.\) Then \[ \overline{abc}=N^2+\dfrac{2N+1}{3}, \] \[ \overline{acb}=N^2+\dfrac{2(2N+1)}{3}. \]

Subtracting, \(\overline{acb}-\overline{abc}=9(c-b)\) \(=\dfrac{2N+1}{3},\) so \(27(c-b)=2N+1.\) Since \(\overline{acb}\) is farther along the interval, \(c-b\) is positive; and because the right side is odd, \(c-b\) is odd. If \(c-b\ge3,\) then \(N\ge40\) and \(N^2\) is not three digits.

So \(c-b=1,\) giving \(N=13.\) The points one third and two thirds of the way from \(13^2=169\) to \(14^2=196\) are \(178\) and \(187,\) so \(a+b+c=1+7+8=16.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

19单选

菱形 \(ABCD\) 的边长为 \(6\)。把 \(\overline{AB}\) 与 \(\overline{DC}\) 粘在一起，卷成体积为 \(6\) 的圆柱。求 \(\sin(\angle ABC)\)。

English（选项同上方）

Rhombus \(ABCD,\) with side length \(6,\) is rolled to form a cylinder of volume \(6\) by taping \(\overline{AB}\) to \(\overline{DC}.\) What is \(\sin(\angle ABC)?\)

令 \(\theta=\angle ABC\)。底面圆周长为 \(6\)，所以半径为 \(\dfrac{6}{2\pi}=\dfrac3\pi\)。圆柱高为菱形的高 \(6\sin\theta\)。

体积为 \[ \pi\left(\dfrac3\pi\right)^2(6\sin\theta)=\dfrac{54}{\pi}\sin\theta=6\text{，} \] 所以 \(\sin\theta=\dfrac{\pi}{9}\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Let \(\theta=\angle ABC.\) The base circle has circumference \(6,\) so its radius is \(\dfrac{6}{2\pi}=\dfrac3\pi.\) The height of the cylinder is the rhombus altitude \(6\sin\theta.\)

The volume is \[ \pi\left(\dfrac3\pi\right)^2(6\sin\theta)=\dfrac{54}{\pi}\sin\theta=6, \] so \(\sin\theta=\dfrac{\pi}{9}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

20单选

由直线 \(y=ax+c\)、\(y=ax+d\)、\(y=bx+c\)、\(y=bx+d\) 围成的平行四边形面积为 \(18\)。由直线 \(y=ax+c\)、\(y=ax-d\)、\(y=bx+c\)、\(y=bx-d\) 围成的平行四边形面积为 \(72\)。已知 \(a\)、\(b\)、\(c\)、\(d\) 为正整数，求 \(a+b+c+d\) 的最小可能值。

English（选项同上方）

The parallelogram bounded by the lines \(y=ax+c,\) \(y=ax+d,\) \(y=bx+c,\) and \(y=bx+d\) has area \(18.\) The parallelogram bounded by the lines \(y=ax+c,\) \(y=ax-d,\) \(y=bx+c,\) and \(y=bx-d\) has area \(72.\) Given that \(a,\) \(b,\) \(c,\) and \(d\) are positive integers, what is the smallest possible value of \(a+b+c+d?\)

第一个平行四边形有两个顶点在 \((0,c)\) 和 \((0,d)\)，另外两个顶点的 \(x\)-坐标为 \(\pm\dfrac{c-d}{b-a}\)。因此第一个平行四边形面积为 \(\dfrac{(c-d)^2}{|b-a|}=18\)，第二个为 \(\dfrac{(c+d)^2}{|b-a|}=72\)。

于是 \((c-d)^2=18|b-a|\)，\((c+d)^2=72|b-a|\)。两式相减得 \(4cd=54|b-a|\)，即 \(2cd=27|b-a|\)。

因此 \(|b-a|\) 是偶数，取 \(\{a,b\}=\{1,3\}\) 时 \(a+b\) 最小；又 \(cd\) 是 \(27\) 的倍数，取 \(\{c,d\}=\{3,9\}\) 时 \(c+d\) 最小。这些取值满足全部条件，于是 \(a+b+c+d=1+3+3+9\) \(=16\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Two vertices of the first parallelogram lie at \((0,c)\) and \((0,d),\) and the other two have \(x\)-coordinates \(\pm\dfrac{c-d}{b-a}.\) Its area works out to \(\dfrac{(c-d)^2}{|b-a|}=18.\) The same computation for the second gives \(\dfrac{(c+d)^2}{|b-a|}=72.\)

So \((c-d)^2=18|b-a|\) and \((c+d)^2=72|b-a|.\) Subtracting, \(4cd=54|b-a|,\) i.e. \(2cd=27|b-a|.\)

Thus \(|b-a|\) is even, so \(a+b\) is smallest with \(\{a,b\}=\{1,3\};\) and \(cd\) is a multiple of \(27,\) so \(c+d\) is smallest with \(\{c,d\}=\{3,9\}.\) These satisfy all conditions, giving \(a+b+c+d=1+3+3+9\) \(=16.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

21单选

前 \(2007\) 个正整数都写成 \(3\) 进制。其中有多少个 \(3\) 进制表示是回文数？（回文数从前往后读与从后往前读相同。）

English（选项同上方）

The first \(2007\) positive integers are each written in base \(3.\) How many of these base-\(3\) representations are palindromes? (A palindrome is a number that reads the same forward and backward.)

回文数由前半部分决定。按 \(3\) 进制位数计数，长度 \(1\) 或 \(2\) 的有 \(2\) 个，长度 \(3\) 或 \(4\) 的有 \(6\) 个，长度 \(5\) 或 \(6\) 的有 \(18\) 个，长度 \(7\) 的有 \(54\) 个。

最多七位的总数为 \(2+2+6+6+18+18\) \(+54=106\)。在 \(7\) 位数中，因为 \(2007=2202100_3\)，超过它的 \(7\) 位回文数为 \(2210122\)、\(2211122\)、\(2212122\)、\(2220222\)、\(2221222\)、\(2222222\)，共有 \(6\) 个。

因此答案为 \(106-6=100\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

A palindrome is fixed by its first half. Counting base-\(3\) palindromes by length gives \(2\) of length \(1\) or \(2,\) \(6\) of length \(3\) or \(4,\) \(18\) of length \(5\) or \(6,\) and \(54\) of length \(7.\)

That totals \(2+2+6+6+18+18\) \(+54=106\) palindromes with at most \(7\) digits. Since \(2007=2202100_3,\) the \(7\)-digit palindromes larger than it are \(2210122,\) \(2211122,\) \(2212122,\) \(2220222,\) \(2221222,\) and \(2222222,\) which is \(6\) of them.

Therefore the count is \(106-6=100.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

22单选

两个粒子沿等边 \(\triangle ABC\) 的边按方向 \[A\to B\to C\to A\text{，}\] 同时同速运动。一个从 \(A\) 出发，另一个从 \(\overline{BC}\) 的中点出发。连接两个粒子的线段的中点所走路径围成区域 \(R\)。求 \(R\) 的面积与 \(\triangle ABC\) 面积之比。

English（选项同上方）

Two particles move along the edges of equilateral \(\triangle ABC\) in the direction \[A\to B\to C\to A,\] starting simultaneously and moving at the same speed. One starts at \(A,\) and the other starts at the midpoint of \(\overline{BC}.\) The midpoint of the line segment joining the two particles traces out a path that encloses a region \(R.\) What is the ratio of the area of \(R\) to the area of \(\triangle ABC?\)

设 \(D,E,F\) 分别为 \(BC,CA,AB\) 的中点，设 \(X,Y,Z\) 分别为 \(AD,BE,CF\) 的中点。追踪一个始终位于两个粒子正中间的第三点：当两个粒子位于 \(A,D\) 时，它位于 \(X\)；当它们位于 \(F,C\) 时，它位于 \(Z\)；当它们位于 \(B,E\) 时，它位于 \(Y\)。

在这些时刻之间，两个粒子的位置都线性变化，所以它们的中点依次描出线段 \(XZ,ZY,YX\)。因此围成的轨迹是等边三角形 \(XYZ\)，由对称性，它与 \(\triangle ABC\) 有同一个中心 \(O\)。因为 \(Z\) 是中线 \(CF\) 的中点，\[ \begin{aligned} OZ&=OC-ZC \\ &=\dfrac23 CF-\dfrac12 CF \\ &=\dfrac16 CF \end{aligned}\text{，}\]而 \(OC=\dfrac23 CF\)。

所以外接圆半径之比为 \(\dfrac{OZ}{OC}=\dfrac14\)，面积之比为 \(\left(\dfrac14\right)^2=\dfrac{1}{16}\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Let \(D,E,F\) be the midpoints of \(BC,CA,AB,\) respectively, and let \(X,Y,Z\) be the midpoints of \(AD,BE,CF,\) respectively. Track a third point halfway between the two particles. It is at \(X\) when the particles are at \(A,D;\) at \(Z\) when they are at \(F,C;\) and at \(Y\) when they are at \(B,E.\)

Between these instants both particle positions vary linearly, so their midpoint traces the segments \(XZ,ZY,YX.\) Thus the enclosed path is the equilateral triangle \(XYZ,\) which by symmetry shares the center \(O\) of \(\triangle ABC.\) Because \(Z\) is the midpoint of the median \(CF,\) \[ \begin{aligned} OZ&=OC-ZC \\ &=\dfrac23 CF-\dfrac12 CF \\ &=\dfrac16 CF, \end{aligned} \] while \(OC=\dfrac23 CF.\)

So the ratio of circumradii is \(\dfrac{OZ}{OC}=\dfrac14,\) and the area ratio is \(\left(\dfrac14\right)^2=\dfrac{1}{16}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

23单选

有多少个互不全等、直角边长为正整数的直角三角形，其面积的数值等于其周长的 \(3\) 倍？

English（选项同上方）

How many non-congruent right triangles with positive integer leg lengths have areas that are numerically equal to \(3\) times their perimeters?

设直角边为 \(a\le b\)。条件为 \(\tfrac12 ab=3\left(a+b+\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\right)\)，所以 \[ ab-6a-6b=6\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\text{。}\]

平方并化简得到 \(ab(ab-12a-12b+72)=0\)，所以 \((a-12)(b-12)=72\)。正整数解为 \((a,b)=(3,4)\)，\((13,84)\)，\((14,48)\)，\((15,36)\)，\((16,30)\)，\((18,24)\)，\((20,21)\)。

其中 \((3,4)\) 是伪解：它的面积为 \(6\)，而它的周长为 \(12\)，周长的三倍是 \(36\)。所以恰有 \(6\) 个三角形。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Let the legs be \(a\le b.\) The condition is \(\tfrac12 ab=3\left(a+b+\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\right),\) so \[ ab-6a-6b=6\sqrt{a^2+b^2}. \]

Squaring and simplifying gives \(ab(ab-12a-12b+72)=0,\) hence \((a-12)(b-12)=72.\) The positive integer solutions are \((a,b)=(3,4),\) \((13,84),\) \((14,48),\) \((15,36),\) \((16,30),\) \((18,24),\) \((20,21).\)

The pair \((3,4)\) is extraneous: its area is \(6,\) while its perimeter is \(12\) and three times that is \(36.\) So exactly \(6\) triangles work.

Thus, the correct answer is A.

24单选

有多少对正整数 \((a,b)\) 满足 \(\gcd(a,b)=1\)，并且 \[\dfrac{a}{b}+\dfrac{14b}{9a}\] 是整数？

English（选项同上方）

How many pairs of positive integers \((a,b)\) are there such that \(\gcd(a,b)=1\) and \[\dfrac{a}{b}+\dfrac{14b}{9a}\] is an integer?

设表达式等于整数 \(k\)。乘以 \(b\) 并减去 \(a\)，得 \(\dfrac{14b^2}{9a}=bk-a\) 为整数。由于 \(\gcd(a,b)=1\)，可得 \(14\) 是 \(a\) 的倍数。另一方面，乘以 \(9a\) 并减去 \(14b\)，得 \(\dfrac{9a^2}{b}=9ak-14b\)，所以 \(9\) 是 \(b\) 的倍数。

因此 \(a\in\{1,2,7,14\}\)，\(b\in\{1,3,9\}\)。检验互质候选，表达式为整数的只有 \((a,b)=(1,3)\)，\((2,3)\)，\((7,3)\)，\((14,3)\)。

所以共有 \(4\) 对。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Let \(k\) be the integer value of the original expression. Multiplying by \(b\) and subtracting \(a\) gives \(\dfrac{14b^2}{9a}=bk-a,\) an integer. Since \(\gcd(a,b)=1,\) it follows that \(14\) is divisible by \(a.\) Multiplying instead by \(9a\) and subtracting \(14b\) gives \(\dfrac{9a^2}{b}=9ak-14b,\) so \(9\) is divisible by \(b.\)

Thus \(a\in\{1,2,7,14\}\) and \(b\in\{1,3,9\}.\) Checking the coprime candidates, the expression is an integer only for \((a,b)=(1,3),\) \((2,3),\) \((7,3),\) \((14,3).\)

So there are \(4\) such pairs.

Thus, the correct answer is A.

25单选

点 \(A\)、\(B\)、\(C\)、\(D\)、\(E\) 位于 \(3\) 维空间中，满足 \(AB=BC=CD\) \(=DE=EA=2\)，且 \(\angle ABC=\angle CDE\) \(=\angle DEA=90^\circ\)。\(\triangle ABC\) 所在平面平行于 \(\overline{DE}\)。求 \(\triangle BDE\) 的面积。

English（选项同上方）

Points \(A,\) \(B,\) \(C,\) \(D,\) and \(E\) are located in \(3\)-dimensional space with \(AB=BC=CD\) \(=DE=EA=2\) and \(\angle ABC=\angle CDE\) \(=\angle DEA=90^\circ.\) The plane of \(\triangle ABC\) is parallel to \(\overline{DE}.\) What is the area of \(\triangle BDE?\)

取 \(D=(-1,0,0)\)、\(E=(1,0,0)\)，令 \(\triangle ABC\) 在平面 \(z=k\gt0\) 中。由于 \(\angle CDE\) 和 \(\angle DEA\) 为直角，\(A\) 和 \(C\) 分别位于以 \(E\) 和 \(D\) 为圆心、半径为 \(2\) 的圆上；这两个圆所在的平面分别为 \(x=1\) 和 \(x=-1\)。因此 \(A=(1,y_1,k),\ C=(-1,y_2,k)\)，其中 \(y_j=\pm\sqrt{4-k^2}\)。

因为 \(\angle ABC=90^\circ\)，所以 \(AC=2\sqrt2\)，这迫使 \(y_1=-y_2\)。取 \(y_1=1\)，\(y_2=-1\)，得 \(k=\sqrt3\)，于是 \(A=(1,1,\sqrt3)\)，\(C=(-1,-1,\sqrt3)\)，而 \(B\) 为 \((1,-1,\sqrt3)\) 或 \((-1,1,\sqrt3)\)。

第一种情况下 \(BE=2\) 且 \(BE\perp DE\)；第二种情况下 \(BD=2\) 且 \(BD\perp DE\)。无论哪种，\(\triangle BDE\) 的两条垂直边长度分别为 \(2\) 和 \(2\)，面积为 \(\tfrac12(2)(2)=2\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Set \(D=(-1,0,0)\) and \(E=(1,0,0),\) and let \(\triangle ABC\) lie in the plane \(z=k\gt0.\) Because \(\angle CDE\) and \(\angle DEA\) are right angles, \(A\) and \(C\) lie on radius-\(2\) circles centered at \(E\) and \(D\) in the planes \(x=1\) and \(x=-1,\) so \(A=(1,y_1,k),\ C=(-1,y_2,k)\) with \(y_j=\pm\sqrt{4-k^2}.\)

Since \(\angle ABC=90^\circ,\) \(AC=2\sqrt2,\) which forces \(y_1=-y_2.\) Taking \(y_1=1,\) \(y_2=-1,\) gives \(k=\sqrt3,\) so \(A=(1,1,\sqrt3),\) \(C=(-1,-1,\sqrt3),\) and \(B\) is \((1,-1,\sqrt3)\) or \((-1,1,\sqrt3).\)

In the first case \(BE=2\) with \(BE\perp DE;\) in the second \(BD=2\) with \(BD\perp DE.\) Either way \(\triangle BDE\) has legs \(2\) and \(2,\) so its area is \(\tfrac12(2)(2)=2.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.
