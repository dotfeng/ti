1单选

求下式的值：\[\dfrac{2+4+6}{1+3+5} - \dfrac{1+3+5}{2+4+6}\]

English（选项同上方）

What is the value of the following expression? \[\dfrac{2+4+6}{1+3+5} - \dfrac{1+3+5}{2+4+6}\]

直接计算：\[\begin{aligned} &\dfrac{2+4+6}{1+3+5} - \dfrac{1+3+5}{2+4+6} \\ &= \dfrac{12}9 - \dfrac 9{12} \\ &= \dfrac {16}{12} - \dfrac 9{12} \\ &=\dfrac 7{12} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Simply solving directly: \[\begin{aligned} &\dfrac{2+4+6}{1+3+5} - \dfrac{1+3+5}{2+4+6} \\ &= \dfrac{12}9 - \dfrac 9{12} \\ &= \dfrac {16}{12} - \dfrac 9{12} \\ &=\dfrac 7{12}. \end{aligned}\]

Thus, the correct answer is C .

2单选

Josanna 到目前为止的考试分数为 \(90\)、\(80\)、\(70\)、\(60\) 和 \(85\)。她的目标是在下一次考试后把考试平均分至少提高 \(3\) 分。她至少需要在下一次考试中得到多少分？

English（选项同上方）

Josanna’s test scores to date are \(90,\) \(80,\) \(70,\) \(60,\) and \(85.\) Her goal is to raise her test average at least \(3\) points with her next test. What is the minimum test score she would need to accomplish this goal?

她当前的平均分为 \[\dfrac{90+80+70+60+85}5 = 77\text{，}\]而总分为 \(385\)。目标平均分为 \(80\)，所以六次考试所需的总分为 \(80\cdot 6\)。因此下一次考试至少需要得 \[80\cdot 6-385 = 95\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Her current average is \[\dfrac{90+80+70+60+85}5 = 77,\] and the sum of her scores is \(385.\) The desired average is then \(80,\) so the sum of scores required is \(80\cdot 6.\) Therefore, the answer is \[80\cdot 6-385 = 95.\]

Thus, the correct answer is E .

At a store, when a length is reported as \(x\) inches that means it is at least \(x - 0.5\) inches and at most \(x + 0.5\) inches. Suppose the dimensions of a rectangular tile are reported as \(2\) inches by \(3\) inches. In square inches, what is the minimum area for the rectangle?

最小可能尺寸为 \(1.5\times 2.5\)，所以面积为 \[1.5\cdot 2.5=3.75\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The smallest possible dimensions are \(1.5\times 2.5,\) so the area is \[1.5\cdot 2.5=3.75.\]

Thus, the correct answer is A .

4单选

LeRoy 和 Bernardo 一起进行了一周旅行，并同意平分费用。一周中，两人分别支付了一些共同费用，例如汽油和租车。旅行结束时，LeRoy 支付了 \(A\) 美元，Bernardo 支付了 \(B\) 美元，其中 \(A < B\)。LeRoy 应给 Bernardo 多少美元，才能使两人平分费用？

English（选项同上方）

LeRoy and Bernardo went on a week-long trip together and agreed to share the costs equally. Over the week, each of them paid for various joint expenses such as gasoline and car rental. At the end of the trip it turned out that LeRoy had paid \(A\) dollars and Bernardo had paid \(B\) dollars, where \(A < B.\) How many dollars must LeRoy give to Bernardo so that they share the costs equally?

两人每人应承担 \(\dfrac{A+B}2\)，而 LeRoy 已支付 \(A\)。因此他还需向 Bernardo 支付 \[\dfrac{A+B}2-A = \dfrac{B-A}2\] 美元。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The amount they each would have to pay is \(\dfrac{A+B}2,\) and LeRoy paid \(A.\) Thus, he has to pay \[\dfrac{A+B}2-A = \dfrac{B-A}2\] more.

Thus, the correct answer is C .

5单选

在计算两个正整数 \(a\) 和 \(b\) 的乘积时，Ron 把两位数 \(a\) 的数字顺序颠倒了。他错误得到的乘积是 \(161\)。\(a\) 与 \(b\) 的正确乘积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

In multiplying two positive integers \(a\) and \(b,\) Ron reversed the digits of the two-digit number \(a.\) His erroneous product was \(161.\) What is the correct value of the product of \(a\) and \(b?\)

数 \(161\) 等于 \(7\cdot 23\)。\(161\) 除了 \(1\cdot 161\) 外没有其他因数对，所以 \(23\) 是唯一的两位因数。因此 \(23\) 是颠倒后的数，原来的乘法应为 \(32\cdot 7\)，结果是 \(224\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The number \(161\) is equal to \(7\cdot 23.\) There are no other pairs of numbers that multiply to \(161\) besides \(1\cdot 161,\) so \(23\) is the only two digit factor. Thus, \(23\) is the number reversed, so he meant to get \(32\cdot 7\) which is \(224.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E .

6单选

万圣节时 Casper 吃掉了自己糖果的 \(\frac{1}{3}\)，然后给了弟弟 \(2\) 颗。第二天，他吃掉剩余糖果的 \(\frac{1}{3}\)，然后给了妹妹 \(4\) 颗。第三天，他吃掉最后的 \(8\) 颗糖果。Casper 一开始有多少颗糖果？

English（选项同上方）

On Halloween Casper ate \(\frac{1}{3}\) of his candies and then gave \(2\) candies to his brother. The next day he ate \(\frac{1}{3}\) of his remaining candies and then gave \(4\) candies to his sister. On the third day he ate his final \(8\) candies. How many candies did Casper have at the beginning?

倒推。给妹妹 \(4\) 颗之前，Casper 有 \(12\) 颗糖果，因为第二天结束时剩 \(8\) 颗。

这 \(12\) 颗是第一天结束后糖果数的 \(\dfrac23\)，所以第一天结束后有 \(18\) 颗。

给弟弟 \(2\) 颗之前有 \(20\) 颗。这是原来糖果数的 \(\dfrac23\)，所以一开始有 \(30\) 颗。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Work backward. Before giving \(4\) candies to his sister, Casper had \(12\) candies, because he ended the second day with \(8\).

Those \(12\) candies were \(\dfrac23\) of what he had after the first day, so after the first day he had \(18\) candies.

Before giving \(2\) candies to his brother, he had \(20\) candies. This was \(\dfrac23\) of his original amount, so he began with \(30\) candies.

Thus, A is the correct answer.

7单选

一个三角形中两个角的和是直角的 \(\frac{6}{5}\)，且这两个角中的一个比另一个大 \(30^{\circ}\)。这个三角形最大角的度数是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The sum of two angles of a triangle is \(\frac{6}{5}\) of a right angle, and one of these two angles is \(30^{\circ}\) larger than the other. What is the degree measure of the largest angle in the triangle?

这两个角之和为 \(\frac 65 \cdot 90 = 108\)。所以第三个角为 \(180-108=72\)。若这两个角中较大的为 \(x\)，较小的为 \(x-30\)，则 \(2x-30=108\)，所以 \(x=69\)。

没有角大于 \(72\)，因此最大角是 \(72\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The two angles add to \(\frac 65 \cdot 90 = 108.\) This makes the other angle \(180-108=72.\) Then, if the larger of the two angles is \(x\) then the smaller of them is \(x-30\) so their sum is \(2x-30=108,\) making \(x=69.\)

This means no angle is larger than \(72,\) making the largest equal to \(72.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B .

8单选

某海滩在气温至少为 \(80^{\circ} F\) 且晴朗时会很拥挤。六月 \(10\) 日该海滩不拥挤。关于六月 \(10\) 日的天气条件，可以推出什么？

English（选项同上方）

At a certain beach if it is at least \(80^{\circ} F\) and sunny, then the beach will be crowded. On June \(10\) the beach was not crowded. What can be concluded about the weather conditions on June \(10?\)

原命题是：若气温至少 \(80^\circ F\) 且晴朗，则海滩拥挤。

因为海滩不拥挤，这两个条件不可能同时成立。因此气温低于 \(80^\circ F\)，或者天气不晴朗，或者两者都成立。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The statement says that if the weather was at least \(80^\circ F\) and sunny, then the beach was crowded.

Because the beach was not crowded, the two conditions could not both have been true. Therefore the temperature was cooler than \(80^\circ F\), or it was not sunny, or both.

Thus, B is the correct answer.

9单选

\(\triangle EBD\) 的面积是 \(3\)-\(4\)-\(5\) 三角形 \(ABC\) 面积的三分之一。线段 \(DE\) 垂直于线段 \(AB\)。\(BD\) 是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The area of \(\triangle EBD\) is one third of the area of the \(3\)-\(4\)-\(5\) triangle \(ABC.\) Segment \(DE\) is perpendicular to segment \(AB.\) What is \(BD?\)

由角角相似，\(BDE \sim BCA \)。

面积比为 \(\frac 13\)，所以对应边长比为 \(\frac{1}{\sqrt 3}\)。

因此 \[\dfrac{BD}{BC} = \dfrac{BD}4 = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt 3}\text{，}\]从而 \[BD = \dfrac 4{ \sqrt 3} = \dfrac{4\sqrt{3}}{3}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

By angle angle similarity, we have \(BDE \sim BCA .\)

Then, since the ratio of the areas is \(\frac 13,\) the ratio of the sidelengths is \(\frac{1}{\sqrt 3}.\)

As such, \[\dfrac{BD}{BC} = \dfrac{BD}4 = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt 3}, \] making \[BD = \dfrac 4{ \sqrt 3} = \dfrac{4\sqrt{3}}{3} .\]

Thus, the correct answer is D .

10单选

考虑集合 \(\{1, 10, 10^2, 10^3, \ldots, 10^{10}\}\)。该集合最大元素与其他十个元素之和的比值最接近哪个整数？

English（选项同上方）

Consider the set of numbers \(\{1, 10, 10^2, 10^3, \ldots, 10^{10}\}.\) The ratio of the largest element of the set to the sum of the other ten elements of the set is closest to which integer?

最大元素为 \(10^{10}\)。其余十个元素之和为 \[S=\sum_{i=0}^9 10^i=\dfrac{10^{10}-1}{9}\text{。}\]因此所求比值为 \[\dfrac{10^{10}}{S}=9\cdot\dfrac{10^{10}}{10^{10}-1}\text{，}\]它略大于 \(9\)，所以最接近 \(9\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The largest number is \(10^{10}.\) The other ten numbers have sum \[S=\sum_{i=0}^9 10^i=\dfrac{10^{10}-1}{9}.\] Therefore their ratio is \[\dfrac{10^{10}}{S}=9\cdot\dfrac{10^{10}}{10^{10}-1},\] which is just slightly greater than \(9\) and hence closest to \(9.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B .

11单选

一个房间里有 \(52\) 人。使“这个房间中至少有 \(n\) 人的生日在同一个月份”这一陈述总为真的最大 \(n\) 是多少？

English（选项同上方）

There are \(52\) people in a room. What is the largest value of \(n\) such that the statement “At least \(n\) people in this room have birthdays falling in the same month” is always true?

\(n\geq 6\) 不一定成立，因为可以让 \(5\) 人的生日落在前 \(4\) 个月中的每个月，而其余八个月各有 \(4\) 人生日。

但一定有某个月至少 \(5\) 人生日，因为平均每月人数为 \(\frac{52}{12}\)，大于 \(4\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

It isn’t necessarily true for \(n\geq 6\) as we could have \(5\) people born in the first \(4\) months and \(4\) people born in the subsequent months.

However, one month must be greater than or equal to \(5\) as the average of the number of people born in each month is \(\frac{52}{12}\) which is greater than \(4,\) and some month must be above average.

Thus, the correct answer is D .

12单选

Keiko 每天以完全相同的恒定速度绕跑道走一圈。跑道两侧为直线，两端为半圆。跑道宽 \(6\) 米，她沿外侧边缘走一圈比沿内侧边缘走一圈多用 \(36\) 秒。Keiko 的速度是多少米每秒？

English（选项同上方）

Keiko walks once around a track at exactly the same constant speed every day. The sides of the track are straight, and the ends are semicircles. The track has a width of \(6\) meters, and it takes her \(36\) seconds longer to walk around the outside edge of the track than around the inside edge. What is Keiko’s speed in meters per second?

设内侧半圆半径为 \(r\)。

内外路径的直线部分总长相同，所以距离差只来自两个半圆端。两个内侧半圆总长为 \(2\pi r\)，两个外侧半圆总长为 \(2\pi(r+6)\)，差为 \(12\pi\)。

Keiko 多用 \(36\) 秒走了 \(12\pi\) 米，所以速度为 \(\frac{12\pi}{36}=\dfrac\pi3\) 米每秒。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Let the inner semicircle radius be \(r\). The straight parts of the inside and outside paths have the same total length, so only the semicircular ends change the distance.

The two inner semicircles have total length \(2\pi r\), while the two outer semicircles have total length \(2\pi(r+6)\). The outside path is therefore \(12\pi\) meters longer.

Keiko takes \(36\) more seconds to walk \(12\pi\) more meters, so her speed is \(\frac{12\pi}{36}=\dfrac\pi3\) meters per second.

Thus, A is the correct answer.

13单选

从区间 \([-20, 10]\) 中独立随机选择两个实数。它们的乘积大于零的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Two real numbers are selected independently at random from the interval \([-20, 10].\) What is the probability that the product of those numbers is greater than zero?

选中的数为负数的概率是 \(\frac{20}{30}=\frac{2}{3}\)，为正数的概率是 \(\frac{10}{30}=\frac{1}{3}\)。（恰好选中 \(0\) 的概率为 \(0\)。）乘积为正当且仅当两个数同号，所以所求概率为 \[\left(\dfrac23\right)^2+\left(\dfrac13\right)^2=\dfrac49+\dfrac19=\dfrac59\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

A selected number is negative with probability \(\frac{20}{30}=\frac{2}{3}\) and positive with probability \(\frac{10}{30}=\frac{1}{3}.\) (Selecting exactly \(0\) has probability \(0.\)) The product is positive exactly when both numbers have the same sign, so the probability is \[\left(\dfrac23\right)^2+\left(\dfrac13\right)^2=\dfrac49+\dfrac19=\dfrac59.\]

Thus, the correct answer is D .

14单选

一个长方形停车场的对角线长 \(25\) 米，面积为 \(168\) 平方米。该停车场的周长是多少米？

English（选项同上方）

A rectangular parking lot has a diagonal of \(25\) meters and an area of \(168\) square meters. In meters, what is the perimeter of the parking lot?

设长方形边长为 \(l,w\)。要求的是 \(2(l+w)\)。由勾股定理，\[25 = \sqrt{l^2+w^2}\] \[l^2+w^2 = 625\] 并且 \(lw = 168\)。

因此 \[\begin{aligned}l^2+2lw+ w^2 &= (l+w)^2 \\&= 961 \\&= 31^2\end{aligned}\text{。}\]所以 \(l+w = 31\)，周长为 \(31\cdot 2=62\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Let the side lengths be \(l,w.\) We wish to find \(2(l+w).\) From the Pythagorean Theorem, we get \[25 = \sqrt{l^2+w^2}\]\[l^2+w^2 = 625\] We also know \(lw = 168.\)

As such \[\begin{aligned}l^2+2lw+ w^2 &= (l+w)^2 \\&= 961 \\&= 31^2.\end{aligned}\] This makes \(l+w = 31,\) and as such, our answer is \(31\cdot 2=62.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C .

15单选

令 \(@\) 表示“与……取平均”的运算：\(a @ b = \frac{a+b}{2}\)。下列哪些分配律对所有数 \(x\)、\(y\) 和 \(z\) 都成立？

I. \(x @ (y + z) = (x @ y) + (x @ z)\)

II. \(x + (y @ z) = (x + y) @ (x + z)\)

III. \(x @ (y @ z) = (x @ y) @ (x @ z)\)

English（选项同上方）

Let \(@\) denote the “averaged with” operation: \(a @ b = \frac{a+b}{2}.\) Which of the following distributive laws hold for all numbers \(x,\) \(y,\) and \(z?\)

I. \(x @ (y + z) = (x @ y) + (x @ z)\)

II. \(x + (y @ z) = (x + y) @ (x + z)\)

III. \(x @ (y @ z) = (x @ y) @ (x @ z)\)

I 的左边为 \(\dfrac{x+y+z}2\)，右边为 \(x+\dfrac{y+z}2\)，不恒相等。

II 的左右两边都等于 \(x+\dfrac{y+z}2\)，所以恒成立。

III 的左右两边都等于 \(\dfrac{2x+y+z}4\)，所以也恒成立。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

In statement I, the left-hand side equals \(\dfrac{x+y+z}2\) and the right-hand side equals \(x+\dfrac{y+z}2,\) so they are not always equal.

In statement II, both sides equal \(x+\dfrac{y+z}2,\) so it holds.

In statement III, both sides equal \(\dfrac{2x+y+z}4,\) so it also holds.

Thus, the correct answer is E .

16单选

一个飞镖靶是如图所示分区的正八边形。假设飞镖落在靶上任意位置的概率相同。飞镖落在中心正方形内的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A dart board is a regular octagon divided into regions as shown. Suppose that a dart thrown at the board is equally likely to land anywhere on the board. What is the probability that the dart lands within the center square?

设中心正方形边长为 \(1\)。则中心面积为 \(1\)。

八边形可看作一个大正方形去掉 \(4\) 个等腰直角三角形。大正方形边长为 \[1 + 2\cdot \dfrac 1{\sqrt 2} = 1 + \sqrt 2\text{，}\]所以面积为 \[(1+\sqrt 2)^2 = 3 + 2 \sqrt 2\text{。}\]

这些直角三角形的直角边长为 \(\dfrac {1}{\sqrt 2} \)，每个面积为 \[\dfrac {\frac {1}{\sqrt 2}^2}{2} = \dfrac 14\text{。}\]四个总面积为 \(1\)，所以八边形面积为 \(2+ 2 \sqrt 2\)。

因此比值为 \[\begin{aligned}&\dfrac 1{2+ 2 \sqrt 2} \\&= \dfrac {-2+ 2 \sqrt 2}{(2+ 2 \sqrt 2)(-2+ 2 \sqrt 2)}\\ &=\dfrac {2(\sqrt 2-1)}{4} \\&=\dfrac {\sqrt 2-1}{2} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Let the side length be \(1.\) Then, the area of the center is \(1.\)

Then, we must find the area of the octagon. It can be found as a square with \(4\) isosceles right triangles taken out. The side length of this square is \[1 + 2\cdot \dfrac 1{\sqrt 2} = 1 + \sqrt 2 .\] It has an area of \[(1+\sqrt 2)^2 = 3 + 2 \sqrt 2.\]

Then, the side length of the right triangles is \(\dfrac {1}{\sqrt 2} ,\) making the area of one equal to \[ \dfrac {\frac {1}{\sqrt 2}^2}{2} = \dfrac 14 .\] This makes them have a total combined area of \(1,\) so the area of the octagon is \(2+ 2 \sqrt 2.\)

Thus, the ratio is \[\begin{aligned}&\dfrac 1{2+ 2 \sqrt 2} \\&= \dfrac {-2+ 2 \sqrt 2}{(2+ 2 \sqrt 2)(-2+ 2 \sqrt 2)}\\ &=\dfrac {2(\sqrt 2-1)}{4} \\&=\dfrac {\sqrt 2-1}{2}. \end{aligned}\]

Thus, the correct answer is A .

17单选

在给定圆中，直径 \(\overline{EB}\) 平行于 \(\overline{DC}\)，且 \(\overline{AB}\) 平行于 \(\overline{ED}\)。角 \(AEB\) 与 \(ABE\) 的比为 \(4 : 5\)。角 \(BCD\) 的度数是多少？

English（选项同上方）

In the given circle, the diameter \(\overline{EB}\) is parallel to \(\overline{DC},\) and \(\overline{AB}\) is parallel to \(\overline{ED}.\) The angles \(AEB\) and \(ABE\) are in the ratio \(4 : 5.\) What is the degree measure of angle \(BCD?\)

因为 \(EB\) 是直径，\(\angle EAB=90^\circ\)。由 \(\angle AEB:\angle ABE=4:5\)，得 \(\angle AEB=40^\circ\)，\(\angle ABE=50^\circ\)。

因为 \(AB\parallel ED\)，所以 \(\angle DEB=50^\circ\)。又 \(EB\parallel DC\)，四边形 \(EBCD\) 是等腰梯形，所以 \(\angle BCD=\angle CDE\)。

角 \(DEB\) 与 \(CDE\) 互补，所以 \(\angle CDE=180^\circ-50^\circ=130^\circ\)，因此 \(\angle BCD=130^\circ\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Since \(EB\) is a diameter, \(\angle EAB=90^\circ\). The ratio \(\angle AEB:\angle ABE=4:5\) then gives \(\angle AEB=40^\circ\) and \(\angle ABE=50^\circ\).

Because \(AB\parallel ED\), \(\angle DEB=50^\circ\). Since \(EB\parallel DC\), quadrilateral \(EBCD\) is an isosceles trapezoid, so \(\angle BCD=\angle CDE\).

Angles \(DEB\) and \(CDE\) are supplementary, so \(\angle CDE=180^\circ-50^\circ=130^\circ\). Hence \(\angle BCD=130^\circ\).

Thus, C is the correct answer.

Rectangle \(ABCD\) has \(AB = 6\) and \(BC = 3.\) Point \(M\) is chosen on side \(AB\) so that \(\angle AMD = \angle CMD.\) What is the degree measure of \(\angle AMD?\)

角 \(\angle AMD\) 与 \(\angle MDC\) 相等，因为 \(AB \parallel DC\)。

因此 \(\angle MDC = \angle DMC \)，所以三角形 \(MDC\) 为等腰三角形，\(MC = DC = 6\)。

在直角三角形中，由边长比例可得 \(\sin (\angle CMB) = \frac 12\)，因此 \(\angle CMB = 30^\circ \)。

因此 \(\angle AMC = 150^\circ \)。由于 \(\angle AMD\) 是它的一半，\(\angle AMD = 75^\circ \)。

English

The angles \(\angle AMD \) and \(\angle MDC \) are equal since \(AB \parallel DC.\)

As such, \(\angle MDC = \angle DMC ,\) making \(MDC \) isosceles and \(MC = DC = 6.\)

As we can see, \(\sin (\angle CMB) = \frac 12,\) making \(\angle CMB = 30^\circ .\)

下列方程所有根的乘积是多少？\[\sqrt{5 | x | + 8} = \sqrt{x^2 - 16}\]

English（选项同上方）

What is the product of all the roots of the equation below? \[\sqrt{5 | x | + 8} = \sqrt{x^2 - 16}\]

因为 \(x^2=|x|^2\)，将方程两边平方可得 \[\begin{aligned} 5|x|+8 &= |x|^2-16,\\ |x|^2-5|x|-24&=0,\\ (|x|-8)(|x|+3)&=0 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]\(|x|\) 在代数上可能为 \(8\) 或 \(-3\)，但绝对值不能为负。因此 \(|x|=8\)，两个根为 \(8\) 和 \(-8\)，乘积为 \(-64\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Because \(x^2=|x|^2,\) squaring the equation gives \[\begin{aligned} 5|x|+8 &= |x|^2-16,\\ |x|^2-5|x|-24&=0,\\ (|x|-8)(|x|+3)&=0. \end{aligned}\] The algebraic possibilities for \(|x|\) are \(8\) and \(-3,\) but an absolute value cannot be negative. Hence \(|x|=8,\) so the roots are \(8\) and \(-8\), with product \(-64.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A .

Rhombus \(ABCD\) has side length \(2\) and \(\angle B = 120^\circ\). Region \(R\) consists of all points inside the rhombus that are closer to vertex \(B\) than any of the other three vertices. What is the area of \(R?\)

三角形 \(ABC\) 面积为 \(\dfrac12\cdot2\cdot2\sin120^\circ=\sqrt3\)。\(BA\) 和 \(BC\) 的垂直平分线切去两个全等的 \(30\)-\(60\)-\(90\) 三角形，每个面积为 \(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{6}\)。

所求面积为 \(\sqrt3-2\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{6}=\dfrac{2\sqrt3}{3}\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The points closer to \(B\) than to another vertex are bounded by the perpendicular bisectors of \(BA\), \(BC\), and \(BD\). The bisector of \(BD\) is diagonal \(AC\), so the desired region lies in \(\triangle ABC\).

Triangle \(ABC\) has area \(\dfrac12\cdot2\cdot2\sin120^\circ=\sqrt3\). The perpendicular bisectors of \(BA\) and \(BC\) cut off two congruent \(30\)-\(60\)-\(90\) triangles, each with area \(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{6}\).

Therefore the desired area is \(\sqrt3-2\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{6}=\dfrac{2\sqrt3}{3}\).

Thus, C is the correct answer.

Brian 写下四个整数 \(w > x > y > z\)，它们的和为 \(44\)。这些数两两之间的正差为 \(1\)、\(3\)、\(4\)、\(5\)、\(6\) 和 \(9\)。\(w\) 的所有可能值之和是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Brian writes down four integers \(w > x > y > z\) whose sum is \(44.\) The pairwise positive differences of these numbers are \(1,\) \(3,\) \(4,\) \(5,\) \(6,\) and \(9.\) What is the sum of the possible values for \(w?\)

最大差为 \(9\)，所以 \(w-z=9\)。对任一中间数 \(n\)，差 \(w-n\) 与 \(n-z\) 的和必须为 \(9\)。

可用的和为 \(9\) 的配对为 \(3+6\) 和 \(4+5\)，剩下两个中间数之间的差为 \(1\)。

一种集合为 \(\{w,w-5,w-6,w-9\}\)，给出 \(4w-20=44\)，所以 \(w=16\)。另一种为 \(\{w,w-3,w-4,w-9\}\)，给出 \(4w-16=44\)，所以 \(w=15\)。

可能的 \(w\) 之和为 \(16+15=31\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The largest difference is \(9\), so \(w-z=9\). For either middle number \(n\), the two differences \(w-n\) and \(n-z\) must add to \(9\).

The available pairs of differences that add to \(9\) are \(3+6\) and \(4+5\), and the remaining difference between the two middle numbers is \(1\).

One possible set is \(\{w,w-5,w-6,w-9\}\), giving \(4w-20=44\) and \(w=16\). The other is \(\{w,w-3,w-4,w-9\}\), giving \(4w-16=44\) and \(w=15\).

The sum of the possible values of \(w\) is \(16+15=31\).

Thus, B is the correct answer.

A pyramid has a square base with sides of length \(1\) and has lateral faces that are equilateral triangles. A cube is placed within the pyramid so that one face is on the base of the pyramid and its opposite face has all its edges on the lateral faces of the pyramid. What is the volume of this cube?

设立方体边长为 \(x\)。作金字塔的一个竖直对角截面，它经过正方形底面的两个相对顶点。

这个截面是斜边为 \(\sqrt2\) 的等腰直角三角形。立方体在其中表现为高 \(x\)、宽 \(\sqrt2x\) 的长方形，两侧留下两个全等的、直角边为 \(x\) 的等腰直角三角形。

Let the cube have side length \(x\). Take a vertical diagonal cross-section of the pyramid through opposite vertices of the square base.

Thus, A is the correct answer.

\(2011^{2011}\) 的百位数字是多少？

What is the hundreds digit of \(2011^{2011}?\)

Because \(2011\equiv11\pmod{1000}\), it is enough to find \(11^{2011}=(10+1)^{2011}\) modulo \(1000\).

在 \(xy\) 坐标系中，格点是指 \((x, y)\) 中 \(x\) 和 \(y\) 都为整数的点。直线 \(y = mx +2\) 不经过任何满足 \(0 < x \le 100\) 的格点，且对所有 \(m\)，若 \(\frac{1}{2} < m < a\)，这一结论都成立。\(a\) 的最大可能值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A lattice point in an \(xy\)-coordinate system is any point \((x, y)\) where both \(x\) and \(y\) are integers. The graph of \(y = mx +2\) passes through no lattice point with \(0 < x \le 100\) for all \(m\) such that \(\frac{1}{2} < m < a.\) What is the maximum possible value of \(a?\)

将图像下移 \(2\)。问题等价于找最小的 \(m\gt\dfrac12\)，使直线 \(y=mx\) 经过某个满足 \(0\lt x\le100\) 的格点。

对固定整数 \(x\)，最小整数 \(y\) 必须满足 \(\frac{y}{x}\gt\frac{1}{2}\)。它为 \(\frac{x}{2}+1\)（若 \(x\) 为偶数），以及 \(\frac{x+1}{2}\)（若 \(x\) 为奇数）。

候选斜率为 \(\dfrac12+\dfrac1x\)（当 \(x\) 为偶数），在 \(x=100\) 时最小为 \(\dfrac{51}{100}\)。候选斜率为 \(\dfrac12+\dfrac1{2x}\)（若 \(x\) 为奇数），在 \(x=99\) 时最小为 \(\dfrac{50}{99}\)。

两类中较小的端点是 \(\dfrac{50}{99}\)。因此当 \(m\) 满足 \(\dfrac12\lt m\lt\dfrac{50}{99}\) 时不会经过这些格点，而这个上端点已经是可能的最大值。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Shift the graph down by \(2\). The problem is equivalent to finding the smallest slope \(m\gt\dfrac12\) for which \(y=mx\) passes through a lattice point with \(0\lt x\le100\).

For a fixed integer \(x\), the smallest integer \(y\) with \(\frac{y}{x}\gt\frac{1}{2}\) is \(\frac{x}{2}+1\) when \(x\) is even, and \(\frac{x+1}{2}\) when \(x\) is odd.

Thus the candidate slopes are \(\dfrac12+\dfrac1x\) for even \(x\), minimized at \(x=100\) as \(\dfrac{51}{100}\), and \(\dfrac12+\dfrac1{2x}\) for odd \(x\), minimized at \(x=99\) as \(\dfrac{50}{99}\).

The smaller of these is \(\dfrac{50}{99}\), so every \(m\) with \(\dfrac12\lt m\lt\dfrac{50}{99}\) avoids such lattice points, and this upper endpoint is best possible.

Thus, B is the correct answer.

设 \(T_1\) 是边长为 \(2011\)、\(2012\) 和 \(2013\) 的三角形。对 \(n \ge 1\)，若 \(T_n = \triangle ABC\)，且 \(D\)、\(E\) 和 \(F\) 分别是 \(\triangle ABC\) 的内切圆与边 \(AB\)、\(BC\) 和 \(AC\) 的切点，则若存在，\(T_{n+1}\) 是边长为 \(AD\)、\(BE\) 和 \(CF\) 的三角形。数列 \(( T_n )\) 中最后一个三角形的周长是多少？

English（选项同上方）

What is the perimeter of the last triangle in the sequence \(( T_n )?\)

对边长 \(a=BC\)、\(b=CA\)、\(c=AB\) 的三角形，同一顶点到内切圆的切线段相等，所以下一个三角形边长为 \[ \begin{gathered} \dfrac{b+c-a}{2}, \\ \quad \dfrac{a+c-b}{2}, \\ \quad \dfrac{a+b-c}{2} \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

English

For a triangle with side lengths \(a=BC\), \(b=CA\), and \(c=AB\), equal tangents from the same vertex give the next side lengths \[ \begin{gathered} \dfrac{b+c-a}{2}, \\ \quad \dfrac{a+c-b}{2}, \\ \quad \dfrac{a+b-c}{2}. \end{gathered} \]

If the current side lengths are \(s-1,s,s+1\), then the next side lengths are \(\dfrac{s}{2}-1,\dfrac{s}{2},\dfrac{s}{2}+1\). Thus the same form persists while the middle side halves each time.

The perimeter is \(3\cdot\dfrac{503}{128}=\dfrac{1509}{128}\).

Thus, D is the correct answer.
