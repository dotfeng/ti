1单选

在一月的某一天，内布拉斯加州 Lincoln 的最高气温比最低气温高 \(16\) 度，最高气温与最低气温的平均值是 \(3^\circ\)。这一天 Lincoln 的最低气温是多少度？

English（选项同上方）

On a particular January day, the high temperature in Lincoln, Nebraska, was \(16\) degrees higher than the low temperature, and the average of the high and low temperatures was \(3^\circ.\) In degrees, what was the low temperature in Lincoln that day?

最高气温比最低气温高 \(16\)，所以最低气温比平均值低 \(8\)。平均值为 \(3^\circ\)，因此最低气温为 \(3 - 8 = -5^\circ\)。所以正确答案是 C。

English

The high exceeds the low by \(16,\) so the low is \(8\) below the average. Since the average is \(3^\circ,\) the low temperature is \(3 - 8 = -5^\circ.\) Thus, the correct answer is C.

2单选

Green 先生通过沿着长方形花园的两条边走路来测量花园，发现它是 \(15\) 步乘 \(20\) 步。他每一步长 \(2\) 英尺。Green 先生预计花园每平方英尺能产半磅土豆。他预计花园能产多少磅土豆？

English（选项同上方）

Mr. Green measures his rectangular garden by walking two of the sides and finds that it is \(15\) steps by \(20\) steps. Each of Mr. Green’s steps is \(2\) feet long. Mr. Green expects a half a pound of potatoes per square foot from his garden. How many pounds of potatoes does Mr. Green expect from his garden?

花园的长和宽分别为 \(2\cdot 15 = 30\) 英尺和 \(2\cdot 20 = 40\) 英尺，面积为 \(1200\) 平方英尺。每平方英尺能产半磅土豆，所以预计产量为 \(\tfrac12\cdot 1200 = 600\) 磅。正确答案是 A。

English

The garden is \(2\cdot 15 = 30\) feet by \(2\cdot 20 = 40\) feet, an area of \(1200\) square feet. At half a pound per square foot, Mr. Green expects \(\tfrac12\cdot 1200 = 600\) pounds. Thus, the correct answer is A.

3单选

从 \(3\) 数到 \(201\) 时，\(53\) 是数到的第 \(51\) 个数。从 \(201\) 倒数到 \(3\) 时，\(53\) 是数到的第 \(n\) 个数。\(n\) 是多少？

English（选项同上方）

When counting from \(3\) to \(201,\) \(53\) is the \(51\)st number counted. When counting backwards from \(201\) to \(3,\) \(53\) is the \(n\)th number counted. What is \(n?\)

从 \(201\) 往下数时，数值 \(x\) 是第 \((202-x)\) 个数。因此 \(53\) 是第 \((202-53) = 149\) 个数。正确答案是 D。

English

Counting down from \(201,\) the value \(x\) is the \((202-x)\)th number. So \(53\) is the \((202-53) = 149\)th number. Thus, the correct answer is D.

4单选

Ray 的车平均每加仑汽油行驶 \(40\) 英里，Tom 的车平均每加仑汽油行驶 \(10\) 英里。Ray 和 Tom 各自行驶相同的英里数。两辆车合起来的每加仑英里数是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Ray’s car averages \(40\) miles per gallon of gasoline, and Tom’s car averages \(10\) miles per gallon of gasoline. Ray and Tom each drive the same number of miles. What is the cars’ combined rate of miles per gallon of gasoline?

若每人行驶 \(D\) 英里，两人共行驶 \(2D\) 英里，耗油量为 \(\dfrac{D}{40}+\dfrac{D}{10} = \dfrac{D}{8}\) 加仑。因此合并油耗率为 \(\dfrac{2D}{\frac{D}{8}} = 16\) 英里每加仑。正确答案是 B。

English

If each drives \(D\) miles, together they cover \(2D\) miles using \(\dfrac{D}{40}+\dfrac{D}{10} = \dfrac{D}{8}\) gallons. The combined rate is \(\dfrac{2D}{\frac{D}{8}} = 16\) miles per gallon. Thus, the correct answer is B.

5单选

\(33\) 名五年级学生的平均年龄是 \(11\)。他们的 \(55\) 位家长的平均年龄是 \(33\)。这些家长和五年级学生全体的平均年龄是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The average age of \(33\) fifth-graders is \(11.\) The average age of \(55\) of their parents is \(33.\) What is the average age of all of these parents and fifth-graders?

家长年龄总和为 \(55\cdot 33\)，五年级学生年龄总和为 \(33\cdot 11\)，合计 \(33\cdot 66\)。总人数为 \(88\)，所以平均年龄为 \(\dfrac{33\cdot 66}{88} = 24.75\)。正确答案是 C。

English

The parents’ ages sum to \(55\cdot 33\) and the fifth-graders’ to \(33\cdot 11,\) a total of \(33\cdot 66.\) Dividing by \(88\) people gives \(\dfrac{33\cdot 66}{88} = 24.75.\) Thus, the correct answer is C.

6单选

实数 \(x\) 和 \(y\) 满足方程 \(x^2 + y^2 = 10x - 6y - 34\)。\(x+y\) 是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Real numbers \(x\) and \(y\) satisfy the equation \(x^2 + y^2 = 10x - 6y - 34.\) What is \(x+y?\)

整理并配方得 \(x^2 - 10x + 25 + y^2 + 6y + 9\) \(= 0\)，即 \((x-5)^2 + (y+3)^2 = 0\)。因此 \(x = 5\)、\(y = -3\)，所以 \(x + y = 2\)。正确答案是 B。

English

Rearranging gives \(x^2 - 10x + 25 + y^2 + 6y + 9\) \(= 0,\) that is \((x-5)^2 + (y+3)^2 = 0.\) Hence \(x = 5\) and \(y = -3,\) so \(x + y = 2.\) Thus, the correct answer is B.

7单选

Jo 和 Blair 轮流从 \(1\) 开始数，每次比对方上一轮说到的最后一个数多说一个数。Jo 先说“\(1\)”，所以 Blair 接着说“\(1\)、\(2\)”。然后 Jo 说“\(1\)、\(2\)、\(3\)”，依此类推。说出的第 \(53\) 个数是什么？

English（选项同上方）

Jo and Blair take turns counting from \(1\) to one more than the last number said by the other person. Jo starts by saying “\(1\)”, so Blair follows by saying “\(1,\) \(2\)”. Jo then says “\(1,\) \(2,\) \(3\)”, and so on. What is the \(53\)rd number said?

到数到 \(n\) 的那一轮结束时，共说了 \(1 + 2 + \cdots + n = \tfrac12 n(n+1)\) 个数。当 \(n = 9\) 时，这个总数为 \(45\)。下一轮从 \(1, 2, \ldots\) 开始，所以第 \(53\) 个数是这一轮的第 \(8\) 个数，即 \(8\)。正确答案是 E。

English

After the turn that counts up to \(n,\) exactly \(1 + 2 + \cdots + n = \tfrac12 n(n+1)\) numbers have been said. For \(n = 9\) that is \(45.\) The next turn starts \(1, 2, \ldots,\) so the \(53\)rd number is the \(8\)th number of that turn, namely \(8.\) Thus, the correct answer is E.

8单选

直线 \(\ell_1\) 的方程为 \(3x - 2y = 1\)，并经过点 \(A = (-1, -2)\)。直线 \(\ell_2\) 的方程为 \(y = 1\)，与 \(\ell_1\) 交于点 \(B\)。直线 \(\ell_3\) 斜率为正，经过点 \(A\)，并与 \(\ell_2\) 交于点 \(C\)。\(\triangle ABC\) 的面积为 \(3\)。\(\ell_3\) 的斜率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Line \(\ell_1\) has equation \(3x - 2y = 1\) and goes through \(A = (-1, -2).\) Line \(\ell_2\) has equation \(y = 1\) and meets line \(\ell_1\) at point \(B.\) Line \(\ell_3\) has positive slope, goes through point \(A,\) and meets \(\ell_2\) at point \(C.\) The area of \(\triangle ABC\) is \(3.\) What is the slope of \(\ell_3?\)

联立 \(3x - 2y = 1\) 与 \(y = 1\)，得 \(B = (1, 1)\)。点 \(A = (-1, -2)\) 到直线 \(y = 1\) 的距离为 \(3\)，所以 \(\tfrac12\cdot BC\cdot 3 = 3\)，从而 \(BC = 2\)。于是 \(C = (3, 1)\) 或 \(C = (-1, 1)\)；后者会使 \(\ell_3\) 竖直，所以 \(C = (3, 1)\)。因此斜率为 \(\dfrac{1 - (-2)}{3 - (-1)} = \dfrac34\)。正确答案是 B。

English

Solving \(3x - 2y = 1\) with \(y = 1\) gives \(B = (1, 1).\) The distance from \(A = (-1, -2)\) to the line \(y = 1\) is \(3,\) so \(\tfrac12\cdot BC\cdot 3 = 3\) gives \(BC = 2.\) Then \(C = (3, 1)\) or \(C = (-1, 1);\) the latter makes \(\ell_3\) vertical, so \(C = (3, 1)\) and the slope is \(\dfrac{1 - (-2)}{3 - (-1)} = \dfrac34.\) Thus, the correct answer is B.

9单选

能整除 \(12!\) 的最大完全平方数，其平方根的质因数分解中各指数之和是多少？

English（选项同上方）

What is the sum of the exponents of the prime factors of the square root of the largest perfect square that divides \(12!\)?

因为 \(12! = 2^{10}\cdot 3^5\cdot 5^2\cdot 7\cdot 11\)，能整除它的最大完全平方数为 \(2^{10}\cdot 3^4\cdot 5^2\)，其平方根为 \(2^5\cdot 3^2\cdot 5\)。指数和为 \(5 + 2 + 1 = 8\)。正确答案是 C。

English

Since \(12! = 2^{10}\cdot 3^5\cdot 5^2\cdot 7\cdot 11,\) the largest perfect square dividing it is \(2^{10}\cdot 3^4\cdot 5^2,\) whose square root is \(2^5\cdot 3^2\cdot 5.\) The exponents sum to \(5 + 2 + 1 = 8.\) Thus, the correct answer is C.

10单选

Alex 有 \(75\) 个红色代币和 \(75\) 个蓝色代币。有一个摊位可以用两个红色代币换一个银色代币和一个蓝色代币；另一个摊位可以用三个蓝色代币换一个银色代币和一个红色代币。Alex 持续交换，直到无法再交换为止。最后 Alex 会有多少个银色代币？

English（选项同上方）

Alex has \(75\) red tokens and \(75\) blue tokens. There is a booth where Alex can give two red tokens and receive in return a silver token and a blue token, and another booth where Alex can give three blue tokens and receive in return a silver token and a red token. Alex continues to exchange tokens until no more exchanges are possible. How many silver tokens will Alex have at the end?

经过 \(m\) 次红色摊位交换和 \(n\) 次蓝色摊位交换后，Alex 有 \(75 - (2m - n)\) 个红色代币、\(75-(3n-m)\) 个蓝色代币，以及 \(m+n\) 个银色代币。停止时，红色代币数为 \(0\) 或 \(1\)，蓝色代币数为 \(0,1\)，或 \(2\)。对这六种情形求解代币方程，只得到 \((m,n)=(59,44)\)，终态为 \((1,2)\)，或 \((m,n)=(60,45)\)，终态为 \((0,0)\)。后者不可能到达：最后一次交换前的状态只能是 \((-1,3)\) 或 \((2,-1)\)。因此 Alex 最终有 \(59+44=103\) 个银色代币。所以正确答案是 E。

English

After \(m\) red-booth and \(n\) blue-booth exchanges, Alex has \(75 - (2m - n)\) red tokens, \(75-(3n-m)\) blue tokens, and \(m+n\) silver tokens. At termination the red count is \(0\) or \(1,\) and the blue count is \(0,1,\) or \(2.\) Solving the token equations over these six cases leaves only \((m,n)=(59,44),\) ending at \((1,2),\) or \((m,n)=(60,45),\) ending at \((0,0).\) The latter is unreachable: its final exchange would have to start at either \((-1,3)\) or \((2,-1).\) Hence Alex finishes with \(59+44=103\) silver tokens. Thus, the correct answer is E.

11单选

两只蜜蜂从同一点出发，并以相同速度按如下方向飞行。蜜蜂 \(A\) 先向北飞 \(1\) 英尺，再向东飞 \(1\) 英尺，再向上飞 \(1\) 英尺，然后不断重复这个模式。蜜蜂 \(B\) 先向南飞 \(1\) 英尺，再向西飞 \(1\) 英尺，然后不断重复这个模式。当两只蜜蜂恰好相距 \(10\) 英尺时，它们分别正朝什么方向飞？

English（选项同上方）

Two bees start at the same spot and fly at the same rate in the following directions. Bee \(A\) travels \(1\) foot north, then \(1\) foot east, then \(1\) foot upwards, and then continues to repeat this pattern. Bee \(B\) travels \(1\) foot south, then \(1\) foot west, and then continues to repeat this pattern. In what directions are the bees traveling when they are exactly \(10\) feet away from each other?

取东、北、上为 \(x, y, z\) 方向。飞行 \(7\) 英尺后，蜜蜂 \(A\) 在 \((2, 3, 2)\)，蜜蜂 \(B\) 在 \((-3, -4, 0)\)，距离为 \(\sqrt{78} \lt 10\)。下一英尺中，\(A\) 向东到 \((3, 3, 2)\)，\(B\) 向西到 \((-4, -4, 0)\)，距离为 \(\sqrt{102} \gt 10\)。所以它们相距 \(10\) 英尺时，\(A\) 正向东，\(B\) 正向西。正确答案是 A。

English

Take east, north, up as \(x, y, z.\) After \(7\) feet bee \(A\) is at \((2, 3, 2)\) and bee \(B\) is at \((-3, -4, 0),\) a distance \(\sqrt{78} \lt 10.\) On the next foot bee \(A\) moves east to \((3, 3, 2)\) and bee \(B\) moves west to \((-4, -4, 0),\) a distance \(\sqrt{102} \gt 10.\) So they pass through \(10\) feet apart while \(A\) heads east and \(B\) heads west. Thus, the correct answer is A.

12单选

城市 \(A\)、\(B\)、\(C\)、\(D\)、\(E\) 由道路 \(AB\)、\(AD\)、\(AE\)、\(BC\)、\(BD\)、\(CD\)、\(DE\) 连接。有多少条从 \(A\) 到 \(B\) 的不同路线，可以恰好使用每条道路一次？（这样的路线必然会多次经过某些城市。）

English（选项同上方）

Cities \(A,\) \(B,\) \(C,\) \(D,\) and \(E\) are connected by roads \(AB,\) \(AD,\) \(AE,\) \(BC,\) \(BD,\) \(CD,\) and \(DE.\) How many different routes are there from \(A\) to \(B\) that use each road exactly once? (Such a route will necessarily visit some cities more than once.)

城市 \(E\)（道路 \(AE, DE\)）是在 \(A\)-\(D\) 旅程上的绕行，城市 \(C\)（道路 \(BC, CD\)）是在 \(B\)-\(D\) 旅程上的绕行。把它们化简后，得到只含 \(A, B, D\) 的图，其中有两条 \(A\)-\(D\) 连接、两条 \(B\)-\(D\) 连接和一条 \(A\)-\(B\) 道路。恰好各用一次的 \(A\) 到 \(B\) 通路有 \(4\) 类：\(ABDADB\)、\(ADABDB\)、\(ADBADB\)、\(ADBDAB\)。每个绕行（经 \(E\)、经 \(C\)）都可放在两次经过中的任一次，所以每类给出 \(4\) 条实际路线，共 \(4\cdot 4 = 16\) 条。正确答案是 D。

English

City \(E\) (roads \(AE, DE\)) is a detour on an \(A\)–\(D\) trip, and city \(C\) (roads \(BC, CD\)) is a detour on a \(B\)–\(D\) trip. Replace them to get a graph on \(A, B, D\) with two \(A\)–\(D\) connections, two \(B\)–\(D\) connections, and one \(A\)–\(B\) road. The trails from \(A\) to \(B\) using each once are of \(4\) types: \(ABDADB,\) \(ADABDB,\) \(ADBADB,\) and \(ADBDAB.\) Each detour (through \(E,\) through \(C\)) can be taken on either passage, so each type gives \(4\) actual routes, for \(4\cdot 4 = 16\) routes. Thus, the correct answer is D.

13单选

四边形 \(ABCD\) 的内角成等差数列。三角形 \(ABD\) 与 \(DCB\) 相似，且 \(\angle DBA = \angle DCB\)、\(\angle ADB = \angle CBD\)。此外，这两个三角形各自的角也都成等差数列。\(ABCD\) 中最大的两个角之和最大可能是多少度？

English（选项同上方）

The internal angles of quadrilateral \(ABCD\) form an arithmetic progression. Triangles \(ABD\) and \(DCB\) are similar with \(\angle DBA = \angle DCB\) and \(\angle ADB = \angle CBD.\) Moreover, the angles in each of these two triangles also form an arithmetic progression. In degrees, what is the largest possible sum of the two largest angles of \(ABCD?\)

三角形的三个角成等差数列，当且仅当中间的角为 \(60^\circ\)。令 \(\angle DBA = x\)、\(\angle ADB = y\)，则四边形 \(ABCD\) 的四个角为 \(x, y, 180 - y, 180-x\)，它们本身也必须成等差数列。按递增顺序排列，它们只能是 \(x,y,180-y,180-x\) 或 \(x,180-y,y,180-x\)，于是分别得到 \(3y=x+180\) 或 \(3y=360-x\)。而三角形的三个角 \(x,y,180-x-y\) 中有一个等于 \(60^\circ\)。代入后只剩下两组角 \((60,80,100,120)\) 和 \((45,75,105,135)\)。最大的两个角之和至多为 \(105 + 135 = 240\)。所以正确答案是 D。

English

The angles of a triangle form an arithmetic progression exactly when the middle one is \(60^\circ.\) With \(\angle DBA = x\) and \(\angle ADB = y,\) the four angles of \(ABCD\) are \(x, y, 180 - y, 180-x,\) which must itself be an arithmetic progression. In increasing order they are either \(x,y,180-y,180-x\) or \(x,180-y,y,180-x,\) giving \(3y=x+180\) or \(3y=360-x.\) One of the triangle angles \(x,y,180-x-y\) is \(60^\circ.\) Substitution leaves the angle sets \((60,80,100,120)\) and \((45,75,105,135).\) The two largest angles sum to at most \(105 + 135 = 240.\) Thus, the correct answer is D.

14单选

两个非负整数的不降序列有不同的首项。每个序列都满足从第三项开始，每项都是前两项之和，并且两个序列的第七项都是 \(N\)。\(N\) 的最小可能值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Two non-decreasing sequences of nonnegative integers have different first terms. Each sequence has the property that each term beginning with the third is the sum of the previous two terms, and the seventh term of each sequence is \(N.\) What is the smallest possible value of \(N?\)

以 \(a_1, a_2\) 开始的序列第七项为 \(5a_1 + 8a_2\)。对两个序列，\(5a_1 + 8a_2 = 5b_1 + 8b_2\)，所以 \(5(b_1 - a_1) = 8(a_2 - b_2)\)。因为 \(\gcd(5, 8) = 1\)，需要 \(b_1 - a_1\) 是 \(8\) 的倍数，且 \(a_2 - b_2\) 是 \(5\) 的倍数。设 \(a_1 \lt b_1\)，并利用不降条件，有 \(a_1 \le b_1 - 8 \le b_2 - 8 \le a_2 - 13\)。取 \(a_1 = 0\)、\(b_1 = b_2 = 8\)、\(a_2 = 13\)，得到 \(N = 5\cdot 0 + 8\cdot 13 = 104\)。正确答案是 C。

English

A sequence starting \(a_1, a_2\) has seventh term \(5a_1 + 8a_2.\) For the two sequences, \(5a_1 + 8a_2 = 5b_1 + 8b_2,\) so \(5(b_1 - a_1) = 8(a_2 - b_2).\) Since \(\gcd(5, 8) = 1,\) we need \(b_1 - a_1\) to be a multiple of \(8\) and \(a_2 - b_2\) to be a multiple of \(5.\) Taking \(a_1 \lt b_1\) with nondecreasing terms gives \(a_1 \le b_1 - 8 \le b_2 - 8 \le a_2 - 13.\) Choosing \(a_1 = 0,\) \(b_1 = b_2 = 8,\) \(a_2 = 13\) yields \(N = 5\cdot 0 + 8\cdot 13 = 104.\) Thus, the correct answer is C.

15单选

数字 \(2013\) 被表示为

\[ 2013 = \frac{a_1!\,a_2!\cdots a_m!}{b_1!\,b_2!\cdots b_n!}\text{，}\]

其中 \(a_1 \ge a_2 \ge \cdots \ge a_m\) 和 \(b_1 \ge b_2 \ge \cdots \ge b_n\) 都是正整数，且 \(a_1 + b_1\) 尽可能小。求 \(|a_1 - b_1|\,\)？

English（选项同上方）

The number \(2013\) is expressed in the form

\[ 2013 = \frac{a_1!\,a_2!\cdots a_m!}{b_1!\,b_2!\cdots b_n!}, \]

where \(a_1 \ge a_2 \ge \cdots \ge a_m\) and \(b_1 \ge b_2 \ge \cdots \ge b_n\) are positive integers and \(a_1 + b_1\) is as small as possible. What is \(|a_1 - b_1|\,?\)

因为系数为实数，非实根成共轭对，所以 \(P(z)\) 可分解为互不相同的线性因子 \((z - c)\)（其中 \(c \in \mathbb{Z}\)）以及二次因子 \((z - (a+ib))(z - (a-ib))\) \(= z^2 - 2az + (a^2 + b^2)\)。每个因子的常数项都整除 \(50\)。对 \(d=1,2,5,10,25,50\)，满足 \(a^2+b^2=d\) 且 \(b\ne0\) 的共轭对个数依次为 \(1,2,4,4,5,6\)。再加上两个线性选择 \(z-d,z+d\)，就得到 \(|B_1|=3\)、\(|B_2|=4\)、\(|B_5|=6\)、\(|B_{10}|=6\)、\(|B_{25}|=7\) 和 \(|B_{50}|=8\)。把 \(50\) 分解成若干个大于 \(1\) 的因子大小之积，方式有 \(50,25\cdot2,10\cdot5\) 和 \(5\cdot5\cdot2\)。在最后一种方式中，各根互不相同要求选取两个不同的 \(B_5\) 因子。最后，再计入 \(z+1\) 和 \(z^2+1\) 可以自由出现（而 \(z-1\) 是否出现由其余因子乘积的符号决定），总数为 \[ \begin{aligned} &2^2\left(8 + 7\cdot 4 + 6\cdot 6 + 4\binom{6}{2}\right) \\ &\quad = 4(8 + 28 + 36 + 60) = 528 \end{aligned}\text{。}\] 所以正确答案是 B。

English

Since the coefficients are real, nonreal roots occur in conjugate pairs, so \(P(z)\) factors into distinct linear factors \((z - c)\) with \(c \in \mathbb{Z}\) and quadratics \((z - (a+ib))(z - (a-ib))\) \(= z^2 - 2az + (a^2 + b^2).\) Each factor’s constant term divides \(50.\) For \(d=1,2,5,10,25,50,\) the numbers of conjugate pairs with \(a^2+b^2=d\) and \(b\ne0\) are \(1,2,4,4,5,6,\) respectively. Adding the two linear choices \(z-d,z+d\) gives \(|B_1|=3,\) \(|B_2|=4,\) \(|B_5|=6,\) \(|B_{10}|=6,\) \(|B_{25}|=7,\) and \(|B_{50}|=8.\) The factor-magnitude partitions of \(50\) using values greater than \(1\) are \(50,25\cdot2,10\cdot5,\) and \(5\cdot5\cdot2.\) Distinct roots require choosing two different \(B_5\) factors in the last case. Finally, account for the free presence of \(z+1\) and \(z^2+1\) (with \(z-1\) forced by the sign of the remaining product), gives \[ \begin{aligned} &2^2\left(8 + 7\cdot 4 + 6\cdot 6 + 4\binom{6}{2}\right) \\ &\quad = 4(8 + 28 + 36 + 60) = 528. \end{aligned} \] Thus, the correct answer is B.
