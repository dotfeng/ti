满足方程 \(2^{2x^2-7x+5}=1\) 的实数 \(x\) 的个数为：

English（选项同上方）

The number of real values of \(x\) satisfying the equation \(2^{2x^2-7x+5}=1\) is:

由于底数为 \(2\)， 指数必须为零。因式分解得 \[ \begin{gathered} 2x^2-7x+5=0,\\ (2x-5)(x-1)=0 \end{gathered}\text{。}\] 因此 \(x=\frac{5}{2}\) 或 \(x=1\)， 共有两个实数解。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Since the base is \(2,\) the exponent must be zero. Factoring gives \[ \begin{gathered} 2x^2-7x+5=0,\\ (2x-5)(x-1)=0. \end{gathered} \] Thus \(x=\frac{5}{2}\) or \(x=1,\) giving two real values.

Therefore, the correct answer is C.

一个正六边形内接于圆。六边形一边的长度与该边所截较短圆弧的长度之比为：

English（选项同上方）

A regular hexagon is inscribed in a circle. The ratio of the length of a side of the hexagon to the length of the shorter of the arcs intercepted by the side is:

若半径为 \(r\)， 则六边形的边长为 \(r\)。 该边所截劣弧的长度为 \(\frac{2\pi r}{6}=\frac{\pi r}{3}\)。 所求比为 \(r:(\frac{\pi r}{3})=3:\pi\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

If the radius is \(r,\) the hexagon side is \(r.\) Its intercepted minor arc has length \(\frac{2\pi r}{6}=\frac{\pi r}{3}.\) The ratio is \(r:(\frac{\pi r}{3})=3:\pi.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is D.

直线 \(l_2\) 与直线 \(l_1\) 相交，直线 \(l_3\) 与 \(l_1\) 平行。这三条直线互不相同且位于同一平面内。到三条直线距离均相等的点共有：

English（选项同上方）

Line \(l_2\) intersects line \(l_1\) and line \(l_3\) is parallel to \(l_1.\) The three lines are distinct and lie in a plane. The number of points equidistant from all three lines is:

到平行线 \(l_1\) 和 \(l_3\) 距离相等的点位于两线之间唯一的平行中线上。到相交直线 \(l_1\) 和 \(l_2\) 距离相等的点位于它们的两条角平分线之一上。每条角平分线都与平行中线相交一次，因此共有两个点。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Points equidistant from parallel lines \(l_1\) and \(l_3\) lie on the unique line midway between them. Points equidistant from intersecting lines \(l_1\) and \(l_2\) lie on either of their two angle bisectors. Each angle bisector meets the midway parallel once, giving two points.

Therefore, the correct answer is C.

方程 \(ax^2+bx+c=0\) 各根的倒数之和为：

English（选项同上方）

The sum of the reciprocals of the roots of the equation \(ax^2+bx+c=0\) is:

根据韦达定理，\(r+s=-\frac{b}{a}\)，且 \(rs=\frac{c}{a}\)。 因此 \[ \begin{aligned} \frac1r+\frac1s &=\frac{r+s}{rs}\\ &=\frac{-\frac{b}{a}}{\frac{c}{a}}=-\frac bc \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

English

By Vieta’s formulas, \(r+s=-\frac{b}{a}\) and \(rs=\frac{c}{a}.\) Therefore \[ \begin{aligned} \frac1r+\frac1s &=\frac{r+s}{rs}\\ &=\frac{-\frac{b}{a}}{\frac{c}{a}}=-\frac bc. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is E.

考虑以下命题：

\[\begin{aligned} \mathrm{I:}\quad &(\sqrt{-4})(\sqrt{-16})\\ &=\sqrt{(-4)(-16)} \end{aligned}\text{，}\]

\[\mathrm{II:}\quad \sqrt{(-4)(-16)}=\sqrt{64}\text{，}\]

以及

\[\mathrm{III:}\quad \sqrt{64}=8\text{。}\]

其中不正确的是：

English（选项同上方）

Consider the statements:

\[\begin{aligned} \mathrm{I:}\quad &(\sqrt{-4})(\sqrt{-16})\\ &=\sqrt{(-4)(-16)}, \end{aligned}\]

\[\mathrm{II:}\quad \sqrt{(-4)(-16)}=\sqrt{64},\]

and

\[\mathrm{III:}\quad \sqrt{64}=8.\]

Of these the following are incorrect:

命题 I 将 \(\sqrt a\sqrt b=\sqrt{ab}\) 错误地用于负被开方数；在实数范围内其左边没有定义，而取复数主值时左边等于 \(-8\)， 而不是 \(8\)。 命题 II 和 III 都能正确化简为 \(8\)。 因此只有 I 不正确。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Statement I improperly applies \(\sqrt a\sqrt b=\sqrt{ab}\) to negative radicands; over the reals its left side is undefined, and with principal complex roots it equals \(-8,\) not \(8.\) Statements II and III both correctly simplify to \(8.\) Thus only I is incorrect.

Therefore, the correct answer is B.

在三角形 \(ABC\) 内作一个菱形，使其一个顶点为 \(A\)，且两条边分别位于 \(AB\) 和 \(AC\) 上。若 \(AC=6\) 英寸，\(AB=12\) 英寸，且 \(BC=8\) 英寸，则菱形的边长（英寸）为：

English（选项同上方）

A rhombus is inscribed in triangle \(ABC\) in such a way that one of its vertices is \(A\) and two of its sides lie along \(AB\) and \(AC.\) If \(AC=6\) inches, \(AB=12\) inches, and \(BC=8\) inches, the side of the rhombus, in inches, is:

以 \(A\) 为原点，并设从原点指向 \(B,C\) 的向量分别为 \(u,v\)。若菱形边长为 \(s\)，则其对角顶点为 \((\frac{s}{12})u+(\frac{s}{6})v\)。当这两个系数之和为 \(1\) 时，该点位于 \(BC\) 上。因此 \[ \frac{s}{12}+\frac{s}{6}=1\text{，}\]从而 \(s=4\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

Use \(A\) as the origin and let the vectors to \(B,C\) be \(u,v.\) If the rhombus side is \(s,\) its opposite vertex is \((\frac{s}{12})u+(\frac{s}{6})v.\) A point lies on \(BC\) when these coefficients sum to \(1.\) Thus \[ \frac{s}{12}+\frac{s}{6}=1,\] giving \(s=4.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is D.

成立的条件是：

English（选项同上方）

If \(a_2\ne0\) and \(r,\) \(s\) are the roots of \(a_0+a_1x+a_2x^2=0,\) then the equality

\[ \begin{aligned} a_0+a_1x+a_2x^2 &=a_0\left(1-\frac xr\right)\\ &\quad\cdot\left(1-\frac xs\right) \end{aligned} \]

Therefore, the correct answer is A.

Let \(n\) be the number of integer values of \(x\) such that \(P=x^4+6x^3+11x^2+3x+31\) is the square of an integer. Then \(n\) is:

令 \(N=x^2+3x+1\)。则 \[P=N^2-3(x-10)\text{。}\]当 \(x=10,\ P=N^2=131^2\) 时，有一个值符合条件。

若 \(x\gt10\) 且 \(P=y^2\)，则 \(N^2-y^2=3(x-10)\)。与 \(N^2\) 不同的整数平方和它的差至少为 \(2|N|-1\)，而这已经大于 \(3(x-10)\)，矛盾。若 \(x\lt10\)，则类似的界 \[ \begin{gathered} 3(10-x)=y^2-N^2\\ \geq2|y|-1\gt2|N|-1 \end{gathered} \] 只有在 \(-6\leq x\leq2\) 时才可能成立。直接代入这九个整数，所得值均不是平方数。因此 \(x=10\) 是唯一解，且 \(n=1\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

Let \(N=x^2+3x+1.\) Then \[P=N^2-3(x-10).\] At \(x=10,\ P=N^2=131^2,\) so one value works.

If \(x\gt10\) and \(P=y^2,\) then \(N^2-y^2=3(x-10).\) Distinct integer squares differing from \(N^2\) differ by at least \(2|N|-1,\) which is already greater than \(3(x-10),\) a contradiction. If \(x\lt10,\) the analogous bound \[ \begin{gathered} 3(10-x)=y^2-N^2\\ \geq2|y|-1\gt2|N|-1 \end{gathered} \] can hold only for \(-6\leq x\leq2.\) Direct substitution for these nine integers gives no square. Hence \(x=10\) is the unique solution and \(n=1.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is D.
