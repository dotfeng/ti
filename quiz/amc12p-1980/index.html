8单选

有多少对非零实数 \((a,b)\) 满足方程 \[ \frac1a+\frac1b=\frac1{a+b}\text{？}\]

English（选项同上方）

How many pairs \((a,b)\) of nonzero real numbers satisfy the equation \[ \frac1a+\frac1b=\frac1{a+b}? \]

消去分母可得 \[ (a+b)^2=ab\text{，}\]即 \(a^2+ab+b^2=0\)。因为 \(b\ne0\)，令 \(t=\frac{a}{b}\)。于是 \(t^2+t+1=0\)，其判别式为 \(1-4=-3\)。因此没有实数对满足条件。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

Clearing denominators gives \[ (a+b)^2=ab, \] or \(a^2+ab+b^2=0.\) Since \(b\ne0,\) let \(t=\frac{a}{b}.\) Then \(t^2+t+1=0,\) whose discriminant is \(1-4=-3.\) Thus there are no real pairs.

Therefore, the correct answer is A.

16单选

立方体的八个顶点中，有四个是一个正四面体的顶点。求立方体表面积与正四面体表面积之比。

Four of the eight vertices of a cube are vertices of a regular tetrahedron. Find the ratio of the surface area of the cube to the surface area of the tetrahedron.

设立方体棱长为 \(s\)。正四面体的每条棱都是立方体的面对角线，所以长度为 \(s\sqrt2\)。正四面体的表面积为 \[ 4\left(\frac{\sqrt3}{4}(s\sqrt2)^2\right) =2\sqrt3\,s^2\text{。}\]立方体的表面积为 \(6s^2\)，所以所求比为 \(\frac{6}{2\sqrt3}=\sqrt3\)。

Therefore, the correct answer is B.

17单选

已知 \(i^2=-1\)，有多少个整数 \(n\) 使 \((n+i)^4\) 为整数？

English（选项同上方）

Given that \(i^2=-1,\) for how many integers \(n\) is \((n+i)^4\) an integer?

展开得 \[ \begin{aligned} (n+i)^4 &=n^4-6n^2+1\\ &\quad+4n(n^2-1)i \end{aligned}\text{。}\]当且仅当 \(n=0\)、\(n=1\) 或 \(n=-1\) 时，虚部为零。对这三个整数，实部都是整数，所以共有 \(3\) 个取值。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

Expansion gives \[ \begin{aligned} (n+i)^4 &=n^4-6n^2+1\\ &\quad+4n(n^2-1)i. \end{aligned} \] The imaginary part vanishes exactly when \(n=0,\) \(n=1,\) or \(n=-1.\) For each of these three integers the real part is an integer, so there are \(3\) values.

Therefore, the correct answer is D.

19单选

English（选项同上方）

Let \(C_1,\) \(C_2,\) and \(C_3\) be three parallel chords of a circle on the same side of the center. The distance between \(C_1\) and \(C_2\) is the same as the distance between \(C_2\) and \(C_3.\) The lengths of the chords are \(20,\) \(16,\) and \(8.\) The radius of the circle is

设半径为 \(r\)，长为 \(20\) 的弦到圆心的距离为 \(u\)，相邻弦之间的共同间距为 \(v\)。则 \[ \begin{aligned} r^2&=u^2+10^2,\\ r^2&=(u+v)^2+8^2,\\ r^2&=(u+2v)^2+4^2 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]相邻两式依次相减，得到 \(2uv+v^2=36\) 和 \(2uv+3v^2=48\)，所以 \(v^2=6\)，且 \(u=\frac{15}{\sqrt6}\)。因此 \[ r^2=u^2+100=\frac{275}{2}\text{，}\]从而 \(r=\frac{5\sqrt{22}}{2}\)。

English

Therefore, the correct answer is D.
