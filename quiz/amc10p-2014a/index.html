1单选

下列表达式的值是多少？

\[ 10\cdot\left(\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{5}+\dfrac{1}{10}\right)^{-1} \]

English（选项同上方）

What is the value of the following expression?

\[ 10\cdot\left(\dfrac{1}{2}+\dfrac{1}{5}+\dfrac{1}{10}\right)^{-1} \]

先计算括号内的和：\[\begin{aligned} &\dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{1}{5} + \dfrac{1}{10} \\ &= \dfrac{5}{10} + \dfrac{2}{10} + \dfrac{1}{10} \\&= \dfrac{4}{5} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因此 \(\left(\dfrac{4}{5}\right)^{-1} = \dfrac{5}{4}\)，最后得到 \[10 \cdot \dfrac{5}{4} = \dfrac{25}{2}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

We get that \[\begin{aligned} &\dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{1}{5} + \dfrac{1}{10} \\ &= \dfrac{5}{10} + \dfrac{2}{10} + \dfrac{1}{10} \\&= \dfrac{4}{5}. \end{aligned}\]

Then \(\left(\dfrac{4}{5}\right)^{-1} = \dfrac{5}{4}\) and then finally, \[10 \cdot \dfrac{5}{4} = \dfrac{25}{2}.\]

Thus, C is the correct answer.

2单选

Roy 的猫每天早上吃 \(\dfrac{1}{3}\) 罐猫粮，每天晚上吃 \(\dfrac{1}{4}\) 罐猫粮。星期一早上喂猫之前，Roy 打开一个装有 \(6\) 罐猫粮的盒子。这只猫在星期几吃完盒子里所有猫粮？

English（选项同上方）

Roy’s cat eats \(\dfrac{1}{3}\) of a can of cat food every morning and \(\dfrac{1}{4}\) of a can of cat food every evening. Before feeding his cat on Monday morning, Roy opened a box containing \(6\) cans of cat food. On what day of the week did the cat finish eating all the cat food in the box?

这只猫每个完整的一天共吃 \[ \dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{7}{12} \] 罐猫粮。经过 \(10\) 个完整的一天，它吃了 \(10\cdot\frac7{12}=\frac{35}{6}\) 罐，还剩 \(\frac16\) 罐。

它会在下一次早晨喂食时吃完剩余部分。从星期一算作第一个早晨，第十一个早晨是星期四。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The cat eats \[ \dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{7}{12} \] cans of food each full day. After \(10\) full days, it has eaten \(10\cdot\frac7{12}=\frac{35}{6}\) cans, leaving \(\frac16\) of a can.

The cat finishes that remainder during its next morning feeding. The eleventh morning, counting Monday as the first, is Thursday.

Thus, C is the correct answer.

3单选

Bridget 为她的面包店烤了 \(48\) 条面包。上午她以每条 \(\$ 2.50\) 卖出一半。下午她卖出剩余面包的三分之二；因为不新鲜了，她只收半价。傍晚她以每条一美元卖出剩下的面包。每条面包的制作成本为 \(\$ 0.75\)。她当天的利润是多少美元？

English（选项同上方）

Bridget bakes \(48\) loaves of bread for her bakery. She sells half of them in the morning for \(\$ 2.50\) each. In the afternoon she sells two thirds of what she has left, and because they are not fresh, she charges only half price. In the late afternoon she sells the remaining loaves at a dollar each. Each loaf costs \(\$ 0.75\) for her to make. In dollars, what is her profit for the day?

上午 Bridget 卖出 \(24\) 条面包，收入 \(24\cdot\$2.50=\$60\)。

她还剩 \(24\) 条面包。下午她以半价卖出 \(\frac23\cdot24=16\) 条，收入 \(16\cdot\$1.25=\$20\)。

剩下的 \(8\) 条卖得 \(\$8\)，所以总收入为 \(\$60+\$20+\$8=\$88\)。成本为 \(48\cdot\$0.75=\$36\)。

她的利润为 \(\$88-\$36=\$52\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

In the morning Bridget sells \(24\) loaves for \(24\cdot\$2.50=\$60\).

She has \(24\) loaves left. In the afternoon she sells \(\frac23\cdot24=16\) loaves at half price, earning \(16\cdot\$1.25=\$20\).

The remaining \(8\) loaves sell for \(\$8\), so her revenue is \(\$60+\$20+\$8=\$88\). Her cost is \(48\cdot\$0.75=\$36\).

Her profit is \(\$88-\$36=\$52\).

Thus, E is the correct answer.

4单选

Ralph 沿 Jane 街走过连续四栋房子，每栋房子颜色不同。他先经过橙色房子，再经过红色房子；他先经过蓝色房子，再经过黄色房子。蓝色房子不与黄色房子相邻。这些彩色房子的排列有多少种可能？

English（选项同上方）

Walking down Jane Street, Ralph passed four houses in a row, each painted a different color. He passed the orange house before the red house, and he passed the blue house before the yellow house. The blue house was not next to the yellow house. How many orderings of the colored houses are possible?

蓝色必须在黄色之前且不相邻，所以蓝黄位置只能是 \((1,3)\)、\((1,4)\)、\((2,4)\)。

每种情况下，橙色和红色填入剩下两个位置，并且橙色必须在红色之前，因此被唯一确定。三种排列依次为：橙、蓝、红、黄；蓝、橙、红、黄；蓝、橙、黄、红。

共有 \(3\) 种排列。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Blue must come before yellow but not next to it, so they sit in positions \((1,3)\) or \((1,4)\) or \((2,4)\).

In each case orange and red fill the two remaining spots with orange before red, which is forced. The three orderings are orange, blue, red, yellow; blue, orange, red, yellow; and blue, orange, yellow, red.

There are \(3\) possible orderings.

Thus, B is the correct answer.

5单选

一次代数测验中，\(10\%\) 的学生得 \(70\) 分，\(35\%\) 得 \(80\) 分，\(30\%\) 得 \(90\) 分，其余学生得 \(100\) 分。学生分数的平均数与中位数之差是多少？

English（选项同上方）

On an algebra quiz, \(10\%\) of the students scored \(70\) points, \(35\%\) scored \(80\) points, \(30\%\) scored \(90\) points, and the rest scored \(100\) points. What is the difference between the mean and the median of the students’ scores on this quiz?

\(45\%\) 的学生低于 \(90\) 分，\(25\%\) 的学生高于 \(90\) 分，所以中位数为 \(90\)。

平均数为 \(0.10\cdot70\) \(+0.35\cdot80\) \(+0.30\cdot90\) \(+0.25\cdot100=87\)。

差为 \(90-87=3\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The median is \(90\), because \(45\%\) of the students scored below \(90\) and \(25\%\) scored above \(90\).

The mean is \(0.10\cdot70\) \(+0.35\cdot80\) \(+0.30\cdot90\) \(+0.25\cdot100=87\).

The difference is \(90-87=3\).

Thus, C is the correct answer.

6单选

假设 \(a\) 头奶牛在 \(c\) 天里产 \(b\) 加仑牛奶。按这个速度，\(d\) 头奶牛在 \(e\) 天里会产多少加仑牛奶？

English（选项同上方）

Suppose that \(a\) cows give \(b\) gallons of milk in \(c\) days. At this rate, how many gallons of milk will \(d\) cows give in \(e\) days?

先用 \(b\) 乘以 \(\dfrac{d}{a}\)，来调整奶牛数量。

再乘以 \(\dfrac{e}{c}\)，来调整产奶天数。

因此产奶量为 \[ b \cdot \dfrac{d}{a} \cdot \dfrac{e}{c} = \dfrac{bde}{ac}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 A。

English

We have to multiply \(b\) by \(\dfrac{d}{a}\) to account for the new number of cows.

We then have to multiply by \(\dfrac{e}{c}\) to account for the new time that we have.

This gives us a final answer of \[ b \cdot \dfrac{d}{a} \cdot \dfrac{e}{c} = \dfrac{bde}{ac}. \]

Thus, A is the correct answer.

7单选

非零实数 \(x\)、\(y\)、\(a\) 和 \(b\) 满足 \(x < a\) 且 \(y < b\)。下面四个不等式中有多少个必然成立？

(I) \(x + y \lt a + b\)

(II) \(x - y \lt a - b\)

(III) \(xy \lt ab\)

(IV) \(\dfrac{x}{y} \lt \dfrac{a}{b}\)

English（选项同上方）

Nonzero real numbers \(x,\) \(y,\) \(a,\) and \(b\) satisfy \(x < a\) and \(y < b.\) How many of the following inequalities must be true?

(I) \(x + y \lt a + b\)

(II) \(x - y \lt a - b\)

(III) \(xy \lt ab\)

(IV) \(\dfrac{x}{y} \lt \dfrac{a}{b}\)

把两个不等式相加，得到

\[ x + y \lt a + b\text{，} \] 所以 (I) 必然成立。

不等式不能直接相减，所以 (II) 不一定成立。

例如取 \(x = 1\)、\(y = 1\)、\(a = 2\)、\(b = 3\)，就会得到错误的不等式 \(0 \lt -1\)。

(III) 也不一定成立，因为 \(x\) 和 \(y\) 可能都是负数。

取 \(x = -3\)、\(y = -2\)、\(a = 1\)、\(b = 1\)，则 \(xy = 6\)，而 \(ab = 1\)，所以 (III) 不成立。

(IV) 也有同样的问题。使用同一组数，有 \(\dfrac{x}{y} = 1.5\)，而 \(\dfrac{a}{b} = 1\)。

因此只有 (I) 必然成立。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Adding the two inequalities together gets us

\[ x + y \lt a + b, \] which shows that (I) is correct.

One cannot subtract inequalities, which means that (II) is not necessarily true.

Consider \(x = 1,\) \(y = 1,\) \(a = 2,\) and \(b = 3\) as a counter-example. This would give us \(0 \lt -1.\)

(III) is also not always true, since \(x\) and \(y\) might be negative numbers.

Let \(x = -3,\) \(y = -2,\) \(a = 1,\) and \(b = 1.\) Then \(xy = 6\) and \(ab = 1\) which shows that (III) is wrong.

The same thing occurs with (IV) . Using the same values as above, we have \(\dfrac{x}{y} = 1.5\) and \(\dfrac{a}{b} = 1.\)

This shows that (I) is the only true statement.

Thus, B is the correct answer.

8单选

下列哪个数是完全平方数？

English（选项同上方）

Which of the following numbers is a perfect square?

注意每个选项都形如 \[ \dfrac{n!(n + 1)!}{2} = \dfrac{(n!)^2(n + 1)}{2}\text{。}\]其中 \((n!)^2\) 已经是完全平方数，所以还需要 \(\dfrac{n + 1}{2}\) 也是完全平方数。

这说明 \(n + 1\) 必须是完全平方数的两倍。选项中只有 \(n + 1 = 18\)，因此 \(n = 17\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Note that all of these answer choices are of the form \[ \dfrac{n!(n + 1)!}{2} = \dfrac{(n!)^2(n + 1)}{2}. \] We have that \((n!)^2\) is square, so we need \(\dfrac{n + 1}{2}\) to be square as well.

This means that \(n + 1\) must be twice a perfect square. The only choice we have is \(n + 1 = 18,\) which gives us \(n = 17.\)

Thus, D is the correct answer.

9单选

一个直角三角形的两条直角边也是高，长度分别为 \(2\sqrt3\) 和 \(6\)。这个三角形的第三条高有多长？

English（选项同上方）

The two legs of a right triangle, which are altitudes, have lengths \(2\sqrt3\) and \(6.\) How long is the third altitude of the triangle?

三角形面积为 \[ \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2\sqrt{3} \cdot 6 = 6\sqrt{3}\text{。} \] 斜边长为 \[ \sqrt{(2\sqrt{3})^2 + 6^2} = \sqrt{48} = 4\sqrt{3}\text{。} \]

设斜边上的高为 \(h\)， \[ \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot h \cdot 4\sqrt{3} = 6\sqrt{3} \] \[ h = 3\text{。} \]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

We get that the area of the triangle is \[ \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2\sqrt{3} \cdot 6 = 6\sqrt{3}. \] The length of the hypotenuse is \[ \sqrt{(2\sqrt{3})^2 + 6^2} = \sqrt{48} = 4\sqrt{3}. \]

Dropping the altitude, \(h,\) from the vertex to the hypotenuse, we get that \[ \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot h \cdot 4\sqrt{3} = 6\sqrt{3} \]\[ h = 3. \]

Thus, C is the correct answer.

10单选

从 \(a\) 开始的五个连续正整数的平均数为 \(b\)。从 \(b\) 开始的 \(5\) 个连续整数的平均数是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Five positive consecutive integers starting with \(a\) have average \(b.\) What is the average of \(5\) consecutive integers that start with \(b?\)

从 \(x\) 开始的 \(5\) 个连续整数的平均数为 \[ \dfrac{5x + 1 + 2 + 3 + 4}{5} = x + 2\text{。}\]

因此，从 \(a\) 开始的 \(5\) 个连续整数的平均数为 \(a + 2\)，也就是 \(b\)。

从 \(b\) 开始的 \(5\) 个连续整数的平均数为 \[b + 2 = a + 4\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Note that the average of \(5\) consecutive numbers starting with \(x\) is \[ \dfrac{5x + 1 + 2 + 3 + 4}{5} = x + 2. \]

This means that the average of \(5\) consecutive integers starting with \(a\) is \(a + 2,\) which we know is \(b.\)

Furthermore, the average of \(5\) consecutive numbers starting with \(b\) is \[b + 2 = a + 4.\]

Thus, B is the correct answer.

11单选

一位打算购买电器的顾客有三张优惠券，但只能使用其中一张：

优惠券 \(1\)：标价至少为 \(\$50\) 时，减价 \(10\%\)

优惠券 \(2\)：标价至少为 \(\$100\) 时，减 \(\$ 20\)

优惠券 \(3\)：对标价超过 \(\$100\) 的部分减价 \(18\%\)

对下列哪个标价，优惠券 \(1\) 的减价幅度会同时大于优惠券 \(2\) 和优惠券 \(3\)？

English（选项同上方）

A customer who intends to purchase an appliance has three coupons, only one of which may be used:

Coupon \(1:\) \(10\%\) off the listed price if the listed price is at least \(\$50\)

Coupon \(2:\) \(\$ 20\) off the listed price if the listed price is at least \(\$100\)

Coupon \(3:\) \(18\%\) off the amount by which the listed price exceeds \(\$100\)

For which of the following listed prices will coupon \(1\) offer a greater price reduction than either coupon \(2\) or coupon \(3?\)

设标价为 \(x\)。

优惠券 \(1\) 使价格变为 \(0.9x\)，优惠券 \(2\) 使价格变为 \(x - 20\)，优惠券 \(3\) 使价格变为 \[ x - 0.18(x - 100) = 0.82x + 18\text{。}\]

需要同时满足 \[ 0.9x \lt x - 20 \] 和 \[ 0.9x \lt 0.82x + 18\text{。} \] 解这两个不等式，得到 \[200 \lt x \lt 225\text{。} \]

选项中只有 \(\$ 219.95\) 符合。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Let us analyze what these coupons do to an arbitrary price, \(x.\)

Coupon \(1\) changes this price to \(0.9x.\) Coupon \(2\) changes the price to \(x - 20.\) Coupon \(3\) changes the price to \[ x - 0.18(x - 100) = 0.82x + 18. \]

We want \[ 0.9x \lt x - 20 \] and \[ 0.9x \lt 0.82x + 18. \] Solving both gives us \[200 \lt x \lt 225. \]

The only answer choice that works is \(\$ 219.95.\)

Thus, C is the correct answer.

12单选

一个正六边形边长为 \(6\)。以每个顶点为圆心画半径为 \(3\) 的全等圆弧，形成如图所示的扇形。六边形内部但扇形外部的区域被涂阴影。阴影区域的面积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A regular hexagon has side length \(6.\) Congruent arcs with radius \(3\) are drawn with the center at each of the vertices, creating circular sectors as shown. The region inside the hexagon but outside the sectors is shaded as shown. What is the area of the shaded region?

正六边形可以分成 \(6\) 个边长为 \(6\) 的等边三角形。

边长为 \(s\) 的等边三角形面积为 \[ \dfrac{s^2 \sqrt{3}}{4}\text{。}\]

因此正六边形的面积为 \[ 6 \cdot \dfrac{6^2 \sqrt{3}}{4} = 54\sqrt{3}\text{。}\]

正六边形每个内角为 \(120^{\circ}\)，所以六个扇形合起来相当于 \(2\) 个完整圆。

因此所有扇形的总面积为 \[ 2 \cdot 3^2 \pi = 18\pi\text{。}\]

所以阴影区域的面积为 \[ 54\sqrt{3} - 18\pi\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Note that we can split the hexagon up into \(6\) equilateral triangles each with side length \(6.\)

Recall that the area of an equilateral triangle with side length \(s\) is \[ \dfrac{s^2 \sqrt{3}}{4}. \]

This means that the area of the hexagon is \[ 6 \cdot \dfrac{6^2 \sqrt{3}}{4} = 54\sqrt{3}. \]

Since each interior angle of a regular hexagon is \(120^{\circ},\) the six sectors form \(2\) full circles.

This means that the area of all the sectors is \[ 2 \cdot 3^2 \pi = 18\pi. \]

The area of the shaded region is then \[ 54\sqrt{3} - 18\pi. \]

Thus, C is the correct answer.

13单选

等边 \(\triangle ABC\) 的边长为 \(1\)，正方形 \(ABDE\)、\(BCHI\)、\(CAFG\) 都在三角形外侧。六边形 \(DEFGHI\) 的面积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Equilateral \(\triangle ABC\) has side length \(1,\) and squares \(ABDE,\) \(BCHI,\) \(CAFG\) lie outside the triangle. What is the area of hexagon \(DEFGHI?\)

求出各个小块的面积，再把它们相加。

中央等边三角形的面积为 \[ \dfrac{1^2 \sqrt{3}}{4} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\text{。}\]

所有正方形的总面积为 \[ 3 \cdot 1^2 = 3\text{。}\]

另外，\[ \angle EAF = 360^{\circ} - 60^{\circ} - 2 \cdot 90^{\circ}\]\[ = 120^{\circ}\text{。}\]

又有 \(AE=AF=1\)，且 \(\angle EAF=120^\circ\)。从 \(A\) 向底边作高可得 \(EF=\sqrt3\)，高为 \(\frac12\)，所以 \([EAF]=\frac{\sqrt3}{4}\)。另外两个外侧三角形面积相同。因此它们的总面积为 \(\frac{3\sqrt3}{4}\)。

总面积为 \[ \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4} + \dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{4} + 3 = 3 + \sqrt{3}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

We can find the areas of all the individual pieces and then add them up together.

The area of the center equilateral triangle is \[ \dfrac{1^2 \sqrt{3}}{4} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}. \]

We have that the areas of all the squares is \[ 3 \cdot 1^2 = 3. \]

We also have that \[ \angle EAF = 360^{\circ} - 60^{\circ} - 2 \cdot 90^{\circ}\]\[ = 120^{\circ}. \]

Also, \(AE=AF=1\) and \(\angle EAF=120^\circ\). Dropping the altitude from \(A\) shows that \(EF=\sqrt3\) and the altitude is \(\frac12\), so \([EAF]=\frac{\sqrt3}{4}\). The other two outer triangles have the same area. Thus their combined area is \(\frac{3\sqrt3}{4}\).

The total area is then \[ \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4} + \dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{4} + 3 = 3 + \sqrt{3}. \]

Thus, C is the correct answer.

14单选

两条相互垂直的直线交于点 \(A(6,8)\)，它们的 \(y\) 轴截距为 \(P\) 和 \(Q\)，且两个截距之和为零。求 \(\triangle APQ\) 的面积。

English（选项同上方）

The \(y\)-intercepts, \(P\) and \(Q,\) of two perpendicular lines intersecting at the point \(A(6,8)\) have a sum of zero. What is the area of \(\triangle APQ?\)

两个 \(y\) 轴截距到原点的距离相等，因为它们的数值之和为 \(0\)。

设这个距离为 \(z\)。因为两条给定直线互相垂直，\(\triangle APQ\) 在 \(A\) 处为直角。原点是斜边 \(PQ\) 的中点，所以它到 \(P\)、\(Q\) 和 \(A\) 的距离相等。因此 \(A\) 到原点的距离也是 \(z\)。

由距离公式，\[ z = \sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = 10\text{。}\]从 \(A\) 到 \(\overline{PQ}\) 的高为 \(6\)（也就是 \(A\) 的 \(x\) 坐标）。

又有 \(PQ = 2 \cdot 10 = 20\)，所以面积为 \[ [APQ] = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 6 \cdot 20 = 60\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

We have that the \(y\)-intercepts are an equal distance from the origin since their values sum to \(0.\)

Let this distance be \(z.\) Because the two given lines are perpendicular, \(\triangle APQ\) is right at \(A\). The origin is the midpoint of its hypotenuse \(PQ\), so it is equidistant from \(P\), \(Q\), and \(A\). Hence the distance from \(A\) to the origin is also \(z\).

We then know that \[ z = \sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = 10 \] by the distance formula. We know the altitude from \(A\) to \(\overline{PQ}\) is \(6\) (it is just the \(x\)-value of \(A\)).

We also know that \(PQ = 2 \cdot 10 = 20,\) which tells us that the area \[ [APQ] = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 6 \cdot 20 = 60. \]

Thus, D is the correct answer.

在长方形 \(ABCD\) 中，\(AB=1\)，\(BC=2\)，点 \(E\)、\(F\)、\(G\) 分别是 \(\overline{BC}\)、\(\overline{CD}\)、\(\overline{AD}\) 的中点。点 \(H\) 是 \(\overline{GE}\) 的中点。阴影区域的面积是多少？

\(\triangle DXF\) 的面积为 \[ \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{1}{6}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 E。

English

We can find the area of the shaded region by finding the area of \(\triangle DHC\) and subtracting out the two unshaded triangles.

Extend \(\overline{DH}\) so that it hits \(B.\) Let the intersection of \(\overline{DB}\) and \(\overline{AF}\) be \(X.\)

We have that \(\triangle DXF\sim\triangle BXA.\) Since \(AB=2\cdot DF\), corresponding sides give \(BX=2\cdot DX\).

This means that \(DX = \dfrac{1}{3} \cdot DB,\) which means that the altitude of \(\triangle DXF\) is \(\dfrac{1}{3}\) the height of the rectangle.

The area of \(\triangle DXF\) is then \[ \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{1}{6}. \]

The area of both unshaded triangles is then \(2 \cdot \dfrac{1}{6} = \dfrac{1}{3}.\) The area of \(\triangle DHC\) is \[ \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 1 \cdot 1 = \dfrac{1}{2}. \]

The area of the shaded region is then \(\dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{1}{6}.\)

掷三枚公平的六面骰子。恰有两枚骰子的点数之和等于剩下一枚骰子的点数的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Three fair six-sided dice are rolled. What is the probability that the values shown on two of the dice sum to the value shown on the remaining die?

若一枚骰子的点数是另外两枚之和，它必然是三枚中最大的。

先选哪一枚是这个和，有 \(3\) 种方式。

最大骰子的点数不能为 \(1\)，因为两个正整数之和不可能是 \(1\)。

它取其余每个点数的概率都是 \(\dfrac{1}{6}\)。

和为 \(2\) 时有 \(1\) 种有序数对，和为 \(3\) 时有 \(2\) 种，和为 \(4\) 时有 \(3\) 种，和为 \(5\) 时有 \(4\) 种，和为 \(6\) 时有 \(5\) 种。

另外两枚骰子共有 \(6^2 = 36\) 种有序结果，所以所求概率为 \[ 3 \cdot \dfrac{1}{6} \cdot \dfrac{1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5}{36} = \dfrac{5}{24}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Note that if one die is the sum of the other two dice, then it is strictly greater than the other two dice.

There are \(3\) ways to choose which of the dice is the sum of the other two, which makes it the greatest.

This die cannot be \(1,\) since there is no way to sum two positive integers to get \(1.\)

There is a \(\dfrac{1}{6}\) chance that this die is any of the other numbers.

There is \(1\) way to get a sum of \(2,\) \(2\) ways for \(3,\) \(3\) for \(4,\) \(4\) for \(5,\) and \(5\) for \(6.\)

We take these numbers of ways out of a total of \(6^2 = 36\) possibilities. The desired probability is then \[ 3 \cdot \dfrac{1}{6} \cdot \dfrac{1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5}{36} = \dfrac{5}{24}. \]

Thus, D is the correct answer.

The side length squared is \(AB^2=x^2+1^2=4^2+1=17\), which is the area of the square.

Thus, B is the correct answer.
