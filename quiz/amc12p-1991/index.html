点 \(P\) 到某圆圆心的距离为 \(9\)，该圆的半径为 \(15\)。经过 \(P\) 且长度为整数的不同弦共有多少条？

English（选项同上方）

Point \(P\) is \(9\) units from the center of a circle of radius \(15.\) How many different chords of the circle contain \(P\) and have integer lengths?

经过 \(P\) 的最长弦是长为 \(30\) 的直径。最短弦垂直于经过 \(P\) 的半径，其长度为 \[ 2\sqrt{15^2-9^2}=24\text{。}\]当弦在这两个位置之间转动时，其长度连续地从 \(24\) 变到 \(30\)。两个端点长度各出现一次，而中间的五个整数长度 \(25,26,27,28,29\) 各出现两次。因此总数为 \(1+2(5)+1=12\)。

因此正确答案为 B。

English

The longest chord through \(P\) is a diameter of length \(30.\) The shortest is perpendicular to the radius through \(P,\) and has length \[ 2\sqrt{15^2-9^2}=24. \] As the chord rotates between these positions, its length varies continuously from \(24\) to \(30.\) The endpoint lengths each occur once, while each of the five interior integer lengths \(25,26,27,28,29\) occurs twice. Thus the count is \(1+2(5)+1=12.\)

Thus the correct answer is B.

11单选

杰克和吉尔跑一个 \(10\) 千米的往返路程。他们从同一点出发，上坡跑 \(5\) 千米，再沿原路返回起点。杰克提前 \(10\) 分钟出发，上坡速度为 \(15\) 千米/小时，下坡速度为 \(20\) 千米/小时。吉尔的上坡速度为 \(16\) 千米/小时，下坡速度为 \(22\) 千米/小时。当他们迎面相遇时，距离山顶还有多远？

English（选项同上方）

Jack and Jill run \(10\) kilometers. They start at the same point, run \(5\) kilometers up a hill, and return to the starting point by the same route. Jack has a \(10\)-minute head start and runs at the rate of \(15\) km/hr uphill and \(20\) km/hr downhill. Jill runs \(16\) km/hr uphill and \(22\) km/hr downhill. How far from the top of the hill are they when they pass going in opposite directions?

杰克在 \(\frac{5}{15}=\frac{1}{3}\) 小时后到达山顶。此时吉尔已经跑了 \(\frac{1}{3}-\frac{1}{6}=\frac{1}{6}\) 小时，上坡跑了 \(\frac{16}{6}=\frac{8}{3}\) 千米，所以两人相距 \(5-\frac{8}{3}=\frac{7}{3}\) 千米。他们的相对接近速度为 \(20+16=36\) 千米/小时，因此经过 \(\frac{\frac{7}{3}}{36}=\frac{7}{108}\) 小时相遇。杰克下坡的路程为 \[ 20\left(\frac7{108}\right)=\frac{35}{27} \] 千米。

因此正确答案为 B。

English

Jack reaches the top in \(\frac{5}{15}=\frac{1}{3}\) hour. Jill has then run for \(\frac{1}{3}-\frac{1}{6}=\frac{1}{6}\) hour and is \(\frac{16}{6}=\frac{8}{3}\) km uphill, so they are \(5-\frac{8}{3}=\frac{7}{3}\) km apart. Their closing speed is \(20+16=36\) km/hr, so they meet after \(\frac{\frac{7}{3}}{36}=\frac{7}{108}\) hour. Jack descends \[ 20\left(\frac7{108}\right)=\frac{35}{27} \] kilometers.

Thus the correct answer is B.

English（选项同上方）

Horses \(X,\) \(Y,\) and \(Z\) are entered in a three-horse race in which ties are not possible. 一张圆桌周围恰有 \(60\) 把椅子。桌边已经坐了 \(N\) 个人，使得下一位入座者无论坐哪一把空椅子，都必须挨着某个人。\(N\) 的最小可能值为

English（选项同上方）

条件说明每把空椅子都与一把已占椅子相邻，所以不可能有三把连续的椅子都空着。因此，每把已占椅子至多可以对应它自己以及其后的两把空椅子，从而 \(60\le3N\)，即 \(N\ge20\)。沿圆桌重复“已占、空、空”的排列即可达到 \(N=20\)。

English（选项同上方）

A positive integer \(N\) is a palindrome if the integer obtained by reversing the sequence of digits of \(N\) is equal to \(N.\) The year \(1991\) is the only year in the current century with the following two properties:

(a) It is a palindrome.

(b) It factors as a product of a \(2\)-digit prime palindrome and a \(3\)-digit prime palindrome.

区间内的回文数形如 \(1dd1\)，其中 \(d\) 是一个数字。代数上，\[ \begin{aligned} 1dd1&=1001+110d\\ &=11(91+10d) \end{aligned}\text{。}\]因此，两位回文质数只能是 \(11\)，且 \(91+10d\) 必须是一个三位回文质数。当 \(d=0,1,\ldots,9\) 时，\(d=1,4,6,9\) 给出质数 \(101,131,151,181\)。所以共有 \(4\) 个年份。

因此正确答案为 D。

English

The palindromes in the interval are \(1dd1,\) where \(d\) is a digit. Algebraically, \[ \begin{aligned} 1dd1&=1001+110d\\ &=11(91+10d). \end{aligned} \] The two-digit prime palindrome must therefore be \(11,\) and \(91+10d\) must be a three-digit prime palindrome. For \(d=0,1,\ldots,9,\) the prime values occur for \(d=1,4,6,9,\) giving \(101,131,151,181.\) Thus there are \(4\) years.

Thus the correct answer is D.
