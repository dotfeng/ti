一长队人从左到右排列。从左数第 \(1013\) 个人也是从右数第 \(1010\) 个人。队伍中共有多少人？

English（选项同上方）

In a long line of people arranged left to right, the \(1013\)th person from the left is also the \(1010\)th person from the right. How many people are in the line?

目标人物左边有 \(1013 - 1 = 1012\) 人，右边有 \(1010 - 1 = 1009\) 人。包括此人自己，总人数为 \(1012 + 1009 + 1 = 2022\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The target person has \(1013 - 1 = 1012\) people to the left and \(1010 - 1 = 1009\) people to the right. Including the person themself, the line has \(1012 + 1009 + 1 = 2022\) people.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

English（选项同上方）

What is \(10! - 7! \cdot 6!?\)

注意 \(6! = 720 = 8 \cdot 9 \cdot 10\)。因此 \[7! \cdot 6! = 7! \cdot (8 \cdot 9 \cdot 10) = 10!\text{。}\] 所以 \(10! - 7! \cdot 6! = 10! - 10! = 0\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Note that \(6! = 720 = 8 \cdot 9 \cdot 10.\) Therefore \[7! \cdot 6! = 7! \cdot (8 \cdot 9 \cdot 10) = 10!.\] So \(10! - 7! \cdot 6! = 10! - 10! = 0.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

For how many integer values of \(x\) is \(|2x| \le 7\pi?\)

不等式 \(|2x| \le 7\pi\) 等价于 \(|x| \le \dfrac{7\pi}{2} \approx 10.99\)。满足条件的整数从 \(-10\) 到 \(10\)，共有 \(10 + 10 + 1 = 21\) 个。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The inequality \(|2x| \le 7\pi\) is equivalent to \(|x| \le \dfrac{7\pi}{2} \approx 10.99.\) The integers satisfying this run from \(-10\) to \(10,\) which is \(10 + 10 + 1 = 21\) values.

Thus, the correct answer is E.

4单选

编号为 \(1\)，\(2\)，\(3\)，\(\ldots\) 的球按如下步骤放入标为 \(A\)，\(B\)，\(C\)，\(D\)、\(E\) 的 \(5\) 个箱子。球 \(1\) 放入箱 \(A\)，球 \(2\) 和 \(3\) 放入箱 \(B\)。接下来的三个球放入箱 \(C\)，接下来的 \(4\) 个球放入箱 \(D\)，如此继续，放入箱 \(E\) 后再循环回箱 \(A\)。（例如球 \(22\)，\(23\)，\(\ldots\)，\(28\) 在此过程第 \(7\) 步放入箱 \(B\)。）球 \(2024\) 放入哪个箱？

English（选项同上方）

Balls numbered \(1,\) \(2,\) \(3,\) \(\ldots\) are deposited in \(5\) bins, labeled \(A,\) \(B,\) \(C,\) \(D,\) and \(E,\) using the following procedure. Ball \(1\) is deposited in bin \(A,\) and balls \(2\) and \(3\) are deposited in \(B.\) The next three balls are deposited in bin \(C,\) the next \(4\) in bin \(D,\) and so on, cycling back to bin \(A\) after balls are deposited in bin \(E.\) (For example, \(22,\) \(23,\) \(\ldots,\) \(28\) are deposited in bin \(B\) at step \(7\) of this process.) In which bin is ball \(2024\) deposited?

第 \(k\) 步放入 \(k\) 个球，所以第 \(k\) 步后共放入 \(\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}\) 个球。又 \(\dfrac{63 \cdot 64}{2} = 2016\) 而 \(\dfrac{64 \cdot 65}{2} = 2080\)，所以球 \(2024\) 在第 \(64\) 步。

步骤按 \(A, B, C, D, E\) 循环，所以第 \(k\) 步使用位置 \((k-1) \bmod 5\) 的箱。这里 \((64 - 1) \bmod 5 = 63 \bmod 5 = 3\)，即第四个箱 \(D\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Step \(k\) deposits \(k\) balls, so after step \(k\) a total of \(\dfrac{k(k+1)}{2}\) balls have been placed. Since \(\dfrac{63 \cdot 64}{2} = 2016\) and \(\dfrac{64 \cdot 65}{2} = 2080,\) ball \(2024\) falls in step \(64.\)

The steps cycle through the bins \(A, B, C, D, E,\) so step \(k\) uses position \((k-1) \bmod 5.\) Here \((64 - 1) \bmod 5 = 63 \bmod 5 = 3,\) which is the fourth bin, \(D.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

5单选

在下面的表达式中，Melanie 把若干个加号改成了减号：

\[1 + 3 + 5 + 7 + \cdots + 97 + 99\]

新表达式求值后为负数。Melanie 至少需要把多少个加号改成减号？

English（选项同上方）

In the following expression, Melanie changed some of the plus signs to minus signs:

\[1 + 3 + 5 + 7 + \cdots + 97 + 99\]

When the new expression was evaluated, it was negative. What is the least number of plus signs that Melanie could have changed to minus signs?

原表达式是前 \(50\) 个正奇数之和，等于 \(50^2 = 2500\)。把值为 \(v\) 的项从 \(+\) 改为 \(-\)，总和会减少 \(2v\)，所以要使结果为负，翻号项的和必须大于 \(\dfrac{2500}{2} = 1250\)。

为使项数尽量少，应从 \(99, 97, 95, \ldots\) 开始翻号。最大的 \(k\) 个奇数之和为 \(k(100 - k)\)。当 \(k = 14\) 时，\(14 \cdot 86 = 1204 \le 1250\)；当 \(k = 15\) 时，\(15 \cdot 85 = 1275 \gt 1250\)。所以 \(15\) 个足够，而 \(14\) 个不够。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The original expression sums the first \(50\) odd numbers, giving \(50^2 = 2500.\) Changing a term of value \(v\) from \(+\) to \(-\) decreases the total by \(2v,\) so to make the result negative the flipped terms must total more than \(\dfrac{2500}{2} = 1250.\)

To use as few terms as possible, flip the largest odd numbers \(99, 97, 95, \ldots\) The largest \(k\) of them sum to \(k(100 - k).\) With \(k = 14\) this is \(14 \cdot 86 = 1204 \le 1250,\) but with \(k = 15\) it is \(15 \cdot 85 = 1275 \gt 1250.\) So \(15\) sign changes suffice and \(14\) do not.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

美国国债预计到 \(2033\) 年将达到 \(5 \cdot 10^{13}\) 美元。这个美元数用 \(5\) 进制数字写出时有多少位？（本题中取 \(\log_{10} 5\) 近似为 \(0.7\) 已足够。）

English（选项同上方）

The national debt of the United States is on track to reach \(5 \cdot 10^{13}\) dollars by \(2033.\) How many digits does this number of dollars have when written as a numeral in base \(5?\) (The approximation of \(\log_{10} 5\) as \(0.7\) is sufficient for this problem.)

\(N\) 的 \(5\) 进制位数是 \(\lfloor \log_5 N \rfloor + 1\)。对于 \(N = 5 \cdot 10^{13}\)，有 \[\log_{10} N = 13 + \log_{10} 5 = 13.7\text{。}\] 换底可得 \(\log_5 N\) \(= \dfrac{13.7}{\log_{10} 5}\) \(= \dfrac{13.7}{0.7}\) \(= 19.57\ldots\)。

因此位数为 \(\lfloor 19.57 \rfloor + 1 = 19 + 1 = 20\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The number of digits of \(N\) in base \(5\) is \(\lfloor \log_5 N \rfloor + 1.\) With \(N = 5 \cdot 10^{13},\) \[\log_{10} N = 13 + \log_{10} 5 = 13.7.\] Converting bases, \(\log_5 N\) \(= \dfrac{13.7}{\log_{10} 5}\) \(= \dfrac{13.7}{0.7}\) \(= 19.57\ldots\)

Thus the number of digits is \(\lfloor 19.57 \rfloor + 1 = 19 + 1 = 20.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

下图中，\(WXYZ\) 是一个长方形，且 \(WX = 4\)、\(WZ = 8\)。点 \(M\) 在 \(\overline{XY}\) 上，点 \(A\) 在 \(\overline{YZ}\) 上，且 \(\angle WMA\) 是直角。三角形 \(\triangle WXM\) 和 \(\triangle WAZ\) 的面积相等。求 \(\triangle WMA\) 的面积。

English（选项同上方）

In the figure below \(WXYZ\) is a rectangle with \(WX = 4\) and \(WZ = 8.\) Point \(M\) lies on \(\overline{XY},\) point \(A\) lies on \(\overline{YZ},\) and \(\angle WMA\) is a right angle. The areas of \(\triangle WXM\) and \(\triangle WAZ\) are equal. What is the area of \(\triangle WMA?\)

设 \(X = (0,0)\)、\(W = (0,4)\)、\(Y = (8,0)\)、\(Z = (8,4)\)，其中 \(M = (m, 0)\) 在 \(\overline{XY}\) 上，\(A = (8, a)\) 在 \(\overline{YZ}\) 上。

因为 \(\angle WMA = 90^\circ\)，所以 \(\overrightarrow{MW} \cdot \overrightarrow{MA}\) \(= (-m)(8-m)\) \(+ 4a = 0\)，即 \(4a = m(8-m)\)。面积为 \([\triangle WXM] = \tfrac12 \cdot 4 \cdot m = 2m\) 而 \([\triangle WAZ]\) \(= \tfrac12 \cdot 8 \cdot (4 - a)\) \(= 4(4 - a)\)。面积相等给出 \(m = 8 - 2a\)。

把 \(a = \tfrac{8-m}{2}\) 代入 \(4a = m(8-m)\)，得 \(2(8-m) = m(8-m)\)，所以 \(m = 2\)，\(a = 3\)。另一个代数根 \(m=8\) 会导致 \(M=A=Y\)，此时 \(\angle WMA\) 没有定义，所以不成立。于是取 \(W = (0,4)\)、\(M = (2,0)\)、\(A = (8,3)\)，得 \[ \begin{aligned} [\triangle WMA] &= \tfrac12\,\bigl|2(3 - 4) + 8(4 - 0)\bigr| \\ &= \tfrac12 (30) = 15 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Set \(X = (0,0),\) \(W = (0,4),\) \(Y = (8,0),\) \(Z = (8,4),\) with \(M = (m, 0)\) on \(\overline{XY}\) and \(A = (8, a)\) on \(\overline{YZ}.\)

Since \(\angle WMA = 90^\circ,\) \(\overrightarrow{MW} \cdot \overrightarrow{MA}\) \(= (-m)(8-m)\) \(+ 4a = 0,\) so \(4a = m(8-m).\) The areas give \([\triangle WXM] = \tfrac12 \cdot 4 \cdot m = 2m\) and \([\triangle WAZ]\) \(= \tfrac12 \cdot 8 \cdot (4 - a)\) \(= 4(4 - a).\) Setting these equal yields \(m = 8 - 2a.\)

Substituting \(a = \tfrac{8-m}{2}\) into \(4a = m(8-m)\) gives \(2(8-m) = m(8-m),\) so \(m = 2\) and \(a = 3.\) The other algebraic root, \(m=8,\) gives \(M=A=Y\) and no defined angle \(\angle WMA,\) so it is invalid. Then with \(W = (0,4),\) \(M = (2,0),\) \(A = (8,3),\) \[ \begin{aligned} [\triangle WMA] &= \tfrac12\,\bigl|2(3 - 4) + 8(4 - 0)\bigr| \\ &= \tfrac12 (30) = 15. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is C.

8单选

哪个 \(x\) 值满足

\[\frac{\log_2 x \cdot \log_3 x}{\log_2 x + \log_3 x} = 2\text{？}\]

English（选项同上方）

What value of \(x\) satisfies

\[\frac{\log_2 x \cdot \log_3 x}{\log_2 x + \log_3 x} = 2?\]

分子分母同除以 \(\log_2 x \cdot \log_3 x\)，左边变为 \[ \begin{gathered} \frac{1}{\dfrac{1}{\log_2 x} + \dfrac{1}{\log_3 x}} \\ = \frac{1}{\log_x 2 + \log_x 3} \\ = \frac{1}{\log_x 6} \end{gathered}\text{。}\] 因此 \(\dfrac{1}{\log_x 6} = 2\)，即 \(\log_x 6 = \dfrac12\)，也就是 \(x^{\frac{1}{2}} = 6\)。

因此 \(x = 36\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Dividing top and bottom by \(\log_2 x \cdot \log_3 x,\) the left side becomes \[ \begin{gathered} \frac{1}{\dfrac{1}{\log_2 x} + \dfrac{1}{\log_3 x}} \\ = \frac{1}{\log_x 2 + \log_x 3} \\ = \frac{1}{\log_x 6}. \end{gathered} \] So \(\dfrac{1}{\log_x 6} = 2,\) meaning \(\log_x 6 = \dfrac12,\) i.e. \(x^{\frac{1}{2}} = 6.\)

Therefore \(x = 36.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

9单选

一个飞镖靶是坐标平面中的区域 \(B\)，由满足 \(|x| + |y| \le 8\) 的点 \((x, y)\) 组成。目标区域 \(T\) 由满足 \((x^2 + y^2 - 25)^2 \le 49\) 的点组成。飞镖随机落在 \(B\) 中一点。飞镖落在 \(T\) 中的概率可表示为 \(\dfrac{m}{n} \cdot \pi\)，其中 \(m\) 和 \(n\) 是互质正整数。求 \(m + n\)。

English（选项同上方）

A dartboard is the region \(B\) in the coordinate plane consisting of points \((x, y)\) such that \(|x| + |y| \le 8.\) A target \(T\) is the region where \((x^2 + y^2 - 25)^2 \le 49.\) A dart is thrown and lands at a random point in \(B.\) The probability that the dart lands in \(T\) can be expressed as \(\dfrac{m}{n} \cdot \pi,\) where \(m\) and \(n\) are relatively prime positive integers. What is \(m + n?\)

飞镖靶 \(|x| + |y| \le 8\) 是一个对角线长为 \(16\) 的正方形，面积为 \(\tfrac12 \cdot 16 \cdot 16 = 128\)。目标条件 \((x^2 + y^2 - 25)^2 \le 49\) 等价于 \(-7 \le x^2 + y^2 - 25 \le 7\)，即 \(18 \le x^2 + y^2 \le 32\)，这是一个面积为 \(\pi(32 - 18) = 14\pi\) 的圆环。

原点到正方形一条边（如 \(x + y = 8\)）的距离为 \(\dfrac{8}{\sqrt2} = \sqrt{32}\)，正好等于环形区域外半径，所以整个环形区域都在 \(B\) 内。概率为 \(\dfrac{14\pi}{128} = \dfrac{7}{64}\pi\)，因此 \(m + n = 7 + 64 = 71\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The dartboard \(|x| + |y| \le 8\) is a square with diagonals \(16,\) so its area is \(\tfrac12 \cdot 16 \cdot 16 = 128.\) The target condition \((x^2 + y^2 - 25)^2 \le 49\) means \(-7 \le x^2 + y^2 - 25 \le 7,\) i.e. \(18 \le x^2 + y^2 \le 32,\) an annulus of area \(\pi(32 - 18) = 14\pi.\)

The distance from the origin to a side of the square (for instance \(x + y = 8\)) is \(\dfrac{8}{\sqrt2} = \sqrt{32},\) exactly the annulus’s outer radius. So the annulus is tangent to the square and lies entirely within \(B.\) The probability is \(\dfrac{14\pi}{128} = \dfrac{7}{64}\pi,\) giving \(m + n = 7 + 64 = 71.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

10单选

一个含 \(9\) 个实数的列表包括 \(1\)、\(2.2\)、\(3.2\)、\(5.2\)、\(6.2\)、\(7\)，以及满足 \(x \le y \le z\) 的 \(x\)，\(y\)，\(z\)。列表的极差为 \(7\)，且平均数和中位数都是正整数。有多少个有序三元组 \((x, y, z)\) 可行？

English（选项同上方）

A list of \(9\) real numbers consists of \(1,\) \(2.2,\) \(3.2,\) \(5.2,\) \(6.2,\) and \(7,\) as well as \(x,\) \(y,\) \(z\) with \(x \le y \le z.\) The range of the list is \(7,\) and the mean and median are both positive integers. How many ordered triples \((x, y, z)\) are possible?

六个固定数之和为 \(24.8\)，取值范围为 \([1,7]\)。整个列表的最小值与最大值有三种可能的安排。

若最小值与最大值为 \(0\) 和 \(7\)，则 \(x=0\) 且 \(z\le7\)。此时平均数为整数只能取 \(3\) 或 \(4\)，分别要求 \(y+z=2.2\) 或 \(11.2\)。前者使中位数变成 \(2.2\)。后者若要中位数为整数，必须 \(y=5\)，从而 \(z=6.2\)。这给出 \((0,5,6.2)\)。

若最小值与最大值为 \(1\) 和 \(8\)，则 \(z=8\)，而平均数为整数要求 \(x+y=3.2\) 或 \(12.2\)。前者使中位数变成 \(3.2\)；后者只有在 \(x=6,\ y=6.2\) 时中位数才是整数。这给出 \((6,6.2,8)\)。

最后，若两个极值都由新数给出，写成 \(x=t,\ z=t+7\)，其中 \(0\lt t\lt1\)。平均数必须为 \(4\)，所以 \(y=4.2-2t\)。中位数是 \(1,2.2,3.2,5.2,6.2,7,y\) 中的第四个数；只有当 \(y=4\) 时它才是整数，此时 \(t=0.1\)。于是第三个三元组是 \((0.1,4,7.1)\)，总共恰有 \(3\) 个。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The six fixed numbers sum to \(24.8\) and span \([1,7].\) There are three possible arrangements of the overall extremes.

If the extremes are \(0\) and \(7,\) then \(x=0\) and \(z\le7.\) The only possible integer means are \(3\) and \(4,\) requiring \(y+z=2.2\) or \(11.2.\) The first makes the median \(2.2.\) In the second, an integer median forces \(y=5,\) hence \(z=6.2.\) This gives \((0,5,6.2).\)

If the extremes are \(1\) and \(8,\) then \(z=8\) and the integer mean forces \(x+y=3.2\) or \(12.2.\) The first makes the median \(3.2;\) the second has an integer median only for \(x=6,\ y=6.2.\) This gives \((6,6.2,8).\)

Finally, if both extremes are new, write \(x=t,\ z=t+7\) with \(0\lt t\lt1.\) The mean must be \(4,\) so \(y=4.2-2t.\) The median is the fourth number among \(1,2.2,3.2,5.2,6.2,7,y;\) it is an integer only when \(y=4,\) giving \(t=0.1.\) Thus the third triple is \((0.1,4,7.1),\) and there are exactly \(3\) in all.

Thus, the correct answer is C.

11单选

令 \(x_n = \sin^2(n^\circ)\)。求 \(x_1\)，\(x_2\)，\(x_3\)，\(\ldots\)，\(x_{90}\) 的平均数。

English（选项同上方）

Let \(x_n = \sin^2(n^\circ).\) What is the mean of \(x_1,\) \(x_2,\) \(x_3,\) \(\ldots,\) \(x_{90}?\)

由 \(\sin^2\theta = \dfrac{1 - \cos 2\theta}{2}\)，可得 \[ \begin{aligned} \sum_{n=1}^{90} \sin^2(n^\circ) &= \frac{90}{2} \\ &\quad {}- \frac12 \sum_{n=1}^{90}\cos(2n^\circ)\text{。} \end{aligned} \] 在余弦和中，\(n\) 与 \(90 - n\) 的项满足 \(\cos(2n^\circ) + \cos(180^\circ - 2n^\circ) = 0\)，且 \(\cos 90^\circ = 0\)，最后只剩 \(\cos 180^\circ = -1\)。

所以总和为 \(45 - \tfrac12(-1) = 45.5\)，平均数为 \(\dfrac{45.5}{90} = \dfrac{91}{180}\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Using \(\sin^2\theta = \dfrac{1 - \cos 2\theta}{2},\) \[ \begin{aligned} \sum_{n=1}^{90} \sin^2(n^\circ) &= \frac{90}{2} \\ &\quad {}- \frac12 \sum_{n=1}^{90}\cos(2n^\circ). \end{aligned} \] In the cosine sum, the terms for \(n\) and \(90 - n\) satisfy \(\cos(2n^\circ) + \cos(180^\circ - 2n^\circ) = 0,\) and \(\cos 90^\circ = 0,\) so everything cancels except \(\cos 180^\circ = -1.\)

Hence the sum is \(45 - \tfrac12(-1) = 45.5,\) and the mean is \(\dfrac{45.5}{90} = \dfrac{91}{180}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

12单选

设 \(z\) 是一个虚部为正、实部大于 \(1\)、且 \(|z| = 2\) 的复数。在复平面中，四点 \(0\)，\(z\)，\(z^2\)、\(z^3\) 是一个面积为 \(15\) 的四边形的顶点。\(z\) 的虚部是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Suppose \(z\) is a complex number with positive imaginary part, with real part greater than \(1,\) and with \(|z| = 2.\) In the complex plane, the four points \(0,\) \(z,\) \(z^2,\) and \(z^3\) are the vertices of a quadrilateral with area \(15.\) What is the imaginary part of \(z?\)

对顶点 \(0, z, z^2, z^3\)，鞋带公式给出面积 \[ \begin{aligned} &\tfrac12\,\bigl|\operatorname{Im}(\bar z z^2 + \overline{z^2}\,z^3)\bigr| \\ &= \tfrac12\,\bigl|\operatorname{Im}\bigl((|z|^2 + |z|^4)z\bigr)\bigr| \\ &= \tfrac12(|z|^2 + |z|^4)\operatorname{Im}(z) \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

由 \(|z| = 2\)，面积为 \(\tfrac12(4 + 16)\operatorname{Im}(z) = 10\operatorname{Im}(z)\)。令 \(10\operatorname{Im}(z) = 15\)，得 \(\operatorname{Im}(z) = \dfrac32\)。又 \(\operatorname{Re}(z) = \sqrt{4 - \tfrac94} = \tfrac{\sqrt7}{2} \gt 1\)，满足条件。

所以正确答案是 D。

将每条边写成 \(\dfrac{2[\triangle]}{h}\)，则半周长为 \([\triangle]\bigl(\tfrac1a + \tfrac1b + \tfrac1c\bigr)\)。由 \(r = \dfrac{[\triangle]}{s}\)，可得 \(\dfrac1r = \dfrac1a + \dfrac1b + \dfrac1c\)。我们需要该和等于正整数 \(r\) 的倒数 \(\dfrac1r\)。边长与 \(\tfrac1a, \tfrac1b, \tfrac1c\) 成比例，所以非退化条件要求 \(\tfrac1a \lt \tfrac1b + \tfrac1c\)。

因为 \(a\le b\le c\le9\)，倒数之和至少为 \(\frac{3}{c}\ge\frac{1}{3}\)，所以整数 \(r\) 只能是 \(1,2,3\) 之一。又 \(\frac{1}{a}\lt\frac{1}{r}\le\frac{3}{a}\)，所以 \(r\lt a\le3r\)。对这少数几组 \(r,a\)，把 \(b=a,a+1,\ldots,9\) 代入 \[ c=\frac{abr}{ab-ar-br}\text{。}\] 只保留满足 \(b\le c\le9\) 的整数 \(c\)，就得到完整的列表 \[ \begin{array}{c|l} r& (a,b,c)\\ \hline 1&(2,3,6),(2,4,4),(3,3,3)\\ 2&(4,8,8),(6,6,6)\\ 3&(9,9,9) \end{array}\text{。}\] 其中三元组 \((2,3,6),(2,4,4)\) 和 \((4,8,8)\) 满足 \(\tfrac1a=\tfrac1b+\tfrac1c\)，因而给出退化三角形。剩下的三元组是 \((3,3,3),(6,6,6)\) 和 \((9,9,9)\)，所以答案是 \(3\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Writing each side as \(\dfrac{2[\triangle]}{h},\) the semiperimeter is \([\triangle]\bigl(\tfrac1a + \tfrac1b + \tfrac1c\bigr),\) so the inradius \(r = \dfrac{[\triangle]}{s}\) satisfies \(\dfrac1r = \dfrac1a + \dfrac1b + \dfrac1c.\) We need this to be \(\dfrac1r\) for a positive integer \(r,\) with the sides (proportional to \(\tfrac1a, \tfrac1b, \tfrac1c\)) forming a non-degenerate triangle, requiring \(\tfrac1a \lt \tfrac1b + \tfrac1c.\)

Because \(a\le b\le c\le9,\) the reciprocal sum is at least \(\frac{3}{c}\ge\frac{1}{3},\) so the integer \(r\) is one of \(1,2,3.\) Also \(\frac{1}{a}\lt\frac{1}{r}\le\frac{3}{a},\) so \(r\lt a\le3r.\) For each of these few values of \(r,a,\) substitute \(b=a,a+1,\ldots,9\) into \[ c=\frac{abr}{ab-ar-br}. \] Keeping only integral \(c\) with \(b\le c\le9\) gives the complete list \[ \begin{array}{c|l} r& (a,b,c)\\ \hline 1&(2,3,6),(2,4,4),(3,3,3)\\ 2&(4,8,8),(6,6,6)\\ 3&(9,9,9). \end{array} \] The triples \((2,3,6),(2,4,4),\) and \((4,8,8)\) have \(\tfrac1a=\tfrac1b+\tfrac1c\) and therefore give degenerate triangles. The remaining triples are \((3,3,3),(6,6,6),\) and \((9,9,9),\) so the answer is \(3.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

Pablo 要用红色或蓝色颜料、条纹或圆点图案装饰 \(6\) 个相同的白球。他会对每个球的颜色和图案各掷一次公平硬币，共 \(12\) 次决策。颜料干后，他把 \(6\) 个球放入一个盒子。Frida 从盒子中随机取一个球，并记录其颜色和图案。事件“Frida 取到的球是红色”和“Frida 取到的球是条纹”是否独立，取决于 Pablo 的掷硬币结果。这两个事件独立的概率可写为 \(\dfrac{m}{n}\)，其中 \(m\) 和 \(n\) 是互质正整数。\(m\) 是多少？（回忆：若两个事件 \(A\) 和 \(B\) 满足 \(P(A \text{ 且 } B) = P(A)\cdot P(B)\)，则它们独立。）

English（选项同上方）

Pablo will decorate each of \(6\) identical white balls with either a striped or a dotted pattern, using either red or blue paint. He will decide on the color and pattern for each ball by flipping a fair coin for each of the \(12\) decisions he must make. After the paint dries, he will place the \(6\) balls in an urn. Frida will randomly select one ball from the urn and note its color and pattern. The events “the ball Frida selects is red” and “the ball Frida selects is striped” may or may not be independent, depending on the outcome of Pablo’s coin flips. The probability that these two events are independent can be written as \(\dfrac{m}{n},\) where \(m\) and \(n\) are relatively prime positive integers. What is \(m?\) (Recall that two events \(A\) and \(B\) are independent if \(P(A \text{ and } B) = P(A)\cdot P(B).\))

每个球独立地属于四种等可能类型之一：红色条纹、红色圆点、蓝色条纹、蓝色圆点。设 \(6\) 个球中有 \(k\) 个红色条纹球，红球总数为 \(R\)，条纹球总数为 \(S\)。对 Frida 的均匀随机选择，\(P(\text{红}) = \tfrac{R}{6}\)，\(P(\text{条纹}) = \tfrac{S}{6}\)，且 \(P(\text{红且条纹}) = \tfrac{k}{6}\)。独立性意味着 \(\dfrac{k}{6} = \dfrac{R}{6}\cdot\dfrac{S}{6}\)，即 \(6k = RS\)。

先计数满足 \(R\in\{0,6\}\) 或 \(S\in\{0,6\}\) 的分配。这四个条件各给另一种属性留下 \(2^6=64\) 种选择，而它们两两交集中的四个分配被重复计算。因此并集贡献 \(4\cdot64-4=252\)。

当 \(1\le R,S\le5\) 时，\(RS\) 是 \(6\) 的倍数这一条件只留下 \((R,S)=(2,3),(3,2)\) 或 \((3,4),(4,3)\)。每种情形都有 \(k=\frac{RS}{6}\)，四种类型的数量恰是 \((1,1,2,2)\) 的一个排列。因此每个数对贡献 \(\dfrac{6!}{1!1!2!2!}=180\) 种分配。有利分配数为 \(252+4\cdot180=972\)。在 \(4^6=4096\) 个等可能分配中，概率为 \(\dfrac{972}{4096}=\dfrac{243}{1024}\)，所以 \(m=243\)。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

Each ball is independently one of four equally likely types: red-striped, red-dotted, blue-striped, blue-dotted. Suppose among the \(6\) balls there are \(k\) red-striped, with \(R\) red and \(S\) striped in total. For Frida’s uniform pick, \(P(\text{red}) = \tfrac{R}{6},\) \(P(\text{striped}) = \tfrac{S}{6},\) and \(P(\text{red and striped}) = \tfrac{k}{6}.\) Independence means \(\dfrac{k}{6} = \dfrac{R}{6}\cdot\dfrac{S}{6},\) i.e. \(6k = RS.\)

First count assignments with \(R\in\{0,6\}\) or \(S\in\{0,6\}.\) Each of these four conditions leaves \(2^6=64\) choices for the other attribute, and the four assignments at their pairwise intersections have been counted twice. Their union therefore contributes \(4\cdot64-4=252.\)

For \(1\le R,S\le5,\) the condition that \(RS\) be divisible by \(6\) leaves only \((R,S)=(2,3),(3,2)\) or \((3,4),(4,3).\) In each case \(k=\frac{RS}{6},\) and the four type counts are a permutation of \((1,1,2,2).\) Thus each pair contributes \(\dfrac{6!}{1!1!2!2!}=180\) assignments. The favorable count is therefore \(252+4\cdot180=972.\) Out of \(4^6=4096\) equally likely assignments, the probability is \(\dfrac{972}{4096}=\dfrac{243}{1024},\) so \(m=243.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.
