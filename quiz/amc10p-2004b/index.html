1单选

Misty Moon Amphitheater 的每一排有 \(33\) 个座位。第 \(12\) 排到第 \(22\) 排预留给一个青年俱乐部。这个俱乐部一共预留了多少个座位？

English（选项同上方）

Each row of the Misty Moon Amphitheater has \(33\) seats. Rows \(12\) through \(22\) are reserved for a youth club. How many seats are reserved for this club?

第 \(12\) 排到第 \(22\) 排共有 \(22 - 12 + 1 = 11\) 排。

每排有 \(33\) 个座位，所以总数为 \(33 \times 11 = 363\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Rows \(12\) through \(22\) inclusive make up \(22 - 12 + 1 = 11\) rows.

Each row has \(33\) seats, so the total is \(33 \times 11 = 363.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

2单选

有多少个两位正整数至少有一个数字是 \(7\)？

English（选项同上方）

How many two-digit positive integers have at least one \(7\) as a digit?

\(70\) 到 \(79\) 给出 \(10\) 个十位为 \(7\) 的两位数。

数列 \(17, 27, \ldots, 97\) 给出 \(9\) 个个位为 \(7\) 的两位数。

因为 \(77\) 被重复计算一次，所以总数为 \(10 + 9 - 1 = 18\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The numbers \(70\) through \(79\) give \(10\) with a \(7\) in the tens place.

The numbers \(17, 27, \ldots, 97\) give \(9\) with a \(7\) in the units place.

Since \(77\) is counted twice, the total is \(10 + 9 - 1 = 18.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

3单选

上周每次篮球训练中，Jenny 罚中球数都是前一次训练的两倍。她第五次训练罚中 \(48\) 个球。她第一次训练罚中了多少个球？

English（选项同上方）

At each basketball practice last week, Jenny made twice as many free throws as she made at the previous practice. At her fifth practice she made \(48\) free throws. How many free throws did she make at the first practice?

从第五次训练倒推，罚中数依次为 \(48, 24, 12, 6\) 和 \(3\)，对应第五、第四、第三、第二、第一次训练。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Working backward from the fifth practice, the counts are \(48, 24, 12, 6,\) and \(3\) at the fourth, third, second, and first practices.

Thus, the correct answer is A.

4单选

掷一个标准六面骰子，令 \(P\) 为可见的五个面上的数字之积。一定能整除 \(P\) 的最大数是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A standard six-sided die is rolled, and \(P\) is the product of the five numbers that are visible. What is the largest number that is certain to divide \(P?\)

因为 \(6! = 720 = 2^4 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5\)，所以可见乘积只会用到质数 \(2\)、\(3\) 和 \(5\)。

遮住 \(4\) 时剩下的因数 \(2\) 最少，为 \(2^2\)。遮住 \(3\) 或 \(6\) 时剩下的因数 \(3\) 最少，为一个；遮住 \(5\) 时则没有因数 \(5\)。

因此 \(P\) 一定能被 \(2^2 \cdot 3 = 12\) 整除，但不一定能被更大的数整除。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Since \(6! = 720 = 2^4 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5,\) the visible product uses only the primes \(2,\) \(3,\) and \(5.\)

Hiding \(4\) leaves the fewest \(2\)’s, namely \(2^2.\) Hiding \(3\) or \(6\) leaves the fewest \(3\)’s, namely one. Hiding \(5\) leaves no factor of \(5.\)

Therefore \(P\) is always divisible by \(2^2 \cdot 3 = 12,\) but not necessarily by any larger number.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

5单选

在表达式 \(c \cdot a^b - d\) 中，\(a\)、\(b\)、\(c\) 和 \(d\) 的值为 \(0\)、\(1\)、\(2\) 和 \(3\)，但顺序不一定如此。结果的最大可能值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

In the expression \(c \cdot a^b - d,\) the values of \(a,\) \(b,\) \(c,\) and \(d\) are \(0,\) \(1,\) \(2,\) and \(3,\) although not necessarily in that order. What is the maximum possible value of the result?

取 \(d = 0\) 可以去掉减法，所以只需用 \(1\)、\(2\)、\(3\) 最大化 \(c \cdot a^b\)。

取 \(c = 1\)、\(a = 3\)、\(b = 2\)，得到 \(3^2 = 9\)。另一种大幂 \(2^3 = 8\) 更小，而让 \(c \gt 1\) 会迫使幂更小，所以最大值为 \(9\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Setting \(d = 0\) removes the subtraction, so we maximize \(c \cdot a^b\) using \(1,\) \(2,\) \(3.\)

Taking \(c = 1,\) \(a = 3,\) \(b = 2\) gives \(3^2 = 9.\) The alternative \(2^3 = 8\) is smaller, and any assignment with \(c \gt 1\) forces a smaller power. The maximum is \(9.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

6单选

下列哪个数是完全平方数？

English（选项同上方）

Which of the following numbers is a perfect square?

当 \(m \lt n\) 时，\(m! \cdot n!\) 等于 \((m!)^2 \cdot (m+1)(m+2)\cdots n\)。前面的平方因子不影响判断，因此只需检查 \((m+1)\cdots n\) 是否为完全平方数。

五个选项的剩余因子分别为 \(99\)、\(99 \cdot 100\)、\(100\)、\(100 \cdot 101\) 和 \(101\)。只有 \(100 = 10^2\) 是完全平方数。

因此 \(99! \cdot 100! = (99! \cdot 10)^2\) 是完全平方数。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

For \(m \lt n,\) \(m! \cdot n!\) equals \((m!)^2 \cdot (m+1)(m+2)\cdots n,\) which is a perfect square precisely when \((m+1)\cdots n\) is a perfect square.

For the five choices this leftover factor is \(99,\) \(99 \cdot 100,\) \(100,\) \(100 \cdot 101,\) and \(101.\) Only \(100 = 10^2\) is a perfect square.

Therefore \(99! \cdot 100! = (99! \cdot 10)^2\) is the perfect square.

Thus, the correct answer is C.

7单选

Isabella 从美国去加拿大旅行，带了 \(d\) 美元。在边境她把钱全部兑换，汇率为每 \(7\) 美元兑换 \(10\) 加元。花掉 \(60\) 加元后，她还剩 \(d\) 加元。\(d\) 的各位数字之和是多少？

English（选项同上方）

On a trip from the United States to Canada, Isabella took \(d\) U.S. dollars. At the border she exchanged them all, receiving \(10\) Canadian dollars for every \(7\) U.S. dollars. After spending \(60\) Canadian dollars, she had \(d\) Canadian dollars left. What is the sum of the digits of \(d?\)

Isabella 得到 \(\dfrac{10}{7}d\) 加元，花掉 \(60\) 加元后剩 \(d\) 加元，所以 \[ \dfrac{10}{7}d - 60 = d\text{。}\]

即 \(\dfrac{3}{7}d = 60\)，所以 \(d = 140\)。数字和为 \(1 + 4 + 0 = 5\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Isabella received \(\dfrac{10}{7}d\) Canadian dollars and spent \(60,\) leaving \(d.\) So \[ \dfrac{10}{7}d - 60 = d. \]

Then \(\dfrac{3}{7}d = 60,\) so \(d = 140.\) The sum of its digits is \(1 + 4 + 0 = 5.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

8单选

Minneapolis-St. Paul International Airport 位于 St. Paul 市中心西南 \(8\) 英里处，也位于 Minneapolis 市中心东南 \(10\) 英里处。下列哪个数最接近 St. Paul 市中心与 Minneapolis 市中心之间的英里数？

English（选项同上方）

Minneapolis-St. Paul International Airport is \(8\) miles southwest of downtown St. Paul and \(10\) miles southeast of downtown Minneapolis. Which of the following is closest to the number of miles between downtown St. Paul and downtown Minneapolis?

两个给定方向互相垂直，因此机场位于一个直角三角形的直角顶点，两条直角边为 \(8\) 和 \(10\)。

两个市中心之间的距离为 \(\sqrt{8^2 + 10^2} = \sqrt{164} \approx 12.8\)，最接近 \(13\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The two given directions are perpendicular, so the airport sits at the right angle of a right triangle with legs \(8\) and \(10.\)

The distance between the downtowns is \(\sqrt{8^2 + 10^2} = \sqrt{164} \approx 12.8,\) which is closest to \(13.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

9单选

一个正方形边长为 \(10\)，一个圆以该正方形的一个顶点为圆心、半径为 \(10\)。正方形和圆所围区域的并集面积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A square has sides of length \(10,\) and a circle centered at one of its vertices has radius \(10.\) What is the area of the union of the regions enclosed by the square and the circle?

正方形面积为 \(10^2 = 100\)，圆面积为 \(\pi(10)^2 = 100\pi\)。

因为圆心在正方形顶点，正好有四分之一圆位于正方形内部，面积为 \(25\pi\)。

并集面积为 \(100 + 100\pi - 25\pi = 100 + 75\pi\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The square has area \(10^2 = 100\) and the circle has area \(\pi(10)^2 = 100\pi.\)

Since the circle is centered at a vertex of the square, exactly one quarter of the circle, area \(25\pi,\) lies inside the square.

The union has area \(100 + 100\pi - 25\pi = 100 + 75\pi.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

一位杂货商摆放罐头，最上面一排有一个罐头，每往下一排比上一排多两个罐头。如果这个展示架共有 \(100\) 个罐头，它有多少排？

English（选项同上方）

A grocer makes a display of cans in which the top row has one can and each lower row has two more cans than the row above it. If the display contains \(100\) cans, how many rows does it contain?

各排罐头数为 \(1, 3, 5, \ldots\)，前 \(n\) 个奇数的和为 \(n^2\)。

令 \(n^2 = 100\)，得 \(n = 10\)。

English

The rows hold \(1, 3, 5, \ldots\) cans, and the sum of the first \(n\) odd numbers is \(n^2.\)

Setting \(n^2 = 100\) gives \(n = 10.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

11单选

两个八面骰子的面都标有 \(1\) 到 \(8\)。掷骰子时，每个面朝上的概率相同。两个朝上数字的乘积大于它们的和的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Two eight-sided dice each have faces numbered \(1\) through \(8.\) When the dice are rolled, each face has an equal probability of appearing on the top. What is the probability that the product of the two top numbers is greater than their sum?

共有 \(8 \cdot 8 = 64\) 个有序结果。条件 \(mn \gt m + n\) 等价于 \((m-1)(n-1) \gt 1\)。

不满足条件的情况只有 \(m = 1\)、\(n = 1\)，或 \(m = n = 2\)。这些情况共有 \(8 + 8 - 1 + 1 = 16\) 个。

因此概率为 \(\dfrac{64 - 16}{64} = \dfrac{48}{64} = \dfrac{3}{4}\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

There are \(8 \cdot 8 = 64\) ordered pairs. The inequality \(mn \gt m + n\) is equivalent to \((m-1)(n-1) \gt 1.\)

This fails only when \(m = 1,\) \(n = 1,\) or \(m = n = 2,\) which account for \(8 + 8 - 1 + 1 = 16\) pairs.

The probability is \(\dfrac{64 - 16}{64} = \dfrac{48}{64} = \dfrac{3}{4}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

12单选

圆环是两个同心圆之间的区域。图中同心圆半径分别为 \(b\) 和 \(c\)，其中 \(b \gt c\)。令 \(\overline{OX}\) 为大圆半径，\(\overline{XZ}\) 在 \(Z\) 点与小圆相切，\(\overline{OY}\) 是经过 \(Z\) 的大圆半径。设 \(a = XZ\)、\(d = YZ\)、\(e = XY\)。这个圆环的面积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

An annulus is the region between two concentric circles. The concentric circles in the figure have radii \(b\) and \(c,\) with \(b \gt c.\) Let \(\overline{OX}\) be a radius of the larger circle, let \(\overline{XZ}\) be tangent to the smaller circle at \(Z,\) and let \(\overline{OY}\) be the radius of the larger circle that contains \(Z.\) Let \(a = XZ,\) \(d = YZ,\) and \(e = XY.\) What is the area of the annulus?

圆环面积是两个圆面积之差，即 \(\pi b^2 - \pi c^2\)。

因为 \(\overline{XZ}\) 在 \(Z\) 点与小圆相切，所以它垂直于半径 \(\overline{OZ}\)。在直角三角形 \(OZX\) 中，\(OX = b\)、\(OZ = c\)、\(XZ = a\)，所以 \(b^2 - c^2 = a^2\)。

因此圆环面积为 \(\pi a^2\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The annulus is the difference of the two circular areas, \(\pi b^2 - \pi c^2.\)

Because \(\overline{XZ}\) is tangent to the small circle at \(Z,\) it is perpendicular to the radius \(\overline{OZ}.\) In right triangle \(OZX\) with \(OX = b,\) \(OZ = c,\) and \(XZ = a,\) we get \(b^2 - c^2 = a^2.\)

Therefore the area of the annulus is \(\pi a^2.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

13单选

美国硬币厚度如下：一分硬币为 \(1.55\) 毫米，五分硬币为 \(1.95\) 毫米，十分硬币为 \(1.35\) 毫米，二十五分硬币为 \(1.75\) 毫米。如果一叠这样的硬币高度正好为 \(14\) 毫米，那么这叠硬币有多少枚？

English（选项同上方）

In the United States, coins have the following thicknesses: penny, \(1.55\) mm; nickel, \(1.95\) mm; dime, \(1.35\) mm; quarter, \(1.75\) mm. If a stack of these coins is exactly \(14\) mm high, how many coins are in the stack?

把每个厚度都用百分之一毫米表示。四种可能的厚度为 \(135\)、\(155\)、\(175\)、\(195\)，它们都同余于 \(15\pmod{20}\)。若一摞有 \(k\) 枚硬币且高度为整数毫米，则以百分之一毫米表示的总厚度能被 \(20\) 整除，所以 \(15k\) 能被 \(20\) 整除。因此 \(k\) 必须是 \(4\) 的倍数。

\(4\) 枚硬币的高度至多为 \(4(1.95) = 7.8\) 毫米，\(12\) 枚硬币的高度至少为 \(12(1.35) = 16.2\) 毫米，所以只有 \(8\) 枚硬币可能恰好高 \(14\) 毫米。

事实上，\(8\) 枚二十五美分硬币的高度为 \(8(1.75) = 14\) 毫米。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Measure every thickness in hundredths of a millimeter. The four possible thicknesses are \(135,\) \(155,\) \(175,\) \(195,\) all congruent to \(15\pmod{20}.\) If the stack has \(k\) coins and an integer height, its total in hundredths is divisible by \(20,\) so \(15k\) is divisible by \(20.\) Therefore \(k\) must be a multiple of \(4.\)

A stack of \(4\) coins is at most \(4(1.95) = 7.8\) mm, and a stack of \(12\) coins is at least \(12(1.35) = 16.2\) mm, so only \(8\) coins can total \(14\) mm.

Indeed, \(8\) quarters give \(8(1.75) = 14\) mm.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

14单选

一个袋子最初只含红色和蓝色弹珠，且蓝色比红色多。向袋中加入红色弹珠，直到蓝色弹珠只占袋中弹珠的 \(\tfrac{1}{3}\)。然后加入黄色弹珠，直到蓝色弹珠只占袋中弹珠的 \(\tfrac{1}{5}\)。最后，将袋中蓝色弹珠的数量加倍。现在袋中弹珠有几分之几是蓝色？

English（选项同上方）

A bag initially contains red marbles and blue marbles only, with more blue than red. Red marbles are added to the bag until only \(\tfrac{1}{3}\) of the marbles in the bag are blue. Then yellow marbles are added to the bag until only \(\tfrac{1}{5}\) of the marbles in the bag are blue. Finally, the number of blue marbles in the bag is doubled. What fraction of the marbles now in the bag are blue?

设蓝色弹珠数为 \(B\)。加入红色弹珠后，总数为 \(3B\)；加入黄色弹珠后，总数为 \(5B\)，蓝色仍为 \(B\)。

将蓝色弹珠数量加倍后，有 \(2B\) 个蓝色弹珠，总数变为 \(6B\)，所以蓝色所占比例为 \(\dfrac{2B}{6B} = \dfrac{1}{3}\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Let there be \(B\) blue marbles. After adding red marbles the total is \(3B;\) after adding yellow marbles the total is \(5B,\) still with \(B\) blue.

Doubling the blue marbles gives \(2B\) blue out of \(6B\) total, which is \(\dfrac{2B}{6B} = \dfrac{1}{3}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

15单选

Patty 有 \(20\) 枚硬币，由五分硬币和十分硬币组成。如果她的五分硬币都变成十分硬币，而十分硬币都变成五分硬币，她会多 \(70\) 美分。她现在的硬币总值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Patty has \(20\) coins consisting of nickels and dimes. If her nickels were dimes and her dimes were nickels, she would have \(70\) cents more. How much are her coins worth?

交换后价值增加，所以 Patty 的五分硬币比十分硬币多。每枚硬币交换后价值变化 \(5\) 美分，所以五分硬币比十分硬币多 \(\frac{70}{5} = 14\) 枚。

两种硬币的数量满足 \(n + d = 20\) 和 \(n - d = 14\)，解得她有 \(17\) 枚五分硬币和 \(3\) 枚十分硬币。

她的硬币总值为 \(17 \cdot 5 + 3 \cdot 10 = 115\) 美分，即 \(\$1.15\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Swapping increases the value, so Patty has more nickels than dimes. Each swapped coin changes the total by \(5\) cents, so she has \(\frac{70}{5} = 14\) more nickels than dimes.

With \(n + d = 20\) and \(n - d = 14,\) she has \(17\) nickels and \(3\) dimes.

Her coins are worth \(17 \cdot 5 + 3 \cdot 10 = 115\) cents, or \(\$1.15.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

16单选

三个半径为 \(1\) 的圆两两外切，并且都与一个较大的圆内切。大圆的半径是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Three circles of radius \(1\) are externally tangent to each other and internally tangent to a larger circle. What is the radius of the large circle?

三个单位圆的圆心形成边长为 \(2\) 的等边三角形；它的中心就是大圆的圆心。

对于边长为 \(2\) 的等边三角形，中心到顶点的距离为 \(\dfrac{2}{\sqrt3} = \dfrac{2\sqrt3}{3}\)。

再加上单位圆半径，大圆半径为 \(1 + \dfrac{2\sqrt3}{3} = \dfrac{3 + 2\sqrt3}{3}\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The centers of the three unit circles form an equilateral triangle with side \(2.\) Its center is the center of the large circle.

For an equilateral triangle of side length \(2,\) the distance from its center to a vertex is \(\dfrac{2}{\sqrt3} = \dfrac{2\sqrt3}{3}.\)

Adding the unit radius, the large radius is \(1 + \dfrac{2\sqrt3}{3} = \dfrac{3 + 2\sqrt3}{3}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

17单选

Jack 年龄的两个数字与 Bill 年龄的两个数字相同，但顺序相反。五年后 Jack 的年龄将是 Bill 那时年龄的两倍。他们现在年龄相差多少？

English（选项同上方）

The two digits in Jack’s age are the same as the digits in Bill’s age, but in reverse order. In five years Jack will be twice as old as Bill will be then. What is the difference in their current ages?

设 Jack 的年龄为 \(10x + y\)，Bill 的年龄为 \(10y + x\)。条件给出 \(10x + y + 5 = 2(10y + x + 5)\)，即 \(8x = 19y + 5\)。

由于 \(x\) 和 \(y\) 是数字，唯一解为 \(y = 1\)、\(x = 3\)。

所以 Jack 为 \(31\) 岁，Bill 为 \(13\) 岁，差为 \(18\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Let Jack’s age be \(10x + y\) and Bill’s be \(10y + x.\) In five years \(10x + y + 5 = 2(10y + x + 5),\) which simplifies to \(8x = 19y + 5.\)

Since \(x\) and \(y\) are digits, the only solution is \(y = 1,\) \(x = 3.\)

So Jack is \(31\) and Bill is \(13,\) a difference of \(18.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

18单选

在直角三角形 \(\triangle ACE\) 中，\(AC = 12\)、\(CE = 16\)、\(EA = 20\)。点 \(B\)、\(D\) 和 \(F\) 分别在 \(AC\)、\(CE\) 和 \(EA\) 上，且 \(AB = 3\)、\(CD = 4\)、\(EF = 5\)。\(\triangle BDF\) 的面积与 \(\triangle ACE\) 的面积之比是多少？

English（选项同上方）

In right triangle \(\triangle ACE,\) we have \(AC = 12,\) \(CE = 16,\) and \(EA = 20.\) Points \(B,\) \(D,\) and \(F\) are located on \(AC,\) \(CE,\) and \(EA,\) respectively, so that \(AB = 3,\) \(CD = 4,\) and \(EF = 5.\) What is the ratio of the area of \(\triangle BDF\) to that of \(\triangle ACE?\)

\(\triangle ACE\) 的面积是 \(\tfrac12(12)(16) = 96\)。

三个角上的三角形 \(\triangle ABF\)、\(\triangle BCD\) 和 \(\triangle DEF\) 的一组底和高，分别是 \(\triangle ACE\) 中对应底和高的 \(\tfrac34\) 和 \(\tfrac14\)。所以每个小三角形的面积都是 \(\triangle ACE\) 面积的 \(\tfrac14 \cdot \tfrac34 = \tfrac{3}{16}\)。

因此 \[ \begin{aligned} \dfrac{[BDF]}{[ACE]} &= 1 - 3 \cdot \dfrac{3}{16} \\ &= 1 - \dfrac{9}{16} = \dfrac{7}{16} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The area of \(\triangle ACE\) is \(\tfrac12(12)(16) = 96.\)

Each corner triangle \(\triangle ABF,\) \(\triangle BCD,\) and \(\triangle DEF\) has a base and an altitude that are \(\tfrac34\) and \(\tfrac14\) of a corresponding base and altitude of \(\triangle ACE.\) So each has area \(\tfrac14 \cdot \tfrac34 = \tfrac{3}{16}\) of \(\triangle ACE.\)

Hence \[ \begin{aligned} \dfrac{[BDF]}{[ACE]} &= 1 - 3 \cdot \dfrac{3}{16} \\ &= 1 - \dfrac{9}{16} = \dfrac{7}{16}. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is E.

19单选

在数列 \(2001\)、\(2002\)、\(2003\)、\(\ldots\) 中，第三项之后每一项等于前面两项的和减去紧前一项。例如第四项为 \(2001 + 2002 - 2003 = 2000\)。这个数列的第 \(2004\) 项是多少？

English（选项同上方）

In the sequence \(2001,\) \(2002,\) \(2003,\) \(\ldots,\) each term after the third is found by subtracting the previous term from the sum of the two terms that precede that term. For example, the fourth term is \(2001 + 2002 - 2003 = 2000.\) What is the \(2004\)th term in this sequence?

递推式 \(a_{k+1} = a_{k-2} + a_{k-1} - a_k\) 给出 \(a_{k+1} - a_{k-1} = -(a_k - a_{k-2})\)。数列开头为 \(2001\)、\(2002\)、\(2003\)、\(2000\)、\(2005\)、\(1998\)、\(\ldots\)。

因此偶数位置项形成等差数列 \(2002, 2000, 1998, \ldots\)，公差为 \(-2\)。第 \(2004\) 项是这个等差数列的第 \(1002\) 项，即 \(2002 + 1001(-2) = 0\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The recurrence \(a_{k+1} = a_{k-2} + a_{k-1} - a_k\) gives \(a_{k+1} - a_{k-1} = -(a_k - a_{k-2}).\) The sequence begins \(2001,\) \(2002,\) \(2003,\) \(2000,\) \(2005,\) \(1998,\) \(\ldots\)

So the even-position terms form the arithmetic sequence \(2002, 2000, 1998, \ldots\) with common difference \(-2.\) The \(2004\)th term is its \(1002\)nd term, \(2002 + 1001(-2) = 0.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

20单选

在 \(\triangle ABC\) 中，点 \(D\) 和 \(E\) 分别在 \(\overline{BC}\) 和 \(\overline{AC}\) 上。若 \(\overline{AD}\) 与 \(\overline{BE}\) 交于 \(T\)，且 \(\frac{AT}{DT} = 3\)、\(\frac{BT}{ET} = 4\)，求 \(\frac{CD}{BD}\)。

English（选项同上方）

In \(\triangle ABC\) points \(D\) and \(E\) lie on \(\overline{BC}\) and \(\overline{AC},\) respectively. If \(\overline{AD}\) and \(\overline{BE}\) intersect at \(T\) so that \(\frac{AT}{DT} = 3\) and \(\frac{BT}{ET} = 4,\) what is \(\frac{CD}{BD}?\)

设 \(F\) 在 \(\overline{AC}\) 上，且 \(DF \parallel BE\)。令 \(ET = x\)，则 \(BT = 4x\)。

由 \(\triangle ATE \sim \triangle ADF\)，得 \(\dfrac{DF}{x} = \dfrac{AD}{AT} = \dfrac{4}{3}\)，所以 \(DF = \dfrac{4x}{3}\)。

由 \(\triangle BEC \sim \triangle DFC\)，得 \(\dfrac{CD}{BC} = \dfrac{DF}{BE} = \dfrac{\frac{4x}{3}}{5x} = \dfrac{4}{15}\)。

因此 \[ \begin{aligned} \dfrac{CD}{BD} &= \dfrac{\frac{CD}{BC}}{1 - \frac{CD}{BC}} \\ &= \dfrac{\frac{4}{15}}{\frac{11}{15}} = \dfrac{4}{11} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Let \(F\) be on \(\overline{AC}\) with \(DF \parallel BE,\) and write \(ET = x,\) \(BT = 4x.\)

From \(\triangle ATE \sim \triangle ADF,\) \(\dfrac{DF}{x} = \dfrac{AD}{AT} = \dfrac{4}{3},\) so \(DF = \dfrac{4x}{3}.\)

From \(\triangle BEC \sim \triangle DFC,\) \(\dfrac{CD}{BC} = \dfrac{DF}{BE} = \dfrac{\frac{4x}{3}}{5x} = \dfrac{4}{15}.\)

Therefore \[ \begin{aligned} \dfrac{CD}{BD} &= \dfrac{\frac{CD}{BC}}{1 - \frac{CD}{BC}} \\ &= \dfrac{\frac{4}{15}}{\frac{11}{15}} = \dfrac{4}{11}. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is D.

21单选

设 \(1\)、\(4\)、\(\ldots\) 和 \(9\)、\(16\)、\(\ldots\) 是两个等差数列。集合 \(S\) 是这两个数列各自前 \(2004\) 项的并集。\(S\) 中有多少个不同的数？

English（选项同上方）

Let \(1,\) \(4,\) \(\ldots\) and \(9,\) \(16,\) \(\ldots\) be two arithmetic progressions. The set \(S\) is the union of the first \(2004\) terms of each sequence. How many distinct numbers are in \(S?\)

第一个数列为 \(1 + 3k\)，最大项为 \(6010\)。第二个数列为 \(9 + 7j\)，其最后一项更大，所以公共项上界由 \(6010\) 限制。

公共项形如 \(16 + 21m\)，因为第一个公共项为 \(16\)，且 \(\mathrm{lcm}(3, 7) = 21\)。由 \(16 + 21m \le 6010\) 得 \(0 \le m \le 285\)，共有 \(286\) 个公共项。

因此不同数的个数为 \(2004 + 2004 - 286 = 3722\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The first sequence is \(1 + 3k\) with largest term \(6010,\) and the second is \(9 + 7j\) with a much larger last term, so the binding limit is \(6010.\)

A common value has the form \(16 + 21m\) (the first shared term is \(16,\) spaced by \(\mathrm{lcm}(3, 7) = 21\)). Requiring \(16 + 21m \le 6010\) gives \(0 \le m \le 285,\) that is \(286\) common numbers.

The number of distinct values is \(2004 + 2004 - 286 = 3722.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

22单选

一个边长为 \(5\)、\(12\) 和 \(13\) 的三角形有内切圆和外接圆。这两个圆的圆心之间的距离是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A triangle with sides of \(5,\) \(12,\) and \(13\) has both an inscribed and a circumscribed circle. What is the distance between the centers of those circles?

因为 \(5^2 + 12^2 = 13^2\)，这是直角三角形。将顶点放在 \((0, 0)\)、\((5, 0)\)、\((0, 12)\)，则外心为斜边中点 \(\left(\tfrac52, 6\right)\)。

内切圆半径满足 \((12 - r) + (5 - r) = 13\)，所以 \(r = 2\)，内心为 \((2, 2)\)。

两圆心之间的距离为 \[ \begin{gathered} \sqrt{\left(\tfrac52 - 2\right)^2 + (6 - 2)^2} \\ = \sqrt{\tfrac14 + 16} \\ = \dfrac{\sqrt{65}}{2} \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Since \(5^2 + 12^2 = 13^2,\) the triangle is right. Place it at \((0, 0),\) \((5, 0),\) \((0, 12).\) The circumcenter is the midpoint of the hypotenuse, \(\left(\tfrac52, 6\right).\)

The inradius satisfies \((12 - r) + (5 - r) = 13,\) so \(r = 2\) and the incenter is \((2, 2).\)

The distance is \[ \begin{gathered} \sqrt{\left(\tfrac52 - 2\right)^2 + (6 - 2)^2} \\ = \sqrt{\tfrac14 + 16} \\ = \dfrac{\sqrt{65}}{2}. \end{gathered} \]

Thus, the correct answer is D.

23单选

一个立方体的每个面独立地以概率 \(\tfrac12\) 涂成红色或蓝色。这个涂色立方体可以放在水平面上，使四个竖直面颜色全相同的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Each face of a cube is painted either red or blue, each with probability \(\tfrac12.\) The color of each face is determined independently. What is the probability that the painted cube can be placed on a horizontal surface so that the four vertical faces are all the same color?

固定立方体方向，共有 \(2^6 = 64\) 种涂色。

可行情况包括：六面同色，有 \(2\) 种；恰好五面同色，有 \(\binom{6}{5} \cdot 2 = 12\) 种；以及四个面同色而剩下的一对相对面为另一种颜色，有 \(3\) 组相对面、\(2\) 种颜色，共 \(6\) 种。

总共有 \(2 + 12 + 6 = 20\) 种，所以概率为 \(\dfrac{20}{64} = \dfrac{5}{16}\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Fixing the orientation, there are \(2^6 = 64\) colorings.

A coloring works if all six faces match (\(2\) ways), exactly five match (\(\binom{6}{5} \cdot 2 = 12\) ways), or four faces share a color with the remaining pair being opposite faces of the other color (\(3\) opposite pairs, \(2\) colors, giving \(6\) ways).

The total is \(2 + 12 + 6 = 20,\) so the probability is \(\dfrac{20}{64} = \dfrac{5}{16}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

24单选

在 \(\triangle ABC\) 中，\(AB = 7\)、\(AC = 8\)、\(BC = 9\)。点 \(D\) 在三角形的外接圆上，使得 \(\overline{AD}\) 平分 \(\angle BAC\)。求 \(\frac{AD}{CD}\)。

English（选项同上方）

In \(\triangle ABC\) we have \(AB = 7,\) \(AC = 8,\) and \(BC = 9.\) Point \(D\) is on the circumscribed circle of the triangle so that \(\overline{AD}\) bisects \(\angle BAC.\) What is the value of \(\frac{AD}{CD}?\)

设 \(\overline{AD}\) 交 \(\overline{BC}\) 于 \(E\)。因为 \(\angle ABC\) 与 \(\angle ADC\) 截同弧，所以它们相等；又有 \(\angle EAB = \angle CAD\)，因此 \(\triangle ABE \sim \triangle ADC\)。

因此 \(\dfrac{AD}{CD} = \dfrac{AB}{BE}\)。

由角平分线定理，\(\dfrac{BE}{EC} = \dfrac{AB}{AC}\)，所以 \(BE = \dfrac{AB \cdot BC}{AB + AC} = \dfrac{7 \cdot 9}{15}\)。

因此 \[ \begin{aligned} \dfrac{AD}{CD} &= \dfrac{AB}{BE} = \dfrac{AB + AC}{BC} \\ &= \dfrac{15}{9} = \dfrac{5}{3} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Let \(\overline{AD}\) meet \(\overline{BC}\) at \(E.\) Since \(\angle ABC\) and \(\angle ADC\) subtend the same arc, they are equal, and \(\angle EAB = \angle CAD,\) so \(\triangle ABE \sim \triangle ADC.\)

Hence \(\dfrac{AD}{CD} = \dfrac{AB}{BE}.\)

By the Angle Bisector Theorem, \(\dfrac{BE}{EC} = \dfrac{AB}{AC},\) so \(BE = \dfrac{AB \cdot BC}{AB + AC} = \dfrac{7 \cdot 9}{15}.\)

Therefore \[ \begin{aligned} \dfrac{AD}{CD} &= \dfrac{AB}{BE} = \dfrac{AB + AC}{BC} \\ &= \dfrac{15}{9} = \dfrac{5}{3}. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is B.

25单选

一个半径为 \(1\) 的圆在点 \(A\) 和 \(B\) 处分别与两个半径为 \(2\) 的圆内切，其中 \(AB\) 是小圆的一条直径。求图中阴影区域面积，该区域位于小圆外部且在两个大圆内部。

English（选项同上方）

A circle of radius \(1\) is internally tangent to two circles of radius \(2\) at points \(A\) and \(B,\) where \(AB\) is a diameter of the smaller circle. What is the area of the region, shaded in the figure, that is outside the smaller circle and inside each of the two larger circles?

设两个大圆圆心为 \(A\) 和 \(B\)，小圆圆心为 \(C\)，两个大圆的一个交点为 \(D\)。

则 \(\triangle ACD\) 是直角三角形，且 \(AC = 1\)、\(AD = 2\)，所以 \(CD = \sqrt3\)、\(\angle CAD = 60^\circ\)，其面积为 \(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\)。

阴影区域的四分之一等于半径为 \(2\) 的大圆中一个 \(60^\circ\) 扇形面积 \(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\)，减去 \(\triangle ACD\) 的面积 \(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\)，再减去小圆四分之一的面积 \(\dfrac{\pi}{4}\)，得到 \(\dfrac{2\pi}{3} - \dfrac{\sqrt3}{2} - \dfrac{\pi}{4} = \dfrac{5\pi}{12} - \dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\)。

乘以 \(4\)，阴影总面积为 \(\dfrac{5\pi}{3} - 2\sqrt3\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Let the large circles have centers \(A\) and \(B,\) let \(C\) be the center of the small circle, and let \(D\) be a point where the two large circles meet.

Then \(\triangle ACD\) is right with \(AC = 1\) and \(AD = 2,\) so \(CD = \sqrt3,\) \(\angle CAD = 60^\circ,\) and its area is \(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}.\)

One quarter of the shaded region equals the \(60^\circ\) sector of the radius-\(2\) circle (area \(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\)) minus \(\triangle ACD\) (area \(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\)) minus a quarter of the small circle (area \(\dfrac{\pi}{4}\)), giving \(\dfrac{2\pi}{3} - \dfrac{\sqrt3}{2} - \dfrac{\pi}{4} = \dfrac{5\pi}{12} - \dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}.\)

Multiplying by \(4,\) the shaded area is \(\dfrac{5\pi}{3} - 2\sqrt3.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.
