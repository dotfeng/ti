Alicia 有两个容器。第一个容器的水占其容量的 \(\frac{5}{6}\)，第二个是空的。她把第一个容器中的水全部倒入第二个容器，此时水占第二个容器容量的 \(\frac{3}{4}\)。较小容器体积与较大容器体积的比是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Alicia had two containers. The first was \(\frac{5}{6}\) full of water and the second was empty. She poured all the water from the first container into the second container, at which point the second container was \(\frac{3}{4}\) full of water. What is the ratio of the volume of the smaller container to the volume of the larger container?

设第一个和第二个容器的体积分别为 \(F\) 和 \(S\)。水量既等于 \(\dfrac56F\)，也等于 \(\dfrac34S\)，所以 \(\dfrac56F=\dfrac34S\)。

因此 \(\dfrac{F}{S}=\dfrac{\frac{3}{4}}{\frac{5}{6}}=\dfrac9{10}\)。第一个容器较小，所以所求体积比为 \(\dfrac9{10}\)。所以正确答案是 D。

English

Let the volumes of the first and second containers be \(F\) and \(S\). The amount of water is both \(\dfrac56F\) and \(\dfrac34S\), so \(\dfrac56F=\dfrac34S\).

Thus \(\dfrac{F}{S}=\dfrac{\frac{3}{4}}{\frac{5}{6}}=\dfrac9{10}\). Since the first container is smaller, the ratio of the smaller container to the larger container is \(\dfrac9{10}\). Thus, D is the correct answer.

考虑命题：“如果 \(n\) 不是质数，那么 \(n-2\) 是质数。”下列哪个 \(n\) 的值是这个命题的反例？

English（选项同上方）

Consider the statement, “If \(n\) is not prime, then \(n-2\) is prime.” Which of the following values of \(n\) is a counterexample to this statement?

反例必须满足 \(n\) 不是质数，所以 \(n\) 的候选值只有 \(15,21,27\)。同时 \(n-2\) 也必须不是质数，只有 \(27\) 符合。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

We need \(n\) to not be prime, so \(n\) can only be \(15,21,27.\) Then, \(n-2\) must be not prime, leaving just \(27.\)

Thus, the answer is E .

3单选

一所高中有 \(500\) 名学生。\(40\%\) 的高年级学生会演奏乐器，而 \(30\%\) 的非高年级学生不会演奏乐器。全校共有 \(46.8\%\) 的学生不会演奏乐器。有多少非高年级学生会演奏乐器？

English（选项同上方）

In a high school with \(500\) students, \(40\%\) of the seniors play a musical instrument, while \(30\%\) of the non-seniors do not play a musical instrument. In all, \(46.8\%\) of the students do not play a musical instrument. How many non-seniors play a musical instrument?

设高年级学生人数为 \(s\)。则非高年级学生有 \(500-s\) 人。高年级学生中 \(60\%\) 不会演奏乐器。因此，不会演奏乐器的人数可表示为 \[ \begin{aligned} &0.3(500-s)+0.6(s) \\ &= 0.3s+150 \end{aligned} \] 另一方面， \[0.468\cdot 500=234\text{。}\] 所以 \[0.3s+150=234\] \[s=280\text{。}\] 非高年级学生有 \(220\) 人，其中 \(70\%\) 会演奏乐器，因此人数为 \[220\cdot 0.7=154\text{。}\]

所以答案是 B。

English

Let the number of seniors be \(s.\) Then, \(500-s\) people aren’t seniors. We know \(60\%\) of seniors don’t play an instrument. Then, the number of students who don’t play an instrument can be represented as \[ \begin{aligned} &0.3(500-s)+0.6(s) \\ &= 0.3s+150 \end{aligned} \] and \[0.468\cdot 500=234.\] Thus, \[0.3s+150=234\] \[s=280.\] This makes the number of non-seniors equal to \(220.\) Since \(70\%\) of non-seniors play instruments, we have the total number as \[220\cdot 0.7=154.\]

Thus, the answer is B .

4单选

所有方程为 \(ax+by=c\) 且 \(a\)，\(b\)，\(c\) 构成等差数列的直线都经过同一个点。这个点的坐标是什么？

English（选项同上方）

All lines with equation \(ax+by=c\) such that \(a,\) \(b,\) \(c\) form an arithmetic progression pass through a common point. What are the coordinates of that point?

令 \(d=b-a\)。

于是 \[(a,b,c)= (a,a+d,a+2d)\text{。}\] 因此 \[ax+(a+d)y=a+2d\text{。}\] 分别比较 \(a\) 和 \(d\) 的部分，可得 \[ax+ay=a\] 以及 \[dy=2d\] 对所有 \(a,d\) 都成立。所以 \[y=2,x+y=1\] 从而 \[x=-1\text{。}\] 公共点为 \((-1,2)\)。

所以答案是 A。

English

Let \(d=b-a.\)

Then, we have \[(a,b,c)= (a,a+d,a+2d).\] Thus, \[ax+(a+d)y=a+2d.\] If we match the parts of \(a\) and \(d,\) we get \[ax+ay=a\] and \[dy=2d\] for all \(a,d.\) Therefore, we have \[y=2,x+y=1\] implying that \[x=-1.\] This makes the pair \((-1,2).\)

Thus, the answer is A .

5单选

三角形 \(ABC\) 位于第一象限。点 \(A\)、\(B\) 和 \(C\) 关于直线 \(y=x\) 的对称点分别为 \(A'\)、\(B'\) 和 \(C'\)。假设三角形的顶点都不在直线 \(y=x\) 上。下列哪一项不一定总为真？

English（选项同上方）

Triangle \(ABC\) lies in the first quadrant. Points \(A,\) \(B,\) and \(C\) are reflected across the line \(y=x\) to points \(A',\) \(B',\) and \(C',\) respectively. Assume that none of the vertices of the triangle lie on the line \(y=x.\) Which of the following statements is <u>not</u> always true?

关于 \(y=x\) 的反射把 \((x,y)\) 映为 \((y,x)\)。因此它既保持第一象限，也保持面积，所以选项 A 和 B 必为真。

从 \((x,y)\) 指向它的像 \((y,x)\) 的方向向量是 \((y-x,x-y)\)，由于 \(x\ne y\)，它的斜率为 \(-1\)。因此选项 C 和 D 也必为真。

对于选项 E，取 \(A=(2,1)\)、\(B=(3,2)\) 和 \(C=(4,1)\)。这些点满足全部条件，但 \(AB\) 和 \(A'B'\) 的斜率都是 \(1\)，所以这两条直线平行而不是垂直。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Reflection across \(y=x\) sends \((x,y)\) to \((y,x)\). It therefore preserves the first quadrant and preserves area, so A and B are always true.

The direction from \((x,y)\) to its image \((y,x)\) is \((y-x,x-y)\), whose slope is \(-1\) because \(x\ne y\). Thus C and D are always true as well.

For E, take \(A=(2,1)\), \(B=(3,2)\), and \(C=(4,1)\). These points satisfy all the conditions, but \(AB\) and \(A'B'\) both have slope \(1\), so the two lines are parallel rather than perpendicular.

Thus, the answer is E .

6单选

正整数 \(n\) 满足方程 \[(n+1)! + (n+2)! = n! \cdot 440\text{。}\] 求 \(n\) 的各位数字之和。

English（选项同上方）

A positive integer \(n\) satisfies the equation \[(n+1)! + (n+2)! = n! \cdot 440.\] What is the sum of the digits of \(n?\)

左边可改写为 \[(n+1)n!+(n+2)(n+1)n!\] \[=((n+2)^2-1)n!\text{，}\] 所以 \[((n+2)^2-1) n! = 440 n!\] 因此 \[(n+2)^2=441\text{，}\] 得 \(n=19\)。各位数字和为 \(10\)。

所以答案是 C。

English

We can rewrite the left side as \[(n+1)n!+(n+2)(n+1)n!\]\[=((n+2)^2-1)n!,\] so \[((n+2)^2-1) n! = 440 n!\] Therefore, \[(n+2)^2=441,\] so \(n=19.\) The sum of its digits is \(10.\)

Thus, the answer is C .

一家商店中每颗糖的价格都是整数美分。Casper 的钱恰好可以买 \(12\) 颗红糖、\(14\) 颗绿糖、\(15\) 颗蓝糖，或者 \(n\) 颗紫糖。一颗紫糖价格为 \(20\) 美分。\(n\) 的最小可能值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Each piece of candy in a shop costs a whole number of cents. Casper has exactly enough money to buy either \(12\) pieces of red candy, \(14\) pieces of green candy, \(15\) pieces of blue candy, or \(n\) pieces of purple candy. A piece of purple candy costs \(20\) cents. What is the least possible value of \(n?\)

设 Casper 有 \(c\) 美分。那么 \(c\) 是 \(12,14\) 和 \(15\) 的公倍数，所以它必为 \(420\) 的倍数。

令 \(c=420k\)，其中 \(k\) 为整数。又有 \(c=20n\)，所以 \(420k=20n\)，即 \(n=21k\)。因为 \(k\) 是正整数，\(n\) 的最小值是 \(21\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Let the number of cents he has \(c.\) Then, \(c\) is a multiple of \(12,14,\) and \(15.\) Thus, it must be a multiple of \(420.\)

Let \(c=420k\) for some \(k.\) Also, \(c=20n,\) so \(420k=20n,\) making \(n=21k.\) Since \(k\) is a whole number, the minimum possible value of \(n\) is \(21.\)

Thus, the answer is B .

8单选

下图显示一个正方形和四个等边三角形。每个三角形都有一条边在正方形的一条边上，每个三角形边长为 \(2\)，且四个三角形的第三个顶点在正方形中心相交。正方形内但三角形外的区域被涂色。涂色区域的面积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The figure below shows a square and four equilateral triangles, with each triangle having a side lying on a side of the square, such that each triangle has side length \(2\) and the third vertices of the triangles meet at the center of the square. The region inside the square but outside the triangles is shaded. What is the area of the shaded region?

每个等边三角形的边长为 \(2\)，所以它的高为 \(\sqrt3\)。这条高恰好从正方形的一条边伸到正方形中心，因此正方形的边长为 \(2\sqrt3\)，面积为 \(12\)。

四个等边三角形的面积都是 \(\frac{\sqrt3}{4}(2^2)=\sqrt3\)。因此涂色区域的面积为 \[12-4\sqrt3\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Each equilateral triangle has side length \(2\), so its altitude is \(\sqrt3\). Because that altitude runs from a side of the square to its center, the square has side length \(2\sqrt3\) and area \(12\).

Each of the four equilateral triangles has area \(\frac{\sqrt3}{4}(2^2)=\sqrt3\). Therefore the shaded area is \[12-4\sqrt3.\]

Thus, the answer is B .

9单选

函数 \(f\) 对所有实数 \(x\) 定义为 \[f(x) = \lfloor|x|\rfloor - |\lfloor x \rfloor|\] 其中 \(\lfloor r \rfloor\) 表示不超过实数 \(r\) 的最大整数。求 \(f\) 的值域。

English（选项同上方）

The function \(f\) is defined by \[f(x) = \lfloor|x|\rfloor - |\lfloor x \rfloor|\] for all real numbers \(x,\) where \(\lfloor r \rfloor\) denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to the real number \(r.\) What is the range of \(f?\)

若 \(x\ge0\)，则 \(\lfloor|x|\rfloor=\lfloor x\rfloor=|\lfloor x\rfloor|\)，所以 \(f(x)=0\)。

若 \(x\) 是负整数，则两项都等于 \(|x|\)，所以仍有 \(f(x)=0\)。

若 \(x\) 是负数且不是整数，可以写成 \(x=-k-t\)，其中 \(k\) 是非负整数，且 \(0<t<1\)。此时 \(\lfloor|x|\rfloor=k\)，而 \(|\lfloor x\rfloor|=|-k-1|=k+1\)，所以 \(f(x)=-1\)。

因此值域为 \(\{-1,0\}\)。

所以答案是 A。

English

If \(x\ge0\), then \(\lfloor|x|\rfloor=\lfloor x\rfloor=|\lfloor x\rfloor|\), so \(f(x)=0\).

If \(x\) is a negative integer, both terms equal \(|x|\), so again \(f(x)=0\).

If \(x\) is negative and not an integer, write \(x=-k-t\), where \(k\) is a nonnegative integer and \(0<t<1\). Then \(\lfloor|x|\rfloor=k\), while \(|\lfloor x\rfloor|=|-k-1|=k+1\), so \(f(x)=-1\).

Therefore, the range is \(\{-1,0\}.\)

Thus, the answer is A .

10单选

在一个平面内，点 \(A\) 和 \(B\) 相距 \(10\) 个单位。平面内有多少个点 \(C\)，使得三角形 \(\triangle ABC\) 的周长为 \(50\) 个单位，三角形 \(\triangle ABC\) 的面积为 \(100\) 平方单位？

English（选项同上方）

In a given plane, points \(A\) and \(B\) are \(10\) units apart. How many points \(C\) are there in the plane such that the perimeter of \(\triangle ABC\) is \(50\) units and the area of \(\triangle ABC\) is \(100\) square units?

面积条件确定了 \(C\) 到直线 \(AB\) 的距离。若该距离为 \(h\)，则 \[\frac{10h}{2}=100\text{，}\] 所以 \(h=20\)。因此 \(C\) 必须位于与 \(AB\) 平行且距离为 \(20\) 的直线上。

因为 \(C\) 到直线 \(AB\) 的垂直距离为 \(20\)，所以 \(AC\) 和 \(BC\) 都至少为 \(20\)。它们不能同时等于 \(20\)，否则从 \(C\) 向直线 \(AB\) 作的垂线就必须同时落在不同的点 \(A\) 和 \(B\) 上。因此 \(AC+BC>40\)，与周长条件 \(AC+BC=40\) 矛盾。所以不存在符合条件的点 \(C\)。

所以答案是 A。

English

The area condition fixes the distance from \(C\) to line \(AB.\) If that distance is \(h,\) then \[\frac{10h}{2}=100,\] so \(h=20.\) Thus \(C\) must lie on a line parallel to \(AB\) at distance \(20.\)

Since the perpendicular distance from \(C\) to line \(AB\) is \(20\), both \(AC\) and \(BC\) are at least \(20\). They cannot both equal \(20\), because that would require the perpendicular from \(C\) to meet line \(AB\) at both distinct points \(A\) and \(B\). Hence \(AC+BC>40\), contradicting the perimeter requirement \(AC+BC=40\). Therefore no point \(C\) works.

Thus, the answer is A .

11单选

两个罐子中各有相同数量的弹珠，每颗弹珠不是蓝色就是绿色。罐子 \(1\) 中蓝珠与绿珠的比为 \(9:1\)，罐子 \(2\) 中蓝珠与绿珠的比为 \(8:1\)。两个罐子中共有 \(95\) 颗绿珠。罐子 \(1\) 比罐子 \(2\) 多多少颗蓝珠？

English（选项同上方）

Two jars each contain the same number of marbles, and every marble is either blue or green. In Jar \(1\) the ratio of blue to green marbles is \(9:1,\) and the ratio of blue to green marbles in Jar \(2\) is \(8:1.\) There are \(95\) green marbles in all. How many more blue marbles are in Jar \(1\) than in Jar \(2?\)

设绿珠数分别为 \(x\) 和 \(y\)，对应罐子为 \(1\) 和 \(2\)。那么两个罐子的弹珠总数分别为 \(10x\) 和 \(9y\)。

两个罐子的弹珠总数相同，所以 \(10x=9y\)。又有 \(x+y=95\)。解得 \(x=45\)，\(y=50\)。

罐子 \(1\) 有 \(9x=405\) 颗蓝珠，罐子 \(2\) 有 \(8y=400\) 颗蓝珠，相差 \(5\) 颗。所以正确答案是 A。

English

Let \(x\) and \(y\) be the numbers of green marbles in Jars \(1\) and \(2\), respectively. Then the total numbers of marbles in the jars are \(10x\) and \(9y\).

The jars contain the same number of marbles, so \(10x=9y\). Also \(x+y=95\). Solving gives \(x=45\) and \(y=50\).

Jar \(1\) has \(9x=405\) blue marbles, and Jar \(2\) has \(8y=400\) blue marbles. The difference is \(5\). Thus, A is the correct answer.

12单选

小于 \(2019\) 的正整数中，其以七为底的表示的各位数字之和最大可能是多少？

English（选项同上方）

What is the greatest possible sum of the digits in the base-seven representation of a positive integer less than \(2019?\)

首先，\(2019=5613_7\)。任何首位不超过 \(4\) 的数，其数位和至多为 \(4+6+6+6=22\)，而 \(4666_7<5613_7\) 达到了这个数位和。

若首位为 \(5\)，则第二位不超过 \(5\) 时，数位和至多为 \(5+5+6+6=22\)。若前两位是 \(56\)，与 \(5613_7\) 比较可知，末两位的数位和至多为 \(1+2\)，得到更小的总和。因此最大的数位和是 \(22\)。

所以答案是 C。

English

First, \(2019=5613_7\). Any number with leading digit at most \(4\) has digit sum at most \(4+6+6+6=22\), and \(4666_7<5613_7\) attains that sum.

If the leading digit is \(5\), then a second digit at most \(5\) gives digit sum at most \(5+5+6+6=22\). If the first two digits are \(56\), comparison with \(5613_7\) forces the last two digits to contribute at most \(1+2\), giving an even smaller sum. Therefore, the largest digit sum is \(22\).

Thus, the answer is C .

13单选

所有满足下列条件的实数 \(x\) 的和是多少：\(4\)，\(6\)，\(8\)，\(17\) 和 \(x\) 这五个数的中位数等于它们的平均数？

English（选项同上方）

What is the sum of all real numbers \(x\) for which the median of the numbers \(4,\) \(6,\) \(8,\) \(17,\) and \(x\) is equal to the mean of those five numbers?

平均数为 \(7+\frac{x}{5}\)。若 \(x<6\)，中位数为 \(6\)，所以 \(7+\frac{x}{5}=6\) 给出有效值 \(x=-5\)。

若 \(6\le x\le8\)，中位数为 \(x\)，但 \(7+\frac{x}{5}=x\) 给出 \(x=\frac{35}{4}\)，不在该区间内。若 \(x>8\)，中位数为 \(8\)，但 \(7+\frac{x}{5}=8\) 给出 \(x=5\)，仍不在所需范围内。

因此唯一可能的 \(x\) 是 \(-5\)，其和为 \(-5\)。

所以答案是 A。

English

The mean is \(7+\frac{x}{5}\). If \(x<6\), the median is \(6\), so \(7+\frac{x}{5}=6\) gives the valid value \(x=-5\).

If \(6\le x\le8\), the median is \(x\), but \(7+\frac{x}{5}=x\) gives \(x=\frac{35}{4}\), outside this interval. If \(x>8\), the median is \(8\), but \(7+\frac{x}{5}=8\) gives \(x=5\), again outside the required range.

Therefore, the only possible \(x\) is \(-5,\) making the sum \(-5.\)

Thus, the answer is A .

14单选

\(19!\) 的十进制表示为 \(121\)，\(6T5\)，\(100\)，\(40M\)，\(832\)，\(H00\)，其中 \(T\)、\(M\)、\(H\) 表示未给出的数字。求 \(T+M+H\)。

English（选项同上方）

The base-ten representation for \(19!\) is \(121,\) \(6T5,\) \(100,\) \(40M,\) \(832,\) \(H00,\) where \(T,\) \(M,\) and \(H\) denote digits that are not given. What is \(T+M+H?\)

因为 \(19!\) 能被 \(1000\) 整除，它的末三位都是零，所以 \(H=0\)。

因为 \(19!\) 能被 \(9\) 整除，它的数位和 \(33+T+M\) 也能被 \(9\) 整除。因此 \(T+M\equiv3\pmod9\)，所以 \(T+M\) 是 \(3\) 或 \(12\)。

能被 \(11\) 整除说明交错数位和 \(T-M-7\) 能被 \(11\) 整除，所以 \(T-M\equiv7\pmod{11}\)。检查这两个同余式下的数字可能性，得到 \(T=4\)、\(M=8\)。因此 \(T+M+H=4+8+0=12\)。

所以答案是 C。

English

Because \(19!\) is divisible by \(1000\), its last three digits are zero, so \(H=0\).

Since \(19!\) is divisible by \(9\), its digit sum \(33+T+M\) is divisible by \(9\). Hence \(T+M\equiv3\pmod9\), so \(T+M\) is either \(3\) or \(12\).

Divisibility by \(11\) says the alternating digit sum \(T-M-7\) is divisible by \(11\), so \(T-M\equiv7\pmod{11}\). Checking the digit possibilities from these two congruences gives \(T=4\) and \(M=8\). Therefore \(T+M+H=4+8+0=12\).

Thus, the answer is C .

15单选

直角三角形 \(T_1\) 和 \(T_2\) 的面积分别为 \(1\) 和 \(2\)。\(T_1\) 的一条边与 \(T_2\) 的一条边全等，并且 \(T_1\) 的另一条边与 \(T_2\) 的另一条边全等。\(T_1\) 和 \(T_2\) 各自剩下的（第三条）边的长度之积的平方是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Define a sequence recursively by \(x_0=5\) and \[x_{n+1}=\frac{x_n^2+5x_n+4}{x_n+6}\] for all nonnegative integers \(n.\) Let \(m\) be the least positive integer such that \[x_m\leq 4+\frac{1}{2^{20}}.\] In which of the following intervals does \(m\) lie?

令 \(a_n=x_n-4\)，则 \(a_0=1\)。化简递推式可得 \[a_{n+1}=\frac{a_n(a_n+9)}{a_n+10}\text{。}\] 只要 \(0<a_n\le1\)，就有 \[\frac9{10}a_n\le a_{n+1}\le\frac{10}{11}a_n\text{。}\]

由归纳法，\(\left(\dfrac9{10}\right)^n\le a_n\le\left(\dfrac{10}{11}\right)^n\)。当 \(n=80\) 时，\(\left(\dfrac{10}{9}\right)^{80}<2^{20}\)，所以 \(\left(\dfrac9{10}\right)^{80}>2^{-20}\)，从而 \(m>80\)。

另一方面，\(\left(\dfrac{11}{10}\right)^8>2\)，所以 \(\left(\dfrac{10}{11}\right)^{160}<2^{-20}\)，从而 \(m\le160\)。于是 \(81\le m\le160\)，所以 \(m\) 位于 \([81,242]\)。正确答案是 C。

English

Let \(a_n=x_n-4\). Then \(a_0=1\), and simplifying the recurrence gives \[a_{n+1}=\frac{a_n(a_n+9)}{a_n+10}.\] As long as \(0<a_n\le1\), this implies \[\frac9{10}a_n\le a_{n+1}\le\frac{10}{11}a_n.\]

By induction, \(\left(\dfrac9{10}\right)^n\le a_n\le\left(\dfrac{10}{11}\right)^n\). For \(n=80\), \(\left(\dfrac{10}{9}\right)^{80}<2^{20}\), so \(\left(\dfrac9{10}\right)^{80}>2^{-20}\), and therefore \(m>80\).

Also \(\left(\dfrac{11}{10}\right)^8>2\), so \(\left(\dfrac{10}{11}\right)^{160}<2^{-20}\), which gives \(m\le160\). Hence \(81\le m\le160\), so \(m\) lies in \([81,242]\). Thus, C is the correct answer.

25单选

由 \(0\) 和 \(1\) 组成的长度为 \(19\) 的序列中，有多少个以 \(0\) 开头、以 \(0\) 结尾，且既不含两个连续的 \(0\)，也不含三个连续的 \(1\)？

English（选项同上方）

How many sequences of \(0\)s and \(1\)s of length \(19\) are there that begin with a \(0,\) end with a \(0,\) contain no two consecutive \(0\)s, and contain no three consecutive \(1\)s?

序列先以一个 \(0\) 开头，之后由若干个 \(110\) 块和 \(10\) 块依次组成，每个块都接在一个 \(0\) 后面。

设 \(110\) 块有 \(x\) 个，\(10\) 块有 \(y\) 个。于是序列的项数为 \[3x+2y+1=19\text{，}\] 即 \[3x+2y=18\text{。}\] 可能的有序对为 \[ \begin{aligned} (x,y)&=(6,0),(4,3),\\ &\quad(2,6),(0,9) \end{aligned}\text{。}\] 于是排列这 \(x+y\) 个块的方法数为 \[\binom{x+y}x\text{，}\] 因为只需选定 \(x+y\) 个位置中哪 \(x\) 个放 \(110\) 块。

因此总数为 \[\binom 66 + \binom 74 + \binom 82 + \binom 90\] \[=1+35+28+1\] \[=65\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Our sequence starts with a \(0\) then has sequences of \(110\) and \(10\) in some order, where they each come after a \(0.\)

Let the number of \(110\) be \(x\) and let the number of \(10\) be \(y.\) Then the number of terms in the sequence is \[3x+2y+1=19,\] making \[3x+2y=18.\] The possible ordered pairs are \[ \begin{aligned} (x,y)&=(6,0),(4,3),\\ &\quad(2,6),(0,9). \end{aligned} \] Then, the number of ways to order the \(x+y\) blocks is \[\binom{x+y}x,\] since we choose which \(x\) of the \(x+y\) positions hold a \(110\) block.

Therefore, the total number of ways is \[\binom 66 + \binom 74 + \binom 82 + \binom 90\]\[=1+35+28+1\]\[=65.\]

Thus, the answer is C .
