由非零实数组成的有序三元组 \((a,b,c)\) 中，满足每个数都等于另外两个数之积的有多少个？

English（选项同上方）

How many ordered triples \((a,b,c)\) of nonzero real numbers have the property that each number is the product of the other two?

将 \(a=bc,\ b=ca,\ c=ab\) 相乘，得到 \[ abc=(abc)^2\text{。}\]由于变量都不为零，所以 \(abc=1\)。此外，\(abc=a^2=b^2=c^2\)，所以每个变量都是 \(1\) 或 \(-1\)。乘积为 \(1\) 时，要么没有负号，要么恰有两个负号，因此共有 \(1+3=4\) 个有序三元组。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Multiplying \(a=bc,\ b=ca,\ c=ab\) gives \[ abc=(abc)^2. \] Since none of the variables is zero, \(abc=1.\) Also \(abc=a^2=b^2=c^2,\) so each variable is \(1\) or \(-1.\) Product \(1\) allows either no negative signs or exactly two, giving \(1+3=4\) ordered triples.

Thus the correct answer is D.

English（选项同上方）

A cryptographer devises the following method for encoding positive integers. First, the integer is expressed in base \(5.\) Second, a \(1\)-to-\(1\) correspondence is established between the digits that appear in the expressions in base \(5\) and the elements of the set \(\{V,W,X,Y,Z\}.\) Using this correspondence, the cryptographer finds that three consecutive integers in increasing order are coded as \(VYZ,\) \(VYX,\) \(VVW,\) respectively. What is the base-\(10\) expression for the integer coded as \(XYZ?\)

由于 \(VYX\) 比 \(VYZ\) 大一，\(X\) 所对应的数字比 \(Z\) 所对应的数字大一。下一次递增使 \(VYX\) 变为 \(VVW\)，所以 \(X=4,\ W=0\)、\(Z=3\)。进位使 \(Y\) 变为 \(V\)，从而 \(Y=1,\ V=2\)。因此 \[ \begin{aligned} XYZ_5&=413_5,\\ 413_5&=4\cdot25+5+3\\ &=108 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

Thus the correct answer is D.

17单选

English（选项同上方）

In a mathematics competition, the sum of the scores of Bill and Dick equalled the sum of the scores of Ann and Carol. If the scores of Bill and Carol had been interchanged, then the sum of the scores of Ann and Carol would have exceeded the sum of the scores of the other two. 设 \(A,B,C,D\) 分别表示安、比尔、卡萝尔、迪克的分数。条件为 \[ \begin{aligned} A+C&=B+D,\\ A+B&\gt C+D,\\ D&\gt B+C \end{aligned}\text{。}\]将前两个比较关系相加，得到 \(A\gt D\)。用不等式减去等式，得到 \(B\gt C\)。最后，\(D\gt B+C\ge B\)。因此 \[ A\gt D\gt B\gt C\text{。}\]

Thus the correct answer is E.
