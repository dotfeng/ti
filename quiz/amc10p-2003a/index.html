1单选

前 \(2003\) 个正偶数的和与前 \(2003\) 个正奇数的和相差多少？

English（选项同上方）

What is the difference between the sum of the first \(2003\) even counting numbers and the sum of the first \(2003\) odd counting numbers?

第 \(k\) 个正偶数 \(2k\) 正好比第 \(k\) 个正奇数 \(2k-1\) 大 \(1\)。

对 \(2003\) 对求和，差为 \(2003 \cdot 1 = 2003\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The \(k\)th even number \(2k\) is exactly \(1\) more than the \(k\)th odd number \(2k-1.\)

Summing this difference over all \(2003\) pairs gives \(2003 \cdot 1 = 2003.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

2单选

罗克汉姆足球联赛的成员购买袜子和 T 恤。袜子每双 \(\$4\)，每件 T 恤比一双袜子贵 \(\$5\)。每位成员主场比赛需要一双袜子和一件 T 恤，客场比赛也需要一双袜子和一件 T 恤。如果总费用为 \(\$2366\)，联盟共有多少名成员？

English（选项同上方）

Members of the Rockham Soccer League buy socks and T-shirts. Socks cost \(\$4\) per pair and each T-shirt costs \(\$5\) more than a pair of socks. Each member needs one pair of socks and a shirt for home games and another pair of socks and a shirt for away games. If the total cost is \(\$2366,\) how many members are in the League?

每件 T 恤价格为 \(\$4 + \$5 = \$9\)。

每位成员需要两双袜子和两件 T 恤，费用为 \(2 \cdot 4 + 2 \cdot 9 = \$26\)。

成员数为 \(2366 \div 26 = 91\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Each T-shirt costs \(\$4 + \$5 = \$9.\)

Each member needs two pairs of socks and two shirts, costing \(2 \cdot 4 + 2 \cdot 9 = \$26.\)

The number of members is \(2366 \div 26 = 91.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

3单选

一个实心长方体盒子的尺寸为 \(15\) 厘米、\(10\) 厘米、\(8\) 厘米。从这个盒子的每个角切去一个边长 \(3\) 厘米的立方体，形成一个新的立体。原体积的百分之多少被切去？

English（选项同上方）

A solid box is \(15\) cm by \(10\) cm by \(8\) cm. A new solid is formed by removing a cube \(3\) cm on a side from each corner of this box. What percent of the original volume is removed?

被切去的八个立方体总体积为 \(8 \cdot 3^3 = 216\) 立方厘米。

原盒子体积为 \(15 \cdot 10 \cdot 8 = 1200\) 立方厘米。

切去的百分比为 \(\dfrac{216}{1200} \cdot 100\% = 18\%\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The eight removed cubes have total volume \(8 \cdot 3^3 = 216\) cubic centimeters.

The original box has volume \(15 \cdot 10 \cdot 8 = 1200\) cubic centimeters.

The percent removed is \(\dfrac{216}{1200} \cdot 100\% = 18\%.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

4单选

玛丽从家到学校上坡走 \(1\) 千米需要 \(30\) 分钟，但沿同一路线从学校回家只需 \(10\) 分钟。她往返全程的平均速度是多少千米每小时？

English（选项同上方）

It takes Mary \(30\) minutes to walk uphill \(1\) km from her home to school, but it takes her only \(10\) minutes to walk from school to home along the same route. What is her average speed, in km/hr, for the round trip?

玛丽总共走了 \(2\) 千米，用时 \(30 + 10 = 40\) 分钟。

因为 \(40\) 分钟是 \(\dfrac{2}{3}\) 小时，平均速度为 \(2 \div \dfrac{2}{3} = 3\) 千米每小时。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Mary walks a total of \(2\) km in \(30 + 10 = 40\) minutes.

Since \(40\) minutes is \(\dfrac{2}{3}\) hour, her average speed is \(2 \div \dfrac{2}{3} = 3\) km/hr.

Thus, the correct answer is A.

5单选

设 \(d\) 和 \(e\) 是方程 \(2x^2 + 3x - 5 = 0\) 的解。求 \((d - 1)(e - 1)\)？

English（选项同上方）

Let \(d\) and \(e\) denote the solutions of \(2x^2 + 3x - 5 = 0.\) What is the value of \((d - 1)(e - 1)?\)

因式分解得 \(2x^2 + 3x - 5 = (2x + 5)(x - 1)\)，所以两个根为 \(-\dfrac{5}{2}\) 和 \(1\)。

因为一个根等于 \(1\)，所以 \(d-1\) 和 \(e-1\) 这两个因子中有一个等于 \(0\)，乘积为 \(0\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Factoring gives \(2x^2 + 3x - 5 = (2x + 5)(x - 1),\) so the roots are \(-\dfrac{5}{2}\) and \(1.\)

Since one root equals \(1,\) one of the two factors \(d-1\) and \(e-1\) equals \(0,\) making the product \(0.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

6单选

对所有实数 \(x\) 和 \(y\)，定义 \(x \heartsuit y\) 为 \(|x - y|\)。下列哪一项不正确？

English（选项同上方）

Define \(x \heartsuit y\) to be \(|x - y|\) for all real numbers \(x\) and \(y.\) Which of the following statements is not true?

选项 C 声称 \(x \heartsuit 0 = x\)，但 \(x \heartsuit 0 = |x - 0| = |x|\)，当 \(x\) 为负数时不成立。例如 \(-1 \heartsuit 0 = 1 \ne -1\)。

其他选项都直接来自绝对值的性质。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Statement (C) claims \(x \heartsuit 0 = x,\) but \(x \heartsuit 0 = |x - 0| = |x|,\) which fails for negative \(x.\) For example, \(-1 \heartsuit 0 = 1 \ne -1.\)

The remaining statements all follow directly from the properties of absolute value.

Thus, the correct answer is C.

7单选

周长为 \(7\)、边长均为整数的互不全等三角形有多少个？

English（选项同上方）

How many non-congruent triangles with perimeter \(7\) have integer side lengths?

最长边不能超过 \(3\)，否则另外两边之和不能大于它。

可行的边长只有 \(1\)、\(3\)、\(3\) 和 \(2\)、\(2\)、\(3\)，因此共有 \(2\) 个三角形。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The longest side cannot exceed \(3,\) since otherwise the other two sides could not reach it.

The only possibilities are side lengths \(1,\) \(3,\) \(3\) and \(2,\) \(2,\) \(3,\) giving \(2\) triangles.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

8单选

从 \(60\) 的正因数中随机抽取一个，它小于 \(7\) 的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

What is the probability that a randomly drawn positive factor of \(60\) is less than \(7?\)

\(60\) 的因数为 \(1\)、\(2\)、\(3\)、\(4\)、\(5\)、\(6\)、\(10\)、\(12\)、\(15\)、\(20\)、\(30\) 和 \(60\)。

其中十二个因数有六个小于 \(7\)，所以概率为 \(\dfrac{6}{12} = \dfrac{1}{2}\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The factors of \(60\) are \(1,\) \(2,\) \(3,\) \(4,\) \(5,\) \(6,\) \(10,\) \(12,\) \(15,\) \(20,\) \(30,\) and \(60.\)

Six of these twelve factors are less than \(7,\) so the probability is \(\dfrac{6}{12} = \dfrac{1}{2}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

9单选

化简 \[\sqrt[3]{x\sqrt[3]{x\sqrt[3]{x\sqrt{x}}}}\text{。}\]

English（选项同上方）

Simplify \[\sqrt[3]{x\sqrt[3]{x\sqrt[3]{x\sqrt{x}}}}.\]

从内向外计算，\(x\sqrt{x} = x^{\frac{3}{2}}\)，它的立方根为 \(x^{\frac{1}{2}}\)。

接着 \(x \cdot x^{\frac{1}{2}} = x^{\frac{3}{2}}\)，它的立方根仍为 \(x^{\frac{1}{2}}\)。

于是下一层又是 \(x \cdot x^{\frac{1}{2}} = x^{\frac{3}{2}}\)，开立方后仍为 \(x^{\frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{x}\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Working outward, \(x\sqrt{x} = x^{\frac{3}{2}},\) and its cube root is \(x^{\frac{1}{2}}.\)

Then \(x \cdot x^{\frac{1}{2}} = x^{\frac{3}{2}},\) whose cube root is again \(x^{\frac{1}{2}}.\)

Repeating once more, \(x \cdot x^{\frac{1}{2}} = x^{\frac{3}{2}},\) whose cube root is \(x^{\frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{x}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

10单选

图中实线围成的多边形由 \(4\) 个全等正方形边对边连接而成。现在在标出的九个位置之一，沿一条边再接上一个全等正方形。所得九个多边形中，有多少个可以折成一个缺少一个面的立方体？

English（选项同上方）

The polygon enclosed by the solid lines in the figure consists of \(4\) congruent squares joined edge-to-edge. One more congruent square is attached to an edge at one of the nine positions indicated. How many of the nine resulting polygons can be folded to form a cube with one face missing?

先折起四个阴影正方形。它们占据立方体的四个不同面，留下两个空面。

逐一检查标号位置：位置 \(1\)、\(2\) 和 \(3\) 会使新正方形折到已被阴影正方形占据的面上。位置 \(4\)、\(5\)、\(6\)、\(7\)、\(8\) 和 \(9\) 会使它折到两个空面之一。因此 \(9\) 个多边形中恰有 \(6\) 个可行。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Fold the four shaded squares first. They occupy four distinct faces of the cube, leaving two faces open.

Checking the numbered attachments, positions \(1,\) \(2,\) and \(3\) fold the new square onto a face already occupied by one of the shaded squares. Positions \(4,\) \(5,\) \(6,\) \(7,\) \(8,\) and \(9\) fold it onto one of the two open faces. Therefore exactly \(6\) of the \(9\) polygons work.

Thus, the correct answer is E.

11单选

两个 \(5\) 位数 \(AMC10\) 和 \(AMC12\) 的和是 \(123422\)。求 \(A + M + C\)？

English（选项同上方）

The sum of the two \(5\)-digit numbers \(AMC10\) and \(AMC12\) is \(123422.\) What is \(A + M + C?\)

两个数分别为 \(100 \cdot \overline{AMC} + 10\) 和 \(100 \cdot \overline{AMC} + 12\)，所以 \(200 \cdot \overline{AMC} + 22 = 123422\)。

所以 \(200 \cdot \overline{AMC} = 123400\)，\(\overline{AMC} = 617\)。

\(A + M + C = 6 + 1 + 7 = 14\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The two numbers equal \(100 \cdot \overline{AMC} + 10\) and \(100 \cdot \overline{AMC} + 12,\) so their sum is \(200 \cdot \overline{AMC} + 22 = 123422.\)

Then \(200 \cdot \overline{AMC} = 123400,\) so \(\overline{AMC} = 617.\)

Therefore \(A + M + C = 6 + 1 + 7 = 14.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

12单选

从顶点为 \((0, 0)\)、\((4, 0)\)、\((4, 1)\) 和 \((0, 1)\) 的长方形内部随机选一点 \((x, y)\)。满足 \(x \lt y\) 的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A point \((x, y)\) is randomly picked from inside the rectangle with vertices \((0, 0),\) \((4, 0),\) \((4, 1),\) and \((0, 1).\) What is the probability that \(x \lt y?\)

条件 \(x \lt y\) 对应由 \(y = x\)、\(y = 1\) 和 \(x = 0\) 围成的三角形，顶点为 \((0, 0)\)、\((0, 1)\) 和 \((1, 1)\)。

这个三角形面积为 \(\dfrac{1}{2}\)，长方形面积为 \(4\)。

概率为 \(\dfrac{\frac{1}{2}}{4} = \dfrac{1}{8}\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The condition \(x \lt y\) holds in the triangle bounded by \(y = x,\) \(y = 1,\) and \(x = 0,\) which has vertices \((0, 0),\) \((0, 1),\) and \((1, 1).\)

This triangle has area \(\dfrac{1}{2},\) while the rectangle has area \(4.\)

The probability is \(\dfrac{\frac{1}{2}}{4} = \dfrac{1}{8}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

13单选

三个数的和为 \(20\)。第一个数是另外两个数之和的 \(4\) 倍。第二个数是第三个数的七倍。三个数的乘积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The sum of three numbers is \(20.\) The first is \(4\) times the sum of the other two. The second is seven times the third. What is the product of all three?

设三个数为 \(a\)、\(b\)、\(c\)。由于 \(a = 4(b + c)\)，可得 \(4(b + c) + (b + c) = 20\)，所以 \(b + c = 4\)，且 \(a = 16\)。

因为 \(b = 7c\)，所以 \(7c + c = 4\)，得 \(c = \dfrac{1}{2}\)，\(b = \dfrac{7}{2}\)。

乘积为 \(16 \cdot \dfrac{7}{2} \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = 28\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Let the numbers be \(a,\) \(b,\) \(c.\) Since \(a = 4(b + c),\) we get \(4(b + c) + (b + c) = 20,\) so \(b + c = 4\) and \(a = 16.\)

With \(b = 7c,\) we have \(7c + c = 4,\) so \(c = \dfrac{1}{2}\) and \(b = \dfrac{7}{2}.\)

The product is \(16 \cdot \dfrac{7}{2} \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = 28.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

14单选

设 \(n\) 是最大的整数，正好是 \(3\) 个不同质数 \(d\)、\(e\)、\(10d + e\) 的乘积，其中 \(d\) 和 \(e\) 是一位数字。求 \(n\) 的各位数字之和。

English（选项同上方）

Let \(n\) be the largest integer that is the product of exactly \(3\) distinct prime numbers, \(d,\) \(e,\) and \(10d + e,\) where \(d\) and \(e\) are single digits. What is the sum of the digits of \(n?\)

\(d\) 和 \(e\) 是集合 \(\{2,3,5,7\}\) 中两个不同的元素，而且 \(10d+e\) 也必须是质数。

从最大的十位数字开始。若 \(d=7\)，取 \(e=5\) 或 \(e=2\) 分别得到合数 \(75\) 和 \(72\)，而取 \(e=3\) 得到质数 \(73\)。于是 \(n=7\cdot3\cdot73=1533\)。

若 \(d=5\)，取 \(e=7\) 或 \(e=2\) 分别得到合数 \(57\) 和 \(52\)，而取 \(e=3\) 只能得到 \(5\cdot3\cdot53=795\)。所有 \(d\le3\) 的情况都不超过 \(3\cdot7\cdot37=777\)。所以 \(1533\) 是最大的有效值。

它的各位数字之和为 \(1 + 5 + 3 + 3 = 12\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Both \(d\) and \(e\) are distinct members of \(\{2,3,5,7\},\) and \(10d+e\) must also be prime.

Start with the largest possible tens digit. For \(d=7,\) the choices \(e=5\) and \(e=2\) give the composite numbers \(75\) and \(72,\) while \(e=3\) gives the prime \(73.\) This produces \(n=7\cdot3\cdot73=1533.\)

For \(d=5,\) the choices \(e=7\) and \(e=2\) give the composite numbers \(57\) and \(52,\) while \(e=3\) gives only \(5\cdot3\cdot53=795.\) Every case with \(d\le3\) is at most \(3\cdot7\cdot37=777.\) Hence \(1533\) is the largest valid value.

The sum of its digits is \(1 + 5 + 3 + 3 = 12.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

15单选

从集合 \(\{1, 2, 3, \ldots, 100\}\) 中随机选一个整数，它能被 \(2\) 整除但不能被 \(3\) 整除的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

What is the probability that an integer in the set \(\{1, 2, 3, \ldots, 100\}\) is divisible by \(2\) and not divisible by \(3?\)

\(100\) 个整数中有 \(50\) 个能被 \(2\) 整除。

其中同时能被 \(3\) 整除的是 \(6\) 的倍数，共 \(16\) 个。

因此符合条件的数有 \(50 - 16 = 34\) 个，概率为 \(\dfrac{34}{100} = \dfrac{17}{50}\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Of the \(100\) integers, \(50\) are divisible by \(2.\)

Among those, the ones also divisible by \(3\) are the multiples of \(6,\) of which there are \(16.\)

So \(50 - 16 = 34\) qualify, giving probability \(\dfrac{34}{100} = \dfrac{17}{50}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

16单选

\(13^{2003}\) 的个位数字是多少？

English（选项同上方）

What is the units digit of \(13^{2003}?\)

\(13^{2003}\) 的个位数字等同于 \(3^{2003}\) 的个位数字。

\(3\) 的幂个位数字按 \(3\)、\(9\)、\(7\)、\(1\) 循环，周期为 \(4\)。

因为 \(2003 = 4 \cdot 500 + 3\)，所以个位数字是循环中的第三个，即 \(7\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The units digit of \(13^{2003}\) matches that of \(3^{2003}.\)

Powers of \(3\) have units digits cycling \(3,\) \(9,\) \(7,\) \(1\) with period \(4.\)

Since \(2003 = 4 \cdot 500 + 3,\) the units digit is the third in the cycle, which is \(7.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

17单选

一个等边三角形周长的英寸数等于其外接圆面积的平方英寸数。这个圆的半径是多少英寸？

English（选项同上方）

The number of inches in the perimeter of an equilateral triangle equals the number of square inches in the area of its circumscribed circle. What is the radius, in inches, of the circle?

设边长为 \(s\)，外接圆半径为 \(R\)。由等边三角形中的 \(30\)-\(60\)-\(90\) 三角形，\(R = \dfrac{s}{\sqrt{3}}\)，所以 \(s = R\sqrt{3}\)。

周长为 \(3s = 3R\sqrt{3}\)，圆面积为 \(\pi R^2\)。

令二者相等，\(3R\sqrt{3} = \pi R^2\)，所以 \(R = \dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{\pi}\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Let the side length be \(s\) and the circumradius be \(R.\) From a \(30\)-\(60\)-\(90\) triangle formed by the center and a side, \(R = \dfrac{s}{\sqrt{3}},\) so \(s = R\sqrt{3}.\)

The perimeter is \(3s = 3R\sqrt{3}\) and the circle’s area is \(\pi R^2.\)

Setting them equal, \(3R\sqrt{3} = \pi R^2,\) so \(R = \dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{\pi}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

18单选

下面方程的各根倒数之和是多少？ \[\dfrac{2003}{2004}x + 1 + \dfrac{1}{x} = 0\text{？}\]

English（选项同上方）

What is the sum of the reciprocals of the roots of the equation \[\dfrac{2003}{2004}x + 1 + \dfrac{1}{x} = 0?\]

设 \(a = \dfrac{2003}{2004}\)。两边乘以 \(x\)，得 \(ax^2 + x + 1 = 0\)。

若根为 \(r\) 和 \(s\)，则由韦达定理 \(r + s = -\dfrac{1}{a}\)，且 \(rs = \dfrac{1}{a}\)。

倒数和为 \(\dfrac{1}{r} + \dfrac{1}{s} = \dfrac{r + s}{rs} = \dfrac{-\frac{1}{a}}{\frac{1}{a}} = -1\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Let \(a = \dfrac{2003}{2004}.\) Multiplying the equation by \(x\) gives \(ax^2 + x + 1 = 0.\)

If the roots are \(r\) and \(s,\) then by Vieta’s formulas \(r + s = -\dfrac{1}{a}\) and \(rs = \dfrac{1}{a}.\)

The sum of reciprocals is \(\dfrac{1}{r} + \dfrac{1}{s} = \dfrac{r + s}{rs} = \dfrac{-\frac{1}{a}}{\frac{1}{a}} = -1.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

19单选

一个直径为 \(1\) 的半圆位于一个直径为 \(2\) 的半圆顶部，如图所示。位于较小半圆内部且位于较大半圆外部的阴影区域称为弓月形。求这个弓月形的面积。

English（选项同上方）

A semicircle of diameter \(1\) sits at the top of a semicircle of diameter \(2,\) as shown. The shaded area inside the smaller semicircle and outside the larger semicircle is called a lune. Determine the area of this lune.

小半圆的直径是大圆中的一条长为 \(1\) 的弦。将这条弦的两端与大圆圆心相连，得到边长为 \(1\) 的等边三角形，面积为 \(\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\)。

弦与小弧之间的区域连同这个三角形，面积为 \(\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4} + \dfrac{1}{2}\pi\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^2 = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4} + \dfrac{\pi}{8}\)。

减去大圆中 \(60^\circ\) 扇形面积 \(\dfrac{1}{6}\pi(1)^2 = \dfrac{\pi}{6}\)，得到弓月形面积为 \(\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4} + \dfrac{\pi}{8} - \dfrac{\pi}{6} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4} - \dfrac{\pi}{24}\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The small semicircle’s diameter is a chord of length \(1\) in the large circle. Joining its endpoints to the large circle’s center gives an equilateral triangle of side \(1\) and area \(\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}.\)

The region between the chord and the small arc, taken together with that triangle, has area \(\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4} + \dfrac{1}{2}\pi\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^2 = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4} + \dfrac{\pi}{8}.\)

Subtracting the \(60^\circ\) sector of the large circle, of area \(\dfrac{1}{6}\pi(1)^2 = \dfrac{\pi}{6},\) leaves the lune: \(\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4} + \dfrac{\pi}{8} - \dfrac{\pi}{6} = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4} - \dfrac{\pi}{24}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

随机选择一个 \(10\) 进制三位数 \(n\)。下列哪个数最接近这样的概率：\(n\) 的 \(9\) 进制表示和 \(11\) 进制表示都是三位数？

English（选项同上方）

A base-\(10\) three-digit number \(n\) is selected at random. Which of the following is closest to the probability that the base-\(9\) representation and the base-\(11\) representation of \(n\) are both three-digit numerals?

最大的 \(9\) 进制三位数是 \(9^3 - 1 = 728\)，最小的 \(11\) 进制三位数是 \(11^2 = 121\)。

所以两种表示都为三位数当且仅当 \(121 \le n \le 728\)，共有 \(608\) 个整数。

全部三位数共有 \(900\) 个，概率为 \(\dfrac{608}{900} \approx 0.7\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The largest three-digit base-\(9\) number is \(9^3 - 1 = 728,\) and the smallest three-digit base-\(11\) number is \(11^2 = 121.\)

So both conditions hold exactly when \(121 \le n \le 728,\) giving \(608\) integers.

Out of \(900\) three-digit numbers, the probability is \(\dfrac{608}{900} \approx 0.7.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

Sally has five red cards numbered \(1\) through \(5\) and four blue cards numbered \(3\) through \(6.\) She stacks the cards so that the colors alternate and so that the number on each red card divides evenly into the number on each neighboring blue card. 设 \(n\) 是 \(5\) 位数，\(n\) 除以 \(100\) 时的商和余数分别为 \(q\) 和 \(r\)。有多少个 \(n\) 满足 \(q + r\) 能被 \(11\) 整除？

English（选项同上方）

Let \(n\) be a \(5\)-digit number, and let \(q\) and \(r\) be the quotient and remainder, respectively, when \(n\) is divided by \(100.\) For how many values of \(n\) is \(q + r\) divisible by \(11?\)

写成 \(n = 100q + r = (q + r) + 99q\)。

因为 \(99q\) 是 \(11\) 的倍数，所以 \(q + r\) 能被 \(11\) 整除当且仅当 \(n\) 也能被整除。

\(5\) 位数中，\(11\) 的倍数满足 \(10000 \le n \le 99999\)，共有

\[ \begin{aligned} &\left\lfloor\dfrac{99999}{11}\right\rfloor - \left\lfloor\dfrac{9999}{11}\right\rfloor \\ &= 9090 - 909 \\ &= 8181 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Write \(n = 100q + r = (q + r) + 99q.\)

Since \(99q\) is a multiple of \(11,\) \(q + r\) is divisible by \(11\) if and only if \(n\) is.

The \(5\)-digit multiples of \(11\) satisfy \(10000 \le n \le 99999,\) and there are

\[ \begin{aligned} &\left\lfloor\dfrac{99999}{11}\right\rfloor - \left\lfloor\dfrac{9999}{11}\right\rfloor \\ &= 9090 - 909 \\ &= 8181. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is B.
