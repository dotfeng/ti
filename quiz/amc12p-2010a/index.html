2单选

一艘渡船从上午 \(10\) 点开始，每小时载游客去一座岛，最后一班在下午 \(3\) 点出发。某天船长注意到上午 \(10\) 点那班有 \(100\) 名游客，并且之后每一班的游客数都比前一班少 \(1\) 人。那天这艘渡船一共载了多少名游客到岛上？

English（选项同上方）

A ferry boat shuttles tourists to an island every hour starting at \(10\) am until its last trip, which starts at \(3\) pm. One day the boat captain notes that on the \(10\) am trip there were \(100\) tourists on the ferry boat, and that on each successive trip, the number of tourists was \(1\) fewer than on the previous trip. How many tourists did the ferry take to the island that day?

渡船共有 \(6\) 班：\(10\) 点、\(11\) 点、\(12\) 点、\(1\) 点、\(2\) 点和 \(3\) 点。

游客数分别为 \(100,99,98,97,96,95\)，所以总数为 \[ \begin{gathered} 6\cdot100-(1+2+3+4+5) \\ =600-15 \\ =585 \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The ferry makes \(6\) trips: at \(10,\) \(11,\) \(12,\) \(1,\) \(2,\) and \(3.\)

The numbers of tourists are \(100,99,98,97,96,95,\) so the total is \[ \begin{gathered} 6\cdot100-(1+2+3+4+5) \\ =600-15 \\ =585. \end{gathered} \]

Thus, A is the correct answer.

3单选

如图所示，长方形 \(ABCD\) 与正方形 \(EFGH\) 有 \(50\%\) 的面积重叠。正方形 \(EFGH\) 与长方形 \(ABCD\) 有 \(20\%\) 的面积重叠。求 \(\dfrac{AB}{AD}\)？

English（选项同上方）

Rectangle \(ABCD,\) pictured below, shares \(50\%\) of its area with square \(EFGH.\) Square \(EFGH\) shares \(20\%\) of its area with rectangle \(ABCD.\) What is \(\dfrac{AB}{AD}?\)

设正方形 \(EFGH\) 的边长为 \(s\)。重叠部分宽为 \(s\)，高为 \(AD\)，所以面积为 \(s\cdot AD\)。

因为重叠部分占长方形面积的 \(50\%\)，所以 \(s\cdot AD=\tfrac12\,AB\cdot AD\)，从而 \(AB=2s\)。又因为它占正方形面积的 \(20\%\)，所以 \(s\cdot AD=\tfrac15 s^2\)，从而 \(AD=\tfrac{s}{5}\)。

因此 \[\dfrac{AB}{AD}=\dfrac{2s}{\frac{s}{5}}=10\text{。}\]

所以 E 是正确答案。

English

Let \(s\) be the side length of square \(EFGH.\) The shaded overlap has width \(s\) and height \(AD,\) so its area is \(s\cdot AD.\)

Because the overlap is \(50\%\) of the rectangle, \(s\cdot AD=\tfrac12\,AB\cdot AD,\) so \(AB=2s.\) Because it is \(20\%\) of the square, \(s\cdot AD=\tfrac15 s^2,\) so \(AD=\tfrac{s}{5}.\)

Therefore \[\dfrac{AB}{AD}=\dfrac{2s}{\frac{s}{5}}=10.\]

Thus, E is the correct answer.

4单选

若 \(x\lt0\)，下列哪一个一定为正？

English（选项同上方）

If \(x\lt0,\) then which of the following must be positive?

选项 D 为 \(-x^{-1}=-\dfrac1x\)。当 \(x\lt0\) 时，\(\dfrac1x\lt0\)，所以 \(-\dfrac1x\gt0\)。

取 \(x=-1\) 可知其他选项不一定为正：\(\dfrac{x}{|x|}=-1\)，\(-x^2=-1\)，\(-2^x=-\tfrac12\)，且 \(\sqrt[3]{x}=-1\)。

因此 D 是正确答案。

English

Choice (D) is \(-x^{-1}=-\dfrac1x.\) When \(x\lt0,\) \(\dfrac1x\lt0,\) so \(-\dfrac1x\gt0.\)

Testing \(x=-1\) shows the other choices need not be positive: \(\dfrac{x}{|x|}=-1,\) \(-x^2=-1,\) \(-2^x=-\tfrac12,\) and \(\sqrt[3]{x}=-1.\)

Thus, D is the correct answer.

5单选

在一场 \(100\) 箭的射箭比赛进行到一半时，Chelsea 领先 \(50\) 分。每箭射中靶心得 \(10\) 分，其他可能得分为 \(8\)、\(4\)、\(2\) 和 \(0\) 分。Chelsea 每箭至少得 \(4\) 分。如果 Chelsea 接下来 \(n\) 箭都射中靶心，她就能保证获胜。\(n\) 的最小值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Halfway through a \(100\)-shot archery tournament, Chelsea leads by \(50\) points. For each shot a bullseye scores \(10\) points, with other possible scores being \(8,\) \(4,\) \(2,\) and \(0\) points. Chelsea always scores at least \(4\) points on each shot. If Chelsea’s next \(n\) shots are bullseyes she will be guaranteed victory. What is the minimum value for \(n?\)

对手在最后 \(50\) 箭中最多可得 \(50\cdot10=500\) 分。Chelsea 已领先 \(50\) 分，所以她还需要超过 \(500-50=450\) 分。

设 Chelsea 有 \(n\) 箭靶心，得 \(10n\) 分，其余 \(50-n\) 箭至少得 \(4(50-n)\) 分。要保证获胜，需要 \[10n+4(50-n)\gt450\text{。}\] 化简得 \(6n\gt250\)，即 \(n\gt41\tfrac23\)。

因此至少需要 \(42\) 箭靶心。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The opponent can score at most \(50\cdot10=500\) on the last \(50\) shots. Since Chelsea leads by \(50,\) she must score more than \(500-50=450\) points on her remaining shots to guarantee victory.

Her \(n\) bullseyes give \(10n\) points, and her other \(50-n\) shots give at least \(4(50-n)\) points, so \[10n+4(50-n)\gt450.\] This simplifies to \(6n\gt250,\) i.e. \(n\gt41\tfrac23.\)

Therefore Chelsea needs at least \(42\) bullseyes.

Thus, C is the correct answer.

6单选

回文数，例如 \(83438\)，是一个数字反过来读仍相同的数。数 \(x\) 和 \(x + 32\) 分别是三位数和四位数回文数。\(x\) 的各位数字之和是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A palindrome, such as \(83438,\) is a number that remains the same when its digits are reversed. The numbers \(x\) and \(x + 32\) are three-digit and four-digit palindromes, respectively. What is the sum of the digits of \(x?\)

因为 \(x\) 至多为 \(999\)，所以 \(x + 32\) 至多为 \(1031\)。

同时 \(x + 32\) 至少为 \(1000\)。

这个范围内唯一的回文数是 \(1001\)，所以 \(x + 32\) 等于这个数。

于是 \(x=1001-32=969\)，它的各位数字之和为 \(9+6+9=24\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Note that \(x\) is at most \(999.\) This means that \(x + 32\) has a maximum of \(1031.\)

Similarly, we have that the minimum value of \(x + 32\) is \(1000.\)

The only palindrome in this range is \(1001,\) so this is what \(x + 32\) equals.

Thus \(x=1001-32=969,\) whose digit sum is \(9+6+9=24.\)

Thus, E is the correct answer.

7单选

Logan 正在制作他的城镇的比例模型。该城市的水塔高 \(40\) 米，顶部是一个可容纳 \(100{,}000\) 升水的球体。Logan 的迷你水塔可容纳 \(0.1\) 升水。他应该把水塔做成多少米高？

English（选项同上方）

Logan is constructing a scaled model of his town. The city’s water tower stands \(40\) meters high, and the top portion is a sphere that holds \(100{,}000\) liters of water. Logan’s miniature water tower holds \(0.1\) liters. How tall, in meters, should Logan make his tower?

模型与原物的体积比为 \(\dfrac{0.1}{100{,}000}=\dfrac{1}{1{,}000{,}000}\)。长度按体积比的立方根缩放，所以模型与原物的高度比为 \(\dfrac1{100}\)。因此模型的高度应为 \(\dfrac{40}{100}=0.4\) 米。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The model-to-original volume ratio is \(\dfrac{0.1}{100{,}000}=\dfrac{1}{1{,}000{,}000}.\) Lengths scale by the cube root, so the model-to-original height ratio is \(\dfrac1{100}.\) Therefore the model should be \(\dfrac{40}{100}=0.4\) meters tall.

Thus, C is the correct answer.

8单选

三角形 \(ABC\) 满足 \(AB=2 \cdot AC\)。点 \(D\) 和 \(E\) 分别在 \(\overline{AB}\) 和 \(\overline{BC}\) 上，且 \(\angle BAE = \angle ACD\)。设 \(F\) 为线段 \(AE\) 和 \(CD\) 的交点，并且 \(\triangle CFE\) 是等边三角形。求 \(\angle ACB\)？

English（选项同上方）

Triangle \(ABC\) has \(AB=2 \cdot AC.\) Let \(D\) and \(E\) be on \(\overline{AB}\) and \(\overline{BC},\) respectively, such that \(\angle BAE = \angle ACD.\) Let \(F\) be the intersection of segments \(AE\) and \(CD,\) and suppose that \(\triangle CFE\) is equilateral. What is \(\angle ACB?\)

设 \(\angle BAE=\angle ACD=x\)。因为 \(\triangle CFE\) 是等边三角形，所以 \(\angle CFE=60^\circ\)。射线 \(FA\) 与 \(FE\) 方向相反，因此 \(\angle AFC=120^\circ\)。

在 \(\triangle AFC\) 中，\(\angle FAC=180^\circ-120^\circ-x\) \(=60^\circ-x\)。因为 \(F\) 位于 \(AE\) 上，这个角就是 \(\angle EAC\)。所以 \(\angle BAC=x+(60^\circ-x)=60^\circ\)。

设 \(AC=s\)，则 \(AB=2s\)。由余弦定理，\[ \begin{aligned} BC^2 &= s^2+(2s)^2 \\ &\quad-2(s)(2s)\cos60^\circ \\ &=3s^2 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]因此三边之比为 \(1:\sqrt3:2\)，且 \(AB\) 为斜边，所以 \(\angle ACB=90^\circ\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Let \(\angle BAE=\angle ACD=x.\) Because \(\triangle CFE\) is equilateral, \(\angle CFE=60^\circ.\) Rays \(FA\) and \(FE\) are opposite, so \(\angle AFC=120^\circ.\)

In \(\triangle AFC,\) we get \(\angle FAC=180^\circ-120^\circ-x\) \(=60^\circ-x.\) Since \(F\) lies on \(AE,\) this is \(\angle EAC.\) Hence \(\angle BAC=x+(60^\circ-x)=60^\circ.\)

Put \(AC=s,\) so \(AB=2s.\) The Law of Cosines gives \[ \begin{aligned} BC^2 &= s^2+(2s)^2 \\ &\quad-2(s)(2s)\cos60^\circ \\ &=3s^2. \end{aligned} \] Thus the side lengths are in the ratio \(1:\sqrt3:2,\) with \(AB\) the hypotenuse, so \(\angle ACB=90^\circ.\)

Thus, C is the correct answer.

9单选

一个实心立方体边长为 \(3\) 英寸。在每个面的中心切出一个 \(2\) 英寸乘 \(2\) 英寸的正方形孔。每个切口的边都与立方体的边平行，并且每个孔都贯穿整个立方体。剩余立体的体积是多少立方英寸？

English（选项同上方）

A solid cube has side length \(3\) inches. A \(2\)-inch by \(2\)-inch square hole is cut into the center of each face. The edges of each cut are parallel to the edges of the cube, and each hole goes all the way through the cube. What is the volume, in cubic inches, of the remaining solid?

三个被切出的长方体都在立方体中心相交。

交集是边长为 \(2\) 的立方体。因此切除区域的体积为 \[ \begin{align*}3 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 - 2 \cdot 2^3 &= 36 - 16 \\&= 20\end{align*}\text{。}\]

中心区域被全部 \(3\) 个孔包含，因此要减去重复计入的两次。

剩余体积为 \[ 3^3 - 20 = 27 - 20 = 7\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Note that all the cut out solids intersect in the middle of the cube.

This region of intersection is a cube with side length \(2.\) Then the volume of the cutout region is \[ \begin{align*}3 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 - 2 \cdot 2^3 &= 36 - 16 \\&= 20.\end{align*} \]

We have to subtract out the center region twice since it is included in all \(3\) regions.

The remaining volume is then \[ 3^3 - 20 = 27 - 20 = 7. \]

Thus, A is the correct answer.

10单选

一个等差数列的前四项为 \(p\)、\(9\)、\(3p-q\) 和 \(3p+q\)。这个数列的第 \(2010\) 项是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The first four terms of an arithmetic sequence are \(p,\) \(9,\) \(3p-q,\) and \(3p+q.\) What is the \(2010\)th term of this sequence?

相邻两项之差都是公差 \(d\)，且 \(d=(3p+q)-(3p-q)=2q\)。

由前两项可得 \(9-p=d=2q\)，由第二、三项可得 \((3p-q)-9=d=2q\)。解得 \(p=5\)，\(q=2\)，\(d=4\)。

因此第 \(2010\) 项为 \[ \begin{aligned} p+2009d &= 5+2009\cdot4 \\ &= 8041 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Consecutive terms differ by a common difference \(d.\) From the last two terms, \(d=(3p+q)-(3p-q)=2q.\)

From the first two terms, \(9-p=d=2q,\) and from the second and third, \((3p-q)-9=d=2q.\) Solving this system gives \(p=5,\) \(q=2,\) and \(d=4.\)

The \(2010\)th term is \[ \begin{aligned} p+2009d &= 5+2009\cdot4 \\ &= 8041. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, A is the correct answer.

11单选

方程 \(7^{x+7}=8^x\) 的解可表示为 \(x=\log_b 7^7\)。求 \(b\)？

English（选项同上方）

The solution of the equation \(7^{x+7}=8^x\) can be expressed in the form \(x=\log_b 7^7.\) What is \(b?\)

因为 \(x=\log_b 7^7\)，所以 \(b^x=7^7\)。

因此 \[ \begin{aligned} (7b)^x=7^x\cdot b^x &= 7^x\cdot7^7 \\ &= 7^{x+7}=8^x \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

由于 \(x\gt0\)，可得 \(7b=8\)，所以 \(b=\dfrac{8}{7}\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Since \(x=\log_b 7^7,\) we have \(b^x=7^7.\)

Then \[ \begin{aligned} (7b)^x=7^x\cdot b^x &= 7^x\cdot7^7 \\ &= 7^{x+7}=8^x. \end{aligned} \]

Because \(x\gt0,\) it follows that \(7b=8,\) so \(b=\dfrac{8}{7}.\)

Thus, C is the correct answer.

12单选

在一片神奇的沼泽中，有两种会说话的两栖动物：蟾蜍总是说真话，青蛙总是说假话。Brian、Chris、LeRoy 和 Mike 四只两栖动物一起住在这片沼泽中，并作出以下陈述。

Brian：“Mike 和我是不同物种。”

Chris：“LeRoy 是青蛙。”

LeRoy：“Chris 是青蛙。”

Mike：“我们四个中至少有两个是蟾蜍。”

这四只两栖动物中有多少只是青蛙？

English（选项同上方）

In a magical swamp there are two species of talking amphibians: toads, whose statements are always true, and frogs, whose statements are always false. Four amphibians, Brian, Chris, LeRoy, and Mike live together in this swamp, and they make the following statements.

Brian: “Mike and I are different species.”

Chris: “LeRoy is a frog.”

LeRoy: “Chris is a frog.”

Mike: “Of the four of us, at least two are toads.”

How many of these four amphibians are frogs?

Chris 和 LeRoy 必定属于不同物种：如果 Chris 是蟾蜍，他的话说明 LeRoy 是青蛙；如果 Chris 是青蛙，他这句假话说明 LeRoy 是蟾蜍。因此他们两个中恰好有一只是蟾蜍。

如果 Brian 是蟾蜍，他说的是真话，于是 Mike 是青蛙。如果 Brian 是青蛙，他这句假话意味着他和 Mike 是同一物种，此时 Mike 仍然是青蛙。所以 Mike 一定是青蛙。

因此 Mike 的陈述是假话，也就是说蟾蜍不足两只。而 Chris 和 LeRoy 中已经恰好有一只蟾蜍，所以 Brian 只能是青蛙。于是四只中有一只蟾蜍和 \(3\) 只青蛙。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Chris and LeRoy are of opposite species: if Chris is a toad, his statement makes LeRoy a frog; if Chris is a frog, his false statement makes LeRoy a toad. Thus exactly one of them is a toad.

If Brian is a toad, his true statement makes Mike a frog. If Brian is a frog, his false statement says that he and Mike are the same species, again making Mike a frog. Therefore Mike is always a frog.

Mike’s statement is therefore false, so there are fewer than two toads. Chris and LeRoy already supply exactly one toad, forcing Brian to be a frog. Hence there is one toad and \(3\) frogs.

Thus, D is the correct answer.

13单选

对多少个整数 \(k\)，图像 \(x^2+y^2=k^2\) 与 \(xy=k\) 不相交？

English（选项同上方）

For how many integer values of \(k\) do the graphs of \(x^2+y^2=k^2\) and \(xy=k\) not intersect?

当 \(k=0\) 时，\(x^2+y^2=0\) 的图像是单点 \((0,0)\)，而 \(xy=0\) 是两条坐标轴，它们在原点相交。

当 \(k\ne0\) 时，圆的半径为 \(|k|\)，双曲线 \(xy=k\) 离原点最近的两个顶点到原点距离为 \(\sqrt{2|k|}\)。两图像相交当且仅当 \(|k|\ge\sqrt{2|k|}\)，即 \(|k|\ge2\)。

所以只有 \(|k|=1\) 时两图像不相交，也就是 \(k=1\) 和 \(k=-1\)，共有 \(2\) 个值。

因此 C 是正确答案。

English

For \(k=0,\) the graph of \(x^2+y^2=0\) is the single point \((0,0)\) and \(xy=0\) is the two axes, which meet at the origin, so the graphs intersect.

For \(k\ne0,\) the circle has radius \(|k|,\) and the hyperbola \(xy=k\) has its two vertices nearest the origin at distance \(\sqrt{2|k|}.\) The graphs meet exactly when \(|k|\ge\sqrt{2|k|},\) that is \(|k|\ge2.\)

So they fail to intersect only when \(|k|=1,\) namely \(k=1\) and \(k=-1,\) giving \(2\) values.

Thus, C is the correct answer.

14单选

非退化三角形 \(\triangle ABC\) 的边长都是整数，\(\overline{BD}\) 是角平分线，\(AD = 3\)，\(DC = 8\)。周长的最小可能值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Nondegenerate \(\triangle ABC\) has integer side lengths, \(\overline{BD}\) is an angle bisector, \(AD = 3,\) and \(DC = 8.\) What is the smallest possible value of the perimeter?

由角平分线定理，\[ \dfrac{AB}{3} = \dfrac{BC}{8} \] \[ AB = \dfrac{3}{8} BC\text{。}\]

为使 \(AB\) 和 \(BC\) 都是整数，\(BC\) 必须是 \(8\) 的倍数。

若为了最小化周长取 \(BC = 8\)、\(AB = 3\)，三角形会退化。

因此 \(BC\) 必须取 \(16\)，此时 \(AB = 6\)。又 \(AC = AD + DC = 11\)，周长为 \[ 16 + 6 + 11 = 33\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Using the Angle Bisector Theorem, we have that \[ \dfrac{AB}{3} = \dfrac{BC}{8} \]\[ AB = \dfrac{3}{8} BC. \]

For \(AB\) and \(BC\) to be integers, we must have that \(BC\) is a multiple of \(8.\)

To minimize the perimeter, we can set \(BC = 8\) and \(AB = 3.\) This, however, makes the triangle degenerate.

\(BC\) must then be \(16\) and \(AB = 6.\) Since \(AC = AD + DC = 11,\) the perimeter is \[ 16 + 6 + 11 = 33. \]

Thus, B is the correct answer.

15单选

一枚硬币被改造后，正面朝上的概率小于 \(\dfrac12\)，抛这枚硬币四次，正面和反面次数相等的概率为 \(\dfrac{1}{6}\)。这枚硬币正面朝上的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A coin is altered so that the probability that it lands on heads is less than \(\dfrac12,\) and when the coin is flipped four times, the probability of an equal number of heads and tails is \(\dfrac{1}{6}.\) What is the probability that the coin lands on heads?

设正面朝上的概率为 \(p\)。抛掷四次恰好出现两次正面和两次反面的概率为 \[ \begin{aligned} \binom{4}{2}p^2(1-p)^2 &= 6p^2(1-p)^2 \\ &= \frac16 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因此 \(p^2(1-p)^2=\dfrac{1}{36}\)，所以 \(p(1-p)=\dfrac16\)。

这给出 \(6p^2-6p+1=0\)，故 \(p=\dfrac{3\pm\sqrt3}{6}\)。由于 \(p\lt\dfrac12\)，取 \(p=\dfrac{3-\sqrt3}{6}\)。

因此 D 是正确答案。

English

Let \(p\) be the probability of heads. The chance of two heads and two tails in four flips is \[ \begin{aligned} \binom{4}{2}p^2(1-p)^2 &= 6p^2(1-p)^2 \\ &= \frac16. \end{aligned} \]

Thus \(p^2(1-p)^2=\dfrac{1}{36},\) so \(p(1-p)=\dfrac16.\)

These \(10\) zeros split \(\big(0,\tfrac12\big)\) into \(11\) subintervals on which \(g\) has constant sign. Near \(0\) every factor is positive, so \(g\gt0\) there, and the sign flips at each zero except \(\tfrac14=\tfrac28\) and \(\tfrac13=\tfrac26,\) where an even number of factors vanish.

Tracking the signs, exactly \(6\) of the \(11\) subintervals have \(g\gt0.\) By symmetry there are \(6\) more in \(\big(\tfrac12,1\big),\) so \(n=12.\)

Thus, B is the correct answer.

如果一个四边形可以通过旋转和平移得到另一个四边形，则认为这两个四边形相同。边长为整数且周长等于 \(32\) 的不同凸圆内接四边形有多少个？

English（选项同上方）

Two quadrilaterals are considered the same if one can be obtained from the other by a rotation and a translation. How many different convex cyclic quadrilaterals are there with integer sides and perimeter equal to \(32?\)

一个凸圆内接四边形由其按圆周顺序排列的边长唯一确定，且存在的条件是最大边长小于其他三边之和。周长为 \(32\) 时，每条边至多为 \(15\)。

先数正整数有序四元组 \((a,b,c,d)\)，满足 \(a+b+c+d=32\) 且每项至多为 \(15\)。若没有上界限制，有 \(\binom{31}{3}=4495\) 个；去掉某一项至少为 \(16\) 的情况，需减去 \(4\binom{16}{3}=2240\)，剩下 \(2255\) 个。

四边形的旋转对应于 \((a,b,c,d)\) 的循环置换。由 Burnside 引理，不同四边形的数量为 \[\frac{1}{4}\big(2255+f_1+f_2+f_3\big)\text{，}\] 其中 \(f_i\) 表示旋转 \(i\) 个位置后不变的四元组数。

旋转一步或三步只固定 \((8,8,8,8)\)，所以 \(f_1=f_3=1\)。旋转两步固定形如 \((a,b,a,b)\) 且 \(a+b=16\)、\(1\le a,b\le15\) 的四元组，所以 \(f_2=15\)。

因此数量为 \[ \begin{aligned} \frac{1}{4}(2255+1+15+1) &= \frac{2272}{4} \\ &= 568\text{。} \end{aligned} \]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

A convex cyclic quadrilateral is determined up to rotation and translation by its cyclic sequence of side lengths, and it exists exactly when the largest side is less than the sum of the others. With perimeter \(32,\) this means each side is at most \(15.\)

First count ordered quadruples \((a,b,c,d)\) of positive integers with \(a+b+c+d=32\) and each entry at most \(15.\) Without the upper bound there are \(\binom{31}{3}=4495;\) removing those with some entry at least \(16\) subtracts \(4\binom{16}{3}=2240,\) leaving \(2255.\)

Rotations of the quadrilateral correspond to cyclic permutations of \((a,b,c,d).\) By Burnside’s lemma the number of distinct quadrilaterals is \[\frac{1}{4}\big(2255+f_1+f_2+f_3\big),\] where \(f_i\) counts quadruples fixed by rotating \(i\) positions.

A one- or three-step rotation fixes only \((8,8,8,8),\) so \(f_1=f_3=1.\) A two-step rotation fixes \((a,b,a,b)\) with \(a+b=16\) and \(1\le a,b\le15,\) giving \(f_2=15.\)

Hence the count is \[ \begin{aligned} \frac{1}{4}(2255+1+15+1) &= \frac{2272}{4} \\ &= 568. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, C is the correct answer.
