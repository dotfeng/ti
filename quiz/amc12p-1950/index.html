若将 \(64\) 按 \(2\)、\(4\) 和 \(6\) 的比例分成三部分，则最小的一部分是：

English（选项同上方）

If \(64\) is divided into three parts proportional to \(2,\) \(4,\) and \(6,\) the smallest part is:

这个比共有 \(2+4+6=12\) 个等份。因此，最小的一部分为 \[ 64\left(\frac{2}{12}\right)=\frac{32}{3}=10\frac{2}{3}\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

The ratio contains \(2+4+6=12\) equal shares. The smallest part is therefore \[ 64\left(\frac{2}{12}\right)=\frac{32}{3}=10\frac{2}{3}. \]

Thus, the correct answer is C.

The sum of the roots of the equation \(4x^2+5-8x=0\) is equal to:

方程的标准形式为 \(4x^2-8x+5=0\)。由韦达定理，两根之和为 \[ -\frac{-8}{4}=2\text{。}\]这个值不在选项 A 至 D 中。

4单选

化为最简式后， \[ \frac{a^2-b^2}{ab}-\frac{ab-b^2}{ab-a^2} \] 等于：

English（选项同上方）

Reduced to lowest terms, \[ \frac{a^2-b^2}{ab}-\frac{ab-b^2}{ab-a^2} \] is equal to:

将各个分子和分母因式分解，得到 \[ \begin{aligned} &\frac{a^2-b^2}{ab} -\frac{ab-b^2}{ab-a^2}\\ &=\left(\frac{a}{b}-\frac{b}{a}\right)\\ &\quad-\frac{b(a-b)}{a(b-a)}\\ &=\frac{a}{b}-\frac{b}{a} +\frac{b}{a}\\ &=\frac{a}{b} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

Factoring the numerators and denominator gives \[ \begin{aligned} &\frac{a^2-b^2}{ab} -\frac{ab-b^2}{ab-a^2}\\ &=\left(\frac{a}{b}-\frac{b}{a}\right)\\ &\quad-\frac{b(a-b)}{a(b-a)}\\ &=\frac{a}{b}-\frac{b}{a} +\frac{b}{a}\\ &=\frac{a}{b}. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is A.

Thus, the correct answer is A.

Increasing \(r\) by \(100\%\) changes it to \(2r.\) The area changes from \(\pi r^2\) to \(\pi(2r)^2=4\pi r^2,\) an increase of \(3\pi r^2.\) This is \(300\%\) of the original area.

Thus, the correct answer is C.

9单选

半径为 \(r\) 的半圆内所能内接的最大三角形的面积为：

English（选项同上方）

The area of the largest triangle that can be inscribed in a semicircle whose radius is \(r\) is:

半圆内三角形的底边至多为直径 \(2r\)，高至多为 \(r\)。因此，其面积至多为 \[ \frac12(2r)(r)=r^2\text{。}\]取直径为底边、半圆的最高点为第三个顶点时，可以达到这个上界。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

A triangle in the semicircle has base at most the diameter \(2r\) and altitude at most \(r.\) Therefore its area is at most \[ \frac12(2r)(r)=r^2. \] This bound is attained by using the diameter as the base and the topmost point of the semicircle as the third vertex.

Thus, the correct answer is A.

English（选项同上方）

If in the formula \(C=\dfrac{en}{R+nr},\) \(n\) is increased while \(e,\) \(R,\) and \(r\) are kept constant, then \(C:\)

将公式改写为 \[ C=\frac{e}{\frac{R}{n}+r}\text{。}\]当正数 \(n\) 增大时，\(\frac{R}{n}\) 减小，所以正分母减小，而分子保持不变。因此，\(C\) 增大。

For the simultaneous equations

\[ \begin{aligned} 2x-3y&=8,\\ 6y-4x&=9, \end{aligned} \]

18单选

下列各式中

\((1)\quad a(x-y)=ax-ay\)

\((2)\quad a^{x-y}=a^x-a^y\)

\((3)\quad \log(x-y)=\log x-\log y\)

\((4)\quad \dfrac{\log x}{\log y}=\log x-\log y\)

\((5)\quad a(xy)=ax\times ay\)

English（选项同上方）

Of the following

\((1)\quad a(x-y)=ax-ay\)

\((2)\quad a^{x-y}=a^x-a^y\)

\((3)\quad \log(x-y)=\log x-\log y\)

\((4)\quad \dfrac{\log x}{\log y}=\log x-\log y\)

\((5)\quad a(xy)=ax\times ay\)

等式 \(1\) 是分配律。等式 \(2\) 错误，因为 \(a^{x-y}=\frac{a^x}{a^y}\)。等式 \(3\) 错误，因为 \(\log x-\log y=\log(\frac{x}{y})\)。等式 \(4\) 把换底公式中的商误写成了差；等式 \(5\) 的右边应化为 \(a^2xy\)，而不是 \(axy\)。

因此，只有等式 \(1\) 正确，所以正确答案是 E。

English

Statement \(1\) is the distributive property. Statement \(2\) is false because \(a^{x-y}=\frac{a^x}{a^y}.\) Statement \(3\) is false because \(\log x-\log y=\log(\frac{x}{y}).\) Statement \(4\) confuses the change-of-base quotient with a difference, and statement \(5\) has right side \(a^2xy,\) not \(axy.\)

Thus, only statement \(1\) is true, so the correct answer is E.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

English

The desired yearly return is \(0.055(10,000)=550\) dollars, so \[ \frac78(12r)-325=550. \] Hence \(10.5r=875,\) giving \(r=83.\overline3,\) which is \(\$83.33\) to the nearest cent.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

24单选

Thus, the correct answer is E.

25单选

\(\log_5\dfrac{(125)(625)}{25}\) 的值等于：

English（选项同上方）

The value of \(\log_5\dfrac{(125)(625)}{25}\) is equal to:

由于 \(125=5^3\)、\(625=5^4\) 且 \(25=5^2\)， \[ \begin{aligned} &\log_5\frac{(125)(625)}{25}\\ &=\log_5\left(5^{3+4-2}\right)\\ &=\log_5(5^5)=5 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 D。

由相似三角形可知，在距底边高度 \(x\) 处，三角形的宽度为 \(b(1-\tfrac{x}{h})\)，这就是内接矩形的底边。由于矩形的高是底边的一半，其底边长为 \(2x\)。因此， \[ 2x=b\left(1-\frac{x}{h}\right)\text{。}\]两边乘以 \(h\) 并求解，得到 \(x(2h+b)=bh\)，所以 \(x=\dfrac{bh}{2h+b}\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

By similarity, the width of the triangle at height \(x\) above its base is \(b(1-\tfrac{x}{h}).\) This is the base of the inscribed rectangle. Since the rectangle’s altitude is half its base, its base is \(2x.\) Hence \[ 2x=b\left(1-\frac{x}{h}\right). \] Multiplying by \(h\) and solving gives \(x(2h+b)=bh,\) so \(x=\dfrac{bh}{2h+b}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

在一个等边三角形内部任取一点，从该点分别向三边作垂线。这三条垂线段的长度之和：

English（选项同上方）

A point is selected at random inside an equilateral triangle. From this point perpendiculars are dropped to each side. The sum of these perpendiculars is:

设等边三角形的边长为 \(s\)，高为 \(h\)，三个垂直距离为 \(d_1,d_2,d_3\)。用内部点将大三角形分成三个小三角形，得到 \[ \frac12s(d_1+d_2+d_3)=\frac12sh\text{。}\]因此 \(d_1+d_2+d_3=h\)，与所选点的位置无关。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Let the equilateral triangle have side length \(s\) and altitude \(h,\) and let the three perpendicular distances be \(d_1,d_2,d_3.\) Splitting the triangle at the interior point gives \[ \frac12s(d_1+d_2+d_3)=\frac12sh. \] Therefore \(d_1+d_2+d_3=h,\) independent of the selected point.

Thus, the correct answer is C.

一个三角形有一条固定底边 \(AB\)，长为 \(2\) 英寸。从 \(A\) 到边 \(BC\) 的中线长为 \(1\dfrac12\) 英寸，并可从 \(A\) 向任意方向引出。顶点 \(C\) 的轨迹为：

English（选项同上方）

A triangle has a fixed base \(AB\) that is \(2\) inches long. The median from \(A\) to side \(BC\) is \(1\dfrac12\) inches long and can have any position emanating from \(A.\) The locus of the vertex \(C\) of the triangle is:

将 \(A\) 置于原点，并把 \(B\)、\(C\) 以及 \(BC\) 的中点 \(M\) 看作向量。由于 \(AM=\tfrac32\)，点 \(M\) 在以 \(A\) 为圆心、半径为 \(\tfrac32\) 的圆上运动。中点关系给出 \[ C=2M-B\text{。}\]因此，\(C\) 的轨迹是先将该圆放大 \(2\) 倍，再平移 \(-B\) 所得的图形，即以 \(-B\) 为圆心、半径为 \(3\) 的圆。

因为 \(AB=2\)，点 \(-B\) 位于射线 \(BA\) 上，且距 \(B\) 为 \(4\) 英寸。因此，正确答案是 D。

English

Put \(A\) at the origin and regard \(B,\) \(C,\) and the midpoint \(M\) of \(BC\) as vectors. Since \(AM=\tfrac32,\) the point \(M\) moves on a circle of radius \(\tfrac32\) centered at \(A.\) The midpoint relation gives \[ C=2M-B. \] Thus the locus of \(C\) is the image of that circle under a dilation by \(2\) followed by translation by \(-B.\) It is a circle of radius \(3\) centered at \(-B.\)

Because \(AB=2,\) the point \(-B\) is \(4\) inches from \(B\) along the ray \(BA.\) Hence the correct answer is D.

上午 \(11{:}45\)，一艘私掠船发现一艘商船位于下风方向 \(10\) 英里处。私掠船借着顺风以每小时 \(11\) 英里的速度追赶，而商船逃跑时只能达到每小时 \(8\) 英里。追逐两小时后，私掠船的上桅帆被风刮走；此后私掠船每航行 \(17\) 英里时，商船航行 \(15\) 英里。私掠船将在何时追上商船？

English（选项同上方）

A privateer discovers a merchantman \(10\) miles to leeward at \(11{:}45\) a.m. and with a good breeze bears down upon her at \(11\) mph, while the merchantman can only make \(8\) mph in her attempt to escape. After a two hour chase, the top sail of the privateer is carried away: she can now make only \(17\) miles while the merchantman makes \(15.\) The privateer will overtake the merchantman at:

最初两小时内，私掠船追近的英里数为 \[ 2(11-8)=6\text{，}\]所以下午 \(1{:}45\) 时，两船距离从 \(10\) 英里缩短为 \(4\) 英里。

上桅帆被刮走后，两船速度之比为 \(17:15\)。由于商船仍以每小时 \(8\) 英里航行，私掠船的新速度为每小时 \(8\cdot\tfrac{17}{15}=\tfrac{136}{15}\) 英里。两船接近的相对速度为每小时 \(\tfrac{136}{15}-8=\tfrac{16}{15}\) 英里，所以追完剩余距离所需的小时数为 \[ \frac{4}{\frac{16}{15}}=\frac{15}{4}=3\frac34\text{。}\]在下午 \(1{:}45\) 后再过 \(3\) 小时 \(45\) 分钟，即为下午 \(5{:}30\)。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

During the first two hours, the number of miles the privateer gains is \[ 2(11-8)=6, \] reducing the gap from \(10\) miles to \(4\) miles at \(1{:}45\) p.m.

After the damage, the ships’ speeds are in the ratio \(17:15.\) Since the merchantman still travels at \(8\) mph, the privateer’s new speed is \(8\cdot\tfrac{17}{15}=\tfrac{136}{15}\) mph. The closing speed is therefore \(\tfrac{136}{15}-8=\tfrac{16}{15}\) mph, so the number of hours needed to close the remaining gap is \[ \frac{4}{\frac{16}{15}}=\frac{15}{4}=3\frac34. \] Adding \(3\) hours \(45\) minutes to \(1{:}45\) p.m. gives \(5{:}30\) p.m.

Thus, the correct answer is E.
