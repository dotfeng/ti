1单选

求 \(\dfrac{11!-10!}{9!}\) 的值。

English（选项同上方）

What is the value of \(\dfrac{11!-10!}{9!}?\)

可以从分子中提取一个 \(9!\) 再化简。\[\begin{aligned} \dfrac{9!(11 \cdot 10 - 10)}{9!} &=11 \cdot 10 - 10 \\&= 110 - 10 \\&= 100 \end{aligned}\text{。}\] 所以正确答案是 B。

English

We can factor a \(9!\) out of the numerator and simplify. \[\begin{aligned} \dfrac{9!(11 \cdot 10 - 10)}{9!} &=11 \cdot 10 - 10 \\&= 110 - 10 \\&= 100. \end{aligned}\] Thus, the correct answer is B .

2单选

求使下式成立的 \(x\) 值：\[10^{x} \cdot 100^{2x}=1000^{5}\text{？}\]

English（选项同上方）

For what value of \(x\) does \[10^{x} \cdot 100^{2x}=1000^{5}?\]

把所有底数写成 \(10\) 的幂：\[\small 10^x\cdot100^{2x}=10^x\cdot(10^2)^{2x}=10^{5x}\text{，}\] 同时 \[1000^5=(10^3)^5=10^{15}\text{。}\]

因此 \(5x=15\)，所以 \(x=3\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Rewrite all bases as powers of \(10\): \[\small 10^x\cdot100^{2x}=10^x\cdot(10^2)^{2x}=10^{5x},\] and \[1000^5=(10^3)^5=10^{15}.\]

Therefore \(5x=15\), so \(x=3\).

Thus, the correct answer is C.

3单选

Ben 每花一美元买贝果，David 就少花 \(25\) 美分。Ben 比 David 多付 \(\$12.50\)。他们两人在贝果店一共花了多少钱？

English（选项同上方）

For every dollar Ben spent on bagels, David spent \(25\) cents less. Ben paid \(\$12.50\) more than David. How much did they spend in the bagel store together?

Ben 每花一美元，David 花 \(75\) 美分，所以两人的差额是 \(25\) 美分。总差额为 \(\$12.50\)，也就是 Ben 花费的四分之一。

Ben 花了 \(12.50\div0.25=50\) 美元，David 花了 \(37.50\) 美元。两人共花 \(87.50\) 美元。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

For each dollar Ben spent, David spent \(75\) cents, so the difference was \(25\) cents. The total difference \(\$12.50\) is therefore one quarter of Ben’s spending.

Ben spent \(12.50\div0.25=50\) dollars, and David spent \(37.50\) dollars. Together they spent \(87.50\) dollars.

Thus, the correct answer is C.

4单选

对所有实数 \(x\) 和满足 \(y \neq 0\) 的实数 \(y\)，余数函数定义为 \[\operatorname{rem}(x,y)=x-y\left\lfloor\frac{x}{y}\right\rfloor\text{，}\] 其中 \(\left \lfloor \frac{x}{y} \right \rfloor\) 表示小于或等于 \(\frac{x}{y}\) 的最大整数。求 \(\text{rem} \left(\frac{3}{8}, -\frac{2}{5} \right)\) 的值。

English（选项同上方）

The remainder function can be defined for all real numbers \(x\) and \(y\) with \(y \neq 0\) by \[\operatorname{rem}(x,y)=x-y\left\lfloor\frac{x}{y}\right\rfloor,\] where \(\left \lfloor \frac{x}{y} \right \rfloor\) denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to \(\frac{x}{y}.\) What is the value of \(\text{rem} \left(\frac{3}{8}, -\frac{2}{5} \right)?\)

代入公式，得到 \[ \begin{aligned} \text{rem} \left(\dfrac{3}{8}, -\dfrac{2}{5}\right) &= \dfrac{3}{8} \\ &{}+ \dfrac{2}{5} \left \lfloor \dfrac{\frac{3}{8}}{-\frac{2}{5}}\right \rfloor \\ &= \dfrac{3}{8} \\ &{}+ \dfrac{2}{5} \left \lfloor -\dfrac{15}{16} \right \rfloor \\ &= \dfrac{3}{8} + \dfrac{2}{5} \cdot -1 \\ &= \dfrac{3}{8} - \dfrac{2}{5} \\ &= - \dfrac{1}{40} \end{aligned} \] 所以正确答案是 B。

English

Using the formula, we get \[ \begin{aligned} \text{rem} \left(\dfrac{3}{8}, -\dfrac{2}{5}\right) &= \dfrac{3}{8} \\ &{}+ \dfrac{2}{5} \left \lfloor \dfrac{\frac{3}{8}}{-\frac{2}{5}}\right \rfloor \\ &= \dfrac{3}{8} \\ &{}+ \dfrac{2}{5} \left \lfloor -\dfrac{15}{16} \right \rfloor \\ &= \dfrac{3}{8} + \dfrac{2}{5} \cdot -1 \\ &= \dfrac{3}{8} - \dfrac{2}{5} \\ &= - \dfrac{1}{40} \end{aligned} \] Thus, the correct answer is B .

5单选

一个长方体的整数边长之比为 \(1:3:4\)。下列哪个数可能是这个长方体的体积？

English（选项同上方）

A rectangular box has integer side lengths in the ratio \(1:3:4.\) Which of the following could be the volume of the box?

设最短边长为 \(s\)，则另外两边为 \(3s\) 和 \(4s\)。

体积为 \[ s \cdot 3s \cdot 4s = 12s^3\text{。} \] 测试 \(s\) 的取值可知，当 \(s = 2\) 时，\(12s^3 = 96\)，这是一个选项。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Let \(s\) be the side length of the smallest side. Then the other two sides are \(3s\) and \(4s.\)

The volume is therefore \[ s \cdot 3s \cdot 4s = 12s^3. \] Testing out values of \(s,\) we see that if \(s = 2,\) then \(12s^3 = 96,\) which is an answer choice.

Thus, the correct answer is D .

6单选

Ximena 把整数 \(1\) 到 \(30\) 各列出一次。Emilio 抄写 Ximena 的数，但把每次出现的数字 \(2\) 都替换成数字 \(1\)。Ximena 把她的数相加，Emilio 把他的数相加。Ximena 的和比 Emilio 的和大多少？

English（选项同上方）

Ximena lists the whole numbers \(1\) through \(30\) once. Emilio copies Ximena’s numbers, replacing each occurrence of the digit \(2\) by the digit \(1.\) Ximena adds her numbers and Emilio adds his numbers. How much larger is Ximena’s sum than Emilio’s?

个位数字 \(2\) 被改成 \(1\) 时，数少 \(1\)。这发生在 \(2,12,22\)，总共少 \(3\)。

十位数字 \(2\) 被改成 \(1\) 时，数少 \(10\)。这发生在 \(20,21,\ldots,29\)，总共少 \(100\)。

因此 Emilio 的和比 Ximena 的和少 \(103\)，所以 Ximena 的和大 \(103\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

When Emilio changes a units digit \(2\) to \(1\), his copied number is smaller by \(1\). This happens in \(2,12,22\), for a loss of \(3\).

When he changes a tens digit \(2\) to \(1\), his copied number is smaller by \(10\). This happens for \(20,21,\ldots,29\), for a loss of \(100\).

Thus Emilio’s sum is \(103\) less than Ximena’s sum, so Ximena’s sum is \(103\) larger.

Thus, the correct answer is D.

7单选

\(7\) 个数据值 \[60, 100, x, 40, 50, 200, 90\] 的平均数、中位数和众数都等于 \(x\)。求 \(x\) 的值。

English（选项同上方）

The mean, median, and mode of the \(7\) data values \[60, 100, x, 40, 50, 200, 90\] are all equal to \(x.\) What is the value of \(x?\)

七个数的和为 \(540+x\)。如果平均数等于 \(x\)，则 \[\frac{540+x}{7}=x\text{，}\] 所以 \(540=6x\)，\(x=90\)。

当 \(x=90\) 时，排序后的数据为 \(40,50,60,90,90,100,200\)。其中位数是 \(90\)，众数也是 \(90\)，所以符合条件。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The sum of the seven data values is \(540+x\). If the mean equals \(x\), then \[\frac{540+x}{7}=x,\] so \(540=6x\) and \(x=90\).

With \(x=90\), the ordered data set is \(40,50,60,90,90,100,200\). Its median is \(90\), and its mode is also \(90\), so the value works.

Thus, the correct answer is D.

8单选

Trickster Rabbit 同意每次 Foolish Fox 经过 Rabbit 家旁边的桥时，都把 Fox 的钱翻倍，但 Fox 每次过桥后必须向 Rabbit 支付 \(40\) 枚硬币的过路费。付款在翻倍之后进行。Fox 起初很高兴，但他发现自己过桥三次后钱全没了。Fox 一开始有多少枚硬币？

English（选项同上方）

Trickster Rabbit agrees with Foolish Fox to double Fox’s money every time Fox crosses the bridge by Rabbit’s house, as long as Fox pays \(40\) coins in toll to Rabbit after each crossing. The payment is made after the doubling. Fox is excited about his good fortune until he discovers that all his money is gone after crossing the bridge three times. How many coins did Fox have at the beginning?

最后 Fox 有 \(0\) 枚硬币，所以第三次付过路费之前有 \(40\) 枚。

因此第三次翻倍前有 \(40 \div 2 = 20\) 枚；第二次付过路费之前有 \(20 + 40 = 60\) 枚。

第二次翻倍前有 \(60 \div 2 = 30\) 枚；第一次付过路费之前有 \(30 + 40 = 70\) 枚。

最后，第一次翻倍前 Fox 有 \(70 \div 2 = 35\) 枚硬币。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

We know that Fox has \(0\) coins at the end. Then before paying the final toll, Fox had \(40\) coins.

Then he had \(40 \div 2 = 20\) coins before the doubling. Then before paying the toll for the second crossing, he had \(20 + 40 = 60\) coins.

Before the doubling on the second crossing, he had \(60 \div 2 = 30\) coins. On the first crossing before the toll, Fox had \(30 + 40 = 70\) coins.

Finally, before the first doubling, Fox had \(70 \div 2 = 35\) coins.

Thus, the correct answer is C .

9单选

一个由 \(2016\) 枚硬币组成的三角形阵列，第一行有 \(1\) 枚硬币，第二行有 \(2\) 枚，第三行有 \(3\) 枚，依此类推，第 \(N\) 行有 \(N\) 枚。求 \(N\) 的各位数字之和。

English（选项同上方）

A triangular array of \(2016\) coins has \(1\) coin in the first row, \(2\) coins in the second row, \(3\) coins in the third row, and so on up to \(N\) coins in the \(N\)th row. What is the sum of the digits of \(N?\)

回忆前 \(N\) 个整数之和为 \(\dfrac{N(N + 1)}{2}\)。

我们要找 \(N\)，使得 \[ \dfrac{N(N + 1)}{2} = 2016\text{。} \] 交叉相乘并化简，得到 \[ N^2 + N - 4032 = 0\text{。} \] 因式分解得 \[ (N - 63)(N + 64) = 0\text{。} \] 取正值，\(N = 63\)。各位数字和为 \(9\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Recall that the sum of the first \(N\) numbers is \(\dfrac{N(N + 1)}{2}.\)

We want to find \(N\) such that \[ \dfrac{N(N + 1)}{2} = 2016. \] Cross-multiplying and simplifying gives us \[ N^2 + N - 4032 = 0. \] Factoring gives us \[ (N - 63)(N + 64) = 0. \] We want the positive value so \(N = 63.\) Adding together the digits gives us \(9.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D .

10单选

一块地毯由如图所示的三种不同颜色组成。三种不同颜色区域的面积构成一个等差数列。最内层长方形宽为一英尺，外面的两个区域在四周各宽 \(1\) 英尺。最内层长方形的长度是多少英尺？

English（选项同上方）

A rug is made with three different colors as shown. The areas of the three differently colored regions form an arithmetic progression. The inner rectangle is one foot wide, and each of the two shaded regions is \(1\) foot wide on all four sides. What is the length in feet of the inner rectangle?

设内层长方形长度为 \(l\)，则内层面积为 \(l\)。

中间区域面积为 \[ (l + 2) \cdot 3 - l = 2l + 6\text{。}\] 外层区域面积为 \[ (l + 4) \cdot 5 - (l + 2) \cdot 3 = 2l + 14\text{。}\]

这 \(3\) 个面积构成等差数列，所以 \[\begin{aligned} l + 2l + 14 &= 2(2l + 6) \\ 3l + 14 &= 4l + 12 \\ l &= 2\end{aligned}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Let \(l\) be the length of the inner rectangle. Then the area of the inner rectangle is \(l.\)

The area of the middle region is going to be \[ (l + 2) \cdot 3 - l = 2l + 6. \] The area of the outer region is \[ (l + 4) \cdot 5 - (l + 2) \cdot 3 = 2l + 14. \]

We know that these \(3\) values form an arithmetic sequence. That means that \[\begin{aligned} l + 2l + 14 &= 2(2l + 6) \\ 3l + 14 &= 4l + 12 \\ l &= 2\end{aligned}\]

Thus, the correct answer is B .

11单选

给定的 \(8\times5\) 长方形中，阴影区域的面积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

What is the area of the shaded region of the given \(8\times5\) rectangle?

可将阴影区域分成 \(4\) 个底为 \(1\) 的三角形。

四个三角形的底都是 \(1\)。其中两个的高为 \(8\div2=4\)，另外两个的高为 \(5\div2=\dfrac52\)。

面积总和为 \[ \begin{aligned} &2 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} \left(1 \cdot \dfrac{5}{2}\right) \\ &\quad {}+ 2 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} \left(1 \cdot 4\right) = 6\dfrac{1}{2} \end{aligned} \]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

We can split the region into \(4\) triangles with bases of \(1.\)

All four triangles have base \(1.\) Two have height \(8\div2=4,\) and the other two have height \(5\div2=\dfrac52.\)

The sum of the areas of the triangles is \[ \begin{aligned} &2 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} \left(1 \cdot \dfrac{5}{2}\right) \\ &\quad {}+ 2 \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} \left(1 \cdot 4\right) = 6\dfrac{1}{2} \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is D .

12单选

从 \(1\) 到 \(2016\) 之间随机选取三个不同的整数。下列哪一句正确描述了这三个整数乘积为奇数的概率 \(p\)？

English（选项同上方）

Three distinct integers are selected at random between \(1\) and \(2016,\) inclusive. Which of the following is a correct statement about the probability \(p\) that the product of the three integers is odd?

乘积为奇数，当且仅当选出的三个整数全是奇数。从 \(1\) 到 \(2016\) 中有 \(1008\) 个奇数和 \(1008\) 个偶数。

因为是不放回抽取， \[p=\frac{1008}{2016}\cdot\frac{1007}{2015}\cdot\frac{1006}{2014}\text{。}\] 第一个因子为 \(\frac12\)，后两个因子都略小于 \(\frac12\)，所以 \(p<\frac18\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The product is odd exactly when all three selected integers are odd. There are \(1008\) odd and \(1008\) even integers from \(1\) to \(2016\).

Because the integers are selected without replacement, \[p=\frac{1008}{2016}\cdot\frac{1007}{2015}\cdot\frac{1006}{2014}.\] The first factor is \(\frac12\), and each of the next two factors is slightly less than \(\frac12\). Therefore \(p<\frac18\).

Thus, the correct answer is A.

13单选

五位朋友坐在电影院一排 \(5\) 个座位中，从左到右编号为 \(1\) 到 \(5\)。（“左”和“右”是从坐在座位上的人的视角看。）

电影期间 Ada 去大厅买爆米花。她回来时发现 Bea 向右移动了两个座位，Ceci 向左移动了一个座位，Dee 和 Edie 交换了座位，并且给 Ada 留下了一个端点座位。Ada 起身前坐在哪个座位？

English（选项同上方）

Five friends sat in a movie theater in a row containing \(5\) seats, numbered \(1\) to \(5\) from left to right. (The directions “left” and “right” are from the point of view of the people as they sit in the seats.)

During the movie Ada went to the lobby to get some popcorn. When she returned, she found that Bea had moved two seats to the right, Ceci had moved one seat to the left, and Dee and Edie had switched seats, leaving an end seat for Ada. In which seat had Ada been sitting before she got up?

移动前后，被占用座位编号之和都是 \(1+2+3+4+5=15\)。Dee 和 Edie 只是交换座位，所以两人的合位移为 \(0\)。Bea 的位移为 \(+2\)，Ceci 的位移为 \(-1\)。因此 Ada 的位移必须为 \(-1\)，才能使总位移为 \(0\)。

Ada 回到了一个端点座位。因为她向左移动了一个座位，所以她必定是从 \(2\) 号座位移到 \(1\) 号座位。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The sum of the occupied seat numbers is \(1+2+3+4+5=15\) both before and after the moves. Dee and Edie merely switch seats, so their combined displacement is \(0.\) Bea’s displacement is \(+2,\) and Ceci’s is \(-1.\) Therefore Ada’s displacement must be \(-1\) so that the total displacement is \(0.\)

Ada returns to an end seat. Since she moves one seat left, she must move from seat \(2\) to seat \(1.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B .

14单选

有多少种方法把 \(2016\) 写成若干个二和若干个三的和，且不考虑顺序？例如，\(1008\cdot 2 + 0\cdot 3\) 和 \(402\cdot 2 + 404\cdot 3\) 是其中两种。

English（选项同上方）

How many ways are there to write \(2016\) as the sum of twos and threes, ignoring order? (For example, \(1008\cdot 2 + 0\cdot 3\) and \(402\cdot 2 + 404\cdot 3\) are two such ways.)

题目可以写成方程 \(2x + 3y = 2016\)，其中 \(x\) 是二的个数，\(y\) 是三的个数。

目标就是数出有多少个 \(3\) 的倍数，能从 \(2016\) 中减去后仍得到偶数。

这对应于从 \((1008, 0)\) 到 \((0, 672)\) 的所有有序对，其中 \(y\) 每次增加 \(2\)。

这样就有 \[\dfrac{672}{2} + 1 = 337\] 组 \(y\) 和 \(x\) 的解。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The problem can be rewritten as an equation \(2x + 3y = 2016,\) where \(x\) is the number of twos and \(y\) is the number of threes.

The goal is to find the number of multiples of \(3\) that can be subtracted from \(2016\) to result in an even number.

This can be achieved by the pairs of \((1008, 0)\) up to \((0, 672)\) with \(y\) being incremented by \(2.\)

This gives us \[\dfrac{672}{2} + 1 = 337\] solutions for \(y\) and \(x.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C .

15单选

如图，从一块圆形饼干面团中切出七块半径为 \(1\) 英寸的饼干。相邻饼干相切，除中心饼干外，其他饼干都与面团边缘相切。剩下的边角料被重新塑造成另一块同样厚度的饼干。边角料饼干的半径是多少英寸？

English（选项同上方）

Seven cookies of radius \(1\) inch are cut from a circle of cookie dough, as shown. Neighboring cookies are tangent, and all except the center cookie are tangent to the edge of the dough. The leftover scrap is reshaped to form another cookie of the same thickness. What is the radius in inches of the scrap cookie?

圆形面团半径为 \(3\) 英寸，因为从中心到边缘经过一个饼干直径再加一个饼干半径。

面团面积为 \(3^2\pi = 9\pi\)，七块饼干的总面积为 \(7 \cdot 1^2\pi = 7\pi\)。

剩余边角料的面积为 \(9\pi - 7\pi = 2\pi\)，所以它的半径为 \(\sqrt{2}\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The circle of cookie dough has a radius of \(3\) inches since it is the same as the diameter plus the radius of a cookie.

The area of the cookie dough is \(3^2\pi = 9\pi,\) and the cookies have an area of \(7 \cdot 1^2\pi = 7\pi.\)

The area of the leftover scrap is therefore \(9\pi - 7\pi = 2\pi.\) This means that its radius is \(\sqrt{2}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A .

16单选

顶点为 \(A(0, 2)\)，\(B(-3, 2)\)，和 \(C(-3, 0)\) 的三角形先关于 \(x\) 轴反射，得到的像 \(\triangle A'B'C'\) 再绕原点逆时针旋转 \(90^{\circ}\)，得到 \(\triangle A''B''C''\)。下列哪个变换能把 \(\triangle A''B''C''\) 变回 \(\triangle ABC\)？

English（选项同上方）

A triangle with vertices \(A(0, 2),\) \(B(-3, 2),\) and \(C(-3, 0)\) is reflected about the \(x\)-axis, then the image \(\triangle A'B'C'\) is rotated counterclockwise about the origin by \(90^{\circ}\) to produce \(\triangle A''B''C''.\) Which of the following transformations will return \(\triangle A''B''C''\) to \(\triangle ABC?\)

为判断如何逆转这些变换，可以跟踪一个任意点。

设该点为 \((x, y)\)。关于 \(x\) 轴反射后，它变为 \((x, -y)\)。

再逆时针旋转后得到 \((y, x)\)。要把它变回 \((x, y)\)，唯一的方法是关于 \(y = x\) 反射。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

To figure out how to reverse the transformations, we can analyze a single point and see what happens to it.

Let \((x, y)\) be the point. After being reflected about the \(x\)-axis, the point would go to \((x, -y).\)

Rotating this counterclockwise would put it at \((y, x).\) The only transformation that puts this back at \((x, y)\) is reflection about \(y = x.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D .

设 \(N\) 是 \(5\) 的正倍数。将一个红球和 \(N\) 个绿球随机排成一行。令 \(P(N)\) 为至少有 \(\frac{3}{5}\) 的绿球在红球同一侧的概率。注意 \(P(5)=1\)，且当 \(N\) 变大时 \(P(N)\) 趋近于 \(\frac{4}{5}\)。使 \(P(N) < \dfrac{321}{400}\) 的最小 \(N\) 的各位数字之和是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Let \(N\) be a positive multiple of \(5.\) One red ball and \(N\) green balls are arranged in a line in random order. Let \(P(N)\) be the probability that at least \(\frac{3}{5}\) of the green balls are on the same side of the red ball. Observe that \(P(5)=1\) and that \(P(N)\) approaches \(\frac{4}{5}\) as \(N\) grows large. What is the sum of the digits of the least value of \(N\) such that \(P(N) < \dfrac{321}{400}?\)

先排好 \(N\) 个绿球，再把红球放入 \(N+1\) 个空隙之一。若红球左侧有 \(k\) 个绿球，则右侧有 \(N-k\) 个。

至少有 \(\frac35N\) 个绿球在一侧，等价于 \(k\le\frac25N\) 或 \(k\ge\frac35N\)。因此不满足条件的空隙为 \[\frac25N+1,\frac25N+2,\ldots,\frac35N-1\text{，}\] 共 \(\frac15N-1\) 个。

所以 \[ \begin{aligned} P(N) &= 1-\frac{\frac{N}{5}-1}{N+1} \\ &= \frac{4N+10}{5N+5} \end{aligned}\text{。}\] 解不等式 \(\frac{4N+10}{5N+5}<\frac{321}{400}\) 得 \(N>479\)。最小的 \(5\) 的正倍数是 \(480\)，其各位数字之和为 \(12\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Think of first arranging the \(N\) green balls, then placing the red ball in one of the \(N+1\) gaps. If \(k\) green balls are to the left of the red ball, then \(N-k\) are to its right.

At least \(\frac35N\) green balls are on one side exactly when \(k\le\frac25N\) or \(k\ge\frac35N\). Thus the bad gaps are \[\frac25N+1,\frac25N+2,\ldots,\frac35N-1,\] a total of \(\frac15N-1\) gaps.

Therefore \[ \begin{aligned} P(N) &= 1-\frac{\frac{N}{5}-1}{N+1} \\ &= \frac{4N+10}{5N+5}. \end{aligned} \] Solving \(\frac{4N+10}{5N+5}<\frac{321}{400}\) gives \(N>479\). The least positive multiple of \(5\) is \(480\), whose digit sum is \(12\).

Thus, the correct answer is A.

一个立方体的每个顶点要标上从 \(1\) 到 \(8\) 的一个整数，每个整数用一次，使得每个面的四个顶点上的数之和都相同。通过立方体旋转可以互相得到的标法视为相同。有多少种不同标法？

English（选项同上方）

Each vertex of a cube is to be labeled with an integer \(1\) through \(8,\) with each integer being used once, in such a way that the sum of the four numbers on the vertices of a face is the same for each face. Arrangements that can be obtained from each other through rotations of the cube are considered to be the same. How many different arrangements are possible?

相对两个面包含全部八个标号，总和为 \(36\)，所以每个面的和必须为 \(18\)。

把标号 \(1\) 放在一个顶点，设与它相邻的三个标号为 \(a,b,c\)。与这三个顶点隔面相邻的另外三个顶点于是被迫为 \[ \begin{gathered} 17-a-b, \\ \quad 17-a-c, \\ \quad 17-b-c \end{gathered}\text{，}\] 而对顶点为 \(a+b+c-16\)。

假设三个相邻标号按递增顺序排列。把 \(2,3,\ldots,8\) 中的三元组代入上面四个被迫的表达式，恰好得到 \[ \begin{array}{c|c} \{a,b,c\} & \text{四个被迫确定的标号} \\ \hline \{4,6,8\} & \{7,5,3,2\} \\ \{4,7,8\} & \{6,5,2,3\} \\ \{6,7,8\} & \{4,3,2,5\} \end{array} \] 对每个相邻标号集合，绕过该顶点的对角线作 \(120^\circ\) 旋转，会把顶点周围的六种排列分成两组各三种。因此每个集合给出两种不能通过旋转互相得到的标法，共有 \(3\cdot2=6\) 种标法。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Opposite faces together use all eight labels, whose sum is \(36\), so every face must have sum \(18\).

Put label \(1\) at one vertex, and let the three adjacent labels be \(a,b,c\). The three vertices adjacent to those across faces are then forced to be \[ \begin{gathered} 17-a-b, \\ \quad 17-a-c, \\ \quad 17-b-c, \end{gathered} \] and the opposite vertex is \(a+b+c-16\).

Assume the three neighbor labels are listed in increasing order. Substituting triples from \(2,3,\ldots,8\) into the four forced expressions above gives exactly \[ \begin{array}{c|c} \{a,b,c\} & \text{four forced labels} \\ \hline \{4,6,8\} & \{7,5,3,2\} \\ \{4,7,8\} & \{6,5,2,3\} \\ \{6,7,8\} & \{4,3,2,5\} \end{array} \] For each neighbor set, the six orders around the vertex fall into two groups of three under the \(120^\circ\) rotations about the diagonal through that vertex. Thus each set gives two non-rotationally equivalent arrangements, for \(3\cdot2=6\) arrangements in all.

Thus, the correct answer is C.

在长方形 \(ABCD\) 中，\(AB=6\)，\(BC=3\)。点 \(E\) 在 \(B\) 与 \(C\) 之间，点 \(F\) 在 \(E\) 与 \(C\) 之间，且 \(BE=EF=FC\)。线段 \(\overline{AE}\) 和 \(\overline{AF}\) 分别与 \(\overline{BD}\) 相交于 \(P\) 和 \(Q\)。

比值 \(BP:PQ:QD\) 可写成 \(r:s:t\)，其中 \(r\)，\(s\) 和 \(t\) 的最大公因数为 \(1\)。求 \(r+s+t\)。

English（选项同上方）

In rectangle \(ABCD,\) \(AB=6\) and \(BC=3.\) Point \(E\) between \(B\) and \(C,\) and point \(F\) between \(E\) and \(C\) are such that \(BE=EF=FC.\) Segments \(\overline{AE}\) and \(\overline{AF}\) intersect \(\overline{BD}\) at \(P\) and \(Q,\) respectively.

The ratio \(BP:PQ:QD\) can be written as \(r:s:t\) where the greatest common factor of \(r,\) \(s,\) and \(t\) is \(1.\) What is \(r+s+t?\)

因为 \(BC=3\)，且 \(BE=EF=FC\)，所以 \(BE=1\)、\(BF=2\)。又 \(AD=3\)。

由 \(\triangle APD\sim\triangle EPB\)， \[\frac{BP}{BD}=\frac{BE}{AD+BE}=\frac14\text{。}\] 由 \(\triangle AQD\sim\triangle FQB\)， \[\frac{BQ}{BD}=\frac{BF}{AD+BF}=\frac25\text{。}\]

因此 \(BP=\frac14BD\)，\(PQ=\left(\frac25-\frac14\right)BD=\frac3{20}BD\)，且 \(QD=\frac35BD\)。于是 \[ \begin{aligned} BP:PQ:QD &= \frac14:\frac3{20}:\frac35 \\ &= 5:3:12\text{。} \end{aligned} \] 和为 \(5+3+12=20\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Since \(BC=3\) and \(BE=EF=FC\), we have \(BE=1\) and \(BF=2\). Also \(AD=3\).

From \(\triangle APD\sim\triangle EPB\), \[\frac{BP}{BD}=\frac{BE}{AD+BE}=\frac14.\] From \(\triangle AQD\sim\triangle FQB\), \[\frac{BQ}{BD}=\frac{BF}{AD+BF}=\frac25.\]

Therefore \(BP=\frac14BD\), \(PQ=\left(\frac25-\frac14\right)BD=\frac3{20}BD\), and \(QD=\frac35BD\). Thus \[ \begin{aligned} BP:PQ:QD &= \frac14:\frac3{20}:\frac35 \\ &= 5:3:12. \end{aligned} \] The sum is \(5+3+12=20\).

Thus, the correct answer is E.

对某个特定的 \(N\)，展开 \((a+b+c+d+1)^N\) 并合并同类项后，所得表达式中恰好有 \(1001\) 项同时包含四个变量 \(a\)、\(b\)、\(c\) 和 \(d\)，且每个变量的指数都为正。求 \(N\)。

English（选项同上方）

For some particular value of \(N,\) when \((a+b+c+d+1)^N\) is expanded and like terms are combined, the resulting expression contains exactly \(1001\) terms that include all four variables \(a,\) \(b,\) \(c,\) and \(d,\) each to some positive power. What is \(N?\)

同时包含四个变量的项形如 \(a^vb^wc^xd^y\)，其中 \(v,w,x,y\ge1\)，此外还要乘以 \(1\) 的某个幂。若这个幂是 \(z\)，则 \[v+w+x+y+z=N\text{。}\]

从 \(v,w,x,y\) 各减去 \(1\) 后，得到 \(5\) 个非负整数，其和为 \(N-4\)。因此这样的项数为 \[\binom{N}{4}\text{。}\]

由于 \(\binom{14}{4}=1001\)，所以 \(N=14\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

A term that includes all four variables has form \(a^vb^wc^xd^y\), with \(v,w,x,y\ge1\), multiplied by some power of \(1\). If that power is \(z\), then \[v+w+x+y+z=N.\]

After subtracting \(1\) from each of \(v,w,x,y\), this becomes a nonnegative stars-and-bars count with \(5\) variables summing to \(N-4\). The number of such terms is \[\binom{N}{4}.\]

Since \(\binom{14}{4}=1001\), we get \(N=14\).

Thus, the correct answer is B.

圆心为 \(P\)，\(Q\) 和 \(R\)、半径分别为 \(1\)，\(2\) 和 \(3\) 的三个圆位于直线 \(l\) 的同侧，并分别在 \(P'\)，\(Q'\) 和 \(R'\) 处与 \(l\) 相切，其中 \(Q'\) 在 \(P'\) 和 \(R'\) 之间。圆心为 \(Q\) 的圆与另外两个圆都外切。求 \(\triangle PQR\) 的面积。

English（选项同上方）

Circles with centers \(P,\) \(Q\) and \(R,\) having radii \(1,\) \(2\) and \(3,\) respectively, lie on the same side of line \(l\) and are tangent to \(l\) at \(P',\) \(Q'\) and \(R',\) respectively, with \(Q'\) between \(P'\) and \(R'.\) The circle with center \(Q\) is externally tangent to each of the other two circles. What is the area of \(\triangle PQR?\)

把这条公切线取作 \(x\) 轴，并令 \(P=(0,1)\)。因为 \(PQ=1+2=3\)，而两个圆心的高度相差 \(1\)，所以 \(P\) 到 \(Q\) 的水平距离为 \[\sqrt{3^2-1^2}=2\sqrt2\text{。}\]同理，\(QR=2+3=5\)，这两个圆心的高度也相差 \(1\)，因此它们的水平距离为 \(2\sqrt6\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Put the tangent line on the \(x\)-axis and take \(P=(0,1).\) Because \(PQ=1+2=3\) and the centers differ in height by \(1,\) the horizontal distance from \(P\) to \(Q\) is \[\sqrt{3^2-1^2}=2\sqrt2.\] Similarly, \(QR=2+3=5\) and its centers also differ in height by \(1,\) so their horizontal distance is \(2\sqrt6.\)

Thus we may use \[ \begin{aligned} P&=(0,1),\qquad Q=(2\sqrt2,2), \\ R&=(2\sqrt2+2\sqrt6,3). \end{aligned} \] The coordinate-area formula gives \[ \begin{aligned} [PQR] &=\frac12\left|4\sqrt2-(2\sqrt2+2\sqrt6)\right| \\ &=\sqrt6-\sqrt2. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is D .

For some positive integer \(n,\) the number \(110n^3\) has \(110\) positive integer divisors, including \(1\) and the number \(110n^3.\) How many positive integer divisors does the number \(81n^4\) have?

English
