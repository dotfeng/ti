1单选

把两个 \(3\) 位数加法算式中的六个方框分别填入数字 \(4\)、\(5\)、\(6\)、\(7\)、\(8\)、\(9\)，每个数字用一次。能够得到的最小的两个 \(3\) 位数之和是多少？

English（选项同上方）

What is the smallest sum of two \(3\)-digit numbers that can be obtained by placing each of the six digits \(4,\) \(5,\) \(6,\) \(7,\) \(8,\) \(9\) into one of the six boxes of a sum of two \(3\)-digit numbers?

要让和最小，两个最小的数字应放在百位，接下来的两个数字放在十位，最大的两个数字放在个位。

例如 \(468 + 579 = 1047\)，这种分配方式都会得到同样的和。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

To make the sum small, the two smallest digits go in the hundreds places, the next two in the tens places, and the two largest in the units places.

One such arrangement is \(468 + 579 = 1047\), and every arrangement of this type gives the same sum.

Thus, the correct answer is C .

2单选

把 \(0.12345\) 中的哪一个数字改成 \(9\)，会得到最大的数？

English（选项同上方）

Which digit of \(0.12345,\) when changed to \(9,\) gives the largest number?

改变十分位上的数字，比改变右边任何一位的影响都大。十分位上的数字是 \(1\)。

把它改成九后得到 \(0.92345\)，这是可能得到的最大数。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Changing a digit in the tenths place changes the number more than changing any digit farther to the right. The digit in the tenths place is \(1\).

Changing it gives \(0.92345\), which is the largest possible result.

Thus, the correct answer is A .

3单选

正方形中有多少比例的面积被涂色？

English（选项同上方）

What fraction of the square is shaded?

从一个顶点到对角顶点的对角线把正方形分成两个面积相等的部分。对角线一侧的每个涂色部分，都是另一侧某个等面积未涂色部分的镜像。

因此涂色面积和未涂色面积相等，正好有 \(\dfrac12\) 的正方形被涂色。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The diagonal from one corner to the opposite corner splits the square into two equal halves. Every shaded piece on one side of the diagonal is the mirror image of an equal unshaded piece on the other side.

So the shaded and unshaded areas are equal, and exactly \(\dfrac12\) of the square is shaded.

Thus, the correct answer is E .

4单选

下列哪一个数不可能是某个整数平方的个位数字？

English（选项同上方）

Which of the following could not be the units digit [one’s digit] of the square of a whole number?

平方数的个位数字由原数的个位数字决定。\(0\) 到 \(9\) 平方后的个位数字依次是 \(0\)、\(1\)、\(4\)、\(9\)、\(6\)、\(5\)、\(6\)、\(9\)、\(4\)、\(1\)。

所以平方数只能以 \(0\)、\(1\)、\(4\)、\(5\)、\(6\) 或 \(9\) 结尾；不可能以 \(2\)、\(3\)、\(7\) 或 \(8\) 结尾。选项中只有 \(8\) 不可能。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The units digit of a square is determined by the units digit of the number squared. Squaring \(0\) through \(9\) gives units digits \(0,\) \(1,\) \(4,\) \(9,\) \(6,\) \(5,\) \(6,\) \(9,\) \(4,\) \(1\).

So a square can only end in \(0,\) \(1,\) \(4,\) \(5,\) \(6,\) or \(9\); it can never end in \(2,\) \(3,\) \(7,\) or \(8\). Among the choices, only \(8\) is impossible.

Thus, the correct answer is E .

Which of the following is closest to the product \((0.48017)(0.48017)(0.48017)?\)

选项中最接近 \(0.125\) 的是 \(0.110\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Since \(0.48017\) is close to \(\dfrac12\), the product is close to

\[\left(\dfrac12\right)^3 = \dfrac18 = 0.125.\]

The choice closest to \(0.125\) is \(0.110\).

Thus, the correct answer is B .

6单选

这五个数中哪一个最大？

English（选项同上方）

Which of these five numbers is the largest?

选项 A、B 和 E 都非常接近 \(13579\)。选项 C 把 \(13579\) 乘以一个很小的数，会变得更小。

选项 D 是除以 \(\dfrac{1}{2468}\)，也就是乘以 \(2468\)。它比 \(13579\) 大几千倍，所以最大。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Choices A, B, and E are all very close to \(13579\), and choice C multiplies \(13579\) by a tiny number, making it much smaller.

Choice D divides by \(\dfrac{1}{2468}\), which is the same as multiplying by \(2468\). This makes it thousands of times larger than \(13579\), so it is the largest.

Thus, the correct answer is D .

7单选

从集合 \(\{ -3, -2, -1, 4, 5 \}\) 中选出三个不同的数相乘，可能得到的最大乘积是

English（选项同上方）

When three different numbers from the set \(\{ -3, -2, -1, 4, 5 \}\) are multiplied, the largest possible product is

三个数的乘积要为正，要么三个全是正数，要么恰好两个是负数。这里只有两个正数，所以必须用两个负数和一个正数。

为了使乘积最大，取两个最小的负数和最大的正数：\((-3)(-2)(5) = 30\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

For the product of three numbers to be positive, either all three are positive or exactly two are negative. There are only two positive numbers, so we must use two negatives and one positive.

To maximize, take the two most negative numbers and the largest positive: \((-3)(-2)(5) = 30\).

Thus, the correct answer is C .

10单选

在这张月历中，把某个字母盖住的日期与 \(C\) 盖住的日期相加。如果这个和等于 \(A\) 与 \(B\) 盖住的日期之和，那么这个字母是

English（选项同上方）

On this monthly calendar, the date behind one of the letters is added to the date behind \(C\). If this sum equals the sum of the dates behind \(A\) and \(B\), then the letter is

向右移动一列日期增加 \(1\)，向下移动一行日期增加 \(7\)。设 \(C\) 盖住的日期为 \(n\)，则右边一列的 \(A\) 盖住 \(n+1\)。\(P\) 在 \(C\) 正下方两行，所以 \(P = n+14\)；\(B\) 在底行且位于 \(P\) 左边一列，所以它盖住 \(n+13\)。

设所求日期为 \(d\)，则 \(d + n = (n+1) + (n+13)\)，所以 \(d = n + 14\)。这个日期被 \(P\) 盖住。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Moving one column right adds \(1\) to the date, and moving one row down adds \(7\). Let \(C\) have date \(n\). Then \(A\), one column to the right, is \(n+1\). The letter \(P\) sits two rows directly below \(C\), so \(P = n+14\), and \(B\), one column left of \(P\) in the bottom row, is \(n+13\).

We need a letter whose date \(d\) satisfies \(d + n = (n+1) + (n+13)\), so \(d = n + 14\). That date is behind \(P\).

Thus, the correct answer is A .

这个立方体各面上的数字是连续的整数。三对相对面上的两个数字之和都相等。这个立方体六个面上数字的总和是

English（选项同上方）

The numbers on the faces of this cube are consecutive whole numbers. The sums of the two numbers on each of the three pairs of opposite faces are equal. The sum of the six numbers on this cube is

六个连续整数包含 \(11\)、\(14\)、\(15\)，所以其中五个数是 \(11\)、\(12\)、\(13\)、\(14\)、\(15\)，第六个数是 \(10\) 或 \(16\)。

如果第六个数是 \(10\)，为了让相对面和相等，配对应为 \((10,15)\)、\((11,14)\)、\((12,13)\)，这会使 \(11\) 和 \(14\) 相对。但图中标有 \(11\)、\(14\)、\(15\) 的三个面在同一个顶点相遇，所以它们任意两个都不相对。因此第六个数是 \(16\)，相对面可配成 \((11,16)\)、\((12,15)\)、\((13,14)\)，每对和为 \(27\)。

六个面的总和是 \(3 \cdot 27 = 81\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The six consecutive numbers include \(11,\) \(14,\) \(15\), so five of the faces are \(11,\) \(12,\) \(13,\) \(14,\) \(15\) and the sixth is \(10\) or \(16\).

If the sixth were \(10\), equal opposite sums would force the pairs \((10,15),\) \((11,14),\) \((12,13)\), making \(11\) and \(14\) opposite. But the figure shows \(11,\) \(14,\) \(15\) meeting at one corner, so no two of them are opposite. Hence the sixth number is \(16\), with pairs \((11,16),\) \((12,15),\) \((13,14)\), each summing to \(27\).

The total is \(3 \cdot 27 = 81\).

Thus, the correct answer is E .

二十四个 \(4\) 位整数可以由数字 \(2\)、\(4\)、\(5\) 和 \(7\) 各使用一次组成。把它们从小到大排列，第 \(17\) 个数是

There are twenty-four \(4\)-digit whole numbers that use each of the four digits \(2,\) \(4,\) \(5,\) and \(7\) exactly once. Listed in numerical order from smallest to largest, the number in the \(17\)th position in the list is

13单选

有人建议新的信件邮资为：第一盎司 \(30\) 美分，每增加一盎司或不足一盎司的部分加收 \(22\) 美分。一封重 \(4.5\) 盎司的信件邮资是

English（选项同上方）

One proposal for new postage rates for a letter was \(30\)¢ for the first ounce and \(22\)¢ for each additional ounce (or fraction of an ounce). The postage for a letter weighing \(4.5\) ounces was

第一盎司花 \(30\) 美分。剩下的 \(3.5\) 盎司要按 \(4\) 个额外盎司收费，因为不足一盎司也按一整盎司算。

额外邮资是 \(4 \cdot 22\) 美分 \(= 88\) 美分，总邮资是 \(30\) 美分 \(+ 88\) 美分 \(= 118\) 美分 \(= \$1.18\)。

English

The first ounce costs \(30\)¢. The remaining \(3.5\) ounces are charged as \(4\) additional ounces, since any fraction rounds up to a full ounce.

That is \(4 \cdot 22\)¢ \(= 88\)¢, so the total is \(30\)¢ \(+ 88\)¢ \(= 118\)¢ \(= \$1.18.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C .

16单选

下列表达式的值是多少？

\[ \begin{aligned} &1990 - 1980 + 1970 - 1960 \\ &\quad {}+ \cdots - 20 + 10 \end{aligned} \]

English（选项同上方）

What is the value of the following expression?

\[ \begin{aligned} &1990 - 1980 + 1970 - 1960 \\ &\quad {}+ \cdots - 20 + 10 \end{aligned} \]

把式子分组为 \((1990 - 1980)\) \(+ (1970 - 1960)\) \(+ \cdots\) \(+ (30 - 20)\) \(+ 10\)。每个括号里的差都是 \(10\)。

各组的第一项为 \(1990, 1970, \ldots, 30\)，共 \(99\) 项，所以共有 \(99\) 组，贡献 \(990\)；再加最后的 \(+10\)，总数为 \(1000\)。

The first elements \(1990, 1970, \ldots, 30\) number \(99\), so there are \(99\) pairs, giving \(990\), plus the leftover \(+10\) at the end for a total of \(1000\).

Thus, the correct answer is D .

17单选

一条笔直的混凝土人行道宽 \(3\) 英尺，长 \(60\) 英尺，厚 \(3\) 英寸。如果混凝土必须按整立方码订购，承包商至少需要订购多少立方码混凝土？

English（选项同上方）

A straight concrete sidewalk is to be \(3\) feet wide, \(60\) feet long, and \(3\) inches thick. How many cubic yards of concrete must a contractor order for the sidewalk if concrete must be ordered in a whole number of cubic yards?

厚度 \(3\) 英寸 \(= \dfrac14\) 英尺，所以体积是 \(3 \cdot 60 \cdot \dfrac14 = 45\) 立方英尺。

因为 \(1\) 立方码 \(= 27\) 立方英尺，所以体积是 \(\dfrac{45}{27} = 1\dfrac23\) 立方码。按整立方码订购时必须向上取整，需订购 \(2\) 立方码。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The thickness is \(3\) inches \(= \dfrac14\) foot, so the volume is \(3 \cdot 60 \cdot \dfrac14 = 45\) cubic feet.

Since \(1\) cubic yard \(= 27\) cubic feet, this is \(\dfrac{45}{27} = 1\dfrac23\) cubic yards. Rounding up to a whole number, the contractor must order \(2\) cubic yards.

Thus, the correct answer is A .

18单选

一个长方体的每个顶点都被一刀切掉，切面穿过该顶点相邻的三条棱，在八个顶点各切去一个小三角形部分。新立体有多少条棱？

English（选项同上方）

Every corner of a rectangular prism is cut off by a straight slice through the three edges meeting at that corner, removing a small triangular piece at each of the eight corners. How many edges does the new figure have?

长方体原来有 \(12\) 条棱。切掉顶点只会把这些棱截短，不会把它们完全去掉。

共有 \(8\) 个顶点，每个顶点切口会产生一个小三角形面，带来 \(3\) 条新棱。所以新增 \(8 \cdot 3 = 24\) 条棱，总数是 \(12 + 24 = 36\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

A rectangular prism starts with \(12\) edges, and cutting corners only shortens them without removing any.

Each of the \(8\) corner cuts creates a small triangular face with \(3\) new edges, adding \(8 \cdot 3 = 24\) edges. The total is \(12 + 24 = 36\).

Thus, the correct answer is C .

19单选

一排有 \(120\) 个座位。至少要占用多少个座位，才能保证下一个入座的人必须坐在某个人旁边？

English（选项同上方）

There are \(120\) seats in a row. What is the fewest number of seats that must be occupied so the next person to be seated must sit next to someone?

要迫使下一个人坐在别人旁边，每个空座位都必须与至少一个已坐座位相邻。每个已坐座位最多覆盖它自己及左右两个相邻座位，所以至少要坐满 \(\frac{120}{3} = 40\) 个座位。

这个下界可以达到：重复采用“空、已坐、空”的排列，每三个座位只坐中间一个。这样恰好有 \(40\) 个已坐座位。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

To force the next person next to someone, every empty seat must be adjacent to an occupied one. Each occupied seat can account for at most itself and its two neighboring seats, so at least \(\frac{120}{3} = 40\) seats must be occupied.

This bound is attainable: use a repeating pattern of empty-occupied-empty, filling the middle seat of every group of three. This uses exactly \(40\) occupied seats.

Thus, the correct answer is B .

20单选

\(1{,}000\) 个家庭的年收入从 \(\$8200\) 到 \(\$98{,}000\) 不等。由于错误，最高收入在电脑中被输入为 \(\$980{,}000\)。错误数据的平均数与实际数据的平均数相差多少？

English（选项同上方）

The annual incomes of \(1{,}000\) families range from \(\$8200\) to \(\$98{,}000.\) In error, the largest income was entered on the computer as \(\$980{,}000.\) The difference between the mean of the incorrect data and the mean of the actual data is

只有一个数据被改错。错误总和比实际总和多 \(\$980{,}000 - \$98{,}000 = \$882{,}000\)。

这多出的金额分摊到 \(1000\) 个家庭上，平均数相差 \(\dfrac{\$882{,}000}{1000} = \$882\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Only one entry changed. The incorrect total exceeds the actual total by \(\$980{,}000 - \$98{,}000 = \$882{,}000.\)

Since this extra amount is spread over \(1000\) families, the means differ by \(\dfrac{\$882{,}000}{1000} = \$882.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A .

一个含 \(8\) 个数的数列以前两个给定的数开始。之后每一个新数都是前两个数的乘积。如果最后三个数是 \(16\)、\(64\)、\(1024\)，求第一个数。

\[?, \underline{\phantom{x}}, \underline{\phantom{x}}, \underline{\phantom{x}}, \underline{\phantom{x}}, 16, 64, 1024\]

English（选项同上方）

A list of \(8\) numbers is formed by beginning with two given numbers. Each new number in the list is the product of the two previous numbers. Find the first number if the last three are \(16,\) \(64,\) \(1024\):

\[?, \underline{\phantom{x}}, \underline{\phantom{x}}, \underline{\phantom{x}}, \underline{\phantom{x}}, 16, 64, 1024\]

因为每一项都是前两项的乘积，所以用某一项除以紧前一项，可以得到它前面第二项。从最后三个数 \(16\)、\(64\)、\(1024\) 倒推：

\[ \begin{gathered} 64 \div 16 = 4, \quad 16 \div 4 = 4, \\ 4 \div 4 = 1, \quad 4 \div 1 = 4, \\ 1 \div 4 = \dfrac14 \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

因此完整数列为 \(\dfrac14\)、\(4\)、\(1\)、\(4\)、\(4\)、\(16\)、\(64\)、\(1024\)，第一个数是 \(\dfrac14\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Since each term is the product of the two before it, dividing any term by the term just before it recovers the term two positions earlier. Working backwards from the last three terms \(16,\) \(64,\) \(1024\):

\[ \begin{gathered} 64 \div 16 = 4, \quad 16 \div 4 = 4, \\ 4 \div 4 = 1, \quad 4 \div 1 = 4, \\ 1 \div 4 = \dfrac14. \end{gathered} \]

These fill in the earlier terms, giving the full list \(\dfrac14,\) \(4,\) \(1,\) \(4,\) \(4,\) \(16,\) \(64,\) \(1024\). The first number is \(\dfrac14\).

Thus, the correct answer is B .

Several students are seated at a large circular table. They pass around a bag containing \(100\) pieces of candy. Each person receives the bag, takes one piece of candy, and then passes the bag to the next person. If Chris takes the first and the last piece of candy, then the number of students at the table could be

Chris 拿第一颗糖，然后袋子一圈圈传递，直到 Chris 拿到最后一颗，也就是第 \(100\) 颗。第一次和最后一次之间相隔 \(99\) 次取糖。

This means the number of students divides \(99\). Among the choices, only \(11\) divides \(99\).

图中表示一架实验飞机在一次飞行中，已飞行距离（英里）与经过时间（小时）的关系。在哪一个小时内，这架飞机的平均速度最大？

English（选项同上方）

The graph relates the distance traveled (in miles) to the time elapsed (in hours) on a trip taken by an experimental airplane. During which hour was the average speed of this airplane the largest?

任意一个小时内的平均速度等于这一个小时内飞行的距离，也就是图上对应一小时区间的竖直上升量。因此最大平均速度出现在曲线最陡的一小时。

从 \(t=1\) 到 \(t=2\)，曲线从约 \(500\) 英里升到约 \(1000\) 英里，上升约 \(500\) 英里，平均速度约 \(500\) 英里每小时。其他小时的上升量都小于约 \(350\) 英里。所以最大平均速度出现在第二个小时。

正确答案是 B。

English

The average speed during any single hour equals the distance the airplane covers in that hour, which on the graph is the vertical rise of the curve over that one-hour interval. So the largest average speed happens during the hour where the curve is steepest.

From \(t=1\) to \(t=2\) the curve climbs from about \(500\) miles to about \(1000\) miles, a rise of roughly \(500\) miles, giving an average speed near \(500\) mph. 三个三角形和一个菱形与九个圆点平衡。另外，一个三角形与一个菱形和一个圆点平衡。两个菱形需要多少个圆点来平衡？

English（选项同上方）

Three triangles and a diamond balance nine dots. Also, one triangle balances a diamond and a dot. How many dots will balance two diamonds?

设三角形、菱形和圆点的重量分别为 \(t\)、\(d\) 和 \(1\)。题意给出 \(3t + d = 9\) 和 \(t = d + 1\)。

English

Let a triangle, diamond, and dot weigh \(t\), \(d\), and \(1\). The conditions give \(3t + d = 9\) and \(t = d + 1\).

Substituting, \(3(d+1) + d = 9\), so \(4d + 3 = 9\) and \(d = \dfrac32\). Then two diamonds weigh \(2d = 3\) dots.

Thus, the correct answer is C .

在一个 \(3 \times 3\) 方格中，恰好给九个单位正方形中的两个涂色，可以形成多少种不同图案？通过翻转或旋转可以重合的图案不算不同。

English（选项同上方）

How many different patterns can be made by shading exactly two of the nine unit squares in a \(3 \times 3\) grid? Patterns that can be matched by flips and/or turns are not considered different.

先分类含有角格的图案：两个相邻角格、两个对角角格、角格与中心格、角格与相邻边中格、角格与较远边中格，共 \(5\) 种。
