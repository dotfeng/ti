将 \(x\) 同时加到分数 \(\frac{a}{b}\) 的分子和分母上，其中 \(a\ne b\)、\(b\ne0\)，所得分数变为 \(\frac{c}{d}\)。则 \(x\) 等于：

English（选项同上方）

When \(x\) is added to both the numerator and the denominator of the fraction \(\frac{a}{b},\) \(a\ne b,\) \(b\ne0,\) the value of the fraction is changed to \(\frac{c}{d}.\) Then \(x\) equals:

由 \[ \frac{a+x}{b+x}=\frac cd \] 得 \(ad+dx=bc+cx\)。因此 \(x(d-c)=bc-ad\)，所以 \[ x=\frac{bc-ad}{d-c}=\frac{ad-bc}{c-d}\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

From \[ \frac{a+x}{b+x}=\frac cd \] we obtain \(ad+dx=bc+cx.\) Hence \(x(d-c)=bc-ad,\) so \[ x=\frac{bc-ad}{d-c}=\frac{ad-bc}{c-d}. \]

Therefore, the correct answer is B.

若某商品以 \(x\) 美元售出，则按成本计算亏损 \(15\%\)。但若同一商品以 \(y\) 美元售出，则按成本计算获利 \(15\%\)。比值 \(y:x\) 为：

English（选项同上方）

If an item is sold for \(x\) dollars, there is a loss of \(15\%\) based on the cost. If, however, the same item is sold for \(y\) dollars, there is a profit of \(15\%\) based on the cost. The ratio \(y:x\) is:

若成本为 \(C\)，则 \(x=0.85C\) 且 \(y=1.15C\)。因此 \[ \begin{aligned} y:x&=1.15C:0.85C\\ &=115:85=23:17 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

If the cost is \(C,\) then \(x=0.85C\) and \(y=1.15C.\) Therefore \[ \begin{aligned} y:x&=1.15C:0.85C\\ &=115:85=23:17. \end{aligned} \]

Therefore, the correct answer is A.

5单选

若一个数 \(N\)（\(N\ne0\)）减去其倒数的四倍等于给定实常数 \(R\)，则对于该给定的 \(R\)，所有可能的 \(N\) 值之和为：

English（选项同上方）

If a number \(N,\) \(N\ne0,\) diminished by four times its reciprocal, equals a given real constant \(R,\) then, for this given \(R,\) the sum of all such possible values of \(N\) is:

将 \(N-\frac{4}{N}=R\) 乘以 \(N\)，得到 \[ N^2-RN-4=0\text{。}\]由韦达定理，两根之和为 \(R\)。因为两根之积为 \(-4\)，所以两根都非零。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Multiplying \(N-\frac{4}{N}=R\) by \(N\) gives \[ N^2-RN-4=0. \] The sum of its two roots is \(R\) by Vieta’s formulas. Both roots are nonzero because their product is \(-4.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is B.

两个同心圆之间圆环的面积为 \(12\dfrac12\pi\) 平方英寸。大圆中与小圆相切的弦长（单位：英寸）为：

English（选项同上方）

The area of the ring between two concentric circles is \(12\dfrac12\pi\) square inches. The length of a chord of the larger circle tangent to the smaller circle, in inches, is:

设两个半径为 \(R\) 与 \(r\)。由圆环面积可得 \[ \pi(R^2-r^2)=\frac{25}{2}\pi\text{。}\]过切点的半径平分该弦。若弦长为 \(L\)，则 \[ \left(\frac L2\right)^2+r^2=R^2\text{。}\]因此 \(L=2\sqrt{R^2-r^2}\)，所以 \(L=2\sqrt{\frac{25}{2}}=5\sqrt2\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Let the radii be \(R\) and \(r.\) The ring area gives \[ \pi(R^2-r^2)=\frac{25}{2}\pi. \] The radius to the tangent point bisects the chord. If its length is \(L,\) then \[ \left(\frac L2\right)^2+r^2=R^2. \] Thus \(L=2\sqrt{R^2-r^2},\) so \(L=2\sqrt{\frac{25}{2}}=5\sqrt2.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is C.

10单选

到一个圆以及该圆的两条平行切线距离均相等的点共有：

English（选项同上方）

The number of points equidistant from a circle and two parallel tangents to the circle is:

设圆心为 \(O\)，半径为 \(r\)。到两条平行切线距离相等的点必在它们的中线上，而该中线经过 \(O\)。若该点到 \(O\) 的距离为 \(t\)，则它到任一切线的距离为 \(r\)，到圆的距离为 \(\lvert t-r\rvert\)。因此 \(\lvert t-r\rvert=r\)，得到 \(t=0\) 或 \(t=2r\)。当 \(t=0\) 时有一个点，当 \(t=2r\) 时有两个点，共 \(3\) 个。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Let the circle have center \(O\) and radius \(r.\) A point equidistant from the two parallel tangents must lie on their midline, which passes through \(O.\) If its distance from \(O\) is \(t,\) its distance from either tangent is \(r,\) while its distance from the circle is \(\lvert t-r\rvert.\) Thus \(\lvert t-r\rvert=r,\) giving \(t=0\) or \(t=2r.\) There is one point with \(t=0\) and two with \(t=2r,\) for a total of \(3.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is C.

11单选

在 \(xy\) 平面中给定点 \(P(-1,-2)\) 与 \(Q(4,2)\)，取点 \(R(1,m)\) 使 \(PR+RQ\) 最小。则 \(m\) 等于：

English（选项同上方）

Given points \(P(-1,-2)\) and \(Q(4,2)\) in the \(xy\)-plane, point \(R(1,m)\) is taken so that \(PR+RQ\) is a minimum. Then \(m\) equals:

由三角不等式，\(PR+RQ\ge PQ\)，当 \(R\) 在线段 \(PQ\) 上时取等号。\(PQ\) 的斜率为 \(\frac{2-(-2)}{4-(-1)}=\frac{4}{5}\)。从 \(x=-1\) 移到 \(x=1\) 时，\(y\) 坐标增加 \((\frac{4}{5})(2)=\frac{8}{5}\)，所以 \[ m=-2+\frac85=-\frac25\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

By the triangle inequality, \(PR+RQ\ge PQ,\) with equality when \(R\) lies on segment \(PQ.\) The slope of \(PQ\) is \(\frac{2-(-2)}{4-(-1)}=\frac{4}{5}.\) Moving from \(x=-1\) to \(x=1\) raises the \(y\)-coordinate by \((\frac{4}{5})(2)=\frac{8}{5},\) so \[ m=-2+\frac85=-\frac25. \]

Therefore, the correct answer is B.

13单选

半径为 \(r\) 的圆位于半径为 \(R\) 的圆所围区域内。大圆所围面积是小圆外、大圆内区域面积的 \(\frac{a}{b}\) 倍。则 \(R:r\) 等于：

English（选项同上方）

A circle with radius \(r\) is contained within the region bounded by a circle with radius \(R.\) The area bounded by the larger circle is \(\frac{a}{b}\) times the area of the region outside the smaller circle and inside the larger circle. Then \(R:r\) equals:

面积条件为 \[ \pi R^2=\frac ab\pi(R^2-r^2)\text{。}\]因此 \(bR^2=aR^2-ar^2\)，所以 \(ar^2=(a-b)R^2\)。从而 \[ \frac Rr=\sqrt{\frac a{a-b}}\text{，}\]且 \(R:r=\sqrt a:\sqrt{a-b}\)。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

The area condition is \[ \pi R^2=\frac ab\pi(R^2-r^2). \] Thus \(bR^2=aR^2-ar^2,\) so \(ar^2=(a-b)R^2.\) Therefore \[ \frac Rr=\sqrt{\frac a{a-b}}, \] and \(R:r=\sqrt a:\sqrt{a-b}.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is B.

14单选

满足不等式 \(\dfrac{x^2-4}{x^2-1}\gt0\) 的全部 \(x\) 值为所有满足下列条件的 \(x\)：

English（选项同上方）

The complete set of \(x\)-values satisfying the inequality \(\dfrac{x^2-4}{x^2-1}\gt0\) is the set of all \(x\) such that:

将式子因式分解为 \[ \frac{(x-2)(x+2)}{(x-1)(x+1)}\text{。}\]在 \(-2\)、\(-1\)、\(1\)、\(2\) 处作符号表，可知它在 \[ (-\infty,-2)\cup(-1,1)\cup(2,\infty) \] 上为正。端点 \(\pm2\) 使式子为零，而 \(\pm1\) 处无定义。

English

15单选

在圆心为 \(O\)、半径为 \(r\) 的圆中，作弦 \(AB\)，其长度为 \(r\) 个单位。从 \(O\) 向 \(AB\) 作垂线，交 \(AB\) 于 \(M\)。从 \(M\) 向 \(OA\) 作垂线，交 \(OA\) 于 \(D\)。用 \(r\) 表示，三角形 \(MDA\) 的面积为：

English（选项同上方）

In a circle with center at \(O\) and radius \(r,\) chord \(AB\) is drawn with length equal to \(r\) units. From \(O\) a perpendicular to \(AB\) meets \(AB\) at \(M.\) From \(M\) a perpendicular to \(OA\) meets \(OA\) at \(D.\) In terms of \(r,\) the area of triangle \(MDA,\) in appropriate square units, is:

三角形 \(OAB\) 为等边三角形。因为 \(OM\perp AB\)，所以 \(M\) 是 \(AB\) 的中点，从而 \(AM=\frac{r}{2}\) 且 \(\angle OAM=60^\circ\)。在直角三角形 \(AMD\) 中，斜边为 \(AM=\frac{r}{2}\)，所以 \[ AD=\frac r4,\qquad MD=\frac{r\sqrt3}{4}\text{。}\]因此 \[ \begin{aligned} [MDA]&=\frac12(AD)(MD)\\ &=\frac{r^2\sqrt3}{32} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

Triangle \(OAB\) is equilateral. Since \(OM\perp AB,\) \(M\) is the midpoint of \(AB,\) so \(AM=\frac{r}{2}\) and \(\angle OAM=60^\circ.\) In right triangle \(AMD,\) the hypotenuse is \(AM=\frac{r}{2},\) giving \[ AD=\frac r4,\qquad MD=\frac{r\sqrt3}{4}. \] Hence \[ \begin{aligned} [MDA]&=\frac12(AD)(MD)\\ &=\frac{r^2\sqrt3}{32}. \end{aligned} \]

Therefore, the correct answer is D.

16单选

用二项式定理展开 \((a-b)^n\)，其中 \(n\ge2\)、\(ab\ne0\)。若令 \(a=kb\)，其中 \(k\) 为正整数，展开式的第二项与第三项之和为零，则 \(n\) 等于：

English（选项同上方）

When \((a-b)^n,\) \(n\ge2,\) \(ab\ne0,\) is expanded by the binomial theorem, it is found that, when \(a=kb,\) where \(k\) is a positive integer, the sum of the second and third terms is zero. Then \(n\) equals:

第二项与第三项之和为 \[ -na^{n-1}b+\frac{n(n-1)}2a^{n-2}b^2\text{。}\]代入 \(a=kb\)，再除以非零量 \(na^{n-2}b\)，得 \[ -k+\frac{n-1}{2}=0\text{。}\]因此 \(n=2k+1\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The sum of the second and third terms is \[ -na^{n-1}b+\frac{n(n-1)}2a^{n-2}b^2. \] Substituting \(a=kb\) and dividing by the nonzero quantity \(na^{n-2}b\) gives \[ -k+\frac{n-1}{2}=0. \] Therefore \(n=2k+1.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is E.

下列两个图形

\[ (x-y+2)(3x+y-4)=0 \]

与

\[ (x+y-2)(2x-5y+7)=0 \]

的公共点个数为：

English（选项同上方）

The number of points common to the graphs of

\[ (x-y+2)(3x+y-4)=0 \]

and

\[ (x+y-2)(2x-5y+7)=0 \]

is:

第一个图形由直线 \(x-y+2=0\)、\(3x+y-4=0\) 组成，第二个图形由直线 \(x+y-2=0\)、\(2x-5y+7=0\) 组成。第一组中的每条直线都与第二组中的每条直线相交。解这四组方程，得到四个互异的点：\[ \begin{gathered} (0,2),\quad(-1,1),\\ (1,1),\quad\left(\frac{13}{17},\frac{29}{17}\right) \end{gathered}\text{。}\]因而共有 \(4\) 个公共点。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The first graph is the pair \(x-y+2=0,\) and \(3x+y-4=0,\) and the second is \(x+y-2=0,\) and \(2x-5y+7=0.\) Each line in the first pair meets each line in the second pair. Solving the four pairings gives four distinct points: \[ \begin{gathered} (0,2),\quad(-1,1),\\ (1,1),\quad\left(\frac{13}{17},\frac{29}{17}\right). \end{gathered} \] Thus there are \(4\) common points.

Therefore, the correct answer is B.

19单选

English

Therefore, the correct answer is B.

令 \(P\) 等于 \(3{,}659{,}893{,}456{,}789{,}325{,}678\) 与 \(342{,}973{,}489{,}379{,}256\) 的乘积。则 \(P\) 的位数为：

English（选项同上方）

Let \(P\) equal the product of \(3{,}659{,}893{,}456{,}789{,}325{,}678\) and \(342{,}973{,}489{,}379{,}256.\) The number of digits in \(P\) is:

两个因数满足 \[ \begin{aligned} 3.6\cdot10^{18}&\lt A\lt3.7\cdot10^{18},\\ 3.4\cdot10^{14}&\lt B\lt3.5\cdot10^{14} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]因此 \[ 12.24\cdot10^{32}\lt AB\lt12.95\cdot10^{32}\text{，}\]从而 \(10^{33}\lt P\lt10^{34}\)。所以 \(P\) 有 \(34\) 位。
