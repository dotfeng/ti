若数字 \(a\) 和 \(b\) 满足 \[ \begin{array}{r} 2a\\[-2pt] {}\times b3\\ \hline 69\\ 92\phantom0\\ \hline 989 \end{array} \] 则 \(a+b=\)

English（选项同上方）

If \(a\) and \(b\) are digits for which \[ \begin{array}{r} 2a\\[-2pt] {}\times b3\\ \hline 69\\ 92\phantom0\\ \hline 989 \end{array} \] then \(a+b=\)

由第一个部分积可得 \(3(20+a)=69\)，所以 \(a=3\)。由第二个部分积可得 \(23b=92\)，所以 \(b=4\)。因此 \(a+b=7\)，正确答案是 C。

English

The first partial product says \(3(20+a)=69,\) so \(a=3.\) The second says \(23b=92,\) so \(b=4.\) Thus \(a+b=7,\) and the correct answer is C.

2单选

图示十边形的相邻边均成直角。它的周长是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The adjacent sides of the decagon shown meet at right angles. What is its perimeter?

向右水平边的长度之和为 \(12\)，所以所有水平边的长度之和为 \(24\)。图形的总高度为 \(8+2=10\)，所以所有竖直边的长度之和为 \(20\)。因此周长为 \(24+20=44\)，正确答案是 D。

English

The total of the rightward horizontal sides is \(12,\) so all horizontal sides total \(24.\) The full height is \(8+2=10,\) so the vertical sides total \(20.\) Therefore the perimeter is \(24+20=44,\) and the correct answer is D.

English（选项同上方）

If \(x,\) \(y,\) and \(z\) are real numbers such that \[ \begin{aligned} (x-3)^2+(y-4)^2\\ {}+(z-5)^2&=0, \end{aligned} \] then \(x+y+z=\)

三个平方项都非负，因此每一项都必须为 \(0\)。于是 \(x=3\)、\(y=4\)、\(z=5\)，且 \(x+y+z=12\)。正确答案是 D。

English

All three squares are nonnegative, so each must be \(0.\) Hence \(x=3,\) \(y=4,\) \(z=5,\) and \(x+y+z=12.\) The correct answer is D.

If \(a\) is \(50\%\) larger than \(c,\) and \(b\) is \(25\%\) larger than \(c,\) then \(a\) is what percent larger than \(b?\)

有 \(a=1.5c\) 且 \(b=1.25c\)。因此 \(\frac{a}{b}=\frac{1.5}{1.25}=1.2\)，所以 \(a\) 比 \(b\) 大 \(20\%\)。正确答案是 A。

5单选

如图，一个周长为 \(176\) 的长方形被分成五个全等的小长方形。每个小长方形的周长是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A rectangle with perimeter \(176\) is divided into five congruent rectangles as shown in the diagram. What is the perimeter of one of the five congruent rectangles?

设小长方形的两条边长为 \(x\) 和 \(y\)。由共同的宽度可得 \(3x=2y\)，而大长方形的长和宽分别为 \(3x\) 与 \(x+y\)。因此其周长满足 \(2(4x+y)=176\)。由于 \(y=\frac{3x}{2}\)，可得 \(11x=176\)，所以 \(x=16\) 且 \(y=24\)。一个小长方形的周长为 \(2(16+24)=80\)，故正确答案是 C。

English

Let a small rectangle have sides \(x\) and \(y.\) The common width gives \(3x=2y,\) while the large rectangle has dimensions \(3x\) by \(x+y.\) Its perimeter is \(2(4x+y)=176.\) Since \(y=\frac{3x}{2},\) this is \(11x=176,\) so \(x=16\) and \(y=24.\) One small perimeter is \(2(16+24)=80,\) making C correct.

考虑数列 \[ 1,-2,3,-4,5,-6,\ldots\text{。}\]它的第 \(n\) 项为 \((-1)^{n+1}n\)。这个数列前 \(200\) 项的平均数是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Consider the sequence \[ 1,-2,3,-4,5,-6,\ldots, \] whose \(n\)th term is \((-1)^{n+1}n.\) What is the average of the first \(200\) terms of the sequence?

每一对 \((2k-1)-2k\) 的和都是 \(-1\)。因此 \(100\) 对的总和为 \(-100\)，这 \(200\) 项的平均数为 \(-\frac{100}{200}=-0.5\)。正确答案是 B。

English

Each pair \((2k-1)-2k\) has sum \(-1.\) The \(100\) pairs therefore total \(-100,\) and their \(200\)-term average is \(-\frac{100}{200}=-0.5.\) The correct answer is B.

The sum of seven integers is \(-1.\) What is the maximum number of the seven integers that can be larger than \(13?\)

如果七个整数都大于 \(13\)，它们的和至少为 \(98\)。但可以有六个整数大于 \(13\)：取六个 \(14\)，再取一个 \(-85\)。因此最多有 \(6\) 个，正确答案是 D。

English

If all seven integers exceeded \(13,\) their sum would be at least \(98.\) Six can exceed \(13\): take six copies of \(14\) and a seventh integer of \(-85.\) Thus the maximum is \(6,\) and the correct answer is D.

8单选

Mientka 出版公司为其畅销书 《沃尔特在哪里？》 制定了如下价格：\[ C(n)= \begin{cases} 12n, & 1\le n\le24,\\ 11n, & 25\le n\le48,\\ 10n, & 49\le n \end{cases}\text{，}\]其中 \(n\) 是订购的书本数，\(C(n)\) 是订购 \(n\) 本书所需的美元数。注意，买 \(25\) 本书比买 \(24\) 本书便宜。对于多少个 \(n\) 的取值，购买多于 \(n\) 本书会比恰好购买 \(n\) 本书便宜？

English（选项同上方）

Mientka Publishing Company prices its best seller Where’s Walter? as follows: \[ C(n)= \begin{cases} 12n, & 1\le n\le24,\\ 11n, & 25\le n\le48,\\ 10n, & 49\le n, \end{cases} \] where \(n\) is the number of books ordered, and \(C(n)\) is the cost in dollars of \(n\) books. Notice that \(25\) books cost less than \(24\) books. For how many values of \(n\) is it cheaper to buy more than \(n\) books than to buy exactly \(n\) books?

在第一个分界点，\(C(25)=275\) 小于 \(C(23)=276\) 和 \(C(24)=288\)，所以 \(n=23\) 和 \(n=24\) 符合条件。在第二个分界点，当 \(n=45\)、\(n=46\)、\(n=47\)、\(n=48\) 时，\(C(49)=490\) 小于 \(C(n)=11n\)。每一段的价格都随 \(n\) 增加，因此没有其他取值符合条件。共有 \(2+4=6\) 个，故正确答案是 D。

English

At the first break, \(C(25)=275\) is below \(C(23)=276\) and \(C(24)=288,\) giving \(n=23\) and \(n=24.\) At the second, \(C(49)=490\) is below \(C(n)=11n\) for \(n=45,\) \(n=46,\) \(n=47,\) \(n=48.\) No other \(n\) works because each piece increases. There are \(2+4=6,\) so D is correct.

如图，\(ABCD\) 是一个 \(2\) 乘 \(2\) 的正方形，\(E\) 是 \(\overline{AD}\) 的中点，且 \(F\) 在 \(\overline{BE}\) 上。若 \(\overline{CF}\) 垂直于 \(\overline{BE}\)，则四边形 \(CDEF\) 的面积为

English（选项同上方）

In the figure, \(ABCD\) is a \(2\) by \(2\) square, \(E\) is the midpoint of \(\overline{AD},\) and \(F\) is on \(\overline{BE}.\) If \(\overline{CF}\) is perpendicular to \(\overline{BE},\) then the area of quadrilateral \(CDEF\) is

按第一个提示建立坐标系后，直线 \(BE\) 的方程为 \(y=2x\)，过 \(C\) 的垂线方程为 \(y=-\frac{(x-2)}{2}\)。两直线交于 \(F=(\frac{2}{5},\frac{4}{5})\)。正方形的面积为 \(4\)，而三角形 \(AEF\) 和 \(BCF\) 的面积分别为 \(\frac{3}{5}\) 和 \(\frac{4}{5}\)。因此 \([CDEF]=4-\frac{3}{5}\) \({}-\frac{4}{5}=\frac{11}{5}\)，故正确答案是 C。

Hence \([CDEF]=4-\frac{3}{5}\) \({}-\frac{4}{5}=\frac{11}{5},\) so C is correct.

10单选

两枚六面骰子的每个面出现的概率都相同。不过，其中一枚骰子把 \(4\) 改成了 \(3\)，另一枚骰子把 \(3\) 改成了 \(4\)。掷这两枚骰子时，点数之和为奇数的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Two six-sided dice are fair in the sense that each face is equally likely to turn up. However, one of the dice has the \(4\) replaced by \(3\) and the other die has the \(3\) replaced by \(4.\) When these dice are rolled, what is the probability that the sum is odd?

第一枚改过的骰子有 \(4\) 个奇数面和 \(2\) 个偶数面；第二枚有 \(2\) 个奇数面和 \(4\) 个偶数面。因此所求概率为 \((\frac{4}{6})(\frac{4}{6})+(\frac{2}{6})(\frac{2}{6})\) \(=\frac{20}{36}=\frac{5}{9}\)。正确答案是 D。

English

The first modified die has \(4\) odd faces and \(2\) even faces; the second has \(2\) odd and \(4\) even. Thus the probability is \((\frac{4}{6})(\frac{4}{6})+(\frac{2}{6})(\frac{2}{6})\) \(=\frac{20}{36}=\frac{5}{9}.\) The correct answer is D.

11单选

在本赛季的第六、七、八、九场篮球比赛中，一名球员分别得到 \(23\)、\(14\)、\(11\) 和 \(20\) 分。打完九场后的场均得分高于前五场的场均得分。若打完十场后的场均得分大于 \(18\)，那么她在第十场最少可能得到多少分？

English（选项同上方）

In the sixth, seventh, eighth, and ninth basketball games of the season, a player scored \(23,\) \(14,\) \(11,\) and \(20\) points, respectively. Her points-per-game average was higher after nine games than it was after the first five games. If her average after ten games was greater than \(18,\) what is the least number of points she could have scored in the tenth game?

第 \(6\) 至第 \(9\) 场的总得分为 \(68\)。由条件 \(\frac{S+68}{9}>\frac{S}{5}\) 可得 \(S<85\)，所以整数 \(S\) 的最大值为 \(84\)。十场后的场均得分大于 \(18\)，意味着总得分至少为 \(181\)，因此第十场至少得到 \(181-(84+68)=29\) 分。这个分数可以达到，所以正确答案是 D。

English

Games \(6\)–\(9\) total \(68.\) The condition \(\frac{S+68}{9}>\frac{S}{5}\) gives \(S<85,\) so the greatest integral \(S\) is \(84.\) A ten-game average above \(18\) requires a total at least \(181,\) hence the tenth score is at least \(181-(84+68)=29.\) This is attainable, so D is correct.

If \(m\) and \(b\) are real numbers and \(mb\gt0,\) then the line whose equation is \(y=mx+b\) cannot contain the point

若 \((1997,0)\) 在直线上，则 \(0=1997m+b\)，所以 \(b=-1997m\)，从而 \(mb=-1997m^2\lt0\)，产生矛盾。对于其余每个点，都可以选取适当的同号 \(m,b\) 使直线经过该点。因此正确答案是 E。

13单选

有多少个两位正整数 \(N\)，满足 \(N\) 与将 \(N\) 的各位数字倒序后所得的数之和是完全平方数？

English（选项同上方）

How many two-digit positive integers \(N\) have the property that the sum of \(N\) and the number obtained by reversing the order of the digits of \(N\) is a perfect square?

这个和为 \(11(x+y)\)。由于 \(1\le x+y\le18\)，它只有在 \(x+y=11\) 时才是完全平方数，此时等于 \(121\)。十位数字 \(x\) 可以是 \(2,3,\ldots,9\) 中的任意一个，而 \(y=11-x\)。共有 \(8\) 个这样的整数，所以正确答案是 E。

English

The sum is \(11(x+y).\) Since \(1\le x+y\le18,\) this is square only when \(x+y=11,\) giving \(121.\) The tens digit \(x\) can be any of \(2,3,\ldots,9,\) with \(y=11-x.\) There are \(8\) integers, so E is correct.

14单选

一群鹅的数量逐年增加，并且第 \(n+2\) 年与第 \(n\) 年的数量之差正比于第 \(n+1\) 年的数量。若 \(1994\) 年、\(1995\) 年和 \(1997\) 年的鹅群数量分别为 \(39\)、\(60\) 和 \(123\)，则 \(1996\) 年的数量为

English（选项同上方）

The number of geese in a flock increases so that the difference between the populations in year \(n+2\) and year \(n\) is directly proportional to the population in year \(n+1.\) If the populations in the years \(1994,\) \(1995,\) and \(1997\) were \(39,\) \(60,\) and \(123,\) respectively, then the population in \(1996\) was

根据规律可得 \(x-39=60k\) 和 \(123-60=kx\)。消去 \(k\)，得到 \(x(x-39)=3780\)，即 \((x-84)(x+45)=0\)。数量为正，因此 \(x=84\)，正确答案是 B。

English

The rule gives \(x-39=60k\) and \(123-60=kx.\) Eliminating \(k\) yields \(x(x-39)=3780,\) or \((x-84)(x+45)=0.\) The population is positive, so \(x=84,\) and B is correct.

15单选

三角形 \(ABC\) 的中线 \(\overline{BD}\) 与 \(\overline{CE}\) 互相垂直，且 \(BD=8\)、\(CE=12\)。三角形 \(ABC\) 的面积为

English（选项同上方）

Medians \(\overline{BD}\) and \(\overline{CE}\) of triangle \(ABC\) are perpendicular, \(BD=8,\) and \(CE=12.\) The area of triangle \(ABC\) is

四边形 \(BCDE\) 的两条对角线 \(BD=8\) 和 \(CE=12\) 互相垂直，所以其面积为 \(\frac12(8)(12)=48\)。因为 \(D,E\) 都是中点，三角形 \(ADE\) 的面积是三角形 \(ABC\) 面积的四分之一，因此四边形 \(BCDE\) 的面积是它的四分之三。于是 \([ABC]=\frac{48\cdot4}{3}=64\)，所以正确答案是 D。

English

Quadrilateral \(BCDE\) has perpendicular diagonals \(BD=8\) and \(CE=12,\) so its area is \(\frac12(8)(12)=48.\) Since \(D,E\) are midpoints, triangle \(ADE\) has one fourth the area of \(ABC,\) making \(BCDE\) three fourths of it. Thus \([ABC]=\frac{48\cdot4}{3}=64,\) so D is correct.

16单选

数组 \[ \begin{bmatrix} 4&9&2\\ 8&1&6\\ 3&5&7 \end{bmatrix} \] 的三个行和与三个列和都相同。至少要改变多少个元素，才能使这六个和互不相同？

English（选项同上方）

The three row sums and the three column sums of the array \[ \begin{bmatrix} 4&9&2\\ 8&1&6\\ 3&5&7 \end{bmatrix} \] are the same. What is the least number of entries that must be altered to make all six sums different from one another?

如果至多改变三个元素，那么要么六条行列中至少有两条没有改变，要么某个被改变的元素是它所在行与所在列中唯一被改变的元素。前一种情况下，两条未改变的行或列的和仍然相等；后一种情况下，该元素使其所在行与列的和发生同样的变化，所以这两个和仍然相等。改变四个元素即可：分别将 \(4\)、\(1\)、\(2\)、\(6\) 改为 \(5\)、\(3\)、\(7\)、\(9\)。新的三个行和为 \(21\)、\(20\)、\(15\)，三个列和为 \(16\)、\(17\)、\(23\)。因此最小值为 \(4\)，正确答案是 D。

English

With at most three alterations, either two of the six lines are unchanged, or some altered entry is the only alteration in both its row and its column. In the first case those two sums remain equal; in the second, that entry changes its row sum and column sum by the same amount, so those two sums remain equal. Four suffice: replace \(4,\) \(1,\) \(2,\) \(6\) by \(5,\) \(3,\) \(7,\) \(9,\) respectively. The resulting row sums are \(21,\) \(20,\) \(15\) and column sums are \(16,\) \(17,\) \(23.\) Hence the minimum is \(4,\) and D is correct.

17单选

直线 \(x=k\) 分别与函数 \(y=\log_5x\) 和 \(y=\log_5(x+4)\) 的图像相交。两个交点之间的距离为 \(0.5\)。已知 \(k=a+\sqrt b\)，其中 \(a\) 和 \(b\) 都是整数，求 \(a+b\)。

English（选项同上方）

A line \(x=k\) intersects the graph of \(y=\log_5x\) and the graph of \(y=\log_5(x+4).\) The distance between the points of intersection is \(0.5.\) Given that \(k=a+\sqrt b,\) where \(a\) and \(b\) are integers, what is \(a+b?\)

两个交点的竖直距离满足 \[ \begin{aligned} \log_5(k+4)-\log_5k &=\frac12,\\ \frac{k+4}{k}&=\sqrt5 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]因此 \(\frac{k+4}{k}=\sqrt5\)，所以 \(k=\frac{4}{\sqrt5-1}=1+\sqrt5\)。于是 \(a+b=1+5=6\)，正确答案是 A。

English

The vertical distance is \[ \begin{aligned} \log_5(k+4)-\log_5k &=\frac12,\\ \frac{k+4}{k}&=\sqrt5. \end{aligned} \] Thus \(\frac{k+4}{k}=\sqrt5,\) so \(k=\frac{4}{\sqrt5-1}=1+\sqrt5.\) Therefore \(a+b=1+5=6,\) and A is correct.

18单选

一列整数的众数为 \(32\)，平均数为 \(22\)，其中最小的数为 \(10\)。中位数 \(m\) 是这列数中的一员。若将 \(m\) 替换为 \(m+10\)，新数列的平均数和中位数分别为 \(24\) 和 \(m+10\)。若改为将 \(m\) 替换为 \(m-8\)，新数列的中位数为 \(m-4\)。求 \(m\)。

English（选项同上方）

A list of integers has mode \(32\) and mean \(22.\) The smallest number in the list is \(10.\) The median \(m\) of the list is a member of the list. If the list member \(m\) were replaced by \(m+10,\) the mean and median of the new list would be \(24\) and \(m+10,\) respectively. If \(m\) were instead replaced by \(m-8,\) the median of the new list would be \(m-4.\) What is \(m?\)

这列数共有 \(\frac{10}{2}=5\) 项。将它们写成 \(10\le a\le m\le b\le c\)。把 \(m\) 替换为 \(m+10\) 后，这个新值成为中位数，所以 \(b\ge m+10\) 且 \(c\ge m+10\)；又因为众数是 \(32\)，必有 \(b=c=32\)。原数列的总和为 \(110\)，所以 \(a+m=36\)。把 \(m\) 替换为 \(m-8\) 后，中位数为 \(a=m-4\)，所以 \(2m-4=36\)，解得 \(m=20\)。因此正确答案是 E。

English

The list has \(\frac{10}{2}=5\) entries. Write them \(10\le a\le m\le b\le c.\) Replacing \(m\) by \(m+10\) makes that value the median, so \(b\ge m+10\) and \(c\ge m+10;\) because the mode is \(32,\) we must have \(b=c=32.\) The original total is \(110,\) giving \(a+m=36.\) Replacing \(m\) by \(m-8\) makes the median \(a=m-4,\) so \(2m-4=36\) and \(m=20.\) Thus E is correct.

19单选

如图，圆心为 \(O\) 的圆与两条坐标轴以及 \(30^\circ\)-\(60^\circ\)-\(90^\circ\) 三角形 \(ABC\) 的斜边都相切，其中 \(AB=1\)。将结果精确到百分位，圆的半径是多少？
