1单选

Pearl Creek 小学每个三年级教室有 \(18\) 名学生和 \(2\) 只宠物兔。全部 \(4\) 个三年级教室中，学生比兔子多多少？

English（选项同上方）

Each third-grade classroom at Pearl Creek Elementary has \(18\) students and \(2\) pet rabbits. How many more students than rabbits are there in all \(4\) of the third-grade classrooms?

每个教室里学生比兔子多 \(18-2=16\) 个。

全部 \(4\) 个教室中，学生比兔子多 \(4\cdot16=64\) 个。

因此正确答案是 C。

English

Each classroom has \(18-2=16\) more students than rabbits.

Across all \(4\) classrooms there are \(4\cdot16=64\) more students than rabbits.

Thus, the correct answer is C.

2单选

如图，一个半径为 \(5\) 的圆内切于一个长方形。长方形的长与宽之比为 \(2:1\)。这个长方形的面积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A circle of radius \(5\) is inscribed in a rectangle as shown. The ratio of the length of the rectangle to its width is \(2:1.\) What is the area of the rectangle?

圆内切于长方形，所以长方形的宽等于圆的直径，即 \(2\cdot5=10\)。

长方形的长为 \(2\cdot10=20\)，所以面积为 \(10\cdot20=200\)。

因此正确答案是 E。

English

The circle is inscribed, so the width of the rectangle equals the diameter, \(2\cdot5=10.\)

The length is then \(2\cdot10=20,\) so the area is \(10\cdot20=200.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

3单选

为了一个科学项目，Sammy 观察了一只花栗鼠和一只松鼠把橡子藏进洞里。花栗鼠在它挖的每个洞里藏了 \(3\) 颗橡子。松鼠在它挖的每个洞里藏了 \(4\) 颗橡子。它们各自藏了相同数量的橡子，不过松鼠少用了 \(4\) 个洞。花栗鼠藏了多少颗橡子？

English（选项同上方）

For a science project, Sammy observed a chipmunk and a squirrel stashing acorns in holes. The chipmunk hid \(3\) acorns in each of the holes it dug. The squirrel hid \(4\) acorns in each of the holes it dug. They each hid the same number of acorns, although the squirrel needed \(4\) fewer holes. How many acorns did the chipmunk hide?

设花栗鼠挖了 \(h\) 个洞。花栗鼠藏了 \(3h\) 颗橡子，松鼠藏了 \(4(h-4)\) 颗橡子。

因为它们藏的橡子数量相同，\(3h=4(h-4)\)，解得 \(h=16\)。

花栗鼠藏了 \(3\cdot16=48\) 颗橡子。

因此正确答案是 D。

English

Let \(h\) be the number of holes the chipmunk dug. The chipmunk hid \(3h\) acorns and the squirrel hid \(4(h-4)\) acorns.

Since they hid the same number, \(3h=4(h-4),\) which gives \(h=16.\)

The chipmunk hid \(3\cdot16=48\) acorns.

Thus, the correct answer is D.

4单选

假设一欧元值 \(1.30\) 美元。如果 Diana 有 \(500\) 美元，Étienne 有 \(400\) 欧元，那么 Étienne 的钱的价值比 Diana 的钱的价值多百分之多少？

English（选项同上方）

Suppose that the euro is worth \(1.30\) dollars. If Diana has \(500\) dollars and Étienne has \(400\) euros, by what percent is the value of Étienne’s money greater than the value of Diana’s money?

Étienne 的钱价值 \(400\cdot1.30=520\) 美元，而 Diana 有 \(500\) 美元。

Étienne 的钱的价值超过 Diana 的百分比为 \[\frac{520-500}{500}\cdot100\%=4\%\text{。}\]

因此正确答案是 B。

English

Étienne’s money is worth \(400\cdot1.30=520\) dollars, while Diana has \(500\) dollars.

The percent by which Étienne’s value exceeds Diana’s is \[\frac{520-500}{500}\cdot100\%=4\%.\]

Thus, the correct answer is B.

5单选

两个整数的和为 \(26\)。当另外两个整数加到前两个整数上时，和为 \(41\)。最后再把另外两个整数加到前四个整数的和上时，和为 \(57\)。这 \(6\) 个整数中偶数的最少个数是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Two integers have a sum of \(26.\) When two more integers are added to the first two integers the sum is \(41.\) Finally when two more integers are added to the sum of the previous four integers the sum is \(57.\) What is the minimum number of even integers among the \(6\) integers?

三个连续的整数对的和分别是 \(26\)，\(41-26=15\)，和 \(57-41=16\)。

两个整数同奇偶时和为偶数，恰好一个是偶数时和为奇数。只有中间那一对的和为奇数，所以它必须至少含有一个偶数。

另外两对都可以全是奇数，所以最少可以只有 \(1\) 个偶数，例如 \(1,25,1,14,1,15\)。

因此正确答案是 A。

English

The three successive pairs have sums \(26,\) \(41-26=15,\) and \(57-41=16.\)

A pair sums to an even number when its two integers share parity, and to an odd number when exactly one is even. Only the middle pair sums to an odd number, so it must contain at least one even integer.

The other two pairs can be all odd, so as few as \(1\) even integer is possible, for example \(1,25,1,14,1,15.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

6单选

为了估计 \(x-y\) 的值，其中 \(x\) 和 \(y\) 是满足 \(x \gt y \gt 0\) 的实数，Xiaoli 把 \(x\) 向上调整了一小段量，把 \(y\) 向下调整了同样的量，然后把她调整后的数相减。下列哪一项一定正确？

English（选项同上方）

In order to estimate the value of \(x-y\) where \(x\) and \(y\) are real numbers with \(x \gt y \gt 0,\) Xiaoli rounded \(x\) up by a small amount, rounded \(y\) down by the same amount, and then subtracted her rounded values. Which of the following statements is necessarily correct?

设 \(d \gt 0\) 为那一小段量。Xiaoli 计算的是 \((x+d)-(y-d)\) \(=(x-y)+2d\)。

因为 \(2d \gt 0\)，她的估计值大于真实值 \(x-y\)。

因此正确答案是 A。

English

Let \(d \gt 0\) be the small amount. Xiaoli computes \((x+d)-(y-d)\) \(=(x-y)+2d.\)

Since \(2d \gt 0,\) her estimate exceeds the true value \(x-y.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

7单选

小灯泡按红、红、绿、绿、绿、红、红、绿、绿、绿这样的顺序挂在一根绳子上，相邻灯泡相距 \(6\) 英寸，并一直重复 \(2\) 个红灯后接 \(3\) 个绿灯的模式。第 \(3\) 个红灯和第 \(21\) 个红灯相距多少英尺？

注：\(1\) 英尺等于 \(12\) 英寸。

English（选项同上方）

Small lights are hung on a string \(6\) inches apart in the order red, red, green, green, green, red, red, green, green, green, and so on continuing this pattern of \(2\) red lights followed by \(3\) green lights. How many feet separate the \(3\)rd red light and the \(21\)st red light?

Note: \(1\) foot is equal to \(12\) inches.

灯泡按每 \(5\) 个一组重复，所以相邻两组的开头相距 \(5\cdot6=30\) 英寸，即 \(2.5\) 英尺。

每一组的开头都是一个奇数编号的红灯。第 \(3\) 个红灯是第 \(2\) 组的开头，第 \(21\) 个红灯是第 \(11\) 组的开头。

它们之间的距离是 \((11-2)\cdot2.5=22.5\) 英尺。

因此正确答案是 E。

English

The lights repeat in blocks of \(5,\) so consecutive blocks start \(5\cdot6=30\) inches, or \(2.5\) feet, apart.

Each block has one odd-numbered red light beginning it. The \(3\)rd red light begins the \(2\)nd block and the \(21\)st red light begins the \(11\)th block.

The distance between them is \((11-2)\cdot2.5=22.5\) feet.

Thus, the correct answer is E.

8单选

一位甜点师从星期日开始，为一周中的每天准备甜点。每天的甜点是蛋糕、派、冰淇淋或布丁。同一种甜点不能连续两天供应。因为有人过生日，星期五必须供应蛋糕。这一周有多少种不同的甜点菜单？

English（选项同上方）

A dessert chef prepares the dessert for every day of a week starting with Sunday. The dessert each day is either cake, pie, ice cream, or pudding. The same dessert may not be served two days in a row. There must be cake on Friday because of a birthday. How many different dessert menus for the week are possible?

星期五固定为蛋糕。从星期五向两边计数。

另外六天（星期六，然后是星期四、星期三、星期二、星期一、星期日）都可以选择除已经确定的相邻那天甜点之外的任意一种甜点，因此各有 \(3\) 种选择。

菜单数为 \(3^6=729\)。

因此正确答案是 A。

English

Friday is fixed as cake. Work outward from Friday.

Each of the other six days (Saturday, then Thursday, Wednesday, Tuesday, Monday, Sunday) can be any dessert except the one served on the neighboring already-chosen day, giving \(3\) choices each.

The number of menus is \(3^6=729.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

9单选

当自动扶梯不运行时，Clea 走下自动扶梯需要 \(60\) 秒；当自动扶梯运行时，她走下自动扶梯只需要 \(24\) 秒。当运行中的自动扶梯带着她下行而她只是站着不动时，需要多少秒？

English（选项同上方）

It takes Clea \(60\) seconds to walk down an escalator when it is not operating, and only \(24\) seconds to walk down the escalator when it is operating. How many seconds does it take Clea to ride down the operating escalator when she just stands on it?

设 \(x\) 为 Clea 的步行速度，\(r\) 为自动扶梯速度，自动扶梯长度为 \(60x\)。在运行中的自动扶梯上步行给出 \(24(x+r)=60x\)，所以 \(r=\tfrac32x\)。

站着不动所需时间 \(t\) 满足 \(rt=60x\)，因此 \(\tfrac32xt=60x\) 得 \(t=40\) 秒。

因此正确答案是 B。

English

Let \(x\) be Clea’s walking rate and \(r\) the escalator’s rate, with the length equal to \(60x.\) Walking on the moving escalator gives \(24(x+r)=60x,\) so \(r=\tfrac32x.\)

Standing takes time \(t\) with \(rt=60x,\) so \(\tfrac32xt=60x\) and \(t=40\) seconds.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

10单选

以曲线 \(x^2 + y^2 = 25\) 与 \((x - 4)^2 + 9y^2 = 81\) 的交点为顶点所形成的多边形，面积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

What is the area of the polygon whose vertices are the points of intersection of the curves \(x^2 + y^2 = 25\) and \((x - 4)^2 + 9y^2 = 81?\)

由 \(x^2+y^2=25\) 得 \(y^2=25-x^2\)。代入 \((x-4)^2+9y^2=81\) 得 \(x^2+x-20=0\)，所以 \(x=4\) 或 \(x=-5\)。

交点为 \((-5,0)\)，\((4,3)\)，和 \((4,-3)\)。

从 \((4,3)\) 到 \((4,-3)\) 的竖直边长为 \(6\)，到 \((-5,0)\) 的水平距离为 \(9\)，所以面积是 \(\tfrac12\cdot6\cdot9=27\)。

因此正确答案是 B。

English

From \(x^2+y^2=25\) we get \(y^2=25-x^2.\) Substituting into \((x-4)^2+9y^2=81\) gives \(x^2+x-20=0,\) so \(x=4\) or \(x=-5.\)

The intersection points are \((-5,0),\) \((4,3),\) and \((4,-3).\)

The vertical side from \((4,3)\) to \((4,-3)\) has length \(6,\) and the horizontal distance to \((-5,0)\) is \(9,\) so the area is \(\tfrac12\cdot6\cdot9=27.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

11单选

在下面的等式中，\(A\) 和 \(B\) 是连续正整数，并且 \(A\)、\(B\) 和 \(A + B\) 都表示数的进制： \[132_A + 43_B = 69_{A+B}\text{。}\] \(A + B\) 等于多少？

English（选项同上方）

In the equation below, \(A\) and \(B\) are consecutive positive integers, and \(A,\) \(B,\) and \(A + B\) represent number bases: \[132_A + 43_B = 69_{A+B}.\] What is \(A + B?\)

展开各个数，\(132_A=A^2+3A+2\)，\(43_B=4B+3\)，且 \(69_{A+B}=6(A+B)+9\)。

当 \(B=A+1\) 时，方程变为 \(A^2+3A+2\) \(+4(A+1)+3\) \(=6(2A+1)+9\)，化简得 \((A-6)(A+1)=0\)。正整数解为 \(A=6\)，所以 \(B=7\)。

（当 \(B=A-1\) 时得到 \(A^2-5A-2=0\)，没有整数解。）

因此 \(A+B=13\)。

因此正确答案是 C。

English

Writing the numerals out, \(132_A=A^2+3A+2,\) \(43_B=4B+3,\) and \(69_{A+B}=6(A+B)+9.\)

With \(B=A+1,\) the equation becomes \(A^2+3A+2\) \(+4(A+1)+3\) \(=6(2A+1)+9,\) which simplifies to \((A-6)(A+1)=0.\) The positive solution is \(A=6,\) so \(B=7.\)

(The case \(B=A-1\) gives \(A^2-5A-2=0,\) which has no integer solution.)

Therefore \(A+B=13.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

12单选

有多少个长度为 \(20\) 的由零和/或一组成的序列满足：所有零连续，或者所有一连续，或者两者都满足？

English（选项同上方）

How many sequences of zeros and/or ones of length \(20\) have all the zeros consecutive, or all the ones consecutive, or both?

设 \(A\) 为所有零连续的序列，\(B\) 为所有一连续的序列。

对于 \(A\)，有一个全是一的序列，\(20\) 个恰有一个零的序列，以及 \(\binom{20}{2}=190\) 个含有至少两个零的序列（选择第一个和最后一个零的位置）。所以 \(|A|=1+20+190=211\)，由对称性 \(|B|=211\)。

\(A\cap B\) 中的序列是一个零的块后接一个一的块，或顺序相反；这样的序列有 \(|A\cap B|=40\) 个。

因此 \(|A\cup B|=211+211-40\) \(=382\)。

因此正确答案是 E。

English

Let \(A\) be the sequences in which all zeros are consecutive and \(B\) those in which all ones are consecutive.

For \(A,\) there is one all-ones sequence, \(20\) sequences with exactly one zero, and \(\binom{20}{2}=190\) sequences with two or more zeros (choose the first and last zero position). So \(|A|=1+20+190=211,\) and by symmetry \(|B|=211.\)

A sequence in \(A\cap B\) is a block of zeros followed by a block of ones, or the reverse; there are \(|A\cap B|=40\) of these.

Therefore \(|A\cup B|=211+211-40\) \(=382.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

13单选

两条抛物线的方程为 \(y = x^2 + ax + b\) 和 \(y = x^2 + cx + d\)，其中 \(a\)，\(b\)，\(c\)，和 \(d\) 是整数（不一定互不相同），每个都通过掷一枚公平的六面骰子独立选出。两条抛物线至少有一个公共点的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Two parabolas have equations \(y = x^2 + ax + b\) and \(y = x^2 + cx + d,\) where \(a,\) \(b,\) \(c,\) and \(d\) are integers (not necessarily different), each chosen independently by rolling a fair six-sided die. What is the probability that the parabolas have at least one point in common?

抛物线相交处满足 \(x^2+ax+b=x^2+cx+d\)，即 \(ax+b=cx+d\)。这个方程无解，恰好发生在两条直线平行且不重合的时候，也就是 \(a=c\) 且 \(b\neq d\)。

\(a=c\) 的概率是 \(\tfrac16\)，且 \(b\neq d\) 的概率是 \(\tfrac56\)，所以没有公共点的概率为 \(\tfrac16\cdot\tfrac56=\tfrac5{36}\)。

至少有一个公共点的概率为 \(1-\tfrac5{36}=\tfrac{31}{36}\)。

因此正确答案是 D。

English

The parabolas meet where \(x^2+ax+b=x^2+cx+d,\) i.e. \(ax+b=cx+d.\) This has no solution exactly when the lines are parallel and distinct: \(a=c\) and \(b\neq d.\)

The probability that \(a=c\) is \(\tfrac16,\) and the probability that \(b\neq d\) is \(\tfrac56,\) so the probability of no common point is \(\tfrac16\cdot\tfrac56=\tfrac5{36}.\)

The probability of at least one common point is \(1-\tfrac5{36}=\tfrac{31}{36}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

14单选

Bernardo 和 Silvia 玩下面的游戏。选取一个从 \(0\) 到 \(999\)（含两端）的整数交给 Bernardo。每当 Bernardo 收到一个数时，他把它加倍并把结果交给 Silvia。每当 Silvia 收到一个数时，她给它加上 \(50\) 并把结果交给 Bernardo。最后一个产生小于 \(1000\) 的数的人获胜。设 \(N\) 为使 Bernardo 获胜的最小初始数。\(N\) 的各位数字之和是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Bernardo and Silvia play the following game. An integer between \(0\) and \(999,\) inclusive, is selected and given to Bernardo. Whenever Bernardo receives a number, he doubles it and passes the result to Silvia. Whenever Silvia receives a number, she adds \(50\) to it and passes the result to Bernardo. The winner is the last person who produces a number less than \(1000.\) Let \(N\) be the smallest initial number that results in a win for Bernardo. What is the sum of the digits of \(N?\)

当 Bernardo 在一轮后给出的加倍数 \(2n+50\ge1000\) 且之前的数都小于 \(1000\) 时，他获胜。满足 \(2n+50\ge1000\) 的最小 \(n\) 是 \(475\)。

倒推，经过二、三、四轮后导致胜利的最小初始值分别是满足 \(2n+50\ge475\)，\(\ge213\)，和 \(\ge82\) 的最小整数，即 \(213\)，\(82\)，和 \(16\)。不会有超过四轮后才获胜的初始值。

所以 \(N=16\)，各位数字之和为 \(1+6=7\)。

因此正确答案是 A。

English

Bernardo wins after a round when his doubled number \(2n+50\ge1000\) but the previous numbers stayed below \(1000.\) The smallest \(n\) with \(2n+50\ge1000\) is \(475.\)

Working backwards, the smallest starting values that lead to a win after two, three, and four rounds are the smallest integers with \(2n+50\ge475,\) \(\ge213,\) and \(\ge82,\) namely \(213,\) \(82,\) and \(16.\) No start wins after more than four rounds.

So \(N=16,\) and the sum of its digits is \(1+6=7.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

15单选

Jesse 沿两条半径剪开一个半径为 \(12\) 的圆形纸片，形成两个扇形，其中较小扇形的圆心角为 \(120\) 度。他用每个扇形作为一个圆锥的侧面，做成两个圆锥。较小圆锥的体积与较大圆锥的体积之比是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Jesse cuts a circular paper disk of radius \(12\) along two radii to form two sectors, the smaller having a central angle of \(120\) degrees. He makes two circular cones, using each sector to form the lateral surface of a cone. What is the ratio of the volume of the smaller cone to that of the larger?

每个扇形形成的圆锥母线长为 \(12\)。较小扇形的弧长为 \(\tfrac{120}{360}\cdot2\pi\cdot12=8\pi\)，因此底面半径为 \(4\)，高为 \(\sqrt{12^2-4^2}=8\sqrt2\)。

较大扇形（圆心角 \(240^\circ\)）的弧长为 \(16\pi\)，底面半径为 \(8\)，高为 \(\sqrt{12^2-8^2}=4\sqrt5\)。

体积之比为 \[\frac{\tfrac13\pi\cdot4^2\cdot8\sqrt2}{\tfrac13\pi\cdot8^2\cdot4\sqrt5} =\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}\text{。}\]

因此正确答案是 C。

English

Each sector forms a cone with slant height \(12.\) The smaller sector’s arc length is \(\tfrac{120}{360}\cdot2\pi\cdot12=8\pi,\) so its base radius is \(4\) and its height is \(\sqrt{12^2-4^2}=8\sqrt2.\)

The larger sector (central angle \(240^\circ\)) has arc length \(16\pi,\) base radius \(8,\) and height \(\sqrt{12^2-8^2}=4\sqrt5.\)

The ratio of volumes is \[\frac{\tfrac13\pi\cdot4^2\cdot8\sqrt2}{\tfrac13\pi\cdot8^2\cdot4\sqrt5} =\frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}.\]

Thus, the correct answer is C.

16单选

Amy、Beth 和 Jo 听了四首不同的歌，并讨论她们喜欢哪些歌。没有一首歌被三人都喜欢。此外，对这三个女生中的每一对，都至少有一首歌被这两人喜欢、但不被第三人喜欢。有多少种不同的可能情况？

English（选项同上方）

它们在 \(400\) 以内的倍数个数分别为 \(\lfloor\frac{400}{32}\rfloor=12\)，\(\lfloor\frac{400}{81}\rfloor=4\)，\(\lfloor\frac{400}{343}\rfloor=1\)，和 \(\lfloor\frac{400}{400}\rfloor=1\)，且没有重叠，总数为 \(12+4+1+1=18\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

If \(N_2\) is a multiple of \(N_1\) then \(f_1(N_2)\) is a multiple of \(f_1(N_1),\) so if \(S_{N_1}\) is unbounded so is \(S_{N_2}.\) Call \(N\) essential if it is unbounded but no proper divisor is. An essential \(N\) must have all exponents at least \(2,\) and \((p_1\cdots p_k)^2\le400\) forces at most two primes.

For \(n=2^a3^b,\) two iterations send the exponent pair to \((2a-4,2b-3).\) Thus the orbit is unbounded exactly when \(a\ge5\) or \(b\ge4,\) producing the essential values \(2^5=32\) and \(3^4=81.\) For a single other prime, the bound leaves only \(5^2,5^3,7^2,7^3,11^2,13^2,17^2,19^2;\) direct application of \(f_1\) leaves only \(7^3=343\) essential. With two primes, \(p_1p_2\le20\) leaves the pairs \((2,5),(2,7),(3,5)\) besides \((2,3).\) Applying \(f_1\) to their possible squareful products leaves only \(2^4\cdot5^2=400.\)

Their multiples up to \(400\) number \(\lfloor\frac{400}{32}\rfloor=12,\) \(\lfloor\frac{400}{81}\rfloor=4,\) \(\lfloor\frac{400}{343}\rfloor=1,\) and \(\lfloor\frac{400}{400}\rfloor=1,\) with no overlaps, for a total of \(12+4+1+1=18.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

设 \(S = \{(x, y) : x \in \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\}\)，\(y \in \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}\)，且 \((x, y) \ne (0, 0)\}\)。设 \(T\) 为所有顶点在 \(S\) 中的直角三角形的集合。对每个直角三角形 \(t = \triangle ABC\)，其顶点 \(A\)，\(B\)，\(C\) 按逆时针顺序排列，且直角在 \(A\)，令 \(f(t) = \tan(\angle CBA)\)。求 \[\prod_{t \in T} f(t)\text{？}\]

English（选项同上方）

Let \(S = \{(x, y) : x \in \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\},\) \(y \in \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5\},\) and \((x, y) \ne (0, 0)\}.\) Let \(T\) be the set of all right triangles whose vertices are in \(S.\) For every right triangle \(t = \triangle ABC\) with vertices \(A,\) \(B,\) and \(C\) in counter-clockwise order and right angle at \(A,\) let \(f(t) = \tan(\angle CBA).\) What is \[\prod_{t \in T} f(t)?\]

等腰直角三角形贡献 \(f(t)=1\)。 对于三边互不相等的直角三角形，关于合适的直线反射可把它与三角形 \(t_1\) 配对，使得 \(f(t)f(t_1)\) \(=\tan(\angle CBA)\tan(\angle ACB)\) \(=1\)。

连续反射（关于 \(x=2\)， 再关于 \(x=y\)， 再关于 \(y=\tfrac52\)）把乘积化简为仅六个形如 \(OYZ\) 且 \(Y\) 在最上排的三角形对应乘积的倒数。

这六个三角形给出 \[ \begin{aligned} &\frac15\cdot\frac25\cdot\frac35\cdot\frac45\cdot\frac{3\sqrt2}{\sqrt2} \\ &\quad {}\cdot\frac{4\sqrt2}{2\sqrt2}=\frac{144}{625} \end{aligned}\text{，}\] 因此所求乘积是其倒数 \(\dfrac{625}{144}\)。

因此正确答案是 B。

English

Isosceles right triangles contribute \(f(t)=1.\) For a scalene right triangle, reflecting across a suitable line pairs it with a triangle \(t_1\) so that \(f(t)f(t_1)\) \(=\tan(\angle CBA)\tan(\angle ACB)\) \(=1.\)

Successive reflections (across \(x=2,\) then \(x=y,\) then \(y=\tfrac52\)) reduce the product to the reciprocal of the product over just six triangles of the form \(OYZ\) with \(Y\) on the top row.

Those six give \[ \begin{aligned} &\frac15\cdot\frac25\cdot\frac35\cdot\frac45\cdot\frac{3\sqrt2}{\sqrt2} \\ &\quad {}\cdot\frac{4\sqrt2}{2\sqrt2}=\frac{144}{625}, \end{aligned} \] so the required product is its reciprocal, \(\dfrac{625}{144}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.
