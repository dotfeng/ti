The ratio \[\dfrac{10^{2000}+10^{2002}}{10^{2001}+10^{2001}}\] is closest to which of the following numbers?

提出公因式得 \[\dfrac{10^{2000}(1+100)}{2\cdot 10^{2001}}=\dfrac{101}{20}=5.05\text{，}\]它最接近 \(5\)。

Factoring gives \[\dfrac{10^{2000}(1+100)}{2\cdot 10^{2001}}=\dfrac{101}{20}=5.05,\] which is closest to \(5.\)

2单选

对非零数 \(a\)、\(b\)、\(c\)，定义 \[(a,b,c)=\dfrac{a}{b}+\dfrac{b}{c}+\dfrac{c}{a}\text{。}\]求 \((2,12,9)\)。

English（选项同上方）

For the nonzero numbers \(a,\) \(b,\) and \(c,\) define \[(a,b,c)=\dfrac{a}{b}+\dfrac{b}{c}+\dfrac{c}{a}.\] Find \((2,12,9).\)

\[ \begin{aligned} (2,12,9) &= \dfrac{2}{12}+\dfrac{12}{9}+\dfrac{9}{2} \\ &= \dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{4}{3}+\dfrac{9}{2} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]通分到分母 \(6\)，得到 \(\dfrac{1+8+27}{6}=\dfrac{36}{6}=6\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

\[ \begin{aligned} (2,12,9) &= \dfrac{2}{12}+\dfrac{12}{9}+\dfrac{9}{2} \\ &= \dfrac{1}{6}+\dfrac{4}{3}+\dfrac{9}{2}. \end{aligned} \] Over a denominator of \(6,\) this is \(\dfrac{1+8+27}{6}=\dfrac{36}{6}=6.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

3单选

按照指数运算的标准约定，\[2^{2^{2^{2}}}=2^{\left(2^{\left(2^{2}\right)}\right)}=2^{16}=65{,}536\text{。}\]如果改变指数运算的执行顺序，还可能得到多少个其他值？

English（选项同上方）

According to the standard convention for exponentiation, \[2^{2^{2^{2}}}=2^{\left(2^{\left(2^{2}\right)}\right)}=2^{16}=65{,}536.\] If the order in which the exponentiations are performed is changed, how many other values are possible?

这个四层指数塔有五种加括号方式。其中 \((2^2)^{\left(2^2\right)}\)、\(\left(2^{\left(2^2\right)}\right)^2\)、\(\left(\left(2^2\right)^2\right)^2\) 都等于 \(2^{8}=256\)。另外两种都给出标准值 \(2^{16}=65{,}536\)。

所以只可能得到一个其他值，即 \(256\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

There are five ways to parenthesize the tower. Three of them, \((2^2)^{\left(2^2\right)},\) \(\left(2^{\left(2^2\right)}\right)^2,\) and \(\left(\left(2^2\right)^2\right)^2,\) all equal \(2^{8}=256.\) The other two both give the standard value \(2^{16}=65{,}536.\)

So exactly one other value, \(256,\) is possible.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

4单选

有多少个正整数 \(m\) 满足：存在至少一个正整数 \(n\)，使得 \(m\cdot n\le m+n\)？

English（选项同上方）

For how many positive integers \(m\) does there exist at least one positive integer \(n\) such that \(m\cdot n\le m+n?\)

取 \(n=1\)。此时 \(m\cdot 1\le m+1\)，也就是 \(m\le m+1\)，对每个正整数 \(m\) 都成立。

所以每个正整数 \(m\) 都符合条件，共有无穷多个。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Take \(n=1.\) Then \(m\cdot 1\le m+1\) becomes \(m\le m+1,\) which holds for every positive integer \(m.\)

So every positive integer \(m\) works, giving infinitely many.

Thus, the correct answer is E.

图中每个小圆的半径都是一。最里面的圆与围绕它的六个圆相切，这六个圆中的每个又与大圆以及相邻的小圆相切。求阴影区域的面积。

English（选项同上方）

Each of the small circles in the figure has radius one. The innermost circle is tangent to the six circles that surround it, and each of those circles is tangent to the large circle and to its small-circle neighbors. Find the area of the shaded region.

外圈小圆的圆心到中心的距离为 \(2\)，再加上它自己的半径 \(1\)，得到大圆半径为 \(3\)。

大圆面积为 \(9\pi\)，七个单位圆总面积为 \(7\pi\)，所以阴影面积为 \(9\pi-7\pi=2\pi\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The center of a surrounding circle is \(2\) from the center (two radii), and adding its own radius \(1\) gives a large radius of \(3.\)

The large circle has area \(9\pi,\) and the seven unit circles have total area \(7\pi,\) so the shaded region is \(9\pi-7\pi=2\pi.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

Thus, the correct answer is A.

Compute the sum of all the roots of \((2x+3)(x-4)\) \(+(2x+3)(x-6)=0.\)

因式分解得 \[ \begin{gathered} (2x+3)\left[(x-4)+(x-6)\right] \\ = (2x+3)(2x-10) \\ = 0 \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

两个根为 \(-\dfrac{3}{2}\) 和 \(5\)，它们的和为 \(\dfrac{7}{2}\)。

The roots are \(-\dfrac{3}{2}\) and \(5,\) which sum to \(\dfrac{7}{2}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

11单选

Jamal 想把 \(30\) 个电脑文件存到软盘上，每张软盘容量为 \(1.44\) 兆字节（MB）。其中三个文件各需要 \(0.8\) MB，另有 \(12\) 个各需要 \(0.7\) MB，剩下 \(15\) 个各需要 \(0.4\) MB。一个文件不能拆分到多张软盘上。最少需要多少张软盘？

English（选项同上方）

Jamal wants to store \(30\) computer files on floppy disks, each of which has a capacity of \(1.44\) megabytes (mb). Three of his files require \(0.8\) mb of memory each, \(12\) more require \(0.7\) mb each, and the remaining \(15\) require \(0.4\) mb each. No file can be split between floppy disks. What is the minimal number of floppy disks that will hold all the files?

文件总大小为 \(3(0.8)+12(0.7)+15(0.4)\) \(=16.8\) 兆字节。任何装有 \(0.8\) 兆字节文件的软盘，只能再放一个 \(0.4\) 兆字节文件，因为 \(0.8+0.7\gt 1.44\)。因此每张这样的软盘至少浪费 \(0.24\) 兆字节，三张共至少浪费 \(0.72\) 兆字节，有效需求至少为 \(16.8+0.72=17.52\) 兆字节，所以至少需要 \(\left\lceil\dfrac{17.52}{1.44}\right\rceil=13\) 张软盘。

这个数可以达到：\(3\) 张各放一个 \(0.8\) 兆字节文件和一个 \(0.4\) 兆字节文件；\(6\) 张各放两个 \(0.7\) 兆字节文件；\(4\) 张各放三个 \(0.4\) 兆字节文件。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The files need \(3(0.8)+12(0.7)+15(0.4)\) \(=16.8\) mb. On any disk holding a \(0.8\) mb file, only one \(0.4\) mb file fits alongside it (since \(0.8+0.7\gt 1.44\)), leaving at least \(0.24\) mb wasted. Across the three such disks that is at least \(0.72\) mb, so the effective demand is at least \(16.8+0.72=17.52\) mb, requiring at least \(\left\lceil\dfrac{17.52}{1.44}\right\rceil=13\) disks.

This is achievable: \(3\) disks each hold one \(0.8\) file and one \(0.4\) file, \(6\) disks each hold two \(0.7\) files, and \(4\) disks each hold three \(0.4\) files.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

12单选

Earl E. Bird 先生每天早上正好在 \(8{:}00\) 离家上班。当他的平均速度为每小时 \(40\) 英里时，会晚到三分钟；当平均速度为每小时 \(60\) 英里时，会早到三分钟。Bird 先生应以多少英里每小时的平均速度行驶，才能准时到达？

English（选项同上方）

Mr. Earl E. Bird leaves his house for work at exactly \(8{:}00\) A.M. every morning. When he averages \(40\) miles per hour, he arrives at his workplace three minutes late. When he averages \(60\) miles per hour, he arrives three minutes early. At what average speed, in miles per hour, should Mr. Bird drive to arrive at his workplace precisely on time?

设准时所需时间为 \(t\) 小时。题中的 \(3\) 分钟等于 \(0.05\) 小时，所以 \(40(t+0.05)=60(t-0.05)\)。化简得 \(40t+2=60t-3\)，从而 \(t=0.25\)。

路程为 \(40(0.30)=12\) 英里，因此准时速度为 \(\dfrac{12}{0.25}=48\) 英里每小时。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Let \(t\) hours be the on-time travel time. Since \(3\) minutes is \(0.05\) hours, \(40(t+0.05)=60(t-0.05).\) Then \(40t+2=60t-3,\) so \(t=0.25.\)

The distance is \(40(0.30)=12\) miles, so the required speed is \(\dfrac{12}{0.25}=48\) mph.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

13单选

一个三角形的边长为 \(15\)、\(20\)、\(25\)。求最短高的长度。

English（选项同上方）

The sides of a triangle have lengths of \(15,\) \(20,\) and \(25.\) Find the length of the shortest altitude.

因为 \(15^2+20^2=225+400\) \(=625=25^2\)，这是一个直角三角形，两条直角边为 \(15\) 和 \(20\)，面积为 \(\dfrac{1}{2}(15)(20)=150\)。

最短高对应最长边 \(25\)，长度为 \(\dfrac{2\cdot 150}{25}=12\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Since \(15^2+20^2=225+400\) \(=625=25^2,\) the triangle is right with legs \(15\) and \(20,\) and area \(\dfrac{1}{2}(15)(20)=150.\)

The shortest altitude falls to the longest side \(25,\) and equals \(\dfrac{2\cdot 150}{25}=12.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

二次方程 \(x^2-63x+k=0\) 的两个根都是质数。\(k\) 可能有多少个取值？

English（选项同上方）

Both roots of the quadratic equation \(x^2-63x+k=0\) are prime numbers. The number of possible values of \(k\) is

设两个质数根为 \(p\)、\(q\)，则 \(p+q=63\)，\(pq=k\)。因为 \(63\) 是奇数，其中一个质数必须是 \(2\)，从而另一个是 \(61\)，它也是质数。

因此 \(k=2\cdot 61=122\)，所以只有这一个可能取值。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

If the roots are primes \(p\) and \(q,\) then \(p+q=63\) and \(pq=k.\) Because \(63\) is odd, one prime must be \(2,\) making the other \(61,\) which is prime.

So \(k=2\cdot 61=122\) is the only possible value.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

15单选

用数字 \(1\)、\(2\)、\(3\)、\(4\)、\(5\)、\(6\)、\(7\)、\(9\) 组成四个两位质数，每个数字恰好使用一次。这四个质数的和是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The digits \(1,\) \(2,\) \(3,\) \(4,\) \(5,\) \(6,\) \(7,\) and \(9\) are used to form four two-digit prime numbers, with each digit used exactly once. What is the sum of these four primes?

两位质数不能以 \(2\)、\(4\)、\(5\)、\(6\) 结尾，所以这些数字必须是十位数字，而 \(1\)、\(3\)、\(7\)、\(9\) 是个位数字。

四个数的和为 \(10(2+4+5+6)\) \(+(1+3+7+9)\) \(=170+20=190\)。例如 \(\{23,47,59,61\}\) 是一组可行构造。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

A two-digit prime cannot end in \(2,\) \(4,\) \(5,\) or \(6,\) so these four are the tens digits and \(1,\) \(3,\) \(7,\) and \(9\) are the units digits.

The sum is \(10(2+4+5+6)\) \(+(1+3+7+9)\) \(=170+20=190.\) One valid set is \(\{23,47,59,61\}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

16单选

若 \(a+1\) \(=b+2\) \(=c+3\) \(=d+4\) \(=a+b+c+d+5\)，则 \(a+b+c+d\) 等于多少？

English（选项同上方）

If \(a+1\) \(=b+2\) \(=c+3\) \(=d+4\) \(=a+b+c+d+5,\) then \(a+b+c+d\) is

设公共值为 \(k\)。则 \(a=k-1\)，\(b=k-2\)，\(c=k-3\)，\(d=k-4\)，所以 \(a+b+c+d=4k-10\)。

因为 \(a+b+c+d+5=k\)，可得 \(4k-10+5=k\)，所以 \(3k=5\)，\(k=\dfrac{5}{3}\)。于是 \(a+b+c+d=k-5\) \(=\dfrac{5}{3}-5=-\dfrac{10}{3}\)，这就是所求值。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Let the common value be \(k.\) Then \(a=k-1,\) \(b=k-2,\) \(c=k-3,\) \(d=k-4,\) so \(a+b+c+d=4k-10.\)

Since \(a+b+c+d+5=k,\) we get \(4k-10+5=k,\) so \(3k=5\) and \(k=\dfrac{5}{3}.\) Then \(a+b+c+d=k-5\) \(=\dfrac{5}{3}-5=-\dfrac{10}{3}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

17单选

English（选项同上方）

Sarah pours four ounces of coffee into an eight-ounce cup and four ounces of cream into a second cup of the same size. She then transfers half the coffee from the first cup to the second and, after stirring thoroughly, transfers half the liquid in the second cup back to the first. What fraction of the liquid in the first cup is now cream?

第一次转移 \(2\) 盎司咖啡后，第 \(1\) 杯有 \(2\) 盎司咖啡，第 \(2\) 杯有 \(2\) 盎司咖啡和 \(4\) 盎司奶油，共 \(6\) 盎司。

从第 \(2\) 杯倒回一半，即 \(3\) 盎司，其中含 \(1\) 盎司咖啡和 \(2\) 盎司奶油，留下第 \(1\) 杯有 \(3\) 盎司咖啡和 \(2\) 盎司奶油。奶油所占比例为 \(\dfrac{2}{2+3}=\dfrac{2}{5}\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

After transferring \(2\) oz of coffee, cup \(1\) has \(2\) oz coffee and cup \(2\) has \(2\) oz coffee plus \(4\) oz cream, a total of \(6\) oz.

Transferring back half of cup \(2\) (that is \(3\) oz, consisting of \(1\) oz coffee and \(2\) oz cream) leaves cup \(1\) with \(3\) oz coffee and \(2\) oz cream. The fraction that is cream is \(\dfrac{2}{2+3}=\dfrac{2}{5}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

18单选

用 \(27\) 个标准骰子粘成一个 \(3\times 3\times 3\) 的立方体。（标准骰子上任意一对相对面的点数之和为 \(7\)。）这个 \(3\times 3\times 3\) 大立方体表面露出的所有点数之和最小可能是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A \(3\times 3\times 3\) cube is formed by gluing together \(27\) standard cubical dice. (On a standard die, the sum of the numbers on any pair of opposite faces is \(7.\)) The smallest possible sum of all the numbers showing on the surface of the \(3\times 3\times 3\) cube is

\(8\) 个角上的骰子各露 \(3\) 个面，最小和为 \(1+2+3=6\)，贡献 \(8\cdot 6=48\)。另外 \(12\) 个棱上骰子各露 \(2\) 个面，最小和为 \(1+2=3\)，贡献 \(12\cdot 3=36\)。

\(6\) 个面中心骰子各露 \(1\) 个面，最小是 \(1\)，贡献 \(6\)；内部骰子贡献 \(0\)。总和为 \(48+36+6=90\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The \(8\) corner dice show \(3\) faces each, minimized at \(1+2+3=6,\) contributing \(8\cdot 6=48.\) The \(12\) edge dice show \(2\) faces, minimized at \(1+2=3,\) contributing \(12\cdot 3=36.\)

The \(6\) face-center dice show \(1\) face, minimized at \(1,\) contributing \(6,\) and the hidden interior die contributes \(0.\) The total is \(48+36+6=90.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

Spot 的狗屋有一个正六边形底面，每边长一码。他被一根两码长的绳子拴在一个顶点上。Spot 在狗屋外能到达的区域面积是多少平方码？

English（选项同上方）

Spot’s doghouse has a regular hexagonal base that measures one yard on each side. He is tethered to a vertex with a two-yard rope. What is the area, in square yards, of the region outside the doghouse that Spot can reach?

在拴点处，狗屋挡住了 \(120^\circ\) 的内角，剩下半径为 \(2\) 的 \(240^\circ\) 扇形，面积为 \(\dfrac{240}{360}\pi(2)^2=\dfrac{8\pi}{3}\)。

绕过两个相邻顶点时，各剩 \(1\) 码绳长，每处扫过 \(60^\circ\) 扇形，总面积为 \(2\cdot\dfrac{60}{360}\pi(1)^2=\dfrac{\pi}{3}\)。总面积为 \(\dfrac{8\pi}{3}+\dfrac{\pi}{3}=3\pi\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

At the tether vertex the hexagon blocks its \(120^\circ\) interior angle, leaving a \(240^\circ\) sector of radius \(2:\) area \(\dfrac{240}{360}\pi(2)^2=\dfrac{8\pi}{3}.\)

Wrapping around each of the two adjacent vertices, \(1\) yard of rope remains and sweeps a \(60^\circ\) sector: \(2\cdot\dfrac{60}{360}\pi(1)^2=\dfrac{\pi}{3}.\) The total is \(\dfrac{8\pi}{3}+\dfrac{\pi}{3}=3\pi.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

点 \(A\)、\(B\)、\(C\)、\(D\)、\(E\)、\(F\) 按此顺序位于线段 \(\overline{AF}\) 上，将其分成五段，每段长 \(1\)。点 \(G\) 不在直线 \(AF\) 上。点 \(H\) 位于 \(\overline{GD}\) 上，点 \(J\) 位于 \(\overline{GF}\) 上。线段 \(\overline{HC}\)、\(\overline{JE}\) 和 \(\overline{AG}\) 平行。求 \(\frac{HC}{JE}\)。

English（选项同上方）

Points \(A,\) \(B,\) \(C,\) \(D,\) \(E,\) and \(F\) lie, in that order, on \(\overline{AF},\) dividing it into five segments, each of length \(1.\) Point \(G\) is not on line \(AF.\) Point \(H\) lies on \(\overline{GD},\) and point \(J\) lies on \(\overline{GF}.\) The line segments \(\overline{HC},\) \(\overline{JE},\) and \(\overline{AG}\) are parallel. Find \(\frac{HC}{JE}.\)

因为 \(HC\parallel AG\)，所以 \(\triangle DHC\sim\triangle DGA\)，于是 \(\dfrac{HC}{AG}=\dfrac{DC}{DA}=\dfrac{1}{3}\)，即 \(HC=\dfrac{AG}{3}\)。

因为 \(JE\parallel AG\)，所以 \(\triangle FJE\sim\triangle FGA\)，于是 \(\dfrac{JE}{AG}=\dfrac{FE}{FA}=\dfrac{1}{5}\)，即 \(JE=\dfrac{AG}{5}\)。

因此 \(\dfrac{HC}{JE}=\dfrac{\frac{AG}{3}}{\frac{AG}{5}}=\dfrac{5}{3}\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Since \(HC\parallel AG,\) \(\triangle DHC\sim\triangle DGA,\) so \(\dfrac{HC}{AG}=\dfrac{DC}{DA}=\dfrac{1}{3},\) giving \(HC=\dfrac{AG}{3}.\)

Since \(JE\parallel AG,\) \(\triangle FJE\sim\triangle FGA,\) so \(\dfrac{JE}{AG}=\dfrac{FE}{FA}=\dfrac{1}{5},\) giving \(JE=\dfrac{AG}{5}.\)

Therefore \(\dfrac{HC}{JE}=\dfrac{\frac{AG}{3}}{\frac{AG}{5}}=\dfrac{5}{3}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

21单选

一组八个整数的平均数、中位数、唯一众数和极差都等于 \(8\)。这组数中可能出现的最大整数是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The mean, median, unique mode, and range of a collection of eight integers are all equal to \(8.\) The largest integer that can be an element of this collection is

八个整数的和是 \(8\cdot 8=64\)。数列 \(6\)、\(6\)、\(6\)、\(8\)、\(8\)、\(8\)、\(8\)、\(14\) 的平均数、中位数、唯一众数和极差都等于 \(8\)，所以最大值 \(14\) 可以达到。

若最大值至少为 \(16\)，极差条件会使最小值至少为 \(8\)。平均数为 \(8\) 就会迫使八个整数全都等于 \(8\)，与极差条件矛盾。

若最大值为 \(15\)，极差 \(8\) 会使最小值为 \(7\)，所以八个整数都至少为 \(7\)。其余七个数的和是 \(64-15=49=7\cdot 7\)，迫使它们全都等于 \(7\)。但这样中位数和众数会是 \(7\)，而不是 \(8\)，矛盾。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The sum is \(8\cdot 8=64.\) The collection \(6,\) \(6,\) \(6,\) \(8,\) \(8,\) \(8,\) \(8,\) \(14\) has mean, median, unique mode, and range all equal to \(8,\) so \(14\) is attainable.

If the largest were at least \(16,\) the range condition would make the smallest at least \(8.\) A mean of \(8\) would then force all eight integers to equal \(8,\) contradicting the range.

If the largest were \(15,\) the range \(8\) forces the smallest to be \(7,\) so all eight integers are at least \(7.\) The other seven then sum to \(64-15=49=7\cdot 7,\) forcing every one of them to equal \(7.\) But then the median and mode would be \(7,\) not \(8,\) a contradiction.

Thus, the correct answer is D.

22单选

一组编号为 \(1\) 到 \(100\) 的瓷砖反复进行如下操作：移除所有编号为完全平方数的瓷砖，然后将剩余瓷砖从 \(1\) 开始重新连续编号。需要进行多少次操作，才能把瓷砖数量减少到一？

English（选项同上方）

A set of tiles numbered \(1\) through \(100\) is modified repeatedly by the following operation: remove all tiles numbered with a perfect square, and renumber the remaining tiles consecutively starting with \(1.\) How many times must the operation be performed to reduce the number of tiles in the set to one?

从 \(n^2\) 块瓷砖开始，一次操作会移除 \(n\) 个完全平方编号，剩 \(n^2-n\) 块。下一次操作会移除 \(n-1\) 个完全平方编号，剩 \(n^2-n-(n-1)=(n-1)^2\) 块。

因此每两次操作会把 \(n^2\) 减到 \((n-1)^2\)。从 \(10^2=100\) 减到 \(1^2=1\)，需要 \(2(10-1)=18\) 次操作。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Starting from \(n^2\) tiles, one operation removes the \(n\) perfect squares, leaving \(n^2-n.\) The next operation removes \(n-1\) perfect squares, leaving \(n^2-n-(n-1)=(n-1)^2.\)

So every two operations reduce \(n^2\) to \((n-1)^2.\) Going from \(10^2=100\) down to \(1^2=1\) takes \(2(10-1)=18\) operations.

Thus, the correct answer is C.

点 \(A\)、\(B\)、\(C\)、\(D\) 按此顺序位于一条直线上，且 \(AB=CD\)、\(BC=12\)。点 \(E\) 不在这条直线上，并且 \(BE=CE=10\)。\(\triangle AED\) 的周长是 \(\triangle BEC\) 周长的两倍。求 \(AB\)。

English（选项同上方）

Points \(A,\) \(B,\) \(C,\) and \(D\) lie on a line, in that order, with \(AB=CD\) and \(BC=12.\) Point \(E\) is not on the line, and \(BE=CE=10.\) The perimeter of \(\triangle AED\) is twice the perimeter of \(\triangle BEC.\) Find \(AB.\)

设 \(M\) 是 \(BC\) 的中点。因为 \(BE=CE\)，所以 \(EM\perp BC\)，且 \(EM=\sqrt{10^2-6^2}=8\)。由对称性 \(AE=ED\)；设 \(AB=CD=x\)、\(AE=ED=y\)。

由周长条件，\(2y+(2x+12)\) \(=2(10+10+12)=64\)，所以 \(x+y=26\)。勾股定理又给出 \(y^2=EM^2+(x+6)^2\) \(=64+(x+6)^2\)。

代入 \(y=26-x\)，得到 \((26-x)^2=64+(x+6)^2\)，即 \(676-52x=100+12x\)，所以 \(64x=576\)，从而 \(x=9\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Let \(M\) be the midpoint of \(BC.\) Since \(BE=CE,\) \(EM\perp BC\) and \(EM=\sqrt{10^2-6^2}=8.\) By symmetry \(AE=ED;\) write \(AB=CD=x\) and \(AE=ED=y.\)

The perimeter condition gives \(2y+(2x+12)\) \(=2(10+10+12)=64,\) so \(x+y=26.\) Also \(y^2=EM^2+(x+6)^2\) \(=64+(x+6)^2.\)

Substituting \(y=26-x,\) \((26-x)^2=64+(x+6)^2,\) which simplifies to \(676-52x=100+12x,\) so \(64x=576\) and \(x=9.\)

Tina randomly selects two distinct numbers from the set \(\{1,2,3,4,5\},\) and Sergio randomly selects a number from the set \(\{1,2,\ldots,10\}.\) The probability that Sergio’s number is larger than the sum of the two numbers chosen by Tina is

English（选项同上方）

In trapezoid \(ABCD\) with bases \(\overline{AB}\) and \(\overline{CD},\) we have \(AB=52,\) \(BC=12,\) \(CD=39,\) and \(DA=5.\) The area of \(ABCD\) is

所以正确答案是 C。

English
