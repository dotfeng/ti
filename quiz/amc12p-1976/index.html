从一中减去 \((1-x)\) 的倒数，所得结果等于 \((1-x)\) 的倒数，则 \(x\) 等于

English（选项同上方）

If one minus the reciprocal of \((1-x)\) equals the reciprocal of \((1-x),\) then \(x\) equals

方程为 \[ 1-\frac1{1-x}=\frac1{1-x}\text{。}\]两边乘以 \(1-x\ne0\)，得 \(1-x-1=1\)，所以 \(x=-1\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The equation is \[ 1-\frac1{1-x}=\frac1{1-x}. \] Multiplying by \(1-x\ne0\) gives \(1-x-1=1,\) so \(x=-1.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is B.

有多少个实数 \(x\) 能使 \(\sqrt{-(x+1)^2}\) 为实数？

English（选项同上方）

For how many real numbers \(x\) is \(\sqrt{-(x+1)^2}\) a real number?

因为 \((x+1)^2\ge0\)，所以它的相反数至多为 \(0\)。它只有在 \((x+1)^2=0\) 时非负，而这只对应一个值 \(x=-1\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Since \((x+1)^2\ge0,\) its negative is at most \(0.\) It is nonnegative only when \((x+1)^2=0,\) which occurs for the single value \(x=-1.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is B.

边长为二的正方形中，从一个顶点到四条边的中点的距离之和为

English（选项同上方）

The sum of the distances from one vertex of a square with sides of length two to the midpoints of each of the sides of the square is

与所选顶点相邻的两条边的中点到该顶点的距离均为 \(1\)。另外两个中点的横、纵坐标差分别为 \(2\) 和 \(1\)，所以每个距离都是 \(\sqrt{2^2+1^2}=\sqrt5\)。总和为 \(2+2\sqrt5\)。

Therefore, the correct answer is E.

4单选

一个有 \(n\) 项的等比数列首项为一、公比为 \(r\)、和为 \(s\)，其中 \(r\) 和 \(s\) 均不为零。把原数列的每一项替换为其倒数后所得等比数列的和为

English（选项同上方）

Let a geometric progression with \(n\) terms have first term one, common ratio \(r\) and sum \(s,\) where \(r\) and \(s\) are not zero. The sum of the geometric progression formed by replacing each term of the original progression by its reciprocal is

设倒数数列的和为 \(S'\)。乘以 \(r^{n-1}\) 后，原数列各项的顺序恰好反转：\[ \begin{aligned} r^{n-1}S' &=r^{n-1}+r^{n-2}+\cdots+1\\ &=s \end{aligned}\text{。}\]因此 \(S'=\frac{s}{r^{n-1}}\)。当 \(r=1\) 时结论也成立。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Let \(S'\) be the reciprocal sum. Multiplying it by \(r^{n-1}\) reverses the original terms: \[ \begin{aligned} r^{n-1}S' &=r^{n-1}+r^{n-2}+\cdots+1\\ &=s. \end{aligned} \] Hence \(S'=\frac{s}{r^{n-1}}.\) This also holds when \(r=1.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is C.

If \(c\) is a real number and the negative of one of the solutions of \(x^2-3x+c=0\) is a solution of \(x^2+3x-c=0,\) then the solutions of \(x^2-3x+c=0\) are

对于这样的根 \(r\)，\[ \begin{aligned} r^2-3r+c&=0,\\ (-r)^2+3(-r)-c&=0 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]两式相减得 \(2c=0\)。于是第一个方程化为 \(x(x-3)=0\)，其根为 \(0\) 和 \(3\)。

If \(x\) is a real number, then the quantity \((1-|x|)(1+x)\) is positive if and only if

两个因子同时为正恰好发生在 \(|x|\lt1\) 时，此时 \(-1\lt x\lt1\)。两个因子同时为负需要 \(|x|\gt1\) 且 \(x\lt-1\)，这化为 \(x\lt-1\)。两个端点都使乘积为零。

English（选项同上方）

A point in the plane, both of whose rectangular coordinates are integers with absolute value less than or equal to four, is chosen at random, with all such points having an equal probability of being chosen. What is the probability that the distance from the point to the origin is at most two units?

共有 \(9^2=81\) 个可能的点。到原点距离不超过 \(2\) 的点包括原点；四个点 \((\pm1,0)\)、\((0,\pm1)\)；四个点 \((\pm1,\pm1)\)；以及四个点 \((\pm2,0)\)、\((0,\pm2)\)。因此共有 \(13\) 个点符合条件，概率为 \(\frac{13}{81}\)。

There are \(9^2=81\) possible points. Within distance \(2\) are the origin; the four points \((\pm1,0),\) \((0,\pm1);\) the four points \((\pm1,\pm1);\) and the four points \((\pm2,0),\) \((0,\pm2).\) Thus \(13\) points qualify, for probability \(\frac{13}{81}.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is A.

English（选项同上方）

In triangle \(ABC,\) \(D\) is the midpoint of \(AB;\) \(E\) is the midpoint of \(DB;\) and \(F\) is the midpoint of \(BC.\) If the area of \(\triangle ABC\) is \(96,\) then the area of \(\triangle AEF\) is

因为 \(D\) 是 \(AB\) 的中点，而 \(E\) 是 \(DB\) 的中点，所以 \(AE=\frac34AB\)。又因为 \(F\) 是 \(BC\) 的中点，所以它到直线 \(AB\) 的距离是 \(C\) 到该直线距离的一半。因此 \[ \begin{aligned} [AEF]&=\frac34\cdot\frac12[ABC]\\ &=\frac38(96)=36 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

10单选

若 \(m\)、\(n\)、\(p\) 和 \(q\) 都是实数，且 \(f(x)=mx+n\)、\(g(x)=px+q\)，则方程 \(f(g(x))=g(f(x))\) 有解的充要条件为

English（选项同上方）

If \(m,\) \(n,\) \(p\) and \(q\) are real numbers and \(f(x)=mx+n\) and \(g(x)=px+q,\) then the equation \(f(g(x))=g(f(x))\) has a solution

有 \[ \begin{aligned} f(g(x))&=mpx+mq+n,\\ g(f(x))&=pmx+pn+q \end{aligned}\text{。}\]对任意 \(x\)，两边的 \(x\) 项都会消去，所以方程有解当且仅当 \(mq+n=pn+q\)。整理得 \(n(1-p)-q(1-m)=0\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

We have \[ \begin{aligned} f(g(x))&=mpx+mq+n,\\ g(f(x))&=pmx+pn+q. \end{aligned} \] The \(x\)-terms cancel for every \(x,\) so a solution exists exactly when \(mq+n=pn+q.\) Rearranging gives \(n(1-p)-q(1-m)=0.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is D.

11单选

下列哪些陈述与“如果阿尔法行星上的粉红色大象长着紫色眼睛，那么贝塔行星上的野猪就没有长鼻子”这一陈述等价？

\(\mathrm{I}\)．“如果贝塔行星上的野猪长着长鼻子，那么阿尔法行星上的粉红色大象就长着紫色眼睛。”

\(\mathrm{II}\)．“如果阿尔法行星上的粉红色大象没有紫色眼睛，那么贝塔行星上的野猪就没有长鼻子。”

\(\mathrm{III}\)．“如果贝塔行星上的野猪长着长鼻子，那么阿尔法行星上的粉红色大象就没有紫色眼睛。”

\(\mathrm{IV}\)．“阿尔法行星上的粉红色大象没有紫色眼睛，或者贝塔行星上的野猪没有长鼻子。”

这里的“或者”取包含式的含义（数学写作中通常如此）。

English（选项同上方）

Which of the following statements is (are) equivalent to the statement “If the pink elephant on planet alpha has purple eyes, then the wild pig on planet beta does not have a long nose”?

\(\mathrm{I}.\) “If the wild pig on planet beta has a long nose, then the pink elephant on planet alpha has purple eyes.”

\(\mathrm{II}.\) “If the pink elephant on planet alpha does not have purple eyes, then the wild pig on planet beta does not have a long nose.”

\(\mathrm{III}.\) “If the wild pig on planet beta has a long nose, then the pink elephant on planet alpha does not have purple eyes.”

\(\mathrm{IV}.\) “The pink elephant on planet alpha does not have purple eyes, or the wild pig on planet beta does not have a long nose.”

The word “or” is used here in the inclusive sense (as is customary in mathematical writing).

令 \(P\) 表示大象长着紫色眼睛，令 \(Q\) 表示野猪长着长鼻子。原命题 \(P\Rightarrow\neg Q\) 等价于其逆否命题 \(Q\Rightarrow\neg P\)，即 \(\mathrm{III}\)；也等价于 \(\neg P\lor\neg Q\)，即 \(\mathrm{IV}\)。陈述 \(\mathrm{I}\) 和 \(\mathrm{II}\) 都是原命题的逆命题，不一定成立。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Let \(P\) mean the elephant has purple eyes and \(Q\) mean the pig has a long nose. The original statement \(P\Rightarrow\neg Q\) is equivalent to its contrapositive \(Q\Rightarrow\neg P,\) which is \(\mathrm{III},\) and to \(\neg P\lor\neg Q,\) which is \(\mathrm{IV}.\) Statements \(\mathrm{I}\) and \(\mathrm{II}\) are converses and need not follow.

Therefore, the correct answer is B.

12单选

一家超市有 \(128\) 箱苹果。每箱至少有 \(120\) 个苹果，至多有 \(144\) 个苹果。至少有 \(n\) 箱装有相同数量的苹果一定成立时，\(n\) 的最大整数值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A supermarket has \(128\) crates of apples. Each crate contains at least \(120\) apples and at most \(144\) apples. What is the largest integer \(n\) such that there must be at least \(n\) crates containing the same number of apples?

可能的苹果数共有 \(144-120+1=25\) 种。因为 \(128>25\cdot5\)，所以某一种数量至少出现 \(6\) 次。这个界可以达到：让三种数量各出现 \(6\) 次，其余二十二种各出现 \(5\) 次，恰好分配 \(128\) 箱。因此能保证的最大 \(n\) 是 \(6\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

There are \(144-120+1=25\) possible apple counts. Since \(128>25\cdot5,\) some count occurs at least \(6\) times. This is sharp: distribute \(128\) crates so that three counts occur \(6\) times and the other twenty-two occur \(5\) times. Thus the largest guaranteed \(n\) is \(6.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is C.

13单选

如果 \(x\) 头奶牛在 \(x+2\) 天内产出 \(x+1\) 罐牛奶，那么 \(x+3\) 头奶牛产出 \(x+5\) 罐牛奶需要多少天？

English（选项同上方）

If \(x\) cows give \(x+1\) cans of milk in \(x+2\) days, how many days will it take \(x+3\) cows to give \(x+5\) cans of milk?

产奶率为每头奶牛每天 \(\frac{x+1}{x(x+2)}\) 罐。设新的用时为 \(T\)，则 \[ \frac{x+5}{(x+3)T}=\frac{x+1}{x(x+2)}\text{。}\]因此 \(T=\frac{x(x+2)(x+5)}{(x+1)(x+3)}\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The production rate is \(\frac{x+1}{x(x+2)}\) cans per cow-day. If the new time is \(T,\) then \[ \frac{x+5}{(x+3)T}=\frac{x+1}{x(x+2)}. \] Hence \(T=\frac{x(x+2)(x+5)}{(x+1)(x+3)}.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is A.

一个凸多边形的各内角度数成等差数列。若最小角为 \(100^\circ\)，最大角为 \(140^\circ\)，则该多边形的边数为

English（选项同上方）

The measures of the interior angles of a convex polygon are in arithmetic progression. If the smallest angle is \(100^\circ\) and the largest angle is \(140^\circ,\) then the number of sides the polygon has is

平均角度为 \(\frac{100+140}{2}=120^\circ\)，所以内角和为 \(120n\)。凸 \(n\) 边形的内角和为 \(180(n-2)\)，故 \(120n=180(n-2)\)，解得 \(n=6\)。

The average angle is \(\frac{100+140}{2}=120^\circ,\) so the angle sum is \(120n.\) A convex \(n\)-gon has angle sum \(180(n-2),\) hence \(120n=180(n-2)\) and \(n=6.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is A.

用整数 \(d\) 分别除 \(1059\)、\(1417\) 和 \(2312\)，所得余数均为 \(r\)，其中 \(d\) 大于一。则 \(d-r\) 等于

English（选项同上方）

If \(r\) is the remainder when each of the numbers \(1059,\) \(1417\) and \(2312\) is divided by \(d,\) where \(d\) is an integer greater than one, then \(d-r\) equals

除数 \(d\) 同时整除 \(358=2\cdot179\) 和 \(895=5\cdot179\)。这两个数的最大公因数为质数 \(179\)，所以 \(d=179\)。又因为 \(1059=5\cdot179+164\)，所以 \(r=164\)，从而 \(d-r=15\)。

16单选

在三角形 \(ABC\) 和 \(DEF\) 中，边长 \(AC\)、\(BC\)、\(DF\) 和 \(EF\) 都相等。\(AB\) 的长度是 \(\triangle DEF\) 中从 \(F\) 到 \(DE\) 的高的两倍。下列哪些陈述正确？

\(\mathrm{I}\)．\(\angle ACB\) 与 \(\angle DFE\) 必须互余。

\(\mathrm{II}\)．\(\angle ACB\) 与 \(\angle DFE\) 必须互补。

\(\mathrm{III}\)．\(\triangle ABC\) 的面积必须等于 \(\triangle DEF\) 的面积。

\(\mathrm{IV}\)．\(\triangle ABC\) 的面积必须等于 \(\triangle DEF\) 面积的两倍。

English（选项同上方）

In triangles \(ABC\) and \(DEF,\) lengths \(AC,\) \(BC,\) \(DF\) and \(EF\) are all equal. Length \(AB\) is twice the length of the altitude of \(\triangle DEF\) from \(F\) to \(DE.\) Which of the following statements is (are) true?

\(\mathrm{I}.\) \(\angle ACB\) and \(\angle DFE\) must be complementary.

\(\mathrm{II}.\) \(\angle ACB\) and \(\angle DFE\) must be supplementary.

\(\mathrm{III}.\) The area of \(\triangle ABC\) must equal the area of \(\triangle DEF.\)

\(\mathrm{IV}.\) The area of \(\triangle ABC\) must equal twice the area of \(\triangle DEF.\)

设四条相等的边长均为 \(s\)，令 \(AB=2a\)，并设从 \(F\) 作出的高为 \(a\)。在两个等腰三角形中，从 \(C\) 作出的高平分 \(AB\)，从 \(F\) 作出的高平分 \(DE\)。\(ABC\) 的一半是斜边为 \(s\)、一条直角边为 \(a\) 的直角三角形；\(DEF\) 的一半也有相同的斜边和长度为 \(a\) 的高。两个直角三角形全等，只是两条直角边互换。因此相应的锐角互余，完整的顶角 \(\angle ACB\) 与 \(\angle DFE\) 之和为 \(180^\circ\)。相应的半三角形面积也相等，所以两个完整三角形的面积相等。陈述 \(\mathrm{II}\) 和 \(\mathrm{III}\) 正确。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Let the equal sides have length \(s,\) let \(AB=2a,\) and let the altitude from \(F\) be \(a.\) In the isosceles triangles, the altitude from \(C\) bisects \(AB,\) while the altitude from \(F\) bisects \(DE.\) A half of \(ABC\) has hypotenuse \(s\) and leg \(a;\) a half of \(DEF\) has the same hypotenuse and altitude leg \(a.\) The two right triangles are congruent with their legs interchanged. Thus their relevant acute angles are complementary, so the full vertex angles \(\angle ACB\) and \(\angle DFE\) sum to \(180^\circ.\) The corresponding half-areas are equal as well, hence the full triangle areas are equal. Statements \(\mathrm{II}\) and \(\mathrm{III}\) hold.

Therefore, the correct answer is E.

17单选

若 \(\theta\) 是锐角，且 \(\sin2\theta=a\)，则 \(\sin\theta+\cos\theta\) 等于

English（选项同上方）

If \(\theta\) is an acute angle and \(\sin2\theta=a,\) then \(\sin\theta+\cos\theta\) equals

有 \[ \begin{aligned} (\sin\theta+\cos\theta)^2 &=\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta\\ &\quad+2\sin\theta\cos\theta\\ &=1+a \end{aligned}\text{。}\]当 \(\theta\) 为锐角时，正弦和余弦都为正，所以所求和为 \(\sqrt{a+1}\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

We have \[ \begin{aligned} (\sin\theta+\cos\theta)^2 &=\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta\\ &\quad+2\sin\theta\cos\theta\\ &=1+a. \end{aligned} \] Both sine and cosine are positive for acute \(\theta,\) so the sum is \(\sqrt{a+1}.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is A.

18单选

在所附图中，\(AB\) 在 \(A\) 点与圆心为 \(O\) 的圆相切；点 \(D\) 在圆内；\(DB\) 与圆交于 \(C\)。若 \(BC=DC=3\)、\(OD=2\)、\(AB=6\)，则圆的半径为

English（选项同上方）

In the adjoining figure, \(AB\) is tangent at \(A\) to the circle with center \(O;\) point \(D\) is interior to the circle; and \(DB\) intersects the circle at \(C.\) If \(BC=DC=3,\) \(OD=2\) and \(AB=6,\) then the radius of the circle is

延长 \(BD\)，使其越过 \(D\) 后与圆交于 \(E\)。因为 \(BC=3\)、\(BD=6\)，切割线定理给出 \[ 3\cdot BE=AB^2=36\text{，}\]所以 \(BE=12\)、\(DE=6\)。点 \(D\) 的幂满足 \[ \begin{aligned} DC\cdot DE&=(R-OD)(R+OD)\\ &=R^2-OD^2 \end{aligned}\text{，}\]因而 \(3\cdot6=(R-2)(R+2)=R^2-4\)。所以 \(R^2=22\)，且 \(R=\sqrt{22}\)。
