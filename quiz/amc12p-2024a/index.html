求 \(9901\cdot101-99\cdot10101\) 的值。

English（选项同上方）

What is the value of \(9901\cdot101-99\cdot10101?\)

直接计算，\(9901\cdot101=990100\) \(+9901=1000001\) 而 \(99\cdot10101=1010100\) \(-10101=999999\)。它们的差是 \(1000001-999999=2\)。因此正确答案是 A。

English

Directly, \(9901\cdot101=990100\) \(+9901=1000001\) and \(99\cdot10101=1010100\) \(-10101=999999.\) Their difference is \(1000001-999999=2.\) Thus, the correct answer is A.

2单选

一个用于估计沿小路登上山顶所需时间的模型形如 \(T=aL+bG\)，其中 \(a\) 和 \(b\) 是常数，\(T\) 是时间，单位为分钟，\(L\) 是小路长度，单位为英里，\(G\) 是海拔上升高度，单位为英尺。模型估计，如果一条小路长 \(1.5\) 英里并上升 \(800\) 英尺，或者一条小路长 \(1.2\) 英里并上升 \(1100\) 英尺，到达山顶都需要 \(69\) 分钟。

如果小路长 \(4.2\) 英里并上升 \(4000\) 英尺，这个模型估计到达山顶需要多少分钟？

English（选项同上方）

A model used to estimate the time it will take to hike to the top of a mountain on a trail is of the form \(T=aL+bG,\) where \(a\) and \(b\) are constants, \(T\) is the time in minutes, \(L\) is the length of the trail in miles, and \(G\) is the altitude gain in feet. The model estimates that it will take \(69\) minutes to hike to the top if a trail is \(1.5\) miles long and ascends \(800\) feet, as well as if a trail is \(1.2\) miles long and ascends \(1100\) feet.

How many minutes does the model estimate it will take to hike to the top if the trail is \(4.2\) miles long and ascends \(4000\) feet?

由 \(1.5a+800b=1.2a+1100b\) 可得 \(0.3a=300b\)，所以 \(a=1000b\)。于是 \(69=1.5a+800b\) \(=1500b+800b=2300b\)，得到 \(b=0.03\)，\(a=30\)。当 \(L=4.2,\ G=4000\) 时，\(T=30(4.2)\) \(+0.03(4000)\) \(=126+120=246\)。因此正确答案是 B。

English

From \(1.5a+800b=1.2a+1100b\) we get \(0.3a=300b,\) so \(a=1000b.\) Then \(69=1.5a+800b\) \(=1500b+800b=2300b,\) giving \(b=0.03\) and \(a=30.\) For \(L=4.2,\ G=4000,\) \(T=30(4.2)\) \(+0.03(4000)\) \(=126+120=246.\) Thus, the correct answer is B.

3单选

数 \(2024\) 被写成若干个不一定互不相同的两位数之和。至少需要多少个两位数才能写出这个和？

English（选项同上方）

The number \(2024\) is written as the sum of not necessarily distinct two-digit numbers. What is the least number of two-digit numbers needed to write this sum?

每个数至多为 \(99\)，所以 \(k\) 个数的和至多为 \(99k\)。因为 \(99\cdot20=1980\lt2024\)，至少需要 \(21\) 个数。用 \(21\) 个数时，可以取二十个 \(99\) 和一个 \(44\)：\(20\cdot99+44=1980+44\) \(=2024\)。因此正确答案是 B。

English

Each number is at most \(99,\) so \(k\) numbers sum to at most \(99k.\) Since \(99\cdot20=1980\lt2024,\) at least \(21\) numbers are required. With \(21,\) we can use twenty \(99\)s and one \(44\): \(20\cdot99+44=1980+44\) \(=2024.\) Thus, the correct answer is B.

4单选

使 \(n!\) 为 \(2024\) 的倍数的最小 \(n\) 是多少？

English（选项同上方）

What is the least value of \(n\) such that \(n!\) is a multiple of \(2024?\)

分解得 \(2024=2^3\cdot11\cdot23\)。阶乘 \(n!\) 只有在 \(n\ge23\) 时才包含质因数 \(23\)。当 \(n=23\) 时，\(23!\) 已经包含 \(23,\ 11\)，以及足够多的因数 \(2\)，所以 \(23!\) 是 \(2024\) 的倍数。因此正确答案是 D。

English

Factoring, \(2024=2^3\cdot11\cdot23.\) The factorial \(n!\) contains the prime \(23\) only when \(n\ge23.\) At \(n=23,\) the product \(23!\) already includes \(23,\ 11,\) and plenty of factors of \(2,\) so \(23!\) is a multiple of \(2024.\) Thus, the correct answer is D.

5单选

一个含有 \(20\) 个数的数据集，其中有一些数是 \(6\)，平均数为 \(45\)。删去所有的 \(6\) 之后，这个数据集的平均数为 \(66\)。原数据集中有多少个 \(6\)？

English（选项同上方）

A data set containing \(20\) numbers, some of which are \(6,\) has mean \(45.\) When all the \(6\)s are removed, the data set has mean \(66.\) How many \(6\)s were in the original data set?

原数据集的总和是 \(20\cdot45=900\)。设有 \(k\) 个六，删去后剩下 \(20-k\) 个数，总和为 \(900-6k\)，平均数为 \(66\)，所以 \(900-6k=66(20-k)\) \(=1320-66k\)。因此 \(60k=420\)，得到 \(k=7\)。所以正确答案是 D。

English

The full set sums to \(20\cdot45=900.\) Removing \(k\) sixes leaves \(20-k\) numbers summing to \(900-6k,\) with mean \(66,\) so \(900-6k=66(20-k)\) \(=1320-66k.\) Then \(60k=420,\) giving \(k=7.\) Thus, the correct answer is D.

6单选

三个整数的乘积为 \(60\)。这三个整数的正的和的最小可能值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The product of three integers is \(60.\) What is the least possible positive sum of the three integers?

要得到较小的正和，可以取两个负整数 \(-p,-q\) 和一个正整数 \(r\)，其中 \(pqr=60\)。不计顺序时，\(60\) 的正因数三元组为 \[ \begin{gathered} (1,1,60),(1,2,30),\\ (1,3,20),(1,4,15),\\ (1,5,12),(1,6,10),\\ (2,2,15),(2,3,10),\\ (2,5,6),(3,4,5) \end{gathered}\text{。}\] 把最大的因数取作 \(r\) 时，\(r-p-q\) 的正值最小。表中得到的正值为 \(58,27,16,10,6,3,11,5\)；最后两个三元组给出负值。因此最小的正和为 \(10-1-6=3\)。（三个正整数之和至少为 \(3\)，而三个负整数的乘积不可能为正。）因此正确答案是 B。

English

To obtain a small positive sum, use two negative integers \(-p,-q\) and one positive integer \(r,\) where \(pqr=60.\) Up to order, the positive factor triples of \(60\) are \[ \begin{gathered} (1,1,60),(1,2,30),\\ (1,3,20),(1,4,15),\\ (1,5,12),(1,6,10),\\ (2,2,15),(2,3,10),\\ (2,5,6),(3,4,5). \end{gathered} \] A positive value of \(r-p-q\) is smallest when the largest factor is chosen as \(r.\) The positive values from the list are \(58,27,16,10,6,3,11,5;\) the last two triples give negative values. Thus the least positive sum is \(10-1-6=3.\) (Three positive integers have sum at least \(3,\) and three negative integers cannot have positive product.) Thus, the correct answer is B.

7单选

在 \(\triangle ABC\) 中，\(\angle ABC=90^\circ\)，且 \(BA=BC=\sqrt2\)。点 \(P_1\)，\(P_2\)，\(\ldots\)，\(P_{2024}\) 在斜边 \(AC\) 上，并满足 \(AP_1=P_1P_2\) \(=P_2P_3\) \(=\cdots\) \(=P_{2023}P_{2024}\) \(=P_{2024}C\)。

下列向量和的长度是多少？\[ \begin{aligned} &\vec{BP_1}+\vec{BP_2}+\vec{BP_3} \\ &\quad {}+\cdots+\vec{BP_{2024}} \end{aligned}\text{？}\]

English（选项同上方）

In \(\triangle ABC,\) \(\angle ABC=90^\circ\) and \(BA=BC=\sqrt2.\) Points \(P_1,\) \(P_2,\) \(\ldots,\) \(P_{2024}\) lie on hypotenuse \(AC\) so that \(AP_1=P_1P_2\) \(=P_2P_3\) \(=\cdots\) \(=P_{2023}P_{2024}\) \(=P_{2024}C.\)

What is the length of the vector sum \[ \begin{aligned} &\vec{BP_1}+\vec{BP_2}+\vec{BP_3} \\ &\quad {}+\cdots+\vec{BP_{2024}}? \end{aligned} \]

点 \(P_k\) 关于 \(AC\) 的中点 \(M\) 对称，所以把 \(P_k\) 和它的对称点配对可得 \(\vec{BP_k}+\vec{BP_{2025-k}}=2\,\vec{BM}\)。因此整个和为 \(2024\,\vec{BM}\)。在直角三角形中，斜边上的中线长度等于斜边的一半；此处 \(AC=2\)，所以 \(BM=1\)。向量和的长度为 \(2024\cdot1=2024\)。因此正确答案是 D。

English

The points \(P_k\) are symmetric about the midpoint \(M\) of \(AC,\) so pairing \(P_k\) with its mirror gives \(\vec{BP_k}+\vec{BP_{2025-k}}=2\,\vec{BM}.\) Hence the whole sum is \(2024\,\vec{BM}.\) In a right triangle the median to the hypotenuse has length half the hypotenuse; here \(AC=2,\) so \(BM=1.\) The length of the sum is \(2024\cdot1=2024.\) Thus, the correct answer is D.

8单选

有多少个满足 \(0\le\theta\le2\pi\) 的角 \(\theta\) 使得 \(\log(\sin(3\theta))+\log(\cos(2\theta))=0\)？

English（选项同上方）

How many angles \(\theta\) with \(0\le\theta\le2\pi\) satisfy \(\log(\sin(3\theta))+\log(\cos(2\theta))=0?\)

方程表示 \(\sin(3\theta)\cos(2\theta)=1\)，且两个因子都必须为正（对数才有定义）。因为 \(\sin(3\theta)\le1\) 且 \(\cos(2\theta)\le1\)，它们的乘积为 \(1\) 只能在 \(\sin(3\theta)=1\) 且 \(\cos(2\theta)=1\) 同时成立时发生。但 \(\cos(2\theta)=1\) 强制 \(\theta\in\{0,\pi,2\pi\}\)，此时 \(\sin(3\theta)=0\ne1\)。没有角满足条件。因此正确答案是 A。

English

The equation means \(\sin(3\theta)\cos(2\theta)=1\) with both factors positive (for the logs to be defined). Since \(\sin(3\theta)\le1\) and \(\cos(2\theta)\le1,\) their product is \(1\) only if \(\sin(3\theta)=1\) and \(\cos(2\theta)=1\) simultaneously. But \(\cos(2\theta)=1\) forces \(\theta\in\{0,\pi,2\pi\},\) where \(\sin(3\theta)=0\ne1.\) No angle works. Thus, the correct answer is A.

9单选

设 \(M\) 是最大的整数，使得 \(M+1213\) 和 \(M+3773\) 都是完全平方数。\(M\) 的个位数字是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Let \(M\) be the greatest integer such that both \(M+1213\) and \(M+3773\) are perfect squares. What is the units digit of \(M?\)

写成 \(M+1213=a^2\)、\(M+3773=b^2\)，则 \(b^2-a^2=2560\)，即 \((b-a)(b+a)=2560\)。两个因数同奇偶，所以都为偶数。要最大化 \(a\)（也就是最大化 \(M\)），应最小化 \(b-a\)：取 \(b-a=2,\ b+a=1280\)，得 \(a=639\)。于是 \(M=639^2-1213\) \(=408321-1213\) \(=407108\)，个位数字为 \(8\)。因此正确答案是 E。

English

Write \(M+1213=a^2\) and \(M+3773=b^2,\) so \(b^2-a^2=2560,\) i.e. \((b-a)(b+a)=2560.\) Both factors have the same parity, hence both even. To maximize \(a\) (and thus \(M\)), minimize \(b-a:\) take \(b-a=2,\ b+a=1280,\) so \(a=639.\) Then \(M=639^2-1213\) \(=408321-1213\) \(=407108,\) whose units digit is \(8.\) Thus, the correct answer is E.

10单选

设 \(\alpha\) 是 \(3\text{-}4\text{-}5\) 直角三角形中最小角的弧度数。设 \(\beta\) 是 \(7\text{-}24\text{-}25\) 直角三角形中最小角的弧度数。用 \(\alpha\) 表示，\(\beta\) 等于什么？

English（选项同上方）

Let \(\alpha\) be the radian measure of the smallest angle in a \(3\text{-}4\text{-}5\) right triangle. Let \(\beta\) be the radian measure of the smallest angle in a \(7\text{-}24\text{-}25\) right triangle. In terms of \(\alpha,\) what is \(\beta?\)

\(3\text{-}4\text{-}5\) 三角形的最小角满足 \(\tan\alpha=\tfrac34\)。因此 \[ \begin{aligned} &\tan2\alpha=\frac{2\cdot\frac34}{1-\frac{9}{16}} \\ &=\frac{\frac{3}{2}}{\frac{7}{16}}=\frac{24}{7} \end{aligned}\text{。}\] \(7\text{-}24\text{-}25\) 三角形的最小角满足 \(\tan\beta=\tfrac{7}{24}=\cot2\alpha\) \(=\tan\!\left(\tfrac{\pi}{2}-2\alpha\right)\)。所以 \(\beta=\tfrac{\pi}{2}-2\alpha\)。因此正确答案是 C。

English

The smallest angle of the \(3\text{-}4\text{-}5\) triangle has \(\tan\alpha=\tfrac34.\) Then \[ \begin{aligned} &\tan2\alpha=\frac{2\cdot\frac34}{1-\frac{9}{16}} \\ &=\frac{\frac{3}{2}}{\frac{7}{16}}=\frac{24}{7}. \end{aligned} \] The smallest angle of the \(7\text{-}24\text{-}25\) triangle has \(\tan\beta=\tfrac{7}{24}=\cot2\alpha\) \(=\tan\!\left(\tfrac{\pi}{2}-2\alpha\right).\) Hence \(\beta=\tfrac{\pi}{2}-2\alpha.\) Thus, the correct answer is C.

11单选

恰有 \(K\) 个满足 \(5\le b\le2024\) 的正整数 \(b\)，使得以 \(b\) 为底的整数 \(2024_b\) 能被 \(16\) 整除（其中 \(16\) 用十进制表示）。\(K\) 的各位数字之和是多少？

English（选项同上方）

There are exactly \(K\) positive integers \(b\) with \(5\le b\le2024\) such that the base-\(b\) integer \(2024_b\) is divisible by \(16\) (where \(16\) is in base ten). What is the sum of the digits of \(K?\)

这里 \(2024_b=2b^3+2b+4\) \(=2(b^3+b+2)\)，所以 \(2024_b\) 是 \(16\) 的倍数当且仅当 \(b^3+b+2\) 是 \(8\) 的倍数。检查 \(\bmod 8\) 的剩余类可知，\(b^3+b+2\equiv0\) 恰好在 \(b\equiv3,6,7\pmod8\) 时成立。

统计 \(b\) 在 \([5,2024]\)：剩余 \(3\) 给出 \(11,\ldots,2019\)，共有 \(252\) 个；剩余 \(6\) 给出 \(6,\ldots,2022\)，共有 \(253\) 个；剩余 \(7\) 给出 \(7,\ldots,2023\)，共有 \(253\) 个。所以 \(K=252+253+253=758\)，各位数字之和为 \(7+5+8=20\)。

因此正确答案是 D。

English

Here \(2024_b=2b^3+2b+4\) \(=2(b^3+b+2),\) so \(2024_b\) is divisible by \(16\) exactly when \(b^3+b+2\) is divisible by \(8.\) Checking residues \(\bmod 8,\) \(b^3+b+2\equiv0\) precisely for \(b\equiv3,6,7\pmod8.\)

Counting \(b\) in \([5,2024]:\) residue \(3\) gives \(11,\ldots,2019\) (\(252\) values), residue \(6\) gives \(6,\ldots,2022\) (\(253\) values), and residue \(7\) gives \(7,\ldots,2023\) (\(253\) values). So \(K=252+253+253=758,\) and its digit sum is \(7+5+8=20.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

12单选

一个等比数列的前三项是整数 \(a\)、\(720\) 和 \(b\)，其中 \(a\lt720\lt b\)。\(b\) 的最小可能值的各位数字之和是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The first three terms of a geometric sequence are the integers \(a,\) \(720,\) and \(b,\) where \(a\lt720\lt b.\) What is the sum of the digits of the least possible value of \(b?\)

因为各项成等比数列，\(720^2=ab\)，所以 \(ab=518400=2^8\cdot3^4\cdot5^2\)。因为 \(b=\frac{518400}{a}\)，要使 \(b\) 最小，就要找 \(518400\) 中大于 \(720\) 的最小因数。严格位于 \(720\) 与 \(768\) 之间的因数并不存在：如果它的 \(5\) 的指数为 \(2,1\) 或 \(0\)，那么分别除以 \(25,5\) 或 \(1\) 之后，形如 \(2^i3^j\) 的数必须位于 \((28.8,30.72),\ (144,153.6)\)，或 \((720,768)\)；而允许的幂 \(i\le8,j\le4\) 中没有符合者。因为 \(768=2^8\cdot3\) 是因数，所以它是最小可能的 \(b\)。与它配对的因数为 \(a=\frac{518400}{768}=675\)，而 \(b\) 的数位和为 \(7+6+8=21\)。因此，正确答案是 E。

English

Since the terms are geometric, \(720^2=ab,\) so \(ab=518400=2^8\cdot3^4\cdot5^2.\) Because \(b=\frac{518400}{a},\) minimizing \(b\) means finding the smallest divisor of \(518400\) greater than \(720.\) There is no divisor strictly between \(720\) and \(768:\) if its exponent of \(5\) is \(2,1,\) or \(0,\) then after dividing by \(25,5,\) or \(1,\) respectively, a number of the form \(2^i3^j\) would have to lie in \((28.8,30.72),\ (144,153.6),\) or \((720,768);\) the allowed powers \(i\le8,j\le4\) give none. Since \(768=2^8\cdot3\) is a divisor, it is the least possible \(b.\) Its paired divisor is \(a=\frac{518400}{768}=675,\) and the digit sum of \(b\) is \(7+6+8=21.\) Thus, the correct answer is E.

13单选

函数 \(y=e^{x+1}+e^{-x}-2\) 的图像有一条对称轴。点 \(\left(-1,\tfrac12\right)\) 关于这条对称轴的反射点是什么？

English（选项同上方）

The graph of \(y=e^{x+1}+e^{-x}-2\) has an axis of symmetry. What is the reflection of the point \(\left(-1,\tfrac12\right)\) over this axis?

曲线 \(y=e^{x+1}+e^{-x}-2\) 关于其最小值所在的竖直直线对称。令导数 \(e^{x+1}-e^{-x}=0\) 得到 \(x+1=-x\)，所以 \(x=-\tfrac12\)。把 \(\left(-1,\tfrac12\right)\) 关于 \(x=-\tfrac12\) 反射，\(y\) 坐标不变，\(x=-1\) 变为 \(x=0\)。反射点是 \(\left(0,\tfrac12\right)\)。因此正确答案是 D。

English

The curve \(y=e^{x+1}+e^{-x}-2\) is symmetric about the vertical line through its minimum. Setting the derivative \(e^{x+1}-e^{-x}=0\) gives \(x+1=-x,\) so \(x=-\tfrac12.\) Reflecting \(\left(-1,\tfrac12\right)\) across \(x=-\tfrac12\) keeps the \(y\)-coordinate and sends \(x=-1\) to \(x=0.\) The image is \(\left(0,\tfrac12\right).\) Thus, the correct answer is D.

14单选

一个 \(5\times5\) 整数数组中，每一行按顺序的五个数，以及每一列按顺序的五个数，都构成一个长度为 \(5\) 的等差数列。位置 \((5,5)\)，\((2,4)\)，\((4,3)\) 和 \((3,1)\) 上的数分别是 \(0\)，\(48\)，\(16\) 和 \(12\)。位置 \((1,2)\) 上的数是多少？

\[ \begin{bmatrix} \cdot & ? & \cdot & \cdot & \cdot \\ \cdot & \cdot & \cdot & 48 & \cdot \\ 12 & \cdot & \cdot & \cdot & \cdot \\ \cdot & \cdot & 16 & \cdot & \cdot \\ \cdot & \cdot & \cdot & \cdot & 0 \end{bmatrix} \]

English（选项同上方）

The numbers, in order, of each row and the numbers, in order, of each column of a \(5\times5\) array of integers form an arithmetic progression of length \(5.\) The numbers in positions \((5,5),\) \((2,4),\) \((4,3),\) and \((3,1)\) are \(0,\) \(48,\) \(16,\) and \(12,\) respectively. What number is in position \((1,2)?\)

\[ \begin{bmatrix} \cdot & ? & \cdot & \cdot & \cdot \\ \cdot & \cdot & \cdot & 48 & \cdot \\ 12 & \cdot & \cdot & \cdot & \cdot \\ \cdot & \cdot & 16 & \cdot & \cdot \\ \cdot & \cdot & \cdot & \cdot & 0 \end{bmatrix} \]

行和列都为等差数列的网格，其条目可写成双线性形式 \(a(i,j)=\alpha+\beta i+\gamma j+\delta ij\)。四个已知值给出 \[ \alpha+5\beta+5\gamma+25\delta=0\text{，}\]\[ \alpha+2\beta+4\gamma+8\delta=48\text{，}\]\[ \alpha+4\beta+3\gamma+12\delta=16\text{，}\]\[ \alpha+3\beta+\gamma+3\delta=12\text{。}\]

解得 \(\delta=-5,\ \beta=5,\ \) \(\gamma=22,\ \alpha=-10\)。于是 \(a(1,2)=\alpha+\beta+2\gamma+2\delta\) \(=-10+5+44-10\) \(=29\)。

因此正确答案是 C。

English

A grid whose rows and columns are all arithmetic has entries of the bilinear form \(a(i,j)=\alpha+\beta i+\gamma j+\delta ij.\) The four givens yield \[ \alpha+5\beta+5\gamma+25\delta=0, \] \[ \alpha+2\beta+4\gamma+8\delta=48, \] \[ \alpha+4\beta+3\gamma+12\delta=16, \] \[ \alpha+3\beta+\gamma+3\delta=12. \]

Solving gives \(\delta=-5,\ \beta=5,\ \) \(\gamma=22,\ \alpha=-10.\) Then \(a(1,2)=\alpha+\beta+2\gamma+2\delta\) \(=-10+5+44-10\) \(=29.\)

For how many quadruples of integers \((a,b,c,d),\) where \(|a|,\) \(|b|,\) \(|c|,\) \(|d|\le5\) and \(c\) and \(d\) are not both \(0,\) is the graph of \[ y=\frac{ax+b}{cx+d} \] symmetric about the line \(y=x?\)

将 \(y=f(x)\) 的图像关于 \(y=x\) 反射，会得到其反函数的图像，所以图像关于 \(y=x\) 对称当且仅当 \(f\) 等于自己的反函数。对 \(f(x)=\tfrac{ax+b}{cx+d}\)，这有两种情况：\(a+d=0\) 且 \(ad-bc\ne0\)（真正的对合，包括 \(c=0\) 时斜率为 \(-1\) 的直线），或者 \(f\) 是恒等函数 \(y=x\)（\(b=c=0,\ a=d\ne0\)）。

对 \(a+d=0\)，令 \(d=-a\)；行列式 \(-a^2-bc\) 必须非零，所以需要 \(a^2+bc\ne0\)，同时 \((c,d)\ne(0,0)\)。当 \(a=0\) 时，\(b\) 和 \(c\) 都必须非零，给出 \(10^2=100\) 种选择。对每个非零的 \(a\)，先有 \((b,c)\) 的 \(11^2=121\) 种选择。若 \(|a|=1,3,4\) 或 \(5\)，恰有 \(2\) 组满足 \(bc=-a^2\)；若 \(|a|=2\)，则恰有 \(6\) 组。因此真正的对合共有 \[ \begin{gathered} 100+8(121-2)+2(121-6)\\ {}=1282 \end{gathered}\text{。}\] 恒等函数的情形再增加 \(10\) 个（\(a=d\in\{\pm1,\ldots,\pm5\}\)），总数为 \(1282+10=1292\)。

因此正确答案是 B。

English

Reflecting the graph of \(y=f(x)\) over \(y=x\) produces the graph of its inverse, so the graph is symmetric about \(y=x\) exactly when \(f\) equals its own inverse. For \(f(x)=\tfrac{ax+b}{cx+d}\) this happens in two ways: when \(a+d=0\) with \(ad-bc\ne0\) (a genuine involution, including the slope\(-1\) lines when \(c=0\)), or when \(f\) is the identity \(y=x\) (\(b=c=0,\ a=d\ne0\)).

For \(a+d=0,\) set \(d=-a;\) the determinant \(-a^2-bc\) must be nonzero, so we need \(a^2+bc\ne0,\) together with \((c,d)\ne(0,0).\) When \(a=0,\) both \(b\) and \(c\) must be nonzero, giving \(10^2=100\) choices. For each nonzero \(a,\) start with \(11^2=121\) choices of \((b,c).\) If \(|a|=1,3,4,\) or \(5,\) exactly \(2\) pairs satisfy \(bc=-a^2;\) if \(|a|=2,\) exactly \(6\) pairs do. Thus the genuine involutions number \[ \begin{gathered} 100+8(121-2)+2(121-6)\\ {}=1282. \end{gathered} \] The identity case adds \(10\) more (\(a=d\in\{\pm1,\ldots,\pm5\}\)), for a total of \(1282+10=1292.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.
