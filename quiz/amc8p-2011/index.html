1单选

Margie 买了 \(3\) 个苹果，每个苹果 \(50\) 美分。她用一张 \(5\) 美元纸币付款。Margie 收到多少找零？

English（选项同上方）

Margie bought \(3\) apples at a cost of \(50\) cents per apple. She paid with a \(5\)-dollar bill. How much change did Margie receive?

苹果总共花了 \(3 \cdot 50 = 150\) 美分，也就是 \(\$1.50\)。因此 Margie 收到的找零是 \(\$5.00-\$1.50=\$3.50\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The apples cost a total of \(3 \cdot 50 = 150\) cents, which equals \(\$1.50.\) This means that Margie received \(\$5.00-\$1.50=\$3.50\) in change.

Thus, E is the correct answer.

2单选

Karl 的长方形菜园是 \(20\) 英尺乘 \(45\) 英尺，Makenna 的菜园是 \(25\) 英尺乘 \(40\) 英尺。谁的菜园面积更大？

English（选项同上方）

Karl’s rectangular vegetable garden is \(20\) feet by \(45\) feet, and Makenna’s is \(25\) feet by \(40\) feet. Whose garden is larger in area?

Karl 菜园的面积为 \[20 \text{ft} \cdot 45 \text{ft} = 900 \text{ft}^2\text{。}\]Makenna 菜园的面积为 \[25 \text{ft} \cdot 40 \text{ft} = 1000 \text{ft}^2\text{。}\]

两者面积差为 \[1000 \text{ft}^2 - 900 \text{ft}^2 = 100 \text{ft}^2\text{。}\]因此 Makenna 的菜园比 Karl 的大 \(100 \text{ft}^2\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The area of Karl’s garden is \[20 \text{ft} \cdot 45 \text{ft} = 900 \text{ft}^2.\] The area of Makenna’s garden is \[25 \text{ft} \cdot 40 \text{ft} = 1000 \text{ft}^2.\]

The difference of these areas is \[1000 \text{ft}^2 - 900 \text{ft}^2 = 100 \text{ft}^2.\] Therefore, Makenna’s garden is \(100 \text{ft}^2\) larger than Karl’s.

Thus, E is the correct answer.

3单选

一个正方形图案中有 \(8\) 块阴影方砖和 \(17\) 块非阴影方砖。若在该正方形周围加上一圈阴影方砖来扩展图案，扩展后阴影方砖与非阴影方砖的比是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Extend the square pattern of \(8\) shaded and \(17\) unshaded square tiles by attaching a border of shaded tiles around the square. What is the ratio of shaded tiles to unshaded tiles in the extended pattern?

原图案是一个 \(5\times5\) 的正方形。每边向外扩展一块方砖后，得到一个 \(7\times7\) 的正方形，所以新增加的阴影边框有 \(7^2-5^2=24\) 块方砖。加上原来的 \(8\) 块阴影方砖，共有 \(24+8=32\) 块阴影方砖。原来的 \(17\) 块非阴影方砖不变，因此阴影方砖与非阴影方砖之比为 \(32:17\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The original pattern is a \(5\times5\) square. Extending it by one tile on every side makes a \(7\times7\) square, so the new shaded border contains \(7^2-5^2=24\) tiles. Together with the original \(8\) shaded tiles, there are \(24+8=32\) shaded tiles. The original \(17\) unshaded tiles do not change, so the ratio of shaded tiles to unshaded tiles is \(32:17.\)

Thus, D is the correct answer.

4单选

下面是 Tyler 去年夏天九次外出钓到的鱼的数量：\[2,0,1,3,0,3,3,1,2\text{。}\]关于这些数的平均数、众数和中位数，哪一个说法正确？

English（选项同上方）

Here is a list of the numbers of fish that Tyler caught in nine outings last summer: \[2,0,1,3,0,3,3,1,2.\] Which statement about the mean, mode, and median of these numbers is true?

为了更容易求这些值，先得到有序列表：\[ 0, 0, 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 3\text{。}\]

由此可知众数是 \(3\)，中位数是 \(2\)，平均数是 \(\frac{15}{9} = \frac{5}{3}\)。

因为 \(\dfrac{5}{3} < 2 < 3\)，所以有：

平均数 \(<\) 中位数 \(<\) 众数。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

To find these values more easily, we can get the following ordered list: \[ 0, 0, 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 3. \]

From this, we see that the mode is \(3,\) the median is \(2,\) and the mean is \(\frac{15}{9} = \frac{5}{3}.\)

Since \(\dfrac{5}{3} < 2 < 3,\) we get that:

mean \(<\) median \(<\) mode.

Thus, C is the correct answer.

5单选

从 \(2011\) 年一月 \(1\) 日开始，经过 \(2011\) 分钟后是什么时间？

English（选项同上方）

What time was it \(2011\) minutes after the beginning of January \(1,\) \(2011?\)

\(2011\) 除以 \(60\) 的余数是 \(31\)。也就是说 \(2011 = 60 \cdot 33 + 31\)，所以 \(2011\) 分钟等于 \(33\) 小时 \(31\) 分钟。

经过 \(24\) 小时到达一月 \(2\) 日，所以此时已过去 \(9\) 小时 \(31\) 分钟，也就是 \(2\) 日上午。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The remainder when \(2011\) is divided by \(60\) is \(31.\) This means that \(2011 = 60 \cdot 33 + 31,\) which means that \(2011\) minutes is the same as \(33\) hours and \(31\) minutes.

\(24\) hours takes us to January \(2,\) so we get that we are \(9\) hours and \(31\) minutes into January \(2.\)

Thus, D is the correct answer.

下列四个大的全等正方形分别被分成若干个全等三角形或长方形，并有部分区域被涂阴影。阴影面积占总面积的百分之多少？

English（选项同上方）

Each of the following four large congruent squares is subdivided into combinations of congruent triangles or rectangles and is partially shaded. What percent of the total area is partially shaded?

左上和右下的阴影区域各占所在正方形的四分之一。右上占八分之一，左下占八分之三。它们的总面积为 \[\dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{1}{8} + \dfrac{3}{8} = 1\text{。}\]

因此阴影区域合起来等于一个正方形的面积，占四个正方形总面积的 \(25 \%\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The top left and the bottom right shaded regions are both a quarter of each square. The top right is one-eighth, and the bottom left is three-eighths. Their combined area is \[\dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{1}{8} + \dfrac{3}{8} = 1.\]

Therefore, the shaded regions combined equal the area of one square, so they are \(25 \%\) of the total area.

Thus, C is the correct answer.

10单选

Gotham City 的出租车起步价为 \(\$2.40\)，涵盖前 \(\dfrac{1}{2}\) 英里；之后的收费标准为 \(\$0.20\) 每额外 \(0.1\) 英里。你计划给司机 \(\$2\) 小费。用 \(\$10\) 最多可以乘坐多少英里？

English（选项同上方）

The taxi fare in Gotham City is \(\$2.40\) for the first \(\dfrac{1}{2}\) mile and additional mileage charged at the rate \(\$0.20\) for each additional \(0.1\) mile. You plan to give the driver a \(\$2\) tip. How many miles can you ride for \(\$10\)?

小费固定为 \(\$2\)，从总额中减去后剩 \(\$8\)。这大于 \(\$2.40\)，所以再减去前半英里的车费，并把 \(\dfrac{1}{2}\) 英里计入总距离。

现在还有 \(\$5.60\) 可用于额外里程。收费标准为 \(\$0.20\) 每 \(0.1\) 英里，等价于 \(\$2\) 每 \(1\) 英里。因此这笔钱还能乘坐 \(\frac{5.60}{2} = 2.8\) 英里。总距离为 \(2.8 + \dfrac{1}{2} = 3.3\) 英里。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

There is a guaranteed \(\$2\) tip, so we can subtract that from the total, leaving \(\$8.\) This is greater than \(\$2.40,\) so we can subtract that and add \(\dfrac{1}{2}\) miles to the total distance.

We now have \(\$5.60\) to use for additional miles. \(\$0.20\) per \(0.1\) mile is the same as \(\$2\) for \(1\) mile. That means one can ride for \(\frac{5.60}{2} = 2.8\) more miles with this much money. This leaves a total of \(2.8 + \dfrac{1}{2} = 3.3\) miles.

Thus, C is the correct answer.

Thus, C is the correct answer.

设 \(A\) 为边长 \(25\)，\(25\) 和 \(30\) 的三角形面积。设 \(B\) 为边长 \(25\)，\(25\) 和 \(40\) 的三角形面积。\(A\) 与 \(B\) 有什么关系？

English（选项同上方）

Let \(A\) be the area of the triangle with sides of length \(25,\) \(25,\) and \(30.\) Let \(B\) be the area of the triangle with sides of length \(25,\) \(25,\) and \(40.\) What is the relationship between \(A\) and \(B?\)

因为这两个三角形都是等腰三角形，图中从顶点作出的高会平分底边，并把每个三角形分成两个全等的直角三角形。

由勾股定理，面积为 \(A\) 的三角形的高为 \[\sqrt{25^2 - 15^2} = 20\text{。}\]类似地，面积为 \(B\) 的三角形的高为 \[\sqrt{25^2 - 20^2} = 15\text{。}\]

因此 \[ A = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 20 \cdot 30 = 300\text{。}\]同样，\[ B = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 15 \cdot 40 = 300\text{。}\]

所以 \(A = B\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Since these triangles are isosceles, the altitude shown from the apex of each triangle bisects its base and creates two congruent right triangles.

Using the Pythagorean theorem, we get that the altitude of the triangle with area \(A\) equals \[\sqrt{25^2 - 15^2} = 20.\] Similarly, we get that the altitude of the triangle with area \(B\) equals \[\sqrt{25^2 - 20^2} = 15.\]

With these altitudes, we can calculate the areas of the triangles. We get that \[ A = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 20 \cdot 30 = 300. \] Similarly, \[ B = \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 15 \cdot 40 = 300. \]

Therefore, \(A = B.\)

Thus, C is the correct answer.

17单选

设 \(w\)、\(x\)、\(y\)、\(z\) 为非负整数。若 \[2^w \cdot 3^x \cdot 5^y \cdot 7^z = 588\text{，}\]那么 \[2w + 3x + 5y + 7z\] 等于多少？

English（选项同上方）

Let \(w,\) \(x,\) \(y,\) and \(z\) be whole numbers. If \[2^w \cdot 3^x \cdot 5^y \cdot 7^z = 588,\] then what does \[2w + 3x + 5y + 7z\] equal?

为了找到所需指数，注意所有底数都是质数。因此质因数分解会有帮助。

有 \[588 = 2^2 \cdot 3^1 \cdot 7^2\text{。}\]

由此可知 \(w = 2\)、\(x = 1\)、\(y = 0\) 且 \(z = 2\)，其中 \(y = 0\) 使 \(5^y\) 这一项等于 \(1\)。

代入这些指数可得 \[ \begin{gather*} 2w + 3x + 5y + 7z \\ = 2 \cdot 2 + 3 \cdot 1 + 5 \cdot 0 + 7 \cdot 2 \\ = 21 \end{gather*}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 A。

English

To find the desired exponents, note that all the bases are prime numbers. This means that finding the prime factorization will be helpful.

We get that \[588 = 2^2 \cdot 3^1 \cdot 7^2.\]

From this, it is clear that \(w = 2,\) \(x = 1,\) \(y = 0,\) and \(z = 2\) (\(y = 0\) since that makes the \(5^y\) term equal \(1\)).

Therefore, \[ \begin{gather*} 2w + 3x + 5y + 7z \\ = 2 \cdot 2 + 3 \cdot 1 + 5 \cdot 0 + 7 \cdot 2 \\ = 21. \end{gather*} \]

Thus, A is the correct answer.

18单选

一枚公平的六面骰子掷两次。第一次掷出的数大于或等于第二次掷出的数的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A fair six-sided die is rolled twice. What is the probability that the first number that comes up is greater than or equal to the second number?

掷骰子两次时有 \(3\) 类结果：第一次大于第二次、两次相等、第一次小于第二次。第一类和第三类由于对称而概率相同。

第二类发生的概率是 \(\dfrac{1}{6}\)，因为第一次可以是任意数，而第二次必须等于第一次。其余两类的总概率为 \(1 - \dfrac{1}{6} = \dfrac{5}{6}\)。因此每一类的概率为 \(\dfrac{5}{6} \div 2 = \dfrac{5}{12}\)。

所求概率是第一类加第二类，共为 \(\dfrac{5}{12} + \dfrac{1}{6} = \dfrac{7}{12}\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

There are \(3\) possible outcomes when rolling a die twice: the first number is greater than the second, both numbers are equal, or the first number is less than the second number. The first and third outcomes have the same probability since they are symmetric.

The second outcome has a \(\dfrac{1}{6}\) chance of happening, since the first number can be anything, and the second number must equal the first number. The other two outcomes have a combined probability of \(1 - \dfrac{1}{6} = \dfrac{5}{6}.\) This means that each outcome has a \(\dfrac{5}{6} \div 2 = \dfrac{5}{12}\) chance of happening.

The desired probability is the first outcome plus the second outcome, for a total probability of \(\dfrac{5}{12} + \dfrac{1}{6} = \dfrac{7}{12}.\)

Thus, D is the correct answer.

19单选

这个图形中有多少个长方形？

English（选项同上方）

How many rectangles are in this figure?

可以把图形分成这些区域，使统计长方形更容易。

图中的长方形是 \(b\)、\(c\)、\(d\)、\(ab\)、\(bc\)、\(cd\)、\(cf\)、\(de\)、\(abcd\)、\(cdef\) 和 \(bcfg\)。这些共有 \(11\) 个长方形。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

We can split the figure into these regions to make it easier to count the rectangles.

The rectangles in this figure are \(b,\) \(c,\) \(d,\) \(ab,\) \(bc,\) \(cd,\) \(cf,\) \(de,\) \(abcd,\) \(cdef,\) and \(bcfg.\) These form \(11\) rectangles.

四边形 \(ABCD\) 是梯形，\(AD = 15\)、\(AB = 50\)、\(BC = 20\)，高为 \(12\)。这个梯形的面积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Quadrilateral \(ABCD\) is a trapezoid, \(AD = 15,\) \(AB = 50,\) \(BC = 20,\) and the altitude is \(12.\) What is the area of the trapezoid?

可以作如下高，更容易求面积。

用勾股定理可得 \[ DE = \sqrt{15^2 - 12^2} = 9 \] 且 \[ FC = \sqrt{20^2 - 12^2} = 16\text{。}\]

还知道 \[ EF = AB = 50\text{，}\]所以 \[ DC = DE + EF + FC = 75\text{。}\]

因此 \(ABCD\) 的面积为 \[ \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot (DC + 50) \cdot 12 = 6 \cdot 125 \] \[ = 750\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

We can drop the following altitudes to more easily find the area.

We can use the Pythagorean Theorem to get that \[ DE = \sqrt{15^2 - 12^2} = 9 \] and \[ FC = \sqrt{20^2 - 12^2} = 16. \]

We also know that \[ EF = AB = 50, \] so \[ DC = DE + EF + FC = 75. \]

Then the area of \(ABCD\) is \[ \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot (DC + 50) \cdot 12 = 6 \cdot 125 \] \[ = 750. \]

Thus, D is the correct answer.

Students guess that Norb’s age is \(24,\) \(28,\) \(30,\) \(32,\) \(36,\) \(38,\) \(41,\) \(44,\) \(47,\) and \(49.\) Norb says, “At least half of you guessed too low, two of you are off by one, and my age is a prime number.” How old is Norb?

22单选

\(7^{2011}\) 的十位数字是什么？

English（选项同上方）

What is the tens digit of \(7^{2011}?\)

要找十位数字，只需跟踪 \(7\) 的幂模 \(100\) 的结果。因为 \(7^4=2401\equiv1\pmod{100}\)，末两位每四次方重复。

因为 \(2011=4\cdot502+3\)，所以 \[\begin{aligned}7^{2011}&\equiv7^3=343\\&\equiv43\pmod{100}\end{aligned}\] 因此十位数字是 \(4\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

To find the tens digit, it is enough to track powers of \(7\) modulo \(100\). Since \(7^4=2401\equiv1\pmod{100}\), the last two digits repeat every four powers.

Because \(2011=4\cdot502+3\), we have \[\begin{aligned}7^{2011}&\equiv7^3=343\\&\equiv43\pmod{100}\end{aligned}\] Thus the tens digit is \(4\).

Thus, D is the correct answer.

23单选

有多少个 \(4\)-位正整数满足：四个数字互不相同，首位不是零，该整数是 \(5\) 的倍数，并且 \(5\) 是最大的数字？

English（选项同上方）

How many \(4\)-digit positive integers have four different digits, where the leading digit is not zero, the integer is a multiple of \(5,\) and \(5\) is the largest digit?

一个数能被 \(5\) 整除，个位数字必须是 \(0\) 或 \(5\)。

如果个位数字是 \(0\)，另外三位中必须有一位是 \(5\)。剩下两位必须从 \(\{1, 2, 3, 4\}\) 中选择。有 \(6\) 种方式选这两个数字，并有 \(6\) 种方式排列这三位数字，共 \(6 \cdot 6 = 36\) 个数。

如果个位数字是 \(5\)，千位数字有 \(4\) 种选择。选定千位后，其他数字共有 \(4 \cdot 3 = 12\) 种方式填入中间 \(2\) 位。因此此情况共有 \(4 \cdot 12 = 48\) 个数。

合并两种情况，总数为 \(36 + 48 = 84\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

For a number to be divisible by \(5,\) the units digit must be either \(0\) or \(5.\)

If the units digit is \(0,\) one of the other three digits must be \(5.\) The remaining two digits must be chosen from \(\{1, 2, 3, 4\}.\) There are \(6\) ways to choose the pair, and there are \(6\) ways to arrange the three digits for a total of \(6 \cdot 6 = 36\) numbers.

If the units digit is \(5,\) there are \(4\) ways to choose the thousands digit. There are \(4 \cdot 3 = 12\) ways to choose the other \(2\) digits. This leaves a total of \(4 \cdot 12 = 48\) numbers for this case.

Combining both cases, we get the total number of such integers is \(36 + 48 = 84.\)

Thus, D is the correct answer.

25单选

一个半径为 \(1\) 的圆内切于一个正方形，并外接于另一个正方形，如图所示。圆内阴影面积与两个正方形之间阴影面积的比，最接近下列哪个分数？

English（选项同上方）

A circle with radius \(1\) is inscribed in a square and circumscribed about another square as shown. Which fraction is closest to the ratio of the circle’s shaded area to the shaded area between the two squares?

圆内阴影面积等于圆面积减去小正方形面积。内正方形的边长可由勾股定理求得：\[\sqrt{1^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{2}\text{。}\]

因此内正方形面积为 \(\sqrt{2}^2 = 2\)。圆内阴影面积为 \(1^2\pi - 2 = \pi - 2\)。

外正方形面积为 \(2^2 = 4\)，所以两个正方形之间的阴影面积为 \(4 - 2 = 2\)。

所求比值为 \[\dfrac{\pi - 2}{2} \approx \dfrac{3.14 - 2}{2} \approx \dfrac{1}{2}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The circle’s shaded area is equal to the area of the circle minus the area of the smaller square. The side length of the inner square can be calculated using the Pythagorean Theorem to get \[\sqrt{1^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{2}.\]

Therefore, the area of the inner square is \(\sqrt{2}^2 = 2.\) The circle’s shaded area is then \(1^2\pi - 2 = \pi - 2.\)

The area of the outside square is \(2^2 = 4,\) so the shaded area between the two squares is \(4 - 2 = 2.\)

The desired fraction is \[\dfrac{\pi - 2}{2} \approx \dfrac{3.14 - 2}{2} \approx \dfrac{1}{2}.\]

Thus, A is the correct answer.
