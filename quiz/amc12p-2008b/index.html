1单选

一名篮球运动员在一场比赛中投进了 \(5\) 个球。每次进球得 \(2\) 分或 \(3\) 分。该运动员总得分可能有多少种不同的数值？

English（选项同上方）

A basketball player made \(5\) baskets during a game. Each basket was worth either \(2\) or \(3\) points. How many different numbers could represent the total points scored by the player?

总分从 \(5 \cdot 2 = 10\)（全是两分球）到 \(5 \cdot 3 = 15\)（全是三分球）。每把一个两分球换成三分球，总分正好增加 \(1\)，所以中间的每个整数都会出现。

可能的总分是 \(10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15\)，共有 \(6\) 个值。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

The total ranges from \(5 \cdot 2 = 10\) (all two-pointers) to \(5 \cdot 3 = 15\) (all three-pointers). Swapping one two-pointer for a three-pointer raises the total by exactly \(1,\) so every integer in between occurs.

The possible totals are \(10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15,\) which is \(6\) values.

Thus, the correct answer is E.

2单选

如图所示，一个 \(4 \times 4\) 的日历日期方块。先将第二行数字的顺序反过来。然后将第四行数字的顺序反过来。最后，把两条对角线上的数字分别相加。两个对角线和的正差是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A \(4 \times 4\) block of calendar dates is shown. The order of the numbers in the second row is to be reversed. Then the order of the numbers in the fourth row is to be reversed. Finally, the numbers on each diagonal are to be added. What will be the positive difference between the two diagonal sums?

将第二行和第四行反向后，数组的各行是 \(1\,2\,3\,4\)、\(11\,10\,9\,8\)、\(15\,16\,17\,18\) 和 \(25\,24\,23\,22\)。

主对角线和为 \(1 + 10 + 17 + 22 = 50\)，另一条对角线和为 \(4 + 9 + 16 + 25 = 54\)。

正差为 \(54 - 50 = 4\)。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Reversing the second and fourth rows gives the array with rows \(1\,2\,3\,4,\) \(11\,10\,9\,8,\) \(15\,16\,17\,18,\) and \(25\,24\,23\,22.\)

The main diagonal sums to \(1 + 10 + 17 + 22 = 50,\) and the other diagonal sums to \(4 + 9 + 16 + 25 = 54.\)

The positive difference is \(54 - 50 = 4.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

3单选

一个半职业棒球联盟中每队有 \(21\) 名球员。联盟规则规定，每名球员工资至少为 \(\$15{,}000\)，并且每队所有球员工资总和不能超过 \(\$700{,}000\)。单名球员最高可能工资是多少美元？

English（选项同上方）

A semipro baseball league has teams with \(21\) players each. League rules state that a player must be paid at least \(\$15{,}000,\) and that the total of all players’ salaries for each team cannot exceed \(\$700{,}000.\) What is the maximum possible salary, in dollars, for a single player?

当其他 \(20\) 名球员每人都拿最低工资 \(\$15{,}000\) 时，一名球员的工资最大。

因此最大工资为 \(\$700{,}000\) \(- 20 \cdot \$15{,}000\) \(= \$700{,}000\) \(- \$300{,}000\) \(= \$400{,}000\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

One player earns the most when the other \(20\) players each receive the minimum salary of \(\$15{,}000.\)

Thus the maximum salary is \(\$700{,}000\) \(- 20 \cdot \$15{,}000\) \(= \$700{,}000\) \(- \$300{,}000\) \(= \$400{,}000.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

4单选

在圆 \(O\) 上，点 \(C\) 和 \(D\) 位于直径 \(\overline{AB}\) 的同一侧，\(\angle AOC = 30^\circ\)，且 \(\angle DOB = 45^\circ\)。较小扇形 \(COD\) 的面积与圆面积之比是多少？

English（选项同上方）

On circle \(O,\) points \(C\) and \(D\) are on the same side of diameter \(\overline{AB},\) \(\angle AOC = 30^\circ,\) and \(\angle DOB = 45^\circ.\) What is the ratio of the area of the smaller sector \(COD\) to the area of the circle?

因为 \(\angle AOC\)、\(\angle COD\) 和 \(\angle DOB\) 填满直径 \(\overline{AB}\) 上方的平角，所以 \[ \begin{aligned} \angle COD &= 180^\circ - 30^\circ - 45^\circ \\ &= 105^\circ \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

这个扇形占圆的比例为 \(\dfrac{105^\circ}{360^\circ} = \dfrac{7}{24}\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

Since \(\angle AOC,\) \(\angle COD,\) and \(\angle DOB\) fill the straight angle over diameter \(\overline{AB},\) \[ \begin{aligned} \angle COD &= 180^\circ - 30^\circ - 45^\circ \\ &= 105^\circ. \end{aligned} \]

The sector’s share of the circle is \(\dfrac{105^\circ}{360^\circ} = \dfrac{7}{24}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

5单选

一个班级筹集了 \(\$50\) 为住院同学买花。玫瑰每朵 \(\$3\)，康乃馨每朵 \(\$2\)。不使用其他花。恰好花完 \(\$50\) 可以购买多少种不同的花束？

English（选项同上方）

A class collects \(\$50\) to buy flowers for a classmate who is in the hospital. Roses cost \(\$3\) each, and carnations cost \(\$2\) each. No other flowers are to be used. How many different bouquets could be purchased for exactly \(\$50?\)

设 \(r\) 为玫瑰数量，\(c\) 为康乃馨数量，则 \(3r + 2c = 50\)，其中 \(r, c \ge 0\)。

因为 \(2c\) 和 \(50\) 都是偶数，\(3r\) 必须是偶数，所以 \(r\) 是偶数。最大的 \(r\) 是 \(16\)（因为 \(3 \cdot 17 \gt 50\)），所以 \(r \in \{0, 2, 4, \ldots, 16\}\)。

这给出 \(9\) 个 \(r\) 的值，每个值都确定一种花束。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Let \(r\) be the number of roses and \(c\) the number of carnations, so \(3r + 2c = 50\) with \(r, c \ge 0.\)

Because \(2c\) and \(50\) are even, \(3r\) must be even, forcing \(r\) to be even. The largest possible \(r\) is \(16\) (since \(3 \cdot 17 \gt 50\)), so \(r \in \{0, 2, 4, \ldots, 16\}.\)

That gives \(9\) values of \(r,\) each determining a bouquet.

Thus, the correct answer is C.

6单选

邮差 Pete 有一个计步器来记录步数。计步器最多显示 \(99999\) 步，下一步会翻转为 \(00000\)。Pete 计划计算自己一年的里程。一月 \(1\) 日，Pete 将计步器设为 \(00000\)。一年中，计步器从 \(99999\) 翻到 \(00000\) 共四十四次。十二月 \(31\) 日，计步器显示 \(50000\)。Pete 每英里走 \(1800\) 步。下列哪一项最接近 Pete 这一年走过的英里数？

English（选项同上方）

Postman Pete has a pedometer to count his steps. The pedometer records up to \(99999\) steps, then flips over to \(00000\) on the next step. Pete plans to determine his mileage for a year. On January \(1\) Pete sets the pedometer to \(00000.\) During the year, the pedometer flips from \(99999\) to \(00000\) forty-four times. On December \(31\) the pedometer reads \(50000.\) Pete takes \(1800\) steps per mile. Which of the following is closest to the number of miles Pete walked during the year?

每次翻转表示 \(100000\) 步，所以这一年的总步数为 \[ \begin{aligned} &44 \cdot 100000 \\ &\quad {}+ 50000 = 4{,}450{,}000 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

每英里 \(1800\) 步，所以里程为 \(\dfrac{4{,}450{,}000}{1800} \approx 2472\)，最接近 \(2500\)。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

Each flip counts \(100000\) steps, so the year’s steps total \[ \begin{aligned} &44 \cdot 100000 \\ &\quad {}+ 50000 = 4{,}450{,}000. \end{aligned} \]

At \(1800\) steps per mile, the mileage is \(\dfrac{4{,}450{,}000}{1800} \approx 2472,\) which is closest to \(2500.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

7单选

对实数 \(a\) 和 \(b\)，定义 \(a \$ b = (a - b)^2\)。\((x - y)^2 \$ (y - x)^2\) 等于多少？

English（选项同上方）

For real numbers \(a\) and \(b,\) define \(a \$ b = (a - b)^2.\) What is \((x - y)^2 \$ (y - x)^2?\)

因为 \((y - x)^2 = (x - y)^2\)，运算的两个输入都是同一个值 \(t = (x - y)^2\)。

因此 \((x - y)^2 \$ (y - x)^2\) \(= t \$ t\) \(= (t - t)^2 = 0\)。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

Since \((y - x)^2 = (x - y)^2,\) both inputs to the operation are the same value \(t = (x - y)^2.\)

Therefore \((x - y)^2 \$ (y - x)^2\) \(= t \$ t\) \(= (t - t)^2 = 0.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

8单选

点 \(B\) 和 \(C\) 在 \(\overline{AD}\) 上。\(\overline{AB}\) 的长度是 \(\overline{BD}\) 的 \(4\) 倍，\(\overline{AC}\) 的长度是 \(\overline{CD}\) 的 \(9\) 倍。\(\overline{BC}\) 的长度是 \(\overline{AD}\) 长度的几分之几？

English（选项同上方）

Points \(B\) and \(C\) lie on \(\overline{AD}.\) The length of \(\overline{AB}\) is \(4\) times the length of \(\overline{BD},\) and the length of \(\overline{AC}\) is \(9\) times the length of \(\overline{CD}.\) The length of \(\overline{BC}\) is what fraction of the length of \(\overline{AD}?\)

因为 \(AB = 4\,BD\) 且 \(AB + BD = AD\)，所以 \(5\,BD = AD\)，于是 \(BD = \tfrac{1}{5}AD\)。

同样，由 \(AC = 9\,CD\) 且 \(AC + CD = AD\) 得到 \(CD = \tfrac{1}{10}AD\)。

因为 \(B\) 和 \(C\) 的位置都从 \(A\) 起量，所以 \(\;BC = BD - CD\) \(= \tfrac{1}{5}AD - \tfrac{1}{10}AD\) \(= \tfrac{1}{10}AD\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Since \(AB = 4\,BD\) and \(AB + BD = AD,\) we have \(5\,BD = AD,\) so \(BD = \tfrac{1}{5}AD.\)

Likewise \(AC = 9\,CD\) with \(AC + CD = AD\) gives \(CD = \tfrac{1}{10}AD.\)

Because \(B\) and \(C\) both measure from \(A,\) \(\;BC = BD - CD\) \(= \tfrac{1}{5}AD - \tfrac{1}{10}AD\) \(= \tfrac{1}{10}AD.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

9单选

点 \(A\) 和 \(B\) 在半径为 \(5\) 的圆上，且 \(AB = 6\)。点 \(C\) 是小弧 \(AB\) 的中点。线段 \(AC\) 的长度是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Points \(A\) and \(B\) are on a circle of radius \(5\) and \(AB = 6.\) Point \(C\) is the midpoint of the minor arc \(AB.\) What is the length of the line segment \(AC?\)

设 \(O\) 为圆心，\(D\) 为 \(\overline{OC}\) 与 \(\overline{AB}\) 的交点。因为 \(C\) 是弧 \(AB\) 的中点，\(\overline{OC}\) 是弦的垂直平分线，所以 \(AD = 3\)。

在直角三角形 \(ADO\) 中，\(OD = \sqrt{5^2 - 3^2} = 4\)，所以 \(DC = OC - OD = 5 - 4 = 1\)。

然后在直角三角形 \(ADC\) 中，\(AC = \sqrt{AD^2 + DC^2}\) \(= \sqrt{3^2 + 1^2}\) \(= \sqrt{10}\)。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

Let \(O\) be the center and \(D\) the point where \(\overline{OC}\) meets \(\overline{AB}.\) Since \(C\) is the midpoint of arc \(AB,\) \(\overline{OC}\) is the perpendicular bisector of the chord, so \(AD = 3.\)

In right triangle \(ADO,\) \(OD = \sqrt{5^2 - 3^2} = 4,\) so \(DC = OC - OD = 5 - 4 = 1.\)

Then in right triangle \(ADC,\) \(AC = \sqrt{AD^2 + DC^2}\) \(= \sqrt{3^2 + 1^2}\) \(= \sqrt{10}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

10单选

砌砖工 Brenda 单独建一个烟囱需要 \(9\) 小时，砌砖工 Brandon 单独建需要 \(10\) 小时。他们一起工作时聊天很多，合计产出每小时减少 \(10\) 块砖。他们一起工作 \(5\) 小时建完烟囱。这个烟囱有多少块砖？

English（选项同上方）

Bricklayer Brenda would take \(9\) hours to build a chimney alone, and bricklayer Brandon would take \(10\) hours to build it alone. When they work together, they talk a lot, and their combined output is decreased by \(10\) bricks per hour. Working together, they build the chimney in \(5\) hours. How many bricks are in the chimney?

设烟囱有 \(n\) 块砖。Brenda 单独工作时每小时砌 \(\tfrac{n}{9}\) 块，Brandon 每小时砌 \(\tfrac{n}{10}\) 块。一起工作时，他们的效率为 \(\tfrac{n}{9} + \tfrac{n}{10} - 10\)。

工作 \(5\) 小时完成烟囱，因此 \[ 5\left(\tfrac{n}{9} + \tfrac{n}{10} - 10\right) = n\text{。} \] 展开得 \(\tfrac{5n}{9} + \tfrac{5n}{10} - 50 = n\)，所以 \(\tfrac{95n}{90} - n = 50\)，从而 \(\tfrac{5n}{90} = 50\)。

因此 \(n = 900\)。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Let \(n\) be the number of bricks. Alone, Brenda lays \(\tfrac{n}{9}\) bricks per hour and Brandon lays \(\tfrac{n}{10}.\) Together, their rate is \(\tfrac{n}{9} + \tfrac{n}{10} - 10.\)

Working for \(5\) hours completes the chimney: \[ 5\left(\tfrac{n}{9} + \tfrac{n}{10} - 10\right) = n. \] Expanding, \(\tfrac{5n}{9} + \tfrac{5n}{10} - 50 = n,\) so \(\tfrac{95n}{90} - n = 50,\) giving \(\tfrac{5n}{90} = 50.\)

Hence \(n = 900.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

11单选

一座圆锥形山的底部在海底，高为 \(8000\) 英尺。山体体积的顶端 \(\tfrac{1}{8}\) 在水面以上。山底处的海水深度是多少英尺？

English（选项同上方）

A cone-shaped mountain has its base on the ocean floor and has a height of \(8000\) feet. The top \(\tfrac{1}{8}\) of the volume of the mountain is above water. What is the depth of the ocean at the base of the mountain, in feet?

水面以上的部分是一个与整座山相似的圆锥，其体积为总体积的 \(\tfrac{1}{8}\)。由于体积按长度的立方缩放，水上圆锥的高度是全高的 \(\sqrt[3]{\tfrac{1}{8}} = \tfrac{1}{2}\)。

所以水面以上的高度为 \(8000 \cdot \tfrac{1}{2} = 4000\) 英尺。

山底处的海水深度就是水下高度，等于 \(8000 - 4000 = 4000\) 英尺。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

The part above the water is a cone similar to the whole mountain, with volume \(\tfrac{1}{8}\) of the total. Since volume scales as the cube of length, the above-water cone’s height is \(\sqrt[3]{\tfrac{1}{8}} = \tfrac{1}{2}\) of the full height.

So the above-water height is \(8000 \cdot \tfrac{1}{2} = 4000\) feet.

The ocean depth at the base is the submerged height, \(8000 - 4000 = 4000\) feet.

Thus, the correct answer is A.

12单选

对每个正整数 \(n\)，某数列前 \(n\) 项的平均数为 \(n\)。这个数列的第 \(2008\) 项是多少？

English（选项同上方）

For each positive integer \(n,\) the mean of the first \(n\) terms of a sequence is \(n.\) What is the \(2008\)th term of the sequence?

因为前 \(n\) 项的平均数为 \(n\)，所以它们的和为 \(n \cdot n = n^2\)。

第 \(n\) 项是相邻两个部分和的差，\(n^2 - (n-1)^2 = 2n - 1\)。

当 \(n = 2008\) 时，该项为 \(2 \cdot 2008 - 1 = 4015\)。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Since the mean of the first \(n\) terms is \(n,\) their sum is \(n \cdot n = n^2.\)

The \(n\)th term is the difference of consecutive sums, \(n^2 - (n-1)^2 = 2n - 1.\)

For \(n = 2008,\) the term is \(2 \cdot 2008 - 1 = 4015.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

13单选

等边三角形 \(\triangle ABE\) 的顶点 \(E\) 在单位正方形 \(ABCD\) 的内部。令 \(R\) 为所有位于 \(ABCD\) 内部、\(\triangle ABE\) 外部，且到 \(\overline{AD}\) 的距离介于 \(\tfrac{1}{3}\) 和 \(\tfrac{2}{3}\) 之间的点组成的区域。\(R\) 的面积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Vertex \(E\) of equilateral \(\triangle ABE\) is in the interior of unit square \(ABCD.\) Let \(R\) be the region consisting of all points inside \(ABCD\) and outside \(\triangle ABE\) whose distance from \(\overline{AD}\) is between \(\tfrac{1}{3}\) and \(\tfrac{2}{3}.\) What is the area of \(R?\)

令 \(A = (0,0)\)、\(B = (1,0)\)、\(C = (1,1)\)、\(D = (0,1)\)，则 \(\overline{AD}\) 在 \(y\) 轴上，到 \(\overline{AD}\) 的距离就是 \(x\) 坐标。区域位于条带 \(\tfrac13 \le x \le \tfrac23\) 中，该条带在正方形内的面积为 \(\tfrac13\)。

等边三角形 \(\triangle ABE\) 有 \(E = \left(\tfrac12, \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\right)\)，边 \(AE\) 在 \(y = \sqrt3\,x\) 上，边 \(BE\) 在 \(y = \sqrt3(1 - x)\) 上。三角形在条带内的面积为 \[ \begin{aligned} &\int_{\frac{1}{3}}^{\frac{1}{2}} \sqrt3\,x\,dx \\ &\quad {}+ \int_{\frac{1}{2}}^{\frac{2}{3}} \sqrt3(1 - x)\,dx \\ &= 2\int_{\frac{1}{3}}^{\frac{1}{2}}\sqrt3\,x\,dx \\ &= \frac{5\sqrt3}{36} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因此 \[ [R] = \frac13 - \frac{5\sqrt3}{36} = \frac{12 - 5\sqrt3}{36}\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Place \(A = (0,0),\) \(B = (1,0),\) \(C = (1,1),\) \(D = (0,1),\) so \(\overline{AD}\) lies along the \(y\)-axis and distance from \(\overline{AD}\) is the \(x\)-coordinate. The region lies in the strip \(\tfrac13 \le x \le \tfrac23,\) which within the square has area \(\tfrac13.\)

Equilateral \(\triangle ABE\) has \(E = \left(\tfrac12, \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\right),\) with side \(AE\) on \(y = \sqrt3\,x\) and side \(BE\) on \(y = \sqrt3(1 - x).\) The area of the triangle inside the strip is \[ \begin{aligned} &\int_{\frac{1}{3}}^{\frac{1}{2}} \sqrt3\,x\,dx \\ &\quad {}+ \int_{\frac{1}{2}}^{\frac{2}{3}} \sqrt3(1 - x)\,dx \\ &= 2\int_{\frac{1}{3}}^{\frac{1}{2}}\sqrt3\,x\,dx \\ &= \frac{5\sqrt3}{36}. \end{aligned} \]

Therefore \[ [R] = \frac13 - \frac{5\sqrt3}{36} = \frac{12 - 5\sqrt3}{36}. \]

Thus, the correct answer is B.

14单选

一个圆的半径为 \(\log_{10}(a^2)\)，周长为 \(\log_{10}(b^4)\)。\(\log_a b\) 等于多少？

English（选项同上方）

A circle has a radius of \(\log_{10}(a^2)\) and a circumference of \(\log_{10}(b^4).\) What is \(\log_a b?\)

周长是半径的 \(2\pi\) 倍，所以 \[ \log_{10}(b^4) = 2\pi \log_{10}(a^2)\text{。}\]

改写得 \(4\log_{10} b = 4\pi \log_{10} a\)，因此 \(\log_{10} b = \pi \log_{10} a\)。

所以 \(\log_a b = \dfrac{\log_{10} b}{\log_{10} a} = \pi\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

The circumference is \(2\pi\) times the radius, so \[ \log_{10}(b^4) = 2\pi \log_{10}(a^2). \]

Rewriting, \(4\log_{10} b = 4\pi \log_{10} a,\) hence \(\log_{10} b = \pi \log_{10} a.\)

Therefore \(\log_a b = \dfrac{\log_{10} b}{\log_{10} a} = \pi.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

15单选

在一个单位正方形的每条边上，向外作一个边长为 \(1\) 的等边三角形。在每个等边三角形的新边上，再作一个边长为 \(1\) 的等边三角形。正方形和这 \(12\) 个三角形的内部没有公共点。令 \(R\) 为正方形和所有三角形的并集，令 \(S\) 为包含 \(R\) 的最小凸多边形。位于 \(S\) 内部但在 \(R\) 外部的区域面积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

On each side of a unit square, an equilateral triangle of side length \(1\) is constructed. Because \(AB \parallel CD,\) \(\angle A + \angle D = 180^\circ,\) so the bisectors of \(\angle A\) and \(\angle D\) meet at right angles, \(\angle APD = 90^\circ.\) Then the midpoint \(M\) of \(\overline{AD}\) is the circumcenter of right triangle \(APD,\) giving \(MP = MA = MD.\) Thus \(\angle MPA = \angle PAM = \angle PAB,\) where the last equality uses the angle bisector at \(A.\) Therefore \(MP \parallel AB.\) The same argument gives \(QN \parallel AB\) for the midpoint \(N\) of \(\overline{BC}.\) Hence \(M, P, Q, N\) are collinear on the midline.

The midline has length \(\tfrac{AB + CD}{2} = 15,\) while \(MP = \tfrac{AD}{2} = \tfrac72\) and \(QN = \tfrac{BC}{2} = \tfrac52.\) Hence \(PQ = 15 - \tfrac72 - \tfrac52 = 9.\)

Drawing \(AE \parallel BC\) with \(E\) on \(\overline{CD}\) gives \(AE = 5\) and \(DE = CD - AB = 8.\) In \(\triangle ADE,\) \(\cos(\angle AED) = \tfrac{8^2 + 5^2 - 7^2}{2 \cdot 8 \cdot 5} = \tfrac12,\) so \(\angle AED = 60^\circ\) and the trapezoid’s height is \(AF = 5\sin 60^\circ = \tfrac{5\sqrt3}{2}.\)

The segment \(PQ\) sits at half the height, so the hexagon splits into two trapezoids and \[ \begin{aligned} &[ABQCDP] \\ &= \frac{AF}{4}\bigl(AB + CD + 2\,PQ\bigr) \\ &= \frac{\frac{5\sqrt3}{2}}{4}(11 + 19 + 18) \\ &= 30\sqrt3. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is B.
