1单选

求下式的值：\[(-1)^1 + (-1)^2 + \cdots + (-1)^{2006}\text{？}\]

English（选项同上方）

What is \[(-1)^1 + (-1)^2 + \cdots + (-1)^{2006}?\]

因为奇数 \(k\) 时 \((-1)^k = -1\)，偶数 \(k\) 时 \((-1)^k = 1\)，各项交替为 \(-1, 1, -1, 1, \ldots\)。

共有 \(2006\) 项，可分成 \(1003\) 对，每对都是 \((-1) + 1 = 0\)。总和为 \(0\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Since \((-1)^k = -1\) for odd \(k\) and \((-1)^k = 1\) for even \(k,\) the terms alternate \(-1, 1, -1, 1, \ldots\)

There are \(2006\) terms, forming \(1003\) pairs, each equal to \((-1) + 1 = 0.\) The total is \(0.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

2单选

对实数 \(x\) 和 \(y\)，定义 \[x \spadesuit y = (x + y)(x - y)\text{。}\] 求 \(3 \spadesuit (4 \spadesuit 5)\)。

English（选项同上方）

For real numbers \(x\) and \(y,\) define \[x \spadesuit y = (x + y)(x - y).\] What is \(3 \spadesuit (4 \spadesuit 5)?\)

因为 \(x \spadesuit y = x^2 - y^2\)，里面的值为 \(4 \spadesuit 5 = 16 - 25 = -9\)。

于是 \(3 \spadesuit (-9) = 3^2 - (-9)^2\) \(= 9 - 81 = -72\)。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

Since \(x \spadesuit y = x^2 - y^2,\) the inner value is \(4 \spadesuit 5 = 16 - 25 = -9.\)

Then \(3 \spadesuit (-9) = 3^2 - (-9)^2\) \(= 9 - 81 = -72.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

3单选

Cougars 队和 Panthers 队进行了一场橄榄球比赛。两队总共得了 \(34\) 分，Cougars 队以 \(14\) 分的优势获胜。Panthers 队得了多少分？

English（选项同上方）

A football game was played between two teams, the Cougars and the Panthers. The two teams scored a total of \(34\) points, and the Cougars won by a margin of \(14\) points. How many points did the Panthers score?

设 Cougars 和 Panthers 的得分分别为 \(c\) 和 \(p\)，则 \(c + p = 34\)，且 \(c - p = 14\)。

相减得 \(2p = 20\)，所以 \(p = 10\)。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

Let \(c\) and \(p\) be the Cougars’ and Panthers’ scores. Then \(c + p = 34\) and \(c - p = 14.\)

Subtracting gives \(2p = 20,\) so \(p = 10.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

4单选

Mary 准备在杂货店为五件商品付款。这些商品的价格分别为 \(\$7.99\)，\(\$4.99\)，\(\$2.99\)，\(\$1.99\)，和 \(\$0.99\)。Mary 将用一张二十美元的纸币付款。她收到的找零约占 \(\$20.00\) 的百分之几？

English（选项同上方）

Mary is about to pay for five items at the grocery store. The prices of the items are \(\$7.99,\) \(\$4.99,\) \(\$2.99,\) \(\$1.99,\) and \(\$0.99.\) Mary will pay with a twenty-dollar bill. Which of the following is closest to the percentage of the \(\$20.00\) that she will receive in change?

商品价格总计 \(\$18.95\)，所以找零为 \(\$1.05\)。它占 \(\$20.00\) 的百分比为 \[ \frac{1.05}{20}\cdot100\% = 5.25\%\text{。}\]最接近的选项是 \(5\%\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The prices total \(\$18.95,\) so the change is \(\$1.05.\) As a percentage of \(\$20.00,\) this is \[ \frac{1.05}{20}\cdot100\% = 5.25\%. \] The closest listed percentage is \(5\%.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

5单选

John 以每小时 \(3\) 英里的速度向东走，Bob 也向东走，但速度为每小时 \(5\) 英里。如果 Bob 现在在 John 以西 \(1\) 英里处，Bob 需要多少分钟才能追上 John？

English（选项同上方）

John is walking east at a speed of \(3\) miles per hour, while Bob is also walking east, but at a speed of \(5\) miles per hour. If Bob is now \(1\) mile west of John, how many minutes will it take for Bob to catch up to John?

Bob 以每小时 \(5 - 3 = 2\) 英里的相对速度缩短距离。追平 \(1\) 英里的差距需要 \[\frac{1}{2}\] 小时，也就是 \(30\) 分钟。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

Bob closes the gap at a relative speed of \(5 - 3 = 2\) miles per hour. To cover the \(1\)-mile gap takes \[\frac{1}{2}\] hour, or \(30\) minutes.

Thus, the correct answer is A.

6单选

Francesca 用 \(100\) 克柠檬汁、\(100\) 克糖和 \(400\) 克水制作柠檬水。\(100\) 克柠檬汁含有 \(25\) 卡路里，\(100\) 克糖含有 \(386\) 卡路里。水不含卡路里。每 \(200\) 克这种柠檬水含有多少卡路里？

English（选项同上方）

Francesca uses \(100\) grams of lemon juice, \(100\) grams of sugar, and \(400\) grams of water to make lemonade. There are \(25\) calories in \(100\) grams of lemon juice and \(386\) calories in \(100\) grams of sugar. Water contains no calories. How many calories are in \(200\) grams of her lemonade?

整批柠檬水重 \(100 + 100 + 400 = 600\) 克，含有 \(25 + 386 = 411\) 卡路里。

因为 \(200\) 克是整批的三分之一，所以它含有 \[\frac{411}{3} = 137\] 卡路里。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

The full batch weighs \(100 + 100 + 400 = 600\) grams and contains \(25 + 386 = 411\) calories.

Since \(200\) grams is one third of the batch, it has \[\frac{411}{3} = 137\] calories.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

Lopez 先生和 Lopez 太太有两个孩子。他们上家庭汽车时，两个人坐在前排，另外两个人坐在后排。Lopez 先生或 Lopez 太太必须坐在驾驶座。共有多少种座位安排？

English（选项同上方）

Mr. and Mrs. Lopez have two children. When they get into their family car, two people sit in the front, and the other two sit in the back. Either Mr. Lopez or Mrs. Lopez must sit in the driver’s seat. How many seating arrangements are possible?

驾驶员是两位父母之一：有 \(2\) 种选择。

剩下的 \(3\) 人中任意一人可以坐副驾驶座，最后 \(2\) 人坐后排，有 \(2\) 种顺序。

总数为 \(2 \cdot 3 \cdot 2 = 12\)。

English

The driver is one of the two parents: \(2\) choices.

Any of the remaining \(3\) people can sit in the front passenger seat, and the last \(2\) people fill the back in \(2\) orders.

The total is \(2 \cdot 3 \cdot 2 = 12.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

8单选

直线 \[x = \tfrac14 y + a, \qquad y = \tfrac14 x + b\] 相交于点 \((1, 2)\)。求 \(a + b\)。

English（选项同上方）

The lines \[x = \tfrac14 y + a, \qquad y = \tfrac14 x + b\] intersect at the point \((1, 2).\) What is \(a + b?\)

代入 \((1, 2)\)，得到 \[1 = \frac{2}{4} + a \quad\Rightarrow\quad a = \frac{1}{2}\text{，}\] 且 \[2 = \frac{1}{4} + b \quad\Rightarrow\quad b = \frac{7}{4}\text{。}\]

因此 \[a + b = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{7}{4} = \frac{9}{4}\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

Substituting \((1, 2)\) gives \[1 = \frac{2}{4} + a \quad\Rightarrow\quad a = \frac{1}{2},\] and \[2 = \frac{1}{4} + b \quad\Rightarrow\quad b = \frac{7}{4}.\]

Therefore \[a + b = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{7}{4} = \frac{9}{4}.\]

Thus, the correct answer is E.

9单选

有多少个三位偶数满足：从左到右读，它们的各位数字严格递增？

English（选项同上方）

How many even three-digit integers have the property that their digits, read left to right, are in strictly increasing order?

设三个数字为 \(a \lt b \lt c\)，且 \(c\) 为偶数。因为 \(a \geq 1\)，没有数字为零，并且 \(c \neq 2\)（没有两个更小的非零数字可选）。

一旦个位数字 \(c\) 固定，任意两个小于它的不同数字都能按递增顺序唯一排列。因此每个 \(c\) 的计数是 \(\binom{c-1}{2}\)。

对 \(c = 4, 6, 8\) 分别计数，得到 \[ \begin{aligned} &\binom{3}{2} + \binom{5}{2} \\ &\quad {}+ \binom{7}{2} = 3 + 10 + 21 \\ &\quad = 34 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Let the digits be \(a \lt b \lt c\) with \(c\) even. Since \(a \geq 1,\) no digit is zero, and \(c \neq 2\) (there is no room for two smaller nonzero digits).

Once the units digit \(c\) is fixed, any two distinct digits below it can be arranged in increasing order in exactly one way. So the count for each \(c\) is \(\binom{c-1}{2}.\)

For \(c = 4, 6, 8\) this gives \[ \begin{aligned} &\binom{3}{2} + \binom{5}{2} \\ &\quad {}+ \binom{7}{2} = 3 + 10 + 21 \\ &\quad = 34. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is B.

10单选

一个三角形的边长都是整数，其中一条边是第二条边的三倍，第三条边长为 \(15\)。这个三角形的最大可能周长是多少？

English（选项同上方）

In a triangle with integer side lengths, one side is three times as long as a second side, and the length of the third side is \(15.\) What is the greatest possible perimeter of the triangle?

设三边为 \(x\)、\(3x\) 和 \(15\)。三角形不等式要求 \(x + 3x \gt 15\)，所以 \(x \geq 4\)；且 \(x + 15 \gt 3x\)，所以 \(x \leq 7\)。

周长 \(4x + 15\) 在 \(x = 7\) 时最大，得到 \(7 + 21 + 15 = 43\)。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

Let the sides be \(x,\) \(3x,\) and \(15.\) The triangle inequality requires \(x + 3x \gt 15,\) so \(x \geq 4,\) and \(x + 15 \gt 3x,\) so \(x \leq 7.\)

The perimeter \(4x + 15\) is largest when \(x = 7,\) giving \(7 + 21 + 15 = 43.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

11单选

Joe 和 JoAnn 各自在 \(16\) 盎司杯中买了 \(12\) 盎司咖啡。Joe 喝掉 \(2\) 盎司咖啡后加入 \(2\) 盎司奶油。JoAnn 先加入 \(2\) 盎司奶油，充分搅拌后再喝掉 \(2\) 盎司。最后 Joe 的咖啡中奶油量与 JoAnn 的咖啡中奶油量之比是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Joe and JoAnn each bought \(12\) ounces of coffee in a \(16\)-ounce cup. Joe drank \(2\) ounces of his coffee and then added \(2\) ounces of cream. JoAnn added \(2\) ounces of cream, stirred the coffee well, and then drank \(2\) ounces. What is the resulting ratio of the amount of cream in Joe’s coffee to that in JoAnn’s coffee?

Joe 最后加入奶油，所以杯中保留了全部 \(2\) 盎司奶油。

JoAnn 的杯中有 \(14\) 盎司混合物，其中含 \(2\) 盎司奶油。喝掉 \(2\) 盎司会按比例移走 \(\tfrac{2}{14}\) 的所有成分，剩下的奶油为 \[2 \cdot \frac{12}{14} = \frac{12}{7}\] 盎司。

比值为 \[\frac{2}{\,\frac{12}{7}\,} = \frac{14}{12} = \frac{7}{6}\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

Joe adds the cream last, so his cup holds all \(2\) ounces of cream.

JoAnn’s cup has \(14\) ounces of mixture containing \(2\) ounces of cream. Drinking \(2\) ounces removes a fraction \(\tfrac{2}{14}\) of everything, leaving \[2 \cdot \frac{12}{14} = \frac{12}{7}\] ounces of cream.

The ratio is \[\frac{2}{\,\frac{12}{7}\,} = \frac{14}{12} = \frac{7}{6}.\]

Thus, the correct answer is E.

12单选

抛物线 \(y = ax^2 + bx + c\) 的顶点为 \((p, p)\)，且 \(y\) 轴截距为 \((0, -p)\)，其中 \(p \neq 0\)。求 \(b\)。

English（选项同上方）

The parabola \(y = ax^2 + bx + c\) has vertex \((p, p)\) and \(y\)-intercept \((0, -p),\) where \(p \neq 0.\) What is \(b?\)

顶点式为 \(y = a(x - p)^2 + p\)。

当 \(x = 0\) 时，\(y = ap^2 + p = -p\)，所以 \(ap^2 = -2p\)，且 \(a = -\dfrac{2}{p}\)。

展开得 \(y = a x^2 - 2ap\, x + ap^2 + p\)，所以 \(b = -2ap = -2\left(-\dfrac{2}{p}\right)p = 4\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

The vertex form is \(y = a(x - p)^2 + p.\)

At \(x = 0,\) \(y = ap^2 + p = -p,\) so \(ap^2 = -2p\) and \(a = -\dfrac{2}{p}.\)

Expanding, \(y = a x^2 - 2ap\, x + ap^2 + p,\) so \(b = -2ap = -2\left(-\dfrac{2}{p}\right)p = 4.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

13单选

菱形 \(ABCD\) 与菱形 \(BFDE\) 相似。菱形 \(ABCD\) 的面积为 \(24\)，且 \(\angle BAD = 60^\circ\)。菱形 \(BFDE\) 的面积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Rhombus \(ABCD\) is similar to rhombus \(BFDE.\) The area of rhombus \(ABCD\) is \(24,\) and \(\angle BAD = 60^\circ.\) What is the area of rhombus \(BFDE?\)

设菱形 \(ABCD\) 的两条对角线交于 \(O\)。它们互相垂直平分；又因为 \(\angle BAD=60^\circ\)，所以三角形 \(ABO\) 是 \(30^\circ\)–\(60^\circ\)–\(90^\circ\) 三角形。因此两条半对角线满足 \(AO=\sqrt3\,BO\)。

线段 \(BD\) 是 \(ABCD\) 的短对角线，也是相似菱形 \(BFDE\) 的长对角线。因此小菱形与大菱形的长度之比为 \(\frac{BD}{AC}=\frac{BO}{AO}=\frac{1}{\sqrt3}\)。面积按这个比的平方缩放，所以 \(BFDE\) 的面积为 \(24\cdot\dfrac13=8\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Let the diagonals of \(ABCD\) meet at \(O.\) They bisect each other at right angles, and since \(\angle BAD=60^\circ,\) triangle \(ABO\) is a \(30^\circ\)–\(60^\circ\)–\(90^\circ\) triangle. Hence the half-diagonals satisfy \(AO=\sqrt3\,BO.\)

The segment \(BD\) is the short diagonal of \(ABCD\) and the long diagonal of the similar rhombus \(BFDE.\) Thus the smaller-to-larger length ratio is \(\frac{BD}{AC}=\frac{BO}{AO}=\frac{1}{\sqrt3}.\) Areas scale by the square of this ratio, so the area of \(BFDE\) is \(24\cdot\dfrac13=8.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

14单选

Elmo 为筹款活动制作 \(N\) 个三明治。每个三明治使用 \(B\) 团花生酱，每团 \(4\)¢；以及 \(J\) 团果酱，每团 \(5\)¢。制作所有三明治所用花生酱和果酱的成本为 \(\$2.53\)。假设 \(B\)、\(J\) 和 \(N\) 都是正整数，且 \(N \gt 1\)。Elmo 制作这些三明治所用果酱的成本是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Elmo makes \(N\) sandwiches for a fundraiser. For each sandwich he uses \(B\) globs of peanut butter at \(4\)¢ per glob and \(J\) blobs of jam at \(5\)¢ per blob. The cost of the peanut butter and jam to make all the sandwiches is \(\$2.53.\) Assume that \(B,\) \(J,\) and \(N\) are positive integers with \(N \gt 1.\) What is the cost of the jam Elmo uses to make the sandwiches?

以美分计，总成本为 \(N(4B + 5J) = 253 = 11 \cdot 23\)。因为 \(N \gt 1\)，\(N\) 的可能值为 \(11\)、\(23\) 或 \(253\)。

若 \(N = 253\) 则 \(4B + 5J = 1\)；若 \(N = 23\) 则 \(4B + 5J = 11\)；二者都没有正整数解。

所以 \(N = 11\)，且 \(4B + 5J = 23\)，唯一的正整数解为 \(B = 2\)、\(J = 3\)。

果酱成本为 \(N \cdot J \cdot 5\)¢ \(= 11 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 = 165\) 美分，即 \(\$1.65\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

The total cost in cents is \(N(4B + 5J) = 253 = 11 \cdot 23.\) Since \(N \gt 1,\) the value of \(N\) is \(11,\) \(23,\) or \(253.\)

If \(N = 253\) then \(4B + 5J = 1,\) and if \(N = 23\) then \(4B + 5J = 11;\) neither has a positive integer solution.

So \(N = 11\) and \(4B + 5J = 23,\) whose only positive solution is \(B = 2,\) \(J = 3.\)

The jam costs \(N \cdot J \cdot 5\)¢ \(= 11 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 = 165\) cents, or \(\$1.65.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

15单选

圆心为 \(O\) 和 \(P\) 的两个圆半径分别为 \(2\) 和 \(4\)，并且外切。点 \(A\) 和 \(B\) 在圆心为 \(O\) 的圆上，点 \(C\) 和 \(D\) 在圆心为 \(P\) 的圆上，使得 \(AD\) 和 \(BC\) 是两个圆的公外切线。六边形 \(AOBCPD\) 的面积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Circles with centers \(O\) and \(P\) have radii \(2\) and \(4,\) respectively, and are externally tangent. Points \(A\) and \(B\) are on the circle centered at \(O,\) and points \(C\) and \(D\) are on the circle centered at \(P,\) such that \(AD\) and \(BC\) are common external tangents to the circles. What is the area of hexagon \(AOBCPD?\)

两圆外切，所以 \(OP = 2 + 4 = 6\)。在四边形 \(AOPD\) 中，\(OA = 2\) 和 \(PD = 4\) 都垂直于切线 \(AD\)，因此它是直角梯形。

过 \(O\) 作平行于 \(AD\) 的直线，可形成一个直角三角形，斜边为 \(OP = 6\)，一条直角边为 \(PD - OA = 2\)，所以 \(AD = \sqrt{6^2 - 2^2} = \sqrt{32} = 4\sqrt2\)。

梯形 \(AOPD\) 的面积为 \[\frac{1}{2}(2 + 4)(4\sqrt2) = 12\sqrt2\text{。}\]

由对称性，六边形 \(AOBCPD\) 由两个这样的梯形组成，所以面积为 \(2 \cdot 12\sqrt2 = 24\sqrt2\)。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

The circles are externally tangent, so \(OP = 2 + 4 = 6.\) In quadrilateral \(AOPD,\) both \(OA = 2\) and \(PD = 4\) are perpendicular to the tangent line \(AD,\) making it a right trapezoid.

Drawing the line through \(O\) parallel to \(AD\) creates a right triangle with hypotenuse \(OP = 6\) and one leg \(PD - OA = 2,\) so \(AD = \sqrt{6^2 - 2^2} = \sqrt{32} = 4\sqrt2.\)

The trapezoid \(AOPD\) has area \[\frac{1}{2}(2 + 4)(4\sqrt2) = 12\sqrt2.\]

By symmetry the hexagon \(AOBCPD\) is made of two such trapezoids, so its area is \(2 \cdot 12\sqrt2 = 24\sqrt2.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

16单选

正六边形 \(ABCDEF\) 的顶点 \(A\) 和 \(C\) 分别为 \((0, 0)\) 和 \((7, 1)\)。它的面积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Regular hexagon \(ABCDEF\) has vertices \(A\) and \(C\) at \((0, 0)\) and \((7, 1),\) respectively. What is its area?

距离 \(AC = \sqrt{7^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{50}\)。正六边形边长为 \(s\) 时，相隔两个顶点的距离为 \(s\sqrt3\)，所以 \(s^2 \cdot 3 = 50\)，即 \(s^2 = \dfrac{50}{3}\)。

六边形面积为 \[\frac{3\sqrt3}{2}s^2 = \frac{3\sqrt3}{2} \cdot \frac{50}{3} = 25\sqrt3\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

The distance is \(AC = \sqrt{7^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{50}.\) In a regular hexagon with side \(s,\) the distance between vertices two apart is \(s\sqrt3,\) so \(s^2 \cdot 3 = 50,\) giving \(s^2 = \dfrac{50}{3}.\)

The hexagon’s area is \[\frac{3\sqrt3}{2}s^2 = \frac{3\sqrt3}{2} \cdot \frac{50}{3} = 25\sqrt3.\]

Thus, the correct answer is C.

17单选

有一对特殊的骰子，每个骰子掷出 \(1\)，\(2\)，\(3\)，\(4\)，\(5\) 和 \(6\) 的概率之比为 \(1 : 2 : 3 : 4 : 5 : 6\)。掷这两个骰子，总和为 \(7\) 的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

For a particular peculiar pair of dice, the probabilities of rolling \(1,\) \(2,\) \(3,\) \(4,\) \(5,\) and \(6\) on each die are in the ratio \(1 : 2 : 3 : 4 : 5 : 6.\) What is the probability of rolling a total of \(7\) on the two dice?

因为权重之和为 \(21\)，掷出 \(k\) 的概率为 \(\dfrac{k}{21}\)。

总和为 \(7\) 来自 \((1,6), (2,5), \ldots, (6,1)\)，所以概率为 \[ \begin{aligned} &\scriptsize \frac{1 \cdot 6 + 2 \cdot 5 + 3 \cdot 4 + 4 \cdot 3 + 5 \cdot 2 + 6 \cdot 1}{21^2} \\ &= \frac{56}{441} = \frac{8}{63} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Since the weights sum to \(21,\) the probability of rolling \(k\) is \(\dfrac{k}{21}.\)

A total of \(7\) comes from \((1,6), (2,5), \ldots, (6,1),\) so the probability is \[ \begin{aligned} &\scriptsize \frac{1 \cdot 6 + 2 \cdot 5 + 3 \cdot 4 + 4 \cdot 3 + 5 \cdot 2 + 6 \cdot 1}{21^2} \\ &= \frac{56}{441} = \frac{8}{63}. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is C.

18单选

平面上的一个物体从一个格点移动到另一个格点。每一步，物体可以向右、向左、向上或向下移动一个单位。如果物体从原点出发，走一条十步路径，那么终点可能有多少个不同的位置？

English（选项同上方）

An object in the plane moves from one lattice point to another. At each step, the object may move one unit to the right, one unit to the left, one unit up, or one unit down. If the object starts at the origin and takes a ten-step path, how many different points could be the final point?

每一步都会使坐标和改变 \(1\)，所以 \(10\) 步后终点 \((a, b)\) 满足 \(a + b\) 为偶数，且 \(|a| + |b| \le 10\)。任何这样的点都可到达：先走 \(|a| + |b|\) 步到达它，再用剩下的偶数步往返。

可到达的点落在直线 \(a + b = 2k\) 上，其中 \(-5 \le k \le 5\)。每条这样的直线与该菱形恰有 \(11\) 个格点。

共有 \(11\) 条直线，每条 \(11\) 个点，因此共有 \(121\) 个点。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Each step changes the coordinate sum by \(1,\) so after \(10\) steps the endpoint \((a, b)\) has \(a + b\) even, and \(|a| + |b| \le 10.\) Any such point is reachable: walk \(|a| + |b|\) steps to it, then use the remaining even number of steps going out and back.

The reachable points lie on the lines \(a + b = 2k\) for \(-5 \le k \le 5.\) Each such line meets the diamond in exactly \(11\) lattice points.

With \(11\) lines and \(11\) points each, there are \(121\) points.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

19单选

Jones 先生有八个年龄各不相同的孩子。在一次家庭旅行中，他最大的孩子 \(9\) 岁，看到一块车牌上的 \(4\) 位数，其中两个数字各出现两次。她喊道：“看，爸爸！那个数能被我们每个孩子的年龄整除！” Jones 先生回答：“没错，而且最后两位数字刚好是我的年龄。”下列哪一个不是 Jones 先生某个孩子的年龄？

English（选项同上方）

Mr. Jones has eight children of different ages. On a family trip his oldest child, who is \(9,\) spots a license plate with a \(4\)-digit number in which each of two digits appears two times. “Look, daddy!” she exclaims. “That number is evenly divisible by the age of each of us kids!” “That’s right,” replies Mr. Jones, “and the last two digits just happen to be my age.” Which of the following is not the age of one of Mr. Jones’s children?

这个数的形式为 \(aabb\)，\(abab\)，或 \(baab\)。能被 \(9\) 整除说明 \(2(a + b)\) 是 \(9\) 的倍数，所以 \(a + b = 9\)。

八个不同年龄是从 \(1\) 到 \(9\) 的九个整数中的八个，所以年龄 \(4\) 和 \(8\) 中至少出现一个。因此这个数能被 \(4\) 整除。可能性变为 \(1188, 2772, 3636\)，\(5544, 6336, 7272, 9900\)。

因为末两位是 Jones 先生的年龄，\(9900\) 不可能，其余数也都不是 \(5\) 的倍数。所以孩子们的年龄不能包含 \(5\)。事实上，\(5544\) 能被 \(1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9\) 整除。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The number has the form \(aabb,\) \(abab,\) or \(baab.\) Divisibility by \(9\) means \(2(a + b)\) is a multiple of \(9,\) so \(a + b = 9.\)

The eight distinct ages are eight of the nine integers from \(1\) through \(9,\) so at least one of ages \(4\) and \(8\) occurs. Therefore the number is divisible by \(4.\) The possibilities become \(1188, 2772, 3636,\) \(5544, 6336, 7272, 9900.\)

Since the last two digits are Mr. Jones’s age, \(9900\) is impossible, and none of the others is a multiple of \(5.\) So the children’s ages cannot include \(5.\) Indeed \(5544\) is divisible by \(1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

20单选

从区间 \((0, 1)\) 中随机选取 \(x\)。满足 \[\lfloor \log_{10} 4x \rfloor - \lfloor \log_{10} x \rfloor = 0\] 的概率是多少？这里 \(\lfloor x \rfloor\) 表示小于或等于 \(x\) 的最大整数。

English（选项同上方）

Let \(x\) be chosen at random from the interval \((0, 1).\) What is the probability that \[\lfloor \log_{10} 4x \rfloor - \lfloor \log_{10} x \rfloor = 0?\] Here \(\lfloor x \rfloor\) denotes the greatest integer that is less than or equal to \(x.\)

方程表示 \(\lfloor \log_{10} x \rfloor = \lfloor \log_{10} 4x \rfloor\)，也就是 \(x\) 和 \(4x\) 位于同一区间 \([10^n, 10^{n+1})\)。

这恰好在 \(10^n \le x\) 且 \(4x \lt 10^{n+1}\) 时成立，即 \(10^n \le x \lt \dfrac{10^{n+1}}{4}\)。

在 \([10^n, 10^{n+1})\) 内，有利部分所占比例为 \[ \begin{aligned} &\frac{\frac{10^{n+1}}{4} - 10^n}{10^{n+1} - 10^n} \\ &= \frac{\frac{10}{4} - 1}{10 - 1} = \frac{1}{6} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因为这个比例在每个这样的区间上都相同，所以总概率为 \(\dfrac{1}{6}\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

The equation says \(\lfloor \log_{10} x \rfloor = \lfloor \log_{10} 4x \rfloor,\) i.e. \(x\) and \(4x\) lie in the same interval \([10^n, 10^{n+1}).\)

This holds exactly when \(10^n \le x\) and \(4x \lt 10^{n+1},\) that is \(10^n \le x \lt \dfrac{10^{n+1}}{4}.\)

Within \([10^n, 10^{n+1}),\) the favorable fraction is \[ \begin{aligned} &\frac{\frac{10^{n+1}}{4} - 10^n}{10^{n+1} - 10^n} \\ &= \frac{\frac{10}{4} - 1}{10 - 1} = \frac{1}{6}. \end{aligned} \]

Since this fraction is the same on every such interval, the overall probability is \(\dfrac{1}{6}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

21单选

矩形 \(ABCD\) 的面积为 \(2006\)。一个面积为 \(2006\pi\) 的椭圆经过 \(A\) 和 \(C\)，焦点在 \(B\) 和 \(D\)。这个矩形的周长是多少？（椭圆面积为 \(\pi ab\)，其中 \(2a\) 和 \(2b\) 是两条轴的长度。）

English（选项同上方）

Rectangle \(ABCD\) has area \(2006.\) An ellipse with area \(2006\pi\) passes through \(A\) and \(C\) and has foci at \(B\) and \(D.\) What is the perimeter of the rectangle? (The area of an ellipse is \(\pi ab,\) where \(2a\) and \(2b\) are the lengths of its axes.)

设矩形边长为 \(x\) 和 \(y\)。点 \(A\) 在以 \(B\) 和 \(D\) 为焦点的椭圆上，所以 \(x + y = AB + AD = 2a\)。两个焦点之间的距离是矩形对角线，所以 \(\sqrt{x^2 + y^2} = 2\sqrt{a^2 - b^2}\)。

因此 \(2xy = (x + y)^2 - (x^2 + y^2)\) \(= 4a^2 - (4a^2 - 4b^2)\) \(= 4b^2\)，所以 \(xy = 2b^2\)。由矩形面积可得 \(2b^2 = 2006\)，从而 \(b^2 = 1003\)。

椭圆面积给出 \(\pi ab = 2006\pi\)，所以 \(ab = 2006\)，且 \(a = \dfrac{2006}{\sqrt{1003}} = 2\sqrt{1003}\)。

矩形周长为 \(2(x + y) = 4a = 8\sqrt{1003}\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Let the rectangle’s sides be \(x\) and \(y.\) Point \(A\) is on the ellipse with foci \(B\) and \(D,\) so \(x + y = AB + AD = 2a.\) The distance between the foci is the diagonal, so \(\sqrt{x^2 + y^2} = 2\sqrt{a^2 - b^2}.\)

Then \(2xy = (x + y)^2 - (x^2 + y^2)\) \(= 4a^2 - (4a^2 - 4b^2)\) \(= 4b^2,\) so \(xy = 2b^2.\) The area gives \(2b^2 = 2006,\) hence \(b^2 = 1003.\)

The ellipse area gives \(\pi ab = 2006\pi,\) so \(ab = 2006\) and \(a = \dfrac{2006}{\sqrt{1003}} = 2\sqrt{1003}.\)

The perimeter is \(2(x + y) = 4a = 8\sqrt{1003}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

22单选

设 \(a\)、\(b\)、\(c\) 为正整数，且 \(a + b + c = 2006\)，并且 \(a!\,b!\,c! = m \cdot 10^n\)，其中 \(m\) 和 \(n\) 为整数，且 \(m\) 不能被 \(10\) 整除。\(n\) 的最小可能值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Suppose \(a,\) \(b,\) and \(c\) are positive integers with \(a + b + c = 2006,\) and \(a!\,b!\,c! = m \cdot 10^n,\) where \(m\) and \(n\) are integers and \(m\) is not divisible by \(10.\) What is the smallest possible value of \(n?\)

因子 \(2\) 比因子 \(5\) 充足，所以 \(n\) 等于 \(a!\,b!\,c!\) 中因子 \(5\) 的个数，即 \[n = \sum_{k \ge 1}\left(\left\lfloor \tfrac{a}{5^k}\right\rfloor + \left\lfloor \tfrac{b}{5^k}\right\rfloor + \left\lfloor \tfrac{c}{5^k}\right\rfloor\right)\text{。}\]

对每个 \(k\)，\(\lfloor \frac{a}{5^k} \rfloor + \lfloor \frac{b}{5^k} \rfloor\) \(+ \lfloor \frac{c}{5^k} \rfloor\) \(\ge \lfloor \frac{2006}{5^k} \rfloor - 2\)。对 \(k = 1, 2, 3, 4\) 求和（因为 \(2006 \lt 5^5\)），得到 \[ \begin{aligned} &n \ge (401 + 80 + 16 + 3) \\ &\quad {}- 4 \cdot 2 = 492 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

等号可以达到，例如取 \(a = b = 624\)、\(c = 758\)。所以最小值为 \(492\)。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Since factors of \(2\) are more plentiful than factors of \(5,\) \(n\) equals the number of factors of \(5\) in \(a!\,b!\,c!,\) namely \[n = \sum_{k \ge 1}\left(\left\lfloor \tfrac{a}{5^k}\right\rfloor + \left\lfloor \tfrac{b}{5^k}\right\rfloor + \left\lfloor \tfrac{c}{5^k}\right\rfloor\right).\]

For each \(k,\) \(\lfloor \frac{a}{5^k} \rfloor + \lfloor \frac{b}{5^k} \rfloor\) \(+ \lfloor \frac{c}{5^k} \rfloor\) \(\ge \lfloor \frac{2006}{5^k} \rfloor - 2.\) Summing over \(k = 1, 2, 3, 4\) (as \(2006 \lt 5^5\)) gives \[ \begin{aligned} &n \ge (401 + 80 + 16 + 3) \\ &\quad {}- 4 \cdot 2 = 492. \end{aligned} \]

Equality is attainable, for example with \(a = b = 624\) and \(c = 758.\) So the minimum is \(492.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

等腰 \(\triangle ABC\) 在 \(C\) 处为直角。点 \(P\) 在 \(\triangle ABC\) 内部，且 \(PA = 11\)、\(PB = 7\)、\(PC = 6\)。直角边 \(\overline{AC}\) 和 \(\overline{BC}\) 的长度为 \(s = \sqrt{a + b\sqrt{2}}\)，其中 \(a\) 和 \(b\) 为正整数。求 \(a + b\)。

English（选项同上方）

Isosceles \(\triangle ABC\) has a right angle at \(C.\) Point \(P\) is inside \(\triangle ABC,\) such that \(PA = 11,\) \(PB = 7,\) and \(PC = 6.\) Legs \(\overline{AC}\) and \(\overline{BC}\) have length \(s = \sqrt{a + b\sqrt{2}},\) where \(a\) and \(b\) are positive integers. What is \(a + b?\)

将 \(\triangle ABC\) 绕 \(C\) 旋转 \(90^\circ\)，使 \(A\) 映到 \(B\)，\(P\) 映到 \(P'\)。则 \(CP' = CP = 6\)，且 \(\angle PCP' = 90^\circ\)，所以 \(\triangle PCP'\) 是等腰直角三角形，\(PP' = 6\sqrt2\)。

另外 \(BP' = AP = 11\)。因为 \((6\sqrt2)^2 + 7^2 = 72 + 49\) \(= 121 = 11^2\)，三角形 \(BPP'\) 在 \(P\) 处为直角。因此 \(\angle BPC = \angle BPP'\) \(+ \angle P'PC = 90^\circ\) \(+ 45^\circ = 135^\circ\)。

所以 \(s^2 = 85 + 42\sqrt2\)，得到 \(a = 85\)、\(b = 42\)，因此 \(a + b = 127\)。

English

Also \(BP' = AP = 11.\) Since \((6\sqrt2)^2 + 7^2 = 72 + 49\) \(= 121 = 11^2,\) triangle \(BPP'\) has a right angle at \(P.\) Hence \(\angle BPC = \angle BPP'\) \(+ \angle P'PC = 90^\circ\) \(+ 45^\circ = 135^\circ.\)

By the Law of Cosines in \(\triangle BPC,\) \[ \begin{aligned} &BC^2 = 6^2 + 7^2 \\ &\quad {}- 2 \cdot 6 \cdot 7 \cos 135^\circ \\ &\quad = 85 + 42\sqrt2. \end{aligned} \]

So \(s^2 = 85 + 42\sqrt2,\) giving \(a = 85,\) \(b = 42,\) and \(a + b = 127.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

24单选

设 \(S\) 为坐标平面中所有满足 \(0 \le x \le \dfrac{\pi}{2}\) 且 \(0 \le y \le \dfrac{\pi}{2}\) 的点 \((x, y)\) 的集合。\(S\) 中满足下式的子集的面积是多少\[\sin^2 x - \sin x \sin y + \sin^2 y \le \frac{3}{4}\text{？}\]

English（选项同上方）

Let \(S\) be the set of all points \((x, y)\) in the coordinate plane such that \(0 \le x \le \dfrac{\pi}{2}\) and \(0 \le y \le \dfrac{\pi}{2}.\) What is the area of the subset of \(S\) for which \[\sin^2 x - \sin x \sin y + \sin^2 y \le \frac{3}{4}?\]

固定 \(y\)，把 \(\sin^2 x - \sin x \sin y + \sin^2 y = \dfrac34\) 看作关于 \(\sin x\) 的二次方程：\[ \begin{aligned} &\sin x = \frac{1}{2}\sin y \\ &\quad {}\pm \frac{\sqrt3}{2}\cos y \\ &\quad = \sin\!\left(y \pm \frac{\pi}{3}\right) \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

在 \(S\) 内，\(\sin x = \sin\!\left(y - \tfrac{\pi}{3}\right)\) 给出直线 \(x = y - \tfrac{\pi}{3}\)；而 \(\sin x = \sin\!\left(y + \tfrac{\pi}{3}\right)\) 在 \(y \le \tfrac{\pi}{6}\) 时给出 \(x = y + \tfrac{\pi}{3}\)，在 \(y \ge \tfrac{\pi}{6}\) 时给出 \(x = -y + \tfrac{2\pi}{3}\)。

这些直线把 \(S\) 分成若干区域；检验角点可知不等式只在中间带状区域成立。其面积为 \[ \begin{aligned} &\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right)^2 - \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{\pi}{3}\right)^2 \\ &\quad {}- 2 \cdot \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{\pi}{6}\right)^2 \\ &\quad = \frac{\pi^2}{6} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Fixing \(y,\) solve \(\sin^2 x - \sin x \sin y + \sin^2 y = \dfrac34\) as a quadratic in \(\sin x:\) \[ \begin{aligned} &\sin x = \frac{1}{2}\sin y \\ &\quad {}\pm \frac{\sqrt3}{2}\cos y \\ &\quad = \sin\!\left(y \pm \frac{\pi}{3}\right). \end{aligned} \]

Within \(S,\) \(\sin x = \sin\!\left(y - \tfrac{\pi}{3}\right)\) gives the line \(x = y - \tfrac{\pi}{3},\) while \(\sin x = \sin\!\left(y + \tfrac{\pi}{3}\right)\) gives \(x = y + \tfrac{\pi}{3}\) for \(y \le \tfrac{\pi}{6}\) and \(x = -y + \tfrac{2\pi}{3}\) for \(y \ge \tfrac{\pi}{6}.\)

These lines split \(S\) into regions; testing the corners shows the inequality holds only in the middle band. Its area is \[ \begin{aligned} &\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right)^2 - \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{\pi}{3}\right)^2 \\ &\quad {}- 2 \cdot \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{\pi}{6}\right)^2 \\ &\quad = \frac{\pi^2}{6}. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is C.

25单选

非负整数序列 \(a_1\)，\(a_2\)，\(\ldots\) 由规则 \(a_{n+2} = |a_{n+1} - a_n|\)（\(n \ge 1\)）定义。若 \(a_1 = 999\)、\(a_2 \lt 999\)，且 \(a_{2006} = 1\)，那么 \(a_2\) 可能有多少个不同的值？

English（选项同上方）

A sequence \(a_1,\) \(a_2,\) \(\ldots\) of non-negative integers is defined by the rule \(a_{n+2} = |a_{n+1} - a_n|\) for \(n \ge 1.\) If \(a_1 = 999,\) \(a_2 \lt 999,\) and \(a_{2006} = 1,\) how many different values of \(a_2\) are possible?

递推规则给出 \(a_n \equiv a_{n+3} \pmod 2\)，所以 \(a_2\) 与 \(a_{2006} = 1\) 的奇偶性相同，因此 \(a_2\) 是奇数。

每一项都是 \(\gcd(a_1, a_2)\) 的倍数，而 \(a_{2006} = 1\) 迫使 \(\gcd(999, a_2) = 1\)。因为 \(999 = 3^3 \cdot 37\)，所以 \(a_2\) 不能被 \(3\) 或 \(37\) 整除。

区间 \([1, 998]\) 中有 \(499\) 个奇数；去掉 \(166\) 个 \(3\) 的倍数和 \(13\) 个 \(37\) 的倍数，再加回 \(4\) 个 \(111\) 的倍数，得到 \[499 - 166 - 13 + 4 = 324\text{。}\]

每个这样的 \(a_2\) 都可行。对连续正项 \(u,v\)，更新 \((u,v)\mapsto(v,|v-u|)\) 至多每两步就会减小两者的最大值。因为初始两项都不超过 \(999\)，所以某个 \(a_N=0\) 会在 \(N\le1999\) 时出现。

每对连续项的最大公因数不变，所以零之前的两个相等项都等于 \(\gcd(999,a_2)=1\)。此后序列循环经过 \(1,1,0\)。最后，\(2006\equiv2\pmod3\)，所以 \(a_{2006}\) 与 \(a_2\) 同为奇数；在这个循环中，它必定为 \(1\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The rule gives \(a_n \equiv a_{n+3} \pmod 2,\) so \(a_2\) has the same parity as \(a_{2006} = 1;\) thus \(a_2\) is odd.

Every term is a multiple of \(\gcd(a_1, a_2),\) and \(a_{2006} = 1\) forces \(\gcd(999, a_2) = 1.\) Since \(999 = 3^3 \cdot 37,\) we need \(a_2\) not divisible by \(3\) or \(37.\)

Among the odd integers in \([1, 998]\) there are \(499;\) removing the \(166\) multiples of \(3\) and \(13\) multiples of \(37,\) then adding back the \(4\) multiples of \(111,\) leaves \[499 - 166 - 13 + 4 = 324.\]

Each such \(a_2\) works. For consecutive positive terms \(u,v,\) the update \((u,v)\mapsto(v,|v-u|)\) reduces their maximum within at most two steps. Since both initial terms are at most \(999,\) some \(a_N=0\) occurs by \(N\le1999.\)

The gcd of each consecutive pair is invariant, so the equal terms immediately before that zero both equal \(\gcd(999,a_2)=1.\) The sequence then cycles through \(1,1,0.\) Finally, \(2006\equiv2\pmod3,\) so \(a_{2006}\) has the same odd parity as \(a_2;\) in this cycle it must therefore be \(1.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.
