1单选

一个男孩以 \(10\) 美分买 \(3\) 个橙子的价格进货，再以 \(20\) 美分卖 \(5\) 个的价格出售。要获利 \(\$1.00\)，他必须卖出：

English（选项同上方）

A boy buys oranges at \(3\) for \(10\) cents. He will sell them at \(5\) for \(20\) cents. In order to make a profit of \(\$1.00,\) he must sell:

每个橙子的进价为 \(\frac{10}{3}\) 美分，售价为 \(4\) 美分，所以每个橙子的利润为 \(4-\frac{10}{3}=\frac{2}{3}\) 美分。要赚取 \(100\) 美分，他必须卖出 \[ \frac{100}{\frac{2}{3}}=150 \] 个橙子。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Each orange costs \(\frac{10}{3}\) cents and sells for \(4\) cents, so the profit is \(4-\frac{10}{3}=\frac{2}{3}\) cent per orange. To earn \(100\) cents, he must sell \[ \frac{100}{\frac{2}{3}}=150 \] oranges.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

English（选项同上方）

A refrigerator is offered for sale at \(\$250.00\) less successive discounts of \(20\%\) and \(15\%.\) The sale price of the refrigerator is:

两次折扣后剩下原价的 \[ (0.80)(0.85)=0.68\text{。}\]因此，售价为 \(\$250.00\) 的 \(68\%\)，即 \(\$170.00\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

The two discounts leave \[ (0.80)(0.85)=0.68 \] of the original price. The sale price is therefore \(68\%\) of \(\$250.00,\) or \(\$170.00.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

English（选项同上方）

二十五美分硬币的枚数之差为 \[ (5q+1)-(q+5)=4q-4\text{。}\]每枚二十五美分硬币折合 \(\frac{5}{2}\) 枚十美分硬币，所以按此换算，得到 \[ \frac52(4q-4)=10(q-1)\text{。}\]

The fraction \[ \frac{\sqrt{a^2+x^2}-\dfrac{x^2-a^2}{\sqrt{a^2+x^2}}}{a^2+x^2} \] reduces to:

原分式的分子为 \[ \begin{aligned} &\frac{a^2+x^2-(x^2-a^2)} {\sqrt{a^2+x^2}}\\ &\qquad{}=\frac{2a^2}{\sqrt{a^2+x^2}} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]再除以 \(a^2+x^2\)，得到 \[ \frac{2a^2}{(a^2+x^2)^{\frac{3}{2}}}\text{。}\]

Thus, the correct answer is D.

The value of \(x\) at the intersection of \(y=\dfrac8{x^2+4}\) and \(x+y=2\) is:

代入后得到 \[ (2-x)(x^2+4)=8\text{。}\]展开并化简，得到 \[ x(x^2-2x+4)=0\text{。}\]二次因式的判别式为负，因此实数交点满足 \(x=0\)。

English

Thus, the correct answer is C.

14单选

两个圆中，较大圆的圆心为 \(P\)，半径为 \(p\)；较小圆的圆心为 \(Q\)，半径为 \(q\)。连接 \(\overline{PQ}\)。下列哪个说法是错误的？

English（选项同上方）

Given the larger of two circles with center \(P\) and radius \(p\) and the smaller with center \(Q\) and radius \(q.\) Draw \(\overline{PQ}.\) Which of the following statements is false?

两圆内切时有 \(PQ=p-q\)，外切时有 \(PQ=p+q\)。两圆相离时可以有 \(PQ\gt p+q\)，而许多相交或相离的情形满足 \(PQ\gt p-q\)。因此 A 至 D 中的每种情况都可能发生，没有一个说法是错误的。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

Internal tangency realizes \(PQ=p-q,\) and external tangency realizes \(PQ=p+q.\) Disjoint circles can have \(PQ\gt p+q,\) and many intersecting or disjoint configurations have \(PQ\gt p-q.\) Thus each of A through D can occur, so none of them is false.

Therefore, the correct answer is E.

15单选

从一块正方形金属片中切出尽可能大的圆形金属片，再从该圆形金属片中切出尽可能大的正方形金属片。总共浪费的金属面积为：

English（选项同上方）

A circular piece of metal of maximum size is cut out of a square piece and then a square piece of maximum size is cut out of the circular piece. The total amount of metal wasted is:

设原正方形的边长为 \(s\)。其内切圆的直径为 \(s\)。这个直径就是从圆中切出的最大正方形的对角线，因此最后得到的正方形边长为 \(\frac{s}{\sqrt2}\)，面积为 \(\frac{s^2}{2}\)。两次切割后留下的是这个正方形，所以浪费的总面积为 \(s^2-\frac{s^2}{2}=\frac{s^2}{2}\)。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Let the original square have side \(s.\) The inscribed circle has diameter \(s.\) That diameter is the diagonal of the largest square cut from the circle, so the final square has side \(\frac{s}{\sqrt2}\) and area \(\frac{s^2}{2}.\) The material left after both cuts is this final square, so the total waste is \(s^2-\frac{s^2}{2}=\frac{s^2}{2}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

The correct answer is B.

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

Reflect \(B=(2,1)\) across the \(y\)-axis to \(B'=(-2,1).\) For \(C\) on the \(y\)-axis, \(BC=B'C,\) so \(AC+BC\) is minimized when \(A,C,B'\) are collinear. The line from \(A=(5,5)\) to \(B'=(-2,1)\) has slope \(\frac{4}{7}.\) At \(x=0,\) its height is \[ 5-\frac47(5)=\frac{15}{7}=2\frac17. \] Hence \(k=\frac{15}{7}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

一个三角形的一条边被内切圆的切点分成长为 \(6\) 和 \(8\) 个单位的两段。若内切圆半径为 \(4\)，则三角形最短边的长度为：

English（选项同上方）

One of the sides of a triangle is divided into segments of \(6\) and \(8\) units by the point of tangency of the inscribed circle. If the radius of the circle is \(4,\) then the length of the shortest side of the triangle is:

设从第三个顶点引出的两条切线段长均为 \(z\)。由同一点引出的切线段相等，所以三边长为 \[ 14,\qquad 6+z,\qquad 8+z\text{，}\]半周长为 \(s=14+z\)。因为内切圆半径为 \(4\)， \[ K=rs=4(14+z)\text{。}\]由海伦公式， \[ \begin{aligned} K^2 &=(14+z)z\cdot8\cdot6\\ &=48z(14+z) \end{aligned}\text{。}\]将两种面积表达式平方后相等，得到 \(16(14+z)=48z\)，所以 \(z=7\)。三边长为 \(14\)、\(13\) 和 \(15\)，最短边长为 \(13\)。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Let the two tangent segments from the third vertex each have length \(z.\) Equal tangents from a common vertex make the side lengths \[ 14,\qquad 6+z,\qquad 8+z, \] with semiperimeter \(s=14+z.\) Since the inradius is \(4,\) \[ K=rs=4(14+z). \] Heron’s formula gives \[ \begin{aligned} K^2 &=(14+z)z\cdot8\cdot6\\ &=48z(14+z). \end{aligned} \] Equating the squares yields \(16(14+z)=48z,\) so \(z=7.\) The sides are \(14,\) \(13,\) and \(15,\) and the shortest is \(13.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.
