Therefore, the correct answer is C.

10单选

某人开车前往海滨，\(150\) 英里的路程用时 \(3\) 小时 \(20\) 分钟。他从海滨返回出发点用时 \(4\) 小时 \(10\) 分钟。设全程平均速度为 \(r\)。则去程平均速度比 \(r\) 高多少英里每小时？

English（选项同上方）

A man drives \(150\) miles to the seashore in \(3\) hours and \(20\) minutes. He returns from the shore to the starting point in \(4\) hours and \(10\) minutes. Let \(r\) be the average rate for the entire trip. Then the average rate for the trip going exceeds \(r,\) in miles per hour, by:

去程速度为每小时 \(\frac{150}{\frac{10}{3}}=45\) 英里。全程在 \[ 3\frac13+4\frac16=7\frac12 \] 小时内行驶 \(300\) 英里，所以 \(r=\frac{300}{\frac{15}{2}}=40\)。速度之差为 \(45-40=5\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The outbound rate is \(\frac{150}{\frac{10}{3}}=45\) miles per hour. The entire trip covers \(300\) miles in \[ 3\frac13+4\frac16=7\frac12 \] hours, so \(r=\frac{300}{\frac{15}{2}}=40.\) The difference is \(45-40=5.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

13单选

\(R\) 与 \(S\) 成正比，与 \(T\) 成反比。当 \(R=\dfrac43\) 且 \(T=\dfrac9{14}\) 时，\(S=\dfrac37\)。求当 \(R=\sqrt{48}\) 且 \(T=\sqrt{75}\) 时的 \(S\)。

English（选项同上方）

\(R\) varies directly as \(S\) and inversely as \(T.\) When \(R=\dfrac43\) and \(T=\dfrac9{14},\) \(S=\dfrac37.\) Find \(S\) when \(R=\sqrt{48}\) and \(T=\sqrt{75}.\)

因为 \(R=\frac{kS}{T}\)，所以 \(S=\frac{RT}{k}\)。于是 \[ \begin{aligned} \frac{S_2}{S_1} &=\frac{R_2T_2}{R_1T_1}\\ &=\frac{\sqrt{48}\sqrt{75}} {(\frac{4}{3})(\frac{9}{14})}\\ &=\frac{60}{\frac{6}{7}}=70 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]因此 \(S_2=(\frac{3}{7})\cdot70=30\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Because \(R=\frac{kS}{T},\) we have \(S=\frac{RT}{k}.\) Hence \[ \begin{aligned} \frac{S_2}{S_1} &=\frac{R_2T_2}{R_1T_1}\\ &=\frac{\sqrt{48}\sqrt{75}} {(\frac{4}{3})(\frac{9}{14})}\\ &=\frac{60}{\frac{6}{7}}=70. \end{aligned} \] Therefore \(S_2=(\frac{3}{7})\cdot70=30.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

已知三角形 \(ABC\) 的底边 \(AB\) 的长度与位置固定。当顶点 \(C\) 沿一条直线移动时，三条中线的交点沿下列哪种轨迹移动：

English（选项同上方）

Given triangle \(ABC\) with base \(AB\) fixed in length and position. As the vertex \(C\) moves on a straight line, the intersection point of the three medians moves on:

也用 \(A\)、\(B\) 和 \(C\) 表示相应的位置向量。重心为 \[ G=\frac{A+B+C}{3}\text{。}\]因为 \(A\) 和 \(B\) 固定，所以这是将 \(C\) 的位置按 \(\frac{1}{3}\) 缩放后再平移。因此直线轨迹仍映射成直线轨迹。

Thus, the correct answer is D.

在三角形 \(ABC\) 中，\(CD\) 是 \(AB\) 边上的高，\(AE\) 是 \(BC\) 边上的高。若 \(AB\)、\(CD\) 和 \(AE\) 的长度已知，则 \(DB\) 的长度：

English（选项同上方）

In triangle \(ABC,\) \(CD\) is the altitude to \(AB\) and \(AE\) is the altitude to \(BC.\) If the lengths of \(AB,\) \(CD,\) and \(AE\) are known, the length of \(DB\) is:

设 \(AB=c\)、\(CD=h\)，且 \(AE=e\)。令两个面积公式相等，得到 \[ \frac12ch=\frac12(BC)e\text{，}\]所以 \(BC=\frac{ch}{e}\)。由于三角形 \(BCD\) 在 \(D\) 处为直角， \[ DB=\sqrt{BC^2-CD^2}\text{。}\]无论原三角形是锐角、直角还是钝角三角形，这都能确定 \(DB\)，所以前四个选项均不正确。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Let \(AB=c,\) \(CD=h,\) and \(AE=e.\) Equating two area formulas gives \[ \frac12ch=\frac12(BC)e, \] so \(BC=\frac{ch}{e}.\) Since triangle \(BCD\) is right at \(D,\) \[ DB=\sqrt{BC^2-CD^2}. \] This determines \(DB\) whether the original triangle is acute, right, or obtuse, so none of the first four choices is correct.

Thus, the correct answer is E.

24单选

三台机器 \(P\)、\(Q\) 和 \(R\) 合作可在 \(x\) 小时内完成一项工作。若单独工作，\(P\) 完成该工作还需多用 \(6\) 小时，\(Q\) 还需多用一小时，而 \(R\) 还需多用 \(x\) 小时。\(x\) 的值为：

English（选项同上方）

Three machines \(P,\) \(Q,\) and \(R,\) working together, can do a job in \(x\) hours. When working alone, \(P\) needs an additional \(6\) hours to do the job; \(Q,\) one additional hour; and \(R,\) \(x\) additional hours. The value of \(x\) is:

工作速度满足 \[ \frac1{x+6}+\frac1{x+1}+\frac1{2x}=\frac1x\text{。}\]因此 \[ \frac1{x+6}+\frac1{x+1}=\frac1{2x}\text{，}\]化简得 \(3x^2+7x-6=0\)，即 \((3x-2)(x+3)=0\)。时间必须为正，所以 \(x=\frac{2}{3}\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The rates satisfy \[ \frac1{x+6}+\frac1{x+1}+\frac1{2x}=\frac1x. \] Thus \[ \frac1{x+6}+\frac1{x+1}=\frac1{2x}, \] which simplifies to \(3x^2+7x-6=0,\) or \((3x-2)(x+3)=0.\) A time must be positive, so \(x=\frac{2}{3}.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is A.

English（选项同上方）

Let \(a\mathbin{@}b\) represent the operation on two numbers, \(a\) and \(b,\) which selects the larger of the two numbers, with \(a\mathbin{@}a=a.\) Let \(a\mathbin{!}b\) represent the operation which selects the smaller of the two numbers, with \(a\mathbin{!}a=a.\) Which of the following three rules is (are) correct? \[ \begin{aligned} (1)\quad&a\mathbin{@}b=b\mathbin{@}a,\\ (2)\quad&a\mathbin{@}(b\mathbin{@}c)=(a\mathbin{@}b)\mathbin{@}c,\\ (3)\quad&a\mathbin{!}(b\mathbin{@}c)\\ &\quad=(a\mathbin{!}b)\mathbin{@}(a\mathbin{!}c). \end{aligned} \]

English

Therefore, the correct answer is E.
