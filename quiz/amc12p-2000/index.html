1单选

\(2001\) 年，美国将主办国际数学奥林匹克。设 \(I\)、\(M\) 和 \(O\) 是互不相同的正整数，且 \(I \cdot M \cdot O = 2001\)。\(I + M + O\) 的最大可能值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

In the year \(2001,\) the United States will host the International Mathematical Olympiad. Let \(I,\) \(M,\) and \(O\) be distinct positive integers such that the product \(I \cdot M \cdot O = 2001.\) What is the largest possible value of the sum \(I + M + O?\)

分解质因数得 \(2001 = 3 \cdot 23 \cdot 29\)。

在乘积固定且三个正整数互不相同的条件下，要使和最大，取 \(I = 1\)，\(M = 3\)，且 \(O = 23 \cdot 29 = 667\)。

最大和为 \(1 + 3 + 667 = 671\)。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

Factoring gives \(2001 = 3 \cdot 23 \cdot 29.\)

To maximize the sum of three distinct positive integers with this product, take \(I = 1,\) \(M = 3,\) and \(O = 23 \cdot 29 = 667.\)

The largest sum is \(1 + 3 + 667 = 671.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

2单选

\(2000(2000^{2000})\) 等于多少？

English（选项同上方）

What is \(2000(2000^{2000})?\)

将 \(2000 = 2000^1\) 得到 \[ \begin{gathered} 2000^1 \cdot 2000^{2000} \\ = 2000^{1 + 2000} \\ = 2000^{2001} \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

其他选项都比这个值大。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

Writing \(2000 = 2000^1,\) we get \[ \begin{gathered} 2000^1 \cdot 2000^{2000} \\ = 2000^{1 + 2000} \\ = 2000^{2001}. \end{gathered} \]

All of the other options are larger than this.

Thus, the correct answer is A.

3单选

Jenny 每天吃掉当天开始时罐子里软糖的 \(20\%\)。第二天结束时还剩 \(32\) 颗。罐子里原来有多少颗软糖？

English（选项同上方）

Each day, Jenny ate \(20\%\) of the jellybeans that were in her jar at the beginning of that day. At the end of the second day, \(32\) remained. How many jellybeans were in the jar originally?

每天吃掉 \(20\%\)，所以每天结束时剩下 \(80\%\)。若原来有 \(x\) 颗，则 \[ (0.8)^2 x = 32\text{。}\]

解得 \(0.64x = 32\)，所以 \(x = 50\)。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Since \(20\%\) is eaten each day, \(80\%\) remains at the end of each day. If \(x\) is the original number, then \[ (0.8)^2 x = 32. \]

Solving gives \(0.64x = 32,\) so \(x = 50.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

5单选

若 \(|x - 2| = p\)，且 \(x \lt 2\)，则 \(x - p\) 是多少？

English（选项同上方）

If \(|x - 2| = p,\) where \(x \lt 2,\) then what is \(x - p?\)

因为 \(x \lt 2\)，所以 \(|x - 2| = 2 - x = p\)，因此 \(x = 2 - p\)。

于是 \[ x - p = (2 - p) - p = 2 - 2p\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Since \(x \lt 2,\) we have \(|x - 2| = 2 - x = p,\) so \(x = 2 - p.\)

Then \[ x - p = (2 - p) - p = 2 - 2p. \]

Thus, the correct answer is C.

6单选

从 \(4\) 和 \(18\) 之间选出两个不同的质数。用它们的乘积减去它们的和，可能得到下列哪个数？

English（选项同上方）

Two different prime numbers between \(4\) and \(18\) are chosen. When their sum is subtracted from their product, which of the following numbers could be obtained?

\(4\) 和 \(18\) 之间的质数是 \(5, 7, 11, 13\) 和 \(17\)。

对这样的两个质数，\(xy - (x + y)\) \(= (x - 1)(y - 1) - 1\) 是两个偶数的乘积减去 \(1\)，因此它同余于 \(3 \pmod 4\)。这样只剩下 \(119\) 和 \(231\)。后者要求 \((x-1)(y-1)=232\)，但 \(\{4,6,10,12,16\}\) 中没有两个不同的数的乘积等于 \(232\)。

实际上，\[ \begin{aligned} 11 \cdot 13 - (11 + 13) &= 143 - 24 \\ &= 119 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

The primes between \(4\) and \(18\) are \(5, 7, 11, 13,\) and \(17.\)

For two such primes, \(xy - (x + y)\) \(= (x - 1)(y - 1) - 1\) is a product of two even numbers minus \(1,\) hence it is \(3 \pmod 4.\) This leaves \(119\) and \(231.\) The latter would require \((x-1)(y-1)=232,\) but no two distinct numbers in \(\{4,6,10,12,16\}\) have product \(232.\)

Indeed, \[ \begin{aligned} 11 \cdot 13 - (11 + 13) &= 143 - 24 \\ &= 119. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is C.

有多少个正整数 \(b\) 满足 \(\log_b 729\) 是正整数？

How many positive integers \(b\) have the property that \(\log_b 729\) is a positive integer?

若 \(\log_b 729 = n\)，则 \(b^n = 729 = 3^6\)，所以 \(n\) 必须是 \(6\) 的正因数。

\(n = 1, 2, 3, 6\) 分别给出 \(b = 3^6, 3^3, 3^2, 3^1\)，也就是 \(729, 27, 9\) 和 \(3\)。

English

The possibilities \(n = 1, 2, 3, 6\) give \(b = 3^6, 3^3, 3^2, 3^1,\) that is, \(729, 27, 9,\) and \(3.\)

There are \(4\) such values of \(b.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

8单选

图形 \(0\)，\(1\)，\(2\) 和 \(3\) 分别由 \(1\)，\(5\)，\(13\) 和 \(25\) 个互不重叠的单位正方形组成。如果继续这个规律，图形 \(100\) 中会有多少个互不重叠的单位正方形？

English（选项同上方）

Figures \(0,\) \(1,\) \(2,\) and \(3\) consist of \(1,\) \(5,\) \(13,\) and \(25\) nonoverlapping unit squares, respectively. If the pattern were continued, how many nonoverlapping unit squares would there be in figure \(100?\)

图形 \(n\) 是一个菱形，各行长度按奇数增大后再减小，所以共有 \(n^2 + (n + 1)^2\) 个单位正方形。这与 \(n = 0, 1, 2, 3\) 时的 \(1, 5, 13, 25\) 相符。

因此图形 \(100\) 中单位正方形的个数为 \[ \begin{aligned} 100^2 + 101^2 &= 10000 + 10201 \\ &= 20201 \end{aligned} \] 个。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Figure \(n\) is a diamond whose row lengths increase through the odd numbers and back down, giving a total of \(n^2 + (n + 1)^2\) unit squares. This matches \(1, 5, 13, 25\) for \(n = 0, 1, 2, 3.\)

Therefore figure \(100\) has \[ \begin{aligned} 100^2 + 101^2 &= 10000 + 10201 \\ &= 20201 \end{aligned} \] unit squares.

Thus, the correct answer is C.

9单选

Walter 老师给一个五人数学班考试。她把成绩按随机顺序输入电子表格，表格在每输入一个成绩后重新计算班级平均分。Walter 老师注意到每次输入后，平均分总是整数。五个成绩按升序为 \(71\)，\(76\)，\(80\)，\(82\) 和 \(91\)。Walter 老师最后输入的成绩是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Mrs. Walter gave an exam in a mathematics class of five students. She entered the scores in random order into a spreadsheet, which recalculated the class average after each score was entered. Mrs. Walter noticed that after each score was entered, the average was always an integer. The scores (listed in ascending order) were \(71,\) \(76,\) \(80,\) \(82,\) and \(91.\) What was the last score Mrs. Walter entered?

总和为 \(71 + 76 + 80 + 82 + 91 = 400\)，可被 \(5\) 整除。前三个成绩的和必须能被 \(3\) 整除。

模 \(3\)，这些成绩的余数是 \(2, 1, 2, 1, 1\)。唯一和为 \(3\) 的倍数的三元组是 \(76 + 82 + 91 = 249\)，所以它们是前三个成绩（其中 \(91\) 第三个，因为前两个 \(76\) 和 \(82\)，必须奇偶性相同）。

因为 \(249 \equiv 1 \pmod 4\)，第四个成绩必须 \(\equiv 3 \pmod 4\)，即 \(71\)。于是最后输入的是 \(80\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

The total is \(71 + 76 + 80 + 82 + 91 = 400,\) which is divisible by \(5.\) The sum of the first three scores must be divisible by \(3.\)

Modulo \(3,\) the scores are \(2, 1, 2, 1, 1.\) The only triple summing to a multiple of \(3\) is \(76 + 82 + 91 = 249,\) so these are the first three (with \(91\) third, since the first two, \(76\) and \(82,\) must have equal parity).

Since \(249 \equiv 1 \pmod 4,\) the fourth score must be \(\equiv 3 \pmod 4,\) which is \(71.\) That leaves \(80\) as the last score entered.

Thus, the correct answer is C.

点 \(P = (1, 2, 3)\) 关于 \(xy\)-平面反射，得到的像 \(Q\) 再绕 \(x\)-轴旋转 \(180^\circ\) 得到 \(R\)，最后 \(R\) 沿正 \(y\) 方向平移 \(5\) 个单位得到 \(S\)。\(S\) 的坐标是什么？

The point \(P = (1, 2, 3)\) is reflected in the \(xy\)-plane, then its image \(Q\) is rotated by \(180^\circ\) about the \(x\)-axis to produce \(R,\) and finally, \(R\) is translated by \(5\) units in the positive \(y\) direction to produce \(S.\) What are the coordinates of \(S?\)

\((1, 2, 3)\) 关于 \(xy\)-平面反射得到 \(Q = (1, 2, -3)\)。

绕 \(x\)-轴旋转 \(180^\circ\) 会使 \(y\) 和 \(z\) 变号，得到 \(R = (1, -2, 3)\)。

11单选

两个非零实数 \(a\) 和 \(b\) 满足 \(ab = a - b\)。求下式的一个可能值：\[\frac{a}{b} + \frac{b}{a} - ab\text{。}\]

English（选项同上方）

Two non-zero real numbers, \(a\) and \(b,\) satisfy \(ab = a - b.\) Find a possible value of \[\frac{a}{b} + \frac{b}{a} - ab.\]

通分得 \[ \frac{a}{b} + \frac{b}{a} - ab = \frac{a^2 + b^2 - (ab)^2}{ab}\text{。}\]

在分子中用 \(a - b\) 替换 \(ab\)，得 \[ \begin{gathered} a^2 + b^2 - (a - b)^2 \\ = a^2 + b^2 \\ {}- (a^2 - 2ab + b^2) \\ = 2ab \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

因此原式等于 \(\dfrac{2ab}{ab} = 2\)。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

Combining over a common denominator, \[ \frac{a}{b} + \frac{b}{a} - ab = \frac{a^2 + b^2 - (ab)^2}{ab}. \]

Replacing \(ab\) with \(a - b\) in the numerator, \[ \begin{gathered} a^2 + b^2 - (a - b)^2 \\ = a^2 + b^2 \\ {}- (a^2 - 2ab + b^2) \\ = 2ab. \end{gathered} \]

Therefore the expression equals \(\dfrac{2ab}{ab} = 2.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

12单选

设 \(A\)、\(M\) 和 \(C\) 是非负整数，且 \(A + M + C = 12\)。求下式的最大值：\[ \begin{aligned} &A \cdot M \cdot C + A \cdot M \\ &\quad {}+ M \cdot C + C \cdot A \end{aligned}\text{？}\]

English（选项同上方）

Let \(A,\) \(M,\) and \(C\) be nonnegative integers such that \(A + M + C = 12.\) What is the maximum value of \[ \begin{aligned} &A \cdot M \cdot C + A \cdot M \\ &\quad {}+ M \cdot C + C \cdot A? \end{aligned} \]

注意 \[ \begin{aligned} &AMC + AM + MC + CA \\ &\quad = (A + 1)(M + 1)(C + 1) \\ &\quad {}- (A + M + C) - 1 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因为 \(A + M + C = 12\)，原式等于 \((A + 1)(M + 1)(C + 1) - 13\)。这三个因数的和为 \(15\)，乘积在它们都等于 \(5\) 时最大，等于 \(5^3 = 125\)。

最大值为 \(125 - 13 = 112\)。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

Observe that \[ \begin{aligned} &AMC + AM + MC + CA \\ &\quad = (A + 1)(M + 1)(C + 1) \\ &\quad {}- (A + M + C) - 1. \end{aligned} \]

Since \(A + M + C = 12,\) this equals \((A + 1)(M + 1)(C + 1) - 13.\) The three factors sum to \(15,\) so their product is maximized when each equals \(5,\) giving \(5^3 = 125.\)

The maximum value is \(125 - 13 = 112.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

13单选

某天早上，Angela 家的每个人都喝了一杯 \(8\)-盎司的咖啡牛奶混合饮品。每杯中咖啡和牛奶的量可能不同，但都不为零。Angela 喝掉了全家牛奶总量的四分之一和咖啡总量的六分之一。这个家庭有多少人？

English（选项同上方）

One morning each member of Angela’s family drank an \(8\)-ounce mixture of coffee with milk. The amounts of coffee and milk varied from cup to cup, but were never zero. Angela drank a quarter of the total amount of milk and a sixth of the total amount of coffee. How many people are in the family?

以 \(8\)-盎司的一杯为单位计量，所以 Angela 的杯中有 \(c\) 杯咖啡和 \(m\) 杯牛奶，且 \(c + m = 1\)。

因为 Angela 喝掉了咖啡总量的六分之一，所以咖啡总量为 \(6c\)；因为她喝掉了牛奶总量的四分之一，所以牛奶总量为 \(4m\)。人数等于总杯数：\[ \begin{aligned} 6c + 4m &= 6c + 4(1 - c) \\ &= 4 + 2c \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

这必须是整数，所以 \(2c\) 是整数。又因 \(0 \lt c \lt 1\)，只能有 \(c = \tfrac12\)，从而人数为 \(4 + 1 = 5\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Measure amounts in \(8\)-ounce cups, so Angela’s cup holds \(c\) coffee and \(m\) milk with \(c + m = 1.\)

Since Angela drank a sixth of the coffee, the total coffee is \(6c\); since she drank a quarter of the milk, the total milk is \(4m.\) The number of people equals the total number of cups, \[ \begin{aligned} 6c + 4m &= 6c + 4(1 - c) \\ &= 4 + 2c. \end{aligned} \]

This is an integer only when \(2c\) is an integer, and since \(0 \lt c \lt 1\) this forces \(c = \tfrac12,\) giving \(4 + 1 = 5\) people.

Thus, the correct answer is C.

14单选

当列表 \[10, 2, 5, 2, 4, 2, x\]

的平均数、中位数和众数按递增顺序排列后，它们形成一个非常数等差数列。所有可能的实数 \(x\) 的和是多少？

English（选项同上方）

When the mean, median, and mode of the list \[10, 2, 5, 2, 4, 2, x\]

are arranged in increasing order, they form a non-constant arithmetic progression. What is the sum of all possible real values of \(x?\)

六个固定数的和为 \(25\)，所以平均数是 \(\dfrac{25 + x}{7}\)，众数是 \(2\)。若 \(x \le 2\)，则 \(2\) 同时是中位数和众数，会迫使等差数列为常数，所以 \(x \gt 2\)。

情形 \(2 \lt x \lt 4\)： 中位数为 \(x\)。要求 \(2, x, \dfrac{25 + x}{7}\) 构成等差数列，得到此范围内唯一的值 \(x = 3\)。

情形 \(x \ge 4\)： 中位数为 \(4\)，等差数列 \(2, 4, 6\) 迫使平均数为 \(6\)，所以 \(\dfrac{25 + x}{7} = 6\)，得 \(x = 17\)。

所有可能值的和为 \(3 + 17 = 20\)。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

The six fixed numbers sum to \(25,\) so the mean is \(\dfrac{25 + x}{7},\) and the mode is \(2.\) If \(x \le 2,\) then \(2\) is both median and mode, forcing a constant progression, so \(x \gt 2.\)

Case \(2 \lt x \lt 4\): the median is \(x.\) Requiring \(2, x, \dfrac{25 + x}{7}\) to form an arithmetic progression yields \(x = 3\) as the only value in this range.

Case \(x \ge 4\): the median is \(4,\) and the progression \(2, 4, 6\) forces the mean to be \(6,\) so \(\dfrac{25 + x}{7} = 6,\) giving \(x = 17.\)

The sum of all possible values is \(3 + 17 = 20.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

15单选

设函数 \(f\) 满足 \(f\!\left(\dfrac{x}{3}\right) = x^2 + x + 1\)。求所有满足 \(f(3z) = 7\) 的 \(z\) 的和。

English（选项同上方）

Let \(f\) be a function for which \(f\!\left(\dfrac{x}{3}\right) = x^2 + x + 1.\) Find the sum of all values of \(z\) for which \(f(3z) = 7.\)

令 \(\dfrac{x}{3} = 3z\) 得到 \(x = 9z\)，所以 \[ \begin{aligned} f(3z) &= (9z)^2 + 9z + 1 \\ &= 81z^2 + 9z + 1 \\ &= 7 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

整理得 \(81z^2 + 9z - 6 = 0\)。

由根和公式，所有 \(z\) 的和为 \(-\dfrac{9}{81} = -\dfrac{1}{9}\)。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Setting \(\dfrac{x}{3} = 3z\) gives \(x = 9z,\) so \[ \begin{aligned} f(3z) &= (9z)^2 + 9z + 1 \\ &= 81z^2 + 9z + 1 \\ &= 7. \end{aligned} \]

This rearranges to \(81z^2 + 9z - 6 = 0.\)

By the sum-of-roots formula, the sum of the values of \(z\) is \(-\dfrac{9}{81} = -\dfrac{1}{9}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

16单选

一个 \(13\) 行 \(17\) 列的棋盘，每个方格中都写有一个数，从左上角开始编号，第一行编号为 \(1\)，\(2\)，\(\ldots\)，\(17\)，第二行编号为 \(18\)，\(19\)，\(\ldots\)，\(34\)，依此向下。如果重新编号，使左列从上到下为 \(1\)，\(2\)，\(\ldots\)，\(13\)，第二列为 \(14\)，\(15\)，\(\ldots\)，\(26\)，依此向右，有些方格在两种编号方式下得到相同的数。求这些方格中数字的和（任一种编号方式下都一样）。

English（选项同上方）

A checkerboard of \(13\) rows and \(17\) columns has a number written in each square, beginning in the upper left corner, so that the first row is numbered \(1,\) \(2,\) \(\ldots,\) \(17,\) the second row \(18,\) \(19,\) \(\ldots,\) \(34,\) and so on down the board. If the board is renumbered so that the left column, top to bottom, is \(1,\) \(2,\) \(\ldots,\) \(13,\) the second column \(14,\) \(15,\) \(\ldots,\) \(26\) and so on across the board, some squares have the same numbers in both numbering systems. Find the sum of the numbers in these squares (under either system).

方格 \((m, n)\) 原来的编号为 \(17(m - 1) + n\)，重新编号后为 \(13(n - 1) + m\)。令它们相等，得 \[ 4m - 3n = 1\text{。}\]

满足 \(1 \le m \le 13\) 且 \(1 \le n \le 17\) 的解为 \((1, 1)\)，\((4, 5)\)，\((7, 9)\)，\((10, 13)\)，和 \((13, 17)\)。

这些方格中的数为 \(1, 56, 111, 166\) 和 \(221\)，总和为 \(555\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

The square \((m, n)\) is numbered \(17(m - 1) + n\) originally and \(13(n - 1) + m\) after renumbering. Setting these equal gives \[ 4m - 3n = 1. \]

The solutions with \(1 \le m \le 13\) and \(1 \le n \le 17\) are \((1, 1),\) \((4, 5),\) \((7, 9),\) \((10, 13),\) and \((13, 17).\)

These squares hold the numbers \(1, 56, 111, 166,\) and \(221,\) whose sum is \(555.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

17单选

一个以 \(O\) 为圆心、半径为 \(1\) 的圆经过点 \(A\)。线段 \(AB\) 在点 \(A\) 处与圆相切，且 \(\angle AOB = \theta\)。若点 \(C\) 位于 \(\overline{OA}\) 上，且 \(BC\) 平分 \(\angle ABO\)，则 \(OC\) 等于多少？

English（选项同上方）

A circle centered at \(O\) has radius \(1\) and contains the point \(A.\) Segment \(AB\) is tangent to the circle at \(A\) and \(\angle AOB = \theta.\) If point \(C\) lies on \(\overline{OA}\) and \(BC\) bisects \(\angle ABO,\) then what is \(OC?\)

因为 \(OA = 1\) 且 \(AB\) 在 \(A\) 点与圆相切，角 \(OAB\) 为直角，所以 \[ BA = \tan\theta, \qquad OB = \sec\theta\text{。}\]

因为 \(BC\) 平分 \(\angle ABO\)，由角平分线定理得 \(\dfrac{OC}{CA} = \dfrac{OB}{BA}\)。又 \(OC + CA = OA = 1\)，\[ \begin{aligned} OC &= \frac{OB}{OB + BA} \\ &= \frac{\sec\theta}{\sec\theta + \tan\theta} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

分子分母同乘 \(\cos\theta\) 得到 \(OC = \dfrac{1}{1 + \sin\theta}\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

Because \(OA = 1\) and \(AB\) is tangent at \(A,\) angle \(OAB\) is right, so \[ BA = \tan\theta, \qquad OB = \sec\theta. \]

Since \(BC\) bisects \(\angle ABO,\) the angle bisector theorem gives \(\dfrac{OC}{CA} = \dfrac{OB}{BA}.\) Using \(OC + CA = OA = 1,\) \[ \begin{aligned} OC &= \frac{OB}{OB + BA} \\ &= \frac{\sec\theta}{\sec\theta + \tan\theta}. \end{aligned} \]

Multiplying numerator and denominator by \(\cos\theta\) gives \(OC = \dfrac{1}{1 + \sin\theta}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

18单选

\(N\) 年的第 \(300\) 天是星期二，\(N + 1\) 年的第 \(200\) 天也是星期二。那么 \(N - 1\) 年的第 \(100\) 天是星期几？

English（选项同上方）

In year \(N,\) the \(300\)th day of the year is a Tuesday. In year \(N + 1,\) the \(200\)th day is also a Tuesday. On what day of the week did the \(100\)th day of year \(N - 1\) occur?

从 \(N\) 年第 \(300\) 天到 \(N + 1\) 年第 \(200\) 天，若 \(N\) 不是闰年，相隔 \(365 - 300 + 200 = 265\) 天。但 \(265 = 7 \cdot 37 + 6\)，会落在星期一，而不是星期二。

所以 \(N\) 是闰年，相隔 \(266 = 7 \cdot 38\) 天，正好仍为星期二。因此 \(N - 1\) 年不是闰年。

\(N - 1\) 年第 \(100\) 天比 \(N\) 年第 \(300\) 天早 \(365 - 100 + 300 = 565\) 天。因为 \(565 = 7 \cdot 80 + 5\)，所以那一天比星期二早 \(5\) 天，即星期四。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

From day \(300\) of year \(N\) to day \(200\) of year \(N + 1,\) the number of days is \(365 - 300 + 200 = 265\) if \(N\) is not a leap year. But \(265 = 7 \cdot 37 + 6,\) which would land on a Monday, not a Tuesday.

So year \(N\) is a leap year, and the gap is \(266 = 7 \cdot 38\) days, giving a Tuesday as stated. It follows that year \(N - 1\) is not a leap year.

The \(100\)th day of year \(N - 1\) precedes the Tuesday on day \(300\) of year \(N\) by \(365 - 100 + 300 = 565\) days. Since \(565 = 7 \cdot 80 + 5,\) that day is \(5\) weekdays before Tuesday, namely Thursday.

Thus, the correct answer is A.

19单选

在三角形 \(ABC\) 中，\(AB = 13\)、\(BC = 14\)、\(AC = 15\)。设 \(D\) 为 \(\overline{BC}\) 的中点，\(E\) 为角 \(BAC\) 的角平分线与 \(\overline{BC}\) 的交点。下列哪一个数最接近三角形 \(ADE\) 的面积？

English（选项同上方）

In triangle \(ABC,\) \(AB = 13,\) \(BC = 14,\) and \(AC = 15.\) Let \(D\) denote the midpoint of \(\overline{BC}\) and let \(E\) denote the intersection of \(\overline{BC}\) with the bisector of angle \(BAC.\) Which of the following is closest to the area of triangle \(ADE?\)

由海伦公式，\(\triangle ABC\) 的面积为 \(\sqrt{21 \cdot 8 \cdot 7 \cdot 6} = 84\)，所以从 \(A\) 到 \(BC\) 的高为 \(\dfrac{2 \cdot 84}{14} = 12\)。

中点 \(D\) 到 \(B\) 的距离为 \(7\)。从 \(A\) 作出的角平分线与 \(BC\) 相交于 \(E\)，且 \(BE : EC = AB : AC = 13 : 15\)，所以 \(BE = 14 \cdot \dfrac{13}{28} = 6.5\)。

\(D\) 和 \(E\) 都在 \(BC\) 上，所以 \(\triangle ADE\) 的底为 \(DE = 7 - 6.5 = 0.5\)，高为 \(12\)，面积为 \[ \tfrac12 \cdot 0.5 \cdot 12 = 3\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

By Heron’s formula, the area of \(\triangle ABC\) is \(\sqrt{21 \cdot 8 \cdot 7 \cdot 6} = 84,\) so the altitude from \(A\) to \(BC\) is \(\dfrac{2 \cdot 84}{14} = 12.\)

The midpoint \(D\) is \(7\) from \(B.\) The bisector from \(A\) meets \(BC\) at \(E\) with \(BE : EC = AB : AC = 13 : 15,\) so \(BE = 14 \cdot \dfrac{13}{28} = 6.5.\)

Both \(D\) and \(E\) lie on \(BC,\) so \(\triangle ADE\) has base \(DE = 7 - 6.5 = 0.5\) and altitude \(12,\) giving area \[ \tfrac12 \cdot 0.5 \cdot 12 = 3. \]

Thus, the correct answer is C.

20单选

若正数 \(x\)、\(y\) 和 \(z\) 满足 \[x + \frac{1}{y} = 4\text{，}\] \[y + \frac{1}{z} = 1\text{，}\] 以及 \[z + \frac{1}{x} = \frac{7}{3}\text{，}\] 则 \(xyz\) 等于多少？

English（选项同上方）

If \(x,\) \(y,\) and \(z\) are positive numbers satisfying \[x + \frac{1}{y} = 4,\] \[y + \frac{1}{z} = 1,\] and \[z + \frac{1}{x} = \frac{7}{3},\] then what is \(xyz?\)

三个方程相加得 \[ \begin{gathered} \left(x + \tfrac1y\right) + \left(y + \tfrac1z\right) \\ {}+ \left(z + \tfrac1x\right) \\ = 4 + 1 + \tfrac73 \\ = \tfrac{22}{3}\text{。} \end{gathered} \]

三个方程相乘得 \[ 4 \cdot 1 \cdot \tfrac73 = \tfrac{28}{3}\text{。} \]

展开乘积，得到 \[ \begin{aligned} &\left(x + \tfrac1y\right) \\ &\quad {}\cdot \left(y + \tfrac1z\right) \\ &\quad {}\cdot \left(z + \tfrac1x\right) \\ &= xyz \\ &\quad {}+ \left(x + y + z + \tfrac1x + \tfrac1y + \tfrac1z\right) \\ &\quad {}+ \frac{1}{xyz}\text{。} \end{aligned} \] 中间括号中的一组之和就是 \(\tfrac{22}{3}\)，所以 \(xyz + \dfrac{1}{xyz} = \tfrac{28}{3} - \tfrac{22}{3} = 2\)。

因此 \((xyz - 1)^2 = 0\)，所以 \(xyz = 1\)。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Adding the three equations gives \[ \begin{gathered} \left(x + \tfrac1y\right) + \left(y + \tfrac1z\right) \\ {}+ \left(z + \tfrac1x\right) \\ = 4 + 1 + \tfrac73 \\ = \tfrac{22}{3}. \end{gathered} \]

Multiplying them gives \[ 4 \cdot 1 \cdot \tfrac73 = \tfrac{28}{3}. \]

Expanding the product, \[ \begin{aligned} &\left(x + \tfrac1y\right) \\ &\quad {}\cdot \left(y + \tfrac1z\right) \\ &\quad {}\cdot \left(z + \tfrac1x\right) \\ &= xyz \\ &\quad {}+ \left(x + y + z + \tfrac1x + \tfrac1y + \tfrac1z\right) \\ &\quad {}+ \frac{1}{xyz}. \end{aligned} \] The middle group is the sum \(\tfrac{22}{3},\) so \(xyz + \dfrac{1}{xyz} = \tfrac{28}{3} - \tfrac{22}{3} = 2.\)

Hence \((xyz - 1)^2 = 0,\) so \(xyz = 1.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

21单选

过直角三角形斜边上的一点，作两条分别平行于两条直角边的直线，把三角形分成一个正方形和两个较小的直角三角形。若其中一个小直角三角形的面积是正方形面积的 \(m\) 倍，则另一个小直角三角形面积与正方形面积的比是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Through a point on the hypotenuse of a right triangle, lines are drawn parallel to the legs of the triangle so that the triangle is divided into a square and two smaller right triangles. The area of one of the two small right triangles is \(m\) times the area of the square. What is the ratio of the area of the other small right triangle to the area of the square?

设正方形边长为 \(1\)。与正方形一边相邻的小三角形有一条长为 \(r\) 的垂直边，所以它的面积为 \(\tfrac12 \cdot 1 \cdot r = m\)，从而 \(r = 2m\)。

两个小三角形相似，所以另一个三角形沿正方形的直角边长为 \(\dfrac1r\)，面积为 \[ \frac12 \cdot 1 \cdot \frac1r = \frac{1}{2r} = \frac{1}{4m}\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

Let the square have side \(1.\) The small triangle sharing one side of the square has a perpendicular leg \(r,\) so its area is \(\tfrac12 \cdot 1 \cdot r = m,\) giving \(r = 2m.\)

The two small triangles are similar, so the other triangle’s leg along the square is \(\dfrac1r,\) and its area is \[ \frac12 \cdot 1 \cdot \frac1r = \frac{1}{2r} = \frac{1}{4m}. \]

Thus, the correct answer is D.

下图显示了由四次多项式 \[P(x) = x^4 + ax^3 + bx^2 + cx + d\] 定义的曲线的一部分。下列哪一项最小？

English（选项同上方）

The graph below shows a portion of the curve defined by the quartic polynomial \[P(x) = x^4 + ax^3 + bx^2 + cx + d.\] Which of the following is the smallest?

图像与 \(x\)-轴恰好相交两次，且两次都在正数处，所以 \(P\) 有两个实零点和两个非实（复共轭）零点。

从图像读出：系数之和为 \(P(1) \gt 3\)；\(P(-1) \gt 4\)；实零点之和大于 \(4.5\)；所有零点的乘积为 \(d\)，即 \(y\)-截距，小于 \(6\)。

English

The graph crosses the \(x\)-axis exactly twice, both times at positive values, so \(P\) has two real zeros and two non-real (complex conjugate) zeros.

Reading off the graph: the sum of the coefficients is \(P(1) \gt 3;\) \(P(-1) \gt 4;\) the sum of the real zeros is greater than \(4.5;\) and the product of all zeros is \(d,\) the \(y\)-intercept, which is less than \(6.\)

Let \(R\) be the product of the real zeros, so \(R \gt 4.5.\) The product of the non-real zeros is \(\dfrac dR,\) which is less than \(\dfrac{6}{4.5} \lt 2.\)

This is smaller than every other listed quantity.

23单选

Gamble 教授买了一张彩票，需要从 \(1\) 到 \(46\)（含）中选出六个不同的整数。他选择的数字满足这六个数字的常用对数之和为整数。碰巧中奖彩票上的整数也有相同性质，即常用对数之和为整数。Gamble 教授持有中奖彩票的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Professor Gamble buys a lottery ticket, which requires that he pick six different integers from \(1\) through \(46,\) inclusive. He chooses his numbers so that the sum of the base-ten logarithms of his six numbers is an integer. It so happens that the integers on the winning ticket have the same property -- the sum of the base-ten logarithms is an integer. What is the probability that Professor Gamble holds the winning ticket?

对数之和为整数 \(k\)，当且仅当六个数的乘积为 \(10^k\)。因为 \(10 = 2 \cdot 5\)，每个选中的数都必须形如 \(2^a 5^b\)，所以只能来自 \[ 1, 2, 4, 5, 8, 10, 16, 20, 25, 32, 40\text{。}\]

对每个数记录因数 \(2\) 的个数比因数 \(5\) 的个数多多少：\(0, 1, 2, -1, 3, 0, 4, 1, -2, 5, 2\)。乘积为 \(10\) 的幂，要求六个所选数中 \(2\) 与 \(5\) 的总指数相等，也就是这些差值之和为 \(0\)。

负差值只有 \(5\) 的 \(-1\) 和 \(25\) 的 \(-2\)。总差值为 \(0\) 的六数彩票必须同时包含两者：缺少任意一个，都没有足够的零和小正差值凑满六个数。因此另外四个数的差值之和必须为 \(3\)。差值为 \(0\) 的数有两个（\(1,10\)），差值为 \(1\) 的数有两个（\(2,20\)），差值为 \(2\) 的数有两个（\(4,40\)）；若使用差值至少为 \(3\) 的数，就没有足够的零差值数凑满四个。因此必须取两个差值为 \(0\) 的数、一个差值为 \(1\) 的数和一个差值为 \(2\) 的数。恰有四张有效彩票：\(\{1, 5, 10, 20, 25, 40\}\)，\(\{1, 2, 5, 10, 25, 40\}\)，\(\{1, 2, 4, 5, 10, 25\}\)，和 \(\{1, 4, 5, 10, 20, 25\}\)。

Gamble 教授持有其中一张，而只有一张与中奖彩票相同，所以概率为 \(\dfrac14\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The sum of the logarithms is an integer \(k\) exactly when the product of the six numbers is \(10^k.\) Since \(10 = 2 \cdot 5,\) each chosen number must be of the form \(2^a 5^b,\) so it comes from \[ 1, 2, 4, 5, 8, 10, 16, 20, 25, 32, 40. \]

For each, record the excess of factors of \(2\) over factors of \(5\): \(0, 1, 2, -1, 3, 0, 4, 1, -2, 5, 2.\) The product is a power of \(10\) only if the six chosen values have equal totals of \(2\)s and \(5\)s, i.e. their excesses sum to \(0.\)

The only negative excesses are \(-1\) for \(5\) and \(-2\) for \(25.\) A six-number ticket with total excess \(0\) must contain both: omitting either leaves too few zero and small positive excesses to reach six numbers. The other four numbers must therefore have total excess \(3.\) There are two numbers of excess \(0\) (\(1,10\)), two of excess \(1\) (\(2,20\)), and two of excess \(2\) (\(4,40\)); any number of excess at least \(3\) would leave too few zeros to complete a four-number selection. Thus we must take both excess-\(0\) numbers, one excess-\(1\) number, and one excess-\(2\) number. This gives exactly four valid tickets: \(\{1, 5, 10, 20, 25, 40\},\) \(\{1, 2, 5, 10, 25, 40\},\) \(\{1, 2, 4, 5, 10, 25\},\) and \(\{1, 4, 5, 10, 20, 25\}.\)

Professor Gamble holds one of these four, and only one matches the winning ticket, so the probability is \(\dfrac14.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

若圆弧 \(AC\) 和 \(BC\) 的圆心分别为 \(B\) 和 \(A\)，则存在一个圆同时与圆弧 \(AC\)、圆弧 \(BC\) 以及线段 \(\overline{AB}\) 相切。若圆弧 \(BC\) 的长度为 \(12\)，则这个圆的周长是多少？

English（选项同上方）

If circular arcs \(AC\) and \(BC\) have centers at \(B\) and \(A,\) respectively, then there exists a circle tangent to both arc \(AC\) and arc \(BC,\) and to \(\overline{AB}.\) If the length of arc \(BC\) is \(12,\) then what is the circumference of the circle?

每条圆弧的半径都是 \(AB\)，且 \(C\) 到 \(A\) 和 \(B\) 的距离都为 \(AB\)，所以 \(\triangle ABC\) 是等边三角形。因此圆弧 \(BC\) 对应半径为 \(AB\) 的圆上的 \(60^\circ\) 圆心角，该大圆的整周长为 \(6 \cdot 12 = 72\)。

设小圆半径为 \(r\)，并在线段 \(\overline{AB}\) 的中点 \(D\) 处相切，其中 \(AD = \tfrac12 AB\)。由点幂定理，\(AD^2 = AB(AB - 2r)\)，所以 \[ \frac{AB^2}{4} = AB^2 - 2r\,AB\text{，} \] 从而 \(2r = \tfrac34 AB\)，因而 \(r = \tfrac38 AB\)。

周长之比等于半径之比，所以小圆的周长为 \(\tfrac38 \cdot 72 = 27\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

Each arc has radius \(AB,\) and \(C\) is at distance \(AB\) from both \(A\) and \(B,\) so \(\triangle ABC\) is equilateral. Thus arc \(BC\) subtends \(60^\circ\) of a circle of radius \(AB,\) whose full circumference is \(6 \cdot 12 = 72.\)

Let the small circle have radius \(r\) and touch \(\overline{AB}\) at its midpoint \(D,\) where \(AD = \tfrac12 AB.\) By Power of a Point, \(AD^2 = AB(AB - 2r),\) so \[ \frac{AB^2}{4} = AB^2 - 2r\,AB, \] giving \(2r = \tfrac34 AB,\) hence \(r = \tfrac38 AB.\)

The circumferences are in the ratio of the radii, so the small circle’s circumference is \(\tfrac38 \cdot 72 = 27.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

25单选

八个全等的等边三角形，每个颜色都不同，被用来构造一个正八面体。构造这个八面体有多少种可区分的方法？（如果两个着色八面体不能通过旋转变得完全一样，则称它们可区分。）

English（选项同上方）

Eight congruent equilateral triangles, each of a different color, are used to construct a regular octahedron. How many distinguishable ways are there to construct the octahedron? (Two colored octahedrons are distinguishable if neither can be rotated to look just like the other.)

将八种不同颜色分配给八个面共有 \(8!\) 种方式。两个分配表示同一个八面体，当且仅当其中一个可以旋转成另一个。

正八面体的旋转群有 \(24\) 个元素。因为八种颜色全都不同，没有非平凡旋转会固定一种着色，所以每个可区分的八面体恰好对应 \(24\) 种分配。

因此可区分的八面体数量为 \[ \frac{8!}{24} = \frac{40320}{24} = 1680\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

There are \(8!\) ways to assign the eight distinct colors to the eight faces. Two assignments give the same octahedron exactly when one is a rotation of the other.

The rotation group of a regular octahedron has \(24\) elements. Because all eight colors are different, no nontrivial rotation fixes a coloring, so each distinguishable octahedron corresponds to exactly \(24\) assignments.

Therefore the number of distinguishable octahedrons is \[ \frac{8!}{24} = \frac{40320}{24} = 1680. \]

Thus, the correct answer is E.
