下列乘积的值是多少？

\[\dfrac{3 \times 5}{9 \times 11} \times \dfrac{7 \times 9 \times 11}{3 \times 5 \times 7}\]

English（选项同上方）

What is the value of the following product?

\[\dfrac{3 \times 5}{9 \times 11} \times \dfrac{7 \times 9 \times 11}{3 \times 5 \times 7}\]

合并两个分数后，分子是 \(3 \cdot 5 \cdot 7 \cdot 9 \cdot 11\)，分母是 \(9 \cdot 11 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 7\)。它们是同一个乘积。

因此这个值是 \(1\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Combining the two fractions, the numerator is \(3 \cdot 5 \cdot 7 \cdot 9 \cdot 11\) and the denominator is \(9 \cdot 11 \cdot 3 \cdot 5 \cdot 7.\) These are the same product.

So the value is \(1.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A .

English（选项同上方）

第 \(n\) 行有 \(2n - 1\) 个方格。由于两端都是未涂色方格且两种方格交替，每行未涂色方格比涂色方格多一个，所以涂色方格数是 \(n - 1\)。

Thus, the correct answer is C .

20单选

某一年，一月正好有四个星期二和四个星期六。那一年的一月 \(1\) 日是星期几？

English（选项同上方）

In a certain year, January had exactly four Tuesdays and four Saturdays. On what day did January \(1\) fall that year?

因为 \(31 = 4 \cdot 7 + 3\)，一月 \(1\) 日、\(2\) 日和 \(3\) 日所对应的三个星期几各出现五次，其余星期几各出现四次。

要让星期二和星期六都只出现四次，它们都不能是一月 \(1\) 日、\(2\) 日或 \(3\) 日。唯一可行的开始日是星期三：这样星期三、星期四、星期五出现五次，而星期二和星期六各出现四次。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Since \(31 = 4 \cdot 7 + 3,\) the weekdays falling on January \(1,\) \(2,\) and \(3\) each occur five times that month, and every other weekday occurs four times.

For Tuesday and Saturday to occur only four times, neither may be January \(1,\) \(2,\) or \(3.\) The only starting day that works is Wednesday: then Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday occur five times, while Tuesday and Saturday each occur four times.

Thus, the correct answer is C .

English（选项同上方）

Assume every \(7\)-digit whole number is a possible telephone number except those that begin with \(0\) or \(1.\) What fraction of telephone numbers begin with \(9\) and end with \(0?\)

第一位是 \(8\) 个允许数字之一（\(2\) 到 \(9\)），所以 \(\dfrac18\) 的号码以 \(9\) 开头。最后一位可以是 \(10\) 个数字之一，所以 \(\dfrac{1}{10}\) 的号码以 \(0\) 结尾。

These conditions are independent, so the fraction is \(\dfrac18 \cdot \dfrac{1}{10} = \dfrac{1}{80}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B .

Each teacher teaches \(4\) classes, so there are \(\dfrac{200}{4} = 50\) teachers.

Thus, the correct answer is E .

English（选项同上方）

In a magic triangle, each of the six whole numbers \(10\) through \(15\) is placed in one of the circles so that the sum \(S\) of the three numbers on each side of the triangle is the same. The largest possible value for \(S\) is

设 \(C\) 为三个顶点数之和。把三条边的和相加可得 \(3S = 75 + C\)，因为六个数之和为 \(75\)，且每个顶点数都被多计算了一次。要使 \(S\) 最大，应把三个最大的数 \(13\)、\(14\)、\(15\) 放在顶点，此时 \(C = 42\)。

于是 \(3S = 75 + 42 = 117\)，所以 \(S = 39\)。这个值可以达到：在顶点 \(13\) 与 \(14\) 之间放 \(12\)，在 \(14\) 与 \(15\) 之间放 \(10\)，在 \(15\) 与 \(13\) 之间放 \(11\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Let \(C\) be the sum of the corner numbers. Adding the three side sums gives \(3S = 75 + C,\) because the six numbers sum to \(75\) and every corner is counted one extra time. To maximize \(S,\) place the three largest numbers \(13,\) \(14,\) \(15\) at the corners, giving \(C = 42.\)

Then \(3S = 75 + 42 = 117,\) so \(S = 39.\) This is achievable: put \(12\) between corners \(13\) and \(14,\) put \(10\) between \(14\) and \(15,\) and put \(11\) between \(15\) and \(13.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D .

桌上放着五张卡片。每张卡片一面有一个字母，另一面有一个整数。可见的面显示 \(P\)、\(Q\)、\(3\)、\(4\) 和 \(6\)。简说：“如果任意一张卡片的一面是元音字母，那么另一面就是偶数。”玛丽翻开一张卡片，证明简错了。玛丽翻开的是哪张卡片？

English（选项同上方）

Five cards are lying on a table. Each card has a letter on one side and a whole number on the other side. The visible faces show \(P,\) \(Q,\) \(3,\) \(4,\) and \(6.\) Jane said, “If a vowel is on one side of any card, then an even number is on the other side.” Mary showed Jane was wrong by turning over one card. Which card did Mary turn over?

要证明“元音字母必对应偶数”这个规则是错的，玛丽需要找到一张一面是元音字母、另一面是奇数的卡片。没有卡片显示元音字母，因为 \(P\) 和 \(Q\) 是辅音。

唯一显示奇数的卡片是 \(3\)。翻开它后露出元音字母，就会反驳简的说法。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

To show the rule “a vowel forces an even number” is false, Mary needs a card with a vowel on one side and an odd number on the other. No card shows a vowel, since \(P\) and \(Q\) are consonants.

The only card showing an odd number is \(3.\) Turning it over reveals a vowel, which contradicts Jane’s claim.

Thus, the correct answer is A .
