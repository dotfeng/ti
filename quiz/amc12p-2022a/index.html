1单选

求下式的值：

\[3+\cfrac{1}{3+\cfrac{1}{3+\frac13}}\text{？}\]

English（选项同上方）

What is the value of

\[3+\cfrac{1}{3+\cfrac{1}{3+\frac13}}?\]

从底部开始化简。最里面的部分是 \(3+\dfrac13=\dfrac{10}{3}\)。

下一层是 \(3+\dfrac{1}{\frac{10}{3}}=3+\dfrac{3}{10}=\dfrac{33}{10}\)。

最后，\(3+\dfrac{1}{\frac{33}{10}}=3+\dfrac{10}{33}=\dfrac{109}{33}\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

Simplify from the bottom. The innermost fraction is \(3+\dfrac13=\dfrac{10}{3}.\)

The next layer is \(3+\dfrac{1}{\frac{10}{3}}=3+\dfrac{3}{10}=\dfrac{33}{10}.\)

Finally, \(3+\dfrac{1}{\frac{33}{10}}=3+\dfrac{10}{33}=\dfrac{109}{33}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

2单选

三个数的和为 \(96\)。第一个数是第三个数的 \(6\) 倍，第三个数比第二个数少 \(40\)。第一个数与第二个数之差的绝对值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The sum of three numbers is \(96.\) The first number is \(6\) times the third number, and the third number is \(40\) less than the second number. What is the absolute value of the difference between the first and second numbers?

令第三个数为 \(t\)。那么第一个数是 \(6t\)，第二个数是 \(t+40\)。它们的和为 \[6t+(t+40)+t=8t+40=96\text{，}\] 所以 \(t=7\)。

第一个数是 \(42\)，第二个数是 \(47\)， 因此差的绝对值为 \(5\)。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

Let the third number be \(t.\) Then the first is \(6t\) and the second is \(t+40.\) Their sum is \[6t+(t+40)+t=8t+40=96,\] so \(t=7.\)

The first number is \(42\) and the second is \(47,\) so the difference has absolute value \(5.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

3单选

五个矩形 \(A\)，\(B\)，\(C\)，\(D\)，和 \(E\)，如下图所示排列成一个正方形。这些矩形的尺寸分别为 \(1\times6\)，\(2\times4\)，\(5\times6\)，\(2\times7\)，和 \(2\times3\)。（图形未按比例绘制。）中间的阴影矩形是这五个矩形中的哪一个？

English（选项同上方）

Five rectangles, \(A,\) \(B,\) \(C,\) \(D,\) and \(E,\) are arranged in a square as shown below. These rectangles have dimensions \(1\times6,\) \(2\times4,\) \(5\times6,\) \(2\times7,\) and \(2\times3,\) respectively. (The figure is not drawn to scale.) Which of the five rectangles is the shaded one in the middle?

五个矩形的面积分别为 \(6,\ 8,\ 30,\ 14\)，和 \(6\)，总和为 \(64\)。所以这个正方形是 \(8\times8\)。

将 \(C\)（\(5\times6\)）放在左上方，\(D\)（\(2\times7\)）沿右边放置，\(E\) （\(2\times3\)）放在左下方，\(A\)（\(1\times6\)）沿底边放置，会留下一个中间的 \(2\times4\) 空隙，正好是矩形 \(B\)。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

The five areas are \(6,\ 8,\ 30,\ 14,\) and \(6,\) which sum to \(64.\) So the square is \(8\times8.\)

Placing \(C\) (\(5\times6\)) across the top left, \(D\) (\(2\times7\)) up the right side, \(E\) (\(2\times3\)) in the lower left, and \(A\) (\(1\times6\)) along the bottom leaves a central \(2\times4\) gap, which is exactly rectangle \(B.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

4单选

正整数 \(n\) 与 \(18\) 的最小公倍数为 \(180\)，且 \(n\) 与 \(45\) 的最大公因数为 \(15\)。求 \(n\) 的各位数字之和。

English（选项同上方）

The least common multiple of a positive integer \(n\) and \(18\) is \(180,\) and the greatest common divisor of \(n\) and \(45\) is \(15.\) What is the sum of the digits of \(n?\)

因为 \(180=2^2\cdot3^2\cdot5\)，且 \(18=2\cdot3^2\)，条件 \(\operatorname{lcm}(n,18)=180\) 强制 \(n\) 提供 \(2^2\) 和 \(5\)，同时 \(3\) 的幂次至多为 \(2\)。

由 \(\gcd(n,45)=\gcd(n,3^2\cdot5)\) \(=15=3\cdot5\)，可知 \(n\) 中 \(3\) 的幂次恰为 \(1\)，\(5\) 的幂次至少为 \(1\)。

因此 \(n=2^2\cdot3\cdot5=60\)，其各位数字之和为 \(6\)。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Since \(180=2^2\cdot3^2\cdot5\) and \(18=2\cdot3^2,\) the condition \(\operatorname{lcm}(n,18)=180\) forces \(n\) to contribute \(2^2\) and \(5,\) with its power of \(3\) at most \(2.\)

From \(\gcd(n,45)=\gcd(n,3^2\cdot5)\) \(=15=3\cdot5,\) the power of \(3\) in \(n\) is exactly \(1\) and the power of \(5\) is at least \(1.\)

Therefore \(n=2^2\cdot3\cdot5=60,\) whose digits sum to \(6.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

5单选

在坐标平面中，点 \((x_1,y_1)\) 与 \((x_2,y_2)\) 的出租车距离定义为 \(|x_1-x_2|+|y_1-y_2|\)。有多少个整数坐标点 \(P\)，使得 \(P\) 到原点的出租车距离小于或等于 \(20\)？

English（选项同上方）

Let the taxicab distance between points \((x_1,y_1)\) and \((x_2,y_2)\) in the coordinate plane be given by \(|x_1-x_2|+|y_1-y_2|.\) For how many points \(P\) with integer coordinates is the taxicab distance between \(P\) and the origin less than or equal to \(20?\)

对每个 \(k\ge1\)，集合 \(|x|+|y|=k\) 恰有 \(4k\) 个格点，而 \(k=0\) 时只有原点一个点。

总数为 \[ \begin{aligned} &1+\sum_{k=1}^{20}4k=1+4\cdot\frac{20\cdot21}{2} \\ &=1+840=841 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

For each \(k\ge1,\) the set \(|x|+|y|=k\) contains exactly \(4k\) lattice points, and \(k=0\) gives the single origin.

The total is \[ \begin{aligned} &1+\sum_{k=1}^{20}4k=1+4\cdot\frac{20\cdot21}{2} \\ &=1+840=841. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is C.

6单选

一个数据集由 \(6\) 个不一定互异的正整数组成：\(1\)，\(7\)，\(5\)，\(2\)，\(5\)，和 \(X\)。这 \(6\) 个数的平均数等于数据集中的某个数。所有正的 \(X\) 值之和是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A data set consists of \(6\) (not distinct) positive integers: \(1,\) \(7,\) \(5,\) \(2,\) \(5,\) and \(X.\) The average (arithmetic mean) of the \(6\) numbers equals a value in the data set. What is the sum of all positive values of \(X?\)

已知数的和为 \(20\)，所以平均数为 \(\dfrac{20+X}{6}\)，且它必须等于数据集中的某个数。

令它等于 \(5\) 得 \(X=10\)；等于 \(7\) 得 \(X=22\)；等于 \(X\) 本身得 \(20+X=6X\)，所以 \(X=4\)。等于 \(1\) 或 \(2\) 会给出负的 \(X\)。

符合条件的正值为 \(10,22,4\)，总和为 \(36\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

The known numbers sum to \(20,\) so the mean is \(\dfrac{20+X}{6},\) which must equal an element of the set.

Setting it to \(5\) gives \(X=10;\) to \(7\) gives \(X=22;\) and to \(X\) itself gives \(20+X=6X,\) so \(X=4.\) Values \(1\) and \(2\) give negative \(X.\)

The positive values are \(10,22,4,\) summing to \(36.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

7单选

一个长方形如图被分成 \(5\) 个区域。每个区域要涂成一种纯色，可选颜色为红、橙、黄、蓝、绿。相接触的区域必须涂成不同颜色，颜色可以重复使用。共有多少种不同的涂色方法？

English（选项同上方）

A rectangle is partitioned into \(5\) regions as shown. Each region is to be painted a solid color - red, orange, yellow, blue, or green - so that regions that touch are painted different colors, and colors can be used more than once. How many different colorings are possible?

底部中间的区域与其他四个区域都共边。先给它涂色，有 \(5\) 种方法。

左上区域与它相邻，有 \(4\) 种选择。其余三个区域各与两个已经涂好的区域相邻，而这两个区域颜色不同，所以各有 \(3\) 种选择。

总数为 \(5\cdot4\cdot3\cdot3\cdot3=540\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

The bottom-middle region shares a border with all four other regions. Color it first in \(5\) ways.

The top-left region borders it, giving \(4\) choices. Each of the three remaining regions borders exactly two already-colored regions, which have different colors, leaving \(3\) choices apiece.

The total is \(5\cdot4\cdot3\cdot3\cdot3=540.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

8单选

无限乘积

\[\sqrt[3]{10}\cdot\sqrt[3]{\sqrt[3]{10}}\cdot\sqrt[3]{\sqrt[3]{\sqrt[3]{10}}}\cdots\]

收敛到一个实数。这个数是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The infinite product

\[\sqrt[3]{10}\cdot\sqrt[3]{\sqrt[3]{10}}\cdot\sqrt[3]{\sqrt[3]{\sqrt[3]{10}}}\cdots\]

evaluates to a real number. What is that number?

第 \(k\) 个因子是对 \(10\) 连取 \(k\) 次立方根，也就是 \(10^{\frac{1}{3^k}}\)。

这个乘积等于 \(10\) 的如下幂：\[ \begin{aligned} &\frac13+\frac19+\frac1{27}+\cdots=\frac{\frac{1}{3}}{1-\frac{1}{3}} \\ &=\frac12 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以乘积的值为 \(10^{\frac{1}{2}}=\sqrt{10}\)。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

The \(k\)th factor is \(10\) raised to the \(k\)-fold cube root, namely \(10^{\frac{1}{3^k}}.\)

The product is \(10\) raised to \[ \begin{aligned} &\frac13+\frac19+\frac1{27}+\cdots=\frac{\frac{1}{3}}{1-\frac{1}{3}} \\ &=\frac12. \end{aligned} \]

So the value is \(10^{\frac{1}{2}}=\sqrt{10}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

9单选

万圣节时，\(31\) 个孩子走进校长办公室要糖果。他们可以分为三类：有些总是说谎；有些总是说真话；有些交替说谎和说真话。交替者可以任意选择第一次回答是谎话还是真话，但之后每句话的真假都与前一句相反。校长按以下顺序问每个人相同的三个问题。

“你是说真话者吗？”校长给每个回答“是”的 \(22\) 个孩子一颗糖。

“你是交替者吗？”校长给每个回答“是”的 \(15\) 个孩子一颗糖。

“你是说谎者吗？”校长给每个回答“是”的 \(9\) 个孩子一颗糖。

校长一共给总是说真话的孩子发了多少颗糖？

English（选项同上方）

On Halloween \(31\) children walked into the principal’s office asking for candy. They can be classified into three types: some always lie; some always tell the truth; and some alternately lie and tell the truth. The alternaters arbitrarily choose their first response, either a lie or the truth, but each subsequent statement has the opposite truth value from its predecessor. The principal asked everyone the same three questions in this order.

“Are you a truth-teller?” The principal gave a piece of candy to each of the \(22\) children who answered yes.

“Are you an alternater?” The principal gave a piece of candy to each of the \(15\) children who answered yes.

“Are you a liar?” The principal gave a piece of candy to each of the \(9\) children who answered yes.

How many pieces of candy in all did the principal give to the children who always tell the truth?

对“你是说真话者吗？”这个问题，说真话者和说谎者都会回答“是”，而交替者中只有这次说谎的人会回答“是”。对“你是交替者吗？”这个问题，说谎者回答“是”，交替者中只有这次说真话的人回答“是”。对“你是说谎者吗？”这个问题，只有这次说谎的交替者回答“是”。

按第一次回答拆分交替者。先说谎的交替者回答模式为（谎话，真话，谎话），所以三个问题都回答“是”；先说真话的交替者回答模式为（真话，谎话，真话），所以三个问题都不回答“是”。最后一个问题的 \(9\) 个“是”正好都是先说谎的交替者，因此他们有 \(9\) 人。

第二个问题的 \(15\) 个“是”来自说谎者和这 \(9\) 个交替者，所以说谎者有 \(6\) 人。第一个问题的 \(22\) 个“是”来自说真话者、说谎者以及这 \(9\) 个交替者，所以说真话者有 \(22-6-9=7\) 人。

说真话者只会在第一个问题回答“是”，每人得到一颗糖，共 \(7\cdot1=7\) 颗。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

To “Are you a truth-teller?” the truth-tellers and liars both answer yes, and only alternaters who lie on this question answer yes. To “Are you an alternater?” the liars answer yes, and among alternaters only those telling the truth on this question answer yes. To “Are you a liar?” only alternaters lying on this question answer yes.

Split the alternaters by first response. Those starting with a lie answer (lie, truth, lie), so they say yes to all three questions; those starting truthful answer (truth, lie, truth) and say yes to none of the three. The \(9\) yeses on the last question are exactly the lie-first alternaters, so there are \(9\) of them.

The second question’s \(15\) yeses are the liars plus these \(9,\) so there are \(6\) liars. The first question’s \(22\) yeses are truth-tellers plus liars plus the \(9,\) so the truth-tellers number \(22-6-9=7.\)

Truth-tellers answer yes only to the first question, receiving one candy each, for \(7\cdot1=7\) pieces.

Thus, the correct answer is A.

10单选

有多少种方法可以把 \(1\) 到 \(14\) 的数分成 \(7\) 对，使得每一对中较大的数至少是较小的数的 \(2\) 倍？

English（选项同上方）

What is the number of ways the numbers from \(1\) to \(14\) can be split into \(7\) pairs such that for each pair, the greater number is at least \(2\) times the smaller number?

任何大于或等于 \(8\) 的数都不能作为较小元素（它的两倍超过 \(14\)），所以 \(8\) 到 \(14\) 都是较大元素，\(1\) 到 \(7\) 都是较小元素。

将每个较小数 \(s\) 配到一个较大数 \(g\ge2s\)。从限制最强的开始：\(s=7\) 强制 \(g=14\)（\(1\) 种）；然后 \(s=6\) 可配剩下的 \(\{12,13\}\)（\(2\) 种）；\(s=5\) 有 \(3\) 种；\(s=4\) 有 \(4\) 种；\(s=3\) 有 \(3\) 种；\(s=2\) 有 \(2\) 种；\(s=1\) 有 \(1\) 种。

匹配数为 \(1\cdot2\cdot3\cdot4\cdot3\cdot2\cdot1=144\)。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

Any number \(8\) or larger cannot be a smaller element (its double exceeds \(14\)), so \(8\)–\(14\) are all larger elements and \(1\)–\(7\) are all smaller elements.

Match each smaller \(s\) to a larger \(g\ge2s.\) Processing from the most restrictive: \(s=7\) forces \(g=14\) (\(1\) way); then \(s=6\) has \(\{12,13\}\) left (\(2\)); \(s=5\) has \(3;\) \(s=4\) has \(4;\) \(s=3\) has \(3;\) \(s=2\) has \(2;\) \(s=1\) has \(1.\)

The number of matchings is \(1\cdot2\cdot3\cdot4\cdot3\cdot2\cdot1=144.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

11单选

求所有满足以下条件的实数 \(x\) 的乘积：数轴上 \(\log_6 x\) 与 \(\log_6 9\) 的距离，等于 \(\log_6 10\) 与 \(1\) 的距离的两倍。

English（选项同上方）

What is the product of all real numbers \(x\) such that the distance on the number line between \(\log_6 x\) and \(\log_6 9\) is twice the distance on the number line between \(\log_6 10\) and \(1?\)

右侧的距离为 \(|\log_6 10-1|=\log_6\dfrac53\)，所以它的两倍为 \(2\log_6\dfrac53=\log_6\dfrac{25}{9}\)。

因此 \(\left|\log_6\dfrac{x}{9}\right|=\log_6\dfrac{25}{9}\)，得 \(\dfrac{x}{9}=\dfrac{25}{9}\) 或 \(\dfrac{x}{9}=\dfrac{9}{25}\)，所以 \(x=25\) 或 \(x=\dfrac{81}{25}\)。

它们的乘积为 \(25\cdot\dfrac{81}{25}=81\)。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

The right-hand distance is \(|\log_6 10-1|=\log_6\dfrac53,\) so twice it is \(2\log_6\dfrac53=\log_6\dfrac{25}{9}.\)

Thus \(\left|\log_6\dfrac{x}{9}\right|=\log_6\dfrac{25}{9},\) giving \(\dfrac{x}{9}=\dfrac{25}{9}\) or \(\dfrac{x}{9}=\dfrac{9}{25},\) so \(x=25\) or \(x=\dfrac{81}{25}.\)

Their product is \(25\cdot\dfrac{81}{25}=81.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

12单选

在正四面体 \(ABCD\) 中，设 \(M\) 是 \(\overline{AB}\) 的中点。求 \(\cos(\angle CMD)\)？

English（选项同上方）

Let \(M\) be the midpoint of \(\overline{AB}\) in regular tetrahedron \(ABCD.\) What is \(\cos(\angle CMD)?\)

取棱长为 \(1\)。因为 \(M\) 是 \(\overline{AB}\) 的中点，线段 \(CM\) 和 \(DM\) 是等边三角形面的高，长度都为 \(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\)。另外 \(CD=1\)。

在 \(\triangle CMD\) 中由余弦定理，\[ \begin{aligned} \cos(\angle CMD) &=\frac{\frac34+\frac34-1}{2\cdot\frac34} \\ &=\frac{\frac{1}{2}}{\frac{3}{2}}=\frac13 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Take edge length \(1.\) Since \(M\) is the midpoint of \(\overline{AB},\) segments \(CM\) and \(DM\) are altitudes of the equilateral faces, each of length \(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}.\) Also \(CD=1.\)

By the Law of Cosines in \(\triangle CMD,\) \[ \begin{aligned} \cos(\angle CMD) &=\frac{\frac34+\frac34-1}{2\cdot\frac34} \\ &=\frac{\frac{1}{2}}{\frac{3}{2}}=\frac13. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is B.

13单选

设 \(\mathcal{R}\) 是复平面中的一个区域，由所有可写成复数 \(z_1\) 与 \(z_2\) 之和的复数 \(z\) 组成，其中 \(z_1\) 在线段上，该线段的端点为 \(3\) 和 \(4i\)，且 \(z_2\) 的模至多为 \(1\)。最接近 \(\mathcal{R}\) 面积的整数是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Let \(\mathcal{R}\) be the region in the complex plane consisting of all complex numbers \(z\) that can be written as the sum of complex numbers \(z_1\) and \(z_2,\) where \(z_1\) lies on the segment with endpoints \(3\) and \(4i,\) and \(z_2\) has magnitude at most \(1.\) What integer is closest to the area of \(\mathcal{R}?\)

给线段上的每个点加上半径为 \(1\) 的圆盘，会扫出所有到该线段距离不超过 \(1\) 的点。从 \(3\) 到 \(4i\) 的线段长度为 \(\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5\)。

这个“体育场”形状由一个 \(5\times2\) 的矩形和两个半径为 \(1\) 的半圆组成，面积为 \[5\cdot2+\pi(1)^2=10+\pi\approx13.14\text{。}\]

最接近的整数是 \(13\)。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

Adding a disk of radius \(1\) to every point of the segment sweeps out all points within distance \(1\) of it. The segment from \(3\) to \(4i\) has length \(\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5.\)

This “stadium” is a \(5\times2\) rectangle plus two half-disks of radius \(1,\) with area \[5\cdot2+\pi(1)^2=10+\pi\approx13.14.\]

The closest integer is \(13.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

14单选

求下式的值：

\[ \begin{aligned} &(\log 5)^3+(\log 20)^3 \\ &\quad {}+(\log 8)(\log 0.25) \end{aligned} \]

其中 \(\log\) 表示常用对数。

English（选项同上方）

What is the value of

\[ \begin{aligned} &(\log 5)^3+(\log 20)^3 \\ &\quad {}+(\log 8)(\log 0.25) \end{aligned} \]

where \(\log\) denotes the base-ten logarithm?

令 \(u=\log 2\)。则 \(\log 5=1-u\)，\(\log 20=1+u\)，\(\log 8=3u\)，且 \(\log 0.25=-2u\)。

设 \(a=1-u,\ b=1+u\)，则 \(a+b=2\)，\(ab=1-u^2\)，所以 \[ \begin{gathered} a^3+b^3 \\ =(a+b)\big((a+b)^2-3ab\big) \\ =2\big(4-3(1-u^2)\big) \\ =2+6u^2 \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

一个半径为整数 \(r\) 的圆以 \((r,r)\) 为圆心。对 \(1\le i\le14\)，有若干条互不相同、长度为 \(c_i\) 的线段，连接点 \((0,a_i)\) 与 \((b_i,0)\)，并且都与该圆相切，其中 \(a_i\)、\(b_i\) 和 \(c_i\) 都是正整数，且 \(c_1\le c_2\le\cdots\le c_{14}\)。当 \(r\) 取尽可能小的值时，\(\dfrac{c_{14}}{c_1}\) 是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A circle with integer radius \(r\) is centered at \((r,r).\) Distinct line segments of length \(c_i\) connect points \((0,a_i)\) to \((b_i,0)\) for \(1\le i\le14\) and are tangent to the circle, where \(a_i,\) \(b_i,\) and \(c_i\) are all positive integers and \(c_1\le c_2\le\cdots\le c_{14}.\) What is the ratio \(\dfrac{c_{14}}{c_1}\) for the least possible value of \(r?\)

圆心为 \((r,r)\)、半径为 \(r\) 的圆与两条坐标轴都相切。从 \((0,a)\) 到 \((b,0)\) 的线段满足 \(a^2+b^2=c^2\)；当 \(r\) 等于以 \(a,b\) 为直角边的直角三角形的内切圆半径 \(\tfrac{a+b-c}{2}\) 或半周长 \(\tfrac{a+b+c}{2}\) 时，该线段与圆相切。

在内切圆半径情形中，令 \(x=a-2r\) 且 \(y=b-2r\)。则 \(xy=2r^2\)，每个正因数 \(x\) 确定一条有方向的线段，其中 \(a=x+2r\)，\(b=\frac{2r^2}{x}+2r\)，\(c=x+\frac{2r^2}{x}+2r\)。因此恰有 \(d(2r^2)\) 条这样的线段。

当 \(r=1,2,3,4,5\) 时，数量分别为 \(2,4,6,6,6\)，且半周长情形不可能，因为最小的整数直角三角形半周长为 \(6\)。当 \(r=6\) 时，\(d(72)=12\)，而 \(3\)-\(4\)-\(5\) 三角形再贡献两条有方向的线段。因此 \(6\) 是最小可能半径，并且恰好给出 \(14\) 条线段。

两个半周长情形的线段满足 \(c=5\)。在内切圆半径族中，\(c=x+\frac{72}{x}+12\) 在 \(x=1\) 或 \(72\) 时最大，得到 \(c=85\)。因此 \(c_1=5\)，\(c_{14}=85\)，并且 \(\frac{c_{14}}{c_1}=17\)。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

The circle centered \((r,r)\) with radius \(r\) is tangent to both axes. A segment from \((0,a)\) to \((b,0)\) with \(a^2+b^2=c^2\) is tangent to it when \(r\) equals either the inradius \(\tfrac{a+b-c}{2}\) or the semiperimeter \(\tfrac{a+b+c}{2}\) of the right triangle with legs \(a,b.\)

In the inradius case, put \(x=a-2r\) and \(y=b-2r.\) Then \(xy=2r^2,\) and every positive divisor \(x\) determines one oriented segment, with \(a=x+2r,\) \(b=\frac{2r^2}{x}+2r,\) and \(c=x+\frac{2r^2}{x}+2r.\) Thus there are exactly \(d(2r^2)\) such segments.

For \(r=1,2,3,4,5,\) these counts are \(2,4,6,6,6,\) and no semiperimeter case is possible because the smallest integer right triangle has semiperimeter \(6.\) At \(r=6,\) \(d(72)=12,\) and the \(3\)-\(4\)-\(5\) triangle contributes two more oriented segments. Hence \(6\) is the least possible radius and gives exactly \(14\) segments.

The two semiperimeter segments have \(c=5.\) In the inradius family, \(c=x+\frac{72}{x}+12\) is largest at \(x=1\) or \(72,\) giving \(c=85.\) Therefore \(c_1=5,\) \(c_{14}=85,\) and \(\frac{c_{14}}{c_1}=17.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.
