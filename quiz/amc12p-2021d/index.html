1单选

求下式的值：\[1234 + 2341 + 3412 + 4123\text{？}\]

English（选项同上方）

What is the value of \[1234 + 2341 + 3412 + 4123?\]

数字 \(1, 2, 3, 4\) 在千位、百位、十位和个位上各恰好出现一次，因此每一列的和都是 \(1 + 2 + 3 + 4 = 10\)。

所以总和为 \(10 \cdot 1111 = 11110\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Each of the digits \(1, 2, 3, 4\) appears exactly once in the thousands, hundreds, tens, and units columns. So each column sums to \(1 + 2 + 3 + 4 = 10.\)

The total is therefore \(10 \cdot 1111 = 11110.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

2单选

下图阴影图形的面积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

What is the area of the shaded figure shown below?

外层三角形的顶点依次为 \((1, 0)\)、\((3, 5)\) 和 \((5, 0)\)，底为 \(4\)，高为 \(5\)，所以面积为 \(\tfrac12 \cdot 4 \cdot 5 = 10\)。

被挖去的三角形顶点依次为 \((1, 0)\)、\((3, 2)\) 和 \((5, 0)\)，底为 \(4\)，高为 \(2\)，所以面积为 \(\tfrac12 \cdot 4 \cdot 2 = 4\)。

阴影面积为 \(10 - 4 = 6\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The outer triangle has vertices \((1, 0),\) \((3, 5),\) and \((5, 0),\) giving base \(4\) and height \(5,\) so its area is \(\tfrac12 \cdot 4 \cdot 5 = 10.\)

Removed from it is the triangle with vertices \((1, 0),\) \((3, 2),\) and \((5, 0),\) which has base \(4\) and height \(2,\) so area \(\tfrac12 \cdot 4 \cdot 2 = 4.\)

The shaded area is \(10 - 4 = 6.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

3单选

某天中午，Minneapolis 比 St. Louis 暖 \(N\) 度。到 \(4{:}00\)，Minneapolis 的温度下降了 \(5\) 度，而 St. Louis 的温度上升了 \(3\) 度，此时两城市温度相差 \(2\) 度。所有可能的 \(N\) 值的乘积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

At noon on a certain day, Minneapolis is \(N\) degrees warmer than St. Louis. At \(4{:}00\) the temperature in Minneapolis has fallen by \(5\) degrees while the temperature in St. Louis has risen by \(3\) degrees, at which time the temperatures in the two cities differ by \(2\) degrees. What is the product of all possible values of \(N?\)

中午温差为 \(N\)。之后 Minneapolis 的温度下降 \(5\) 度，St. Louis 的温度上升 \(3\) 度，所以温差改变了 \(8\) 度。新的绝对温差为 \(|N - 8| = 2\)。

由此得到 \(N = 10\) 或 \(N = 6\)，乘积为 \(60\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

At noon the gap is \(N.\) Minneapolis then loses \(5\) degrees and St. Louis gains \(3,\) so the gap changes by \(8.\) The new absolute difference is \(|N - 8| = 2.\)

This gives \(N = 10\) or \(N = 6,\) whose product is \(60.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

设 \(n = 8^{2022}\)。下列哪一个等于 \[\dfrac{n}{4}\text{？}\]

English（选项同上方）

Let \(n = 8^{2022}.\) Which of the following is equal to \[\dfrac{n}{4}?\]

写成二的幂，有 \(n = 8^{2022} = 2^{6066}\)。于是 \[\dfrac{n}{4} = \dfrac{2^{6066}}{2^{2}} = 2^{6064}\text{。}\]

又因为 \(2^{6064} = 4^{3032}\)，所以它对应最后一个选项。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Write \(n = 8^{2022} = 2^{6066}.\) Then \[\dfrac{n}{4} = \dfrac{2^{6066}}{2^{2}} = 2^{6064}.\]

Since \(2^{6064} = 4^{3032},\) this matches the last option.

Thus, the correct answer is E.

5单选

若分数 \(\dfrac{a}{b}\) 的正整数分子 \(a\) 与分母 \(b\) 之和为 \(15\) 则称该分数为特殊分数，分数不必是最简形式。有多少个不同的整数可以表示为两个特殊分数之和？这两个分数可以相同。

English（选项同上方）

Call a fraction \(\dfrac{a}{b},\) not necessarily in the simplest form, special if \(a\) and \(b\) are positive integers whose sum is \(15.\) How many distinct integers can be written as the sum of two, not necessarily different, special fractions?

对 \(1\le a\le14\) 逐一列出 \(\frac{a}{15-a}\)：其中的整数是 \(2,4,14\)；小数部分为 \(\frac{1}{2}\) 的分数是 \(\tfrac12,\tfrac32,\tfrac{13}{2}\)；而 \(\tfrac14,\tfrac{11}{4}\) 的小数部分互补，分别为 \(\frac{1}{4},\frac{3}{4}\)。

其余的小数部分是 \(\frac{1}{14},\frac{2}{13},\frac{4}{11},\frac{2}{3},\frac{7}{8},\frac{1}{7}\)，而它们各自的补数都没有出现。因此只有整数、半整数和四分之一这三类才可能凑出整数和。

整数对给出 \(4, 6, 8, 16, 18, 28\)。半整数对 \(\left(\tfrac12, \tfrac32, \tfrac{13}{2}\right)\) 给出 \(1, 2, 3, 7, 8, 13\)。四分之一型的一对 \(\tfrac14 + \tfrac{11}{4}\) 给出 \(3\)。

去重后得到 \(1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 13, 16, 18, 28\) 共 \(11\) 个不同整数。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Listing \(\frac{a}{15-a}\) for \(1\le a\le14,\) the integers are \(2,4,14;\) the fractions with fractional part \(\frac{1}{2}\) are \(\tfrac12,\tfrac32,\tfrac{13}{2};\) and \(\tfrac14,\tfrac{11}{4}\) have complementary fractional parts \(\frac{1}{4},\frac{3}{4}.\)

The remaining fractional parts are \(\frac{1}{14},\frac{2}{13},\frac{4}{11},\frac{2}{3},\frac{7}{8},\frac{1}{7},\) and none of their complements occurs. Thus only the integer, half-integer, and quarter cases can give integer sums.

Integer pairs give \(4, 6, 8, 16, 18, 28.\) Half-integer pairs \(\left(\tfrac12, \tfrac32, \tfrac{13}{2}\right)\) give \(1, 2, 3, 7, 8, 13.\) The quarter pair \(\tfrac14 + \tfrac{11}{4}\) gives \(3.\)

The distinct integers are \(1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 13, 16, 18, 28,\) a total of \(11.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

6单选

\(16{,}384\) 的最大质因数是 \(2\)，因为 \(16{,}384 = 2^{14}\)。\(16{,}383\) 的最大质因数的数字和是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The greatest prime number that is a divisor of \(16{,}384\) is \(2\) because \(16{,}384 = 2^{14}.\) What is the sum of the digits of the greatest prime number that is a divisor of \(16{,}383?\)

因为 \(16{,}383 = 2^{14} - 1\) \(= (2^{7} - 1)(2^{7} + 1)\) \(= 127 \cdot 129\)，而 \(129 = 3 \cdot 43\)，所以 \(16{,}383 = 3 \cdot 43 \cdot 127\)。

最大质因数是 \(127\)，其各位数字之和为 \(1 + 2 + 7 = 10\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

We have \(16{,}383 = 2^{14} - 1\) \(= (2^{7} - 1)(2^{7} + 1)\) \(= 127 \cdot 129.\) Then \(129 = 3 \cdot 43,\) so \(16{,}383 = 3 \cdot 43 \cdot 127.\)

The greatest prime factor is \(127,\) whose digit sum is \(1 + 2 + 7 = 10.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

7单选

下列哪一个条件足以保证整数 \(x\)、\(y\)、\(z\) 满足方程 \[ \begin{aligned} &x(x - y) + y(y - z) \\ &\quad {}+ z(z - x) = 1 \end{aligned}\text{？}\]

English（选项同上方）

Which of the following conditions is sufficient to guarantee that integers \(x,\) \(y,\) and \(z\) satisfy the equation \[ \begin{aligned} &x(x - y) + y(y - z) \\ &\quad {}+ z(z - x) = 1? \end{aligned} \]

有恒等式 \[ \small \begin{aligned} &2\bigl[x(x-y) + y(y-z) + z(z-x)\bigr] \\ &= (x-y)^2 + (y-z)^2 + (z-x)^2\text{。} \end{aligned} \] 所以原方程成立恰好当这个平方和等于 \(2\)。

由于三个差的和为 \(0\)，这要求其中两个为 \(\pm 1\)，一个为 \(0\)。

选项 D 给出 \(z - x = 0\)、\(x - y = -1\)、\(y - z = 1\)，平方和为 \(1 + 1 + 0 = 2\)。因此对所有满足该条件的整数都成立。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The expression satisfies \[ \small \begin{aligned} &2\bigl[x(x-y) + y(y-z) + z(z-x)\bigr] \\ &= (x-y)^2 + (y-z)^2 + (z-x)^2. \end{aligned} \] So the equation holds exactly when this sum of squares equals \(2.\)

Since the three differences sum to \(0,\) this requires two of them to be \(\pm 1\) and one to be \(0.\)

Option D gives \(z - x = 0,\) \(x - y = -1,\) and \(y - z = 1,\) so the squares are \(1 + 1 + 0 = 2.\) This works for all such integers.

Thus, the correct answer is D.

8单选

一个钝角等腰三角形的两条相等边长度的乘积，等于底边长度与该底边上高的两倍的乘积。这个三角形顶角的度数是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The product of the lengths of the two congruent sides of an obtuse isosceles triangle is equal to the product of the base and twice the triangle’s height to the base. What is the measure, in degrees, of the vertex angle of this triangle?

设两条相等边长为 \(s\)，底边为 \(b\)，到底边的高为 \(h\)。题设条件为 \(s^2 = 2bh\)。

三角形面积既等于 \(\tfrac12 bh\)，也等于 \(\tfrac12 s^2 \sin\theta\)，其中 \(\theta\) 是顶角。因此 \(bh = s^2 \sin\theta\)。

代入 \(s^2 = 2bh\)，得到 \(bh = 2bh\sin\theta\)，所以 \(\sin\theta = \tfrac12\)。由于三角形为钝角，\(\theta = 150^\circ\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Let the congruent sides have length \(s,\) the base be \(b,\) and the height to the base be \(h.\) The given condition is \(s^2 = 2bh.\)

The area equals \(\tfrac12 bh\) and also \(\tfrac12 s^2 \sin\theta,\) where \(\theta\) is the vertex angle. So \(bh = s^2 \sin\theta.\)

Substituting \(s^2 = 2bh\) gives \(bh = 2bh\sin\theta,\) so \(\sin\theta = \tfrac12.\) Since the triangle is obtuse, \(\theta = 150^\circ.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

9单选

等边三角形 \(ABC\) 的边长为 \(6\)。设 \(O\) 是该三角形内切圆的圆心。经过 \(A\)、\(O\) 和 \(C\) 三点的圆的面积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Triangle \(ABC\) is equilateral with side length \(6.\) Suppose that \(O\) is the center of the inscribed circle of this triangle. What is the area of the circle passing through \(A,\) \(O,\) and \(C?\)

对于等边三角形，\(O\) 也是外心，所以 \(OA = OC = \dfrac{6}{\sqrt3} = 2\sqrt3\)。中心角 \(\angle AOC = 120^\circ\)。

在三角形 \(AOC\) 中，边 \(AC = 6\) 所对的角为 \(120^\circ\)，因此该三角形的外接圆半径 \(R'\) 满足 \[2R' = \dfrac{6}{\sin 120^\circ} = 4\sqrt3\text{，}\] 从而 \(R' = 2\sqrt3\)。

该圆的面积为 \(\pi (2\sqrt3)^2 = 12\pi\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

For an equilateral triangle, \(O\) is also the circumcenter, so \(OA = OC = \dfrac{6}{\sqrt3} = 2\sqrt3.\) The central angle \(\angle AOC = 120^\circ.\)

In triangle \(AOC,\) side \(AC = 6\) is opposite the \(120^\circ\) angle, so the circumradius \(R'\) of this triangle satisfies \[2R' = \dfrac{6}{\sin 120^\circ} = 4\sqrt3,\] giving \(R' = 2\sqrt3.\)

The area of the circle is \(\pi (2\sqrt3)^2 = 12\pi.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

10单选

坐标平面上的三点 \((\cos 40^\circ, \sin 40^\circ),\ \) \((\cos 60^\circ, \sin 60^\circ)\)，和 \(\ \ (\cos t^\circ, \sin t^\circ)\) 构成等腰三角形。求 \(0\) 到 \(360\) 之间所有可能的 \(t\) 之和。

English（选项同上方）

What is the sum of all possible values of \(t\) between \(0\) and \(360\) such that the triangle in the coordinate plane whose vertices are \((\cos 40^\circ, \sin 40^\circ),\ \) \((\cos 60^\circ, \sin 60^\circ),\) and \(\ \ (\cos t^\circ, \sin t^\circ)\) is isosceles?

三个点位于单位圆上，对应角分别为 \(40^\circ\)，\(60^\circ\)，\(t^\circ\)。

若第三点到其余两点的距离相等，它必在这两点连线的垂直平分线上，故 \(t = 50\) 或 \(t = 230\)。

若它到 \(40^\circ\) 所对点的距离等于两个已知点间的弦长（角度差为 \(20^\circ\)），则 \(t = 20\)（\(t = 60\) 时三角形退化）。同理，若它到 \(60^\circ\) 所对点的距离相等，则 \(t = 80\)（\(t = 40\) 时退化）。

有效值为 \(50, 230, 20, 80\)，总和为 \(380\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The three points lie on the unit circle at angles \(40^\circ,\) \(60^\circ,\) and \(t^\circ.\) A chord’s length depends only on the angular separation of its endpoints.

If the third point is equidistant from the other two, it lies on the perpendicular bisector: \(t = 50\) or \(t = 230.\)

If its distance to \(40^\circ\) equals the fixed chord (separation \(20^\circ\)), then \(t = 20\) (since \(t = 60\) is degenerate). If its distance to \(60^\circ\) matches, then \(t = 80\) (since \(t = 40\) is degenerate).

The valid values are \(50, 230, 20, 80,\) summing to \(380.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

11单选

Una 同时掷 \(6\) 个标准 \(6\) 面骰，并计算掷出的 \(6\) 个数的乘积。该乘积能被 \(4\) 整除的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Una rolls \(6\) standard \(6\)-sided dice simultaneously and calculates the product of the \(6\) numbers obtained. What is the probability that the product is divisible by \(4?\)

当乘积中至多含一个因子 \(2\) 时，它不能被 \(4\) 整除。每个骰子掷出奇数的概率为 \(\tfrac12\)；恰好贡献一个因子 \(2\)（即掷出 \(2\) 或 \(6\)）的概率为 \(\tfrac13\)；贡献两个因子（即掷出 \(4\)）的概率为 \(\tfrac16\)。

六个骰子全为奇数：\(\left(\tfrac12\right)^6 = \tfrac{1}{64}\)。恰有一个骰子为 \(2\) 或 \(6\)，其余为奇数：\(6 \cdot \tfrac13 \cdot \left(\tfrac12\right)^5 = \tfrac{1}{16} = \tfrac{4}{64}\)。

不能被四整除的概率为 \(\tfrac{1}{64} + \tfrac{4}{64} = \tfrac{5}{64}\)，因此所求概率为 \(1 - \tfrac{5}{64} = \tfrac{59}{64}\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The product fails to be divisible by \(4\) when it has at most one factor of \(2.\) Each die is odd with probability \(\tfrac12,\) contributes exactly one factor of \(2\) (a \(2\) or \(6\)) with probability \(\tfrac13,\) and two factors (a \(4\)) with probability \(\tfrac16.\)

All six odd: \(\left(\tfrac12\right)^6 = \tfrac{1}{64}.\) Exactly one die a \(2\) or \(6\) and the rest odd: \(6 \cdot \tfrac13 \cdot \left(\tfrac12\right)^5 = \tfrac{1}{16} = \tfrac{4}{64}.\)

The complement is \(\tfrac{1}{64} + \tfrac{4}{64} = \tfrac{5}{64},\) so the answer is \(1 - \tfrac{5}{64} = \tfrac{59}{64}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

12单选

对正整数 \(n\)，令 \(f(n)\) 为 \(n\) 的所有正因数之和除以 \(n\) 得到的商。例如， \[ \begin{aligned} f(14) &= (1 + 2 + 7 + 14) \div 14 \\ &= \dfrac{12}{7}\text{。} \end{aligned} \] 求 \(f(768) - f(384)\)？

English（选项同上方）

For \(n\) a positive integer, let \(f(n)\) be the quotient obtained when the sum of all positive divisors of \(n\) is divided by \(n.\) For example, \[ \begin{aligned} f(14) &= (1 + 2 + 7 + 14) \div 14 \\ &= \dfrac{12}{7}. \end{aligned} \] What is \(f(768) - f(384)?\)

因为 \(768 = 2^8 \cdot 3\)，它的因数和是 \((2^9 - 1)(1 + 3)\) \(= 511 \cdot 4 = 2044\)，所以 \(f(768) = \dfrac{2044}{768} = \dfrac{511}{192}\)。

因为 \(384 = 2^7 \cdot 3\)，它的因数和是 \((2^8 - 1)(1 + 3)\) \(= 255 \cdot 4 = 1020\)，所以 \(f(384) = \dfrac{1020}{384} = \dfrac{510}{192}\)。

差为 \(\dfrac{511 - 510}{192} = \dfrac{1}{192}\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Since \(768 = 2^8 \cdot 3,\) its divisor sum is \((2^9 - 1)(1 + 3)\) \(= 511 \cdot 4 = 2044,\) so \(f(768) = \dfrac{2044}{768} = \dfrac{511}{192}.\)

Since \(384 = 2^7 \cdot 3,\) its divisor sum is \((2^8 - 1)(1 + 3)\) \(= 255 \cdot 4 = 1020,\) so \(f(384) = \dfrac{1020}{384} = \dfrac{510}{192}.\)

The difference is \(\dfrac{511 - 510}{192} = \dfrac{1}{192}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

13单选

设 \(c = \dfrac{2\pi}{11}\)。求下式的值：\[\small \dfrac{\sin 3c \cdot \sin 6c \cdot \sin 9c \cdot \sin 12c \cdot \sin 15c}{\sin c \cdot \sin 2c \cdot \sin 3c \cdot \sin 4c \cdot \sin 5c}\text{？}\]

English（选项同上方）

Let \(c = \dfrac{2\pi}{11}.\) What is the value of \[\small \dfrac{\sin 3c \cdot \sin 6c \cdot \sin 9c \cdot \sin 12c \cdot \sin 15c}{\sin c \cdot \sin 2c \cdot \sin 3c \cdot \sin 4c \cdot \sin 5c}?\]

每个角都可写成 \(kc = \dfrac{2\pi k}{11}\)。按 \(2\pi\) 取模后，\(\sin 12c = \sin c\)，且 \(\sin 15c = \sin 4c\)。

因此分子为 \(\sin 3c \cdot \sin 6c \cdot \sin 9c\) \(\cdot \sin c \cdot \sin 4c\)。约去公共因子 \(\sin c\)、\(\sin 3c\)、\(\sin 4c\)，剩下 \(\dfrac{\sin 6c \cdot \sin 9c}{\sin 2c \cdot \sin 5c}\)。

又有 \(\sin 9c = \sin\left(2\pi - 2c\right) = -\sin 2c\)，并且 \(\sin 6c = \sin\left(2\pi - 5c\right) = -\sin 5c\)，所以比值为 \(\dfrac{(-\sin 5c)(-\sin 2c)}{\sin 2c \cdot \sin 5c} = 1\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Write each angle as \(kc = \dfrac{2\pi k}{11}.\) Reducing modulo \(2\pi,\) \(\sin 12c = \sin c\) and \(\sin 15c = \sin 4c.\)

So the numerator is \(\sin 3c \cdot \sin 6c \cdot \sin 9c\) \(\cdot \sin c \cdot \sin 4c.\) Cancelling the common factors \(\sin c,\) \(\sin 3c,\) \(\sin 4c\) leaves \(\dfrac{\sin 6c \cdot \sin 9c}{\sin 2c \cdot \sin 5c}.\)

Now \(\sin 9c = \sin\left(2\pi - 2c\right) = -\sin 2c\) and \(\sin 6c = \sin\left(2\pi - 5c\right) = -\sin 5c,\) so the ratio equals \(\dfrac{(-\sin 5c)(-\sin 2c)}{\sin 2c \cdot \sin 5c} = 1.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

14单选

设 \(P(z)\)、\(Q(z)\)、\(R(z)\) 是实系数多项式，次数分别为 \(2\)、\(3\)、\(6\)，常数项分别为 \(1\)、\(2\)、\(3\)。令 \(N\) 为满足方程 \[P(z) \cdot Q(z) = R(z)\] 的不同复数 \(z\) 的个数。\(N\) 的最小可能值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Suppose that \(P(z),\) \(Q(z),\) and \(R(z)\) are polynomials with real coefficients, having degrees \(2,\) \(3,\) and \(6,\) respectively, and constant terms \(1,\) \(2,\) and \(3,\) respectively. Let \(N\) be the number of distinct complex numbers \(z\) that satisfy the equation \[P(z) \cdot Q(z) = R(z).\] What is the minimum possible value of \(N?\)

令 \(D(z) = P(z)Q(z) - R(z)\)。因为 \(P Q\) 的次数为 \(5\)，而 \(R\) 的次数为 \(6\)，所以 \(D\) 的次数为 \(6\)。它的常数项为 \(1 \cdot 2 - 3 = -1 \neq 0\)。

由于 \(R\) 除这些限制外没有其他限制，\(D\) 可以成为任意次数为 \(6\)、常数项为 \(-1\) 的实系数多项式，例如 \(-(z - 1)^6\)。

这样的多项式只有一个不同的根，所以最小值为 \(N = 1\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Let \(D(z) = P(z)Q(z) - R(z).\) Since \(P Q\) has degree \(5\) and \(R\) has degree \(6,\) the degree of \(D\) is \(6.\) Its constant term is \(1 \cdot 2 - 3 = -1 \neq 0.\)

Because \(R\) is otherwise unconstrained, \(D\) can be made equal to any real degree-\(6\) polynomial with constant term \(-1,\) for instance \(-(z - 1)^6.\)

Such a polynomial has a single distinct root, so the minimum is \(N = 1.\)

从 \(n\) 变为 \(n + 1\) 时，每个余数 \(n \bmod m\) 都增加 \(1\)，除非 \(n + 1\) 是 \(m\) 的倍数，此时它从 \(m - 1\) 降为 \(0\)。所以 \[ \begin{aligned} &R(n+1) - R(n) \\ &= 9 - \sum_{\substack{2 \le m \le 10 \\ m \mid n+1}} m \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

从 \(2\) 到 \(10\) 的互异整数之和等于 \(9\) 的方式只有 \(\{9\},\{2,7\},\{3,6\},\{4,5\}\)，或 \(\{2,3,4\}\)。因数的闭包性质排除了除 \(\{2,7\}\) 外的所有情形：例如，\(9\) 的倍数也有因数 \(3\)，而 \(6\) 的倍数也有因数 \(2\) 和 \(3\)。因此 \(n+1\) 必须是 \(14\) 的倍数，并且在 \(\{2,\ldots,10\}\) 中没有其他因数。

检查所需范围内的倍数 \(14,28,42,56,70,84,98\)，只留下 \(14\) 和 \(98\)。所以 \(n=13\) 或 \(n=97\)，共有 \(2\) 个值。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Going from \(n\) to \(n + 1,\) each remainder \(n \bmod m\) increases by \(1\) unless \(n + 1\) is divisible by \(m,\) in which case it drops from \(m - 1\) to \(0.\) So \[ \begin{aligned} &R(n+1) - R(n) \\ &= 9 - \sum_{\substack{2 \le m \le 10 \\ m \mid n+1}} m. \end{aligned} \]

Distinct integers from \(2\) through \(10\) can sum to \(9\) only as \(\{9\},\{2,7\},\{3,6\},\{4,5\},\) or \(\{2,3,4\}.\) Divisibility closure eliminates every case except \(\{2,7\}:\) for example, a multiple of \(9\) also has divisor \(3,\) and a multiple of \(6\) also has divisors \(2\) and \(3.\) Thus \(n+1\) must be a multiple of \(14\) with no other divisor in \(\{2,\ldots,10\}.\)

Testing the multiples \(14,28,42,56,70,84,98\) in the required range leaves only \(14\) and \(98.\) Hence \(n=13\) or \(n=97,\) giving \(2\) values.

Thus, the correct answer is C.
