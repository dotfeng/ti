Thus, the correct answer is A.

For what real values of \(k,\) other than \(k=0,\) does the equation \(x^2+kx+k^2=0\) have real roots?

判别式为 \[ k^2-4k^2=-3k^2\text{。}\]对每个非零实数 \(k\)，它都为负，因此该二次方程没有实根。

The discriminant is \[ k^2-4k^2=-3k^2. \] For every nonzero real \(k,\) this is negative, so the quadratic has no real roots.

Thus, the correct answer is E.

在一次舞会上，一群男孩和女孩按如下方式轮流跳舞：第一个男孩与 \(5\) 个女孩跳舞，第二个男孩与 \(6\) 个女孩跳舞，依此类推，最后一个男孩与所有女孩跳舞。若 \(b\) 表示男孩人数，\(g\) 表示女孩人数，则：

English（选项同上方）

At a dance party a group of boys and girls exchange dances as follows: one boy dances with \(5\) girls, a second boy dances with \(6\) girls, and so on, the last boy dancing with all the girls. If \(b\) represents the number of boys and \(g\) the number of girls, then:

数列 \(5,6,7,\ldots\) 的 \(b\) 项中，末项为 \[ 5+(b-1)=b+4\text{。}\]这个末项等于女孩总数 \(g\)。因此 \(g=b+4\)，即 \(b=g-4\)。

一个四边形内接于圆。在四边形外部的四个弓形中各作一个圆周角，则这四个角的度数之和为：

English（选项同上方）

A quadrilateral is inscribed in a circle. If an angle is inscribed into each of the four segments outside the quadrilateral, the sum of these four angles, expressed in degrees, is:

设各边所截的四段劣弧度数为 \(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_4\)，其和为 \(360^\circ\)。与弧 \(\alpha_i\) 对应的外侧弓形内的圆周角所对的是另一段弧，因此其度数为 \[ \frac{360^\circ-\alpha_i}{2}\text{。}\]所以四个角的度数之和为 \[ \frac{4(360^\circ)-360^\circ}{2}=540^\circ\text{。}\]

所以正确答案为 D。

English

Let the four minor arcs cut off by the sides have measures \(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_4,\) whose sum is \(360^\circ.\) The angle in the outside segment corresponding to arc \(\alpha_i\) subtends the other arc, so its measure is \[ \frac{360^\circ-\alpha_i}{2}. \] The sum of the four angles is therefore \[ \frac{4(360^\circ)-360^\circ}{2}=540^\circ. \]

Thus, the correct answer is D.

Thus, the correct answer is A.

English（选项同上方）

令 \(t=x-1\)。则 \(-5\lt t\lt0\)，且表达式变为 \[ \frac{t^2+1}{2t} =\frac12\left(t+\frac1t\right)\text{。}\]当 \(t\lt0\) 时，\(t+\frac{1}{t}\le-2\)，等号在 \(t=-1\) 时成立。此值允许且对应 \(x=0\)。因此表达式至多为 \(-1\)，其最大值为 \(-1\)。

所以正确答案为 E。

English

Set \(t=x-1.\) Then \(-5\lt t\lt0,\) and the expression becomes \[ \frac{t^2+1}{2t} =\frac12\left(t+\frac1t\right). \] For \(t\lt0,\) \(t+\frac{1}{t}\le-2,\) with equality when \(t=-1.\) That value is allowed and corresponds to \(x=0.\) Hence the expression is at most \(-1,\) and its maximum is \(-1.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

由于 \(AF\perp BD\) 且 \(PQ\perp AF\)，有 \(PQ\parallel BD\)。又因 \(PS\perp BD\)，所以 \(PS\parallel AF\)。因此四边形 \(QPSF\) 是矩形，且 \[ PS=QF\text{。}\]令 \(T=PQ\cap AC\)。矩形的两条对角线与边 \(AB\) 所成角相等，所以 \(\angle PAT=\angle APT\)，从而 \(AT=PT\)。直角三角形 \(ATQ\) 和 \(PTR\) 在 \(T\) 处有相同的角，故相似。由于它们的斜边 \(AT\) 和 \(PT\) 相等，\(AQ=PR\)。因此 \[ PR+PS=AQ+QF=AF\text{。}\]

English
