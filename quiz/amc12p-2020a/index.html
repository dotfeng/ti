1单选

Carlos 拿走了一个完整馅饼的 \(70\%\)。Maria 拿走了剩余部分的三分之一。整个馅饼还剩下多少？

English（选项同上方）

Carlos took \(70\%\) of a whole pie. Maria took one third of the remainder. What portion of the whole pie was left?

Carlos 拿走 \(70\%\) 后，剩余部分为 \(30\%\)。

Maria 拿走其中三分之一，即 \(\tfrac13 \cdot 30\% = 10\%\)，所以还剩 \(30\% - 10\% = 20\%\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

After Carlos takes \(70\%,\) the remaining portion is \(30\%.\)

Maria takes one third of this, namely \(\tfrac13 \cdot 30\% = 10\%,\) leaving \(30\% - 10\% = 20\%.\)

Thus, C is the correct answer.

2单选

缩写 AMC 显示在下面的矩形网格中，网格线间距为 \(1\) 个单位。组成缩写 AMC 的所有线段长度之和是多少个单位？

English（选项同上方）

The acronym AMC is shown in the rectangular grid below with grid lines spaced \(1\) unit apart. In units, what is the sum of the lengths of the line segments that form the acronym AMC?

把每个字母拆成线段。字母 \(A\) 包含一条长为 \(2\sqrt{2}\) 的斜线、一条长为 \(2\) 的竖线，以及一条长为 \(1\) 的横线。

字母 \(M\) 有两条长为 \(2\) 的竖线和两条各长 \(\sqrt{2}\) 的斜线。字母 \(C\) 由三条长为 \(2\) 的边组成。

直线段的总长为 \(2 + 1 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 13\)，斜线段的总长为 \(2\sqrt2 + \sqrt2 + \sqrt2 = 4\sqrt2\)。

总和为 \(13 + 4\sqrt2\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Split each letter into its segments. The \(A\) is a diagonal of length \(2\sqrt{2},\) a vertical of length \(2,\) and a crossbar of length \(1.\)

The \(M\) has two verticals of length \(2\) and two diagonals of length \(\sqrt{2}\) each. The \(C\) is three sides of length \(2.\)

The straight pieces total \(2 + 1 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 13,\) and the diagonal pieces total \(2\sqrt2 + \sqrt2 + \sqrt2 = 4\sqrt2.\)

The sum is \(13 + 4\sqrt2.\)

Thus, C is the correct answer.

3单选

一名司机以每小时 \(60\) 英里的速度行驶 \(2\) 小时，在此期间她的汽车每加仑汽油可行驶 \(30\) 英里。她每英里获得 \(\$0.50\) 报酬，唯一开销是每加仑 \(\$2.00\) 的汽油。扣除这项开销后，她每小时的净报酬是多少美元？

English（选项同上方）

A driver travels for \(2\) hours at \(60\) miles per hour, during which her car gets \(30\) miles per gallon of gasoline. She is paid \(\$0.50\) per mile, and her only expense is gasoline at \(\$2.00\) per gallon. What is her net rate of pay, in dollars per hour, after this expense?

\(2\) 小时内她行驶 \(120\) 英里，收入为 \(120 \cdot \$0.50 = \$60\)。

她用掉 \(120 \div 30 = 4\) 加仑汽油，花费 \(4 \cdot \$2.00 = \$8\)。

净收入为 \(\$60 - \$8 = \$52\)，因此每小时为 \(\$52 \div 2 = \$26\)。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

In \(2\) hours she drives \(120\) miles, earning \(120 \cdot \$0.50 = \$60.\)

She uses \(120 \div 30 = 4\) gallons, costing \(4 \cdot \$2.00 = \$8.\)

Her net earnings are \(\$60 - \$8 = \$52,\) so her rate is \(\$52 \div 2 = \$26\) per hour.

Thus, E is the correct answer.

4单选

有多少个 \(4\) 位正整数，也就是从 \(1000\) 到 \(9999\)（含端点）的整数，只含偶数数字且能被 \(5\) 整除？

English（选项同上方）

How many \(4\)-digit positive integers (that is, integers between \(1000\) and \(9999,\) inclusive) having only even digits are divisible by \(5?\)

要能被 \(5\) 整除，末位必须是 \(0\) 或 \(5\)，又因为所有数字都要是偶数，所以末位只能是 \(0\)。

首位是非零偶数：\(2, 4, 6, 8\)，共有 \(4\) 种选择。中间两位各可以是任意偶数 \(0, 2, 4, 6, 8\)，各有 \(5\) 种选择。

总数为 \(4 \cdot 5 \cdot 5 \cdot 1 = 100\)。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

To be divisible by \(5\) the last digit is \(0\) or \(5,\) and to be even it must be \(0.\) So the units digit is fixed.

The leading digit is a nonzero even digit: \(2, 4, 6, 8\) give \(4\) choices. Each of the two middle digits is any even digit \(0, 2, 4, 6, 8,\) giving \(5\) choices each.

The total is \(4 \cdot 5 \cdot 5 \cdot 1 = 100.\)

Thus, B is the correct answer.

5单选

从 \(-10\) 到 \(14\)（含端点）的 \(25\) 个整数可以排列成一个 \(5\) 乘 \(5\) 的方阵，使得每一行的和、每一列的和以及两条主对角线上的数之和都相同。这个共同的和是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The \(25\) integers from \(-10\) to \(14,\) inclusive, can be arranged to form a \(5\)-by-\(5\) square in which the sum of the numbers in each row, the sum of the numbers in each column, and the sum of the numbers along each of the main diagonals are all the same. What is the value of this common sum?

这 \(25\) 个整数的和为 \(\dfrac{(-10 + 14) \cdot 25}{2} = 50\)。

五行的和都相同，并且合起来就是总和，所以每行的和为 \(50 \div 5 = 10\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

The sum of the \(25\) integers is \(\dfrac{(-10 + 14) \cdot 25}{2} = 50.\)

The five rows each have the same sum and together account for the total, so each row sums to \(50 \div 5 = 10.\)

Thus, C is the correct answer.

6单选

在下图所示的平面图形中，有 \(3\) 个单位正方形已被涂色。至少还需要涂色多少个额外的单位正方形，才能使所得图形有两条对称轴？

English（选项同上方）

In the plane figure shown below, \(3\) of the unit squares have been shaded. What is the least number of additional unit squares that must be shaded so that the resulting figure has two lines of symmetry?

要同时具有这两种对称性，对称轴必须是 \(5\) 乘 \(4\) 网格的竖直和水平中心线。每个已涂色正方形都会迫使它关于每条轴反射得到的正方形也被涂色。

顶部的正方形不在中心线上，所以它的反射组共有 \(4\) 个正方形，需要再涂 \(3\) 个。中间的正方形在中央列上，所以它的反射组共有 \(2\) 个正方形，需要再涂 \(1\) 个。右下的正方形同样有一个大小为 \(4\) 的反射组，需要再涂 \(3\) 个。

至少还需涂色的正方形数为 \(3 + 1 + 3 = 7\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

For both symmetries, the lines must be the vertical and horizontal center lines of the \(5\)-by-\(4\) grid. Every shaded square then forces the squares obtained by reflecting it across each line.

The top square lies off-center, so its reflection group has \(4\) squares, requiring \(3\) more. The middle square sits on the central column, so its group has \(2\) squares, requiring \(1\) more. The bottom-right square again has a group of \(4,\) requiring \(3\) more.

The least number of additional squares is \(3 + 1 + 3 = 7.\)

Thus, D is the correct answer.

7单选

七个体积分别为 \(1\)、\(8\)、\(27\)、\(64\)、\(125\)、\(216\) 和 \(343\) 立方单位的立方体竖直堆叠成一座塔，其中立方体的体积从下到上递减。除了最底下的立方体外，每个立方体的底面都完全落在它下方立方体的顶面上。这座塔的总表面积（包括底面）是多少平方单位？

English（选项同上方）

Seven cubes, whose volumes are \(1,\) \(8,\) \(27,\) \(64,\) \(125,\) \(216,\) and \(343\) cubic units, are stacked vertically to form a tower in which the volumes of the cubes decrease from bottom to top. Except for the bottom cube, the bottom face of each cube lies completely on top of the cube below it. What is the total surface area of the tower (including the bottom) in square units?

这些立方体的边长为 \(1, 2, \ldots, 7\)。边长为 \(k\) 的立方体的四个侧面贡献 \(4k^2\)，所以竖直侧面的总面积为 \(4(1^2 + 2^2 + \cdots + 7^2)\) \(= 4 \cdot 140 = 560\)。

从正上方看，每一块朝上的水平面都无重叠地投影到底部的 \(7 \times 7\) 正方形上，面积为 \(49\)。从下方看同理，再得到 \(49\)。

总表面积为 \(560 + 49 + 49 = 658\)。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

The side lengths are \(1, 2, \ldots, 7.\) The four side faces of cube \(k\) contribute \(4k^2,\) so the vertical faces total \(4(1^2 + 2^2 + \cdots + 7^2)\) \(= 4 \cdot 140 = 560.\)

Viewed from directly above, every upward-facing horizontal patch projects onto the \(7 \times 7\) base without overlap, giving \(49.\) Viewed from below, the same is true, giving another \(49.\)

The total surface area is \(560 + 49 + 49 = 658.\)

Thus, B is the correct answer.

8单选

下列 \(4040\) 个数的中位数是多少？

\(1\)，\(2\)，\(3\)，\(\ldots\)，\(2020\)，\(1^2\)，\(2^2\)，\(3^2\)，\(\ldots\)，\(2020^2\)。

English（选项同上方）

What is the median of the following list of \(4040\) numbers?

\(1,\) \(2,\) \(3,\) \(\ldots,\) \(2020,\) \(1^2,\) \(2^2,\) \(3^2,\) \(\ldots,\) \(2020^2\)

中位数是第 \(2020\) 小和第 \(2021\) 小的值的平均。

不超过 \(2020\) 的完全平方数为 \(1^2, \ldots, 44^2\)（因为 \(44^2 = 1936\) 且 \(45^2 = 2025\)），所以共有 \(44\) 个。

在这个列表中，数值 \(\le 1976\) 的项包括 \(1976\) 个整数 \(1, \ldots, 1976\) 以及这 \(44\) 个平方数，总数为 \(1976 + 44 = 2020\)。

因此第 \(2020\) 个值是 \(1976\)，第 \(2021\) 个值是 \(1977\)，中位数为 \(\dfrac{1976 + 1977}{2} = 1976.5\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

The median is the average of the \(2020\)th and \(2021\)st smallest values.

The perfect squares that are at most \(2020\) are \(1^2, \ldots, 44^2\) (since \(44^2 = 1936\) and \(45^2 = 2025\)), so there are \(44\) of them.

Among the list, the numbers \(\le 1976\) are the \(1976\) integers \(1, \ldots, 1976\) together with those \(44\) squares, totaling \(1976 + 44 = 2020.\)

Thus the \(2020\)th value is \(1976\) and the \(2021\)st value is \(1977,\) making the median \(\dfrac{1976 + 1977}{2} = 1976.5.\)

Thus, C is the correct answer.

9单选

方程 \(\tan(2x) = \cos\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)\) 在区间 \([0, 2\pi]\) 上有多少个解？

English（选项同上方）

How many solutions does the equation \(\tan(2x) = \cos\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)\) have on the interval \([0, 2\pi]?\)

在 \([0, 2\pi]\) 上，\(\cos\left(\tfrac{x}{2}\right)\) 的图像是一条从 \(1\) 单调下降到 \(-1\) 的弧。

函数 \(\tan(2x)\) 的周期为 \(\tfrac{\pi}{2}\)，竖直渐近线位于 \(x = \tfrac{\pi}{4}, \tfrac{3\pi}{4}, \tfrac{5\pi}{4}, \tfrac{7\pi}{4}\)。它们将区间分成五个分支。在每个分支上，\(\tan(2x)\) 严格递增，而 \(\cos(\tfrac{x}{2})\) 递减，所以每个分支至多有一个交点。

中间三个分支都从 \(-\infty\) 变化到 \(+\infty\)，所以各有一个交点。在第一个分支上，\(\tan(0)=0\lt1=\cos(0)\)，而正切趋于 \(+\infty\)。在最后一个分支上，正切从 \(-\infty\) 开始，在终点为 \(0\gt-1=\cos\pi\)。因此两个外侧分支也各有一个交点，总计 \(5\) 个。

所以 E 是正确答案。

English

On \([0, 2\pi],\) the graph of \(\cos\left(\tfrac{x}{2}\right)\) is a single arc decreasing from \(1\) down to \(-1.\)

The function \(\tan(2x)\) has period \(\tfrac{\pi}{2}\) with vertical asymptotes at \(x = \tfrac{\pi}{4}, \tfrac{3\pi}{4}, \tfrac{5\pi}{4}, \tfrac{7\pi}{4}.\) These split the interval into five branches. On every branch \(\tan(2x)\) is strictly increasing, while \(\cos(\tfrac{x}{2})\) is decreasing, so there is at most one intersection per branch.

Each of the three interior branches runs from \(-\infty\) to \(+\infty,\) so each has one intersection. On the first branch, \(\tan(0)=0\lt1=\cos(0)\) and the tangent tends to \(+\infty.\) On the last, the tangent starts at \(-\infty\) and ends at \(0\gt-1=\cos\pi.\) Thus the two outer branches also have one intersection each, for \(5\) total.

Thus, E is the correct answer.

10单选

存在唯一的正整数 \(n\) 满足 \[\log_2(\log_{16} n) = \log_4(\log_4 n)\text{。}\]

\(n\) 的各位数字之和是多少？

English（选项同上方）

There is a unique positive integer \(n\) such that \[\log_2(\log_{16} n) = \log_4(\log_4 n).\]

What is the sum of the digits of \(n?\)

因为 \(\log_{16} n = \tfrac12 \log_4 n\)，令 \(y = \log_4 n\)。方程变为 \(\log_2\left(\tfrac{y}{2}\right) = \log_4 y = \tfrac12 \log_2 y\)。

两边乘以 \(2\) 得到 \(\log_2\left(\tfrac{y}{2}\right)^2 = \log_2 y\)，所以 \(\left(\tfrac{y}{2}\right)^2 = y\)，从而 \(y = 4\)。

因此 \(\log_4 n = 4\)，所以 \(n = 4^4 = 256\)，各位数字之和为 \(2 + 5 + 6 = 13\)。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

Since \(\log_{16} n = \tfrac12 \log_4 n,\) set \(y = \log_4 n.\) The equation becomes \(\log_2\left(\tfrac{y}{2}\right) = \log_4 y = \tfrac12 \log_2 y.\)

Multiplying by \(2\) gives \(\log_2\left(\tfrac{y}{2}\right)^2 = \log_2 y,\) so \(\left(\tfrac{y}{2}\right)^2 = y,\) which yields \(y = 4.\)

Then \(\log_4 n = 4,\) so \(n = 4^4 = 256,\) and the digit sum is \(2 + 5 + 6 = 13.\)

Thus, E is the correct answer.

11单选

一只青蛙坐在点 \((1, 2)\) 处，开始一系列跳跃。每次跳跃都平行于一条坐标轴，长度为 \(1\)，且每次跳跃的方向（上、下、右、左）都独立随机选择。当青蛙到达顶点为 \((0, 0)\)、\((0, 4)\)、\((4, 4)\) 和 \((4, 0)\) 的正方形的一条边时，跳跃序列结束。跳跃序列在该正方形的竖直边上结束的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A frog sitting at the point \((1, 2)\) begins a sequence of jumps, where each jump is parallel to one of the coordinate axes and has length \(1,\) and the direction of each jump (up, down, right, or left) is chosen independently at random. The sequence ends when the frog reaches a side of the square with vertices \((0, 0),\) \((0, 4),\) \((4, 4),\) and \((4, 0).\) What is the probability that the sequence of jumps ends on a vertical side of the square?

令 \(P(x, y)\) 为在竖直边上结束的概率。在竖直边上 \(P = 1\)，在水平边上 \(P = 0\)，在内部点处 \(P\) 是四个相邻点概率的平均。

由左右对称性，\(P(2, 2) = \tfrac12\)。令 \(a = P(1, 2)\)，\(b = P(1, 1) = P(1, 3)\)，且 \(c = P(2, 1) = P(2, 3)\)。则

\(a = \tfrac14\left(1 + \tfrac12 + 2b\right)\)，\(\;b = \tfrac14(1 + c + a)\)，且 \(c = \tfrac14\left(2b + \tfrac12\right)\)。

代入可得 \(b = \tfrac12\)，因此 \(a = \tfrac38 + \tfrac12 b = \tfrac58\)。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Let \(P(x, y)\) be the probability of ending on a vertical side. On a vertical side \(P = 1,\) on a horizontal side \(P = 0,\) and at an interior point \(P\) is the average of its four neighbors.

By left-right symmetry \(P(2, 2) = \tfrac12.\) Let \(a = P(1, 2),\) \(b = P(1, 1) = P(1, 3),\) and \(c = P(2, 1) = P(2, 3).\) Then

\(a = \tfrac14\left(1 + \tfrac12 + 2b\right),\) \(\;b = \tfrac14(1 + c + a),\) and \(c = \tfrac14\left(2b + \tfrac12\right).\)

Substituting gives \(b = \tfrac12,\) hence \(a = \tfrac38 + \tfrac12 b = \tfrac58.\)

Thus, B is the correct answer.

12单选

坐标平面中的直线 \(\ell\) 方程为 \(3x - 5y + 40 = 0\)。将这条直线绕点 \((20, 20)\) 逆时针旋转 \(45^\circ\)，得到直线 \(k\)。直线 \(k\) 的 \(x\) 轴截距的 \(x\) 坐标是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Line \(\ell\) in the coordinate plane has the equation \(3x - 5y + 40 = 0.\) This line is rotated \(45^\circ\) counterclockwise about the point \((20, 20)\) to obtain line \(k.\) What is the \(x\)-coordinate of the \(x\)-intercept of line \(k?\)

注意 \((20, 20)\) 满足 \(3x - 5y + 40 = 0\)，所以它在 \(\ell\) 上，旋转后仍在 \(k\) 上。\(\ell\) 的斜率为 \(\tfrac{3}{5}\)。

旋转 \(45^\circ\) 后的斜率为 \(\dfrac{\tfrac35 + 1}{1 - \tfrac35} = \dfrac{\tfrac85}{\tfrac25} = 4\)。

直线 \(k\) 为 \(y - 20 = 4(x - 20)\)。令 \(y = 0\) 得到 \(-20 = 4(x - 20)\)，所以 \(x - 20 = -5\)，即 \(x = 15\)。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Note \((20, 20)\) satisfies \(3x - 5y + 40 = 0,\) so it is on \(\ell\) and remains on \(k.\) The slope of \(\ell\) is \(\tfrac{3}{5}.\)

Rotating by \(45^\circ\) gives slope \(\dfrac{\tfrac35 + 1}{1 - \tfrac35} = \dfrac{\tfrac85}{\tfrac25} = 4.\)

Line \(k\) is \(y - 20 = 4(x - 20).\) Setting \(y = 0\) gives \(-20 = 4(x - 20),\) so \(x - 20 = -5\) and \(x = 15.\)

Thus, B is the correct answer.

13单选

存在都大于 \(1\) 的整数 \(a\)、\(b\) 和 \(c\)，使得对所有 \(N \gt 1\) 都有 \[\sqrt[a]{N \sqrt[b]{N \sqrt[c]{N}}} = \sqrt[36]{N^{25}}\text{。}\]\(b\) 是多少？

English（选项同上方）

There are integers \(a,\) \(b,\) and \(c,\) each greater than \(1,\) such that \[\sqrt[a]{N \sqrt[b]{N \sqrt[c]{N}}} = \sqrt[36]{N^{25}}\] for all \(N \gt 1.\) What is \(b?\)

左边等于 \(N\) 的 \(\dfrac{1}{a} + \dfrac{1}{ab} + \dfrac{1}{abc}\) 次幂，而这个指数必须等于 \(\dfrac{25}{36}\)。

因为 \(b,c\ge2\)，这个指数至多为 \(\dfrac{7}{4a}\)。如果 \(a\ge3\)，它至多为 \(\dfrac{7}{12}\lt\dfrac{25}{36}\)，所以 \(a=2\)。方程变为 \(\dfrac{1}{2b}+\dfrac{1}{2bc}=\dfrac{7}{36}\)。

清除分母得到 \(c(7b-18)=18\)。令 \(d=7b-18\)，则 \(d\) 是 \(18\) 的正因数。在 \(1,2,3,6,9,18\) 中，只有 \(d=3\) 能使 \(b=\dfrac{d+18}{7}\) 为整数。因此 \(b=3\) 且 \(c=6\)。

所以 B 是正确答案。

English

The left side equals \(N\) raised to the exponent \(\dfrac{1}{a} + \dfrac{1}{ab} + \dfrac{1}{abc},\) which must equal \(\dfrac{25}{36}.\)

Because \(b,c\ge2,\) this exponent is at most \(\dfrac{7}{4a}.\) If \(a\ge3,\) it is at most \(\dfrac{7}{12}\lt\dfrac{25}{36},\) so \(a=2.\) The equation becomes \(\dfrac{1}{2b}+\dfrac{1}{2bc}=\dfrac{7}{36}.\)

Clearing denominators gives \(c(7b-18)=18.\) Set \(d=7b-18,\) so \(d\) is a positive divisor of \(18.\) Among \(1,2,3,6,9,18,\) only \(d=3\) makes \(b=\dfrac{d+18}{7}\) an integer. Hence \(b=3\) and \(c=6.\)

Thus, B is the correct answer.

14单选

正八边形 \(ABCDEFGH\) 的面积为 \(n\)。设四边形 \(ACEG\) 的面积为 \(m\)。\(\dfrac{m}{n}\) 是多少？

English（选项同上方）

With \(p^2 = 1,\) \(q^2 = 3,\) \(r^2 = 4,\) letting \(S = s^2\) gives \(3(26 + S^2) = (8 + S)^2,\) so \(S^2 - 8S + 7 = 0\) and \(S = 1\) or \(S = 7.\)

A triangle of side \(1\) cannot contain a point at distance \(2\) from a vertex, so \(S = 7\) and \(s = \sqrt{7}.\)

Thus, B is the correct answer.

数 \(a = \dfrac{p}{q}\)，其中 \(p\) 和 \(q\) 是互质的正整数，具有如下性质：所有满足 \[\lfloor x \rfloor \cdot \{x\} = a \cdot x^2\] 的实数 \(x\) 的和为 \(420\)，其中 \(\lfloor x \rfloor\) 表示不超过 \(x\) 的最大整数，\(\{x\} = x - \lfloor x \rfloor\) 表示 \(x\) 的小数部分。\(p + q\) 是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The number \(a = \dfrac{p}{q},\) where \(p\) and \(q\) are relatively prime positive integers, has the property that the sum of all real numbers \(x\) satisfying \[\lfloor x \rfloor \cdot \{x\} = a \cdot x^2\] is \(420,\) where \(\lfloor x \rfloor\) denotes the greatest integer less than or equal to \(x\) and \(\{x\} = x - \lfloor x \rfloor\) denotes the fractional part of \(x.\) What is \(p + q?\)

没有负数解，而 \(x=0\) 总是一个解。对 \(n\ge1\) 且 \(x\in[n,n+1)\)，令 \(y=\frac{x}{n}\)。方程变为 \(ay^2-y+1=0\)。它的根必须为实数，所以 \(0\lt a\le\tfrac14\)。

若两个根为 \(\alpha\le\beta\)，它们的和与积都等于 \(\frac{1}{a}\)，所以 \((\alpha-1)(\beta-1)=1\)。写成 \[ \alpha=1+\dfrac1u,\qquad \beta=1+u \] 其中 \(u\ge1\)。此时 \(a=\dfrac{u}{(u+1)^2}\)。根 \(x=n\alpha\) 位于 \([n,n+1)\) 中，当且仅当 \(n\lt u\)，而对正整数 \(n\)，\(n\beta\) 永远不在该区间中。

所要求的正总和保证 \(u\gt1\)。令 \(N\) 为小于 \(u\) 的最大正整数，则 \(N\lt u\le N+1\)。所有解的和为 \[ \dfrac{u+1}{u}\cdot\dfrac{N(N+1)}2=420\text{。}\]因为 \(\dfrac{u+1}{u}\) 随 \(u\) 递减，上述不等式给出 \[ \dfrac{N(N+2)}2\le420\lt\dfrac{(N+1)^2}{2}\text{，}\]从而迫使 \(N=28\)。

代入得到 \(406\cdot\dfrac{u+1}{u}=420\)，所以 \(u=29\)。因此 \(a=\dfrac{29}{30^2}=\dfrac{29}{900}\)。确实，正数解是 \(x=\dfrac{30n}{29}\)，其中 \(1\le n\le28\)，它们的和为 \(420\)。所以 \(p+q=29+900=929\)。

所以 C 是正确答案。

English

There are no negative solutions, while \(x=0\) is always a solution. For \(n\ge1\) and \(x\in[n,n+1),\) put \(y=\frac{x}{n}.\) The equation becomes \(ay^2-y+1=0.\) Its roots must be real, so \(0\lt a\le\tfrac14.\)

If the two roots are \(\alpha\le\beta,\) their sum and product are both \(\frac{1}{a},\) so \((\alpha-1)(\beta-1)=1.\) Write \[ \alpha=1+\dfrac1u,\qquad \beta=1+u \] with \(u\ge1.\) Then \(a=\dfrac{u}{(u+1)^2}.\) The root \(x=n\alpha\) lies in \([n,n+1)\) exactly when \(n\lt u,\) while \(n\beta\) never lies there for a positive integer \(n.\)

The required positive total ensures \(u\gt1.\) Let \(N\) be the largest positive integer less than \(u,\) so \(N\lt u\le N+1.\) The sum of all solutions is therefore \[ \dfrac{u+1}{u}\cdot\dfrac{N(N+1)}2=420. \] Because \(\dfrac{u+1}{u}\) decreases with \(u,\) these inequalities imply \[ \dfrac{N(N+2)}2\le420\lt\dfrac{(N+1)^2}{2}, \] which forces \(N=28.\)

Substitution gives \(406\cdot\dfrac{u+1}{u}=420,\) so \(u=29.\) Hence \(a=\dfrac{29}{30^2}=\dfrac{29}{900}.\) Indeed the positive solutions are \(x=\dfrac{30n}{29}\) for \(1\le n\le28,\) and their sum is \(420.\) Therefore \(p+q=29+900=929.\)

Thus, C is the correct answer.
