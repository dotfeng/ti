1单选

求下列方程的所有根之和。\[ \begin{aligned} &(2x+3)(x-4) \\ &\quad {}+(2x+3)(x-6)=0 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

English（选项同上方）

Compute the sum of all the roots of \[ \begin{aligned} &(2x+3)(x-4) \\ &\quad {}+(2x+3)(x-6)=0. \end{aligned} \]

提出 \(2x+3\) 得到 \((2x+3)\big[(x-4)+(x-6)\big]\) \(= (2x+3)(2x-10)\) \(= 0\)。

两个根为 \(-\dfrac{3}{2}\) 和 \(5\)，它们的和是 \(-\dfrac{3}{2} + 5 = \dfrac{7}{2}\)。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

Factoring out \(2x+3\) gives \((2x+3)\big[(x-4)+(x-6)\big]\) \(= (2x+3)(2x-10)\) \(= 0.\)

The roots are \(-\dfrac{3}{2}\) and \(5,\) whose sum is \(-\dfrac{3}{2} + 5 = \dfrac{7}{2}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

English（选项同上方）

Cindy was asked by her teacher to subtract \(3\) from a certain number and then divide the result by \(9.\) Instead, she subtracted \(9\) and then divided the result by \(3,\) giving an answer of \(43.\) What would her answer have been had she worked the problem correctly?

设原数为 \(x\)。Cindy 计算了 \(\dfrac{x-9}{3} = 43\)，所以 \(x - 9 = 129\)，\(x = 138\)。

正确计算为 \(\dfrac{138-3}{9} = \dfrac{135}{9} = 15\)。

English

Let \(x\) be the number. Cindy computed \(\dfrac{x-9}{3} = 43,\) so \(x - 9 = 129\) and \(x = 138.\)

The correct computation gives \(\dfrac{138-3}{9} = \dfrac{135}{9} = 15.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

3单选

按照指数运算的标准约定，\[2^{2^{2^2}} = 2^{\left(2^{\left(2^2\right)}\right)} = 2^{16} = 65{,}536\text{。}\] 如果改变指数运算的执行顺序，还可能得到多少个其他值？

English（选项同上方）

According to the standard convention for exponentiation, \[2^{2^{2^2}} = 2^{\left(2^{\left(2^2\right)}\right)} = 2^{16} = 65{,}536.\] If the order in which the exponentiations are performed is changed, how many other values are possible?

\(2^{2^{2^2}}\) 的五种加括号方式给出 \[ \begin{aligned} &\left(\left(2^2\right)^2\right)^2 = 2^8, \\ &\left(2^{2^2}\right)^2 = 2^8, \\ &\left(2^2\right)^{2^2} = 2^8 \end{aligned}\text{，}\]\[2^{\left(2^2\right)^2} = 2^{16},\quad 2^{2^{2^2}} = 2^{16}\text{。}\]

因此只可能得到 \(2^{16} = 65{,}536\) 和 \(2^8 = 256\)。除标准值外，恰好还有 \(1\) 个其他值。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

The five parenthesizations of \(2^{2^{2^2}}\) give \[ \begin{aligned} &\left(\left(2^2\right)^2\right)^2 = 2^8, \\ &\left(2^{2^2}\right)^2 = 2^8, \\ &\left(2^2\right)^{2^2} = 2^8, \end{aligned} \] \[2^{\left(2^2\right)^2} = 2^{16},\quad 2^{2^{2^2}} = 2^{16}.\]

So the only values are \(2^{16} = 65{,}536\) and \(2^8 = 256.\) Besides the standard value there is exactly \(1\) other.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

English（选项同上方）

Find the degree measure of an angle whose complement is \(25\%\) of its supplement.

设这个角为 \(x\)，则 \(90 - x = \dfrac14(180 - x)\)，所以 \(360 - 4x = 180 - x\)。

English

Let the angle be \(x.\) Then \(90 - x = \dfrac14(180 - x),\) so \(360 - 4x = 180 - x.\)

This gives \(3x = 180,\) so \(x = 60.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

图中每个小圆的半径都是一。最里面的圆与围绕它的六个圆相切，这六个圆中的每个又与大圆以及相邻的小圆相切。求阴影区域的面积。

English（选项同上方）

Each of the small circles in the figure has radius one. The innermost circle is tangent to the six circles that surround it, and each of those circles is tangent to the large circle and to its small-circle neighbors. Find the area of the shaded region.

六个外圈单位圆都与中心单位圆相切，所以它们的圆心到中心距离为 \(2\)。再加上一个半径，大圆半径为 \(3\)，面积为 \(9\pi\)。

七个单位圆的总面积为 \(7\pi\)，所以阴影区域面积为 \(9\pi - 7\pi = 2\pi\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Each of the six outer unit circles is tangent to the central unit circle, so its center is \(2\) units from the center. Adding one more radius, the large circle has radius \(3\) and area \(9\pi.\)

The seven unit circles have total area \(7\pi,\) so the shaded region has area \(9\pi - 7\pi = 2\pi.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

If an arc of \(45^\circ\) on circle \(A\) has the same length as an arc of \(30^\circ\) on circle \(B,\) then the ratio of the area of circle \(A\) to the area of circle \(B\) is

The ratio of areas is \(\left(\dfrac{R_A}{R_B}\right)^2 = \dfrac{4}{9}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

9单选

Jamal 想把 \(30\) 个电脑文件存到软盘上，每张软盘容量为 \(1.44\) 兆字节（MB）。其中三个文件各需要 \(0.8\) MB，另有 \(12\) 个各需要 \(0.7\) MB，剩下 \(15\) 个各需要 \(0.4\) MB。一个文件不能拆分到多张软盘上。装下所有文件最少需要多少张软盘？

English（选项同上方）

Jamal wants to store \(30\) computer files on floppy disks, each of which has a capacity of \(1.44\) megabytes (mb). Three of his files require \(0.8\) mb of memory each, \(12\) more require \(0.7\) mb each, and the remaining \(15\) require \(0.4\) mb each. No file can be split between floppy disks. What is the minimal number of floppy disks that will hold all the files?

文件总大小为 \(3(0.8)+12(0.7)\) \(+15(0.4) = 16.8\) mb，所以仅按容量至少需要 \(\dfrac{16.8}{1.44} = 11\tfrac{2}{3}\) 张软盘。

含有 \(0.8\)-mb 文件的软盘只能再放一个 \(0.4\)-mb 文件，至少留下 \(0.24\) mb 空间。三个 \(0.8\)-mb 文件总共至少浪费 \(3(0.24) = 0.72\) mb，超过半张软盘容量，因此至少需要 \(13\) 张。

十三张也足够：六张各放两个 \(0.7\)-mb 文件，三张各放一个 \(0.8\)-mb 文件和一个 \(0.4\)-mb 文件，四张各放三个 \(0.4\)-mb 文件。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

The files need \(3(0.8)+12(0.7)\) \(+15(0.4) = 16.8\) mb, so at least \(\dfrac{16.8}{1.44} = 11\tfrac{2}{3}\) disks by volume alone.

A disk containing a \(0.8\)-mb file has room for only one more \(0.4\)-mb file, leaving at least \(0.24\) mb unused. Across the three \(0.8\)-mb files this wastes at least \(3(0.24) = 0.72\) mb, over half a disk, forcing at least \(13\) disks.

Thirteen suffice: six disks each hold two \(0.7\)-mb files, three disks each hold one \(0.8\)-mb file plus one \(0.4\)-mb file, and four disks each hold three \(0.4\)-mb files.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

10单选

Sarah 将四盎司咖啡倒入一个八盎司杯中，又将四盎司奶油倒入另一个同样大小的杯子。她把第一杯中一半的咖啡倒入第二杯，充分搅拌后，再把第二杯中一半的液体倒回第一杯。现在第一杯中的液体有几分之几是奶油？

English（选项同上方）

Sarah pours four ounces of coffee into an eight-ounce cup and four ounces of cream into a second cup of the same size. She then transfers half the coffee from the first cup to the second and, after stirring thoroughly, transfers half the liquid in the second cup back to the first. What fraction of the liquid in the first cup is now cream?

倒出一半咖啡后，杯 \(1\) 有 \(2\) oz 咖啡；杯 \(2\) 有 \(2\) oz 咖啡和 \(4\) oz 奶油，共 \(6\) oz。

杯 \(2\) 倒回一半，会转移 \(1\) oz 咖啡和 \(2\) oz 奶油。于是杯 \(1\) 有 \(2 + 1 = 3\) oz 咖啡和 \(2\) oz 奶油。

奶油所占比例为 \(\dfrac{2}{2+3} = \dfrac{2}{5}\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

After transferring half the coffee, cup \(1\) has \(2\) oz coffee and cup \(2\) has \(2\) oz coffee and \(4\) oz cream, a total of \(6\) oz.

Transferring half of cup \(2\) back moves \(1\) oz coffee and \(2\) oz cream. Cup \(1\) then holds \(2 + 1 = 3\) oz coffee and \(2\) oz cream.

The fraction that is cream is \(\dfrac{2}{2+3} = \dfrac{2}{5}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

11单选

Earl E. Bird 先生每天上午 \(8{:}00\) 整离家上班。当他的平均速度为每小时 \(40\) 英里时，会晚到三分钟；当平均速度为每小时 \(60\) 英里时，会早到三分钟。Bird 先生应以多少英里每小时的平均速度行驶，才能准时到达工作地点？

English（选项同上方）

Mr. Earl E. Bird leaves his house for work at exactly \(8{:}00\) A.M. every morning. When he averages \(40\) miles per hour, he arrives at his workplace three minutes late. When he averages \(60\) miles per hour, he arrives three minutes early. At what average speed, in miles per hour, should Mr. Bird drive to arrive at his workplace precisely on time?

设准时所需时间为 \(t\) 小时。三分钟是 \(0.05\) 小时，所以 \(40(t+0.05) = 60(t-0.05)\)。

得 \(40t + 2 = 60t - 3\)，所以 \(t = 0.25\)。路程为 \(40(0.25+0.05) = 12\) 英里，所需速度为 \(\dfrac{12}{0.25} = 48\) 英里每小时。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Let \(t\) be the time in hours to arrive on time. Since three minutes is \(0.05\) hours, \(40(t+0.05) = 60(t-0.05).\)

This gives \(40t + 2 = 60t - 3,\) so \(t = 0.25.\) The distance is \(40(0.25+0.05) = 12\) miles, and the required speed is \(\dfrac{12}{0.25} = 48\) miles per hour.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

12单选

二次方程 \[x^2 - 63x + k = 0\] 的两个根都是质数。\(k\) 可能取值的个数是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Both roots of the quadratic equation \[x^2 - 63x + k = 0\] are prime numbers. The number of possible values of \(k\) is

如果两个根是质数 \(p\) 和 \(q\)，则由韦达定理 \(p + q = 63\)，且 \(pq = k\)。

因为 \(63\) 是奇数，所以一个质数必须是偶数，即 \(2\)，另一个是 \(61\)。两者都是质数，所以 \(k = 2\cdot 61 = 122\) 是唯一可能值。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

If the roots are primes \(p\) and \(q,\) then by Vieta’s formulas \(p + q = 63\) and \(pq = k.\)

Since \(63\) is odd, one prime must be even, namely \(2,\) and the other is \(61.\) Both are prime, so \(k = 2\cdot 61 = 122\) is the only possible value.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

13单选

两个不同的正数 \(a\) 和 \(b\) 各自与其倒数相差 \(1\)。\(a + b\) 是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Two different positive numbers \(a\) and \(b\) each differ from their reciprocals by \(1.\) What is \(a + b?\)

正数 \(x\) 与其倒数相差 \(1\) 时，\(x - \dfrac1x = 1\) 或 \(x - \dfrac1x = -1\)，即 \(x^2 - x - 1 = 0\) 或 \(x^2 + x - 1 = 0\)。

正根为 \(\dfrac{1+\sqrt5}{2}\) 和 \(\dfrac{-1+\sqrt5}{2}\)，它们互为倒数。它们的和是 \(a + b = \sqrt5\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

A positive number \(x\) differs from its reciprocal by \(1\) when \(x - \dfrac1x = 1\) or \(x - \dfrac1x = -1,\) i.e. \(x^2 - x - 1 = 0\) or \(x^2 + x - 1 = 0.\)

The positive roots are \(\dfrac{1+\sqrt5}{2}\) and \(\dfrac{-1+\sqrt5}{2},\) which are reciprocals of each other. Their sum is \(a + b = \sqrt5.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

15单选

一组八个整数的平均数、中位数、唯一众数和极差都等于 \(8\)。这组数中可能出现的最大整数是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The mean, median, unique mode, and range of a collection of eight integers are all equal to \(8.\) The largest integer that can be an element of this collection is

集合 \(6, 6, 6, 8, 8, 8, 8, 14\) 的平均数、中位数、唯一众数和极差都等于 \(8\)，所以 \(14\) 可以达到。

假设最大值为 \(15\)。极差 \(8\) 迫使最小值为 \(7\)。因为 \(8\) 是众数，它在排好序的八个数中重复出现；再结合中位数 \(8\)，中间两个数只能是 \(8, 8\)。于是 \(7 + 8 + 8 + 15 = 38\)，剩下四个数的和为 \(64 - 38 = 26\)，平均为 \(6.5\)。这样至少有一个数会小于 \(7\)，与最小值矛盾。因此 \(15\) 不可能。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

The collection \(6, 6, 6, 8, 8, 8, 8, 14\) has mean, median, unique mode, and range all equal to \(8,\) so \(14\) is attainable.

Suppose the largest were \(15.\) The range \(8\) forces the smallest to be \(7.\) Because \(8\) is the mode, it occurs in the sorted list; together with median \(8,\) this forces the two middle values to be \(8, 8.\) Then \(7 + 8 + 8 + 15 = 38,\) so the remaining four values sum to \(64 - 38 = 26,\) averaging \(6.5.\) At least one would be below \(7,\) contradicting the minimum. So \(15\) is impossible.

Thus, the correct answer is D.

16单选

Tina 从集合 \(\{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}\) 中随机选择两个不同的数，Sergio 从集合 \(\{1, 2, \ldots, 10\}\) 中随机选择一个数。Sergio 选的数大于 Tina 所选两个数之和的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Tina randomly selects two distinct numbers from the set \(\{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\},\) and Sergio randomly selects a number from the set \(\{1, 2, \ldots, 10\}.\) The probability that Sergio’s number is larger than the sum of the two numbers chosen by Tina is

Tina 的十个数对的和为 \(3, 4, 5, 5, 6, 6, 7, 7, 8, 9\)。对于和 \(s\)，Sergio 的数大于它的概率为 \(\dfrac{10 - s}{10}\)。

相应的 \(10 - s\) 值为 \(7, 6, 5, 5, 4, 4, 3, 3, 2, 1\)，总和为 \(40\)。总概率为 \(\dfrac{40}{10\cdot 10} = \dfrac{2}{5}\)。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

Tina’s ten pairs have sums \(3, 4, 5, 5, 6, 6, 7, 7, 8, 9.\) For a sum \(s,\) Sergio’s number exceeds it with probability \(\dfrac{10 - s}{10}.\)

The corresponding values of \(10 - s\) are \(7, 6, 5, 5, 4, 4, 3, 3, 2, 1,\) totaling \(40.\) The overall probability is \(\dfrac{40}{10\cdot 10} = \dfrac{2}{5}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

17单选

有若干组质数，例如 \(\{7, 83, 421, 659\}\)，恰好各使用九个非零数字一次。这样的质数组的和最小可能是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Several sets of prime numbers, such as \(\{7, 83, 421, 659\},\) use each of the nine nonzero digits exactly once. What is the smallest possible sum such a set of primes could have?

偶数字 \(4, 6, 8\) 不能作为多位质数的个位，所以每个都必须出现在十位或更高位，至少贡献 \(40 + 60 + 80 = 180\)。另外六个数字至少贡献 \(1 + 2 + 3 + 5 + 7 + 9 = 27\)，因此总和至少为 \(207\)。

这个下界可以达到，例如 \(\{2, 3, 5, 41, 67, 89\}\)，它们的和为 \(207\)。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

The even digits \(4, 6, 8\) cannot be the units digit of a multi-digit prime, so each must appear in a tens place or higher, contributing at least \(40 + 60 + 80 = 180.\) The other six digits contribute at least \(1 + 2 + 3 + 5 + 7 + 9 = 27,\) so the sum is at least \(207.\)

This bound is achieved, for example by \(\{2, 3, 5, 41, 67, 89\},\) whose sum is \(207.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

English（选项同上方）

Let \(C_1\) and \(C_2\) be circles defined by \[(x - 10)^2 + y^2 = 36\] and \[(x + 15)^2 + y^2 = 81,\] respectively. What is the length of the shortest line segment \(\overline{PQ}\) that is tangent to \(C_1\) at \(P\) and to \(C_2\) at \(Q?\)

圆心为 \(A = (10, 0)\) 和 \(B = (-15, 0)\)，半径分别为 \(6\) 和 \(9\)，所以 \(AB = 25\)。最短切线是内公切线，它在点 \(D\) 与 \(\overline{AB}\) 相交，并按 \(6 : 9\) 分割该线段，因此 \(D = (0, 0)\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

The centers are \(A = (10, 0)\) and \(B = (-15, 0),\) with radii \(6\) and \(9,\) so \(AB = 25.\) The shortest tangent is the internal one, meeting \(\overline{AB}\) at a point \(D\) that splits it in the ratio \(6 : 9,\) giving \(D = (0, 0).\)

Suppose that \(a\) and \(b\) are digits, not both nine and not both zero, and the repeating decimal \(0.\overline{ab}\) is expressed as a fraction in lowest terms. How many different denominators are possible?

English

The denominator \(1\) would require \(\overline{ab} = 99,\) i.e. \(a = b = 9,\) which is excluded. Each is achievable: numerators \(33, 11, 9, 3,\) and \(1\) reduce to denominators \(3, 9, 11, 33,\) and \(99,\) respectively. Thus there are \(5\) possible denominators.

22单选

三角形 \(ABC\) 是直角三角形，\(\angle ACB\) 为直角，\(m\angle ABC = 60^\circ\)，且 \(AB = 10\)。在 \(\triangle ABC\) 内随机选一点 \(P\)，再将 \(\overline{BP}\) 延长交 \(\overline{AC}\) 于 \(D\)。求 \(BD \gt 5\sqrt{2}\) 的概率。

English（选项同上方）

Triangle \(ABC\) is a right triangle with \(\angle ACB\) as its right angle, \(m\angle ABC = 60^\circ,\) and \(AB = 10.\) Let \(P\) be randomly chosen inside \(\triangle ABC,\) and extend \(\overline{BP}\) to meet \(\overline{AC}\) at \(D.\) What is the probability that \(BD \gt 5\sqrt{2}?\)

因为 \(AB = 10\) 且 \(\angle ABC = 60^\circ\)，这个 \(30\)-\(60\)-\(90\) 三角形满足 \(BC = 5\)，\(AC = 5\sqrt3\)。

在 \(\overline{AC}\) 上取点 \(E\)，使 \(CE = 5\)，则 \(BE = \sqrt{5^2 + 5^2} = 5\sqrt2\)。当 \(D\) 沿 \(\overline{AC}\) 移动时，\(BD = \sqrt{25 + CD^2}\)。它超过 \(5\sqrt2\) 正好等价于 \(CD \gt 5\)，也就是 \(D\) 位于 \(E\) 的外侧；这又当且仅当 \(P\) 在 \(\triangle ABE\) 内。

所求概率为 \[ \begin{aligned} \dfrac{[ABE]}{[ABC]} &= \dfrac{EA}{CA} \\ &= \dfrac{5\sqrt3 - 5}{5\sqrt3} \\ &= \dfrac{3 - \sqrt3}{3} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Since \(AB = 10\) and \(\angle ABC = 60^\circ,\) the \(30\)-\(60\)-\(90\) triangle has \(BC = 5\) and \(AC = 5\sqrt3.\)

Place \(E\) on \(\overline{AC}\) with \(CE = 5;\) then \(BE = \sqrt{5^2 + 5^2} = 5\sqrt2.\) As \(D\) moves along \(\overline{AC},\) \(BD = \sqrt{25 + CD^2}\) exceeds \(5\sqrt2\) exactly when \(CD \gt 5,\) i.e. when \(D\) lies beyond \(E,\) which happens iff \(P\) is inside \(\triangle ABE.\)

The probability is \[ \begin{aligned} \dfrac{[ABE]}{[ABC]} &= \dfrac{EA}{CA} \\ &= \dfrac{5\sqrt3 - 5}{5\sqrt3} \\ &= \dfrac{3 - \sqrt3}{3}. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is C.

23单选

在三角形 \(ABC\) 中，边 \(\overline{AC}\) 与 \(\overline{BC}\) 的垂直平分线交于点 \(D\)，且 \(\overline{BD}\) 平分 \(\angle ABC\)。若 \(AD = 9\)，\(DC = 7\)，求三角形 \(ABD\) 的面积。

English（选项同上方）

In triangle \(ABC,\) side \(\overline{AC}\) and the perpendicular bisector of \(\overline{BC}\) meet in point \(D,\) and \(\overline{BD}\) bisects \(\angle ABC.\) If \(AD = 9\) and \(DC = 7,\) what is the area of triangle \(ABD?\)

因为 \(D\) 在 \(\overline{BC}\) 的垂直平分线上，所以 \(DB = DC = 7\)。角平分线 \(\overline{BD}\) 给出 \(\dfrac{AB}{BC} = \dfrac{AD}{DC} = \dfrac{9}{7}\)，设 \(AB = 9x\)，\(BC = 7x\)。

令 \(\theta = \angle ABD = \angle DBC\)。在等腰三角形 \(\triangle BDC\) 中，垂足是 \(\overline{BC}\) 的中点 \(M\)，所以 \(\cos\theta = \dfrac{BM}{BD} = \dfrac{\frac{7x}{2}}{7} = \dfrac{x}{2}\)。

在 \(\triangle ABD\) 中应用余弦定理：\[9^2 = (9x)^2 + 7^2 - 2(9x)(7)\cdot\dfrac{x}{2}\text{，}\]化简得 \(81 = 18x^2 + 49\)，所以 \(x = \dfrac43\)，\(AB = 12\)。

现在 \(\triangle ABD\) 的三边为 \(9, 7, 12\)。由海伦公式，半周长 \(s = 14\)，面积为 \(\sqrt{14\cdot 5\cdot 7\cdot 2} = \sqrt{980} = 14\sqrt5\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

Since \(D\) lies on the perpendicular bisector of \(\overline{BC},\) \(DB = DC = 7.\) The angle bisector \(\overline{BD}\) gives \(\dfrac{AB}{BC} = \dfrac{AD}{DC} = \dfrac{9}{7},\) so write \(AB = 9x\) and \(BC = 7x.\)

Let \(\theta = \angle ABD = \angle DBC.\) In isosceles \(\triangle BDC,\) the foot of the perpendicular is the midpoint \(M\) of \(\overline{BC},\) so \(\cos\theta = \dfrac{BM}{BD} = \dfrac{\frac{7x}{2}}{7} = \dfrac{x}{2}.\)

Applying the Law of Cosines in \(\triangle ABD:\) \[9^2 = (9x)^2 + 7^2 - 2(9x)(7)\cdot\dfrac{x}{2},\] which simplifies to \(81 = 18x^2 + 49,\) so \(x = \dfrac43\) and \(AB = 12.\)

Now \(\triangle ABD\) has sides \(9, 7, 12.\) By Heron’s formula with \(s = 14,\) the area is \(\sqrt{14\cdot 5\cdot 7\cdot 2} = \sqrt{980} = 14\sqrt5.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

Find the number of ordered pairs of real numbers \((a, b)\) such that \((a + bi)^{2002} = a - bi.\)
