Alicia 每小时赚 \(\$20\)，其中 \(1.45\%\) 被扣除用于缴纳地方税。Alicia 每小时的工资中有多少美分用于缴纳地方税？

English（选项同上方）

Alicia earns \(\$20\) per hour, of which \(1.45\%\) is deducted to pay local taxes. How many cents per hour of Alicia’s wages are used to pay local taxes?

因为 \(\$20\) 是 \(2000\) 美分，所以每小时的税额为 \[ 0.0145 \times 2000 = 29 \] 美分。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Since \(\$20\) is \(2000\) cents, the tax is \[ 0.0145 \times 2000 = 29 \] cents per hour.

Thus, the correct answer is E.

2单选

在 AMC \(12\) 中，每道答对的题得 \(6\) 分，每道答错的题得 \(0\) 分，每道未作答的题得 \(2.5\) 分。如果 Charlyn 在 \(25\) 道题中有 \(8\) 道未作答，那么剩下的题中她至少要答对多少道，才能得到至少 \(100\) 分？

English（选项同上方）

On the AMC \(12,\) each correct answer is worth \(6\) points, each incorrect answer is worth \(0\) points, and each problem left unanswered is worth \(2.5\) points. If Charlyn leaves \(8\) of the \(25\) problems unanswered, how many of the remaining problems must she answer correctly in order to score at least \(100?\)

\(8\) 道未作答的题值 \(2.5 \times 8 = 20\) 分，所以 Charlyn 还需要从答对的题中得到至少 \(100 - 20 = 80\) 分。

每道答对的题值 \(6\) 分，而不小于 \(80\) 的最小 \(6\) 的倍数是 \(84 = 6 \times 14\)。所以她至少需要答对 \(14\) 道题。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The \(8\) unanswered problems are worth \(2.5 \times 8 = 20\) points, so Charlyn needs at least \(100 - 20 = 80\) more points from correct answers.

Each correct answer is worth \(6\) points, and the smallest multiple of \(6\) that is at least \(80\) is \(84 = 6 \times 14.\) So she needs at least \(14\) correct answers.

Thus, the correct answer is C.

3单选

有多少个正整数有序对 \((x, y)\) 满足 \(x + 2y = 100\)？

English（选项同上方）

For how many ordered pairs of positive integers \((x, y)\) is \(x + 2y = 100?\)

写成 \(x = 100 - 2y\)。当且仅当 \(y\) 是满足 \(1 \le y \le 49\) 的正整数时，\(x\) 才是正整数。

因此共有 \(49\) 个有效有序对。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Writing \(x = 100 - 2y,\) the value of \(x\) is a positive integer precisely when \(y\) is a positive integer with \(1 \le y \le 49.\)

This gives \(49\) valid ordered pairs.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

4单选

Bertha 有 \(6\) 个女儿，没有儿子。她的一些女儿各有 \(6\) 个女儿，其余女儿没有女儿。Bertha 一共有 \(30\) 个女儿和外孙女，并且没有曾外孙女。Bertha 的女儿和外孙女中，有多少人没有女儿？

English（选项同上方）

Bertha has \(6\) daughters and no sons. Some of her daughters have \(6\) daughters, and the rest have none. Bertha has a total of \(30\) daughters and granddaughters, and no great-granddaughters. How many of Bertha’s daughters and granddaughters have no daughters?

Bertha 有 \(30 - 6 = 24\) 个外孙女，她们都没有女儿。

这些外孙女是 Bertha 的 \(\frac{24}{6} = 4\) 个女儿所生，所以恰好有 \(4\) 人有女儿。

因此没有女儿的人数是 \(30 - 4 = 26\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Bertha has \(30 - 6 = 24\) granddaughters, none of whom have daughters.

These granddaughters are the children of \(\frac{24}{6} = 4\) of Bertha’s daughters, so exactly \(4\) women have daughters.

Therefore the number of women with no daughters is \(30 - 4 = 26.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

5单选

直线 \(y = mx + b\) 的图像如下。下列哪一项正确？

English（选项同上方）

The graph of a line \(y = mx + b\) is shown. Which of the following is true?

\(y\) 截距在 \(0\) 和 \(1\) 之间，所以 \(0 \lt b \lt 1\)。

斜率为负且较平缓，在 \(-1\) 和 \(0\) 之间，所以 \(-1 \lt m \lt 0\)。

因此乘积 \(mb\) 为负，且绝对值小于 \(1\)，从而得到 \(-1 \lt mb \lt 0\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The \(y\)-intercept of the line is between \(0\) and \(1,\) so \(0 \lt b \lt 1.\)

The slope is negative and shallow, between \(-1\) and \(0,\) so \(-1 \lt m \lt 0.\)

The product \(mb\) is therefore negative with absolute value less than \(1,\) giving \(-1 \lt mb \lt 0.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

设 \(U = 2 \cdot 2004^{2005}\)，\(V = 2004^{2005}\)，\(W = 2003 \cdot 2004^{2004}\)，\(X = 2 \cdot 2004^{2004}\)，\(Y = 2004^{2004}\)，且 \(Z = 2004^{2003}\)。下列哪一个最大？

English（选项同上方）

Let \(U = 2 \cdot 2004^{2005},\) \(V = 2004^{2005},\) \(W = 2003 \cdot 2004^{2004},\) \(X = 2 \cdot 2004^{2004},\) \(Y = 2004^{2004}\) and \(Z = 2004^{2003}.\) Which of the following is largest?

逐个因式分解这些差：

\(U - V = 2004^{2005}\)，\(V - W = 2004^{2004}\)，\(W - X = 2001 \cdot 2004^{2004}\)，\(X - Y = 2004^{2004}\)，且 \(Y - Z = 2003 \cdot 2004^{2003}\)。

因为 \(2004^{2005} = 2004 \cdot 2004^{2004}\) 大于其他各项，而其他各项都达不到 \(2004^{2005}\)，所以 \(U - V\) 最大。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Compute each difference by factoring:

\(U - V = 2004^{2005},\) \(V - W = 2004^{2004},\) \(W - X = 2001 \cdot 2004^{2004},\) \(X - Y = 2004^{2004},\) and \(Y - Z = 2003 \cdot 2004^{2003}.\)

Since \(2004^{2005} = 2004 \cdot 2004^{2004}\) exceeds each of the others, none of which reaches \(2004^{2005},\) the difference \(U - V\) is the largest.

Thus, the correct answer is A.

一个游戏按照以下规则使用筹码。每一轮中，筹码最多的玩家给其他每个玩家各一个筹码，并且还把一个筹码放入弃置堆。当某个玩家没有筹码时，游戏结束。玩家 \(A\)、\(B\) 和 \(C\) 分别以 \(15\)、\(14\) 和 \(13\) 个筹码开始。这个游戏会进行多少轮？

English（选项同上方）

A game is played with tokens according to the following rule. In each round, the player with the most tokens gives one token to each of the other players and also places one token into a discard pile. The game ends when some player runs out of tokens. Players \(A,\) \(B,\) and \(C\) start with \(15,\) \(14,\) and \(13\) tokens, respectively. How many rounds will there be in the game?

三轮后，玩家 \(A\)、\(B\) 和 \(C\) 分别有 \(14\)、\(13\) 和 \(12\) 个筹码。之后每三轮都会使每个玩家的筹码数减少一个。

经过 \(36\) 轮后，他们分别有 \(3\)、\(2\) 和 \(1\) 个筹码；第 \(37\) 轮中，玩家 \(A\) 把自己的三个筹码全部给出并放入弃置堆后归零，游戏结束。

English

After three rounds the players \(A,\) \(B,\) and \(C\) have \(14,\) \(13,\) and \(12\) tokens, respectively. Every subsequent three rounds reduces each player’s supply by one token.

After \(36\) rounds they have \(3,\) \(2,\) and \(1\) tokens. In the \(37\)th round player \(A,\) who has the most, gives away all three of their tokens and runs out, ending the game.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

如图，\(\angle EAB\) 和 \(\angle ABC\) 是直角，\(AB = 4\)，\(BC = 6\)，\(AE = 8\)，且 \(\overline{AC}\) 与 \(\overline{BE}\) 交于 \(D\)。\(\triangle ADE\) 与 \(\triangle BDC\) 的面积之差是多少？

English（选项同上方）

In the figure, \(\angle EAB\) and \(\angle ABC\) are right angles, \(AB = 4,\) \(BC = 6,\) \(AE = 8,\) and \(\overline{AC}\) and \(\overline{BE}\) intersect at \(D.\) What is the difference between the areas of \(\triangle ADE\) and \(\triangle BDC?\)

Then \(\triangle ABE\) has area \(\tfrac12 \cdot 4 \cdot 8 = 16 = x + z,\) and \(\triangle ABC\) has area \(\tfrac12 \cdot 4 \cdot 6 = 12 = y + z.\)

The requested difference is \[ \begin{aligned} x - y &= (x + z) - (y + z) \\ &= 16 - 12 = 4. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is B.

9单选

一家公司用圆柱形罐子销售花生酱。市场研究表明，使用更宽的罐子会增加销量。如果罐子的直径增加 \(25\%\)，但体积不变，那么高度必须减少百分之多少？

English（选项同上方）

A company sells peanut butter in cylindrical jars. Marketing research suggests that using wider jars will increase sales. If the diameter of the jars is increased by \(25\%\) without altering the volume, by what percent must the height be decreased?

直径乘以 \(\tfrac54\) 会使底面积乘以 \(\left(\tfrac54\right)^2 = \tfrac{25}{16}\)。

为保持体积不变，高度必须乘以 \(\tfrac{16}{25} = 0.64\)，也就是减少 \(1 - 0.64 = 0.36\)，即 \(36\%\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Multiplying the diameter by \(\tfrac54\) multiplies the base area by \(\left(\tfrac54\right)^2 = \tfrac{25}{16}.\)

To keep the volume fixed, the height must be multiplied by \(\tfrac{16}{25} = 0.64.\) That is a decrease of \(1 - 0.64 = 0.36,\) or \(36\%.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

10单选

\(49\) 个连续整数的和为 \(7^5\)。它们的中位数是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The sum of \(49\) consecutive integers is \(7^5.\) What is their median?

一组连续整数的和等于项数乘以平均数，而对连续整数来说，平均数等于中位数。

所以中位数为 \[ \dfrac{7^5}{49} = \dfrac{7^5}{7^2} = 7^3 = 343\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The sum of a set of consecutive integers equals the number of terms times their mean, and for consecutive integers the mean equals the median.

So the median is \[ \dfrac{7^5}{49} = \dfrac{7^5}{7^2} = 7^3 = 343. \]

Thus, the correct answer is C.

11单选

Paula 钱包中所有一美分、五美分、十美分和二十五美分硬币的平均币值是 \(20\) 美分。如果她再有一枚二十五美分硬币，平均币值会变成 \(21\) 美分。她的钱包中有多少枚十美分硬币？

English（选项同上方）

The average value of all the pennies, nickels, dimes, and quarters in Paula’s purse is \(20\) cents. If she had one more quarter, the average value would be \(21\) cents. How many dimes does she have in her purse?

如果 Paula 有 \(n\) 枚硬币，它们的总币值是 \(20n\) 美分。再加入一枚二十五美分硬币后共有 \(n + 1\) 枚硬币，总币值为 \(20n + 25\) 美分，而它也必须等于 \(21(n + 1)\) 美分。

因此 \(20n + 25 = 21(n + 1)\)，解得 \(n = 4\)。

这四枚硬币合计 \(80\) 美分，只能是三枚二十五美分硬币和一枚五美分硬币，所以十美分硬币的数量为 \(0\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

If Paula has \(n\) coins, their total value is \(20n\) cents. Adding a quarter gives \(n + 1\) coins worth \(20n + 25\) cents, which must also equal \(21(n + 1)\) cents.

So \(20n + 25 = 21(n + 1),\) giving \(n = 4.\)

Four coins totalling \(80\) cents must be three quarters and one nickel, so the number of dimes is \(0.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

12单选

设 \(A = (0, 9)\) 且 \(B = (0, 12)\)。点 \(A'\) 和 \(B'\) 在直线 \(y = x\) 上，并且 \(\overline{AA'}\) 与 \(\overline{BB'}\) 交于 \(C = (2, 8)\)。\(\overline{A'B'}\) 的长度是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Let \(A = (0, 9)\) and \(B = (0, 12).\) Points \(A'\) and \(B'\) are on the line \(y = x,\) and \(\overline{AA'}\) and \(\overline{BB'}\) intersect at \(C = (2, 8).\) What is the length of \(\overline{A'B'}?\)

直线 \(AC\) 经过 \((0, 9)\)，斜率为 \(\tfrac{8 - 9}{2 - 0} = -\tfrac12\)，所以方程是 \(y = -\tfrac12 x + 9\)。令 \(y = x\)，得到 \(A' = (6, 6)\)。

直线 \(BC\) 经过 \((0, 12)\)，斜率为 \(-2\)，所以方程是 \(y = -2x + 12\)。令 \(y = x\)，得到 \(B' = (4, 4)\)。

因此 \[ \begin{aligned} A'B' &= \sqrt{(6 - 4)^2 + (6 - 4)^2} \\ &= 2\sqrt{2} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Line \(AC\) passes through \((0, 9)\) with slope \(\tfrac{8 - 9}{2 - 0} = -\tfrac12,\) so its equation is \(y = -\tfrac12 x + 9.\) Setting \(y = x\) gives \(A' = (6, 6).\)

Line \(BC\) passes through \((0, 12)\) with slope \(-2,\) so \(y = -2x + 12.\) Setting \(y = x\) gives \(B' = (4, 4).\)

Then \[ \begin{aligned} A'B' &= \sqrt{(6 - 4)^2 + (6 - 4)^2} \\ &= 2\sqrt{2}. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is B.

13单选

设 \(S\) 为坐标平面中的点集 \((a, b)\)，其中 \(a\) 和 \(b\) 都可以是 \(-1\)，\(0\)，或 \(1\)。有多少条不同的直线至少经过 \(S\) 中的两个点？

English（选项同上方）

Let \(S\) be the set of points \((a, b)\) in the coordinate plane, where each of \(a\) and \(b\) may be \(-1,\) \(0,\) or \(1.\) How many distinct lines pass through at least two members of \(S?\)

共有 \(\binom{9}{2} = 36\) 对点，每一对点确定一条直线。

但是有三条水平线、三条竖直线和两条对角线各经过 \(S\) 中的 \(3\) 个共线点，因此每条这样的直线被多算 \(2\) 次。

这样的直线共有 \(8\) 条，所以不同直线的数量为 \(36 - 2 \cdot 8 = 20\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

There are \(\binom{9}{2} = 36\) pairs of points, and each pair determines a line.

However, there are three horizontal, three vertical, and two diagonal lines that each pass through three collinear points of \(S.\) Each such line is counted \(3\) times, an overcount of \(2\) per line.

With \(8\) such lines, the number of distinct lines is \(36 - 2 \cdot 8 = 20.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

14单选

三个实数组成的数列是等差数列，首项为 \(9\)。如果给第二项加上 \(2\)，给第三项加上 \(20\) 得到的三个数构成等比数列。这个等比数列第三项的最小可能值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A sequence of three real numbers forms an arithmetic progression with a first term of \(9.\) If \(2\) is added to the second term and \(20\) is added to the third term, the three resulting numbers form a geometric progression. What is the smallest possible value for the third term of the geometric progression?

等差数列为 \(9\)，\(9 + d\)，\(9 + 2d\)。加上指定数后，等比数列为 \(9\)，\(11 + d\)，\(29 + 2d\)。

等比条件给出 \((11 + d)^2 = 9(29 + 2d)\)，化简为 \(d^2 + 4d - 140 = 0\)，所以 \(d = 10\) 或 \(d = -14\)。

对应的第三项 \(29 + 2d\) 分别为 \(49\) 和 \(1\)，所以最小可能值为 \(1\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The arithmetic progression is \(9,\) \(9 + d,\) \(9 + 2d.\) After the additions, the geometric progression is \(9,\) \(11 + d,\) \(29 + 2d.\)

The geometric condition gives \((11 + d)^2 = 9(29 + 2d),\) which simplifies to \(d^2 + 4d - 140 = 0,\) so \(d = 10\) or \(d = -14.\)

The corresponding third terms \(29 + 2d\) are \(49\) and \(1,\) so the smallest possible value is \(1.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

15单选

Brenda 和 Sally 在一条圆形跑道上沿相反方向跑步，从直径两端的点同时出发。她们第一次相遇时，Brenda 已跑了 \(100\) 米。她们下一次相遇时，Sally 已经从第一次相遇点又跑了 \(150\) 米。两人的速度都恒定。跑道长多少米？

English（选项同上方）

Brenda and Sally run in opposite directions on a circular track, starting at diametrically opposite points. They first meet after Brenda has run \(100\) meters. They next meet after Sally has run \(150\) meters past their first meeting point. Each girl runs at a constant speed. What is the length of the track in meters?

从相对两端出发时，她们第一次相遇时合计跑了半个跑道长度。

第一次和第二次相遇之间，她们合计跑了一个完整跑道长度。因为 Brenda 第一次相遇前跑了 \(100\) 米，所以这段时间她跑了 \(2 \cdot 100 = 200\) 米。

同一时间段内 Sally 跑了 \(150\) 米，因此跑道长度为 \(200 + 150 = 350\) 米。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Starting at opposite ends, when they first meet they have together run half the track. Between the first and second meetings, they together run a full track length.

Since Brenda runs at a constant speed and covered \(100\) meters before the first meeting, she covers \(2 \cdot 100 = 200\) meters between the two meetings.

Adding Sally’s \(150\) meters over that same interval gives a track length of \(200 + 150 = 350\) meters.

Thus, the correct answer is C.

使 \[\small \log_{2004}(\log_{2003}(\log_{2002}(\log_{2001} x)))\] 有定义的所有实数 \(x\) 的集合是 \(\{x \mid x \gt c\}\)。\(c\) 的值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The set of all real numbers \(x\) for which \[\small \log_{2004}(\log_{2003}(\log_{2002}(\log_{2001} x)))\] is defined is \(\{x \mid x \gt c\}.\) What is the value of \(c?\)

该表达式有定义当且仅当 \(\log_{2003}(\log_{2002}(\log_{2001} x)) \gt 0\)，也就是 \(\log_{2002}(\log_{2001} x) \gt 1\)。

这当且仅当 \(\log_{2001} x \gt 2002\)，等价于 \(x \gt 2001^{2002}\)。

因此 \(c = 2001^{2002}\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The expression is defined if and only if \(\log_{2003}(\log_{2002}(\log_{2001} x)) \gt 0,\) that is, \(\log_{2002}(\log_{2001} x) \gt 1.\)

This holds if and only if \(\log_{2001} x \gt 2002,\) which is equivalent to \(x \gt 2001^{2002}.\)

Therefore \(c = 2001^{2002}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

17单选

设 \(f\) 是满足以下性质的函数：

(i) \(f(1) = 1\)，且

(ii) 对任意正整数 \(n\)，都有 \(f(2n) = n \cdot f(n)\)。

\(f(2^{100})\) 的值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Let \(f\) be a function with the following properties:

(i) \(f(1) = 1,\) and

(ii) \(f(2n) = n \cdot f(n)\) for any positive integer \(n.\)

What is the value of \(f(2^{100})?\)

在 \(f(2n) = n \cdot f(n)\) 中令 \(n = 2^{k}\) 得到 \(f(2^{k+1}) = 2^{k} \cdot f(2^{k})\)。

从 \(f(2^1) = f(2) = 1 \cdot f(1) = 2^0\)，展开，指数累加为 \[ \begin{aligned} f(2^n) &= 2^{0 + 1 + 2 + \cdots + (n-1)} \\ &= 2^{\frac{n(n-1)}{2}} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因此 \(f(2^{100}) = 2^{\frac{100 \cdot 99}{2}} = 2^{4950}\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Applying \(f(2n) = n \cdot f(n)\) with \(n = 2^{k},\) we get \(f(2^{k+1}) = 2^{k} \cdot f(2^{k}).\)

Unwinding from \(f(2^1) = f(2) = 1 \cdot f(1) = 2^0,\) the exponents accumulate: \[ \begin{aligned} f(2^n) &= 2^{0 + 1 + 2 + \cdots + (n-1)} \\ &= 2^{\frac{n(n-1)}{2}}. \end{aligned} \]

Therefore \(f(2^{100}) = 2^{\frac{100 \cdot 99}{2}} = 2^{4950}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

正方形 \(ABCD\) 的边长为 \(2\)。在正方形内部作以 \(\overline{AB}\) 为直径的半圆，从 \(C\) 向该半圆作切线，切线与边 \(\overline{AD}\) 交于 \(E\)。\(\overline{CE}\) 的长度是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Square \(ABCD\) has side length \(2.\) A semicircle with diameter \(\overline{AB}\) is constructed inside the square, and the tangent to the semicircle from \(C\) intersects side \(\overline{AD}\) at \(E.\) What is the length of \(\overline{CE}?\)

设 \(F\) 为 \(CE\) 与半圆的切点。由于 \(CB\) 和 \(CF\) 都是从 \(C\) 作出的切线，所以 \(CF = CB = 2\)。同理，令 \(x = AE\)，从 \(E\) 作出的切线给出 \(EF = EA = x\)。

因此 \(CE = CF + FE = 2 + x\)。在直角三角形 \(CDE\) 中，\(CD = 2\) 且 \(DE = 2 - x\)，所以 \[ (2 - x)^2 + 2^2 = (2 + x)^2\text{。}\]

展开得 \(8x = 4\)，所以 \(x = \tfrac12\)，并且 \(CE = 2 + \tfrac12 = \tfrac52\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Let \(F\) be the point where \(CE\) touches the semicircle. Since \(CB\) and \(CF\) are both tangents from \(C,\) we have \(CF = CB = 2.\) Similarly, with \(x = AE,\) the tangents from \(E\) give \(EF = EA = x.\)

Thus \(CE = CF + FE = 2 + x.\) In right triangle \(CDE,\) where \(CD = 2\) and \(DE = 2 - x,\) \[ (2 - x)^2 + 2^2 = (2 + x)^2. \]

Expanding gives \(8x = 4,\) so \(x = \tfrac12\) and \(CE = 2 + \tfrac12 = \tfrac52.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

19单选

圆 \(A\)、\(B\) 和 \(C\) 两两外切，并且都内切于圆 \(D\)。圆 \(B\) 和 \(C\) 全等。圆 \(A\) 的半径为 \(1\)，并且经过圆 \(D\) 的圆心。圆 \(B\) 的半径是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Circles \(A,\) \(B,\) and \(C\) are externally tangent to each other and internally tangent to circle \(D.\) Circles \(B\) and \(C\) are congruent. Circle \(A\) has radius \(1\) and passes through the center of \(D.\) What is the radius of circle \(B?\)

圆 \(A\) 半径为 \(1\)，经过圆 \(D\) 的圆心并与圆 \(D\) 内切，所以圆 \(D\) 的半径为 \(2\)。

将圆 \(D\) 的圆心放在原点，圆 \(A\) 的圆心在 \((-1, 0)\)。由对称性，\(B\) 的圆心为 \((x, y)\)，半径为 \(r\) 而 \(C\) 是它关于水平轴的镜像，所以两圆在该轴上相切，且 \(y = r\)。

与圆 \(D\) 内切给出 \(x^2 + y^2 = (2 - r)^2\)，与圆 \(A\) 外切给出 \((x + 1)^2 + y^2 = (1 + r)^2\)。

两式相减并使用 \(y = r\) 得到 \(x = \tfrac23\) 且 \(r = \tfrac89\)。因此圆 \(B\) 的半径是 \(\tfrac89\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Circle \(A\) has radius \(1\) and passes through the center of \(D\) while being internally tangent to \(D,\) so \(D\) has radius \(2.\)

Place the center of \(D\) at the origin, with \(A\) centered at \((-1, 0).\) By symmetry, \(B\) has center \((x, y)\) and radius \(r,\) with \(C\) its mirror image across the horizontal axis, so the two congruent circles touch on that axis and \(y = r.\)

Internal tangency to \(D\) gives \(x^2 + y^2 = (2 - r)^2,\) and external tangency to \(A\) gives \((x + 1)^2 + y^2 = (1 + r)^2.\)

Subtracting and using \(y = r\) yields \(x = \tfrac23\) and \(r = \tfrac89.\) The radius of circle \(B\) is \(\tfrac89.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

20单选

独立随机地选取 \(0\) 和 \(1\) 之间的数 \(a\) 与 \(b\)，并令 \(c\) 为它们的和。将 \(a\)，\(b\)，和 \(c\) 分别四舍五入到最接近的整数，所得结果为 \(A\)，\(B\)，和 \(C\)。\(A + B = C\) 的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Select numbers \(a\) and \(b\) between \(0\) and \(1\) independently and at random, and let \(c\) be their sum. Let \(A,\) \(B,\) and \(C\) be the results when \(a,\) \(b,\) and \(c,\) respectively, are rounded to the nearest integer. What is the probability that \(A + B = C?\)

将选择表示为单位正方形中的点 \((a, b)\)。\(a\) 和 \(b\) 小于 \(\tfrac12\) 时四舍五入为 \(0\)，否则四舍五入为 \(1\)；而 \(c = a + b\) 的分界点为 \(\tfrac12\) 和 \(\tfrac32\)。

这个等式恰好在两个区域内不成立。若 \(a, b \lt \tfrac12\)，则当 \(a + b \ge \tfrac12\) 时不成立，这是一个面积为 \(\tfrac18\) 的直角三角形。若 \(a, b \ge \tfrac12\)，则当 \(a + b \lt \tfrac32\) 时不成立，这是另一个面积为 \(\tfrac18\) 的直角三角形。当 \(a, b\) 中恰好有一个不小于 \(\tfrac12\) 时，等式总是成立。

因此不成立的概率为 \(\tfrac18 + \tfrac18 = \tfrac14\)，所以所求概率为 \(1 - \tfrac14 = \tfrac34\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Represent the choices as a point \((a, b)\) in the unit square. Each of \(a\) and \(b\) rounds to \(0\) if below \(\tfrac12\) and to \(1\) otherwise, while \(c = a + b\) rounds based on \(\tfrac12\) and \(\tfrac32.\)

The equation fails in exactly two regions. If \(a, b \lt \tfrac12,\) it fails when \(a + b \ge \tfrac12;\) this is a right triangle of area \(\tfrac18.\) If \(a, b \ge \tfrac12,\) it fails when \(a + b \lt \tfrac32;\) this is another right triangle of area \(\tfrac18.\) When exactly one of \(a, b\) is at least \(\tfrac12,\) the equation always holds.

Thus the failure probability is \(\tfrac18 + \tfrac18 = \tfrac14,\) so the requested probability is \(1 - \tfrac14 = \tfrac34.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

21单选

如果 \[\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \cos^{2n} \theta = 5\text{，}\] 那么 \(\cos 2\theta\) 的值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

If \[\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \cos^{2n} \theta = 5,\] what is the value of \(\cos 2\theta?\)

该级数首项为 \(1\)，公比为 \(\cos^2 \theta\)，所以和为 \(\dfrac{1}{1 - \cos^2 \theta} = \dfrac{1}{\sin^2 \theta} = 5\)。

因此 \(\sin^2 \theta = \tfrac15\)，并且 \[ \begin{aligned} \cos 2\theta &= 1 - 2\sin^2 \theta \\ &= 1 - \tfrac25 = \tfrac35 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The series is geometric with first term \(1\) and ratio \(\cos^2 \theta,\) so its sum is \(\dfrac{1}{1 - \cos^2 \theta} = \dfrac{1}{\sin^2 \theta} = 5.\)

Thus \(\sin^2 \theta = \tfrac15,\) and \[ \begin{aligned} \cos 2\theta &= 1 - 2\sin^2 \theta \\ &= 1 - \tfrac25 = \tfrac35. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is D.

22单选

三个互相相切、半径为 \(1\) 的球放在一个水平平面上。一个半径为 \(2\) 的球放在它们上面。从平面到较大球顶部的距离是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Three mutually tangent spheres of radius \(1\) rest on a horizontal plane. A sphere of radius \(2\) rests on them. What is the distance from the plane to the top of the larger sphere?

设三个单位球的球心为 \(A\)，\(B\)，\(C\)，它们在离平面高度 \(1\) 处形成边长为 \(2\) 的等边三角形；设 \(E\) 为大球球心，位于 \(\triangle ABC\) 的重心 \(D\) 正上方。

从顶点到重心的距离为 \(AD = \tfrac{2\sqrt3}{3}\)，且 \(AE = 1 + 2 = 3\)，所以 \[ \begin{aligned} DE &= \sqrt{3^2 - \left(\tfrac{2\sqrt3}{3}\right)^2} \\ &= \sqrt{9 - \tfrac{4}{3}} \\ &= \dfrac{\sqrt{69}}{3} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因为 \(D\) 位于平面上方 \(1\) 个单位，而大球顶部在 \(E\) 上方 \(2\) 个单位，所以总高度为 \[ 1 + \dfrac{\sqrt{69}}{3} + 2 = 3 + \dfrac{\sqrt{69}}{3}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Let the centers of the three unit spheres be \(A,\) \(B,\) \(C,\) forming an equilateral triangle of side \(2\) at height \(1\) above the plane, and let \(E\) be the center of the large sphere directly above the centroid \(D\) of \(\triangle ABC.\)

The distance from a vertex to the centroid is \(AD = \tfrac{2\sqrt3}{3},\) and \(AE = 1 + 2 = 3,\) so \[ \begin{aligned} DE &= \sqrt{3^2 - \left(\tfrac{2\sqrt3}{3}\right)^2} \\ &= \sqrt{9 - \tfrac{4}{3}} \\ &= \dfrac{\sqrt{69}}{3}. \end{aligned} \]

Since \(D\) is \(1\) unit above the plane and the top of the large sphere is \(2\) units above \(E,\) the total height is \[ 1 + \dfrac{\sqrt{69}}{3} + 2 = 3 + \dfrac{\sqrt{69}}{3}. \]

Thus, the correct answer is B.

23单选

多项式 \[ \begin{aligned} &P(x) = c_{2004} x^{2004} + c_{2003} x^{2003} \\ &\quad {}+ \cdots + c_1 x + c_0 \end{aligned} \] 的系数均为实数，且 \(c_{2004} \ne 0\)。它有 \(2004\) 个不同的复数零点 \(z_k = a_k + b_k i\)，其中 \(1 \le k \le 2004\)，\(a_k\) 和 \(b_k\) 均为实数，\(a_1 = b_1 = 0\)，并且 \[\sum_{k=1}^{2004} a_k = \sum_{k=1}^{2004} b_k\text{。}\] 下列哪个量可能是非零数？

English（选项同上方）

A polynomial \[ \begin{aligned} &P(x) = c_{2004} x^{2004} + c_{2003} x^{2003} \\ &\quad {}+ \cdots + c_1 x + c_0 \end{aligned} \] has real coefficients with \(c_{2004} \ne 0\) and \(2004\) distinct complex zeros \(z_k = a_k + b_k i,\) \(1 \le k \le 2004\) with \(a_k\) and \(b_k\) real, \(a_1 = b_1 = 0,\) and \[\sum_{k=1}^{2004} a_k = \sum_{k=1}^{2004} b_k.\] Which of the following quantities can be a nonzero number?

因为 \(z_1 = a_1 + b_1 i = 0\) 是根，所以 \(c_0 = P(0) = 0\)。

非实零点成共轭对出现，所以 \(\sum b_k = 0\) 而题设于是强制 \(\sum a_k = 0\)。系数 \(c_{2003}\) 等于 \(-c_{2004}\) 乘以根之和 \(\sum a_k + i \sum b_k = 0\)，所以 \(c_{2003} = 0\)。

因为次数为偶数，\(z_2, \ldots, z_{2004}\) 中至少有一个实根，使得某个 \(b_k = 0\)，所以 \(b_2 b_3 \cdots b_{2004} = 0\)。因此 (A) 到 (D) 都必须为 \(0\)。

另一方面，\(\sum_{k=1}^{2004} c_k = P(1)\)。一个有效多项式例如 \(P(x) = x(x - 2)(x - 3) \cdots\) \(\cdot (x - 2003)\) \(\cdot \left(x + \sum_{k=2}^{2003} k\right)\) 满足 \(P(1) \ne 0\)，所以只有 \(\sum c_k\) 可能非零。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Since \(z_1 = a_1 + b_1 i = 0\) is a root, \(c_0 = P(0) = 0.\)

The nonreal zeros occur in conjugate pairs, so \(\sum b_k = 0,\) and the hypothesis then forces \(\sum a_k = 0.\) The coefficient \(c_{2003}\) equals \(-c_{2004}\) times the sum of the roots \(\sum a_k + i \sum b_k = 0,\) so \(c_{2003} = 0.\)

Because the degree is even, at least one of \(z_2, \ldots, z_{2004}\) is real, making one \(b_k = 0,\) so \(b_2 b_3 \cdots b_{2004} = 0.\) Thus (A) through (D) all must be \(0.\)

On the other hand, \(\sum_{k=1}^{2004} c_k = P(1),\) and a valid polynomial such as \(P(x) = x(x - 2)(x - 3) \cdots\) \(\cdot (x - 2003)\) \(\cdot \left(x + \sum_{k=2}^{2003} k\right)\) has \(P(1) \ne 0.\) So only \(\sum c_k\) can be nonzero.

Thus, the correct answer is E.

24单选

一个平面中有点 \(A\) 和 \(B\)，且 \(AB = 1\)。设 \(S\) 为平面中所有覆盖线段 \(\overline{AB}\) 的半径为 \(1\) 的圆盘的并集。\(S\) 的面积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A plane contains points \(A\) and \(B\) with \(AB = 1.\) Let \(S\) be the union of all disks of radius \(1\) in the plane that cover \(\overline{AB}.\) What is the area of \(S?\)

一个半径为 \(1\) 的圆盘覆盖线段 \(\overline{AB}\)，当且仅当它的圆心到 \(A\) 和 \(B\) 的距离都不超过 \(1\)。这个区域 \(R\) 是以 \(A\) 和 \(B\) 为圆心的两个单位圆的重叠透镜。

每个单位圆都经过另一个圆的圆心，所以透镜由两段 \(120^\circ\) 圆弧围成。两个 \(120^\circ\) 扇形的面积各为 \(\tfrac{\pi}{3}\)，重叠中扣掉的两个等边三角形总面积为 \(\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\)，所以 \(R\) 的面积为 \(\tfrac{2\pi}{3} - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\)。

集合 \(S\) 由所有到 \(R\) 距离不超过 \(1\) 的点组成。除 \(R\) 本身外，还增加两个 \(60^\circ\) 半径 \(1\) 的扇形（每个面积 \(\tfrac{\pi}{6}\)）和两个 \(120^\circ\) 外半径 \(2\)、内半径 \(1\) 的环形区域（每个面积 \(\pi\)）。

因此 \(S\) 的面积为 \[ \begin{aligned} &\left(\tfrac{2\pi}{3} - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\right) + 2 \cdot \tfrac{\pi}{6} \\ &\quad {}+ 2\pi = 3\pi - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

A radius-\(1\) disk covers segment \(\overline{AB}\) exactly when its center is within \(1\) of both \(A\) and \(B.\) That region \(R\) is the lens where the two unit circles centered at \(A\) and \(B\) overlap.

Each unit circle passes through the other’s center, so the lens is bounded by two \(120^\circ\) arcs. Two \(120^\circ\) sectors of area \(\tfrac{\pi}{3}\) overlap in two equilateral triangles of total area \(\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2},\) giving \(R\) area \(\tfrac{2\pi}{3} - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}.\)

The set \(S\) consists of all points within \(1\) of \(R.\) Beyond \(R\) itself, this adds two \(60^\circ\) sectors of radius \(1\) (each area \(\tfrac{\pi}{6}\)) and two \(120^\circ\) annuli of outer radius \(2\) and inner radius \(1\) (each area \(\pi\)).

Therefore the area of \(S\) is \[ \begin{aligned} &\left(\tfrac{2\pi}{3} - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\right) + 2 \cdot \tfrac{\pi}{6} \\ &\quad {}+ 2\pi = 3\pi - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is C.

对每个整数 \(n \ge 4\)，令 \(a_n\) 表示 \(n\) 进制数 \(0.\overline{133}_n\)。乘积 \(a_4 a_5 \ldots a_{99}\) 可表示为 \(\dfrac{m}{n!}\)，其中 \(m\) 和 \(n\) 是正整数，且 \(n\) 尽可能小。\(m\) 的值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

For each integer \(n \ge 4,\) let \(a_n\) denote the base-\(n\) number \(0.\overline{133}_n.\) The product \(a_4 a_5 \ldots a_{99}\) can be expressed as \(\dfrac{m}{n!},\) where \(m\) and \(n\) are positive integers and \(n\) is as small as possible. What is the value of \(m?\)

因为 \(n^3 \cdot a_n = 133.\overline{133}_n\) \(= a_n + n^2 + 3n + 3\)，得到 \[ \begin{aligned} a_n &= \dfrac{n^2 + 3n + 3}{n^3 - 1} \\ &= \dfrac{(n+1)^3 - 1}{n(n^3 - 1)} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

写成 \(n^3 - 1 = (n - 1)(n^2 + n + 1)\)，以及 \((n+1)^3 - 1\) \(= n\big((n+1)^2 + (n+1) + 1\big)\)，乘积 \(a_4 a_5 \cdots a_{99}\) 裂项相消为 \[ \begin{gathered} \dfrac{3!}{99!} \cdot \dfrac{100^3 - 1}{4^3 - 1} \\ {}= \dfrac{3!}{99!} \cdot \dfrac{99(100^2 + 100 + 1)}{63} \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

它化简为 \(\dfrac{(2)(10101)}{(21)(98!)} = \dfrac{962}{98!}\)。若 \(n \le 97\)，把这个分数改写成以 \(n!\) 为分母会要求 \(98\) 整除 \(962\)，但并不成立。因此最小可能的 \(n\) 是 \(98\)，且 \(m = 962\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Since \(n^3 \cdot a_n = 133.\overline{133}_n\) \(= a_n + n^2 + 3n + 3,\) we get \[ \begin{aligned} a_n &= \dfrac{n^2 + 3n + 3}{n^3 - 1} \\ &= \dfrac{(n+1)^3 - 1}{n(n^3 - 1)}. \end{aligned} \]

Writing \(n^3 - 1 = (n - 1)(n^2 + n + 1)\) and \((n+1)^3 - 1\) \(= n\big((n+1)^2 + (n+1) + 1\big),\) the product \(a_4 a_5 \cdots a_{99}\) telescopes to \[ \begin{gathered} \dfrac{3!}{99!} \cdot \dfrac{100^3 - 1}{4^3 - 1} \\ {}= \dfrac{3!}{99!} \cdot \dfrac{99(100^2 + 100 + 1)}{63}. \end{gathered} \]

This simplifies to \(\dfrac{(2)(10101)}{(21)(98!)} = \dfrac{962}{98!}.\) If \(n \le 97,\) then rewriting this fraction with denominator \(n!\) would require \(98\) to divide \(962,\) which it does not. Hence the smallest possible \(n\) is \(98,\) and \(m = 962.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.
