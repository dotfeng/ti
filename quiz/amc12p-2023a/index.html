1单选

城市 \(A\) 和 \(B\) 相距 \(45\) 英里。Alicia 住在 \(A\)，Beth 住在 \(B\)。Alicia 以每小时 \(18\) 英里的速度骑车前往 \(B\)。Beth 同时出发，以每小时 \(12\) 英里的速度骑车前往 \(A\)。她们相遇时距离城市 \(A\) 多少英里？

English（选项同上方）

Cities \(A\) and \(B\) are \(45\) miles apart. Alicia lives in \(A\) and Beth lives in \(B.\) Alicia bikes towards \(B\) at \(18\) miles per hour. Leaving at the same time, Beth bikes toward \(A\) at \(12\) miles per hour. How many miles from City \(A\) will they be when they meet?

两人之间的距离以每小时 \(18+12=30\) 英里的速度缩短，所以她们在 \(\dfrac{45}{30}=1.5\) 小时后相遇。

在这段时间里，Alicia 从城市 \(A\) 出发骑了 \(18\cdot 1.5=27\) 英里。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The gap between them closes at \(18+12=30\) miles per hour, so they meet after \(\dfrac{45}{30}=1.5\) hours.

In that time Alicia has ridden \(18\cdot 1.5=27\) miles from City \(A.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

2单选

一个大披萨的 \(\tfrac13\) 加上 \(3\tfrac12\) 杯橙子片的重量，等于一个大披萨的 \(\tfrac34\) 加上 \(\tfrac12\) 杯橙子片的重量。一杯橙子片重 \(\tfrac14\) 磅。一个大披萨重多少磅？

English（选项同上方）

The weight of \(\tfrac13\) of a large pizza together with \(3\tfrac12\) cups of orange slices is the same as the weight of \(\tfrac34\) of a large pizza together with \(\tfrac12\) cup of orange slices. A cup of orange slices weighs \(\tfrac14\) of a pound. What is the weight, in pounds, of a large pizza?

设披萨重 \(P\) 磅。则 \(3\tfrac12\) 杯重 \(\tfrac72\cdot\tfrac14=\tfrac78\) 而 \(\tfrac12\) 杯重 \(\tfrac12\cdot\tfrac14=\tfrac18\)。

方程为 \[ \tfrac13 P+\tfrac78=\tfrac34 P+\tfrac18\text{。}\]

相减得 \(\tfrac78-\tfrac18=\left(\tfrac34-\tfrac13\right)P\)，所以 \(\tfrac34=\tfrac{5}{12}P\)，从而 \(P=\dfrac{9}{5}=1\tfrac45\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Let the pizza weigh \(P\) pounds. Then \(3\tfrac12\) cups weigh \(\tfrac72\cdot\tfrac14=\tfrac78\) and \(\tfrac12\) cup weighs \(\tfrac12\cdot\tfrac14=\tfrac18.\)

The equation is \[ \tfrac13 P+\tfrac78=\tfrac34 P+\tfrac18. \]

Subtracting gives \(\tfrac78-\tfrac18=\left(\tfrac34-\tfrac13\right)P,\) so \(\tfrac34=\tfrac{5}{12}P\) and \(P=\dfrac{9}{5}=1\tfrac45.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

3单选

小于 \(2023\) 且能被 \(5\) 整除的正完全平方数有多少个？

English（选项同上方）

How many positive perfect squares less than \(2023\) are divisible by \(5?\)

一个完全平方数能被 \(5\) 整除，只有它的平方根也能被五整除，因此这些平方数形如 \((5k)^2=25k^2\)。

因为 \(44^2=1936\lt 2023\)，而且这个数 \(\lt 2025=45^2\)，所以平方根可以是 \(5,10,\ldots,40\)，也就是 \(k=1\) 到 \(8\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

A perfect square is divisible by \(5\) only if its root is, so the squares are \((5k)^2=25k^2.\)

Since \(44^2=1936\lt 2023\) \(\lt 2025=45^2,\) the root can be \(5,10,\ldots,40,\) which is \(k=1\) through \(8.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

4单选

\(8^5\cdot 5^{10}\cdot 15^5\) 的通常数字表示有多少位数字？

English（选项同上方）

How many digits are in the base-ten representation of \(8^5\cdot 5^{10}\cdot 15^5?\)

全部分解为质因数：\[ \begin{gathered} 8^5\cdot 5^{10}\cdot 15^5\\ {}=2^{15}\cdot 5^{10}\cdot 3^5\cdot 5^5\\ {}=3^5\cdot 2^{15}\cdot 5^{15} \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

这等于 \(3^5\cdot 10^{15}=243\cdot 10^{15}\) 也就是 \(243\) 后接 \(15\) 个零，总共有 \(18\) 位数字。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Writing everything in primes, \[ \begin{gathered} 8^5\cdot 5^{10}\cdot 15^5\\ {}=2^{15}\cdot 5^{10}\cdot 3^5\cdot 5^5\\ {}=3^5\cdot 2^{15}\cdot 5^{15}. \end{gathered} \]

This equals \(3^5\cdot 10^{15}=243\cdot 10^{15},\) which is \(243\) followed by \(15\) zeros, for a total of \(18\) digits.

Thus, the correct answer is E.

5单选

Janet 掷一个标准 \(6\) 面骰子 \(4\) 次，并持续记录掷出点数的累加和。她的累加和在某一时刻等于 \(3\) 的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Janet rolls a standard \(6\)-sided die \(4\) times and keeps a running total of the numbers she rolls. What is the probability that at some point, her running total will equal \(3?\)

累加和递增，所以它恰好到达 \(3\) 当且仅当出现以下互斥开头之一：第一次掷出 \(3\)；前两次为 \(1,2\)；前两次为 \(2,1\)；或前三次为 \(1,1,1\)。

它们的概率之和为 \[ \begin{gathered} \dfrac16+\dfrac1{36}+\dfrac1{36}+\dfrac1{216}\\ {}=\dfrac{36+6+6+1}{216}\\ {}=\dfrac{49}{216} \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The running total is increasing, so it hits \(3\) exactly when one of these disjoint openings occurs: a first roll of \(3;\) rolls \(1,2;\) rolls \(2,1;\) or rolls \(1,1,1.\)

Their probabilities are \[ \begin{gathered} \dfrac16+\dfrac1{36}+\dfrac1{36}+\dfrac1{216}\\ {}=\dfrac{36+6+6+1}{216}\\ {}=\dfrac{49}{216}. \end{gathered} \]

Thus, the correct answer is B.

6单选

点 \(A\) 和 \(B\) 在 \(y=\log_2 x\) 的图像上。线段 \(\overline{AB}\) 的中点是 \((6,2)\)。\(A\) 和 \(B\) 的 \(x\) 坐标的正差是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Points \(A\) and \(B\) lie on the graph of \(y=\log_2 x.\) The midpoint of \(\overline{AB}\) is \((6,2).\) What is the positive difference between the \(x\)-coordinates of \(A\) and \(B?\)

设两个 \(x\) 坐标为 \(x_1\) 和 \(x_2\)。由中点可得 \(x_1+x_2=12\)，由两个 \(y\) 值的平均数可得 \(\log_2 x_1+\log_2 x_2=4\)，所以 \(x_1x_2=2^4=16\)。

因此 \[ \begin{gathered} |x_1-x_2|\\ {}=\sqrt{(x_1+x_2)^2-4x_1x_2}\\ {}=\sqrt{144-64}\\ {}=\sqrt{80}\\ {}=4\sqrt5 \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Let the \(x\)-coordinates be \(x_1\) and \(x_2.\) The midpoint gives \(x_1+x_2=12,\) and the average of the \(y\)-values gives \(\log_2 x_1+\log_2 x_2=4,\) so \(x_1x_2=2^4=16.\)

Then \[ \begin{gathered} |x_1-x_2|\\ {}=\sqrt{(x_1+x_2)^2-4x_1x_2}\\ {}=\sqrt{144-64}\\ {}=\sqrt{80}\\ {}=4\sqrt5. \end{gathered} \]

Thus, the correct answer is D.

7单选

一个电子显示屏把当前日期显示为一个 \(8\) 位整数：先是 \(4\) 位年份，再是 \(2\) 位月份，最后是该月内的 \(2\) 位日期。例如，今年的植树节显示为 \(20230428\)。在 \(2023\) 年中，有多少个日期的 \(8\) 位显示中每个数字都出现偶数次？

English（选项同上方）

A digital display shows the current date as an \(8\)-digit integer consisting of a \(4\)-digit year, followed by a \(2\)-digit month, followed by a \(2\)-digit date within the month. For example, Arbor Day this year is displayed as \(20230428.\) For how many dates in \(2023\) will each digit appear an even number of times in the \(8\)-digit display for that date?

年份贡献数字 \(2,0,2,3\)，所以 \(2\) 出现两次，而 \(0\) 和 \(3\) 各出现一次。为了让每个数字最终出现偶数次，月份和日期的四个数字必须提供奇数个 \(0\)、奇数个 \(3\)，以及偶数个其他每种数字。

只有四个数字可用，因此月份日期串必须恰好包含一个 \(0\)、一个 \(3\)，以及由某个数字组成的一对。检查合法月份和日期，得到九个日期：\(01\text{-}13\)、\(01\text{-}31\)、\(02\text{-}23\)、\(03\text{-}11\)、\(03\text{-}22\)、\(10\text{-}13\)、\(10\text{-}31\)、\(11\text{-}03\) 和 \(11\text{-}30\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The year contributes the digits \(2,0,2,3,\) so \(2\) appears twice while \(0\) and \(3\) each appear once. For every digit to end up with an even count, the four digits of the month and day must supply an odd number of \(0\)’s, an odd number of \(3\)’s, and an even number of every other digit.

With only four digits available, the month-day string must use exactly one \(0,\) one \(3,\) and a repeated pair of some digit. Checking valid months and days leaves nine dates: \(01\text{-}13,\) \(01\text{-}31,\) \(02\text{-}23,\) \(03\text{-}11,\) \(03\text{-}22,\) \(10\text{-}13,\) \(10\text{-}31,\) \(11\text{-}03,\) and \(11\text{-}30.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

8单选

Maureen 正在记录本学期小测成绩的平均分。如果 Maureen 下一次小测得 \(11\) 分，她的平均分会增加 \(1\)。如果她接下来三次小测都得 \(11\) 分，她的平均分会增加 \(2\)。她当前的小测平均分是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Maureen is keeping track of the mean of her quiz scores this semester. If Maureen scores an \(11\) on the next quiz, her mean will increase by \(1.\) If she scores an \(11\) on each of the next three quizzes, her mean will increase by \(2.\) What is the mean of her quiz scores currently?

设当前 \(n\) 次小测的平均分为 \(m\)，所以总分为 \(S=mn\)。加入一次 \(11\) 分后，有 \[ \dfrac{mn+11}{n+1}=m+1\text{，} \] 化简得 \(m+n=10\)。

加入三次 \(11\) 分后，有 \[ \dfrac{mn+33}{n+3}=m+2\text{，} \] 化简得 \(3m+2n=27\)。

解 \(m+n=10\) 和 \(3m+2n=27\) 得到 \(m=7\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Let the current mean be \(m\) over \(n\) quizzes, so the total is \(S=mn.\) Adding one \(11\) gives \[ \dfrac{mn+11}{n+1}=m+1, \] which simplifies to \(m+n=10.\)

Adding three \(11\)’s gives \[ \dfrac{mn+33}{n+3}=m+2, \] which simplifies to \(3m+2n=27.\)

Solving \(m+n=10\) and \(3m+2n=27\) gives \(m=7.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

9单选

一个面积为 \(2\) 的正方形内接于一个面积为 \(3\) 的正方形中，形成四个全等三角形，如下图所示。阴影直角三角形的较短直角边与较长直角边之比是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A square of area \(2\) is inscribed in a square of area \(3,\) creating four congruent triangles, as shown below. What is the ratio of the shorter leg to the longer leg in the shaded right triangle?

外正方形边长为 \(\sqrt3\)，内正方形边长为 \(\sqrt2\)。每个三角形是直角三角形，其两条直角边 \(p\) 和 \(q\) 沿着外正方形的一条边，所以 \(p+q=\sqrt3\)；其斜边是内正方形的一条边，所以 \(p^2+q^2=2\)。

由 \((p+q)^2=3\) 可得 \(2pq=1\)，所以 \(p\) 和 \(q\) 是 \(t^2-\sqrt3\,t+\tfrac12=0\) 的两个根，即 \(\dfrac{\sqrt3\pm 1}{2}\)。

较短边与较长边之比为 \[ \begin{gathered} \dfrac{\sqrt3-1}{\sqrt3+1}\\ {}=\dfrac{(\sqrt3-1)^2}{2}\\ {}=2-\sqrt3 \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The outer square has side \(\sqrt3\) and the inner square has side \(\sqrt2.\) Each triangle is right, with legs \(p\) and \(q\) along an outer side, so \(p+q=\sqrt3,\) and with hypotenuse an inner side, so \(p^2+q^2=2.\)

Then \((p+q)^2=3\) gives \(2pq=1,\) so \(p\) and \(q\) are the roots of \(t^2-\sqrt3\,t+\tfrac12=0,\) namely \(\dfrac{\sqrt3\pm 1}{2}.\)

The ratio of shorter to longer leg is \[ \begin{gathered} \dfrac{\sqrt3-1}{\sqrt3+1}\\ {}=\dfrac{(\sqrt3-1)^2}{2}\\ {}=2-\sqrt3. \end{gathered} \]

Thus, the correct answer is C.

10单选

正实数 \(x\) 和 \(y\) 满足 \(y^3=x^2\) 且 \((y-x)^2=4y^2\)。求 \(x+y\)？

English（选项同上方）

Positive real numbers \(x\) and \(y\) satisfy \(y^3=x^2\) and \((y-x)^2=4y^2.\) What is \(x+y?\)

由 \((y-x)^2=4y^2\)，可得 \(y-x=\pm 2y\)。若 \(y-x=2y\)，则 \(x=-y\lt 0\)，不可能；因此 \(y-x=-2y\)，也就是 \(x=3y\)。

代入 \(y^3=x^2=9y^2\) 得到 \(y=9\)，所以 \(x=27\)，\(x+y=36\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

From \((y-x)^2=4y^2\) we get \(y-x=\pm 2y.\) The choice \(y-x=2y\) gives \(x=-y\lt 0,\) impossible, so \(y-x=-2y,\) meaning \(x=3y.\)

Substituting into \(y^3=x^2=9y^2\) gives \(y=9,\) hence \(x=27\) and \(x+y=36.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

11单选

斜率为 \(2\) 和 \(\tfrac13\) 的两条直线所成锐角的度数是多少？

English（选项同上方）

What is the degree measure of the acute angle formed by lines with slopes \(2\) and \(\tfrac13?\)

两直线夹角的正切为 \[ \left|\dfrac{2-\tfrac13}{1+2\cdot\tfrac13}\right| =\left|\dfrac{\tfrac53}{\tfrac53}\right|=1\text{。}\]

正切为 \(1\) 的锐角是 \(45^\circ\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The tangent of the angle between the lines is \[ \left|\dfrac{2-\tfrac13}{1+2\cdot\tfrac13}\right| =\left|\dfrac{\tfrac53}{\tfrac53}\right|=1. \]

The acute angle with tangent \(1\) is \(45^\circ.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

12单选

求下式的值：\[ \begin{gathered} 2^3-1^3+4^3-3^3+6^3-5^3\\ {}+\cdots+18^3-17^3 \end{gathered}\text{？}\]

English（选项同上方）

What is the value of \[ \begin{gathered} 2^3-1^3+4^3-3^3+6^3-5^3\\ {}+\cdots+18^3-17^3? \end{gathered} \]

配成 \((2k)^3-(2k-1)^3\)，其中 \(k=1,\ldots,9\)。展开得 \[ \begin{gathered} (2k)^3-(2k-1)^3\\ {}=12k^2-6k+1 \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

对 \(k=1\) 到 \(9\)，求和，并用 \(\sum k^2=285\)、\(\sum k=45\) 得到 \[ \begin{gathered} 12\cdot 285-6\cdot 45+9\\ {}=3420-270+9\\ {}=3159 \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Group into pairs \((2k)^3-(2k-1)^3\) for \(k=1,\ldots,9.\) Expanding, \[ \begin{gathered} (2k)^3-(2k-1)^3\\ {}=12k^2-6k+1. \end{gathered} \]

Summing for \(k=1\) to \(9,\) with \(\sum k^2=285\) and \(\sum k=45,\) gives \[ \begin{gathered} 12\cdot 285-6\cdot 45+9\\ {}=3420-270+9\\ {}=3159. \end{gathered} \]

Thus, the correct answer is D.

13单选

在一场乒乓球锦标赛中，每位参赛者都与其他每位参赛者恰好比赛一次。虽然右手选手的人数是左手选手的两倍，但左手选手赢的场数比右手选手赢的场数多 \(40\%\)。（没有平局，也没有双利手选手。）总共进行了多少场比赛？

English（选项同上方）

In a table tennis tournament every participant played every other participant exactly once. Although there were twice as many right-handed players as left-handed players, the number of games won by left-handed players was \(40\%\) more than the number of games won by right-handed players. (There were no ties and no ambidextrous players.) What is the total number of games played?

设有 \(L\) 名左手选手和 \(2L\) 名右手选手，则共有 \(3L\) 人，总场数为 \(\binom{3L}{2}\)。

若右手选手赢 \(R\) 场，则左手选手赢 \(1.4R\) 场，所以总场数为 \(2.4R=\tfrac{12}{5}R\)。要使它是整数场数，总场数必须是 \(12\) 的倍数。

左手选手至多能赢下所有至少有一名左手选手参加的比赛，即 \(\binom{L}{2}+2L^2=\tfrac{5L^2-L}{2}\) 场。另一方面，他们必须赢 \(\tfrac{7}{12}\binom{3L}{2}=\tfrac{7L(3L-1)}8\) 场。比较这两个量可得 \(21L-7\le 20L-4\)，所以 \(L\le 3\)。

依次试 \(L=1,2,3\)，总场数为 \(3,15,36\)；其中只有 \(36\) 是 \(12\) 的倍数，且可实现：\(3\) 名左手选手可以赢下全部 \(18\) 场混合对局以及左手内部的 \(3\) 场，共 \(21=1.4\cdot 15\) 场。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Let there be \(L\) left-handed and \(2L\) right-handed players, for \(3L\) players and \(\binom{3L}{2}\) games total.

If right-handers win \(R\) games, left-handers win \(1.4R,\) so the total is \(2.4R=\tfrac{12}{5}R.\) For this to be an integer count, the total number of games must be a multiple of \(12.\)

The left-handers can win at most every game involving at least one left-hander, namely \(\binom{L}{2}+2L^2=\tfrac{5L^2-L}{2}.\) On the other hand, they must win \(\tfrac{7}{12}\binom{3L}{2}=\tfrac{7L(3L-1)}8\) games. Comparing these quantities gives \(21L-7\le 20L-4,\) so \(L\le 3.\)

Testing \(L=1,2,3\) gives totals \(3,15,36;\) only \(36\) is a multiple of \(12.\) It is achievable: the \(3\) left-handers can win all \(18\) mixed games and their \(3\) internal games, giving \(21=1.4\cdot 15\) wins.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

14单选

有多少个复数满足方程 \(z^5=\overline{z}\)，其中 \(\overline{z}\) 是复数 \(z\) 的共轭？

English（选项同上方）

How many complex numbers satisfy the equation \(z^5=\overline{z},\) where \(\overline{z}\) is the conjugate of the complex number \(z?\)

取模长得 \(|z|^5=|z|\)，所以 \(|z|=0\) 或 \(|z|=1\)。\(z=0\) 满足方程，给出一个解。

若 \(|z|=1\)，把原方程乘以 \(z\) 得到 \(z^6=z\overline{z}=|z|^2=1\)。这有 \(6\) 个不同的根，并且这些根的模长都等于 \(1\)。

总共有 \(1+6=7\) 个解。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Taking magnitudes gives \(|z|^5=|z|,\) so \(|z|=0\) or \(|z|=1.\) The value \(z=0\) works, giving one solution.

If \(|z|=1,\) multiply the equation by \(z\) to get \(z^6=z\overline{z}=|z|^2=1.\) This has \(6\) distinct roots, all of modulus \(1.\)

Altogether there are \(1+6=7\) solutions.

Thus, the correct answer is E.

15单选

Usain 为了锻炼，在一块 \(100\) 米乘 \(30\) 米的矩形场地中之字形行走，从点 \(A\) 出发并在 \(\overline{BC}\) 上结束。他想像下图所示（\(APQRS\)）通过之字形增加行走距离。什么角 \(\theta=\angle PAB\) \(=\angle QPC\) \(=\angle RQB=\cdots\) 会使路径长度为 \(120\) 米？（不要假设之字形路径恰好有图中所示的四段；段数可能更多或更少。）

English（选项同上方）

Usain is walking for exercise by zigzagging across a \(100\)-meter by \(30\)-meter rectangular field, beginning at point \(A\) and ending on the segment \(\overline{BC}.\) He wants to increase the distance walked by zigzagging as shown in the figure below (\(APQRS\)). What angle \(\theta=\angle PAB\) \(=\angle QPC\) \(=\angle RQB=\cdots\) will produce a length that is \(120\) meters? (Do not assume the zigzag path has exactly four segments as shown; there could be more or fewer.)

之字形的每一段都与场地的水平边成角 \(\theta\)。因此，长度为 \(s\) 的一段在水平方向前进 \(s\cos\theta\) 米。即使最后一段在横跨场地完整宽度之前结束，这一点仍然成立。

在这条闭合有界曲线上 \(y\) 取最大值的点处，隐函数求导（或拉格朗日乘数法）给出 \((1+2x)\bigl(P+2y^2\bigr)=0\)，其中 \(P=1+x+x^2-y^2\)。但 \(P+2y^2=x^2+x+1+y^2\gt 0\)，所以 \(x=-\tfrac12\)。

English

Here \(m=19\) and \(n=2,\) so \(m+n=21.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

因此落在 \(10\) 上的概率是 \(\dfrac12\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Let \(a_n\) be the probability that Flora ever lands exactly on \(n,\) with \(a_0=1.\) Conditioning on the first jump, \[ a_n=\sum_{k=1}^{n}\dfrac{1}{2^k}\,a_{n-k}. \]

We prove by induction that \(a_n=\tfrac12\) for every \(n\ge 1.\) The case \(n=1\) is immediate. If the claim holds through \(n-1,\) then \[ \begin{aligned} a_n&=\frac{1}{2^n}a_0 +\sum_{k=1}^{n-1}\frac{1}{2^k}\cdot\frac12\\ &=\frac{1}{2^n} +\frac12\left(1-\frac{1}{2^{n-1}}\right)\\ &=\frac12. \end{aligned} \]

What is the product of all the solutions to the equation \[ \begin{gathered} \log_{7x}2023\cdot\log_{289x}2023\\ {}=\log_{2023x}2023? \end{gathered} \]

English

Let \(a=\log_{2023}7\) and \(b=\log_{2023}289.\) Since \(2023=7\cdot 289,\) we have \(a+b=1.\) Writing \(t=\log_{2023}x,\) each logarithm becomes a reciprocal, and the equation turns into \[ (1+t)=(a+t)(b+t). \]

English（选项同上方）

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Let \(S_n\) be the sum of row \(n.\) Each interior entry is \(1\) more than the sum of the two entries above it, and summing over the row gives the recurrence \[ S_n=2S_{n-1}+(n-2). \]

So \(S_{2023}=2^{2023}-2023.\) Since powers of \(2\) cycle with units digits \(2,4,8,6\) and \(2023\equiv 3\pmod 4,\) \(2^{2023}\) ends in \(8.\) Then \(8-3=5\) gives units digit \(5.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.
