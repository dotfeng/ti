当 \(x = 7\) 时，下列哪一个最小？

English（选项同上方）

For \(x = 7,\) which of the following is the smallest?

代入 \(x = 7\)，五个选项的值依次为 \(\dfrac{6}{7}\)、\(\dfrac{6}{8}\)、\(1\)、\(\dfrac{7}{6}\) 和 \(\dfrac{4}{3}\)。

只有前两个值小于 \(1\)。这两个分数的分子相同，所以分母较大的分数较小。因此正确答案是 B。

English

Substituting \(x = 7\) gives the five values \(\dfrac{6}{7},\) \(\dfrac{6}{8},\) \(1,\) \(\dfrac{7}{6},\) and \(\dfrac{4}{3}.\)

The first two are the only values less than \(1.\) Because they have the same numerator, the fraction with the larger denominator is smaller. Thus, the correct answer is B .

下列表达式的值是多少？\[ \begin{aligned} &2\left(1-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)+3\left(1-\dfrac{1}{3}\right) \\ &\quad {}+4\left(1-\dfrac{1}{4}\right)+\cdots \\ &\quad {}+10\left(1-\dfrac{1}{10}\right) \end{aligned} \]

English（选项同上方）

What is the value of the following expression? \[ \begin{aligned} &2\left(1-\dfrac{1}{2}\right)+3\left(1-\dfrac{1}{3}\right) \\ &\quad {}+4\left(1-\dfrac{1}{4}\right)+\cdots \\ &\quad {}+10\left(1-\dfrac{1}{10}\right) \end{aligned} \]

令 \(n\) 依次取从 \(2\) 到 \(10\) 的整数，

\[n\left(1-\frac{1}{n}\right)=n-1\text{。}\]

因此原式为 \(1+2+\cdots+9=45\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

For each integer \(n\) from \(2\) through \(10,\)

\[n\left(1-\frac{1}{n}\right)=n-1.\]

The expression is therefore \(1+2+\cdots+9=45.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A .

在 Annville Junior High School，数学俱乐部中 \(30\%\) 的学生也参加科学俱乐部，科学俱乐部中 \(80\%\) 的学生也参加数学俱乐部。科学俱乐部有 \(15\) 名学生。数学俱乐部有多少名学生？

English（选项同上方）

At Annville Junior High School, \(30\%\) of the students in the Math Club are in the Science Club, and \(80\%\) of the students in the Science Club are in the Math Club. There are \(15\) students in the Science Club. How many students are in the Math Club?

由于 \(80\%\) 的 \(15\) 名科学俱乐部学生也参加数学俱乐部，所以两个俱乐部共有的学生数是 \(0.8\cdot 15=12\)。这相当于数学俱乐部人数的 \(30\%\)，因此数学俱乐部有 \(\dfrac{12}{0.3}=40\) 名学生。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Since \(80\%\) of the \(15\) Science Club students are also in the Math Club, the two clubs overlap in \(0.8\cdot 15=12\) students. This is \(30\%\) of the Math Club, so the Math Club has \(\dfrac{12}{0.3}=40\) students.

Thus, the correct answer is E .

English（选项同上方）

Tamika selects two different numbers at random from the set \(\{8,9,10\}\) and adds them. Carlos takes two different numbers at random from the set \(\{3,5,6\}\) and multiplies them. What is the probability that Tamika’s result is greater than Carlos’ result?

Tamika 可能得到 \(17\)、\(18\) 或 \(19\)，Carlos 可能得到 \(15\)、\(18\) 或 \(30\)。

从两人各选一个结果，形成九个等可能配对。Tamika 的结果较大的情况是 \((17,15)\)、\((18,15)\)、\((19,15)\) 和 \((19,18)\)，所以这 \(9\) 个配对中有 \(4\) 个符合条件。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Tamika can get \(17,\) \(18,\) or \(19,\) and Carlos can get \(15,\) \(18,\) or \(30.\)

The nine equally likely pairs are formed by choosing one result from each person. Tamika’s result is greater in \((17,15),\) \((18,15),\) \((19,15),\) and \((19,18),\) so \(4\) of the \(9\) pairs work.

Thus, the correct answer is A .

Terri 按三条规则生成一个正整数序列。她从一个正整数开始，然后对结果应用合适的规则，并持续这样做。

规则 \(1\)：如果整数小于 \(10\)，则乘以 \(9\)。

规则 \(2\)：如果整数是偶数且大于 \(9\)，则除以 \(2\)。

规则 \(3\)：如果整数是奇数且大于 \(9\)，则减去 \(5\)。

例如，样例序列为 \(23\)，\(18\)，\(9\)，\(81\)，\(76\)，\(\ldots\)。

求以 \(98\)、\(49\)、\(\ldots\) 开头的这个序列的第 \(98\) 项。

English（选项同上方）

Terri produces a sequence of positive integers by following three rules. She starts with a positive integer, then applies the appropriate rule to the result, and continues in this fashion.

Rule \(1:\) If the integer is less than \(10,\) multiply it by \(9.\)

Rule \(2:\) If the integer is even and greater than \(9,\) divide it by \(2.\)

Rule \(3:\) If the integer is odd and greater than \(9,\) subtract \(5\) from it.

For example, consider the sample sequence \(23,\) \(18,\) \(9,\) \(81,\) \(76,\) \(\ldots.\)

Find the \(98\)th term of the sequence that begins \(98,\) \(49,\) \(\ldots.\)

序列开始为

\[ \begin{gathered} 98,49,44,22,11, \\ 6,54,27,22,\ldots \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

前三项之后，循环 \((22,11,6,54,27)\) 重复。因为 \(98-3=95\) 是 \(5\) 的倍数，第 \(98\) 项是循环中的第五项，即 \(27\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The sequence begins

\[ \begin{gathered} 98,49,44,22,11, \\ 6,54,27,22,\ldots. \end{gathered} \]

After the first three terms, the cycle \((22,11,6,54,27)\) repeats. Since \(98-3=95\) is a multiple of \(5,\) the \(98\)th term is the fifth term of the cycle, \(27.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D .

If the pattern in the diagram continues, what fraction of the interior would be shaded in the eighth triangle?

第 \(n\) 个三角形有 \(n^2\) 个小三角形。

涂色小三角形数为 \(1+2+\cdots+(n-1)\) \(=\dfrac{n(n-1)}{2}\)。

当 \(n=8\) 时，涂色比例为 \(\dfrac{\frac{8\cdot 7}{2}}{8^2}=\dfrac{7}{16}\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The \(n\)th triangle has \(n^2\) small triangles.

The number of shaded small triangles is \(1+2+\cdots+(n-1)\) \(=\dfrac{n(n-1)}{2}.\)

For \(n=8,\) the shaded fraction is \(\dfrac{\frac{8\cdot 7}{2}}{8^2}=\dfrac{7}{16}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C .

一个长方形板有 \(8\) 列小方格，从左上角开始编号，第一行从左到右编号为 \(1\) 到 \(8\)，第二行为 \(9\) 到 \(16\)，依此类推。一个学生涂色方格 \(1\)，然后跳过一个方格涂色方格 \(3\)，跳过两个方格涂色方格 \(6\)，跳过 \(3\) 个方格涂色方格 \(10\)，并继续这样做，直到每一列至少有一个涂色方格。

第一次达到这个结果时，被涂色方格的编号是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A rectangular board of \(8\) columns has squares numbered beginning in the upper left corner and moving left to right so row one is numbered \(1\) through \(8,\) row two is \(9\) through \(16,\) and so on. A student shades square \(1,\) then skips one square and shades square \(3,\) skips two squares and shades square \(6,\) skips \(3\) squares and shades square \(10,\) and continues in this way until there is at least one shaded square in each column.

What is the number of the shaded square that first achieves this result?

被涂色的方格编号是三角形数 \(1\)、\(3\)、\(6\)、\(10\)、\(15\)、\(\ldots\)。列对应模 \(8\) 的余数，其中余数 \(0\) 表示第八列。

到 \(105\) 为止的三角形数模八的余数为 \(1\)、\(3\)、\(6\)、\(2\)、\(7\)、\(5\)、\(4\)、\(4\)、\(5\)、\(7\)、\(2\)、\(6\)、\(3\) 和 \(1\)。因此还没有出现第八列的余数。

下一个三角形数是 \(120\)，且 \(120\equiv 0\pmod{8}\)。这是第一次每一列都有涂色方格。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The shaded squares are the triangular numbers \(1,\) \(3,\) \(6,\) \(10,\) \(15,\) \(\ldots.\) Columns correspond to residues modulo \(8,\) with residue \(0\) representing the eighth column.

The triangular numbers through \(105\) have residues \(1,\) \(3,\) \(6,\) \(2,\) \(7,\) \(5,\) \(4,\) \(4,\) \(5,\) \(7,\) \(2,\) \(6,\) \(3,\) and \(1.\) Thus, the eighth-column residue has not appeared yet.

The next triangular number is \(120,\) and \(120\equiv 0\pmod{8}.\) This is the first time every column has a shaded square.

Thus, the correct answer is E .

三位慷慨的朋友各有一些现金，并按如下方式重新分配：Ami 给 Jan 和 Toy 足够的钱，使他们各自的钱数加倍。然后 Jan 给 Ami 和 Toy 足够的钱，使他们各自的钱数加倍。最后 Toy 给 Ami 和 Jan 足够的钱，使他们各自的钱数加倍。如果 Toy 开始时有 \(\$36\)，结束时也有 \(\$36\)，那么三位朋友一共有多少钱？

English（选项同上方）

Three generous friends, each with some cash, redistribute their money as follows: Ami gives enough money to Jan and Toy to double the amount that each has. Jan then gives enough to Ami and Toy to double their amounts. Finally, Toy gives Ami and Jan enough to double their amounts. If Toy has \(\$36\) when they begin and \(\$36\) when they end, what is the total amount that all three friends have?

Toy 开始有 \(\$36\)。Ami 使 Toy 的钱加倍后，Toy 有 \(\$72\)。Jan 再使 Toy 的钱加倍后，Toy 有 \(\$144\)。

Toy 最后结束时有 \(\$36\)，所以 Toy 给出了 \(\$144-\$36=\$108\)。这笔钱使 Ami 和 Jan 的总钱数加倍，因此 Toy 最后行动前 Ami 和 Jan 合计有 \(\$108\)。

总钱数不变，所以总数是 \(\$144+\$108=\$252\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Toy begins with \(\$36.\) After Ami doubles Toy’s amount, Toy has \(\$72.\) After Jan doubles Toy’s amount, Toy has \(\$144.\)

On Toy’s final turn, Toy ends with \(\$36,\) so Toy gives away \(\$144-\$36=\$108.\) That gift doubles the combined amount of Ami and Jan, so Ami and Jan together had \(\$108\) just before Toy’s final turn.

The total amount of money is constant, so the total is \(\$144+\$108=\$252.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D .
