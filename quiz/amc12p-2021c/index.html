下式的值是多少？\[ \frac{(2112 - 2021)^2}{169}\text{？}\]

English（选项同上方）

What is the value of \[ \frac{(2112 - 2021)^2}{169}? \]

因为 \(2112 - 2021 = 91 = 7 \cdot 13\)，且 \(169 = 13^2\)，所以这个分数为 \(\dfrac{(7 \cdot 13)^2}{13^2} = 7^2 = 49\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Since \(2112 - 2021 = 91 = 7 \cdot 13\) and \(169 = 13^2,\) the fraction is \(\dfrac{(7 \cdot 13)^2}{13^2} = 7^2 = 49.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

2单选

Menkara 有一张 \(4 \times 6\) 的索引卡片。如果她把这张卡片的一边长度缩短 \(1\) 英寸，卡片的面积会变成 \(18\) 平方英寸。如果她改为把另一边的长度缩短 \(1\) 英寸，卡片的面积会是多少平方英寸？

English（选项同上方）

Menkara has a \(4 \times 6\) index card. If she shortens the length of one side of this card by \(1\) inch, the card would have area \(18\) square inches. What would the area of the card be in square inches if instead she shortens the length of the other side by \(1\) inch?

原卡片是 \(4 \times 6\)。把一边缩短 \(1\) 英寸后面积为 \(18\)，必须是 \(3 \times 6 = 18\)，所以被缩短的是 \(4\) 英寸的边。

若改为把另一边缩短 \(1\) 英寸，则面积为 \(4 \times 5 = 20\) 平方英寸。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The original card is \(4 \times 6.\) Shortening a side by \(1\) inch gives area \(18,\) which requires \(3 \times 6 = 18,\) so the reduced side was the \(4\)-inch side.

Shortening the other side by \(1\) inch instead gives \(4 \times 5 = 20\) square inches.

Thus, the correct answer is E.

3单选

Lopez 先生上班有两条路线可选。路线 A 长 \(6\) 英里，他在这条路线上的平均速度为每小时 \(30\) 英里。路线 B 长 \(5\) 英里，他在这条路线上的平均速度为每小时 \(40\) 英里，但其中有一段 \(\tfrac{1}{2}\) 英里的学校区域，平均速度为每小时 \(20\) 英里。路线 B 比路线 A 快多少分钟？

English（选项同上方）

Mr. Lopez has a choice of two routes to get to work. Route A is \(6\) miles long, and his average speed along this route is \(30\) miles per hour. Route B is \(5\) miles long, and his average speed along this route is \(40\) miles per hour, except for a \(\tfrac{1}{2}\)-mile stretch in a school zone where his average speed is \(20\) miles per hour. By how many minutes is Route B quicker than Route A?

路线 A 用时 \(\dfrac{6}{30} = \dfrac{1}{5}\) 小时 \(= 12\) 分钟。

路线 B 中 \(4.5\) 英里按每小时 \(40\) 英里行驶，\(0.5\) 英里按每小时 \(20\) 英里行驶，用时 \(\dfrac{4.5}{40} + \dfrac{0.5}{20}\) \(= 0.1125 + 0.025\) \(= 0.1375\) 小时 \(= 8.25\) 分钟。

差值为 \(12 - 8.25 = 3.75 = 3\tfrac{3}{4}\) 分钟。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Route A takes \(\dfrac{6}{30} = \dfrac{1}{5}\) hour \(= 12\) minutes.

Route B has \(4.5\) miles at \(40\) mph and \(0.5\) mile at \(20\) mph, taking \(\dfrac{4.5}{40} + \dfrac{0.5}{20}\) \(= 0.1125 + 0.025\) \(= 0.1375\) hour \(= 8.25\) minutes.

The difference is \(12 - 8.25 = 3.75 = 3\tfrac{3}{4}\) minutes.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

4单选

六位数 \(\underline{2}\,\underline{0}\,\underline{2}\,\underline{1}\,\underline{0}\,\underline{A}\) 只有在某一个数字 \(A\) 下是质数。\(A\) 是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The six-digit number \(\underline{2}\,\underline{0}\,\underline{2}\,\underline{1}\,\underline{0}\,\underline{A}\) is prime for only one digit \(A.\) What is \(A?\)

这个数是 \(202100 + A\)。任何偶数 \(A\) 都会使它为偶数，而 \(A = 5\) 时它能被 \(5\) 整除，所以 \(A\) 必须是奇数且不是 \(5\)。

\(A = 1\) 时数字和为 \(6\)（能被 \(3\) 整除）；\(A = 7\) 时数字和为 \(12\) （能被 \(3\) 整除）；并且 \(202103 = 11 \cdot 18373\)。只有 \(202109\) 通过这些检验，而它是质数。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The number is \(202100 + A.\) Any even \(A\) makes it even, and \(A = 5\) makes it divisible by \(5,\) so \(A\) must be odd and not \(5.\)

For \(A = 1\) the digit sum is \(6\) (divisible by \(3\)); for \(A = 7\) the digit sum is \(12\) (divisible by \(3\)); and \(202103 = 11 \cdot 18373.\) Only \(202109\) survives all tests, and it is prime.

Thus, the correct answer is E.

鸸鹋 Elmer 在乡村道路上相邻两根电线杆之间行走需要 \(44\) 个等长步幅。鸵鸟 Oscar 用 \(12\) 个等长跃步可以走完同一距离。电线杆等距排列，这条路上的第 \(41\) 根电线杆距离第一根电线杆正好一英里（\(5280\) 英尺）。Oscar 的一个跃步比 Elmer 的一个步幅长多少英尺？

English（选项同上方）

Elmer the emu takes \(44\) equal strides to walk between consecutive telephone poles on a rural road. Oscar the ostrich can cover the same distance in \(12\) equal leaps. The telephone poles are evenly spaced, and the \(41\)st pole along this road is exactly one mile (\(5280\) feet) from the first pole. How much longer, in feet, is Oscar’s leap than Elmer’s stride?

第一根和第 \(41\) 根电线杆之间有 \(40\) 个间隔，所以每个间隔长 \(\dfrac{5280}{40} = 132\) 英尺。

Elmer 的步幅为 \(\dfrac{132}{44} = 3\) 英尺，Oscar 的跃步为 \(\dfrac{132}{12} = 11\) 英尺，差为 \(11 - 3 = 8\) 英尺。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

There are \(40\) gaps between the first and \(41\)st poles, so each gap is \(\dfrac{5280}{40} = 132\) feet.

Elmer’s stride is \(\dfrac{132}{44} = 3\) feet and Oscar’s leap is \(\dfrac{132}{12} = 11\) feet, a difference of \(11 - 3 = 8\) feet.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

如下图所示，点 \(E\) 位于直线 \(CD\) 所确定的、与点 \(A\) 相反的半平面内，且 \(\angle CDE = 110^\circ\)。点 \(F\) 在 \(\overline{AD}\) 上，使得 \(DE = DF\)，并且 \(ABCD\) 是正方形。\(\angle AFE\) 的度数是多少？

English（选项同上方）

As shown in the figure below, point \(E\) lies on the opposite half-plane determined by line \(CD\) from point \(A\) so that \(\angle CDE = 110^\circ.\) Point \(F\) lies on \(\overline{AD}\) so that \(DE = DF,\) and \(ABCD\) is a square. What is the degree measure of \(\angle AFE?\)

因为 \(ABCD\) 是正方形，\(\angle ADC = 90^\circ\)。又因为 \(E\) 与 \(A\) 在直线 \(CD\) 的两侧，射线 \(DE\) 越过了 \(DC\)，所以三角形 \(DFE\) 在 \(D\) 处的角 （其中 \(F\) 在 \(\overline{AD}\) 上）为 \(\angle FDE = 360^\circ\) \(- (\angle ADC + \angle CDE)\) \(= 360^\circ - (90^\circ + 110^\circ)\) \(= 160^\circ\)。

因为 \(DF = DE\)，三角形 \(DFE\) 是等腰三角形，其底角 \(\angle DFE = \tfrac{180^\circ - 160^\circ}{2} = 10^\circ\)。

因为 \(A\)、\(F\)、\(D\) 共线，所以 \(\angle AFE = 180^\circ - \angle DFE = 170^\circ\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Because \(ABCD\) is a square, \(\angle ADC = 90^\circ.\) Since \(E\) and \(A\) lie on opposite sides of line \(CD,\) ray \(DE\) is swung past \(DC,\) so the angle of triangle \(DFE\) at \(D\) (with \(F\) on \(\overline{AD}\)) is \(\angle FDE = 360^\circ\) \(- (\angle ADC + \angle CDE)\) \(= 360^\circ - (90^\circ + 110^\circ)\) \(= 160^\circ.\)

Since \(DF = DE,\) triangle \(DFE\) is isosceles with base angles \(\angle DFE = \tfrac{180^\circ - 160^\circ}{2} = 10^\circ.\)

As \(A,\) \(F,\) \(D\) are collinear, \(\angle AFE = 180^\circ - \angle DFE = 170^\circ.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

7单选

一所学校有 \(100\) 名学生和 \(5\) 名老师。第一节课中，每名学生上一门课，每名老师教一门课。五门课的人数分别为 \(50\)，\(20\)，\(20\)，\(5\)，和 \(5\)。随机选一名老师，记录其班级的学生人数，所得数值的平均值记为 \(t\)。随机选一名学生，记录其所在班级的学生人数（包括这名学生本人），所得数值的平均值记为 \(s\)。\(t - s\) 是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A school has \(100\) students and \(5\) teachers. In the first period, each student is taking one class, and each teacher is teaching one class. The enrollments in the classes are \(50,\) \(20,\) \(20,\) \(5,\) and \(5.\) Let \(t\) be the average value obtained if a teacher is picked at random and the number of students in their class is noted. Let \(s\) be the average value obtained if a student was picked at random and the number of students in their class, including the student, is noted. What is \(t - s?\)

老师平均值为 \(t = \dfrac{50 + 20 + 20 + 5 + 5}{5}\) \(= \dfrac{100}{5} = 20\)。

学生平均值按每个班级中的学生人数给班级人数加权：\[ \begin{aligned} s &= \small \frac{50^2 + 20^2 + 20^2 + 5^2 + 5^2}{100} \\ &= \small \frac{2500 + 400 + 400 + 25 + 25}{100} \\ &= 33.5 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以 \(t - s = 20 - 33.5 = -13.5\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The teacher average is \(t = \dfrac{50 + 20 + 20 + 5 + 5}{5}\) \(= \dfrac{100}{5} = 20.\)

The student average weights each class size by how many students are in it: \[ \begin{aligned} s &= \small \frac{50^2 + 20^2 + 20^2 + 5^2 + 5^2}{100} \\ &= \small \frac{2500 + 400 + 400 + 25 + 25}{100} \\ &= 33.5. \end{aligned} \]

So \(t - s = 20 - 33.5 = -13.5.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

8单选

令 \(M\) 为从 \(10\) 到 \(30\)（含端点）所有整数的最小公倍数。令 \(N\) 为 \(M\)，\(32\)，\(33\)，\(34\)，\(35\)，\(36\)，\(37\)，\(38\)，\(39\) 和 \(40\) 的最小公倍数。\(\dfrac{N}{M}\) 的值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Let \(M\) be the least common multiple of all the integers \(10\) through \(30,\) inclusive. Let \(N\) be the least common multiple of \(M,\) \(32,\) \(33,\) \(34,\) \(35,\) \(36,\) \(37,\) \(38,\) \(39,\) and \(40.\) What is the value of \(\dfrac{N}{M}?\)

\(M = \operatorname{lcm}(10, \ldots, 30)\) 含有 \(2^4\)（来自 \(16\)），\(3^3\)（来自 \(27\)），\(5^2\)（来自 \(25\)），\(7\)，以及直到 \(29\) 的每个质数。

在 \(32, \ldots, 40\) 中，唯一的新贡献是 \(32 = 2^5\)，它把 \(2\) 的幂次从 \(2^4\) 提高到 \(2^5\)，以及新的质数 \(37\)。其他数都只分解成 \(M\) 中已经有的质数和幂次。

因此 \(\dfrac{N}{M} = 2 \cdot 37 = 74\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

\(M = \operatorname{lcm}(10, \ldots, 30)\) contains \(2^4\) (from \(16\)), \(3^3\) (from \(27\)), \(5^2\) (from \(25\)), \(7,\) and every prime up to \(29.\)

Among \(32, \ldots, 40,\) the only new contributions are \(32 = 2^5,\) which raises the power of \(2\) from \(2^4\) to \(2^5,\) and the new prime \(37.\) Everything else factors into primes and powers already in \(M.\)

Therefore \(\dfrac{N}{M} = 2 \cdot 37 = 74.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

9单选

一个长方体的表面积和体积在数值上相等，它的边长为 \(\log_2 x\)，\(\log_3 x\)，和 \(\log_4 x\)。\(x\) 是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A right rectangular prism whose surface area and volume are numerically equal has edge lengths \(\log_2 x,\) \(\log_3 x,\) and \(\log_4 x.\) What is \(x?\)

令 \(a = \log_2 x\)，\(b = \log_3 x\)，\(c = \log_4 x\)。表面积等于体积给出 \(2(ab + bc + ca) = abc\)。两边除以 \(abc\)，得 \[ 1 = 2\left(\frac{1}{c} + \frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b}\right)\text{。}\]

因为 \(\dfrac{1}{a} = \log_x 2\)，等等，所以和为 \(\log_x 2 + \log_x 3 + \log_x 4 = \log_x 24\)。于是 \(1 = 2\log_x 24\)，所以 \(\log_x 24 = \tfrac{1}{2}\)，即 \(x^{\frac{1}{2}} = 24\)，从而 \(x = 576\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Let \(a = \log_2 x,\) \(b = \log_3 x,\) \(c = \log_4 x.\) Surface area equals volume gives \(2(ab + bc + ca) = abc.\) Dividing by \(abc,\) \[ 1 = 2\left(\frac{1}{c} + \frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b}\right). \]

Since \(\dfrac{1}{a} = \log_x 2,\) etc., the sum is \(\log_x 2 + \log_x 3 + \log_x 4 = \log_x 24.\) Thus \(1 = 2\log_x 24,\) so \(\log_x 24 = \tfrac{1}{2},\) meaning \(x^{\frac{1}{2}} = 24\) and \(x = 576.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

10单选

数 \(N\) 以九为底的表示为 \(27{,}006{,}000{,}052_9\)。\(N\) 除以 \(5\) 的余数是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The base-nine representation of the number \(N\) is \(27{,}006{,}000{,}052_9.\) What is the remainder when \(N\) is divided by \(5?\)

因为 \(9 \equiv -1 \pmod 5\)，所以每个幂 \(9^k \equiv (-1)^k\)，因此 \(N\) 同余于其以九为底的数字的交错和。

非零数字及其从右往左的位置为：\(2\)（位置 \(0\)），\(5\)（位置 \(1\)），\(6\)（位置 \(6\)），\(7\)（位置 \(9\)），以及 \(2\)（位置 \(10\)）。交错和为 \(2 - 5 + 6 - 7 + 2 = -2\) \(\equiv 3 \pmod 5\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Since \(9 \equiv -1 \pmod 5,\) each power \(9^k \equiv (-1)^k,\) so \(N\) is congruent to the alternating sum of its base-nine digits.

The nonzero digits, with their positions from the right, are \(2\) (position \(0\)), \(5\) (position \(1\)), \(6\) (position \(6\)), \(7\) (position \(9\)), and \(2\) (position \(10\)). The alternating sum is \(2 - 5 + 6 - 7 + 2 = -2\) \(\equiv 3 \pmod 5.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

11单选

考虑两个同心圆，半径分别为 \(17\) 和 \(19\)。较大的圆有一条弦，其中一半位于较小圆内。这条较大圆中的弦长是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Consider two concentric circles of radius \(17\) and \(19.\) The larger circle has a chord, half of which lies inside the smaller circle. What is the length of the chord in the larger circle?

设这条弦到共同圆心的距离为 \(d\)。它的总长度为 \(2\sqrt{361 - d^2}\)，位于较小圆内的部分长度为 \(2\sqrt{289 - d^2}\)。

因为弦的一半位于小圆内，\(2\sqrt{289 - d^2} = \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot 2\sqrt{361 - d^2}\)。平方得 \(4(289 - d^2) = 361 - d^2\)，所以 \(3d^2 = 795\)，\(d^2 = 265\)。

弦长为 \(2\sqrt{361 - 265} = 2\sqrt{96} = 8\sqrt{6}\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Let the chord lie at distance \(d\) from the common center. Its total length is \(2\sqrt{361 - d^2},\) and the portion inside the smaller circle has length \(2\sqrt{289 - d^2}.\)

Since half the chord lies inside, \(2\sqrt{289 - d^2} = \tfrac{1}{2}\cdot 2\sqrt{361 - d^2}.\) Squaring gives \(4(289 - d^2) = 361 - d^2,\) so \(3d^2 = 795\) and \(d^2 = 265.\)

The chord length is \(2\sqrt{361 - 265} = 2\sqrt{96} = 8\sqrt{6}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

12单选

下式的展开式共有 \(1001\) 项，其中系数为有理数的项有多少个？\[ \left(x\sqrt[3]{2} + y\sqrt{3}\right)^{1000}\text{？}\]

English（选项同上方）

What is the number of terms with rational coefficients among the \(1001\) terms in the expansion of \[ \left(x\sqrt[3]{2} + y\sqrt{3}\right)^{1000}? \]

通项为 \(\binom{1000}{k}(x\sqrt[3]{2})^{1000-k}(y\sqrt{3})^{k}\)，其系数含有 \(2^{\frac{1000-k}{3}}\) 和 \(3^{\frac{k}{2}}\)。它有理当且仅当 \((1000 - k)\) 是 \(3\) 的倍数且 \(k\) 为偶数。

因为 \(1000 \equiv 1 \pmod 3\)，所以需要 \(k \equiv 1 \pmod 3\) 且 \(k\) 为偶数，合并得 \(k \equiv 4 \pmod 6\)。有效值为 \(k = 4, 10, \ldots, 1000\)，共有 \(\dfrac{1000 - 4}{6} + 1 = 167\) 个。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The general term is \(\binom{1000}{k}(x\sqrt[3]{2})^{1000-k}(y\sqrt{3})^{k},\) whose coefficient contains \(2^{\frac{1000-k}{3}}\) and \(3^{\frac{k}{2}}.\) This is rational exactly when \((1000 - k)\) is a multiple of \(3\) and \(k\) is even.

Since \(1000 \equiv 1 \pmod 3,\) we need \(k \equiv 1 \pmod 3\) and \(k\) even, which combine to \(k \equiv 4 \pmod 6.\) The valid values \(k = 4, 10, \ldots, 1000\) number \(\dfrac{1000 - 4}{6} + 1 = 167.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

13单选

直线 \(y = x\) 与 \(y = 3x\) 的图像在原点形成的锐角的角平分线方程为 \(y = kx\)。\(k\) 是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The angle bisector of the acute angle formed at the origin by the graphs of the lines \(y = x\) and \(y = 3x\) has equation \(y = kx.\) What is \(k?\)

角平分线沿两条直线的单位方向向量之和：\(\dfrac{(1,1)}{\sqrt2} + \dfrac{(1,3)}{\sqrt{10}}\)。它的斜率为 \[ k = \frac{\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2} + \tfrac{3}{\sqrt{10}}}{\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2} + \tfrac{1}{\sqrt{10}}} = \frac{\sqrt5 + 3}{\sqrt5 + 1}\text{。}\]

分子分母同乘 \(\sqrt5 - 1\)，得 \(\dfrac{(\sqrt5 + 3)(\sqrt5 - 1)}{4}\) \(= \dfrac{2 + 2\sqrt5}{4}\) \(= \dfrac{1 + \sqrt5}{2}\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The bisector points along the sum of the unit vectors of the two lines: \(\dfrac{(1,1)}{\sqrt2} + \dfrac{(1,3)}{\sqrt{10}}.\) Its slope is \[ k = \frac{\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2} + \tfrac{3}{\sqrt{10}}}{\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2} + \tfrac{1}{\sqrt{10}}} = \frac{\sqrt5 + 3}{\sqrt5 + 1}. \]

Multiplying numerator and denominator by \(\sqrt5 - 1\) gives \(\dfrac{(\sqrt5 + 3)(\sqrt5 - 1)}{4}\) \(= \dfrac{2 + 2\sqrt5}{4}\) \(= \dfrac{1 + \sqrt5}{2}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

14单选

如图，等边六边形 \(ABCDEF\) 有三个互不相邻的锐内角，每个都是 \(30^\circ\)。该六边形围成的面积为 \(6\sqrt{3}\)。这个六边形的周长是多少？

English（选项同上方）

In the figure, equilateral hexagon \(ABCDEF\) has three nonadjacent acute interior angles that each measure \(30^\circ.\) The enclosed area of the hexagon is \(6\sqrt{3}.\) What is the perimeter of the hexagon?

设公共边长为 \(s\)。三个锐角顶点是三个等腰三角形的尖端，这些三角形的两边为 \(s\)，顶角为 \(30^\circ\)；每个面积为 \(\tfrac12 s^2 \sin 30^\circ = \tfrac{s^2}{4}\)。

三个凹角顶点形成一个内侧等边三角形，边长为 \(2s\sin 15^\circ\)，面积为 \(\sqrt3\,s^2\sin^2 15^\circ\)。利用 \(\sin^2 15^\circ = \tfrac{2 - \sqrt3}{4}\)，总面积为 \[ \frac{3s^2}{4} + \sqrt3\,s^2\cdot\frac{2 - \sqrt3}{4} = \frac{s^2\sqrt3}{2}\text{。}\]

令 \(\tfrac{s^2\sqrt3}{2} = 6\sqrt3\)，得 \(s^2 = 12\)，所以 \(s = 2\sqrt3\)，周长为 \(6s = 12\sqrt3\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

令 \(m \ge 5\) 为奇整数，并令 \(D(m)\) 表示满足下列条件的四元组 \((a_1, a_2, a_3, a_4)\) 的个数：四个分量是互不相同的整数，且对所有 \(i\) 都有 \(1 \le a_i \le m\)，并且 \(m\) 整除 \(a_1 + a_2 + a_3 + a_4\)。存在一个多项式 \[ q(x) = c_3x^3 + c_2x^2 + c_1x + c_0 \] 使得对所有奇整数 \(m \ge 5\) 都有 \(D(m) = q(m)\)。\(c_1\) 是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Let \(m \ge 5\) be an odd integer, and let \(D(m)\) denote the number of quadruples \((a_1, a_2, a_3, a_4)\) of distinct integers with \(1 \le a_i \le m\) for all \(i\) such that \(m\) divides \(a_1 + a_2 + a_3 + a_4.\) There is a polynomial \[ q(x) = c_3x^3 + c_2x^2 + c_1x + c_0 \] such that \(D(m) = q(m)\) for all odd integers \(m \ge 5.\) What is \(c_1?\)

把 \(1,2,\ldots,m\) 看作模 \(m\) 的全部剩余类。若不要求各分量互不相同，则前三个分量可以任取，第四个分量随之唯一确定，于是共有 \(m^3\) 个有序四元组。

按哪些坐标相等来划分并做容斥，即可得到其余各项。恰有一对坐标相等的选法有 \(6\) 种，每种留下 \(m^2\) 组解，共贡献 \(-6m^2\)。\(3\) 种“两对相等”的划分贡献 \(+3m\)。\(4\) 种“三个相等加一个单独”的划分的容斥系数为 \(2\)，贡献 \(+8m\)。最后，四个全相等的划分系数为 \(-6\)，且只有一组解。（这里 \(m\) 为奇数保证了 \(2\) 和 \(4\) 模 \(m\) 可逆。）因此\[ \begin{aligned} D(m)&=m^3-6m^2 \\ &\quad {}+11m-6 \\ &=(m-1)(m-2) \\ &\quad {}\cdot(m-3) \end{aligned}\text{。}\] 于是 \(c_1=11\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Regard \(1,2,\ldots,m\) as all residues modulo \(m.\) Without the distinctness condition, the first three entries are arbitrary and the fourth is determined, giving \(m^3\) ordered quadruples.

Inclusion-exclusion over equal-coordinate partitions gives the remaining terms. There are \(6\) choices of one equal pair, each leaving \(m^2\) solutions, for \(-6m^2.\) The \(3\) two-pair partitions contribute \(+3m.\) The \(4\) triple-and-single partitions have inclusion-exclusion coefficient \(2\) and contribute \(+8m.\) Finally, the all-equal partition has coefficient \(-6\) and one solution. (Here odd \(m\) makes \(2\) and \(4\) invertible modulo \(m.\)) Therefore \[ \begin{aligned} D(m)&=m^3-6m^2 \\ &\quad {}+11m-6 \\ &=(m-1)(m-2) \\ &\quad {}\cdot(m-3). \end{aligned} \] Hence \(c_1=11.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.
