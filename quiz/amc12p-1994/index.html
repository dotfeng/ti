English（选项同上方）

For how many \(n\) in \(\{1,2,3,\ldots,100\}\) is the tens digit of \(n^2\) odd?

写成 \(n=10a+b\)。模 \(100\) 意义下，\[ n^2\equiv20ab+b^2\text{。}\]项 \(20ab\) 使十位数字改变一个偶数，所以只需考虑 \(b^2\) 的十位数字。在 \(b=0,1,\ldots,9\) 中，只有 \(b=4\) 和 \(b=6\) 时该数字为奇数。从 \(1\) 到 \(100\) 中，每种个位数字都出现 \(10\) 次，所以共有 \(2\cdot10=20\) 个。因此正确答案是 B。

English

Write \(n=10a+b.\) Modulo \(100,\) \[ n^2\equiv20ab+b^2. \] The term \(20ab\) changes the tens digit by an even amount, so only the tens digit of \(b^2\) matters. English（选项同上方）

Nine chairs in a row are to be occupied by six students and Professors Alpha, Beta and Gamma. In how many ways can Professors Alpha, Beta and Gamma choose their chairs?

English

The professors must occupy three nonconsecutive positions among chairs \(2,3,\ldots,8.\) The number of such \(3\)-subsets of \(7\) consecutive positions is \[ \binom{7-3+1}{3}=\binom53=10. \] The three distinct professors can be assigned to the selected chairs in \(3!=6\) ways, for \(10\cdot6=60.\) Thus the correct answer is C.
