一个正方形和一个圆的周长相等。圆的面积与正方形面积之比是：

English（选项同上方）

A square and a circle have equal perimeters. The ratio of the area of the circle to the area of the square is:

若共同的周长为 \(p\)，则圆的半径为 \(\frac{p}{2\pi}\)，正方形的边长为 \(\frac{p}{4}\)。因此 \[ \frac{\pi\left(\frac{p}{2\pi}\right)^2}{\left(\frac p4\right)^2} =\frac{\frac{p^2}{4\pi}}{\frac{p^2}{16}}=\frac4\pi\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

If the common perimeter is \(p,\) then the circle has radius \(\frac{p}{2\pi}\) and the square has side \(\frac{p}{4}.\) Therefore \[ \frac{\pi\left(\frac{p}{2\pi}\right)^2}{\left(\frac p4\right)^2} =\frac{\frac{p^2}{4\pi}}{\frac{p^2}{16}}=\frac4\pi. \]

Therefore, the correct answer is A.

一个半径为 \(r\) 的圆与矩形 \(ABCD\) 的边 \(AB\)、\(AD\) 和 \(CD\) 相切，并经过对角线 \(AC\) 的中点。用 \(r\) 表示矩形的面积为：

English（选项同上方）

A circle with radius \(r\) is tangent to sides \(AB,\) \(AD,\) and \(CD\) of rectangle \(ABCD\) and passes through the midpoint of diagonal \(AC.\) The area of the rectangle, in terms of \(r,\) is:

圆与平行边 \(AB\) 和 \(CD\) 相切。两边之间的距离就是圆的直径，所以 \(AD=BC=2r\)。设 \(R\) 为 \(AD\) 上的切点，\(Q\) 为圆心，\(M\) 为 \(AC\) 的中点。

直线 \(RQ\) 平行于 \(AB\)，并且位于 \(AB\) 与 \(CD\) 的正中间，所以 \(R\) 是 \(AD\) 的中点，且该直线经过 \(M\)。由于 \(M\) 在圆上，且 \(R,Q,M\) 共线，所以 \(RM=2r\)。因此 \(RM\) 是三角形 \(ADC\) 的中位线，从而 \(DC=2RM=4r\)。所以面积为 \[ (2r)(4r)=8r^2\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Let the circle be tangent to the parallel sides \(AB\) and \(CD.\) Their separation is its diameter, so \(AD=BC=2r.\) Let \(R\) be the tangency point on \(AD,\) let \(Q\) be the center, and let \(M\) be the midpoint of \(AC.\)

The line \(RQ\) is parallel to \(AB\) and halfway between \(AB\) and \(CD,\) so \(R\) is the midpoint of \(AD\) and the line contains \(M.\) Since \(M\) lies on the circle and \(R,Q,M\) are collinear, \(RM=2r.\) Thus \(RM\) is a midsegment of triangle \(ADC,\) so \(DC=2RM=4r.\) Therefore the area is \[ (2r)(4r)=8r^2. \]

Therefore, the correct answer is C.

一个公比为 \(r\)、满足 \(|r|\lt1\) 的无穷等比级数的和为 \(15\)，而该级数各项平方的和为 \(45\)。该级数的首项是：

English（选项同上方）

The sum of an infinite geometric series with common ratio \(r\) such that \(|r|\lt1\) is \(15,\) and the sum of the squares of the terms of this series is \(45.\) The first term of the series is:

设首项为 \(a\)。则 \[ \frac{a}{1-r}=15,\qquad \frac{a^2}{1-r^2}=45\text{。}\]因为 \(1-r^2=(1-r)(1+r)\)，用第二个方程除以第一个方程，得到 \(\frac{a}{1+r}=3\)。因此 \[ a=15(1-r)=3(1+r)\text{。}\]解得 \(r=\frac{2}{3}\)，且 \(a=5\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Let the first term be \(a.\) Then \[ \frac{a}{1-r}=15,\qquad \frac{a^2}{1-r^2}=45. \] Since \(1-r^2=(1-r)(1+r),\) dividing the second equation by the first gives \(\frac{a}{1+r}=3.\) Thus \[ a=15(1-r)=3(1+r). \] Solving gives \(r=\frac{2}{3}\) and \(a=5.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is C.
