\(\left[\log_{10}\left(5\log_{10}100\right)\right]^2\) 的值是多少？

What is the value of \(\left[\log_{10}\left(5\log_{10}100\right)\right]^2?\)

由于 \(\log_{10}100=2\)，方括号内的表达式为 \(\log_{10}(5\cdot2)=\log_{10}10=1\)。它的平方是 \(1\)。

English

Since \(\log_{10}100=2,\) the expression inside the square brackets is \(\log_{10}(5\cdot2)=\log_{10}10=1.\) Its square is \(1.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is E.

Therefore, the correct answer is D.

若 \(x\)、\(2x+2\)、\(3x+3\)、\(\ldots\) 构成等比数列，则第四项是：

English（选项同上方）

If \(x,\) \(2x+2,\) \(3x+3,\) \(\ldots\) are in geometric progression, the fourth term is:

等比中项关系给出 \[(2x+2)^2=x(3x+3)\text{。}\]按本试题对等比数列的公比约定，相邻项之比必须有定义，所以 \(x\ne-1\)。两边除以 \(x+1\)，得 \(4(x+1)=3x\)，从而 \(x=-4\)。前三项为 \(-4,-6,-9\)，公比为 \(\frac{3}{2}\)。第四项是 \(-9(\frac{3}{2})=-\frac{27}{2}\)。

English

Therefore, the correct answer is B.

一名批发商按 \(\$24\) 再减去 \(12\dfrac12\%\) 的价格买入一件商品。他想在标价打 \(20\%\) 折扣后，仍获得相当于成本 \(33\dfrac13\%\) 的利润。这件商品应标价多少美元？

English（选项同上方）

A jobber buys an article at \(\$24\) less \(12\dfrac12\%.\) He then wishes to sell the article at a gain of \(33\dfrac13\%\) of his cost after allowing a \(20\%\) discount on his marked price. At what price, in dollars, should the article be marked?

成本为 \(24(1-\frac{1}{8})=21\) 美元。增加三分之一的利润后，目标售价为 \(21(\frac{4}{3})=28\) 美元。设标价为 \(M\)，则 \(0.8M=28\)，所以 \(M=35\)。选项中没有这个数值。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

The cost is \(24(1-\frac{1}{8})=21\) dollars. A gain of one third makes the desired selling price \(21(\frac{4}{3})=28\) dollars. If \(M\) is the marked price, then \(0.8M=28,\) so \(M=35.\) This is not listed.

Thus, the correct answer is E.

12单选

下列哪一项是此命题的否定：对于某个集合中的所有 \(x\)，都有 \(x^2\gt0\)？

English（选项同上方）

Which of the following is the negation of the statement: For all \(x\) of a certain set, \(x^2\gt0?\)

全称命题的否定是存在命题，而 \(x^2\gt0\) 的否定是 \(x^2\leq0\)。因此否定命题是：存在某个 \(x\)，使 \(x^2\leq0\)。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

The negation of a universal statement is an existential statement, and the negation of \(x^2\gt0\) is \(x^2\leq0.\) Thus the negation is: for some \(x,\) \(x^2\leq0.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is E.

一个三角形的三边长为 \(8\)、\(13\) 和 \(17\)，其内切圆与长度为 \(8\) 的边相切，并将该边分成长度为 \(r\) 和 \(s\) 的两段，其中 \(r\lt s\)。求 \(r:s\)。

English（选项同上方）

A circle is inscribed in a triangle with side lengths \(8,\) \(13,\) and \(17.\) Let the segments of the side of length \(8,\) made by a point of tangency, be \(r\) and \(s,\) with \(r\lt s.\) What is the ratio \(r:s?\)

半周长为 \(\frac{8+13+17}{2}=19\)。长度为 \(8\) 的边两端对应的切线段长分别为 \(19-13=6\) 和 \(19-17=2\)。因此 \(r:s=2:6=1:3\)。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

The semiperimeter is \(\frac{8+13+17}{2}=19.\) At the endpoints of the side of length \(8,\) the tangent lengths are \(19-13=6\) and \(19-17=2.\) Thus \(r:s=2:6=1:3.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is A.

一位农民买了 \(749\) 只羊。他卖出其中 \(700\) 只，所得正好等于购买 \(749\) 只羊的总成本。剩余 \(49\) 只羊的每只售价与前 \(700\) 只相同。相对于成本，整笔交易的利润百分比是：

English（选项同上方）

A farmer bought \(749\) sheep. He sold \(700\) of them for the price paid for the \(749\) sheep. The remaining \(49\) sheep were sold at the same price per head as the other \(700.\) Based on the cost, the percent gain on the entire transaction is:

设总成本为 \(C\)。由于前 \(700\) 只羊共卖得 \(C\)，每只售价为 \(\frac{C}{700}\)。因此总收入为 \(\frac{749C}{700}=1.07C\)，利润为 \(7\%\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Let the total cost be \(C.\) Since the first \(700\) sheep sell for \(C,\) each sheep sells for \(\frac{C}{700}.\) Total revenue is therefore \(\frac{749C}{700}=1.07C,\) a \(7\%\) gain.

Thus, the correct answer is C.

设 \(f(x)=x^2+3x+2\)，并设 \(S\) 为整数集合 \(\{0,1,2,\ldots,25\}\)。使 \(f(s)\) 除以 \(6\) 的余数为零的 \(S\) 中元素 \(s\) 的个数是：

English（选项同上方）

Let \(f(x)=x^2+3x+2\) and let \(S\) be the set of integers \(\{0,1,2,\ldots,25\}.\) The number of members \(s\) of \(S\) such that \(f(s)\) has remainder zero when divided by \(6\) is:

有 \(f(s)=(s+1)(s+2)\)，这是两个连续整数的乘积，所以总是偶数。除非 \(s\equiv0\pmod3\)，否则它能被 \(3\) 整除。在 \(0,1,\ldots,25\) 中，有九个数是 \(3\) 的倍数。因此有 \(26-9=17\) 个数符合条件。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

We have \(f(s)=(s+1)(s+2),\) a product of consecutive integers, so it is always even. It is divisible by \(3\) unless \(s\equiv0\pmod3.\) Among \(0,1,\ldots,25,\) nine values are multiples of \(3.\) Thus \(26-9=17\) values work.

Therefore, the correct answer is E.

English（选项同上方）

Given the distinct points \(P(x_1,y_1),\) \(Q(x_2,y_2)\) and \(R(x_1+x_2,y_1+y_2).\) Line segments are drawn connecting these points to each other and to the origin \(O.\) Of the three possibilities: \((1)\) parallelogram, \((2)\) straight line, \((3)\) trapezoid, figure \(OPRQ,\) depending upon the location of the points \(P,\) \(Q,\) and \(R,\) can be:

当 \(P\) 和 \(Q\) 不共线时，关系 \(\overrightarrow{OR}=\overrightarrow{OP}+\overrightarrow{OQ}\) 使 \(OPRQ\) 的两组对边分别平行且相等，所以图形是平行四边形。若两个向量线性相关，则四点都在一条直线上。它不可能是严格意义上的梯形。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

The relation \(\overrightarrow{OR}=\overrightarrow{OP}+\overrightarrow{OQ}\) makes opposite sides of \(OPRQ\) parallel and equal whenever \(P\) and \(Q\) are not collinear, so the figure is a parallelogram. If the two vectors are dependent, all four points lie on a straight line. It cannot be a genuine trapezoid.

Thus, the correct answer is D.

在平行四边形 \(ABCD\) 中，\(E\) 是对角线 \(BD\) 的中点。将点 \(E\) 与边 \(DA\) 上一点 \(F\) 相连，使 \(DF=\frac13DA\)。三角形 \(DFE\) 的面积与四边形 \(ABEF\) 的面积之比是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Given parallelogram \(ABCD\) with \(E\) the midpoint of diagonal \(BD.\) Point \(E\) is connected to a point \(F\) in \(DA\) so that \(DF=\frac13DA.\) What is the ratio of the area of triangle \(DFE\) to the area of quadrilateral \(ABEF?\)

设平行四边形面积为 \(S=|u\times v|\)，并取 \(A=0\)、\(B=u\)、\(D=v\)。则 \(E=\frac{u+v}{2}\)，\(F=\frac{2v}{3}\)。由行列式得 \([DFE]=\frac{S}{12}\)。对于四边形 \(ABEF\)，两部分面积给出 \[ [ABEF]=\frac S4+\frac S6=\frac{5S}{12}\text{。}\]所求比为 \(1:5\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Let the parallelogram’s area be \(S=|u\times v|,\) with \(A=0,\) \(B=u,\) and \(D=v.\) Then \(E=\frac{u+v}{2}\) and \(F=\frac{2v}{3}.\) Determinants give \([DFE]=\frac{S}{12}.\) For quadrilateral \(ABEF,\) the two determinant contributions give \[ [ABEF]=\frac S4+\frac S6=\frac{5S}{12}. \] The desired ratio is \(1:5.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is C.
