1单选

下式的值是多少 \[ \begin{aligned} &1 - (-2) - 3 - (-4) \\ &\quad {}- 5 - (-6) \end{aligned}\text{？}\]

English（选项同上方）

What is the value of \[ \begin{aligned} &1 - (-2) - 3 - (-4) \\ &\quad {}- 5 - (-6)? \end{aligned} \]

减去负数等于加上对应的正数，所以 \[ \begin{aligned} &1-(-2)-3-(-4) \\ &\quad {}-5-(-6) \\ &\quad = 1+2-3+4-5+6\text{。} \end{aligned} \] 合并各项可得 \[ \begin{aligned} &(1+2+4+6)-(3+5) \\ &\quad = 13-8=5\text{。} \end{aligned} \]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Subtracting a negative is the same as adding the corresponding positive number, so \[ \begin{aligned} &1-(-2)-3-(-4) \\ &\quad {}-5-(-6) \\ &\quad = 1+2-3+4-5+6. \end{aligned} \] Now combine the terms: \[ \begin{aligned} &(1+2+4+6)-(3+5) \\ &\quad = 13-8=5. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, D is the correct answer.

2单选

Carl 有 \(5\) 个边长为 \(1\) 的立方体，Kate 有 \(5\) 个边长为 \(2\) 的立方体。这 \(10\) 个立方体的总体积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Carl has \(5\) cubes each having side length \(1,\) and Kate has \(5\) cubes each having side length \(2.\) What is the total volume of these \(10\) cubes?

边长为 \(s\) 的立方体体积为 \(s^3\)。Carl 的五个立方体总体积为 \(5\cdot 1^3=5\)，Kate 的五个立方体总体积为 \(5\cdot 2^3=40\)。

总体积为 \(5+40=45\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

A cube with side length \(s\) has volume \(s^3.\) Carl’s five cubes have total volume \(5\cdot 1^3=5,\) and Kate’s five cubes have total volume \(5\cdot 2^3=40.\)

The total volume is \(5+40=45.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E .

3单选

\(w\) 与 \(x\) 的比为 \(4:3\)，\(y\) 与 \(z\) 的比为 \(3:2\)，\(z\) 与 \(x\) 的比为 \(1:6\)。\(w\) 与 \(y\) 的比是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The ratio of \(w\) to \(x\) is \(4:3,\) the ratio of \(y\) to \(z\) is \(3:2,\) and the ratio of \(z\) to \(x\) is \(1:6.\) What is the ratio of \(w\) to \(y?\)

题中比例给出 \[ \begin{aligned} &\frac wx=\frac43,\qquad \frac yz=\frac32, \\ &\quad \frac zx=\frac16\text{。} \end{aligned} \] 因此 \[ \begin{aligned} &\frac wy=\frac wx\cdot\frac xz\cdot\frac zy \\ &\quad =\frac43\cdot 6\cdot\frac23=\frac{16}{3}\text{。} \end{aligned} \] 所以 \(w:y=16:3\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The ratios give \[ \begin{aligned} &\frac wx=\frac43,\qquad \frac yz=\frac32, \\ &\quad \frac zx=\frac16. \end{aligned} \] Then \[ \begin{aligned} &\frac wy=\frac wx\cdot\frac xz\cdot\frac zy \\ &\quad =\frac43\cdot 6\cdot\frac23=\frac{16}{3}. \end{aligned} \] Therefore \(w:y=16:3.\)

Thus, E is the correct answer.

4单选

一个直角三角形的两个锐角分别为 \(a^{\circ}\) 和 \(b^{\circ}\)，其中 \(a>b\)，且 \(a\) 和 \(b\) 都是质数。\(b\) 的最小可能值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The acute angles of a right triangle are \(a^{\circ}\) and \(b^{\circ},\) where \(a>b\) and both \(a\) and \(b\) are prime numbers. What is the least possible value of \(b?\)

两个锐角满足 \(a+b=90\)。从小到大尝试质数 \(b\)，若 \(b=2,3,5\)，则 \(a=88,87,85\)，都不是质数。但当 \(b=7\) 时，\(a=83\) 是质数，且大于 \(b\)。

因此 \(b\) 的最小可能值为 \(7\)。所以正确答案是 D。

English

The acute angles satisfy \(a+b=90.\) Testing primes \(b\) from least to greatest, \(b=2,3,5\) would give \(a=88,87,85,\) none of which is prime. For \(b=7,\) however, \(a=83\) is prime and is greater than \(b.\)

Therefore the least possible value of \(b\) is \(7.\) Thus, D is the correct answer.

5单选

将 \(1\) 块棕色砖、\(1\) 块紫色砖、\(2\) 块绿色砖和 \(3\) 块黄色砖从左到右排成一行，有多少种可区分的排列？同色砖不可区分。

English（选项同上方）

How many distinguishable arrangements are there of \(1\) brown tile, \(1\) purple tile, \(2\) green tiles, and \(3\) yellow tiles in a row from left to right? (Tiles of the same color are indistinguishable.)

砖块总数为 \(1+1+2+3=7\)。若七块都不同有 \(7!\) 种排列。两块绿色砖不可区分，三块黄色砖不可区分，所以要除以 \(2!\) 和 \(3!\)。

因此可区分的排列数为 \[\frac{7!}{2!3!}=420\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

There are \(1+1+2+3=7\) total tiles. If all seven tiles were distinct, there would be \(7!\) arrangements. The two green tiles are indistinguishable, and the three yellow tiles are indistinguishable, so we divide by \(2!\) and \(3!.\)

Thus the number of arrangements is \[\frac{7!}{2!3!}=420.\]

Thus, B is the correct answer.

6单选

Megan 在高速公路上开车时，注意到里程表显示 \(15951\) 英里。这个数是回文数，也就是正读倒读相同。\(2\) 小时后，里程表显示下一个更大的回文数。在这 \(2\) 小时内，她的平均速度是多少英里每小时？

English（选项同上方）

Driving along a highway, Megan noticed that her odometer showed \(15951\) (miles). This number is a palindrome—it reads the same forward and backward. Then \(2\) hours later, the odometer displayed the next higher palindrome. What was her average speed, in miles per hour, during this \(2\)-hour period?

五位回文数由它的前三位数字唯一确定。\(15951\) 的前半部分是 \(159\)；把它增加到 \(160\)，再把前两位数字对称地反射到后两位，就得到下一个回文数 \(16061\)。

Megan 行驶了 \(16061-15951=110\) 英里，用时 \(2\) 小时，所以平均速度为每小时 \(\frac{110}{2}=55\) 英里。所以正确答案是 B。

English

A five-digit palindrome is determined by its first three digits. The first half of \(15951\) is \(159\); increasing this to \(160\) and reflecting the first two digits gives the next palindrome, \(16061.\)

Megan traveled \(16061-15951=110\) miles in \(2\) hours, so her average speed was \(\frac{110}{2}=55\) miles per hour. Thus, B is the correct answer.

7单选

小于 \(2020\) 的正偶数且为 \(3\) 的倍数的数中，有多少个是完全平方数？

English（选项同上方）

How many positive even multiples of \(3\) less than \(2020\) are perfect squares?

一个数既是偶数又是 \(3\) 的倍数时，它是 \(6\) 的倍数。若它还是完全平方数，则其平方根必须同时被 \(2\) 和 \(3\) 整除，因而被 \(6\) 整除。因此所求平方数恰好为 \[(6k)^2<2020\text{，}\]其中 \(k\) 为正整数。

因为 \(42^2=1764<2020\) 且 \(48^2=2304>2020\)，所以 \(k=1,2,\ldots,7\)，共有 \(7\) 个数。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

A number that is both even and a multiple of \(3\) is a multiple of \(6.\) If such a number is also a perfect square, its square root must be divisible by both \(2\) and \(3,\) hence by \(6.\) Therefore the numbers counted are exactly \[(6k)^2<2020\] for positive integers \(k.\)

Since \(42^2=1764<2020\) and \(48^2=2304>2020,\) we have \(k=1,2,\ldots,7,\) for a total of \(7\) numbers.

Thus, A is the correct answer.

8单选

平面内点 \(P\) 和 \(Q\) 满足 \(PQ=8\)。平面内有多少个点 \(R\)，使得以 \(P\)、\(Q\)、\(R\) 为顶点的三角形是直角三角形，且面积为 \(12\) 平方单位？

English（选项同上方）

Points \(P\) and \(Q\) lie in a plane with \(PQ=8.\) How many locations for point \(R\) in this plane are there such that the triangle with vertices \(P,\) \(Q,\) and \(R\) is a right triangle with area \(12\) square units?

取 \(P=(-4,0)\)、\(Q=(4,0)\)。因为面积为 \(12\) 且 \(PQ=8\)，点 \(R\) 到直线 \(PQ\) 的距离为 \(3\)，所以 \(R=(x,\pm 3)\)。

若直角在 \(P\)，则 \(R=(-4,\pm 3)\)，给出 \(2\) 点。若直角在 \(Q\)，则 \(R=(4,\pm 3)\)，再给出 \(2\) 点。

若直角在 \(R\)，则 \(PQ\) 是斜边，所以 \[ \begin{aligned} &64=PR^2+QR^2 \\ &\quad =(x+4)^2+9 \\ &\quad {}+(x-4)^2+9 \\ &\quad =2x^2+50\text{。} \end{aligned} \] 因此 \(x^2=7\)，给出 \(R=(\pm\sqrt7,\pm 3)\)，另有 \(4\) 点。

总数为 \(2+2+4=8\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Place \(P=(-4,0)\) and \(Q=(4,0).\) Since the area is \(12\) and \(PQ=8,\) the distance from \(R\) to line \(PQ\) is \(3,\) so \(R=(x,\pm 3).\)

If the right angle is at \(P,\) then \(R=(-4,\pm 3),\) giving \(2\) points. If it is at \(Q,\) then \(R=(4,\pm 3),\) giving \(2\) more points.

If the right angle is at \(R,\) then \(PQ\) is the hypotenuse, so \[ \begin{aligned} &64=PR^2+QR^2 \\ &\quad =(x+4)^2+9 \\ &\quad {}+(x-4)^2+9 \\ &\quad =2x^2+50. \end{aligned} \] Thus \(x^2=7,\) giving \(R=(\pm\sqrt7,\pm 3),\) another \(4\) points.

The total is \(2+2+4=8.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D .

9单选

有多少个整数有序对 \((x, y)\) 满足下面的方程 \[x^{2020}+y^2=2y\text{？}\]

English（选项同上方）

How many ordered pairs of integers \((x, y)\) satisfy the equation \[x^{2020}+y^2=2y?\]

移项并配方：\[ \begin{aligned} &x^{2020}+y^2=2y \\ &\quad \Longrightarrow x^{2020}+(y-1)^2=1\text{。} \end{aligned} \] 因为 \((y-1)^2\ge 0\)，所以 \(x^{2020}\le 1\)。由于 \(x\) 是整数，只可能有 \(x=-1,0,1\)。

若 \(x=\pm1\)，则 \((y-1)^2=0\)，所以 \(y=1\)。若 \(x=0\)，则 \((y-1)^2=1\)，所以 \(y=0\) 或 \(2\)。共有 \(4\) 个有序对。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Move all terms to one side and complete the square: \[ \begin{aligned} &x^{2020}+y^2=2y \\ &\quad \Longrightarrow x^{2020}+(y-1)^2=1. \end{aligned} \] Because \((y-1)^2\ge 0,\) we must have \(x^{2020}\le 1.\) Since \(x\) is an integer, \(x=-1,0,1.\)

If \(x=\pm1,\) then \((y-1)^2=0,\) so \(y=1.\) If \(x=0,\) then \((y-1)^2=1,\) so \(y=0\) or \(2.\) This gives \(4\) ordered pairs.

Thus, D is the correct answer.

10单选

一个半径为 \(4\) 英寸的圆的四分之三扇形连同内部，可以沿图中所示的两条半径粘合卷成一个直圆锥的侧面。这个圆锥的体积是多少立方英寸？

English（选项同上方）

A three-quarter sector of a circle of radius \(4\) inches together with its interior can be rolled up to form the lateral surface of a right circular cone by taping together along the two radii shown. What is the volume of the cone in cubic inches?

扇形的弧成为圆锥底面的周长，所以若底面半径为 \(r\)，则 \[2\pi r=\frac34(2\pi\cdot4)=6\pi\text{，}\] 即 \(r=3\)。扇形的半径成为圆锥的母线长，所以圆锥的高为 \[h=\sqrt{4^2-3^2}=\sqrt7\text{。}\]

因此圆锥的体积为 \[\frac13\pi r^2h=\frac13\pi(3)^2\sqrt7=3\pi\sqrt7\text{。}\] 所以正确答案是 C。

English

The sector’s arc becomes the base circumference, so if the base radius is \(r,\) then \[2\pi r=\frac34(2\pi\cdot4)=6\pi,\] giving \(r=3.\) The sector radius becomes the cone’s slant height, so the cone’s height is \[h=\sqrt{4^2-3^2}=\sqrt7.\]

Hence the volume is \[\frac13\pi r^2h=\frac13\pi(3)^2\sqrt7=3\pi\sqrt7.\] Thus, C is the correct answer.

11单选

Carr 老师要求学生阅读书单上 \(10\) 本书中的任意 \(5\) 本。Harold 从书单中随机选 \(5\) 本，Betty 也随机选五本。他们恰好有 \(2\) 本书都选中的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Ms. Carr asks her students to read any \(5\) of the \(10\) books on a reading list. Harold randomly selects \(5\) books from this list, and Betty does the same. What is the probability that there are exactly \(2\) books that they both select?

固定 Harold 选的 \(5\) 本书。Betty 从这五本中恰好选两本有 \(\binom{5}{2}\) 种方式，另外三本从 Harold 没选的 \(5\) 本中选，有 \(\binom{5}{3}\) 种方式。

因此在 \(\binom{10}{5}=252\) 种等可能的选法中，Betty 有 \(\binom{5}{2}\binom{5}{3}=100\) 种有利的选法。所求概率为 \[\frac{100}{252}=\frac{25}{63}\text{。}\] 所以正确答案是 D。

English

Assume that Harold has already picked his \(5\) books. Of these five books, there are \(\binom{5}{2}\) ways that Betty can have picked exactly two of the same books as Harold, and \(\binom{5}{3}\) ways that Betty can choose her other three books from the \(5\) books not on Harold’s list.

Thus there are \(\binom{5}{2}\binom{5}{3}=100\) favorable choices for Betty out of \(\binom{10}{5}=252\) equally likely choices. The probability is \[\frac{100}{252}=\frac{25}{63}.\] Thus, D is the correct answer.

12单选

数 \[\frac{1}{20^{20}}\] 的小数表示在小数点后先出现一串零，然后是一个 \(9\)，后面还有若干数字。小数点后最初的一串零有多少个？

English（选项同上方）

The decimal representation of \[\frac{1}{20^{20}}\] consists of a string of zeros after the decimal point, followed by a \(9\) and then several more digits. How many zeros are in that initial string of zeros after the decimal point?

可以写成 \[\frac{1}{20^{20}}=\frac{1}{(4\cdot5)^{20}}=\frac{5^{20}}{10^{40}}\text{。}\]又因为 \(5^{20}=95{,}367{,}431{,}640{,}625\)，这是一个 \(14\) 位数，且首位为 \(9\)。除以 \(10^{40}\) 后，将这个 \(14\) 位数放在小数点后，并在首位数字前放置 \(40-14=26\) 个零。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

We can write \[\frac{1}{20^{20}}=\frac{1}{(4\cdot5)^{20}}=\frac{5^{20}}{10^{40}}.\] Now \(5^{20}=95{,}367{,}431{,}640{,}625,\) which has \(14\) digits and begins with \(9.\) Dividing by \(10^{40}\) places this \(14\)-digit number after the decimal point with \(40-14=26\) zeros before the first digit.

Thus, the correct answer is D .

13单选

蚂蚁 Andy 生活在坐标平面上，目前位于 \((-20, 20)\)，面朝东方，也就是 \(x\) 轴正方向。Andy 先移动 \(1\) 个单位，然后向左转 \(90^{\circ}\)。接着他向北移动 \(2\) 个单位，再向左转 \(90^{\circ}\)。然后他向西移动 \(3\) 个单位，再向左转 \(90^{\circ}\)。Andy 继续这样移动，每次移动距离增加 \(1\) 个单位，并且总是左转。他第 \(2020\text{次}\) 左转所在点的坐标是什么？

English（选项同上方）

Andy the Ant lives on a coordinate plane and is currently at \((-20, 20)\) facing east (that is, in the positive \(x\)-direction). Andy moves \(1\) unit and then turns \(90^{\circ}\) left. From there, Andy moves \(2\) units (north) and then turns \(90^{\circ}\) left. He then moves \(3\) units (west) and again turns \(90^{\circ}\) left. Andy continues this process, increasing his distance each time by \(1\) unit and always turning left. What is the location of the point at which Andy makes the \(2020\text{th}\) left turn?

前四步依次为向东 \(1\)、向北 \(2\)、向西 \(3\)、向南 \(4\)。净位移为 \[(1-3,2-4)=(-2,-2)\text{，}\] 而且他重新面向东方。

因为 \(2020=4\cdot 505\)，Andy 共完成 \(505\) 个这样的循环。从 \((-20,20)\) 出发，最终位置为 \[ \begin{aligned} &(-20,20)+505(-2,-2) \\ &\quad =(-1030,-990) \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

In the first four moves, Andy goes \(1\) east, \(2\) north, \(3\) west, and \(4\) south. The net change is \[(1-3,2-4)=(-2,-2),\] and he is again facing east.

Since \(2020=4\cdot 505,\) Andy completes \(505\) such cycles. Starting from \((-20,20),\) his final position is \[ \begin{aligned} &(-20,20)+505(-2,-2) \\ &\quad =(-1030,-990). \end{aligned} \]

Thus, B is the correct answer.

14单选

如下图所示，六个半圆位于边长为 \(2\) 的正六边形内部，并且这些半圆的直径分别与正六边形的边重合。阴影区域，也就是六边形内但所有半圆外的区域，面积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

As shown in the figure below, six semicircles lie in the interior of a regular hexagon with side length \(2\) so that the diameters of the semicircles coincide with the sides of the hexagon. What is the area of the shaded region—inside the hexagon but outside all of the semicircles?

由对称性，阴影区域由六个全等部分组成。每一部分是两个边长为 \(1\) 的等边三角形的并，减去一个半径为 \(1\)、圆心角为 \(60^\circ\) 的扇形。

两个等边三角形的总面积为 \[2\cdot\frac{\sqrt3}{4}=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\text{。}\] 扇形面积为 \[\frac{60^\circ}{360^\circ}\cdot\pi(1)^2=\frac{\pi}{6}\text{。}\] 因此每一块阴影的面积为 \(\frac{\sqrt3}{2}-\frac{\pi}{6}\)，阴影总面积为 \[6\left(\frac{\sqrt3}{2}-\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=3\sqrt3-\pi\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

By symmetry, the shaded region is made of six congruent pieces. One such piece is the union of two equilateral triangles with side length \(1,\) minus a \(60^\circ\) sector of a circle of radius \(1.\)

The two equilateral triangles have total area \[2\cdot\frac{\sqrt3}{4}=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}.\] The sector has area \[\frac{60^\circ}{360^\circ}\cdot\pi(1)^2=\frac{\pi}{6}.\] Thus one shaded piece has area \(\frac{\sqrt3}{2}-\frac{\pi}{6},\) and the total shaded area is \[6\left(\frac{\sqrt3}{2}-\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=3\sqrt3-\pi.\]

Thus, D is the correct answer.

15单选

Steve 从左到右反复写数字 \(1\)、\(2\)、\(3\)、\(4\) 和 \(5\)，形成一个有 \(10{,}000\) 位数字的列表，开头为 \(123451234512\ldots\)。然后他擦掉列表中每第三个数字，也就是从左数第 \(3\)、\(6\)、\(9\)、\(\ldots\) 个数字；接着在剩下的列表中擦掉每第四个数字，也就是剩余列表中第 \(4\)、\(8\)、\(12\)、\(\ldots\) 个数字；然后在此时剩下的列表中擦掉每第五个数字。此后位于第 \(2019\)、\(2020\)、\(2021\) 位的三个数字之和是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Steve wrote the digits \(1,\) \(2,\) \(3,\) \(4,\) and \(5\) in order repeatedly from left to right, forming a list of \(10{,}000\) digits, beginning \(123451234512\ldots\) He then erased every third digit from his list (that is, the \(3\)rd, \(6\)th, \(9\)th, \(\ldots\) digits from the left), then erased every fourth digit from the resulting list (that is, the \(4\)th, \(8\)th, \(12\)th, \(\ldots\) digits from the left in what remained), and then erased every fifth digit from what remained at that point. What is the sum of the three digits that were then in positions \(2019,\) \(2020,\) and \(2021?\)

令 \(D(n)\) 表示把正整数 \(n\) 写成乘积 \[n = f_1\cdot f_2\cdots f_k\] 的方式数，其中 \(k\ge1\)，每个 \(f_i\) 都是严格大于 \(1\) 的整数，并且因子的排列顺序有区别，也就是说仅因因子顺序不同的表示也算不同。例如，\(6\) 可写为 \(6\)、\(2\cdot 3\) 和 \(3\cdot2\)，所以 \(D(6) = 3\)。\(D(96)\) 是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Let \(D(n)\) denote the number of ways of writing the positive integer \(n\) as a product \[n = f_1\cdot f_2\cdots f_k,\] where \(k\ge1,\) the \(f_i\) are integers strictly greater than \(1,\) and the order in which the factors are listed matters (that is, two representations that differ only in the order of the factors are counted as distinct). For example, the number \(6\) can be written as \(6,\) \(2\cdot 3,\) and \(3\cdot2,\) so \(D(6) = 3.\) What is \(D(96)?\)

写作 \(96=2^5\cdot3\)。假设一个有序分解有 \(k\) 个因子。唯一的质因子 \(3\) 必须出现在恰好一个因子中，可用 \(k\) 种方式选择这个位置。

其余 \(k-1\) 个因子都必须至少含有一个因子 \(2\)，而含 \(3\) 的那个因子可以含有任意个因子 \(2\)。按这些条件分配五个因子 \(2\)，有 \[\binom{5}{k-1}\] 种方式。因此含有 \(k\) 个因子的有序分解数为 \(k\binom{5}{k-1}\)，其中 \(1\le k\le6\)。

所以 \[D(96)=\sum_{k=1}^6 k\binom{5}{k-1}\text{。}\] 令 \(j=k-1\)，得到 \[ \begin{aligned} &\sum_{j=0}^5 (j+1)\binom5j \\ &\quad =\sum_{j=0}^5 j\binom5j+\sum_{j=0}^5\binom5j \\ &\quad =5\cdot2^4+2^5 \\ &\quad =80+32=112\text{。} \end{aligned} \]

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Write \(96=2^5\cdot3.\) Suppose an ordered factorization has \(k\) factors. Exactly one factor contains the single prime factor \(3\); choose its position in \(k\) ways.

The other \(k-1\) factors must each contain at least one factor of \(2,\) while the factor containing \(3\) may contain any number of factors of \(2.\) Distributing the five factors of \(2\) under these conditions can be done in \[\binom{5}{k-1}\] ways. Therefore the number of ordered factorizations with \(k\) factors is \(k\binom{5}{k-1},\) where \(1\le k\le6.\)

Thus \[D(96)=\sum_{k=1}^6 k\binom{5}{k-1}.\] Letting \(j=k-1,\) this becomes \[ \begin{aligned} &\sum_{j=0}^5 (j+1)\binom5j \\ &\quad =\sum_{j=0}^5 j\binom5j+\sum_{j=0}^5\binom5j \\ &\quad =5\cdot2^4+2^5 \\ &\quad =80+32=112. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, A is the correct answer.
