English（选项同上方）

The value of \(x+x(x^x)\) when \(x=2\) is:

代入 \(x=2\)，并先计算幂，得 \[ \begin{aligned} x+x(x^x)&=2+2(2^2)\\ &=2+8=10 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

English

Substituting \(x=2,\) and evaluating the exponent first, gives \[ \begin{aligned} x+x(x^x)&=2+2(2^2)\\ &=2+8=10. \end{aligned} \]

2单选

琼斯先生以每根 \(\$1.20\) 的价格卖出两根烟斗。按成本计算，其中一根获利 \(20\%\)，另一根亏损 \(20\%\)。在这笔交易中，他：

English（选项同上方）

Mr. Jones sold two pipes at \(\$1.20\) each. Based on the cost his profit on one was \(20\%\) and his loss on the other was \(20\%.\) On the sale of the pipes, he:

获利 \(20\%\) 的烟斗成本为 \[ \frac{\$1.20}{1.20}=\$1.00\text{，}\]而亏损 \(20\%\) 的烟斗成本为 \[ \frac{\$1.20}{0.80}=\$1.50\text{。}\]两根烟斗的总成本为 \(\$2.50\)，但总售价为 \(\$2.40\)，所以琼斯先生亏损 \(10\) 美分。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

The pipe sold at a \(20\%\) profit cost \[ \frac{\$1.20}{1.20}=\$1.00, \] while the pipe sold at a \(20\%\) loss cost \[ \frac{\$1.20}{0.80}=\$1.50. \] Their combined cost was \(\$2.50,\) but they sold for \(\$2.40,\) so Mr. Jones lost \(10\) cents.

Thus, the correct answer is D.

一名男子有 \(\$10{,}000\) 可供投资。他将 \(\$4{,}000\) 按 \(5\%\) 的利率投资，又将 \(\$3{,}500\) 按 \(4\%\) 的利率投资。为了获得每年 \(\$500\) 的收入，他必须将余款按下列利率投资：

English（选项同上方）

A man has \(\$10{,}000\) to invest. He invests \(\$4{,}000\) at \(5\%\) and \(\$3{,}500\) at \(4\%.\) In order to have a yearly income of \(\$500,\) he must invest the remainder at:

剩余可投资金额为 \[ 10000-4000-3500=2500\text{。}\]前两笔投资获得 \(0.05(4000)+0.04(3500)=340\) 美元，所以余款必须获得 \(500-340=160\) 美元。所需利率为 \[ \frac{160}{2500}=0.064=6.4\%\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

The amount left to invest is \[ 10000-4000-3500=2500. \] The first two investments earn \(0.05(4000)+0.04(3500)=340\) dollars, so the remaining investment must earn \(500-340=160\) dollars. Its required rate is \[ \frac{160}{2500}=0.064=6.4\%. \]

Thus, the correct answer is E.

将一枚五美分硬币放在桌上。围绕它最多可以放置多少枚五美分硬币，使每一枚都与中间的硬币及另外两枚硬币相切？

English（选项同上方）

A nickel is placed on a table. The number of nickels which can be placed around it, each tangent to it and to two others is:

中间硬币与任意两枚相邻硬币的圆心两两相距两个半径，所以它们构成等边三角形。因此，相邻两枚外圈硬币的圆心在中间硬币圆心处所成的角为 \(60^\circ\)。恰有 \[ \frac{360^\circ}{60^\circ}=6 \] 枚硬币可以围在它周围。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

The centers of the middle nickel and any two neighboring nickels are pairwise two radii apart, so they form an equilateral triangle. Therefore consecutive outer centers subtend \(60^\circ\) at the center of the middle nickel. Exactly \[ \frac{360^\circ}{60^\circ}=6 \] nickels fit around it.

Thus, the correct answer is C.

English（选项同上方）

In a group of cows and chickens, the number of legs was \(14\) more than twice the number of heads. The number of cows was:

按每个头计算两条腿，正好计入每只鸡的两条腿，以及每头牛四条腿中的两条。因此，每头牛恰好贡献 \(14\) 条额外腿中的两条。所以牛的数量为 \[ \frac{14}{2}=7\text{。}\]

English

Counting two legs for every head accounts for both legs of each chicken and two of each cow’s four legs. Thus every cow contributes exactly two of the \(14\) extra legs. Hence the number of cows is \[ \frac{14}{2}=7. \]

Thus, the correct answer is B.

一个半径为 \(10\) 英寸的圆以等边三角形 \(ABC\) 的顶点 \(C\) 为圆心，并经过另外两个顶点。边 \(AC\) 经过 \(C\) 延长后与圆交于 \(D\)。角 \(ADB\) 的度数为：

English（选项同上方）

A circle of radius \(10\) inches has its center at the vertex \(C\) of an equilateral triangle \(ABC\) and passes through the other two vertices. The side \(AC\) extended through \(C\) intersects the circle at \(D.\) The number of degrees of angle \(ADB\) is:

由于 \(ABC\) 是等边三角形，圆心角 \(\angle ACB\) 为 \(60^\circ\)。圆周角 \(\angle ADB\) 所对的是同一条小弧 \(AB\)，所以其度数为圆心角的一半：\[ \angle ADB=\frac{60^\circ}{2}=30^\circ\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Since \(ABC\) is equilateral, the central angle \(\angle ACB\) is \(60^\circ.\) The inscribed angle \(\angle ADB\) intercepts the same minor arc \(AB,\) so its measure is half the central angle: \[ \angle ADB=\frac{60^\circ}{2}=30^\circ. \]

Thus, the correct answer is B.

表达式 \(1-\dfrac1{1+\sqrt3}+\dfrac1{1-\sqrt3}\) 等于：

English（选项同上方）

The expression \(1-\dfrac1{1+\sqrt3}+\dfrac1{1-\sqrt3}\) equals:

合并两个分式项，\[ \begin{gathered} -\frac1{1+\sqrt3}+\frac1{1-\sqrt3} \\ =\frac{(1+\sqrt3)-(1-\sqrt3)} {(1+\sqrt3)(1-\sqrt3)}\\ =\frac{2\sqrt3}{-2}\\ =-\sqrt3 \end{gathered}\text{。}\]再加上开头的 \(1\)，得 \(1-\sqrt3\)。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

Combining the fractional terms, \[ \begin{gathered} -\frac1{1+\sqrt3}+\frac1{1-\sqrt3} \\ =\frac{(1+\sqrt3)-(1-\sqrt3)} {(1+\sqrt3)(1-\sqrt3)}\\ =\frac{2\sqrt3}{-2}\\ =-\sqrt3. \end{gathered} \] Adding the initial \(1\) gives \(1-\sqrt3.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

点 \(A\)、\(B\) 和 \(C\) 位于圆 \(O\) 上。圆在 \(A\) 点处的切线与割线 \(BC\) 交于 \(P\)，且 \(B\) 位于 \(C\) 与 \(P\) 之间。若 \(BC=20\) 且 \(PA=10\sqrt3\)，则 \(PB\) 等于：

English（选项同上方）

The points \(A,\) \(B,\) and \(C\) are on a circle \(O.\) The tangent line at \(A\) and the secant \(BC\) intersect at \(P,\) \(B\) lying between \(C\) and \(P.\) If \(BC=20\) and \(PA=10\sqrt3,\) then \(PB\) equals:

设 \(PB=t\)。由于 \(B\) 位于 \(P\) 与 \(C\) 之间，所以 \(PC=t+20\)。由切割线定理，\[ (10\sqrt3)^2=t(t+20)\text{。}\]因此 \(t^2+20t-300=0\)，即 \((t-10)(t+30)=0\)。长度为正，所以 \(t=10\)。

English

Therefore, the correct answer is B.

分式 \(\dfrac{5x-11}{2x^2+x-6}\) 是由分式 \(\dfrac A{x+2}\) 与 \(\dfrac B{2x-3}\) 相加得到的。\(A\) 和 \(B\) 的值依次必须为：

English（选项同上方）

The fraction \(\dfrac{5x-11}{2x^2+x-6}\) was obtained by adding the two fractions \(\dfrac A{x+2}\) and \(\dfrac B{2x-3}.\) The values of \(A\) and \(B\) must be, respectively:

合并给出的部分分式，得 \[ \frac{A(2x-3)+B(x+2)}{(x+2)(2x-3)}\text{。}\]将其分子与 \(5x-11\) 比较，得 \[ \begin{aligned} 2A+B&=5,\\ -3A+2B&=-11 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]解得 \(A=3\)，且 \(B=-1\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

Combining the proposed partial fractions gives \[ \frac{A(2x-3)+B(x+2)}{(x+2)(2x-3)}. \] Matching its numerator with \(5x-11\) yields \[ \begin{aligned} 2A+B&=5,\\ -3A+2B&=-11. \end{aligned} \] Solving gives \(A=3\) and \(B=-1.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

19单选

两根等高的蜡烛同时点燃。第一根在 \(4\) 小时内烧完，第二根在 \(3\) 小时内烧完。假设每根蜡烛都以恒定速度燃烧，点燃多少小时后，第一根蜡烛的高度是第二根的两倍？

English（选项同上方）

Two candles of the same height are lighted at the same time. The first is consumed in \(4\) hours and the second in \(3\) hours. Assuming that each candle burns at a constant rate, in how many hours after being lighted was the first candle twice the height of the second?

设每根蜡烛的初始高度为 \(1\)。\(t\) 小时后，剩余高度分别为 \(1-\frac{t}{4}\) 和 \(1-\frac{t}{3}\)。所需条件为 \[ 1-\frac t4=2\left(1-\frac t3\right)\text{。}\]两边乘以 \(12\)，得 \(12-3t=24-8t\)，所以 \(5t=12\)，且 \(t=\frac{12}{5}=2\dfrac25\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

Let each initial height be \(1.\) After \(t\) hours the remaining heights are \(1-\frac{t}{4}\) and \(1-\frac{t}{3}.\) The required condition is \[ 1-\frac t4=2\left(1-\frac t3\right). \] Multiplying by \(12\) gives \(12-3t=24-8t,\) so \(5t=12\) and \(t=\frac{12}{5}=2\dfrac25.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

English（选项同上方）

两边取常用对数，得 \[ x\log(0.2)=\log2\text{。}\]由于 \(\log(0.2)=\log2-1=-0.6990\)， \[ x=\frac{0.3010}{-0.6990}\approx-0.4306\text{。}\]精确到十分位为 \(-0.4\)。

Thus, the correct answer is C.

21单选

若两条相交直线都与一条双曲线相交，且都不与该双曲线相切，则它们与双曲线的交点数可能为：

English（选项同上方）

If each of two intersecting lines intersects a hyperbola and neither line is tangent to the hyperbola, then the possible number of points of intersection with the hyperbola is:

当直线方向平行于一条渐近线时，它可以与双曲线有一个有限交点；否则可以有两个有限交点。适当选取两条直线，使它们在双曲线上的有限交点互不重合，便可分别得到 \(1+1=2\)、\(1+2=3\) 或 \(2+2=4\) 个不同交点。若两条直线自身的交点恰好在双曲线上，则这个交点只计一次。

因此，\(2\)、\(3\) 或 \(4\) 都有可能，正确答案是 E。

English

A line can meet a hyperbola in either one finite point, when its direction is parallel to an asymptote, or two finite points. By choosing the two lines so that their finite intersection sets on the hyperbola are disjoint, they can therefore contribute \(1+1=2,\) \(1+2=3,\) or \(2+2=4\) distinct intersections. If their own intersection lies on the hyperbola, one point is shared instead.

Thus, \(2,\) \(3,\) or \(4\) are possible, and the correct answer is E.

22单选

琼斯第一次旅行走了 \(50\) 英里。后来一次旅行中，他以原来三倍的速度走了 \(300\) 英里。新旅行时间与原旅行时间相比是：

English（选项同上方）

Jones covered a distance of \(50\) miles on his first trip. On a later trip he traveled \(300\) miles while going three times as fast. His new time compared with the old time was:

若第一次的速度为 \(v\)，则原旅行时间为 \(\frac{50}{v}\)。后一次的旅行时间为 \[ \frac{300}{3v}=\frac{100}{v}=2\left(\frac{50}{v}\right)\text{。}\]因此，新旅行时间是原来的两倍。

正确答案是 B。

English

If the first speed was \(v,\) the old time was \(\frac{50}{v}.\) The later time was \[ \frac{300}{3v}=\frac{100}{v}=2\left(\frac{50}{v}\right). \] Thus the new time was twice the old time.

The correct answer is B.

方程 \(ax^2-2x\sqrt2+c=0\) 中，\(a\) 和 \(c\) 为实常数，且已知判别式为零。它的根必定：

English（选项同上方）

About the equation \(ax^2-2x\sqrt2+c=0,\) with \(a\) and \(c\) real constants, we are told that the discriminant is zero. The roots are necessarily:

实系数且判别式为零的二次方程有重根 \[ x=\frac{-(-2\sqrt2)}{2a}=\frac{\sqrt2}{a}\text{。}\]由于 \(a\) 是非零实数，此根为实数。它不一定总是有理数，也不一定总是无理数。

交叉相乘得到 \[ N(1+x)=(N+1)(1-x)\text{，}\]所以 \(x(2N+1)=1\)，并且 \[ x=\frac1{2N+1}\text{。}\]每个正数 \(N\) 都给出 \(0\lt x\lt1\)。反过来，对任意 \(0\lt x\lt1\)，有 \[ N=\frac{1-x}{2x}>0\text{，}\]因此存在这样的 \(N\)。

因此，\(x\) 可以是任意小于 \(1\) 的正数，正确答案是 A。

English

Cross-multiplication gives \[ N(1+x)=(N+1)(1-x), \] so \(x(2N+1)=1\) and \[ x=\frac1{2N+1}. \] Every positive \(N\) gives \(0\lt x\lt1.\) Conversely, for any \(0\lt x\lt1,\) \[ N=\frac{1-x}{2x}>0, \] so such an \(N\) exists.

Thus, \(x\) may be any positive value less than \(1,\) and the correct answer is A.

一位工程师说，凭现有的某种机器，他可以在 \(3\) 天内完成一段公路工程。然而，若再增加 \(3\) 台同类机器，工程可在 \(2\) 天内完成。若所有机器的工作效率相同，一台机器单独完成这项工程需要多少天？

English（选项同上方）

An engineer said he could finish a highway section in \(3\) days with his present supply of a certain type of machine. However, with \(3\) more of these machines the job could be done in \(2\) days. If the machines all work at the same rate, how many days would it take to do the job with one machine?

若现有 \(m\) 台机器，则这项工程需要 \(3m\) 个机器·天。增加三台机器后，需要 \(2(m+3)\) 个机器·天，所以 \[ 3m=2(m+3)\text{，}\]解得 \(m=6\)。因此，这项工程需要 \(3m=18\) 个机器·天，一台机器单独完成需要 \(18\) 天。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

If there are presently \(m\) machines, the job requires \(3m\) machine-days. With three more machines it requires \(2(m+3)\) machine-days, so \[ 3m=2(m+3), \] giving \(m=6.\) The job therefore requires \(3m=18\) machine-days, so one machine would take \(18\) days.

Thus, the correct answer is D.

若 \(p\) 为正整数，则 \(\dfrac{3p+25}{2p-5}\) 为正整数，当且仅当 \(p\) 为：

English（选项同上方）

If \(p\) is a positive integer, then \(\dfrac{3p+25}{2p-5}\) can be a positive integer, if and only if \(p\) is:

令 \(q=2p-5\)。商为正数要求 \(q>0\)，而 \(q\) 是奇数。由于 \(p=\frac{q+5}{2}\)，\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{3p+25}{2p-5} &=\frac{3q+65}{2q}\\ &=\frac{3+\frac{65}{q}}{2} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]所以 \(q\) 必须是 \(65\) 的正因数。四种可能 \(q=1\)、\(q=5\)、\(q=13\)、\(q=65\) 分别给出 \(p=3\)、\(p=5\)、\(p=9\)、\(p=35\)，且每个都符合。不存在其他符合条件的正整数 \(p\)。

因此，数学上正确的集合是 \(\{3,5,9,35\}\)，即调整后的选项 B。

English

Set \(q=2p-5.\) A positive quotient requires \(q>0,\) and \(q\) is odd. Since \(p=\frac{q+5}{2},\) \[ \begin{aligned} \frac{3p+25}{2p-5} &=\frac{3q+65}{2q}\\ &=\frac{3+\frac{65}{q}}{2}. \end{aligned} \] Thus \(q\) must be a positive divisor of \(65.\) The possibilities \(q=1,\) \(q=5,\) \(q=13,\) and \(q=65\) give \(p=3,\) \(p=5,\) \(p=9,\) and \(p=35,\) respectively, and each works. No other positive integer \(p\) works.

Therefore the mathematically correct set is \(\{3,5,9,35\},\) shown in the adjusted choice B.

三角形 \(PAB\) 由圆 \(O\) 的三条切线围成，且 \(\angle APB=40^\circ\)；则角 \(AOB\) 等于：

English（选项同上方）

Triangle \(PAB\) is formed by three tangents to circle \(O\) and \(\angle APB=40^\circ;\) then angle \(AOB\) equals:

圆位于边 \(AB\) 的另一侧，与顶点 \(P\) 相对，所以 \(O\) 是 \(P\) 所对的旁心。因此，\(AO\) 和 \(BO\) 分别平分 \(A\) 与 \(B\) 处的外角。若 \(A\) 与 \(B\) 处的内角分别为 \(\alpha\) 和 \(\beta\)，则三角形 \(AOB\) 在 \(A\) 与 \(B\) 处的角分别为 \(90^\circ-\frac{\alpha}{2}\) 和 \(90^\circ-\frac{\beta}{2}\)。因此 \[ \begin{aligned} \angle AOB &=180^\circ-\left(90^\circ-\frac\alpha2\right)\\ &\mathrel{\phantom{=}}-\left(90^\circ-\frac\beta2\right)\\ &=\frac{\alpha+\beta}{2} =\frac{180^\circ-40^\circ}{2}\\ &=70^\circ \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

The circle lies opposite \(P\) across side \(AB,\) so \(O\) is the excenter opposite \(P.\) Thus \(AO\) and \(BO\) bisect the exterior angles at \(A\) and \(B.\) If the interior angles at \(A\) and \(B\) are \(\alpha\) and \(\beta,\) then triangle \(AOB\) has angles \(90^\circ-\frac{\alpha}{2}\) and \(90^\circ-\frac{\beta}{2}\) at \(A\) and \(B.\) Hence \[ \begin{aligned} \angle AOB &=180^\circ-\left(90^\circ-\frac\alpha2\right)\\ &\mathrel{\phantom{=}}-\left(90^\circ-\frac\beta2\right)\\ &=\frac{\alpha+\beta}{2} =\frac{180^\circ-40^\circ}{2}\\ &=70^\circ. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is E.

在三角形 \(ABC\) 中，\(CA=CB\)。以 \(CB\) 为边，在三角形外部作正方形 \(BCDE\)。若角 \(DAB\) 的度数为 \(x\)，则

English（选项同上方）

In triangle \(ABC,\) \(CA=CB.\) On \(CB\) square \(BCDE\) is constructed away from the triangle. If \(x\) is the number of degrees in angle \(DAB,\) then

将两条相等的边缩放为 \(1\)，并令 \[ \begin{aligned} C&=(0,0),\qquad B=(1,0),\\ A&=(\cos\theta,\sin\theta) \end{aligned}\text{。}\]因为正方形 \(BCDE\) 作在三角形外部，所以 \(D=(0,-1)\)。令 \(c=\cos\theta\) 且 \(s=\sin\theta\)。则 \[ \begin{aligned} \overrightarrow{AB}&=(1-c,-s),\\ \overrightarrow{AD}&=(-c,-1-s) \end{aligned}\text{。}\]两个向量的点积为 \(1+s-c\)，二维叉积的绝对值也为 \(1+s-c\)。因此 \[ \tan\angle DAB=1\text{，}\]所以 \(\angle DAB=45^\circ\)，它与 \(\theta\) 无关，因而也与三角形的形状无关。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Scale the equal sides to \(1\) and place \[ \begin{aligned} C&=(0,0),\qquad B=(1,0),\\ A&=(\cos\theta,\sin\theta). \end{aligned} \] Since square \(BCDE\) is constructed away from the triangle, \(D=(0,-1).\) Put \(c=\cos\theta\) and \(s=\sin\theta.\) Then \[ \begin{aligned} \overrightarrow{AB}&=(1-c,-s),\\ \overrightarrow{AD}&=(-c,-1-s). \end{aligned} \] Their dot product is \(1+s-c,\) while the absolute value of their two-dimensional cross product is also \(1+s-c.\) Therefore \[ \tan\angle DAB=1, \] so \(\angle DAB=45^\circ,\) independent of \(\theta\) and hence independent of the triangle’s shape.

Thus, the correct answer is B.
