1单选

下列式子的值是多少？\[10 \cdot \left(\dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{1}{5} + \dfrac{1}{10}\right)^{-1}\]

English（选项同上方）

What is \[10 \cdot \left(\dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{1}{5} + \dfrac{1}{10}\right)^{-1}?\]

括号内的和为 \(\dfrac12+\dfrac15+\dfrac{1}{10}=\dfrac{5+2+1}{10}=\dfrac{4}{5}\)。

它的倒数是 \(\dfrac54\)，所以原式等于 \(10\cdot\dfrac54=\dfrac{25}{2}\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The sum inside the parentheses is \(\dfrac12+\dfrac15+\dfrac{1}{10}=\dfrac{5+2+1}{10}=\dfrac{4}{5}.\)

Its reciprocal is \(\dfrac54,\) so the expression equals \(10\cdot\dfrac54=\dfrac{25}{2}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

2单选

在剧院，儿童票半价。\(5\) 张成人票和 \(4\) 张儿童票共 \(\$24.50\)。\(8\) 张成人票和 \(6\) 张儿童票要花多少钱？

English（选项同上方）

At the theater children get in for half price. The price for \(5\) adult tickets and \(4\) child tickets is \(\$24.50.\) How much would \(8\) adult tickets and \(6\) child tickets cost?

儿童票半价，所以 \(5\) 张成人票和 \(4\) 张儿童票相当于 \(5+\tfrac12\cdot4=7\) 张成人票，因此一张成人票价格为 \(\dfrac{24.50}{7}=\$3.50\)。

第二次购买相当于 \(8+\tfrac12\cdot6=11\) 张成人票，费用为 \(11\cdot 3.50=\$38.50\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Since a child ticket is half an adult ticket, \(5\) adult and \(4\) child tickets equal \(5+\tfrac12\cdot4=7\) adult tickets, so one adult ticket costs \(\dfrac{24.50}{7}=\$3.50.\)

The second purchase equals \(8+\tfrac12\cdot6=11\) adult tickets, costing \(11\cdot 3.50=\$38.50.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

3单选

Ralph 沿 Jane 街走，依次经过四栋房子，每栋房子颜色不同。他先经过橙色房子，再经过红色房子；并且先经过蓝色房子，再经过黄色房子。蓝色房子不与黄色房子相邻。彩色房子的排列顺序有多少种可能？

English（选项同上方）

Walking down Jane Street, Ralph passed four houses in a row, each painted a different color. He passed the orange house before the red house, and he passed the blue house before the yellow house. The blue house was not next to the yellow house. How many orderings of the colored houses are possible?

若橙色先出现，则蓝色和黄色不能相邻，迫使顺序为橙、蓝、红、黄。

若蓝色先出现，则黄色可以在第三或第四个位置（不能在第二个位置以避免相邻），得到蓝、橙、黄、红和蓝、橙、红、黄。

这就是全部 \(3\) 种有效顺序。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

If orange comes first, then blue and yellow cannot be adjacent, forcing the order orange, blue, red, yellow.

If blue comes first, yellow can be in the third or fourth position (never second, to avoid adjacency), giving blue, orange, yellow, red and blue, orange, red, yellow.

These are the only \(3\) valid orderings.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

4单选

假设 \(a\) 头奶牛在 \(c\) 天内产 \(b\) 加仑牛奶。按这个速率，\(d\) 头奶牛在 \(e\) 天内会产多少加仑牛奶？

English（选项同上方）

Suppose that \(a\) cows give \(b\) gallons of milk in \(c\) days. At this rate, how many gallons of milk will \(d\) cows give in \(e\) days?

产奶速率为每头奶牛每天 \(\dfrac{b}{ac}\) 加仑。

所以 \(d\) 头奶牛在 \(e\) 天内产 \(\dfrac{b}{ac}\cdot d\cdot e=\dfrac{bde}{ac}\) 加仑。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The rate is \(\dfrac{b}{ac}\) gallons per cow per day.

So \(d\) cows over \(e\) days produce \(\dfrac{b}{ac}\cdot d\cdot e=\dfrac{bde}{ac}\) gallons.

Thus, the correct answer is A.

5单选

一次代数小测中，\(10\%\) 的学生得 \(70\) 分，\(35\%\) 得 \(80\) 分，\(30\%\) 得 \(90\) 分，其余得 \(100\) 分。本次小测成绩的平均数与中位数之差是多少？

English（选项同上方）

On an algebra quiz, \(10\%\) of the students scored \(70\) points, \(35\%\) scored \(80\) points, \(30\%\) scored \(90\) points, and the rest scored \(100\) points. What is the difference between the mean and the median of the students’ scores on this quiz?

其余 \(25\%\) 的学生得 \(100\) 分。由于 \(45\%\) 的学生得分不超过 \(80\)，而 \(75\%\) 的学生得分不超过 \(90\)，所以中位数是 \(90\)。

平均数为 \[ \begin{aligned} &0.10(70)+0.35(80)\\ &\quad{}+0.30(90)+0.25(100)\\ &\quad{}=7+28+27+25=87 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

两者之差为 \(90-87=3\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The remaining \(25\%\) scored \(100.\) Since \(45\%\) scored at most \(80\) and \(75\%\) scored at most \(90,\) the median is \(90.\)

The mean is \[ \begin{aligned} &0.10(70)+0.35(80)\\ &\quad{}+0.30(90)+0.25(100)\\ &\quad{}=7+28+27+25=87. \end{aligned} \]

The difference is \(90-87=3.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

6单选

一个两位数与把它的数字倒序得到的数之差，是这两个数任一者数字和的 \(5\) 倍。这个两位数与其倒序数之和是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The difference between a two-digit number and the number obtained by reversing its digits is \(5\) times the sum of the digits of either number. What is the sum of the two-digit number and its reverse?

设较大的数为 \(10a+b\)，则 \[ \begin{aligned} &(10a+b)-(10b+a)=9(a-b)\\ &\quad{}=5(a+b)\text{，} \end{aligned} \] 化简得 \(2a=7b\)。

满足条件的非零数字只有 \(a=7\)、\(b=2\)，所以这个数是 \(72\)，倒序数是 \(27\)。

两数之和为 \(72+27=99\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Let the larger number be \(10a+b.\) Then \[ \begin{aligned} &(10a+b)-(10b+a)=9(a-b)\\ &\quad{}=5(a+b), \end{aligned} \] which simplifies to \(2a=7b.\)

The only nonzero digits satisfying this are \(a=7\) and \(b=2,\) so the number is \(72\) and its reverse is \(27.\)

Their sum is \(72+27=99.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

7单选

一个等比数列的前三项为 \(\sqrt{3}\)、\(\sqrt[3]{3}\) 和 \(\sqrt[6]{3}\)。第四项是什么？

English（选项同上方）

The first three terms of a geometric progression are \(\sqrt{3},\) \(\sqrt[3]{3},\) and \(\sqrt[6]{3}.\) What is the fourth term?

把各项写成 \(3\) 的幂，前三项为 \(3^{\frac{1}{2}}\)、\(3^{\frac{1}{3}}\)、\(3^{\frac{1}{6}}\)。公比是 \(\dfrac{3^{\frac{1}{3}}}{3^{\frac{1}{2}}}=3^{-\frac{1}{6}}\)。

第四项为 \(3^{\frac{1}{6}}\cdot3^{-\frac{1}{6}}=3^{0}=1\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Writing the terms as powers of \(3,\) they are \(3^{\frac{1}{2}},\) \(3^{\frac{1}{3}},\) \(3^{\frac{1}{6}}.\) The common ratio is \(\dfrac{3^{\frac{1}{3}}}{3^{\frac{1}{2}}}=3^{-\frac{1}{6}}.\)

The fourth term is \(3^{\frac{1}{6}}\cdot3^{-\frac{1}{6}}=3^{0}=1.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

8单选

一位顾客打算购买一件电器，有三张优惠券，但只能使用其中一张：

优惠券 \(1\)：标价优惠 \(10\%\)，但标价须至少为 \(\$50\)。

优惠券 \(2\)：标价减 \(\$20\)，但标价须至少为 \(\$100\)。

优惠券 \(3\)：标价超过 \(\$100\) 的部分优惠 \(18\%\)。

对下列哪个标价，优惠券 \(1\) 的降价幅度会同时大于优惠券 \(2\) 和优惠券 \(3\)？

English（选项同上方）

A customer who intends to purchase an appliance has three coupons, only one of which may be used:

Coupon \(1:\) \(10\%\) off the listed price if the listed price is at least \(\$50\)

Coupon \(2:\) \(\$20\) off the listed price if the listed price is at least \(\$100\)

Coupon \(3:\) \(18\%\) off the amount by which the listed price exceeds \(\$100\)

For which of the following listed prices will coupon \(1\) offer a greater price reduction than either coupon \(2\) or coupon \(3?\)

当价格 \(P\gt100\) 时，三张优惠券的降价金额分别为 \(\dfrac{P}{10}\)、\(20\) 和 \(\dfrac{18}{100}(P-100)\)。

优惠券 \(1\) 优于优惠券 \(2\) 需要 \(\dfrac{P}{10}\gt20\)，即 \(P\gt200\)。优惠券 \(1\) 优于优惠券 \(3\) 需要 \(\dfrac{P}{10}\gt\dfrac{18}{100}(P-100)\)，即 \(P\lt225\)。

所列价格中，只有 \(\$219.95\) 落在 \((200,225)\) 内。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

For a price \(P\gt100,\) the reductions are \(\dfrac{P}{10},\) \(20,\) and \(\dfrac{18}{100}(P-100).\)

Coupon \(1\) beats coupon \(2\) when \(\dfrac{P}{10}\gt20,\) that is \(P\gt200.\) Coupon \(1\) beats coupon \(3\) when \(\dfrac{P}{10}\gt\dfrac{18}{100}(P-100),\) that is \(P\lt225.\)

The only listed price in \((200,225)\) is \(\$219.95.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

9单选

从 \(a\) 开始的五个正连续整数的平均数为 \(b\)。从 \(b\) 开始的 \(5\) 个连续整数的平均数是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Five positive consecutive integers starting with \(a\) have average \(b.\) What is the average of \(5\) consecutive integers that start with \(b?\)

整数 \(a,a+1,a+2,a+3,a+4\) 的平均数为 \(a+2\)，所以 \(b=a+2\)。

从 \(b\) 开始的五个整数平均数为 \(b+2=(a+2)+2=a+4\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The integers \(a,a+1,a+2,a+3,a+4\) have average \(a+2,\) so \(b=a+2.\)

The integers starting at \(b\) have average \(b+2=(a+2)+2=a+4.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

10单选

在边长为 \(1\) 的等边三角形的三条边上，分别以这些边为底作三个全等的等腰三角形。这三个等腰三角形的面积和等于原等边三角形的面积。每个等腰三角形的一条腰长是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Three congruent isosceles triangles are constructed with their bases on the sides of an equilateral triangle of side length \(1.\) The sum of the areas of the three isosceles triangles is the same as the area of the equilateral triangle. What is the length of one of the two congruent sides of one of the isosceles triangles?

等边三角形的面积为 \(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}\)。每个等腰三角形的底边为 \(1\)，设其高为 \(h\)，则 \(3\cdot\dfrac12 h=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}\)，所以 \(h=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{6}\)。

一条腰是从顶点到一个底边端点的斜边，因此其长度为 \[ \begin{gathered} \sqrt{\left(\dfrac12\right)^2+\left(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{6}\right)^2}\\ =\sqrt{\dfrac14+\dfrac{1}{12}}\\ =\sqrt{\dfrac13}=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3} \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The equilateral triangle has area \(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}.\) Each isosceles triangle has base \(1\) and height \(h,\) so \(3\cdot\dfrac12 h=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4},\) giving \(h=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{6}.\)

A congruent side is the hypotenuse from the apex to a base endpoint: \[ \begin{gathered} \sqrt{\left(\dfrac12\right)^2+\left(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{6}\right)^2}\\ =\sqrt{\dfrac14+\dfrac{1}{12}}\\ =\sqrt{\dfrac13}=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3}. \end{gathered} \]

Thus, the correct answer is B.

11单选

David 从家开车去机场赶飞机。他第一小时开了 \(35\) 英里，但发现如果继续保持这个速度会迟到 \(1\) 小时。于是他在剩余路程中把速度提高了 \(15\) 英里每小时，结果提前 \(30\) 分钟到达。机场离他家多少英里？

English（选项同上方）

David drives from his home to the airport to catch a flight. He drives \(35\) miles in the first hour, but realizes that he will be \(1\) hour late if he continues at this speed. He increases his speed by \(15\) miles per hour for the rest of the way to the airport and arrives \(30\) minutes early. How many miles is the airport from his home?

设第一小时后的剩余距离为 \(d\)，距离航班还有 \(t\) 小时。若继续以 \(35\) 英里每小时行驶，他会迟到一小时，所以 \(d=35(t+1)\)。改以 \(50\) 英里每小时行驶，他会提前半小时，所以 \(d=50\left(t-\tfrac12\right)\)。

令两式相等，得到 \(35t+35=50t-25\)，所以 \(t=4\)，且 \(d=175\)。

总路程为 \(175+35=210\) 英里。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Let \(d\) be the remaining distance after one hour and \(t\) the remaining time until the flight. At \(35\) mph he would be an hour late, so \(d=35(t+1).\) At \(50\) mph he is half an hour early, so \(d=50\left(t-\tfrac12\right).\)

Setting these equal gives \(35t+35=50t-25,\) so \(t=4\) and \(d=175.\)

The total distance is \(175+35=210\) miles.

Thus, the correct answer is C.

12单选

两个圆交于点 \(A\) 和 \(B\)。小弧 \(AB\) 在其中一个圆上为 \(30^\circ\)，在另一个圆上为 \(60^\circ\)。大圆面积与小圆面积之比是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Two circles intersect at points \(A\) and \(B.\) The minor arcs \(AB\) measure \(30^\circ\) on one circle and \(60^\circ\) on the other circle. What is the ratio of the area of the larger circle to the area of the smaller circle?

设对应 \(30^\circ\) 弧的圆半径为 \(R\)，对应 \(60^\circ\) 弧的圆半径为 \(r\)。公共弦的长度满足 \(2R\sin15^\circ=2r\sin30^\circ\)，所以 \(\dfrac{R}{r}=\dfrac{\sin30^\circ}{\sin15^\circ}\)。

较小的圆心角对应较大的半径，因此 \(R\gt r\)。所求面积比为 \[ \begin{gathered} \left(\dfrac{R}{r}\right)^2\\ =\dfrac{1}{4\sin^2 15^\circ}\\ =\dfrac{1}{2(1-\cos30^\circ)}\\ =\dfrac{1}{2-\sqrt3}=2+\sqrt3 \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Let the circles have radii \(R\) (with the \(30^\circ\) arc) and \(r\) (with the \(60^\circ\) arc). The common chord has length \(2R\sin15^\circ=2r\sin30^\circ,\) so \(\dfrac{R}{r}=\dfrac{\sin30^\circ}{\sin15^\circ}.\)

The smaller central angle gives the larger radius, so \(R\gt r.\) The area ratio is \[ \begin{gathered} \left(\dfrac{R}{r}\right)^2\\ =\dfrac{1}{4\sin^2 15^\circ}\\ =\dfrac{1}{2(1-\cos30^\circ)}\\ =\dfrac{1}{2-\sqrt3}=2+\sqrt3. \end{gathered} \]

Thus, the correct answer is D.

13单选

一家精致的住宿加早餐旅馆有 \(5\) 个房间，每个房间都有独特的颜色主题装饰。某天 \(5\) 位朋友来过夜，当晚没有其他客人。这些朋友可以按任何组合住房，但每个房间最多住 \(2\) 人。店主有多少种方式把客人分配到房间？

English（选项同上方）

A fancy bed and breakfast inn has \(5\) rooms, each with a distinctive color-coded decor. One day \(5\) friends arrive to spend the night. There are no other guests that night. The friends can room in any combination they wish, but with no more than \(2\) friends per room. In how many ways can the innkeeper assign the guests to the rooms?

全是单人：把 \(5\) 位朋友分到 \(5\) 个房间有 \(5!=120\) 种。

一对同住：选这对有 \(\binom52=10\) 种，再把 \(4\) 个组放进房间有 \(5\cdot4\cdot3\cdot2=120\) 种，共 \(10\cdot120=1200\) 种。

两对同住：选出单独住的人有 \(5\) 种，剩余四人分成两对有 \(3\) 种，因此共有 \(15\) 种分组方式。再把这 \(3\) 组安排进房间，有 \(5\cdot4\cdot3=60\) 种方式，共 \(15\cdot60=900\) 种。

总数为 \(120+1200+900=2220\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

All singles: assign \(5\) friends to \(5\) rooms in \(5!=120\) ways.

One pair: choose the pair in \(\binom52=10\) ways, then place the \(4\) groups into rooms in \(5\cdot4\cdot3\cdot2=120\) ways, giving \(10\cdot120=1200.\)

Two pairs: choose the solo friend in \(5\) ways and split the rest into two pairs in \(3\) ways (\(15\) groupings), then place the \(3\) groups into rooms in \(5\cdot4\cdot3=60\) ways, giving \(15\cdot60=900.\)

The total is \(120+1200+900=2220.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

14单选

设 \(a\lt b\lt c\) 为三个整数，使得 \(a\)，\(b\)，\(c\) 成等差数列，而 \(a\)，\(c\)，\(b\) 成等比数列。\(c\) 的最小可能值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Let \(a\lt b\lt c\) be three integers such that \(a,\) \(b,\) \(c\) is an arithmetic progression and \(a,\) \(c,\) \(b\) is a geometric progression. What is the smallest possible value for \(c?\)

设 \(d=b-a\gt0\)，则 \(b=a+d\)，\(c=a+2d\)。因为 \(a,c,b\) 成等比数列， \[ \begin{gathered} \dfrac{c}{a}=\dfrac{b}{c}\\ \implies(a+2d)^2=a(a+d)\text{，} \end{gathered} \] 化简得 \(3ad+4d^2=0\)，所以 \(3a+4d=0\)。

于是 \(a=-4k\)、\(d=3k\)，其中 \(k\) 为正整数，因此 \(c=a+2d=2k\)。最小值为 \(c=2\)，此时 \(a=-4\)、\(b=-1\)、\(c=2\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Let \(d=b-a\gt0,\) so \(b=a+d\) and \(c=a+2d.\) Since \(a,c,b\) is geometric, \[ \begin{gathered} \dfrac{c}{a}=\dfrac{b}{c}\\ \implies(a+2d)^2=a(a+d), \end{gathered} \] which simplifies to \(3ad+4d^2=0,\) so \(3a+4d=0.\)

Then \(a=-4k\) and \(d=3k\) for a positive integer \(k,\) giving \(c=a+2d=2k.\) The smallest value is \(c=2\) (with \(a=-4,\) \(b=-1,\) \(c=2\)).

Thus, the correct answer is C.

15单选

五位回文数是形如 \(abcba\) 的正整数，其中 \(a\) 不为零。设 \(S\) 为所有五位回文数之和。\(S\) 的各位数字之和是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A five-digit palindrome is a positive integer with respective digits \(abcba,\) where \(a\) is not zero. Let \(S\) be the sum of all five-digit palindromes. What is the sum of the digits of \(S?\)

写 \(\overline{abcba}=10001a+1010b+100c\)。对所有回文数求和时，每个 \(a\in\{1,\dots,9\}\) 与 \(10\cdot10=100\) 种 \(b,c\) 搭配；每个 \(b\) 或 \(c\) 的值与另外两位有 \(9\cdot10=90\) 种搭配。

利用 \(\sum a=\sum b=\sum c=45\)，\[ \begin{gathered} \scriptsize S=45\big(10001\cdot100+1010\cdot90+100\cdot90\big)\\ =45\cdot1{,}100{,}000\\ =49{,}500{,}000 \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

\(S\) 的各位数字之和为 \(4+9+5=18\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Write \(\overline{abcba}=10001a+1010b+100c.\) Summing over all palindromes, each value of \(a\in\{1,\dots,9\}\) occurs with \(10\cdot10=100\) choices of \(b,c,\) and each value of \(b\) or \(c\) occurs with \(9\cdot10=90\) choices of the other two digits.

Using \(\sum a=\sum b=\sum c=45,\) \[ \begin{gathered} \scriptsize S=45\big(10001\cdot100+1010\cdot90+100\cdot90\big)\\ =45\cdot1{,}100{,}000\\ =49{,}500{,}000. \end{gathered} \]

The sum of the digits of \(S\) is \(4+9+5=18.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

16单选

在乘积 \((8)(888\ldots8)\) 中，第二个因数有 \(k\) 位数字。该乘积是一个各位数字之和为 \(1000\) 的整数。\(k\) 是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The product \((8)(888\ldots8),\) where the second factor has \(k\) digits, is an integer whose digits have a sum of \(1000.\) What is \(k?\)

按位乘法并处理进位可得 \(8\cdot\underbrace{88\ldots8}_{k}=7\underbrace{1\ldots1}_{k-2}04\)，其中有 \(k-2\) 个数字一。

各位数字之和为 \(7+(k-2)+0+4=k+9\)。令 \(k+9=1000\)，得到 \(k=991\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

By carrying out the multiplication, \(8\cdot\underbrace{88\ldots8}_{k}=7\underbrace{1\ldots1}_{k-2}04,\) which has \(k-2\) ones.

The digit sum is \(7+(k-2)+0+4=k+9.\) Setting \(k+9=1000\) gives \(k=991.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

一个 \(4\times4\times h\) 的长方体盒子内装有一个半径为 \(2\) 的球和八个半径为 \(1\) 的小球。每个小球都与盒子的三个面相切，大球与每个小球相切。\(h\) 是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A \(4\times4\times h\) rectangular box contains a sphere of radius \(2\) and eight smaller spheres of radius \(1.\) The smaller spheres are each tangent to three sides of the box, and the larger sphere is tangent to each of the smaller spheres. What is \(h?\)

把盒子的一个角放在原点。每个小球位于一个角落，其球心到相邻三个面各相距 \(1\)。上方四个小球的球心构成边长为 \(2\) 的正方形，正方形中心在盒子的中轴线上，一个顶点到中心的距离为 \(\sqrt2\)。

大球球心在中轴线上，到每个上方小球球心的距离为 \(2+1=3\)，因此两者的竖直距离为 \(\sqrt{3^2-\sqrt2^2}=\sqrt7\)。

大球球心的高度是 \(\dfrac h2\)，上方小球球心的高度是 \(h-1\)，所以 \((h-1)-\dfrac h2=\sqrt7\)。由此 \(\dfrac h2=1+\sqrt7\)，并得到 \(h=2+2\sqrt7\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Place the box with a corner at the origin. Each small sphere sits in a corner with center \(1\) unit from three faces. The four top small-sphere centers form a square of side \(2,\) whose center lies on the box axis; a corner of that square is \(\sqrt2\) from the center.

The big sphere’s center is on the axis, at distance \(2+1=3\) from each top small center. The vertical gap between them is \(\sqrt{3^2-\sqrt2^2}=\sqrt7.\)

The big center is at height \(\dfrac h2\) and the top small centers at height \(h-1,\) so \((h-1)-\dfrac h2=\sqrt7,\) giving \(\dfrac h2=1+\sqrt7\) and \(h=2+2\sqrt7.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

English

Since the base \(\tfrac14\lt1,\) this means \(0\lt\log_{16}\!\left(\log_{\frac{1}{16}}x\right)\lt\tfrac14,\) hence \(1\lt\log_{\frac{1}{16}}x\lt16^{\frac{1}{4}}=2.\)

As \(\tfrac{1}{16}\lt1,\) this reverses to \(\left(\tfrac{1}{16}\right)^2\lt x\lt\left(\tfrac{1}{16}\right)^1,\) i.e. \(\tfrac{1}{256}\lt x\lt\tfrac{1}{16}.\) The length is \(\tfrac{1}{16}-\tfrac{1}{256}=\tfrac{15}{256},\) so \(m+n=15+256=271.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

There are exactly \(N\) distinct rational numbers \(k\) such that \(|k|\lt200\) and \[5x^2+kx+12=0\] has at least one integer solution for \(x.\) What is \(N?\)

English

If an integer \(x\) is a root, then \(k=-\left(5x+\dfrac{12}{x}\right),\) so \(x\ne0.\) For \(x\ge2,\) \(|k|=5|x|+\dfrac{12}{|x|}\) increases, and \(|x|=39\) gives \(|k|\approx195.3\lt200,\) while \(|x|=40\) gives \(|k|\gt200.\)

Thus \(x\) ranges over \(\pm1,\pm2,\dots,\pm39,\) which is \(78\) values. If two integers \(a\ne b\) gave the same \(k,\) then \(5a+\tfrac{12}{a}=5b+\tfrac{12}{b}\) forces \(5ab=12,\) which has no integer solutions, so all \(78\) values of \(k\) are distinct.

在 \(\triangle BAC\) 中，\(\angle BAC=40^\circ\)，\(AB=10\)，\(AC=6\)。点 \(D\) 和 \(E\) 分别在 \(\overline{AB}\) 和 \(\overline{AC}\) 上。\(BE+DE+CD\) 的最小可能值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

In \(\triangle BAC,\) \(\angle BAC=40^\circ,\) \(AB=10,\) and \(AC=6.\) Points \(D\) and \(E\) lie on \(\overline{AB}\) and \(\overline{AC},\) respectively. What is the minimum possible value of \(BE+DE+CD?\)

将 \(B\) 关于直线 \(AC\) 反射到 \(B'\)，将 \(C\) 关于直线 \(AB\) 反射到 \(C'\)。于是 \(BE=B'E\)、\(CD=C'D\)，从而 \(BE+DE+CD\) \(=B'E+ED+DC'\)，这是从 \(B'\) 到 \(C'\) 的一条折线路径。

当这条路径成为直线段 \(B'C'\) 时长度最小。此时 \(AB'=AB=10\)、\(AC'=AC=6\)，并且 \(\angle B'AC'=3\cdot40^\circ=120^\circ\)。

由余弦定理， \[ \begin{gathered} B'C'^2=10^2+6^2\\ {}-2\cdot10\cdot6\cos120^\circ\\ =136+60\\ =196\text{，} \end{gathered} \] 所以 \(B'C'=14\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Reflect \(B\) across line \(AC\) to get \(B',\) and reflect \(C\) across line \(AB\) to get \(C'.\) Then \(BE=B'E\) and \(CD=C'D,\) so \(BE+DE+CD\) \(=B'E+ED+DC',\) a broken path from \(B'\) to \(C'.\)

This is minimized when the path is the straight segment \(B'C'.\) We have \(AB'=AB=10,\) \(AC'=AC=6,\) and \(\angle B'AC'=3\cdot40^\circ=120^\circ.\)

By the Law of Cosines, \[ \begin{gathered} B'C'^2=10^2+6^2\\ {}-2\cdot10\cdot6\cos120^\circ\\ =136+60\\ =196, \end{gathered} \] so \(B'C'=14.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

English（选项同上方）

For every real number \(x,\) let \(\lfloor x\rfloor\) denote the greatest integer not exceeding \(x,\) and let \[f(x)=\lfloor x\rfloor\left(2014^{\,x-\lfloor x\rfloor}-1\right).\] The set of all numbers \(x\) such that \(1\le x\lt2014\) and \(f(x)\le1\) is a union of disjoint intervals. English

The number \(5^{867}\) is between \(2^{2013}\) and \(2^{2014}.\) How many pairs of integers \((m,n)\) are there such that \(1\le m\le2012\) and \[5^n\lt2^m\lt2^{m+2}\lt5^{n+1}?\]

English

于是 \(|4x+3y|=|50u+25|\le1000\) 等价于 \(|2u+1|\le39\)，即 \(-20\le u\le19\)。因此共有 \(40\) 个整数坐标点。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Thus, the correct answer is B.
