若 \(b\) 名工人用 \(c\) 天砌好 \(f\) 块砖，那么按相同速度，\(c\) 名工人砌好 \(b\) 块砖所需的天数是：

English（选项同上方）

If \(b\) men take \(c\) days to lay \(f\) bricks, then the number of days it will take \(c\) men working at the same rate to lay \(b\) bricks is:

每个工日的效率是 \(\frac{f}{bc}\) 块砖。因此，\(c\) 名工人每天砌 \(\frac{f}{b}\) 块砖，所以砌好 \(b\) 块砖需要 \[ \frac{b}{\frac{f}{b}}=\frac{b^2}{f} \] 天。

因此，正确答案为 D。

English

The rate is \(\frac{f}{bc}\) bricks per man-day. Thus \(c\) men lay \(\frac{f}{b}\) bricks per day, so laying \(b\) bricks takes \[ \frac{b}{\frac{f}{b}}=\frac{b^2}{f} \] days.

Therefore, the correct answer is D.

6单选

设符号 \(*\) 表示非零实数集合 \(S\) 上如下定义的二元运算：对任意 \(a\)、\(b\in S\)，都有 \(a*b=2ab\)。下列哪一项不正确？

English（选项同上方）

Let \(*\) be a symbol denoting the binary operation on the set \(S\) of all nonzero real numbers as follows: for any \(a,\) \(b\in S,\) \(a*b=2ab.\) Which statement is not true?

该运算满足交换律，并且 \[ (a*b)*c=4abc=a*(b*c)\text{，} \] 所以它也满足结合律。其单位元为 \(\frac{1}{2}\)。\(a\) 的逆元必须满足 \(2ax=\frac{1}{2}\)，故逆元为 \(x=\frac{1}{4a}\)，而不是 \(\frac{1}{2a}\)。

因此，正确答案为 E。

English

The operation is commutative, and \[ (a*b)*c=4abc=a*(b*c), \] so it is associative. Its identity is \(\frac{1}{2}.\) The inverse of \(a\) must satisfy \(2ax=\frac{1}{2},\) so it is \(x=\frac{1}{4a},\) not \(\frac{1}{2a}.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is E.

一条不交叉且没有松弛的皮带绕在半径分别为 \(14\) 英寸和 \(4\) 英寸的两个圆形皮带轮上。若皮带与两皮带轮的两个切点之间相距 \(24\) 英寸，则两皮带轮圆心之间的距离（英寸）为：

English（选项同上方）

An uncrossed belt is fitted without slack around two circular pulleys with radii of \(14\) inches and \(4\) inches. If the distance between the points of contact of the belt with the pulleys is \(24\) inches, then the distance between the centers of the pulleys in inches is:

公外切线与连接圆心和切点的两条半径构成一个直角三角形，其两条直角边长为 \(24\) 和 \(14-4=10\)。因此圆心距为 \[ \sqrt{24^2+10^2}=\sqrt{676}=26\text{。} \]

English

The common external tangent and the radii to its contact points form a right triangle whose legs are \(24\) and \(14-4=10.\) Thus the center distance is \[ \sqrt{24^2+10^2}=\sqrt{676}=26. \]

Therefore, the correct answer is D.

\(a\) 进制数 \(47\) 与 \(b\) 进制数 \(74\) 表示同一个数。假设两个进制的基数都是正整数，则 \(a+b\) 的最小可能值写成罗马数字是：

English（选项同上方）

The numeral \(47\) in base \(a\) represents the same number as \(74\) in base \(b.\) Assuming both bases are positive integers, the least possible value of \(a+b,\) written as a Roman numeral, is:

等式为 \(4a+7=7b+4\)，即 \(4a-7b=-3\)。由于两个数码都必须有效，所以 \(a,b\gt7\)。模 \(7\) 化简得 \(a\equiv1\pmod7\)。第一个有效值是 \(a=15\)，对应 \(b=9\)。因此 \(a+b=24\)，写成罗马数字为 \(\mathrm{XXIV}\)。

因此，正确答案为 D。

English

The equality is \(4a+7=7b+4,\) or \(4a-7b=-3.\) Since both digits must be valid, \(a,b\gt7.\) Reducing modulo \(7\) gives \(a\equiv1\pmod7.\) The first valid value is \(a=15,\) which gives \(b=9.\) Thus \(a+b=24,\) written \(\mathrm{XXIV}.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is D.

一个放在水平桌面上的长方体水族箱宽 \(10\) 英寸、高 \(8\) 英寸。将水族箱倾斜时，水恰好覆盖一个 \(8\) 英寸乘 \(10\) 英寸的端面，而只覆盖长方形底面的四分之三。将底面重新放平后，水深为：

English（选项同上方）

An aquarium on a level table has rectangular faces and is \(10\) inches wide and \(8\) inches high. When it was tilted, the water in it just covered an \(8\)-inch by \(10\)-inch end but only three-fourths of the rectangular bottom. The depth of the water when the bottom was again made level was:

设水族箱长为 \(L\)，水平放置时的水深为 \(h\)。倾斜时，水的纵向截面是一个底为 \(\frac{3L}{4}\)、高为 \(8\) 的三角形。因此 \[ 10\left(\frac12\cdot\frac{3L}{4}\cdot8\right)=10Lh\text{，} \] 所以 \(h=3\) 英寸。

English

Let the aquarium length be \(L\) and the level-water depth be \(h.\) In the tilted position the longitudinal cross-section of the water is a triangle with base \(\frac{3L}{4}\) and height \(8.\) Therefore \[ 10\left(\frac12\cdot\frac{3L}{4}\cdot8\right)=10Lh, \] so \(h=3\) inches.

Therefore, the correct answer is B.

17单选

用 \(2n\) 条等间隔的半径 \((n\gt0)\) 和一条割线分割一个圆盘。该圆盘最多能被分成多少个互不重叠的区域：

English（选项同上方）

A circular disk is divided by \(2n\) equally spaced radii \((n\gt0)\) and one secant line. The maximum number of nonoverlapping areas into which the disk can be divided is:

这些半径先形成 \(2n\) 个扇形。一条不过圆心的割线在 \(n\) 对反向半径中的每一对里至多与一条相交，因此至多与 \(n\) 条半径相交。这些交点把圆内的割线弦段分成 \(n+1\) 段，每段都会增加一个区域。因此最大区域数为 \(2n+(n+1)=3n+1\)。

因此，正确答案为 E。

English

The radii first make \(2n\) sectors. A secant not through the center can meet at most one radius in each of \(n\) opposite pairs, hence at most \(n\) radii. Those intersections divide the secant chord into \(n+1\) pieces, each of which adds one region. The maximum is therefore \(2n+(n+1)=3n+1.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is E.

Therefore, the correct answer is C.

20单选

方程 \(x^2+2hx=3\) 的两根平方和为 \(10\)。\(h\) 的绝对值等于：

The sum of the squares of the roots of the equation \(x^2+2hx=3\) is \(10.\) The absolute value of \(h\) is equal to:

Therefore, the correct answer is E.
