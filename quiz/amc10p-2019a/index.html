1单选

求下式的值：\[2^{\left(0^{\left(1^9\right)}\right)}+\left(\left(2^0\right)^1\right)^9\text{？}\]

English（选项同上方）

What is the value of \[2^{\left(0^{\left(1^9\right)}\right)}+\left(\left(2^0\right)^1\right)^9?\]

可以从每座指数塔的最上层往下逐步计算： \[ \begin{aligned} &2^{\left(0^{\left(1^9\right)}\right)}\\ &\quad+\left(\left(2^0\right)^1\right)^9\\ &=2^{0^1}+(1^1)^9\\ &=2^0+1^9\\ &=2 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

We can evaluate from the top of each exponent tower downward: \[ \begin{aligned} &2^{\left(0^{\left(1^9\right)}\right)}\\ &\quad+\left(\left(2^0\right)^1\right)^9\\ &=2^{0^1}+(1^1)^9\\ &=2^0+1^9\\ &=2. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, C is the correct answer.

2单选

\((20!-15!)\) 的百位数字是多少？

English（选项同上方）

What is the hundreds digit of \((20!-15!)?\)

\(20!\) 和 \(15!\) 都至少含有三个因数 \(5\) 和三个因数 \(2\)，所以两者都能被 \(2^3\cdot5^3=1000\) 整除。

因此它们的差也能被 \(1000\) 整除。

一个数是 \(1000\) 的倍数，说明末三位都是 \(0\)，所以百位数字也是 \(0\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Both \(20!\) and \(15!\) contain at least three factors of \(5\) and three factors of \(2\), so both are divisible by \(2^3\cdot5^3=1000.\)

Their difference is therefore also divisible by \(1000.\)

Being a multiple of \(1000\) makes the last three digits \(0,\) which shows that the hundreds digit is also \(0.\)

Thus, A is the correct answer.

3单选

Ana 和 Bonita 的生日是同月同日，但出生年份相差 \(n\) 年。去年 Ana 的年龄是 Bonita 的 \(5\) 倍。今年 Ana 的年龄是 Bonita 年龄的平方。求 \(n\)。

English（选项同上方）

Ana and Bonita were born on the same date in different years, \(n\) years apart. Last year Ana was \(5\) times as old as Bonita. This year Ana’s age is the square of Bonita’s age. What is \(n?\)

设 Ana 今年 \(a\) 岁，Bonita 今年 \(b\) 岁。于是 \[ \begin{aligned} a-1&=5(b-1),\\ a&=b^2 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

代入可得 \[ \begin{aligned} b^2-1&=5b-5,\\ b^2-5b+4&=0,\\ (b-4)(b-1)&=0 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

可以看出 \(b \neq 1\)，因为那会使 Ana 和 Bonita 同龄，所以 \(b = 4\)。

于是 \(a = 4^2 = 16\)，而年龄差 \(n = 16 - 4 = 12\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Let \(a\) be Ana’s current age and \(b\) be Bonita’s current age. Then \[ \begin{aligned} a-1&=5(b-1),\\ a&=b^2. \end{aligned} \]

Substitution gives \[ \begin{aligned} b^2-1&=5b-5,\\ b^2-5b+4&=0,\\ (b-4)(b-1)&=0. \end{aligned} \]

We can see that \(b \neq 1\) since that would make Ana and Bonita the same age, so we know that \(b = 4.\)

This gives us that \(a = 4^2 = 16\) and \(n = 16 - 4 = 12.\)

Thus, D is the correct answer.

4单选

一个盒子里有 \(28\) 个红球、\(20\) 个绿球、\(19\) 个黄球、\(13\) 个蓝球、\(11\) 个白球和 \(9\) 个黑球。从盒中不放回地取球，最少要取多少个球，才能保证至少取到 \(15\) 个同色球？

English（选项同上方）

A box contains \(28\) red balls, \(20\) green balls, \(19\) yellow balls, \(13\) blue balls, \(11\) white balls, and \(9\) black balls. What is the minimum number of balls that must be drawn from the box without replacement to guarantee that at least \(15\) balls of a single color will be drawn?

在还不能保证有 \(15\) 个同色球的最坏情况下，可以取出所有黑球、白球、蓝球，以及红、绿、黄各 \(14\) 个。

此时红、绿、黄三种颜色都只有 \(14\) 个，而其余三种颜色本来就少于十五个，所以仍可能没有任何颜色达到十五个。

总数为 \[ 9 + 11 + 13 + 3 \cdot 14 \] \[= 33 + 42 \] \[= 75\text{。} \] 再多取一个球，就必定使某种颜色达到 \(15\) 个，因此答案为 \(75 + 1 = 76\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Note that we can pull as many as \(14\) balls of each color without ensuring that \(15\) balls of one color are drawn.

This means that we can draw all of the black, white and blue balls, along with \(14\) red, green, and yellow balls.

This gives us a total of \[ 9 + 11 + 13 + 3 \cdot 14 \]\[= 33 + 42 \]\[= 75. \] We need to add one at the end, however, to ensure that we get that \(15\)th ball of some color, \(75 + 1 = 76.\)

Thus, B is the correct answer.

5单选

和为 \(45\) 的连续整数最多可以有多少个？

English（选项同上方）

What is the greatest number of consecutive integers whose sum is \(45?\)

设连续整数共有 \(k\) 项，首项为 \(a\)。它们的和为 \(\dfrac{k(2a+k-1)}{2}=45\)，所以 \(k\) 必须整除 \(90\)。因此项数不可能超过 \(90\)。

这个上界确实可以达到：\[ -44, -43, \cdots, 44, 45 \] 共有 \(90\) 项，其和为 \(45\)。

所以连续整数的最大项数是 \(90\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Suppose there are \(k\) consecutive integers with first term \(a.\) Their sum is \(\dfrac{k(2a+k-1)}{2}=45,\) so \(k\) must divide \(90.\) Therefore the number of terms cannot exceed \(90.\)

This bound is attained by \[ -44, -43, \cdots, 44, 45 \] which has \(90\) terms and sum \(45.\)

Thus the greatest possible number of consecutive integers is \(90.\)

Thus, D is the correct answer.

6单选

对下面多少种四边形，存在一个位于该四边形所在平面内的点，它到四个顶点的距离都相等？

• 正方形

• 非正方形的矩形

• 非正方形的菱形

• 既不是矩形也不是菱形的平行四边形

• 不是平行四边形的等腰梯形

English（选项同上方）

For how many of the following types of quadrilaterals does there exist a point in the plane of the quadrilateral that is equidistant from all four vertices of the quadrilateral?

• a square

• a rectangle that is not a square

• a rhombus that is not a square

• a parallelogram that is not a rectangle or a rhombus

• an isosceles trapezoid that is not a parallelogram

注意，如果某个点到四个顶点的距离都相等，那么这个点就是该图形外接圆的圆心。

于是问题变成：这些图形中哪些是圆内接四边形（存在外接圆）。可以使用的一个条件是：对角互补。

显然，正方形和非正方形的矩形都可以（对角都是直角，和为 \(180^{\circ}\)）。

非正方形的菱形不行，因为它的对角虽然相等，但不是 \(90^{\circ}\)。

既不是矩形也不是菱形的平行四边形面临同样的问题，所以它同样不是圆内接四边形。

不是平行四边形的等腰梯形，按定义其对角互补，所以它是圆内接四边形。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Note that if a point is equidistant from all the vertices, then that point is the center of the shape’s circumcircle.

The question then becomes which of these shapes is cyclic (has a circumcircle). One condition that we can use is that opposite angles are supplementary.

Clearly, a square and rectangle that is not a square work (opposite angles are right, adding up to \(180^{\circ}\)).

A rhombus that is not a square does not work, since opposite angles are equal, but they are not \(90^{\circ}.\)

A parallelogram that is not a rectangle or a rhombus faces the same problem as above, making it not cyclic as well.

An isosceles trapezoid that is not a parallelogram by definition has supplementary opposite angles, making it cyclic.

Thus, C is the correct answer.

7单选

两条斜率分别为 \(\frac{1}{2}\) 和 \(2\) 的直线相交于 \((2, 2)\)。这两条直线与直线 \(x + y = 10\) 围成的三角形面积是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Two lines with slopes \(\frac{1}{2}\) and \(2\) intersect at \((2, 2).\) What is the area of the triangle enclosed by these two lines and the line \(x + y = 10?\)

过 \((2,2)\) 的两条直线的方程为 \[ \begin{aligned} y&=\frac12x+1,\\ y&=2x-2 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

它们与 \(x+y=10\) 的交点分别是 \((6,4)\) 和 \((4,6)\)。

因此三角形的三个顶点是 \((2,2),(6,4)\) 和 \((4,6)\)。连接后两个顶点的线段长为 \(2\sqrt2\)，它的中点是 \((5,5)\)，而从 \((2,2)\) 到这个中点的距离为 \(3\sqrt2\)。

因此面积为 \[\frac12(2\sqrt2)(3\sqrt2)=6\text{。}\] 所以正确答案是 C。

English

The two lines through \((2,2)\) have equations \[ \begin{aligned} y&=\frac12x+1,\\ y&=2x-2. \end{aligned} \]

Their intersections with \(x+y=10\) are \((6,4)\) and \((4,6)\), respectively.

Thus the vertices are \((2,2),(6,4),\) and \((4,6)\). The segment joining the last two points has length \(2\sqrt2\), its midpoint is \((5,5)\), and the distance from \((2,2)\) to that midpoint is \(3\sqrt2\).

The area is therefore \[\frac12(2\sqrt2)(3\sqrt2)=6.\] Thus, C is the correct answer.

8单选

下图显示了一条直线 \(\ell\)，其上有由正方形和线段组成的规则无限重复图案。

除恒等变换外，在下面四类平面刚体运动中，有多少类的某个变换会把该图形变到自身？

• 绕直线 \(\ell\) 上某点旋转

• 沿平行于直线 \(\ell\) 的方向平移

• 关于直线 \(\ell\) 反射

• 关于垂直于直线 \(\ell\) 的某条直线反射

English（选项同上方）

The figure below shows line \(\ell\) with a regular, infinite, recurring pattern of squares and line segments.

How many of the following four kinds of rigid motion transformations of the plane in which this figure is drawn, other than the identity transformation, will transform this figure into itself?

• some rotation around a point of line \(\ell\)

• some translation in the direction parallel to line \(\ell\)

• the reflection across line \(\ell\)

• some reflection across a line perpendicular to line \(\ell\)

第一种变换可行：可以绕 \(\ell\) 上某点旋转 \(180^{\circ}\)，旋转中心取在一个朝上的正方形与一个朝下的正方形之间的中点。

第二种变换也可行：只要把图形沿 \(\ell\) 向右平移，直到各正方形重新对齐即可。

第三种不行，因为这样的反射会使线段方向相反。

第四种变换同样不行，因为斜线段又会指向错误的方向。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The first transformation works, as we can rotate \(\ell\) \(180^{\circ}\) around the midpoint between an upward-facing and downward-facing square.

The second also works, as we can just move \(\ell\) to the right until the squares line up with each other again.

The third fails, as a reflection would cause the line segments to face the opposite direction.

The fourth transformation also doesn’t work since the diagonal lines would again be facing in the wrong direction.

Thus, C is the correct answer.

9单选

最大的三位正整数 \(n\) 是多少，使得前 \(n\) 个正整数之和 <span style="text-decoration: underline; font-style: italic">不是</span> 前 \(n\) 个正整数之积的因子？

English（选项同上方）

What is the greatest three-digit positive integer \(n\) for which the sum of the first \(n\) positive integers is <span style="text-decoration: underline; font-style: italic">not</span> a divisor of the product of the first \(n\) positive integers?

前 \(n\) 个正整数之和为 \[\dfrac{n(n + 1)}{2}\text{。}\] 我们需要它不能整除 \(n!\)。

设 \(m=n+1\)。若 \(m\) 是合数，把它写成 \(m=ab\)，其中 \(2\le a\le b\)。当 \(a<b\) 时，两个不同的因数 \(a\) 和 \(b\) 都出现在 \((m-2)!=(n-1)!\) 中。当 \(a=b\) 时，有 \(a\ge3\)，于是两个不同的倍数 \(a\) 和 \(2a\) 都出现在 \((m-2)!\) 中，所以 \(a^2=m\) 也整除这个阶乘。因此 \((n-1)!\) 能被 \(m\) 整除，从而 \[n! \text{ 是 } \frac{n(n+1)}2 \text{ 的倍数}\text{。}\]

反之，若 \(n+1\) 是质数，这个质因子就不会出现在 \(n!\) 中，整除性不成立。由于 \(997\) 是质数，而 \(998,999\) 和 \(1000\) 都是合数，所以最大的三位数值为 \[997-1=996\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The sum of the first \(n\) numbers is \[\dfrac{n(n + 1)}{2}.\] We need this to not divide \(n!.\)

Put \(m=n+1\). If \(m\) is composite, write \(m=ab\) with \(2\le a\le b\). When \(a<b\), the distinct factors \(a\) and \(b\) both occur in \((m-2)!=(n-1)!\). When \(a=b\), we have \(a\ge3\), and the two multiples \(a\) and \(2a\) both occur in \((m-2)!\), so \(a^2=m\) divides that factorial as well. Thus \((n-1)!\) is divisible by \(m\), and consequently \[n! \text{ is divisible by } \frac{n(n+1)}2.\]

Conversely, if \(n+1\) is prime, that prime factor does not occur in \(n!\), so the divisibility fails. Since \(997\) is prime while \(998,999,\) and \(1000\) are composite, the greatest three-digit value is \[997-1=996.\]

Thus, B is the correct answer.

10单选

一个 \(10\) 英尺宽、\(17\) 英尺长的矩形地板铺有 \(170\) 块一英尺见方的方砖。一只虫子从一个角沿直线走到对角。包括第一块和最后一块方砖在内，它经过多少块方砖？

English（选项同上方）

A rectangular floor that is \(10\) feet wide and \(17\) feet long is tiled with \(170\) one-foot square tiles. A bug walks from one corner to the opposite corner in a straight line. Including the first and the last tile, how many tiles does the bug visit?

每穿过一条内部网格线，虫子就进入一块新方砖。

因此它经过的方砖数等于 \(1\)（第一块方砖）加上它穿过的网格线条数。

因为 \(10\) 与 \(17\) 互质，对角线不会经过内部格点，所以不会同时穿过一条竖线和一条横线。

它穿过 \(16\) 条水平线和 \(9\) 条竖直线，因此一共经过 \[ 1 + 16 + 9 = 26 \] 块方砖。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Note that every time the bug crosses a vertical or horizontal line, the bug visits one new tile.

This means that the number of tiles the bug visits is \(1\) (the first tile) plus the number of lines it crosses.

The bug never walks over a corner since \(10\) and \(17\) are relatively prime, so we don’t have to worry about that.

The bug crosses \(16\) horizontal lines and \(9\) vertical lines for a total of \[ 1 + 16 + 9 = 26 \] tiles.

Thus, C is the correct answer.

11单选

\(201^9\) 的正整数因子中，有多少个是完全平方数或完全立方数（或两者都是）？

English（选项同上方）

How many positive integer divisors of \(201^9\) are perfect squares or perfect cubes (or both)?

对 \(201^9\) 作质因数分解，得到 \(3^9 \cdot 67^9\)。

注意完全平方数的质因数指数为偶数，完全立方数的质因数指数能被 \(3\) 整除。

偶数指数有 \(5\) 种选择，从 \(0\) 到 \(8\)；\(3\) 的倍数指数有 \(4\) 种选择，从 \(0\) 到 \(9\)。

因此平方数有 \(5^2\) 种选择，立方数有 \(4^2\) 种选择；但还要减去重复计数的六次幂。

同理，六次幂的质因数指数必须能被 \(6\) 整除。每个指数有 \(2\) 种选择，即 \(0\) 和 \(6\)。

因此六次幂共有 \(2^2 = 4\) 个。于是完全平方数或完全立方数总共有 \[ 25 + 16 - 4 = 37 \] 个。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Taking the prime factorization of \(201^9,\) we get \(3^9 \cdot 67^9.\)

Note that a perfect square has even exponents for its prime factors, and a cube’s exponents are divisible by \(3.\)

There are \(5\) options for an even exponent, from \(0\) through \(8,\) and \(4\) options for multiples of \(3,\) from \(0\) through \(9.\)

This gives us \(5^2\) options for the squares and \(4^2\) options for the cubes. We have to subtract out the sixth powers, however.

Using the same logic, sixth powers have to have exponents of prime factors be divisible by \(6.\) There are \(2\) options, \(0\) and \(6.\)

This means that there are \(2^2 = 4\) sixth powers. This gives us a total of \[ 25 + 16 - 4 = 37. \] perfect squares or perfect cubes.

Thus, C is the correct answer.

12单选

Melanie 计算 \(2019\) 年各月日期所组成的 \(365\) 个数的平均数 \(\mu\)、中位数 \(M\) 和众数。因此数据中有 \(12\) 个 \(1\)、\(12\) 个 \(2\)、……、\(12\) 个 \(28\)、\(11\) 个 \(29\)、\(11\) 个 \(30\) 和 \(7\) 个 \(31\)。令 \(d\) 为所有众数的中位数。下面哪个说法正确？

English（选项同上方）

Melanie computes the mean \(\mu,\) the median \(M,\) and the modes of the \(365\) values that are the dates in the months of \(2019.\) Thus her data consist of \(12\) copies of \(1,\) \(12\) copies of \(2,\) and so on through \(12\) copies of \(28,\) then \(11\) copies of \(29,\) \(11\) copies of \(30,\) and \(7\) copies of \(31.\) Let \(d\) be the median of the modes. Which of the following statements is true?

众数是从 \(1\) 到 \(28\) 的所有整数，所以它们的中位数为 \(d=\dfrac{14+15}{2}=14.5\)。

数据共有 \(365\) 项，所以中位数 \(M\) 是第 \(183\) 个数。日期 \(1\) 到 \(15\) 共占 \(15 \cdot 12 = 180\) 个位置，所以 \(M=16\)。

所有日期之和为 \[\begin{aligned}12(1+\cdots+28)&+11(29+30)\\&+7\cdot31=5738\text{。}\end{aligned}\] 所以 \(\mu=\dfrac{5738}{365}\approx15.72\)。

因此 \[ d \lt \mu \lt M\text{。} \] 所以正确答案是 E。

English

The modes are all the integers from \(1\) through \(28,\) so their median is \(d=\dfrac{14+15}{2}=14.5.\)

There are \(365\) entries, so \(M\) is the \(183\)rd number. The dates from \(1\) through \(15\) occupy \(15 \cdot 12 = 180\) positions, so \(M=16.\)

The sum of all dates is \[\begin{aligned}12(1+\cdots+28)&+11(29+30)\\&+7\cdot31=5738.\end{aligned}\] Hence \(\mu=\dfrac{5738}{365}\approx15.72.\)

Therefore \[ d \lt \mu \lt M. \] Thus, E is the correct answer.

13单选

设 \(\triangle ABC\) 是等腰三角形，\(BC = AC\)，且 \(\angle ACB = 40^{\circ}\)。以 \(\overline{BC}\) 为直径作圆，令 \(D\) 和 \(E\) 分别为该圆与边 \(\overline{AC}\) 和 \(\overline{AB}\) 的另一个交点。令 \(F\) 为四边形 \(BCDE\) 的两条对角线交点。求 \(\angle BFC \) 的度数。

English（选项同上方）

Let \(\triangle ABC\) be an isosceles triangle with \(BC = AC\) and \(\angle ACB = 40^{\circ}.\) Construct the circle with diameter \(\overline{BC},\) and let \(D\) and \(E\) be the other intersection points of the circle with the sides \(\overline{AC}\) and \(\overline{AB},\) respectively. Let \(F\) be the intersection of the diagonals of the quadrilateral \(BCDE.\) What is the degree measure of \(\angle BFC ?\)

因为 \(\overline{BC}\) 是圆的直径，\(\angle BDC\) 和 \(\angle BEC\) 都是直角。

可知 \(\angle ABC = 70^{\circ}\)，这是因为 \(\triangle ABC\) 是等腰三角形。

在 \(\triangle BCE\) 和 \(\triangle BCD\) 中，分别有 \[ \begin{aligned} \angle ECB&=180^{\circ}-70^{\circ}-90^{\circ}\\ &=20^{\circ} \end{aligned} \] 以及 \[ \begin{aligned} \angle DBC&=180^{\circ}-40^{\circ}-90^{\circ}\\ &=50^{\circ} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因为 \(F\) 在 \(BD\) 和 \(CE\) 上，所以 \(\triangle BFC\) 的另外两个角分别是 \(50^{\circ}\) 和 \(20^{\circ}\)。于是 \[ \begin{aligned} \angle BFC&=180^{\circ}-50^{\circ}-20^{\circ}\\ &=110^{\circ} \end{aligned}\text{。}\] 所以正确答案是 D。

English

For \(n=36\), this total is \(1926\), while for \(n=37\) it is \(2035\). Therefore Tadd’s \(2019\)th number is the \((2019-1926)=93\)rd number of his \(37\)th turn.

Before that turn, the children have completed turns of lengths \(1\) through \(108\), saying \(1+2+\cdots+108=5886\) numbers. The \(93\)rd number of the next turn is \(5886+93=5979\). Thus, C is the correct answer.

设 \(p\)、\(q\) 和 \(r\) 是多项式 \[x^3 - 22x^2 + 80x - 67\] 的三个不同的根。存在实数 \(A\)、\(B\) 和 \(C\)，使得 \[ \begin{gathered} \frac{1}{s^3-22s^2+80s-67}\\ =\frac{A}{s-p}+\frac{B}{s-q}\\ \quad+\frac{C}{s-r} \end{gathered} \] 对每个满足 \(s\notin\{p,q,r\}\) 的实数 \(s\) 都成立。求下式的值： \[\dfrac1A+\dfrac1B+\dfrac1C\text{。}\]

English（选项同上方）

Let \(p,\) \(q,\) and \(r\) be the distinct roots of the polynomial \[x^3 - 22x^2 + 80x - 67.\] There exist real numbers \(A,\) \(B,\) and \(C\) such that \[ \begin{gathered} \frac{1}{s^3-22s^2+80s-67}\\ =\frac{A}{s-p}+\frac{B}{s-q}\\ \quad+\frac{C}{s-r} \end{gathered} \] for all real numbers \(s\) with \(s\notin\{p,q,r\}.\) What is \[\dfrac1A+\dfrac1B+\dfrac1C?\]

等式两边同乘 \((s-p)(s-q)(s-r)\)，得到 \[ \begin{aligned} 1={}&A(s-q)(s-r)\\ &+B(s-p)(s-r)\\ &+C(s-p)(s-q) \end{aligned}\text{。}\] 依次令 \(s=p,q,r\)，得到 \[ \begin{aligned} \frac1A&=(p-q)(p-r),\\ \frac1B&=(q-p)(q-r),\\ \frac1C&=(r-p)(r-q) \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

三式相加并展开，得到 \[ \begin{aligned} \frac1A+\frac1B+\frac1C &=p^2+q^2+r^2\\ &\quad-pq-pr-qr \end{aligned}\text{。}\] 由韦达定理，\(p+q+r=22\)，\(pq+pr+qr=80\)，所以 \[ \begin{aligned} p^2+q^2+r^2&=22^2-2(80)\\ &=324 \end{aligned}\text{。}\] 因此所求的值为 \(324-80=244\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Multiplying the identity by \((s-p)(s-q)(s-r)\) gives \[ \begin{aligned} 1={}&A(s-q)(s-r)\\ &+B(s-p)(s-r)\\ &+C(s-p)(s-q). \end{aligned} \] Setting \(s=p,q,r\), in turn, yields \[ \begin{aligned} \frac1A&=(p-q)(p-r),\\ \frac1B&=(q-p)(q-r),\\ \frac1C&=(r-p)(r-q). \end{aligned} \]

Adding and expanding gives \[ \begin{aligned} \frac1A+\frac1B+\frac1C &=p^2+q^2+r^2\\ &\quad-pq-pr-qr. \end{aligned} \] By Vieta’s formulas, \(p+q+r=22\) and \(pq+pr+qr=80\), so \[ \begin{aligned} p^2+q^2+r^2&=22^2-2(80)\\ &=324. \end{aligned} \] Therefore the requested value is \(324-80=244.\)

Thus, B is the correct answer.

对从 \(1\) 到 \(50\)（含端点）的多少个整数 \(n\)，下式是整数？\[\dfrac{(n^2-1)!}{(n!)^n}\]（规定 \(0! = 1\)。）

English（选项同上方）

For how many integers \(n\) between \(1\) and \(50,\) inclusive, is \[\dfrac{(n^2-1)!}{(n!)^n}\] an integer? (Recall that \(0! = 1.\))

解决本题的一个重要事实是 \[ \dfrac{(n^2)!}{(n!)^{n + 1}} \] 始终是整数。

这是因为它等于把 \(n^2\) 个物体分成 \(n\) 个无序的、每组大小为 \(n\) 的组的方法数。

现在有 \[ \dfrac{(n^2 - 1)!}{(n!)^n} = \dfrac{(n^2)!}{(n!)^{n + 1}} \cdot \dfrac{n!}{n^2}\text{。}\]

因此，只要 \(n^2\) 整除 \(n!\)，原式就是整数；这等价于 \(n\) 整除 \((n - 1)!\)。

设 \(n\) 为合数。若 \(n=ab\)，其中 \(2\le a<b<n\)，则两个不同因数 \(a\) 和 \(b\) 都出现在 \((n-1)!\) 中，所以 \((n-1)!\) 能被 \(n\) 整除。若 \(n=a^2\)，其中 \(a\ge3\)，则 \((n-1)!\) 含有两个不同因数 \(a\) 和 \(2a\)，其乘积是 \(n\) 的倍数。因此每个合数 \(n\ne4\) 都符合条件。另外，\(n=1\) 的情形也可以直接验证成立。

反过来，若 \(n=p\) 是质数，分母中 \(p\) 的指数为 \(p\)，而 \((p^2-1)!\) 中该质因子的指数为 \(p-1\)，所以原式不是整数。

当 \(n=4\) 时，分母含有 \(2^{12}\)，而 \(15!\) 只含有 \(2^{11}\)，所以这种情况也不成立。

不超过 \(50\) 的质数有 \(15\) 个，再加上 \(4\)，共有 \(16\) 个不符合条件的 \(n\) 值。

因此所求答案为 \(50 - 16 = 34\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

One fact that greatly helps with this problem is realizing that \[ \dfrac{(n^2)!}{(n!)^{n + 1}} \] is always an integer.

This is because it is the number of ways to split up \(n^2\) objects into \(n\) unordered groups of size \(n.\)

Now, we get that \[ \dfrac{(n^2 - 1)!}{(n!)^n} = \dfrac{(n^2)!}{(n!)^{n + 1}} \cdot \dfrac{n!}{n^2}. \]

Therefore, whenever \(n^2\) divides \(n!,\) the original expression is an integer; this is equivalent to \(n\) dividing \((n - 1)!.\)

Suppose \(n\) is composite. If \(n=ab\) with \(2\le a<b<n\), then the distinct factors \(a\) and \(b\) both occur in \((n-1)!\), so \((n-1)!\) is divisible by \(n\). If \(n=a^2\) with \(a\ge3\), then \((n-1)!\) contains the distinct factors \(a\) and \(2a\), whose product is a multiple of \(n\). Thus every composite \(n\ne4\) works. The case \(n=1\) also works directly.

Conversely, if \(n=p\) is prime, the exponent of \(p\) in the denominator is \(p,\) while its exponent in \((p^2-1)!\) is \(p-1,\) so the expression is not an integer.

For \(n=4,\) the denominator contains \(2^{12},\) while \(15!\) contains only \(2^{11},\) so this case also fails.

There are \(15\) primes at most \(50,\) and adding \(4,\) we get \(16\) values for \(n\) that do not work.

Therefore, the desired answer is \(50 - 16 = 34.\)

Thus, D is the correct answer.
