1单选

考虑下面这个列表：

\[ \begin{gathered} n,\ n+3,\ n+4,\ n+5,\ n+6, \\ \ n+8,\ n+10,\ n+12,\ n+15 \end{gathered} \]

它的中位数是 \(10\)。平均数是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The median of the list

\[ \begin{gathered} n,\ n+3,\ n+4,\ n+5,\ n+6, \\ \ n+8,\ n+10,\ n+12,\ n+15 \end{gathered} \]

is \(10.\) What is the mean?

这 \(9\) 个数已经按递增顺序排列，中位数是第 \(5\) 项 \(n+6\)，所以 \(n+6=10\)，得 \(n=4\)。

代入后，各项之和的计算过程为 \(9n\) \(\small {}+(3+4+5+6+8+10+12+15)\) \(=9n+63\) \(=99\)，所以平均数是 \(\frac{99}{9}=11\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The list has \(9\) numbers in increasing order, so the median is the \(5\)th term, \(n+6.\) Setting \(n+6=10\) gives \(n=4.\)

The sum of the terms is \(9n\) \(\small {}+(3+4+5+6+8+10+12+15)\) \(=9n+63\) \(=99,\) so the mean is \(\frac{99}{9}=11.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

2单选

一个数 \(x\) 比它的倒数与它的加法逆元的乘积多 \(2\)。这个数位于哪个区间？

English（选项同上方）

A number \(x\) is \(2\) more than the product of its reciprocal and its additive inverse. In which interval does the number lie?

\(x\) 的倒数是 \(\dfrac1x\)，加法逆元是 \(-x\)，它们的乘积为 \(\dfrac1x\cdot(-x)=-1\)。

因此 \(x=2+(-1)=1\)，位于区间 \(0\lt x\le2\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The reciprocal of \(x\) is \(\dfrac1x\) and its additive inverse is \(-x.\) Their product is \(\dfrac1x\cdot(-x)=-1.\)

So \(x=2+(-1)=1,\) which lies in the interval \(0\lt x\le2.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

3单选

两个数的和是 \(S\)。若每个数都先加上 \(3\)，然后将所得的两个数各自加倍，那么最后两个数的和是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The sum of two numbers is \(S.\) Suppose \(3\) is added to each number and then each of the resulting numbers is doubled. What is the sum of the final two numbers?

设两个数为 \(a\) 和 \(b\)，且 \(a+b=S\)。每个数加上 \(3\) 再加倍后，所得两数之和为 \(2(a+3)\) \(+2(b+3)\) \(=2(a+b)+12\) \(=2S+12\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Let the numbers be \(a\) and \(b,\) so \(a+b=S.\) After adding \(3\) to each and doubling, the sum is \(2(a+3)\) \(+2(b+3)\) \(=2(a+b)+12\) \(=2S+12.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

4单选

一个圆和一个三角形最多可能有多少个交点？

English（选项同上方）

What is the maximum number of possible points of intersection of a circle and a triangle?

三角形的每条边都是线段，最多与圆相交于 \(2\) 个点；三角形有 \(3\) 条边，所以最多有 \(3\cdot2=6\) 个交点，而且这个上界可以达到。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Each side of the triangle is a segment, which can intersect a circle in at most \(2\) points. With \(3\) sides, the maximum is \(3\cdot2=6\) points, and this is achievable.

Thus, the correct answer is E.

How many of the twelve pentominoes pictured below have at least one line of symmetry?

逐一检查这些五连方的反射对称性，恰好有六个至少有一条对称轴：直条形、十字形、\(T\) 形、\(U\) 形、\(V\) 形和 \(W\) 形。其余六个都没有对称轴。

6单选

设 \(P(n)\) 和 \(S(n)\) 分别表示整数 \(n\) 的各位数字之积与各位数字之和。例如，\(P(23)=6\)，\(S(23)=5\)。若 \(N\) 是一个满足 \(N=P(N)+S(N)\) 的两位数，求 \(N\) 的个位数字。

English（选项同上方）

Let \(P(n)\) and \(S(n)\) denote the product and the sum, respectively, of the digits of the integer \(n.\) For example, \(P(23)=6\) and \(S(23)=5.\) Suppose \(N\) is a two-digit number such that \(N=P(N)+S(N).\) What is the units digit of \(N?\)

设 \(N=10a+b\)，则 \(P(N)=ab\)，\(S(N)=a+b\)，所以 \(10a+b=ab+a+b\)。化简得 \(9a=ab\)，因 \(a\ne0\)，故 \(b=9\)。

因此该数的个位数字是 \(9\)。所以正确答案是 E。

English

Write \(N=10a+b.\) Then \(P(N)=ab\) and \(S(N)=a+b,\) so \(10a+b=ab+a+b.\) This reduces to \(9a=ab,\) and since \(a\ne0,\) we get \(b=9.\)

The units digit is \(9.\) Thus, the correct answer is E.

7单选

将某个正小数的小数点向右移动四位后，得到的新数等于原数倒数的四倍。原数是多少？

English（选项同上方）

When the decimal point of a certain positive decimal number is moved four places to the right, the new number is four times the reciprocal of the original number. What is the original number?

设原数为 \(x\)。小数点向右移动四位相当于乘以 \(10000\)，所以 \(10000x=4\cdot\dfrac1x\)，即 \(x^2=\dfrac{4}{10000}\)。

因为 \(x\gt0\)，所以 \(x=\dfrac{2}{100}=0.02\)，这就是原数。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Moving the decimal four places right multiplies \(x\) by \(10000.\) So \(10000x=4\cdot\dfrac1x,\) giving \(x^2=\dfrac{4}{10000}.\)

Since \(x\gt0,\) \(x=\dfrac{2}{100}=0.02.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

8单选

旺达、达伦、比阿特丽斯和奇是学校数学实验室的辅导员。他们的排班如下：达伦每三个上课日工作一次，旺达每四个上课日工作一次，比阿特丽斯每六个上课日工作一次，奇每七个上课日工作一次。今天他们四人都在数学实验室工作。从今天起再过多少个上课日，他们下一次会同时在实验室辅导？

English（选项同上方）

Wanda, Darren, Beatrice, and Chi are tutors in the school math lab. Their schedule is as follows: Darren works every third school day, Wanda works every fourth school day, Beatrice works every sixth school day, and Chi works every seventh school day. Today they are all working in the math lab. In how many school days from today will they next be together tutoring in the lab?

他们再次同时工作需要经过 \(\text{lcm}(3,4,6,7)\) 个上课日。因为 \(4=2^2\)，\(6=2\cdot3\)，所以最小公倍数为 \(2^2\cdot3\cdot7=84\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

They meet again after \(\text{lcm}(3,4,6,7)\) days. Since \(4=2^2\) and \(6=2\cdot3,\) the least common multiple is \(2^2\cdot3\cdot7=84.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

9单选

克里斯汀所住州的所得税税率为：年收入前 \(\$28000\) 按 \(p\%\) 征税，超过 \(\$28000\) 的部分按 \((p+2)\%\) 征税。克里斯汀注意到她缴纳的州所得税等于年收入的 \((p+0.25)\%\)。她的年收入是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The state income tax where Kristin lives is levied at the rate of \(p\%\) of the first \(\$28000\) of annual income plus \((p+2)\%\) of any amount above \(\$28000.\) Kristin noticed that the state income tax she paid amounted to \((p+0.25)\%\) of her annual income. What was her annual income?

设年收入为 \(x\ge28000\)。

\[ \begin{aligned} &\tfrac{p}{100}(28000) \\ &\quad {}+\tfrac{p+2}{100}(x-28000) \\ &\quad =\tfrac{p+0.25}{100}x \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

两边乘以 \(100\) 并展开，所有含 \(p\) 的项抵消，得到 \(2x-56000=0.25x\)。因此 \(1.75x=56000\)，所以 \(x=32000\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Let her income be \(x\ge28000.\) Then

\[ \begin{aligned} &\tfrac{p}{100}(28000) \\ &\quad {}+\tfrac{p+2}{100}(x-28000) \\ &\quad =\tfrac{p+0.25}{100}x. \end{aligned} \]

Multiplying by \(100\) and expanding, all the \(p\) terms cancel, leaving \(2x-56000=0.25x.\) So \(1.75x=56000\) and \(x=32000.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

若 \(x\)、\(y\)、\(z\) 为正数，且 \(xy=24\)、\(xz=48\)、\(yz=72\)，则 \(x+y+z\) 等于多少？

English（选项同上方）

If \(x,\) \(y,\) and \(z\) are positive with \(xy=24,\) \(xz=48,\) and \(yz=72,\) then \(x+y+z\) is

用 \(xz=48\) 除以 \(xy=24\)，得 \(z=2y\)。再由 \(yz=2y^2=72\)，可得 \(y=6\)，于是 \(z=12\)，\(x=\frac{24}{y}=4\)。

因此 \(x+y+z=22\)。所以正确答案是 D。

English

Hence \(x+y+z=22.\) Thus, the correct answer is D.

11单选

考虑单位正方形阵列中的深色正方形，图中只显示了一部分。围绕中心正方形的第一圈含有 \(8\) 个单位正方形，第二圈含有 \(16\) 个单位正方形。若继续这个过程，第 \(100\) 圈中有多少个单位正方形？

English（选项同上方）

Consider the dark square in an array of unit squares, part of which is shown. The first ring of squares around this center square contains \(8\) unit squares. The second ring contains \(16\) unit squares. If we continue this process, the number of unit squares in the \(100\)th ring is

第 \(n\) 圈是一个 \((2n+1)\times(2n+1)\) 正方形的外边框，内部去掉一个 \((2n-1)\times(2n-1)\) 正方形，因此含有 \((2n+1)^2-(2n-1)^2=8n\) 个单位正方形。

当 \(n=100\) 时，个数为 \(800\)。所以正确答案是 C。

English

The \(n\)th ring is the border of a \((2n+1)\times(2n+1)\) square surrounding a \((2n-1)\times(2n-1)\) square, so it contains \((2n+1)^2-(2n-1)^2=8n\) unit squares.

For \(n=100,\) that is \(800.\) Thus, the correct answer is C.

12单选

假设 \(n\) 是三个连续整数的乘积，并且 \(n\) 能被 \(7\) 整除。下列哪一个不一定是 \(n\) 的因数？

English（选项同上方）

Suppose that \(n\) is the product of three consecutive integers and that \(n\) is divisible by \(7.\) Which of the following is not necessarily a divisor of \(n?\)

三个连续整数中，一定有一个是 \(3\) 的倍数，且乘积 \(n\) 也有一个偶数因子，因此能被 \(6\) 整除。再加上题设的因数 \(7\)，它一定能被 \(6\)、\(14\)、\(21\) 和 \(42\) 整除。

但 \(28=2^2\cdot7\) 需要两个因数 \(2\)，这不一定保证；例如 \(5\cdot6\cdot7=210\) 能被 \(7\) 整除，却不能被 \(28\) 整除。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Among three consecutive integers, at least one is even and one is a multiple of \(3,\) so \(n\) is divisible by \(6.\) With the given factor of \(7,\) it is divisible by \(6,\) \(14,\) \(21,\) and \(42.\)

But \(28=2^2\cdot7\) requires two factors of \(2,\) which is not guaranteed: \(5\cdot6\cdot7=210\) is divisible by \(7\) but not by \(28.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

13单选

一个电话号码形式为 \(ABC-DEF-GHIJ\)，其中每个字母代表一个不同数字。号码每一部分中的数字都按递减顺序排列，即 \(A\gt B\gt C\)、\(D\gt E\gt F\)、\(G\gt H\gt I\gt J\)。此外，\(D\)、\(E\)、\(F\) 是连续偶数数字，\(G\)、\(H\)、\(I\)、\(J\) 是连续奇数数字，且 \(A+B+C=9\)。求 \(A\)。

English（选项同上方）

A telephone number has the form \(ABC-DEF-GHIJ,\) where each letter represents a different digit. The digits in each part of the number are in decreasing order; that is, \(A\gt B\gt C,\) \(D\gt E\gt F,\) and \(G\gt H\gt I\gt J.\) Furthermore, \(D,\) \(E,\) and \(F\) are consecutive even digits; \(G,\) \(H,\) \(I,\) and \(J\) are consecutive odd digits; and \(A+B+C=9.\) Find \(A.\)

连续奇数数字 \(GHIJ\) 是 \(9753\) 或 \(7531\)，因此留给 \(A\)、\(B\)、\(C\) 的奇数数字是 \(1\) 或 \(9\)。由于 \(A+B+C=9\)，这个奇数数字只能是 \(1\)，所以 \(ABC\) 中两个偶数数字的和为 \(8\)。

连续偶数数字组成的 \(DEF\) 可能是 \(864\)、\(642\) 或 \(420\)，于是留给 \(ABC\) 的偶数数字对分别为 \(\{2,0\}\)、\(\{8,0\}\) 或 \(\{8,6\}\)。只有 \(\{8,0\}\) 的和为 \(8\)，所以 \(ABC=810\)，从而 \(A=8\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The consecutive odd digits \(GHIJ\) are \(9753\) or \(7531,\) leaving one odd digit (\(1\) or \(9\)) for \(A,\) \(B,\) \(C.\) Since \(A+B+C=9,\) the odd digit there must be \(1,\) so the two even digits in \(ABC\) sum to \(8.\)

The consecutive even digits \(DEF\) are \(864,\) \(642,\) or \(420,\) leaving even-digit pairs \(\{2,0\},\) \(\{8,0\},\) or \(\{8,6\}\) for \(ABC.\) Only \(\{8,0\}\) sums to \(8,\) so \(ABC=810\) and \(A=8.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

14单选

一个慈善机构卖出 \(140\) 张公益票，共收入 \(\$2001\)。一些票按全价出售，全价为整数美元；其余票按半价出售。全价票共筹得多少钱？

English（选项同上方）

A charity sells \(140\) benefit tickets for a total of \(\$2001.\) Some tickets sell for full price (a whole dollar amount), and the rest sell for half price. How much money is raised by the full-price tickets?

设 \(n\) 张全价票每张 \(p\) 美元。\(np+(140-n)\dfrac p2=2001\)，\(p(n+140)\) \(=4002\) \(=2\cdot3\cdot23\cdot29\)。

则 \(140\le n+140\le280\)。在 \(4002\) 的因数中，落在这个范围内的只有 \(174=2\cdot3\cdot29\)。所以 \(n+140=174\)，得 \(n=34\)、\(p=23\)，全价票收入为 \(34\cdot23=782\) 美元。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Let \(n\) full-price tickets sell at \(p\) dollars each. Then \(np+(140-n)\dfrac p2=2001,\) so \(p(n+140)\) \(=4002\) \(=2\cdot3\cdot23\cdot29.\)

Since \(140\le n+140\le280,\) the only factor of \(4002\) in range is \(174=2\cdot3\cdot29.\) So \(n+140=174,\) giving \(n=34\) and \(p=23.\) The full-price tickets raise \(34\cdot23=782\) dollars.

Thus, the correct answer is A.

15单选

一条街的两侧路缘平行，相距 \(40\) 英尺。一条由两条平行条纹围成的人行横道斜穿过街道。两条条纹在路缘上截出的长度为 \(15\) 英尺，每条条纹长 \(50\) 英尺。求两条条纹之间的距离，单位为英尺。

English（选项同上方）

A street has parallel curbs \(40\) feet apart. A crosswalk bounded by two parallel stripes crosses the street at an angle. The length of the curb between the stripes is \(15\) feet and each stripe is \(50\) feet long. Find the distance, in feet, between the stripes.

人行横道是一个平行四边形。以路缘上的 \(15\) 英尺为底、街道宽 \(40\) 英尺为高，其面积为 \(15\cdot40=600\)。

以一条 \(50\) 英尺的条纹为底，面积等于 \(50\) 乘以两条条纹之间的距离 \(d\)，所以 \(d=\frac{600}{50}=12\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The crosswalk is a parallelogram. Using the curb (\(15\) ft) as base and the street width (\(40\) ft) as height, its area is \(15\cdot40=600\) square feet.

Using a stripe (\(50\) ft) as base, the area equals \(50\) times the distance \(d\) between the stripes, so \(d=\frac{600}{50}=12.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

16单选

三个数的平均数比其中最小的数大 \(10\)，并且比其中最大的数小 \(15\)。这三个数的中位数是 \(5\)。它们的和是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The mean of three numbers is \(10\) more than the least of the numbers and \(15\) less than the greatest. The median of the three numbers is \(5.\) What is their sum?

设平均数为 \(m\)。最小数为 \(m-10\)，最大数为 \(m+15\)，中位数为 \(5\)。

于是 \(m\) 满足 \[ \begin{aligned} &\tfrac13\big((m-10)+5+(m+15)\big) \\ &\quad =m \end{aligned} \] 化简得 \(m=10\)。

因此三个数的和为 \(3m=30\)。所以正确答案是 D。

English

Let \(m\) be the mean. The least number is \(m-10\) and the greatest is \(m+15,\) with median \(5.\)

Since the mean of the three is \(m,\) \[ \begin{aligned} &\tfrac13\big((m-10)+5+(m+15)\big) \\ &\quad =m, \end{aligned} \] which gives \(m=10.\)

The sum is \(3m=30.\) Thus, the correct answer is D.

17单选

下列哪个圆锥可以由半径为 \(10\)、圆心角为 \(252^\circ\) 的圆形扇形，将两条直边对齐后卷成？

English（选项同上方）

Which of the cones below can be formed from a \(252^\circ\) sector of a circle of radius \(10\) by aligning the two straight sides?

卷成圆锥时，扇形半径 \(10\) 成为圆锥的母线长，扇形弧长成为底面周长。

弧长为 \(\dfrac{252}{360}\cdot2\pi\cdot10=14\pi\)，所以 \(2\pi r=14\pi\)，底面半径 \(r=7\)。圆锥母线长为 \(10\)，底面半径为 \(7\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

When rolled into a cone, the sector’s radius \(10\) becomes the slant height, and the arc length becomes the base circumference.

The arc length is \(\dfrac{252}{360}\cdot2\pi\cdot10=14\pi,\) so \(2\pi r=14\pi\) gives base radius \(r=7.\) The cone has slant height \(10\) and base radius \(7.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

The plane is tiled by congruent squares and congruent pentagons as indicated. The percent of the plane that is enclosed by the pentagons is closest to

19单选

帕特想从充足供应的三种甜甜圈中买四个：糖霜、巧克力和糖粉。共有多少种不同选择？

English（选项同上方）

Pat wants to buy four donuts from an ample supply of three types of donuts: glazed, chocolate, and powdered. How many different selections are possible?

不同选择的数量等于方程 \(g+c+p=4\) 的非负整数解个数。由插板法，解的个数为 \(\dbinom{4+2}{2}=\dbinom62=15\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The number of selections is the number of nonnegative integer solutions of \(g+c+p=4.\) By stars and bars, this is \(\dbinom{4+2}{2}=\dbinom62=15.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

20单选

从边长为 \(2000\) 的正方形四个角各切去一个等腰直角三角形，形成一个正八边形。这个八边形每条边的长度是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A regular octagon is formed by cutting an isosceles right triangle from each of the corners of a square with sides of length \(2000.\) What is the length of each side of the octagon?

设八边形边长为 \(x\)。每个被切去的等腰直角三角形的斜边也是八边形的一条边，所以它的直角边为 \(\dfrac{x}{\sqrt2}\)。

沿正方形的一条边，有两段这样的直角边和一条八边形边，因此 \(2\cdot\dfrac{x}{\sqrt2}+x=2000\)，即 \(x(\sqrt2+1)=2000\)，所以 \(x=\dfrac{2000}{\sqrt2+1}=2000(\sqrt2-1)\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Let each octagon side be \(x.\) It is the hypotenuse of each cut isosceles right triangle, whose legs are \(\dfrac{x}{\sqrt2}.\)

Along one side of the square, two legs and one octagon side give \(2\cdot\dfrac{x}{\sqrt2}+x=2000,\) so \(x(\sqrt2+1)=2000\) and \(x=\dfrac{2000}{\sqrt2+1}=2000(\sqrt2-1).\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

一个直圆柱内接于一个直圆锥，圆柱的直径等于它的高。圆锥直径为 \(10\)，高为 \(12\)，且圆柱与圆锥的轴重合。求圆柱的半径。

English（选项同上方）

A right circular cylinder with its diameter equal to its height is inscribed in a right circular cone. The cone has diameter \(10\) and altitude \(12,\) and the axes of the cylinder and cone coincide. Find the radius of the cylinder.

取轴截面。圆锥底面半径为 \(5\)、高为 \(12\)；圆柱截面的宽为 \(2r\)、高为 \(2r\)。

由相似三角形，\(\dfrac{12-2r}{r}=\dfrac{12}{5}\)，所以 \(5(12-2r)=12r\)，即 \(60=22r\)，从而 \(r=\dfrac{30}{11}\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Take an axial cross-section. The cone has base radius \(5\) and height \(12;\) the cylinder appears as a rectangle of width \(2r\) and height \(2r.\)

By similar triangles, \(\dfrac{12-2r}{r}=\dfrac{12}{5},\) so \(5(12-2r)=12r,\) giving \(60=22r\) and \(r=\dfrac{30}{11}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

如图所示的幻方中，每一行、每一列和每条对角线上的数之和都相同。其中五个数分别用 \(v\)、\(w\)、\(x\)、\(y\) 和 \(z\) 表示。求 \(y+z\)。

English（选项同上方）

In the magic square shown, the sums of the numbers in each row, column, and diagonal are the same. Five of these numbers are represented by \(v,\) \(w,\) \(x,\) \(y,\) and \(z.\) Find \(y+z.\)

因为 \(v\) 位于第一行、第一列和主对角线上，这三条线中另外两个数的和相等：\(25+18=24+w=21+x\)。所以 \(w=19\)，\(x=22\)。

反对角线上的 \(25\)、\(22\)、\(19\) 之和为 \(66\)，所以幻方和为 \(66\)。于是 \(v=66-24-19=23\)，\(y=66-18-22=26\)，\(z=66-25-21=20\)。

因此 \(y+z=46\)。所以正确答案是 D。

English

Since \(v\) sits in the first row, first column, and main diagonal, the remaining two entries of each of those lines have equal sums: \(25+18=24+w=21+x.\) So \(w=19\) and \(x=22.\)

The anti-diagonal \(25,\) \(22,\) \(19\) sums to \(66,\) so the magic sum is \(66.\) Then \(v=66-24-19=23,\) \(y=66-18-22=26,\) and \(z=66-25-21=20.\)

Hence \(y+z=46.\) Thus, the correct answer is D.

23单选

一个盒子里正好有五个筹码，其中三个红色、两个白色。每次随机取出一个且不放回，直到所有红色筹码都被取出或所有白色筹码都被取出为止。最后一次取出的筹码是白色的概率是多少？

English（选项同上方）

A box contains exactly five chips, three red and two white. Chips are randomly removed one at a time without replacement until all the red chips are drawn or all the white chips are drawn. What is the probability that the last chip drawn is white?

想象先给五个筹码排一个随机顺序。游戏停在白色筹码上，恰好表示两个白色筹码都在所有三个红色筹码取完之前出现，也就是完整顺序中的最后一个筹码是红色。

最后一个筹码为红色的概率为 \(\dfrac{3}{5}\)。所以正确答案是 D。

English

Imagine drawing all five chips in a random order. The drawing stops on a white chip exactly when both white chips come out before all three reds, which happens precisely when the very last chip in the full ordering is red.

That probability is \(\dfrac{3}{5}.\) Thus, the correct answer is D.

24单选

在梯形 \(ABCD\) 中，\(\overline{AB}\) 和 \(\overline{CD}\) 都垂直于 \(\overline{AD}\)，且 \(AB+CD=BC\)、\(AB\lt CD\)、\(AD=7\)。求 \(AB\cdot CD\)。

English（选项同上方）

In trapezoid \(ABCD,\) \(\overline{AB}\) and \(\overline{CD}\) are perpendicular to \(\overline{AD},\) with \(AB+CD=BC,\) \(AB\lt CD,\) and \(AD=7.\) What is \(AB\cdot CD?\)

从 \(B\) 向 \(CD\) 作垂线，交于 \(E\)。于是 \(BE=AD=7\)，\(CE=CD-AB\)，并且 \(BC^2=BE^2+CE^2\)。

又因为 \(BC=CD+AB\)，所以 \[ \begin{aligned} &(CD+AB)^2 \\ &\quad {}-(CD-AB)^2 \\ &\quad =BE^2=49 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

左边等于 \(4\cdot AB\cdot CD\)，因此 \(AB\cdot CD=\dfrac{49}{4}=12.25\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Drop a perpendicular from \(B\) to \(CD,\) meeting it at \(E.\) Then \(BE=AD=7\) and \(CE=CD-AB.\) By the Pythagorean theorem, \(BC^2=BE^2+CE^2.\)

Since \(BC=CD+AB,\) \[ \begin{aligned} &(CD+AB)^2 \\ &\quad {}-(CD-AB)^2 \\ &\quad =BE^2=49. \end{aligned} \]

The left side equals \(4\cdot AB\cdot CD,\) so \(AB\cdot CD=\dfrac{49}{4}=12.25.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

25单选

不超过 \(2001\) 的正整数中，有多少个是 \(3\) 或 \(4\) 的倍数，但不是 \(5\) 的倍数？

English（选项同上方）

How many positive integers not exceeding \(2001\) are multiples of \(3\) or \(4\) but not \(5?\)

不超过 \(2001\) 的 \(3\) 或 \(4\) 的倍数共有 \(\left\lfloor\tfrac{2001}{3}\right\rfloor\) \(+\left\lfloor\tfrac{2001}{4}\right\rfloor\) \(-\left\lfloor\tfrac{2001}{12}\right\rfloor\) \(=667+500-166\) \(=1001\)。

其中是 \(5\) 的倍数者，也就是 \(15\) 或 \(20\) 的倍数，共有 \(133\) 加 \(100\) 个，但要减去重复计算的 \(60\) 的倍数，共 \(33\) 个，因此共有 \(133+100-33=200\) 个。

所以所求个数为 \(1001-200=801\)。正确答案是 B。

English

Multiples of \(3\) or \(4\) up to \(2001:\) \(\left\lfloor\tfrac{2001}{3}\right\rfloor\) \(+\left\lfloor\tfrac{2001}{4}\right\rfloor\) \(-\left\lfloor\tfrac{2001}{12}\right\rfloor\) \(=667+500-166\) \(=1001.\)

Among these, remove the multiples of \(5:\) there are \(133\) multiples of \(15\) and \(100\) multiples of \(20,\) re-adding the \(33\) multiples of \(60:\) \(133+100-33=200.\)

So the count is \(1001-200=801.\) Thus, the correct answer is B.
