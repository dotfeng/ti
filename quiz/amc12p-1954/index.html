方程 \[ \begin{aligned} \frac{2x^2}{x-1}-\frac{2x+7}{3} &{}+\frac{4-6x}{x-1}\\ &{}+1=0 \end{aligned} \] 消去分母后可化为方程 \(x^2-5x+4=0\)。后一个方程的根为 \(4\) 和 \(1\)。那么原方程的根为：

English（选项同上方）

The equation \[ \begin{aligned} \frac{2x^2}{x-1}-\frac{2x+7}{3} &{}+\frac{4-6x}{x-1}\\ &{}+1=0 \end{aligned} \] can be transformed by eliminating fractions to the equation \(x^2-5x+4=0.\) The roots of the latter equation are \(4\) and \(1.\) Then the roots of the first equation are:

变换后的二次方程可因式分解为 \((x-1)(x-4)=0\)。但原方程的分母含有 \(x-1\)，所以在 \(x=1\) 时无定义。代入 \(x=4\) 合法，并且满足原方程。

因此，唯一的根是 \(4\)，正确答案是 C。

English

The transformed quadratic factors as \((x-1)(x-4)=0.\) However, the original equation has denominator \(x-1,\) so it is undefined at \(x=1.\) Substitution of \(x=4\) is valid and satisfies the original equation.

Thus, the only root is \(4,\) and the correct answer is C.

English（选项同上方）

The two roots of the equation \[ \begin{aligned} a(b-c)x^2+b(c-a)x\\ {}+c(a-b)&=0 \end{aligned} \] are \(1\) and:

由韦达定理，两根之积为 \[ \frac{c(a-b)}{a(b-c)}\text{。}\]因为其中一个根是 \(1\)，所以另一个根等于这个乘积。

Let \(MT=t.\) Since \(AM=BM=RT=\frac{p}{2},\) the Pythagorean theorem gives \[ t^2+q^2=\frac{p^2}{4}. \] Also, \[ AT=\frac p2+t,\qquad TB=\frac p2-t. \] Their sum is \(p,\) and their product is \[ \frac{p^2}{4}-t^2=q^2. \] Therefore they are the roots of \(x^2-px+q^2=0.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

48单选

一列火车出发一小时后发生事故，耽误了半小时；之后以原速度的 \(\dfrac34\) 继续行驶，最终晚点 \(3\dfrac12\) 小时。若事故发生在前方 \(90\) 英里处，火车将只晚点 \(3\) 小时。全程长度为多少英里？

English（选项同上方）

A train, an hour after starting, meets with an accident which detains it a half hour, after which it proceeds at \(\dfrac34\) of its former rate and arrives \(3\dfrac12\) hours late. Had the accident happened \(90\) miles farther along the line, it would have arrived only \(3\) hours late. The length of the trip in miles was:

设正常速度为 \(v\)，全程长度为 \(L\)。一段路程以 \(\frac{3v}{4}\) 而非 \(v\) 的速度行驶，会增加该路段正常行驶时间的三分之一。第一种情况下， \[ \frac12+\frac13\left(\frac Lv-1\right)=\frac72\text{，}\]所以 \(\frac{L}{v}=10\)。若事故在前方 \(90\) 英里处发生，则 \[ \frac12+\frac13\left(\frac Lv-1-\frac{90}{v}\right)=3\text{。}\]代入 \(\frac{L}{v}=10\)，得到 \(\frac{90}{v}=\frac{3}{2}\)，因此 \(v=60\)，且 \[ L=10v=600\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Let the normal speed be \(v\) and the trip length be \(L.\) Traveling a distance at \(\frac{3v}{4}\) rather than \(v\) adds one-third of its normal travel time. In the first case, \[ \frac12+\frac13\left(\frac Lv-1\right)=\frac72, \] so \(\frac{L}{v}=10.\) If the accident happens \(90\) miles farther along, \[ \frac12+\frac13\left(\frac Lv-1-\frac{90}{v}\right)=3. \] Substituting \(\frac{L}{v}=10\) gives \(\frac{90}{v}=\frac{3}{2},\) hence \(v=60\) and \[ L=10v=600. \]

Thus, the correct answer is C.

两个奇数的平方差总能被 \(8\) 整除。若 \(a>b\)，且 \(2a+1\) 与 \(2b+1\) 是这两个奇数，为证明这一命题，应将平方差写成：

English（选项同上方）

The difference of the squares of two odd numbers is always divisible by \(8.\) If \(a>b,\) and \(2a+1\) and \(2b+1\) are the odd numbers, to prove the given statement we put the difference of the squares in the form:

展开并重新分组， \[ \begin{aligned} &(2a+1)^2-(2b+1)^2\\ &\qquad=4[a(a+1)-b(b+1)] \end{aligned}\text{。}\]\(a(a+1)\) 和 \(b(b+1)\) 都是偶数，所以两者之差也是偶数。因此所示表达式能被 \(4\cdot2=8\) 整除。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Expanding and regrouping, \[ \begin{aligned} &(2a+1)^2-(2b+1)^2\\ &\qquad=4[a(a+1)-b(b+1)]. \end{aligned} \] Each of \(a(a+1)\) and \(b(b+1)\) is even, so their difference is even. The displayed expression is consequently divisible by \(4\cdot2=8.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

在 \(7\) 点与 \(8\) 点之间，时针与分针夹角为 \(84\) 度的时刻，精确到最近一分钟，分别为：

The times between \(7\) and \(8\) o’clock, correct to the nearest minute, when the hands of a clock will form an angle of \(84\) degrees are:
