3单选

风寒温度衡量人在室外有风时感觉到的寒冷程度。风寒温度可用下面的计算式近似：

\[W=T-0.7\cdot S\]

其中 \(W\) 表示风寒温度，\(T\) 表示以华氏度 \((^{\circ}F)\) 计的气温，\(S\) 表示以英里每小时计的风速。

若气温是 \(36^{\circ}F\)，风速是每小时 \(18\) 英里，下列哪一项最接近估算出的风寒温度？

English（选项同上方）

Wind chill is a measure of how cold people feel when exposed to wind outside. A good estimate for wind chill can be found using this calculation:

\[W=T-0.7\cdot S\]

Here \(W\) represents the wind chill, \(T\) represents air temperature measured in degrees Fahrenheit \((^{\circ}F),\) and \(S\) represents wind speed measured in miles per hour (mph).

Suppose the air temperature is \(36^{\circ}F\) and the wind speed is \(18\) mph. Which of the following is closest to the approximate wind chill?

代入公式，风寒温度为

\[\begin{align*} 36 - 0.7 \cdot 18 &= 36 - 12.6 \\ &=23.4\text{。} \end{align*}\]

最接近的选项是 \(23\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Using the formula, the wind chill is

\[\begin{align*} 36 - 0.7 \cdot 18 &= 36 - 12.6 \\ &=23.4. \end{align*}\]

The closest choice is \(23.\)

Thus, B is the correct answer.

5单选

一个湖中有 \(250\) 条鳟鱼，还有其他多种鱼。一位海洋生物学家从湖中捕捉并放回 \(180\) 条鱼作为样本，其中 \(30\) 条被识别为鳟鱼。假设样本中鳟鱼占总鱼数的比例与湖中相同，湖中共有多少条鱼？

English（选项同上方）

A lake contains \(250\) trout, along with a variety of other fish. When a marine biologist catches and releases a sample of \(180\) fish from the lake, \(30\) are identified as trout. Assume that the ratio of trout to the total number of fish is the same in both the sample and the lake. How many fish are there in the lake?

样本中鳟鱼比例为 \(\dfrac{30}{180} = \dfrac{1}{6}\)。因此湖中所有鱼数是鳟鱼数的 \(6\) 倍。

湖中鱼的总数为 \(6 \cdot 250 = 1500\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Note that \(\dfrac{30}{180} = \dfrac{1}{6}.\) This means that a sixth of the fish in the lake are trout, or in other words, the total number of fish is \(6\) times the number of trout.

Therefore, there are \(6 \cdot 250 = 1500\) fish in the lake.

Thus, B is the correct answer.

6单选

把数字 \(2\)、\(0\)、\(2\) 和 \(3\) 填入下列表达式中，每个方框放一个数字。这个表达式的最大可能值是多少？

English（选项同上方）

The digits \(2,\) \(0,\) \(2,\) and \(3\) are placed in the expression below, one digit per box. What is the maximum possible value of the expression?

不要把 \(0\) 放在底数位置，因为那样会使整个表达式等于 \(0\)。

所以 \(0\) 必须放在指数位置。指数为 \(0\) 的那一项会自动等于 \(1\)。

把两个 \(2\) 中的一个用于 \(2^0\)，就能把另一个 \(2\) 和 \(3\) 留给另一项。

另一项可以是 \(2^3\) 或 \(3^2\)。因为 \(3^2\) 更大，所以取 \(3^2\)。

于是最大值为 \[ 2^0 \times 3^2 = 1 \times 9 = 9\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Note that we do not want \(0\) as a base, since that would make the expression equal to \(0.\)

This means that \(0\) must be an exponent. The number whose exponent is \(0\) will automatically evaluate to \(1.\)

Using one of the two copies of \(2\) in the factor \(2^0\) leaves \(2\) and \(3\) for the other factor.

The other factor can then be \(2^3\) or \(3^2.\) Since \(3^2\) is larger, we use \(3^2.\)

This gives us a final value of \[ 2^0 \times 3^2 = 1 \times 9 = 9. \]

Thus, C is the correct answer.

洛拉、洛洛、蒂娅和蒂约参加乒乓球比赛。每位选手都与另外三位选手各比赛两次。下方显示了选手们的胜负记录。数字 \(1\) 和 \(0\) 分别表示胜和负。例如，洛拉赢了五场，输了第四场。蒂约的胜负记录是什么？

9单选

玛莱卡在山上滑雪。下图显示了她沿雪道滑行时，相对于山脚的海拔高度（米）。她总共有多少秒处在 \(4\) 米到 \(7\) 米之间的海拔高度？

English（选项同上方）

Malaika is skiing on a mountain. The graph below shows her elevation, in meters, above the base of the mountain as she skis along a trail. In total, how many seconds does she spend at an elevation between \(4\) and \(7\) meters?

她第一次达到 \(7\) 米是在 \(2\) 秒时。

随后她在 \(4\) 秒后低于 \(4\) 米，这一段贡献 \(4 - 2 = 2\) 秒。

接着她在 \(6\) 秒时重新高于 \(4\) 米，并在 \(10\) 秒时再次达到 \(7\) 米。

这一段贡献 \(10 - 6 = 4\) 秒。最后她在 \(12\) 秒时低于 \(7\) 米。

再到 \(14\) 秒时她低于 \(4\) 米，最后一段贡献 \(14 - 12 = 2\) 秒。

三个时间段的总长度为 \[ 2 + 4 + 2 = 8 \] 所以正确答案是 B。

English

The first time that she hits an elevation of \(7\) meters is at \(2\) seconds.

She then dips below \(4\) meters after \(4\) seconds. This adds \(4 - 2 = 2\) seconds to the total answer.

Malaika then goes above \(4\) meters at \(6\) seconds. She hits \(7\) meters again at \(10\) seconds.

This adds \(10 - 6 = 4\) more seconds to the total. She finally dips below \(7\) meters for the last time at \(12\) seconds.

She then falls below \(4\) meters at \(14\) seconds, finally adding \(14 - 12 = 2\) seconds to the total time.

The desired total is \[ 2 + 4 + 2 = 8 \] Thus, B is the correct answer.

10单选

哈罗德做了一个李子派去野餐。他只吃了这个派的 \(\frac{1}{4}\)，把剩下的留给朋友。一只驼鹿经过，吃掉了哈罗德留下部分的 \(\frac{1}{3}\)。之后，一只豪猪又吃掉了驼鹿留下部分的 \(\frac{1}{3}\)。豪猪离开后，原来的派还剩多少？

English（选项同上方）

Harold made a plum pie to take on a picnic. He was able to eat only \(\frac{1}{4}\) of the pie, and he left the rest for his friends. A moose came by and ate \(\frac{1}{3}\) of what Harold left behind. After that, a porcupine ate \(\frac{1}{3}\) of what the moose left behind. How much of the original pie still remained after the porcupine left?

哈罗德把整个派的 \(1 - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{3}{4}\) 留给了朋友。

驼鹿吃掉其中 \(\frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{3}{4} = \frac{1}{4}\)，所以剩下 \(\frac{3}{4} - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{2}\)。

豪猪再吃掉剩余的 \(\frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{6}\)，所以最后剩下 \(\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{6} = \frac{1}{3}\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Harold left \(1 - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{3}{4}\) of the pie for his friends.

The moose ate \(\frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{3}{4} = \frac{1}{4}\) of the pie, leaving \(\frac{3}{4} - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{2}\) of the pie.

Finally, the porcupine ate \(\frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{6}\) of the pie. This leaves \(\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{6} = \frac{1}{3}\) of the pie.

Thus, D is the correct answer.

NASA’s Perseverance Rover was launched on July \(30,\) \(2020.\) After traveling \(292{,}526{,}838\) miles, it landed on Mars in Jezero Crater about \(6.5\) months later. Which of the following is closest to the Rover’s average interplanetary speed in miles per hour?

把距离近似为 \(300{,}000{,}000\) 英里，并把 \(6.5\) 个月近似为 \[ 30 \times 6.5 = 195 \approx 200 \] 天。

于是每天约行进 \[ 300{,}000{,}000 \div 200 = 1{,}500{,}000 \] 英里。

再除以 \(24\)，得到约 \(62{,}500\) 英里每小时，最接近 \(60{,}000\)。

Thus, C is the correct answer.

12单选

下图显示一个大的未涂色圆，内部有若干较小的未涂色圆和阴影圆。大未涂色圆内部的面积中，阴影部分占几分之几？

English（选项同上方）

The figure below shows a large unshaded circle with a number of smaller unshaded and shaded circles in its interior. What fraction of the interior of the large unshaded circle is shaded?

不妨设每个最小圆的半径为 \(1\)。

这样有 \(3\) 个阴影单位圆，总面积为 \(3 \cdot 1^2 \pi = 3 \pi\)。

另外还有一个半径为 \(4\) 的阴影圆，其中挖去了两个半径为 \(2\) 的未涂色圆。

这部分额外阴影面积为 \[\begin{align*} 4^2 \pi - 2 \cdot 2^2 \pi &= 16 \pi - 8 \pi \\ &= 8 \pi\text{。} \end{align*}\]

阴影总面积为 \[8\pi + 3\pi = 11\pi\text{。}\]大未涂色圆的面积为 \[6^2\pi = 36\pi\text{。}\]所以所求分数为 \[\dfrac{11\pi}{36\pi} = \dfrac{11}{36}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Let each smallest circle have radius \(1.\)

This means that there are \(3\) shaded unit circles, which total to \(3 \cdot 1^2 \pi = 3 \pi\) area.

There is also a shaded circle with radius \(4\) with two unshaded circles of radius \(2\) inside.

This gives us an extra shaded area of \[\begin{align*} 4^2 \pi - 2 \cdot 2^2 \pi &= 16 \pi - 8 \pi \\ &= 8 \pi. \end{align*}\]

The total shaded area is \[ 8\pi + 3\pi = 11\pi. \] The area of the large unshaded circle is \[ 6^2\pi = 36\pi. \] Therefore, the desired fraction is \[ \dfrac{11\pi}{36\pi} = \dfrac{11}{36}. \]

Thus, B is the correct answer.

13单选

在自行车赛路线中，起点和终点之间均匀分布着 \(7\) 个补水站，如下图所示。另外还有 \(2\) 个维修站也均匀分布在起点和终点之间。第 \(3\) 个补水站位于第 \(1\) 个维修站之后 \(2\) 英里处。这场比赛全长多少英里？

English（选项同上方）

Along the route of a bicycle race, \(7\) water stations are evenly spaced between the start and finish lines, as shown in the figure below. There are also \(2\) repair stations evenly spaced between the start and finish lines. The \(3\)rd water station is located \(2\) miles after the \(1\)st repair station. How long is the race in miles?

第 \(3\) 个补水站在全程的 \(\frac{3}{8}\) 处，因为补水站把全程分成 \(8\) 个相等间隔。

第一个维修站在全程的 \(\frac{1}{3}\) 处。两站之间的距离占全程的 \[ \dfrac{3}{8} - \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{1}{24}\text{。}\]这段距离是 \(2\) 英里，所以全程为 \(24 \cdot 2 = 48\) 英里。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The \(3\)rd water station is located \(\frac{3}{8}\) of the way along the race (the water stations split the race up into \(8\) equal spaces).

The first repair station is located \(\frac{1}{3}\) of the way along the race. The distance between the stations is \[ \dfrac{3}{8} - \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{1}{24} \] of the race length. This distance is \(2\) miles, so the race is \(24 \cdot 2 = 48\) miles long.

Thus, D is the correct answer.

14单选

尼古拉计划给朋友安东寄一个包裹，安东是集邮爱好者。为了支付邮资，尼古拉想用很多邮票贴满包裹。假设他有 \(5\) 分、\(10\) 分和 \(25\) 分邮票，每种正好 \(20\) 张。尼古拉最多能用多少张邮票凑成正好 \(\$7.10\) 的邮资？

（注意：\(\$7.10\) 表示 \(7\) 美元 \(10\) 美分。一美元等于 \(100\) 美分。）

English（选项同上方）

Nicolas is planning to send a package to his friend Anton, who is a stamp collector. To pay for the postage, Nicolas would like to cover the package with a large number of stamps. Suppose he has a collection of \(5\)-cent, \(10\)-cent, and \(25\)-cent stamps, with exactly \(20\) of each type. What is the greatest number of stamps Nicolas can use to make exactly \(\$7.10\) in postage?

(Note: The amount \(\$7.10\) corresponds to \(7\) dollars and \(10\) cents. One dollar is worth \(100\) cents.)

设 \(a\)、\(b\)、\(c\) 分别为 \(5\) 分、\(10\) 分、\(25\) 分邮票的张数，并设 \(n=a+b+c\)。金额方程除以 \(5\) 得 \[ a+2b+5c=142\text{，}\]所以 \(n+b+4c=142\)。

假设 \(n\ge56\)，则 \(b+4c\le86\)。又因为 \[ a=142-2b-5c\le20\text{，}\]所以 \(2b+5c\ge122\)。合并这两个不等式可得 \(c\le16\)，但这样 \(2b+5c\le2(20)+5(16)=120\)，矛盾。因此最多只能使用 \(55\) 张邮票。

取 \(a=19\)、\(b=19\)、\(c=17\) 时可以达到这个上界：\[ 19(5)+19(10)+17(25)=710\text{。}\]此时邮票总数为 \(19+19+17=55\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Let \(a,\) \(b,\) \(c\) be the numbers of \(5\)-, \(10\)-, and \(25\)-cent stamps, and let \(n=a+b+c.\) Dividing the value equation by \(5\) gives \[ a+2b+5c=142, \] so \(n+b+4c=142.\)

Suppose \(n\ge56.\) Then \(b+4c\le86.\) Also \[ a=142-2b-5c\le20, \] so \(2b+5c\ge122.\) Combining these inequalities gives \(c\le16,\) but then \(2b+5c\le2(20)+5(16)=120,\) a contradiction. Thus at most \(55\) stamps can be used.

The bound is attainable with \(a=19,\) \(b=19,\) \(c=17:\) \[ 19(5)+19(10)+17(25)=710. \] Hence the greatest possible number of stamps is \(19+19+17=55.\)

Thus, E is the correct answer.

15单选

维斯瓦姆每天步行半英里去学校。他的路线由 \(10\) 个等长街区组成，每走一个街区需要 \(1\) 分钟。今天，在走了 \(5\) 个街区后，维斯瓦姆发现他必须绕路，要走 \(3\) 个等长街区而不是一个街区才能到达下一个街角。从他开始绕路时起，他必须以多少英里每小时的速度步行，才能按平常时间到达学校？

English（选项同上方）

Viswam walks half a mile to get to school each day. His route consists of \(10\) city blocks of equal length and he takes one minute to walk each block. Today, after walking \(5\) blocks, Viswam discovers that he has to make a detour, walking \(3\) blocks of equal length instead of \(1\) block to reach the next corner. From the time he starts his detour, at what speed, in miles per hour, must Viswam walk in order to arrive at school at his usual time?

半英里对应 \(10\) 个街区，所以一个街区长 \(\dfrac{1}{2} \div 10 = \dfrac{1}{20}\) 英里。

从绕路开始，维斯瓦姆需要走 \(7 \cdot \dfrac{1}{20} = \dfrac{7}{20}\) 英里。

正常情况下，从这个位置到学校需要 \(5\) 分钟。现在他需要在这 \(5\) 分钟内走完 \(\dfrac{7}{20}\) 英里。

注意 \(5\) 分钟等于 \(\dfrac{5}{60} = \dfrac{1}{12}\) 小时，所以所需速度为 \[\begin{align*} \dfrac{\frac{7}{20}}{\frac{1}{12}} &= 12 \cdot \dfrac{7}{20} \\&= \dfrac{21}{5} \\ &= 4.2 \end{align*}\] 英里每小时。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

If half a mile is the same as \(10\) blocks, then one block is \(\dfrac{1}{2} \div 10 = \dfrac{1}{20}\) miles.

Starting from the detour, Viswam has to walk \(7 \cdot \dfrac{1}{20} = \dfrac{7}{20}\) miles.

Normally, from this spot Viswam would take \(5\) minutes to walk to school. Now he has to travel \(\dfrac{7}{20}\) miles in \(5\) minutes.

Note that \(5\) minutes is \(\dfrac{5}{60} = \dfrac{1}{12}\) hours. This means his speed must be \[\begin{align*} \dfrac{\frac{7}{20}}{\frac{1}{12}} &= 12 \cdot \dfrac{7}{20} \\&= \dfrac{21}{5} \\ &= 4.2 \end{align*}\] miles per hour.

Thus, B is the correct answer.

The letters \(P,\) \(Q,\) and \(R\) are entered into a \(20 \times 20\) table according to the pattern shown below. How many \(P\)s, \(Q\)s, and \(R\)s will appear in the completed table?

因为 \(20 = 3 \cdot 6 + 2\)，每列底部的 \(2\) 个字母会比第三个字母多出现一次。

所以每列中第三个字母出现 \(6\) 次，另外 \(2\) 个字母各出现 \(7\) 次。

在 \(20\) 列中，有 \(7\) 列的 \(P\) 只出现 \(6\) 次，其余 \(13\) 列中则出现 \(7\) 次。\(R\) 的情况相同。

因此 \(P\) 和 \(R\) 都各出现 \(7\cdot6+13\cdot7=42+91=133\) 次。

因此 \(Q\) 出现了 \[\begin{align*} 20 \cdot 20 - 2 \cdot 133 &=400 - 266 \\ &= 134 \end{align*}\] 次。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Since \(20 = 3 \cdot 6 + 2,\) the bottom \(2\) letters in each column will occur one more time than the third letter.

This means that the third letter in each column will occur \(6\) times, whereas the other \(2\) will appear \(7\) times.

Across the \(20\) columns, \(P\) is the letter that appears \(6\) times in \(7\) columns and appears \(7\) times in the other \(13\) columns. The same is true of \(R.\)

Thus \(P\) and \(R\) each appear \(7\cdot6+13\cdot7=42+91=133\) times.

\(Q\) will therefore appear \[\begin{align*} 20 \cdot 20 - 2 \cdot 133 &=400 - 266 \\ &= 134 \end{align*}\] times.

Thus, C is the correct answer.

Greta Grasshopper sits on a long line of lily pads in a pond. From any lily pad, Greta can jump \(5\) pads to the right or \(3\) pads to the left. What is the fewest number of jumps Greta must make to reach the lily pad located \(2023\) pads to the right of her starting position?

设向右跳 \(r\) 次，向左跳 \(l\) 次，则需要 \[ 5r-3l=2023\text{。}\]模 \(5\) 化简得 \(-3l\equiv3\pmod5\)，所以 \(l\equiv4\pmod5\)。

为了最少跳跃，取满足条件的最小 \(l\)，也就是 \(l=4\)。此时 \(5r-12=2023\)，所以 \(r=407\)。

总跳数为 \(407+4=411\)。

English

Let \(r\) be the number of right jumps and \(l\) be the number of left jumps. We need \[ 5r-3l=2023. \] Reducing modulo \(5\) gives \(-3l\equiv3\pmod5\), so \(l\equiv4\pmod5\).

To minimize the total number of jumps, use the smallest possible \(l\), namely \(l=4\). Then \(5r-12=2023\), so \(r=407\).

The fewest number of jumps is \(407+4=411\).

Thus, D is the correct answer.

一个等边三角形放在一个更大的等边三角形内部，使得它们之间的区域可分成三个全等的梯形，如下图所示。内三角形边长是大三角形边长的 \(\frac{2}{3}\)。一个梯形的面积与内三角形的面积之比是多少？

English（选项同上方）

An equilateral triangle is placed inside a larger equilateral triangle so that the region between them can be divided into three congruent trapezoids, as shown below. The side length of the inner triangle is \(\frac{2}{3}\) the side length of the larger triangle. What is the ratio of the area of one trapezoid to the area of the inner triangle?

内三角形边长是大三角形的 \(\frac{2}{3}\)，所以面积是大三角形的 \(\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^2 = \frac{4}{9}\)。

三个梯形总面积是大三角形的 \(1 - \frac{4}{9} = \frac{5}{9}\)。

一个梯形面积是大三角形的 \(\frac{5}{9} \div 3 = \frac{5}{27}\)。

它与内三角形的面积比为 \[ \dfrac{\frac{5}{27}}{\frac{4}{9}} = \dfrac{5}{27} \cdot \dfrac{9}{4} = \dfrac{5}{12}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Since the inner triangle’s side length is \(\frac{2}{3}\) the side length of the outer triangle, its area is \(\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^2 = \frac{4}{9}\) the area of the outer triangle.

This means that the three trapezoids are \(1 - \frac{4}{9} = \frac{5}{9}\) the area of the outer triangle.

Therefore, one trapezoid is \(\frac{5}{9} \div 3 = \frac{5}{27}\) the area of the outer triangle.

This makes the ratio of the areas of one trapezoid and the inner triangle \[ \dfrac{\frac{5}{27}}{\frac{4}{9}} = \dfrac{5}{27} \cdot \dfrac{9}{4} = \dfrac{5}{12}. \]

Thus, C is the correct answer.

20单选

在列表 \(3\)、\(3\)、\(8\)、\(11\)、\(28\) 中插入两个整数，使其极差变为原来的两倍。众数和中位数保持不变。这两个新增数字的和最大可能是多少？

English（选项同上方）

Two integers are inserted into the list \(3,\) \(3,\) \(8,\) \(11,\) \(28\) to double its range. The mode and median remain unchanged. What is the maximum possible sum of the two additional numbers?

原来的极差是 \(28-3=25\)，所以新极差必须是 \(50\)。要保持中位数为 \(8\)，新增的一个数 \(x\) 必须小于 \(8\)，另一个数 \(y\) 必须大于 \(8\)。

若 \(x\ge3\)，最小值仍为 \(3\)，所以新的最大值必须是 \(53\)。取 \(x=3\) 会改变众数，因此 \(x\le7\)。所以 \(x+y\le7+53=60\)。

若 \(x<3\) 且 \(y>28\)，则 \(y-x=50\)，所以 \(x+y=2x+50\le54\)。若 \(y\le28\)，最大值仍为 \(28\)，这会迫使 \(x=-22\)，得到的和更小。因此没有任何情况能超过 \(60\)。

取 \(x=7\) 和 \(y=53\) 时，众数和中位数都不变，极差为 \(50\)，所以最大和是 \(60\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The original range is \(28-3=25,\) so the new range must be \(50.\) To keep the median \(8,\) one added number \(x\) must be less than \(8\) and the other, \(y,\) must be greater than \(8.\)

If \(x\ge3,\) the minimum remains \(3,\) so the new maximum must be \(53.\) The value \(x=3\) would change the mode, so \(x\le7.\) Hence \(x+y\le7+53=60.\)

If \(x<3\) and \(y>28,\) then \(y-x=50,\) so \(x+y=2x+50\le54.\) If \(y\le28,\) the maximum remains \(28,\) forcing \(x=-22\) and giving an even smaller sum. Therefore no case exceeds \(60.\)

The values \(x=7\) and \(y=53\) preserve the mode and median and give range \(50,\) so the maximum sum is \(60.\)

Thus, D is the correct answer.

Alina writes the numbers \(1,\) \(2,\) \(\cdots,\) \(9\) on separate cards, one number per card. She wishes to divide the cards into \(3\) groups of \(3\) cards so that the sum of the numbers in each group will be the same. In how many ways can this be done?

含 \(9\) 的组中，另外两个数和为 \(6\)。可能是 \(1\) 和 \(5\)，或 \(2\) 和 \(4\)。

情况 \(1\)：一组是 \(1\)、\(5\) 和 \(9\)。

考虑含 \(8\) 的那组，另外两个数必须和为 \(7\)。唯一选择是 \(3\) 和 \(4\)。

剩下的一组是 \(2\)、\(6\) 和 \(7\)，其和为 \(15\)，所以这种情况给出一种分法。

情况 \(2\)：一组是 \(2\)、\(4\) 和 \(9\)。

同理，含 \(8\) 的那组另外两个数和为 \(7\)。唯一选择是 \(1\) 和 \(6\)。

最后一组是 \(3\)、\(5\) 和 \(7\)，其和为 \(15\)，这又给出一种分法。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The sum of all the numbers is \(\dfrac{9 \cdot 10}{2} = 45.\) This means that the sum of each group is \(45 \div 3 = 15.\)

Consider the group with \(9\) in it. The other two numbers must add to \(6.\) Therefore, the other cards in this group are \(1\) and \(5\) or \(2\) and \(4.\)

Case \(1:\) One group is \(1,\) \(5,\) and \(9.\)

Consider the group with \(8\) in it. The other numbers must add to \(7.\) The only option is \(3\) and \(4\) with the remaining cards.

The other group is then \(2,\) \(6,\) and \(7.\) This adds to \(15,\) so this case contributes one possibility.

Case \(2:\) One group is \(2,\) \(4,\) and \(9.\)

Consider the group with \(8\) in it. As above, the other numbers have to add to \(7.\) The only option is \(1\) and \(6.\)

The final group is \(3,\) \(5,\) and \(7,\) which adds to \(15.\) This is another configuration.

We have gone through all the cases, which revealed that there are only \(2\) possible groupings.

Thus, C is the correct answer.

23单选

一个 \(3 \times 3\) 网格中的每个小方格随机填入右下方所示 \(4\) 种阴影和非阴影瓷砖之一。

English（选项同上方）

Each square in a \(3 \times 3\) grid is randomly filled with one of the \(4\) shaded-and-unshaded tiles shown below on the right.

What is the probability that the tiling will contain a large shaded diamond in one of the smaller \(2 \times 2\) grids? Below is an example of such a tiling.

总铺法数为 \(4^9\)。可形成大阴影菱形的 \(2\times2\) 小网格有 \(4\) 个。

选定其中一个 \(2\times2\) 小网格后，它内部四块瓷砖的方向就被确定了，其余 \(5\) 个方格可以任意填。所以每选定一个这样的 \(2\times2\) 小网格，都对应 \(4^5\) 种铺法。

两个不同的 \(2\times2\) 小网格不能同时形成大阴影菱形，因为重叠方格会要求不同方向。因此有利铺法数为 \(4\cdot4^5=4^6\)。

所求概率为 \[ \dfrac{4^6}{4^9} = \dfrac{1}{4^3} = \dfrac{1}{64}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

There are \(4^9\) possible tilings. There are \(4\) possible \(2\times2\) grids where a large shaded diamond could appear.

After one of these \(2\times2\) grids is chosen, the four tile orientations inside it are forced, and the other \(5\) squares can be filled in any way. This gives \(4^5\) tilings for each chosen \(2\times2\) grid.

Two different \(2\times2\) grids cannot both contain a large shaded diamond, because their overlapping squares would require incompatible tile orientations. Therefore the number of favorable tilings is \(4\cdot4^5=4^6.\)

The desired probability is then \[ \dfrac{4^6}{4^9} = \dfrac{1}{4^3} = \dfrac{1}{64}. \]

Thus, C is the correct answer.

Fifteen integers \(a_1,\) \(a_2,\) \(a_3,\) \(\cdots,\) \(a_{15}\) are arranged in order on a number line. The integers are equally spaced and have the property that

\[ 1 \leq a_1 \leq 10, \]
