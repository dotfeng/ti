1单选

一个圆的直径增加 \(\pi\) 个单位后，周长增加 \(P\) 个单位。则 \(P\) 等于：

English（选项同上方）

Let \(P\) units be the increase in the circumference of a circle resulting from an increase of \(\pi\) units in the diameter. Then \(P\) equals:

原周长为 \(\pi d\)。直径增加 \(\pi\) 后，周长为 \(\pi(d+\pi)\)。因此增加量为 \(\pi(d+\pi)-\pi d=\pi^2\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

The original circumference is \(\pi d.\) After the diameter increases by \(\pi,\) it is \(\pi(d+\pi).\) The increase is therefore \(\pi(d+\pi)-\pi d=\pi^2.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is D.

English（选项同上方）

The real value of \(x\) such that \(64^{x-1}\) divided by \(4^{x-1}\) equals \(256^{2x}\) is:

左边为 \((\frac{64}{4})^{x-1}=16^{x-1}\)，而 \(256^{2x}=(16^2)^{2x}=16^{4x}\)。因此 \(x-1=4x\)，所以 \(x=-\frac{1}{3}\)。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

The left side is \((\frac{64}{4})^{x-1}=16^{x-1},\) while \(256^{2x}=(16^2)^{2x}=16^{4x}.\) Hence \(x-1=4x,\) so \(x=-\frac{1}{3}.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is B.

English（选项同上方）

If \(f(n)=\dfrac13n(n+1)(n+2),\) then \(f(r)-f(r-1)\) equals:

有 \(f(r)=\frac13r(r+1)(r+2)\) 和 \(f(r-1)=\frac13(r-1)r(r+1)\)。两者之差可写成 \(\frac13r(r+1)\) 乘以 \((r+2)-(r-1)=3\)。结果为 \(r(r+1)\)。

English

Therefore, the correct answer is A.

English（选项同上方）

Let side \(AD\) of convex quadrilateral \(ABCD\) be extended through \(D,\) and let side \(BC\) be extended through \(C,\) to meet in point \(E.\) Let \(S\) represent the degree-sum of angles \(CDE\) and \(DCE,\) and let \(S'\) represent the degree-sum of angles \(BAD\) and \(ABC.\) If \(r=\frac{S}{S'},\) then:

在三角形 \(ECD\) 中，\(S=180^\circ-\angle E\)。由于 \(D\) 在 \(AE\) 上，\(C\) 在 \(BE\) 上，由三角形 \(EAB\) 同样得到 \(S'=180^\circ-\angle E\)。因此 \(S=S'\)，且 \(r=1\)。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

In triangle \(ECD,\) \(S=180^\circ-\angle E.\) Since \(D\) lies on \(AE\) and \(C\) lies on \(BE,\) triangle \(EAB\) gives \(S'=180^\circ-\angle E\) as well. Thus \(S=S'\) and \(r=1.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is E.

7单选

设 \(O\) 是三角形 \(ABC\) 的两条中线 \(AP\) 与 \(CQ\) 的交点。若 \(OQ\) 长 \(3\) 英寸，则 \(OP\) 的长度（英寸）为：

English（选项同上方）

Let \(O\) be the intersection point of medians \(AP\) and \(CQ\) of triangle \(ABC.\) If \(OQ\) is \(3\) inches, then \(OP,\) in inches, is:

重心按 \(2:1\) 分割每条中线，所以 \(OQ=3\) 可得 \(CQ=9\)。但这并未给出两条不同中线 \(CQ\) 与 \(AP\) 的长度关系。满足 \(CQ=9\) 的三角形可以有不同的 \(AP\)，所以 \(OP\) 无法确定。

English

The centroid divides each median in a \(2:1\) ratio, so \(OQ=3\) determines \(CQ=9.\) It gives no relation between the lengths of the two different medians \(CQ\) and \(AP.\) Triangles with \(CQ=9\) can have different \(AP,\) so \(OP\) is undetermined.

Therefore, the correct answer is E.

假设对某所学校，下列陈述为真：

\(\mathrm{I}\)：有些学生不诚实。

\(\mathrm{II}\)：所有兄弟会成员都诚实。

必然可以得出的结论是：

English（选项同上方）

Assume that, for a certain school, it is true that

\(\mathrm{I}:\) Some students are not honest.

\(\mathrm{II}:\) All fraternity members are honest.

A necessary conclusion is:

陈述 \(\mathrm{I}\) 保证存在一名不诚实的学生。陈述 \(\mathrm{II}\) 说明每位兄弟会成员都诚实，所以这名不诚实的学生不可能是兄弟会成员。因此，有些学生不是兄弟会成员。

设 \(P\) 是任意三个连续正奇数的乘积。所有这样的 \(P\) 都能被整除的最大整数为：

English（选项同上方）

Let \(P\) be the product of any three consecutive positive odd integers. The largest integer dividing all such \(P\) is:

三个连续奇数模 \(3\) 的余数依次连续，所以其中一个能被 \(3\) 整除。因此每个这样的乘积都能被 \(3\) 整除。两个乘积 \(1\cdot3\cdot5=15\) 与 \(7\cdot9\cdot11=693\) 的最大公因数为 \(3\)，所以不存在总能整除该乘积的更大整数。

English

Three consecutive odd integers occupy three consecutive residues modulo \(3,\) so one is divisible by \(3.\) Thus every product is divisible by \(3.\) The products \(1\cdot3\cdot5=15\) and \(7\cdot9\cdot11=693\) have greatest common divisor \(3,\) so no larger integer always divides the product.

Therefore, the correct answer is D.

17单选

设 \(f(n)=\dfrac{x_1+x_2+\cdots+x_n}{n}\)，其中 \(n\) 为正整数。若 \(x_k=(-1)^k\)，其中 \(k=1\)、\(2\)、\(\ldots\)、\(n\)，则 \(f(n)\) 的所有可能值组成的集合为：

English（选项同上方）

Let \(f(n)=\dfrac{x_1+x_2+\cdots+x_n}{n},\) where \(n\) is a positive integer. If \(x_k=(-1)^k,\) \(k=1,\) \(2,\) \(\ldots,\) \(n,\) the set of possible values of \(f(n)\) is:

若 \(n\) 为偶数，各项两两配对后和为 \(0\)，所以 \(f(n)=0\)。若 \(n\) 为奇数，最后还剩一个 \(-1\)，所以 \(f(n)=-\frac{1}{n}\)。因此所有可能值为 \(\{0,-\frac{1}{n}\}\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

If \(n\) is even, the terms pair to give sum \(0,\) so \(f(n)=0.\) If \(n\) is odd, one final \(-1\) remains, so \(f(n)=-\frac{1}{n}.\) The possible values are therefore \(\{0,-\frac{1}{n}\}.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is C.

18单选

三角形 \(ABC\) 的边 \(AB\) 长 \(8\) 英寸。作直线 \(DEF\) 平行于 \(AB\)，其中 \(D\) 在线段 \(AC\) 上，\(E\) 在线段 \(BC\) 上。直线 \(AE\) 的延长线平分角 \(FEC\)。若 \(DE\) 长 \(5\) 英寸，则 \(CE\) 的长度（英寸）为：

English（选项同上方）

Side \(AB\) of triangle \(ABC\) has length \(8\) inches. Line \(DEF\) is drawn parallel to \(AB\) so that \(D\) is on segment \(AC,\) and \(E\) is on segment \(BC.\) Line \(AE\) extended bisects angle \(FEC.\) If \(DE\) has length \(5\) inches, then the length of \(CE,\) in inches, is:

在 \(AE\) 过 \(E\) 的延长线上取点 \(G\)。由角平分线条件，\(\angle FEG=\angle GEC\)。由于 \(FE\parallel AB\)，有 \(\angle FEG=\angle BAE\)。又因为射线 \(EG\) 与 \(EA\) 方向相反，射线 \(EC\) 与 \(EB\) 方向也相反，所以 \(\angle GEC=\angle AEB\)。因此三角形 \(ABE\) 为等腰三角形，且 \(BE=AB=8\)。由相似三角形 \(CDE\) 与 \(CAB\) 得 \(\frac{CE}{CE+8}=\frac58\)。因此 \(8CE=5CE+40\)，所以 \(CE=\frac{40}{3}\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

Put \(G\) on the extension of \(AE\) beyond \(E.\) The bisector condition gives \(\angle FEG=\angle GEC.\) Since \(FE\parallel AB,\) we have \(\angle FEG=\angle BAE.\) Also, rays \(EG\) and \(EA\) are opposite, as are rays \(EC\) and \(EB,\) so \(\angle GEC=\angle AEB.\) Thus triangle \(ABE\) is isosceles and \(BE=AB=8.\) Similar triangles \(CDE\) and \(CAB\) give \(\frac{CE}{CE+8}=\frac58.\) Hence \(8CE=5CE+40,\) so \(CE=\frac{40}{3}.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is D.

19单选

将 \(10\) 美元兑换成一角硬币和二角五分硬币，且每种硬币至少使用一枚。设兑换方法数为 \(n\)，则 \(n\) 等于：

English（选项同上方）

Let \(n\) be the number of ways that \(10\) dollars can be changed into dimes and quarters, with at least one of each coin being used. Then \(n\) equals:

方程 \(10d+25q=1000\) 化简为 \(2d+5q=200\)。因此 \(q\) 必须为偶数；令 \(q=2k\)，则 \(d=100-5k\)。由两者均为正数可得 \(k=1\)、\(2\)、\(\ldots\)、\(19\)，所以共有 \(19\) 种方法。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

The equation \(10d+25q=1000\) reduces to \(2d+5q=200.\) Thus \(q\) must be even; write \(q=2k,\) giving \(d=100-5k.\) Positivity requires \(k=1,\) \(2,\) \(\ldots,\) \(19,\) so there are \(19\) ways.

Therefore, the correct answer is E.

20单选

一个 \(n\) 边凸多边形的内角度数成等差数列。若公差为 \(5^\circ\)，最大角为 \(160^\circ\)，则 \(n\) 等于：

English（选项同上方）

The measures of the interior angles of a convex polygon of \(n\) sides are in arithmetic progression. If the common difference is \(5^\circ\) and the largest angle is \(160^\circ,\) then \(n\) equals:

最小角为 \(160-5(n-1)\)。内角和为 \(\frac n2\) 乘以 \(320-5(n-1)\)，并且等于 \(180(n-2)\)。化简得 \[ \begin{aligned} n^2+7n-144&=0,\\ (n-9)(n+16)&=0\text{。} \end{aligned} \] 因此 \(n=9\)。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

The smallest angle is \(160-5(n-1).\) The angle sum is \(\frac n2\) times \(320-5(n-1),\) and it equals \(180(n-2).\) This simplifies to \[ \begin{aligned} n^2+7n-144&=0,\\ (n-9)(n+16)&=0. \end{aligned} \] Thus \(n=9.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is A.

23单选

若所有对数均为实数，则等式

\[ \begin{aligned} \log(x+3)+\log(x-1)\\ =\log(x^2-2x-3) \end{aligned} \]

对下列哪种情形成立：

English（选项同上方）

If all the logarithms are real numbers, the equality

\[ \begin{aligned} \log(x+3)+\log(x-1)\\ =\log(x^2-2x-3) \end{aligned} \]

is satisfied for:

合并对数要求 \((x+3)(x-1)=x^2-2x-3\)。由此得到 \(x=0\)。但此时 \(x-1=-1\)，所以左边的对数不是实数。因此没有实数解。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English
