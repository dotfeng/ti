3 答案 A

第 \(n\) 项由 \(n\) 个一、一个 \(2\)，再接 \(n\) 个一组成。它可分解为循环单位数与形如 \(10^n + 1\) 的数之积。例如 \[ \begin{aligned} &121 = 11 \cdot 11, \\ &\quad 11211 = 111 \cdot 101, \\ &\quad 1112111 = 1111 \cdot 1001\text{，} \end{aligned} \] 一般地，第 \(n\) 项等于 \(\underbrace{1\cdots1}_{n+1} \cdot (10^n + 1)\)。

对每个 \(n \ge 1\)，两个因子都大于 \(1\)，所以每一项都是合数。前十项中没有质数。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The \(n\)th term consists of \(n\) ones, then a \(2,\) then \(n\) ones. It factors as a repunit times a number of the form \(10^n + 1:\) \[ \begin{aligned} &121 = 11 \cdot 11, \\ &\quad 11211 = 111 \cdot 101, \\ &\quad 1112111 = 1111 \cdot 1001, \end{aligned} \] and in general the \(n\)th term equals \(\underbrace{1\cdots1}_{n+1} \cdot (10^n + 1).\)

For every \(n \ge 1\) both factors exceed \(1,\) so every term is composite. None of the ten numbers is prime.

Thus, the correct answer is A.

4 答案 B

面积和为 \(4 \cdot \pi(3)^2 + 4 \cdot \pi(5)^2\) \(= 36\pi + 100\pi = 136\pi\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The first two circles are concentric with radii \(2\) and \(8.\) A circle tangent to both either has radius \(3\) with center at distance \(5\) from the origin, or radius \(5\) with center at distance \(3\) from the origin.

The third circle has center \((5, 0)\) and radius \(\sqrt3.\) For each candidate radius \(s\in\{3,5\},\) tangency requires the center’s distance from \((5,0)\) to be \(s+\sqrt3\) or \(s-\sqrt3.\) Each of these two distance circles intersects the appropriate center-locus in two symmetric points. Hence exactly four radius-\(3\) circles and four radius-\(5\) circles work.

The sum of the areas is \(4 \cdot \pi(3)^2 + 4 \cdot \pi(5)^2\) \(= 36\pi + 100\pi = 136\pi.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

22 答案 C

因为每个 \(t_n \lt 1\)，Amelia 一定至少完成两步。她恰好两步后停止当且仅当 \(t_1 + t_2 \gt 1\)，其概率为 \(\tfrac12\)；否则她走满三步。

位移与时间独立。若走两步，位置为 \(x_1 + x_2\)，且 \(P(x_1 + x_2 \gt 1) = \tfrac12\)。若走三步，位置为 \(x_1 + x_2 + x_3\)，且 \(P(x_1 + x_2 + x_3 \gt 1)\) \(= 1 - \tfrac16 = \tfrac56\)。

所求概率为 \(\tfrac12 \cdot \tfrac12 + \tfrac12 \cdot \tfrac56 = \tfrac14 + \tfrac{5}{12} = \tfrac23\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Because each \(t_n \lt 1,\) Amelia always completes at least two steps. She stops after exactly two steps when \(t_1 + t_2 \gt 1,\) which happens with probability \(\tfrac12;\) otherwise she takes all three steps.

The increments are independent of the times. If she takes two steps, her position is \(x_1 + x_2,\) and \(P(x_1 + x_2 \gt 1) = \tfrac12.\) If she takes three, her position is \(x_1 + x_2 + x_3,\) and \(P(x_1 + x_2 + x_3 \gt 1)\) \(= 1 - \tfrac16 = \tfrac56.\)

The answer is \(\tfrac12 \cdot \tfrac12 + \tfrac12 \cdot \tfrac56 = \tfrac14 + \tfrac{5}{12} = \tfrac23.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

23 答案 A

因为 \(S_n\) 是由低 \(n\) 位构成的整数，条件 \(7S_n \equiv 1\) 表示 \(S_n \equiv 7^{-1} \pmod{2^n}\) 对每个 \(n\) 都成立。因此 \(x_k\) 是以 \(2\) 为底表示 \(\tfrac17\) 时的数位，也就是它作为 \(2\)-进数时的数位。

用 \(2\) 进制长除可得 \(x_0 = x_1 = x_2 = 1\)，之后以 \(3\) 为周期重复：当 \(k \ge 1\) 时，恰在 \(k\) 是 \(3\) 的倍数时有 \(x_k = 0\)，否则 \(x_k = 1\)。

因为 \(2019\) 和 \(2022\) 都是 \(3\) 的倍数，而 \(2020 \equiv 1\)、\(2021 \equiv 2 \pmod 3\)，所以 \(x_{2019} = 0\)、\(x_{2020} = 1\)、\(x_{2021} = 1\)、\(x_{2022} = 0\)。所求和为 \(0 + 2 + 4 + 0 = 6\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Since \(S_n\) is the integer formed by the low \(n\) bits, the condition \(7S_n \equiv 1\) means \(S_n \equiv 7^{-1} \pmod{2^n}\) for every \(n.\) Thus the digits \(x_k\) are the base-\(2\) digits of \(\tfrac17\) as a \(2\)-adic number.

Long division in base \(2\) gives digits \(x_0 = x_1 = x_2 = 1,\) and thereafter the block repeats with period \(3:\) for \(k \ge 1,\) \(x_k = 0\) exactly when \(k\) is divisible by \(3,\) and \(x_k = 1\) otherwise.

Since \(2019\) and \(2022\) are both divisible by \(3,\) while \(2020 \equiv 1\) and \(2021 \equiv 2 \pmod 3,\) we get \(x_{2019} = 0,\) \(x_{2020} = 1,\) \(x_{2021} = 1,\) \(x_{2022} = 0.\) The sum is \(0 + 2 + 4 + 0 = 6.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

24 答案 C

相隔 \(d\) 步的两个顶点之间弦长平方为 \(2 - 2\cos\dfrac{2\pi d}{7}\)，且 \(d = 1, 2, 3\) 时每类有 \(7\) 条弦。因此所求和为 \[ 7 \sum_{d=1}^{3} \left(2 - 2\cos\tfrac{2\pi d}{7}\right)^2\text{。}\]

利用 \(\displaystyle\sum_{d=1}^{3} \cos\tfrac{2\pi d}{7} = -\tfrac12\) 以及 \(\displaystyle\sum_{d=1}^{3} \cos^2\tfrac{2\pi d}{7} = \tfrac54\)，括号内的和展开为 \(4\left(3 + 1 + \tfrac54\right) = 21\)。

因此总和是 \(7 \cdot 21 = 147\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

A chord joining two vertices \(d\) steps apart has squared length \(2 - 2\cos\dfrac{2\pi d}{7},\) and there are \(7\) chords for each of \(d = 1, 2, 3.\) The required sum is \[ 7 \sum_{d=1}^{3} \left(2 - 2\cos\tfrac{2\pi d}{7}\right)^2. \]

Using \(\displaystyle\sum_{d=1}^{3} \cos\tfrac{2\pi d}{7} = -\tfrac12\) and \(\displaystyle\sum_{d=1}^{3} \cos^2\tfrac{2\pi d}{7} = \tfrac54,\) the inner sum expands to \(4\left(3 + 1 + \tfrac54\right) = 21.\)

Therefore the total is \(7 \cdot 21 = 147.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

25 答案 B

将正方形中心置于原点，顶点为 \(\left(\pm\tfrac12, \pm\tfrac12\right)\)。每个六边形与正方形共用一条边，并向正方形的对侧延伸。例如底边上的六边形，其远端上边从 \(\left(-\tfrac12, \sqrt3 - \tfrac12\right)\) 到 \(\left(\tfrac12, \sqrt3 - \tfrac12\right)\)。

外边界是一个 \(12\) 边形，其平边到中心的距离为 \(\sqrt3 - \tfrac12\)，凸顶点包括 \(\left(\sqrt3 - \tfrac12, \tfrac12\right)\)，而相邻六边形的斜边在 \(\left(\tfrac52 - \sqrt3, \tfrac52 - \sqrt3\right)\) 及其对称点相交，形成四个凹入的顶点。

对这 \(12\) 个顶点使用鞋带公式，面积为 \(16\sqrt3 - 23\)，所以 \(m = 16\)，\(n = 3\)，\(p = -23\)，从而 \(m + n + p = -4\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Center the square at the origin with vertices \(\left(\pm\tfrac12, \pm\tfrac12\right).\) Each hexagon shares one edge with the square and extends across to the opposite side; the hexagon on the bottom edge, for instance, has its far (top) edge from \(\left(-\tfrac12, \sqrt3 - \tfrac12\right)\) to \(\left(\tfrac12, \sqrt3 - \tfrac12\right).\)

The outer boundary is a \(12\)-gon with flat edges at distance \(\sqrt3 - \tfrac12\) from the center, convex vertices such as \(\left(\sqrt3 - \tfrac12, \tfrac12\right),\) and four reflex notches where adjacent hexagons’ slanted edges meet, at \(\left(\tfrac52 - \sqrt3, \tfrac52 - \sqrt3\right)\) and its symmetric images.

Applying the shoelace formula to these \(12\) vertices gives area \(16\sqrt3 - 23,\) so \(m = 16,\) \(n = 3,\) \(p = -23,\) and \(m + n + p = -4.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.
