逐题解析

1 答案 B

这个位置左边有 \(1012\) 个人，右边有 \(1009\) 个人。再加上这个人自己，总人数为 \(1012 + 1009 + 1 = 2022\)。也可以看作两个位置计数在这个人处重合一次，所以 \(1013 + 1010 - 1 = 2022\)。因此正确答案是 B。

English

There are \(1012\) people to the left of this spot and \(1009\) to the right. Add those two groups plus the person themselves: \(1012 + 1009 + 1 = 2022.\) Or, just as fast, the two positions overlap on one person, so \(1013 + 1010 - 1 = 2022.\) Thus, B is the correct answer.

2 答案 B

有 \(10! = 10 \cdot 9 \cdot 8 \cdot 7! = 720 \cdot 7!\)。而 \(720 = 6!\)，所以 \(10! = 6! \cdot 7! = 7! \cdot 6!\)。两项相同，因此 \(10! - 7! \cdot 6! = 0\)。正确答案是 B。

English

Write \(10! = 10 \cdot 9 \cdot 8 \cdot 7! = 720 \cdot 7!.\) But \(720 = 6!\) too, so \(10! = 6! \cdot 7! = 7! \cdot 6!.\) The two terms are the same. That makes \(10! - 7! \cdot 6! = 0.\) Therefore, the answer is B.

3 答案 E

两边除以 \(2\)，得 \(|x| \le 3.5\pi \approx 10.996\)。满足条件的整数从 \(-10\) 到 \(10\)，共有 \(21\) 个。因此正确答案是 E。

English

Divide by \(2\) to get \(|x| \le 3.5\pi \approx 10.996.\) The integers that fit run from \(-10\) up to \(10,\) and there are \(21\) of them. Thus, E is the correct answer.

4 答案 D

第 \(g\) 组含有 \(g\) 个球，因此前 \(g\) 组一共用掉 \(\tfrac{g(g+1)}{2}\) 个球。现在 \(\tfrac{63 \cdot 64}{2} = 2016\)，而 \(\tfrac{64 \cdot 65}{2} = 2080\)，所以球 \(2024\) 在第 \(64\) 组中（球 \(2017\) 到 \(2080\)）。箱子按 \(A, B, C, D, E\) 循环，所以第 \(g\) 组进入编号为 \((g - 1) \bmod 5\) 的箱子。对 \(g = 64\)，有 \(63 \bmod 5 = 3\)，即箱子 \(D\)。因此正确答案是 D。

English

Group \(g\) holds \(g\) balls, so the first \(g\) groups swallow \(\tfrac{g(g+1)}{2}\) of them. Now \(\tfrac{63 \cdot 64}{2} = 2016\) and \(\tfrac{64 \cdot 65}{2} = 2080,\) which puts ball \(2024\) in group \(64\) (balls \(2017\) through \(2080\)). The bins cycle \(A, B, C, D, E,\) so group \(g\) lands in bin number \((g - 1) \bmod 5.\) For \(g = 64\) that’s \(63 \bmod 5 = 3,\) bin \(D.\) Therefore, the answer is D.

5 答案 B

完整和为 \(1 + 3 + \cdots + 99 = 50^2 = 2500\)。把一项 \(t\) 从加号改为减号，会使总和减少 \(2t\)，所以被变号的项之和必须大于 \(1250\)。为了使用最少项，应选择最大的奇数。最大的 \(k\) 个奇数之和为 \(99 + 97 + \cdots = k(100 - k)\)。需要 \(k(100 - k) \gt 1250\)。当 \(k = 14\) 时只有 \(1204\)，当 \(k = 15\) 时为 \(1275\)。所以 \(15\) 次变号可以做到。因此正确答案是 B。

English

The full sum is \(1 + 3 + \cdots + 99 = 50^2 = 2500.\) Flipping a term \(t\) drops the total by \(2t,\) so to go negative the flipped terms have to add up to more than \(1250.\) The greedy move is to flip the biggest odd numbers: flipping the top \(k\) gives \(99 + 97 + \cdots = k(100 - k).\) We want \(k(100 - k) \gt 1250.\) At \(k = 14\) it’s only \(1204,\) but at \(k = 15\) it jumps to \(1275.\) So \(15\) flips do it. 重新标记砖块的尺寸，使新块的边长为 \(7a,2b,2c\)。这三条边必定就是旧块的三条边长 \(3a,3b,3c\) 各增加 \(1\) 之后的结果。新边不可能与字母相同的旧边对应：\(7a=3a+1\) 没有正整数解，而 \(2b=3b+1\) 和 \(2c=3c+1\) 会给出负的边长。因此这个对应必定是两个三轮换之一。在其中一种排法下，\[ \begin{aligned} 7a&=3c+1,\\ 2b&=3a+1,\\ 2c&=3b+1 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]后两个方程给出 \(b=\frac{3a+1}{2}\) 和 \(c=\frac{9a+5}{4}\)，代入第一个方程得 \(28a=27a+19\)，所以 \((a,b,c)=(19,29,44)\)。另一个三轮换只是把 \(b\) 和 \(c\) 互换。这三个长度两两互质，且 \(a+b+c=19+29+44=92\)。因此正确答案是 E。

English

Relabel the brick dimensions so the new block has sides \(7a,2b,2c.\) These must be the three old side lengths \(3a,3b,3c,\) each increased by \(1.\) A new side cannot match the old side with the same letter: \(7a=3a+1\) has no positive integer solution, while \(2b=3b+1\) and \(2c=3c+1\) would give negative lengths. Therefore the matching must be one of the two three-cycles. In one orientation, \[ \begin{aligned} 7a&=3c+1,\\ 2b&=3a+1,\\ 2c&=3b+1. \end{aligned} \] The last two equations give \(b=\frac{3a+1}{2}\) and \(c=\frac{9a+5}{4}.\) Substituting into the first gives \(28a=27a+19,\) so \((a,b,c)=(19,29,44).\) The other cycle merely exchanges \(b\) and \(c.\) These lengths are pairwise relatively prime, and \(a+b+c=19+29+44=92.\) Thus, E is the correct answer.
