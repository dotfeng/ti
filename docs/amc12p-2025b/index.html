1 答案 B

每杯需要 \(20\) 克，而 \(\dfrac{350}{20} = 17.5\)。由于只能冲泡完整的一杯，最多可冲 \(17\) 杯。

所以正确答案是 B。

Thus, the correct answer is B.

2 答案 D

\(1^2, 2^2, \ldots, 10^2\) 的个位数字依次为 \(1, 4, 9, 6, 5, 6, 9, 4, 1, 0\)，其和为 \(45\)。这 \(2025\) 项包含 \(202\) 个完整的循环块（共 \(2020\) 项），另外还有 \(5\) 项，它们的个位数字为 \(1, 4, 9, 6, 5\)，和为 \(25\)。因此总和为 \(202 \cdot 45 + 25 = 9090 + 25\) \(= 9115\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The ones digits of \(1^2, 2^2, \ldots, 10^2\) are \(1, 4, 9, 6, 5, 6, 9, 4, 1, 0,\) which sum to \(45.\) The \(2025\) terms contain \(202\) full blocks (\(2020\) terms) plus \(5\) more with digits \(1, 4, 9, 6, 5\) summing to \(25.\) The total is \(202 \cdot 45 + 25 = 9090 + 25\) \(= 9115.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

3 答案 D

\(i(i-1) = i^2 - i = -1 - i\)，而 \((i-2)(i-3) = i^2 - 5i + 6\) \(= 5 - 5i\)。于是 \[ \begin{gathered} (-1-i)(5-5i) = -5 \\ {}+ 5i - 5i \\ {}+ 5i^2 \\ = -5 - 5 = -10 \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

\(i(i-1) = i^2 - i = -1 - i,\) and \((i-2)(i-3) = i^2 - 5i + 6\) \(= 5 - 5i.\) Then \[ \begin{gathered} (-1-i)(5-5i) = -5 \\ {}+ 5i - 5i \\ {}+ 5i^2 \\ = -5 - 5 = -10. \end{gathered} \]

Applying the same argument to \(-\sin(20\pi x)+\log_{20}x\) handles a negative lobe. The total is \(20+379=399.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

25 答案 E

公共圆心到边长为 \(s\) 的等边三角形三个顶点的距离为 \(1, 2, 3\)，因此有恒等式 \(3(1 + 16 + 81 + s^4)\) \(= (1 + 4 + 9 + s^2)^2\)。化简得 \(2s^4 - 28s^2 + 98 = 0\)，即 \((s^2 - 7)^2 = 0\)，所以 \(s^2 = 7\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

For the common center at distances \(1, 2, 3\) from the vertices of an equilateral triangle of side \(s,\) the identity \(3(1 + 16 + 81 + s^4)\) \(= (1 + 4 + 9 + s^2)^2\) holds. This simplifies to \(2s^4 - 28s^2 + 98 = 0,\) i.e. \((s^2 - 7)^2 = 0,\) so \(s^2 = 7.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.
