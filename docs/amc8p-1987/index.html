7 答案 C

先数没有任何涂色面的立方体，再从 \(27\) 中减去。一个小立方体未涂色，当且仅当它所有外露的小方格都是空白。

三种面图案如下：顶面和底面只有中心小方格涂色；一对相对侧面是四个角和中心涂色；另一对相对侧面是四条边的中点涂色。

恰有 \(7\) 个小立方体避开所有涂色小方格：大立方体最中心隐藏的一个；那对边中点涂色面中心处的两个小立方体，它们唯一外露的小方格是空白中心；以及四个在“角和中心涂色”的面与顶面或底面相交边中点处的小立方体，因为那里每个外露小方格都是空白边格。

因此有 \(27 - 7 = 20\) 个小立方体至少有一个涂色面。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Count the cubes with no shaded face and subtract from \(27.\) A small cube is unshaded exactly when every one of its exposed squares is blank.

The three patterns are: the top and bottom faces show only their center square shaded; one pair of opposite side faces shows the four corners and the center shaded; the remaining pair shows the four edge-midpoints shaded.

Exactly \(7\) small cubes avoid every shaded square: the one hidden cube at the very center of the block; the two cubes at the centers of the edge-midpoints faces, whose only exposed square is that blank center; and the four cubes at the midpoints of the edges where a corners-and-center face meets the top or bottom face, since there each exposed square is a blank edge cell.

Hence \(27 - 7 = 20\) cubes have at least one shaded face.

Thus, the correct answer is C .

8 答案 B

因为 \(A\) 和 \(B\) 至少为 \(1\)，所以和至少是 \(9876 + 132 + 11 = 10{,}019\)，有 \(5\) 位。

另一端，当 \(A = B = 9\) 时，和为 \(9876 + 932 + 91 = 10{,}899\)，仍然是 \(5\) 位。因此和总是恰好有 \(5\) 位。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Since \(A\) and \(B\) are at least \(1,\) the sum is at least \(9876 + 132 + 11 = 10{,}019,\) which has \(5\) digits.

At the other extreme, with \(A = B = 9\) the sum is \(9876 + 932 + 91 = 10{,}899,\) still \(5\) digits. So the sum always has exactly \(5\) digits.

Thus, the correct answer is B .

9 答案 C

最小公倍数必须包含 \(4 = 2^2\)、\(3\)、\(5\) 和 \(7\)（因数 \(6 = 2 \times 3\) 已经被覆盖）。

所以它是 \(4 \times 3 \times 5 \times 7 = 420\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The least common multiple must include \(4 = 2^2,\) \(3,\) \(5,\) and \(7\) (the factor \(6 = 2 \times 3\) is already covered).

So it is \(4 \times 3 \times 5 \times 7 = 420.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C .

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The first three terms combine to \((4 + 3 + 2)(299)\) \(= 9 \times 299 = 2691.\)

Adding the last term gives \(2691 + 298 = 2989.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B .

11 答案 B

整数部分的和是 \(10\)，分数部分是 \(\dfrac12 + \dfrac17 + \dfrac{1}{19}\)。

因为 \(\dfrac17 + \dfrac{1}{19}\) 是一个较小的正数，所以分数部分总和大于 \(\dfrac12\)，但远小于 \(1\)。因此原和位于 \(10\dfrac12\) 和 \(11\) 之间。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The whole-number parts sum to \(10.\) The fractional parts are \(\dfrac12 + \dfrac17 + \dfrac{1}{19}.\)

Since \(\dfrac17 + \dfrac{1}{19}\) is a small positive amount, the fractional total is more than \(\dfrac12\) but well under \(1.\) So the sum lies between \(10\dfrac12\) and \(11.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B .

12 答案 C

整个区域面积是 \(12 \times 18 = 216\)。涂色区域宽 \(3\)、高 \(6\)，面积为 \(3 \times 6 = 18\)。

所以涂色比例是 \(\dfrac{18}{216} = \dfrac{1}{12}\)。等价地，它是整个区域的 \(\dfrac14\) 的 \(\dfrac13\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The whole region has area \(12 \times 18 = 216.\) The shaded region is \(3\) wide and \(6\) tall, with area \(3 \times 6 = 18.\)

So the shaded fraction is \(\dfrac{18}{216} = \dfrac{1}{12}.\) Equivalently, it is \(\dfrac13\) of \(\dfrac14\) of the whole.

Thus, the correct answer is C .

13 答案 E

对 \(\dfrac37\)、\(\dfrac49\)、\(\dfrac{17}{35}\) 和 \(\dfrac{100}{201}\)，每个分子都小于分母的一半，所以它们都小于 \(\dfrac12\)。

对 \(\dfrac{151}{301}\)，分子 \(151\) 大于 \(301\) 的一半，所以这个分数大于 \(\dfrac12\)，也是最大的。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

For \(\dfrac37,\) \(\dfrac49,\) \(\dfrac{17}{35},\) and \(\dfrac{100}{201},\) each numerator is less than half its denominator, so each is less than \(\dfrac12.\)

For \(\dfrac{151}{301},\) the numerator \(151\) is more than half of \(301,\) so this fraction exceeds \(\dfrac12\) and is the largest.

Thus, the correct answer is E .

14 答案 B

一小时有 \(3600\) 秒，所以计算机可以做 \(10{,}000 \times 3600 = 36{,}000{,}000\) 次加法。

也就是 \(36\) 百万。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

One hour has \(3600\) seconds, so the computer does \(10{,}000 \times 3600 = 36{,}000{,}000\) additions.

That is \(36\) million.

Thus, the correct answer is B .

15 答案 D

所以一条轮胎的原价是 \(\dfrac{\$237}{3} = \$79\)。

English

16 答案 E

第四场后她共投篮 \(40\) 次。\(50\%\) 命中率表示她总共命中 \(\tfrac12 \times 40 = 20\) 次。

她之前已经命中 \(12\) 次，所以最后 \(10\) 次中命中 \(20 - 12 = 8\) 次。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

After the fourth game she has taken \(40\) shots. A \(50\%\) average means she made \(\tfrac12 \times 40 = 20\) shots in total.

She had already made \(12,\) so she made \(20 - 12 = 8\) of the last \(10.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E .

17 答案 D

布雷特在 \(3\) 号座位。因为“布雷特挨着卡尔”是假的，卡尔不在 \(2\) 号或 \(4\) 号座位，所以卡尔必须在 \(1\) 号座位。

布雷特（\(3\) 号）和卡尔（\(1\) 号）之间的座位是 \(2\) 号。因为“艾比在布雷特和卡尔之间”是假的，艾比不在 \(2\) 号，所以艾比必须在 \(4\) 号。

因此达娜坐在 \(2\) 号座位。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Bret is in seat \(3.\) Since “Bret is next to Carl” is false, Carl is not in seat \(2\) or seat \(4,\) so Carl must be in seat \(1.\)

The seat between Bret (seat \(3\)) and Carl (seat \(1\)) is seat \(2.\) Since “Abby is between Bret and Carl” is false, Abby is not in seat \(2,\) so Abby must be in seat \(4.\)

That leaves Dana in seat \(2.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D .

18 答案 C

在留下的人中，\(\dfrac13\) 跳舞，所以 \(\dfrac23\) 没跳舞。因此剩余人数的 \(\dfrac23\) 等于 \(12\)，得剩余 \(18\) 人。

这 \(18\) 人是原来人数的一半，所以房间一开始有 \(2 \times 18 = 36\) 人。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Of those remaining, \(\dfrac13\) danced, so \(\dfrac23\) did not. Thus \(\dfrac23\) of the remaining people equals \(12,\) giving \(18\) remaining.

Those \(18\) are half the original group, so the room started with \(2 \times 18 = 36\) people.

Thus, the correct answer is C .

19 答案 A

重复按键得到 \(2 \to 4 \to 16 \to 256 \to 65536\)。

因为 \(256 \lt 500 \lt 65536\)，显示数第一次超过 \(500\) 是第四次按键。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Pressing the key repeatedly gives \(2 \to 4 \to 16 \to 256 \to 65536.\)

Since \(256 \lt 500 \lt 65536,\) the display first exceeds \(500\) on the fourth press.

Thus, the correct answer is A .

20 答案 A

反例需要 \(n\) 不是质数，但 \(n - 2\) 是质数。当 \(n = 9\) 时，\(9\) 不是质数，而 \(9 - 2 = 7\) 是质数，所以它推翻了这个命题。

其他选项不能作为反例：\(12 - 2 = 10\) 和 \(16 - 2 = 14\) 不是质数，而 \(13\) 和 \(23\) 本身就是质数。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

A counterexample needs \(n\) not prime but \(n - 2\) prime. For \(n = 9,\) the number \(9\) is not prime while \(9 - 2 = 7\) is prime, so it breaks the statement.

The others fail to be counterexamples: \(12 - 2 = 10\) and \(16 - 2 = 14\) are not prime, while \(13\) and \(23\) are themselves prime.

Thus, the correct answer is A .

21 答案 C

\(\mathrm{(i)}\) \(\dfrac13 + \dfrac16 = \dfrac12\)，但 \(9^\ast = \dfrac19\)，所以为假。\(\mathrm{(ii)}\) \(\dfrac16 - \dfrac14 = -\dfrac{1}{12}\)，但 \(2^\ast = \dfrac12\)，所以为假。

\(\mathrm{(iii)}\) \(\dfrac12 \cdot \dfrac16 = \dfrac{1}{12} = 12^\ast\)，为真。\(\mathrm{(iv)}\) \(\dfrac{1}{10} \div \dfrac12 = \dfrac15 = 5^\ast\)，为真。

因此正好有 \(2\) 个命题为真。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

\(\mathrm{(i)}\) \(\dfrac13 + \dfrac16 = \dfrac12,\) but \(9^\ast = \dfrac19,\) so false. \(\mathrm{(ii)}\) \(\dfrac16 - \dfrac14 = -\dfrac{1}{12},\) but \(2^\ast = \dfrac12,\) so false.

\(\mathrm{(iii)}\) \(\dfrac12 \cdot \dfrac16 = \dfrac{1}{12} = 12^\ast,\) true. \(\mathrm{(iv)}\) \(\dfrac{1}{10} \div \dfrac12 = \dfrac15 = 5^\ast,\) true.

So exactly \(2\) of the statements are true.

Thus, the correct answer is C .

22 答案 D

因为 \(AD = 4\) 且 \(CD = 3\)，所以对角线 \(DB = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = 5\)，这就是半径。

阴影区域是以 \(D\) 为圆心的四分之一圆减去矩形：\[ \begin{gathered} \dfrac14 \pi (5)^2 - (3)(4) = \dfrac{25\pi}{4} - 12 \\ \approx 19.6 - 12 = 7.6 \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

这个值位于 \(7\) 和 \(8\) 之间。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Since \(AD = 4\) and \(CD = 3,\) the diagonal \(DB = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = 5,\) which is the radius.

The shaded region is the quarter circle at \(D\) minus the rectangle: \[ \begin{gathered} \dfrac14 \pi (5)^2 - (3)(4) = \dfrac{25\pi}{4} - 12 \\ \approx 19.6 - 12 = 7.6. \end{gathered} \]

This lies between \(7\) and \(8.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D .

24 答案 D

设 \(c\) 为答对题数，\(w\) 为答错题数，则 \(5c - 2w = 48\)。于是 \(2w = 5c - 48\)，所以 \(c\) 必须为偶数。

试取 \(c = 14\)，得到 \(w = 11\)，但 \(c + w = 25 \gt 20\)，超过了题目总数；任何更大的偶数 \(c\) 都会需要回答更多题。试取 \(c = 12\)，得到 \(w = 6\)，且 \(c + w = 18 \le 20\)，符合条件。因此最大值是 \(12\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Let \(c\) be the number correct and \(w\) the number wrong, so \(5c - 2w = 48.\) Then \(2w = 5c - 48,\) which requires \(c\) to be even.

Trying \(c = 14\) gives \(w = 11,\) but \(c + w = 25 \gt 20,\) too many; every larger even \(c\) would require still more answered questions. Trying \(c = 12\) gives \(w = 6\) and \(c + w = 18 \le 20,\) which works. So the maximum is \(12.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D .

25 答案 A

和为偶数时，两个球同为奇数或同为偶数。全奇的配对有 \(\binom{5}{2} = 10\) 对，全偶的配对也有 \(\binom{5}{2} = 10\) 对，共 \(20\) 对有利情况。

总配对数是 \(\binom{10}{2} = 45\)，所以概率是 \(\dfrac{20}{45} = \dfrac49\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The sum is even when both balls are odd or both are even. There are \(\binom{5}{2} = 10\) all-odd pairs and \(\binom{5}{2} = 10\) all-even pairs, for \(20\) favorable pairs.

The total number of pairs is \(\binom{10}{2} = 45,\) so the probability is \(\dfrac{20}{45} = \dfrac49.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A .
