1

两个数的和是 \(S\)。给每个数都加上 \(3\)，再将所得的两个数分别翻倍。最后这两个数的和是多少？

A\(2S + 3\) B\(3S + 2\) C\(3S + 6\) D\(2S + 6\) E\(2S + 12\)

English

The sum of two numbers is \(S.\) Suppose \(3\) is added to each number and then each of the resulting numbers is doubled. What is the sum of the final two numbers?

(A) \(2S + 3\)(B) \(3S + 2\)(C) \(3S + 6\)(D) \(2S + 6\)(E) \(2S + 12\)

2

设 \(P(n)\) 和 \(S(n)\) 分别表示整数 \(n\) 的各位数字之积和各位数字之和。例如，\(P(23) = 6\) 且 \(S(23) = 5\)。假设 \(N\) 是一个两位数，并且 \(N = P(N) + S(N)\)。\(N\) 的个位数字是多少？

A\(2\) B\(3\) C\(6\) D\(8\) E\(9\)

English

Let \(P(n)\) and \(S(n)\) denote the product and the sum, respectively, of the digits of the integer \(n.\) For example, \(P(23) = 6\) and \(S(23) = 5.\) Suppose \(N\) is a two-digit number such that \(N = P(N) + S(N).\) What is the units digit of \(N?\)

(A) \(2\)(B) \(3\)(C) \(6\)(D) \(8\)(E) \(9\)

A\(\$28000\) B\(\$32000\) C\(\$35000\) D\(\$42000\) E\(\$56000\)

English

The state income tax where Kristin lives is levied at the rate of \(p\%\) of the first \(\$28000\) of annual income plus \((p + 2)\%\) of any amount above \(\$28000.\) Kristin noticed that the state income tax she paid amounted to \((p + 0.25)\%\) of her annual income. What was her annual income?

(A) \(\$28000\)(B) \(\$32000\)(C) \(\$35000\)(D) \(\$42000\)(E) \(\$56000\)

1 答案 E

给每个数加 \(3\) 后，总和从 \(S\) 变为 \(S + 6\)。再把每个数翻倍，总和也翻倍，得到 \[ 2(S + 6) = 2S + 12\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

Adding \(3\) to each number raises the sum from \(S\) to \(S + 6.\) Doubling each number doubles the sum, giving \[ 2(S + 6) = 2S + 12. \]

Thus, the correct answer is E.

2 答案 E

设 \(N = 10a + b\)，则 \(P(N) = ab\)，且 \(S(N) = a + b\)，所以 \[ 10a + b = ab + a + b\text{。} \] 化简得 \(9a = ab\)。因为 \(a \neq 0\)，可除以 \(a\)，得到 \(b = 9\)。

\(N\) 的个位数字是 \(9\)。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

Write \(N = 10a + b.\) Then \(P(N) = ab\) and \(S(N) = a + b,\) so \[ 10a + b = ab + a + b. \] This reduces to \(9a = ab.\) Since \(a \neq 0,\) we can divide by \(a\) to get \(b = 9.\)

The units digit of \(N\) is \(9.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

3 答案 B

设她的收入为 \(x \gt 28000\) 美元。用两种描述写出税额，并同乘以 \(100\)，\[ \begin{aligned} &p \cdot 28000 \\ &\quad {}+ (p + 2)(x - 28000) \\ &= (p + 0.25)x\text{。} \end{aligned} \]

展开后，所有含 \(p\) 的项都抵消，剩下 \[ 2x - 56000 = 0.25x\text{，} \] 所以 \(1.75x = 56000\)，且 \(x = 32000\)。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Let her income be \(x \gt 28000\) dollars. Writing the tax with both descriptions and multiplying by \(100,\) \[ \begin{aligned} &p \cdot 28000 \\ &\quad {}+ (p + 2)(x - 28000) \\ &= (p + 0.25)x. \end{aligned} \]

Expanding, every term containing \(p\) cancels, leaving \[ 2x - 56000 = 0.25x, \] so \(1.75x = 56000\) and \(x = 32000.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

4 答案 D

设平均数为 \(m\)。最小的数是 \(m - 10\)，最大的数是 \(m + 15\)，中间的数是中位数 \(5\)。它们的和是 \(3m\)，因此 \[ (m - 10) + 5 + (m + 15) = 3m\text{。}\]

由此得 \(m = 10\)，所以三个数的和是 \(3m = 30\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

Let \(m\) be the mean. The least number is \(m - 10,\) the greatest is \(m + 15,\) and the middle number is the median \(5.\) Their sum is \(3m,\) so \[ (m - 10) + 5 + (m + 15) = 3m. \]

This gives \(m = 10,\) so the sum of the three numbers is \(3m = 30.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

5 答案 D

从 \(1\) 到 \(10000\) 的所有整数的乘积是 \(10000!\)，所以所有奇数的乘积等于 \(10000!\) 除以所有偶数的乘积。

偶数的乘积可分解为 \[ \begin{gathered} 2 \cdot 4 \cdots 10000 \\ = 2^{5000}(1 \cdot 2 \cdots 5000) \\ = 2^{5000} \cdot 5000! \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

因此奇整数的乘积是 \[ \dfrac{10000!}{2^{5000} \cdot 5000!}\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

The product of every integer from \(1\) to \(10000\) is \(10000!,\) so the product of the odd ones is \(10000!\) divided by the product of the even ones.

The even numbers factor as \[ \begin{gathered} 2 \cdot 4 \cdots 10000 \\ = 2^{5000}(1 \cdot 2 \cdots 5000) \\ = 2^{5000} \cdot 5000!. \end{gathered} \]

Therefore the product of the odd integers is \[ \dfrac{10000!}{2^{5000} \cdot 5000!}. \]

Thus, the correct answer is D.

6 答案 E

25 答案 D

若 \(a, b, c\) 是连续三项，则 \(b = ac - 1\)，所以 \(c = \dfrac{1 + b}{a}\)。反复应用这个关系，前五项依次为 \[ a,\ b,\ \dfrac{1 + b}{a},\ \dfrac{1 + a + b}{ab},\ \dfrac{1 + a}{b}\text{，}\]此后 \(a\) 和 \(b\) 又重新出现，因此数列以 \(5\) 为周期。

此处 \(b = 2000\) 是第二项。令上面另外四项分别等于 \(2001\)，依次解得 \[ \begin{gathered} a=2001,\quad a=1,\\ a=\frac{2001}{4001999},\quad a=4001999 \end{gathered}\text{。}\]这四个值都是正数且互不相同。而第二项本身等于 \(2000\)，再由周期性可知没有其他位置需要考虑。

所以共有 \(4\) 个 \(x\) 值。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

If \(a, b, c\) are consecutive terms then \(b = ac - 1,\) so \(c = \dfrac{1 + b}{a}.\) Applying this repeatedly, the first five terms are \[ a,\ b,\ \dfrac{1 + b}{a},\ \dfrac{1 + a + b}{ab},\ \dfrac{1 + a}{b}, \] after which \(a\) and \(b\) recur, so the sequence is periodic with period \(5.\)

Here \(b = 2000\) is the second term. Setting each of the other four displayed terms equal to \(2001\) gives, respectively, \[ \begin{gathered} a=2001,\quad a=1,\\ a=\frac{2001}{4001999},\quad a=4001999. \end{gathered} \] These four values are positive and distinct. The second term itself is \(2000,\) and periodicity shows there are no other positions to consider.

So there are \(4\) values of \(x.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.
