1 答案 E

Thus, the answer is E .

2 答案 B

阴影部分的面积是一个三角形的面积 \[\dfrac {4\cdot 5}2 - \dfrac{2\cdot 4}2 = 10-4 \]\[= 6\] 因为这是用较大三角形的面积减去较小三角形的面积得到的。

所以答案是 B。

English

The area is a triangle of area \[\dfrac {4\cdot 5}2 - \dfrac{2\cdot 4}2 = 10-4 \]\[= 6\] since we subtract the area of a smaller triangle from a larger triangle.

Thus, the answer is B .

3 答案 E

通分得 \[\dfrac{2021\cdot 2021}{2020\cdot 2021} -\dfrac{2020\cdot 2020}{2020\cdot 2021}\] \[= \dfrac{2021^2-2020^2}{2020\cdot 2021}\text{。}\]

利用平方差公式，可进一步化为 \[\dfrac{(2021-2020)(2021+2020)}{2020\cdot 2021} \] \[=\dfrac{4041}{2020\cdot 2021}\text{。}\]

因为 \(4041\) 与 \(2020\)、\(2021\) 互质，所以分子为 \(4041\)。

所以答案是 E。

English

We can rewrite this as \[\dfrac{2021\cdot 2021}{2020\cdot 2021} -\dfrac{2020\cdot 2020}{2020\cdot 2021}\] \[= \dfrac{2021^2-2020^2}{2020\cdot 2021}.\]

This can be simplified to \[\dfrac{(2021-2020)(2021+2020)}{2020\cdot 2021} \] \[=\dfrac{4041}{2020\cdot 2021}.\]

Since \(4041\) is coprime with both \(2020\) and \(2021,\) we know \(4041\) is the numerator.

Thus, the answer is E .

4 答案 C

设中午两城气温差为 \(N=m-s\)。到 \(4{:}00\) 时，新温差为 \((m-5)-(s+3)=N-8\)。

此时两城气温相差 \(2\) 度，所以 \(|N-8|=2\)。因此 \(N=6\) 或 \(N=10\)，所有可能值的乘积为 \(6\cdot10=60\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Let \(N=m-s\) be the noon temperature difference. At \(4{:}00,\) the new difference is \((m-5)-(s+3)=N-8.\)

The temperatures then differ by \(2\) degrees, so \(|N-8|=2.\) Hence \(N=6\) or \(N=10,\) and the product of all possible values is \(6\cdot10=60.\)

Thus, the answer is C .

计算补事件：将 \(10\) 条边组成的 \(K_5\) 用两种颜色染色，且没有单色三角形。

在任意顶点，如果有 \(3\) 条关联边同色，那么这三条边另一端之间的边都必须是另一种颜色；但这又会形成单色三角形。因此每个顶点恰有 \(2\) 条红边和 \(2\) 条蓝边。

所以红边构成一个 \(2\) 正则图，顶点数为 \(5\)，只能是一个 \(5\) 环。带标号的 \(5\) 环共有 \(\frac{(5-1)!}{2}=12\) 个。

总染色数为 \(2^{10}=1024\)，所以所求概率为 \[1-\frac{12}{1024}=\frac{253}{256}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Count the complement: colorings of the \(10\) edges of \(K_5\) with no monochromatic triangle.

At any vertex, if \(3\) incident edges had the same color, then the edges among their other endpoints would all have to be the other color, making a monochromatic triangle. Thus each vertex has exactly \(2\) red and \(2\) blue incident edges.

So the red edges form a \(2\)-regular graph on \(5\) vertices, which must be a \(5\)-cycle. The number of labeled \(5\)-cycles is \(\frac{(5-1)!}{2}=12.\)

There are \(2^{10}=1024\) total colorings, so the desired probability is \[1-\frac{12}{1024}=\frac{253}{256}.\]

Thus, the answer is D .

24 答案 A

问题等价于选择哪 \(4\) 个位置涂蓝，位置总数为 \(8\)，并对立方体的 \(24\) 个旋转使用 Burnside 引理。

恒等旋转固定 \(\binom84=70\) 种染色。\(6\) 个绕面中心轴的四分之一转各固定 \(2\) 种；\(3\) 个绕面中心轴的半转各固定 \(6\) 种；\(8\) 个绕相对顶点轴的旋转各固定 \(4\) 种；\(6\) 个绕相对棱中点轴的半转各固定 \(6\) 种。

因此不等价构造数为 \[ \begin{gathered} \frac{70+6\cdot2+3\cdot6+8\cdot4+6\cdot6}{24}\\ =7 \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 A。

English

This is the number of ways to choose which \(4\) of the \(8\) cube vertices are blue, up to cube rotation. Use Burnside’s lemma on the \(24\) rotations of the cube.

The identity fixes \(\binom84=70\) colorings. The \(6\) quarter-turn face rotations each fix \(2.\) The \(3\) half-turn face rotations each fix \(6.\) The \(8\) rotations about opposite vertices each fix \(4.\) The \(6\) half-turn rotations about opposite edges each fix \(6.\)

Thus the number of inequivalent constructions is \[ \begin{gathered} \frac{70+6\cdot2+3\cdot6+8\cdot4+6\cdot6}{24}\\ =7. \end{gathered} \]

Thus, the answer is A .

25 答案 E

如图使用相似三角形。大正方形的两条边长分别为 \(4x+2y\) 与 \(3y+x\)，所以 \(3y+x=4x+2y\)，得到 \(y=3x\)，大正方形边长为 \(10x\)。

在图形上部，设标出的水平线段为 \(m\)。矩形 \(R\) 两侧形成的两个直角三角形具有平行的对应边和相等的斜边，因此全等，由此得到下图标出的长度。

由相似三角形，\[\frac{m}{3x}=\frac{4x-\frac m3}{6x-m}\text{。}\] 因而 \(6xm-m^2=12x^2-xm\)，所以 \[ \begin{gathered} m^2-7xm+12x^2=0\\ =(m-3x)(m-4x)\text{。} \end{gathered} \]

若 \(m=3x\)，矩形 \(R\) 的两边均为 \(3\sqrt2x\)，面积为 \(18x^2\)；若 \(m=4x\)，两边为 \(5x\) 与 \(\frac{10}{3}x\)，面积为 \(\frac{50}{3}x^2\)。

两个可能面积之和为 \(\frac{104}{3}x^2\)。由 \(1\times3\) 矩形可知 \(x^2+(3x)^2=1\)，所以 \(x^2=\frac1{10}\)。因此面积和为 \[\frac{104}{3}\cdot\frac1{10}=\frac{52}{15}\text{。}\]

于是 \(m+n=52+15=67\)，正确答案是 E。

English

Use similar triangles as shown in the diagram. The left side of the large square has length \(4x+2y,\) and the bottom side has length \(3y+x.\) Since these are equal, \(3y+x=4x+2y,\) so \(y=3x.\) The side length of the large square is therefore \(10x.\)

In the upper part of the figure, let the marked horizontal segment be \(m.\) The two right triangles formed by the sides of rectangle \(R\) have parallel corresponding sides and equal hypotenuses, so they are congruent. This gives the lengths shown below.

Similar triangles give \[\frac{m}{3x}=\frac{4x-\frac m3}{6x-m}.\] Hence \(6xm-m^2=12x^2-xm,\) so \[ \begin{gathered} m^2-7xm+12x^2=0\\ =(m-3x)(m-4x). \end{gathered} \]

If \(m=3x,\) rectangle \(R\) has side length \(3\sqrt2x\) in both directions, so its area is \(18x^2.\) If \(m=4x,\) its side lengths are \(5x\) and \(\frac{10}{3}x,\) so its area is \(\frac{50}{3}x^2.\)

The two possible areas sum to \(\frac{104}{3}x^2.\) Since the \(1\times3\) rectangle gives \(x^2+(3x)^2=1,\) we have \(x^2=\frac1{10}.\) The sum of the possible areas is \[\frac{104}{3}\cdot\frac1{10}=\frac{52}{15}.\]

Thus \(m+n=52+15=67,\) and the answer is E .
