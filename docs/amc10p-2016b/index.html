1 答案 D

原式等于 \[2a^{-2}+\frac{a^{-2}}{2} = 2.5 (a^{-1})^2\text{。}\] 此时 \(a^{-1} \) 等于 \(\dfrac{1}{\frac 12} = 2\)，所以原式为 \(2.5\cdot 2^2 = 10\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The expression is equivalent to \[2a^{-2}+\frac{a^{-2}}{2} = 2.5 (a^{-1})^2.\] Then \(a^{-1} \) is equal to \(\dfrac{1}{\frac 12} = 2,\) so our expression is equal to \(2.5\cdot 2^2 = 10.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D .

2 答案 B

对任意非零 \(a,b\)，有 \[\dfrac{a\heartsuit b}{b\heartsuit a} = \dfrac{a^3b^2}{b^3 a^2} = \dfrac ab \text{。}\] 令 \(a=2,b=4\)，得到 \(\frac{ 2}{4} = \frac 12\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

For any nonzero \(a,b,\) we have \[\dfrac{a\heartsuit b}{b\heartsuit a} = \dfrac{a^3b^2}{b^3 a^2} = \dfrac ab .\] Using \(a=2,b=4,\) we get that \(\frac{ 2}{4} = \frac 12.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B .

3 答案 D

由 \(x=-2016<0\) 可知 \(|x|=2016\)，所以 \[ \begin{aligned} |x|-x&=2016-(-2016) \\ &=4032 \end{aligned}\text{，}\] 最内层的绝对值为 \(4032\)。因此整个表达式为 \[|4032-2016|+2016=4032\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Since \(x=-2016<0,\) we have \(|x|=2016.\) Thus \[ \begin{aligned} |x|-x&=2016-(-2016) \\ &=4032, \end{aligned} \] so the innermost absolute value is \(4032.\) Therefore the full expression is \[|4032-2016|+2016=4032.\]

Thus, the correct answer is D .

4 答案 B

读完 \(15\) 本书共需要 \(\dfrac{ 15\cdot 16}{2} =120\) 天。因此第十五本读完是在第一本读完后的 \(120-1=119\) 天。因为这是 \(7\) 的倍数，所以第 \(15\) 本是在与第 \(1\) 本相同的星期几读完的，也就是星期一。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The number of days it takes to read \(15\) books is \(\dfrac{ 15\cdot 16}{2} =120.\) Therefore, it is \(120-1=119 \) days after the first book is read. This is a multiple of \(7,\) so the \(15\)th book was finished on the same day as the \(1\)st book. Therefore, it was finished on a Monday.

Thus, the correct answer is B .

5 答案 D

四个年龄的总和是 \(4\cdot8=32\)。由于中位数是 \(5\)，中间两个年龄的平均数是 \(5\)，所以它们的和是 \(10\)。

因此最小年龄与最大年龄的和为 \(32-10=22\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The total of the four cousin ages is \(4\cdot8=32\). Since the median is \(5\), the two middle ages have average \(5\), so their sum is \(10\).

Therefore the youngest and oldest ages sum to \(32-10=22\).

Thus, the correct answer is D.

6 答案 B

为使两个加数的和尽量小，百位取两个最小的非零数字 \(1\) 和 \(2\)，剩下的四个数位取余下最小的四个数字 \(0,3,4,5\)。把较小的数字放在十位上可知，任何可能的和都至少是 \(104+235=339\)。另一方面，数字和小于 \(4\) 的三位数最大也只有 \(300\)。因此 \(S\) 的数字和至少为 \(4\)。

例子 \(157+243=400\) 使用六个互不相同的数字，且数字和为 \(4\)。因此最小可能值是 \(4\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

To minimize the sum of the addends, use the two smallest nonzero hundreds digits, \(1\) and \(2\), and then the four smallest remaining digits, \(0,3,4,5\). 若公差为 \(2\)，则 \(1 \leq a \leq 3\)，给出 \(3\) 个解。若公差至少为 \(3\)，就会迫使 \(d=a+3r>9\)。因此本情况给出 \(9\) 个解。

总解数为 \(8+9 = 17\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

From \(ab<bc<cd\), we have \(a\le b\le c.\) The arithmetic-sequence condition is \[ \begin{aligned} 2(10b+c)&=(10a+b) \\ &\quad +(10c+d), \end{aligned} \] which rearranges to \[10(a-2b+c)=-b+2c-d.\] The right side is a multiple of \(10.\) Because \(b\le c\) and all four symbols are digits with \(b\ge1,\) it lies between \(-9\) and \(17\). Hence it is either \(0\) or \(10.\)

Case \(1:\) \[ \begin{aligned} -b+2c-d&=10, \\ a&=2b-c+1. \end{aligned} \]

We can look at the possible values of \(c.\)

\(c=6:\) \(b+d=2, 2b-5=a.\) Thus, \(b\leq 2\) from the first equation, but can’t work for the second equation.

\(c=7:\) \(b+d=4, 2b-6=a.\) Thus, \(b\leq 4\) from the first equation, and \(b > 3\) from the second equation. This makes one case for \(b=4.\)

\(c=8:\) \(b+d=6, 2b-7=a.\) Thus, \(b\leq 6\) from the first equation, and \(b > 3\) from the second equation. This makes three cases for \(b=4,5,6.\)

\(c=9:\) \(b+d=8, 2b-8=a.\) Thus, \(b\leq 8\) from the first equation, and \(b > 4\) from the second equation. This makes four cases for \(b=5,6,7,8.\) Altogether this case gives \[ \begin{gathered} 2470,1482,3581,5680, \\ 2593,4692,6791,8890, \end{gathered} \] for \(8\) solutions.

Case \(2:\) \[-b+2c-d=0,\]\[2b-a-c=0,\] which means the digits are an arithmetic sequence.

If the difference is \(1,\) then \(1 \leq a \leq 6\) makes \(6\) solutions.

If the difference is \(2,\) then \(1 \leq a \leq 3\) makes \(3\) solutions. A difference of at least \(3\) would force \(d=a+3r>9.\) This case therefore gives \(9\) solutions.

In total, the number of solutions is \(8+9 = 17.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D .

25 答案 A

写 \(x=\lfloor x\rfloor+t\)，其中 \(0\le t\lt1\)。则 \[\lfloor kx\rfloor-k\lfloor x\rfloor=\lfloor kt\rfloor\text{，}\] 所以 \(f(x)\) 只取决于小数部分 \(t\)。

\(f\) 的值只会在 \(t\) 跨过某个分数 \(\frac{i}{k}\) 时改变，其中 \(2\le k\le10\)，\(1\le i\lt k\)。在 \((0,1)\) 内这些不同分数的个数是 \[ \begin{aligned} &\varphi(2)+\varphi(3) \\ &\quad {}+\cdots+\varphi(10) \\ &=1+2+2+4+2 \\ &\quad {}+6+4+6+4 \\ &=31 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

加上第一个断点之前的初始取值，\(f\) 共有 \(31+1=32\) 个不同的值。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Write \(x=\lfloor x\rfloor+t\), where \(0\le t\lt1\). Then \[\lfloor kx\rfloor-k\lfloor x\rfloor=\lfloor kt\rfloor,\] so \(f(x)\) depends only on the fractional part \(t\).

The value of \(f\) changes only when \(t\) crosses a fraction \(\frac{i}{k}\), where \(2\le k\le10\) and \(1\le i\lt k\). The number of distinct such fractions in \((0,1)\) is \[ \begin{aligned} &\varphi(2)+\varphi(3) \\ &\quad {}+\cdots+\varphi(10) \\ &=1+2+2+4+2 \\ &\quad {}+6+4+6+4 \\ &=31. \end{aligned} \]

Including the initial value before the first breakpoint, \(f\) assumes \(31+1=32\) distinct values.

Thus, the correct answer is A.
