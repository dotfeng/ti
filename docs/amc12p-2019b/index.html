所以正确答案是 C。

English

Let \(P=(x,0)\) be the intersection. Equal tangent lengths give \(PA=PB,\) so \((x-6)^2+13^2\) \(=(x-12)^2+11^2,\) yielding \(x=5\) and \(P=(5,0).\)

The center \(O=(h,k)\) satisfies \(OA\perp PA\) and \(OB\perp PB.\) With \(PA=(1,13)\) and \(PB=(7,11),\) these give \(h+13k=175\) and \(7h+11k=205,\) so \(O=\left(\dfrac{37}{4},\dfrac{51}{4}\right).\)

Then \(r^2=OA^2\) \(=\left(\dfrac{13}{4}\right)^2+\left(\dfrac14\right)^2\) \(=\dfrac{170}{16}=\dfrac{85}{8},\) so the area is \(\dfrac{85\pi}{8}.\)

Thus, C is the correct answer.

21 答案 B

如果三个系数都等于同一个值 \(u\)，多项式就是 \(u(x^2+x+1)\)，其根不等于 \(u\)。因此系数集合和根集合都有两个不同的值，所以恰有两个系数相同。由韦达定理，\(r+s=-\dfrac{b}{a}\) 且 \(rs=\dfrac{c}{a}\)。

先设 \(a=b=u\) 且 \(c=v\)。根为 \(u,v\)，所以韦达定理给出 \(u+v=-1\) 且 \(uv=\frac{v}{u}\)。因而 \(v(u^2-1)=0\)，得到 \(x^2+x-2\) 和 \(-x^2-x\)。

如果 \(b=c=v\) 且 \(a=u\)，同一个乘积方程给出 \(v(u^2-1)=0\)，而和的方程只留下 \(u=1,\ v=-\tfrac12\)。这给出 \(x^2-\tfrac12x-\tfrac12\)。

最后，如果 \(a=c=u\) 且 \(b=v\)，乘积方程给出 \(uv=1\)，所以 \(v=\frac{1}{u}\)。和的方程变为 \(u^3+u+1=0\)。这个严格递增的三次函数有唯一实根 \(u\)，因此恰好再得到一个多项式 \(ux^2+\dfrac1u x+u\)。所以共有 \(4\) 个多项式。

所以 B 是正确答案。

English

If all three coefficients had one value \(u,\) the polynomial would be \(u(x^2+x+1),\) whose roots do not equal \(u.\) Thus the coefficient and root sets both have two distinct values, so exactly two coefficients coincide. By Vieta’s formulas, \(r+s=-\dfrac{b}{a}\) and \(rs=\dfrac{c}{a}.\)

First suppose \(a=b=u\) and \(c=v.\) The roots are \(u,v,\) so Vieta gives \(u+v=-1\) and \(uv=\frac{v}{u}.\) Hence \(v(u^2-1)=0,\) producing \(x^2+x-2\) and \(-x^2-x.\)

If \(b=c=v\) and \(a=u,\) the same product equation gives \(v(u^2-1)=0,\) while the sum equation leaves only \(u=1,\ v=-\tfrac12.\) This gives \(x^2-\tfrac12x-\tfrac12.\)

Finally, if \(a=c=u\) and \(b=v,\) the product equation gives \(uv=1,\) so \(v=\frac{1}{u}.\) The sum equation becomes \(u^3+u+1=0.\) This strictly increasing cubic has one real root \(u,\) producing exactly one more polynomial, \(ux^2+\dfrac1u x+u.\) Therefore there are \(4\) polynomials.

Thus, B is the correct answer.

22 答案 C

令 \(a_n=x_n-4\)。简单计算得到 \[ \begin{gathered} a_{n+1}=x_{n+1}-4 \\ =\dfrac{(x_n+5)(x_n-4)}{x_n+6} \\ =a_n\cdot\dfrac{x_n+5}{x_n+6} \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

从 \(a_0=1\) 开始，各项保持为正并递减。因为 \(x_n\) 从 \(5\) 递减趋向 \(4\)，每个比值 \(\dfrac{x_n+5}{x_n+6}\) 都严格介于 \(\dfrac{9}{10}\) 和 \(\dfrac{10}{11}\) 之间。

因此 \(a_m\) 被夹在 \(\left(\dfrac{9}{10}\right)^m\) 与 \(\left(\dfrac{10}{11}\right)^m\) 之间。求解 \(a_m\le2^{-20}\) 可知 \(m\) 大约在 \(132\) 到 \(146\) 之间，落在 \([81,242]\) 中。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Let \(a_n=x_n-4.\) A short computation gives \[ \begin{gathered} a_{n+1}=x_{n+1}-4 \\ =\dfrac{(x_n+5)(x_n-4)}{x_n+6} \\ =a_n\cdot\dfrac{x_n+5}{x_n+6}. \end{gathered} \]

Starting from \(a_0=1,\) the terms stay positive and decrease. Because \(x_n\) decreases from \(5\) toward \(4,\) each ratio \(\dfrac{x_n+5}{x_n+6}\) lies strictly between \(\dfrac{9}{10}\) and \(\dfrac{10}{11}.\)

Hence \(a_m\) is squeezed between \(\left(\dfrac{9}{10}\right)^m\) and \(\left(\dfrac{10}{11}\right)^m.\) Solving \(a_m\le2^{-20}\) puts \(m\) between about \(132\) and \(146,\) which lies in \([81,242].\)

Thus, C is the correct answer.

23 答案 C

没有两个 \(0\) 相邻，所以 \(0\) 之间由若干 \(1\) 的块隔开，每块大小为 \(1\) 或 \(2\) （不能为 \(3\)）。若有 \(k\) 个零，则有 \(k-1\) 个这样的块，合计 \(19-k\) 个一。

大小为 \(2\) 的块数为 \((19-k)-(k-1)=20-2k\)，它必须满足 \(0\le20-2k\le k-1\)，即 \(7\le k\le10\)。

对 \(k=7,8,9,10\) 求和 \(\binom{k-1}{20-2k}\) 得到 \(\binom66+\binom74+\binom82+\binom90\) \(=1+35+28+1\) \(=65\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

No two \(0\)s are adjacent, so the \(0\)s are separated by blocks of \(1\)s, each of size \(1\) or \(2\) (never \(3\)). If there are \(k\) zeros, there are \(k-1\) such blocks summing to \(19-k\) ones.

The number of size-\(2\) blocks is \((19-k)-(k-1)=20-2k,\) which must satisfy \(0\le20-2k\le k-1,\) i.e. \(7\le k\le10.\)

Summing \(\binom{k-1}{20-2k}\) over \(k=7,8,9,10\) gives \(\binom66+\binom74+\binom82+\binom90\) \(=1+35+28+1\) \(=65.\)

Thus, C is the correct answer.

24 答案 C

当 \(a,b,c\) 在 \([0,1]\) 中变化时，集合 \(S\) 是沿向量 \(v_1=1=(1,0)\)，\(\ v_2=\omega=\left(-\dfrac12,\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\right)\)，\(\ v_3=\omega^2=\left(-\dfrac12,-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\right)\) 的三个单位线段的闵可夫斯基和。

这是一个带状多边形，其面积等于所有向量对的叉积大小之和。每一对都给出 \(|v_i\times v_j|=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\)。

因此面积为 \(3\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}=\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

As \(a,b,c\) range over \([0,1],\) the set \(S\) is the Minkowski sum of the three unit segments along \(v_1=1=(1,0),\) \(\ v_2=\omega=\left(-\dfrac12,\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\right),\) \(\ v_3=\omega^2=\left(-\dfrac12,-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\right).\)

This is a zonogon whose area is the sum of the cross-product magnitudes over pairs. Each pair gives \(|v_i\times v_j|=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}.\)

Therefore the area is \(3\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}=\dfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}.\)

Thus, C is the correct answer.

25 答案 C

三个重心分别为 \(\dfrac{A+B+C}{3}\)，\(\ \dfrac{B+C+D}{3}\)，\(\ \dfrac{A+C+D}{3}\)。它们两两之差为 \(\dfrac{A-D}{3},\ \dfrac{B-A}{3},\ \dfrac{B-D}{3}\)，所以重心三角形为等边三角形迫使 \(AB=BD=DA\)；也就是说，\(\triangle ABD\) 是边长为 \(s=BD\) 的等边三角形。

沿 \(BD\) 分割，\[ \begin{gathered} [ABCD]=[ABD]+[BCD] \\ =\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}s^2 \\ {}+\dfrac12\cdot2\cdot6\sin C \end{gathered}\text{，}\]其中 \(C=\angle BCD\)。由余弦定理，\(s^2=40-24\cos C\)，所以 \[ \begin{gathered} [ABCD]=10\sqrt3 \\ {}-6\sqrt3\cos C+6\sin C \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

表达式 \(6\sin C-6\sqrt3\cos C\) 的最大值为 \(\sqrt{6^2+(6\sqrt3)^2}=12\)，所以最大面积是 \(10\sqrt3+12=12+10\sqrt3\)。当 \(C=150^\circ\) 时取等号；构造具有这个角的 \(\triangle BCD\)，并在 \(\overline{BD}\) 另一侧作等边 \(\triangle ABD\)，可以得到凸四边形，所以最大值能够达到。

所以 C 是正确答案。

English

The centroids are \(\dfrac{A+B+C}{3},\) \(\ \dfrac{B+C+D}{3},\) \(\ \dfrac{A+C+D}{3}.\) Their pairwise differences are \(\dfrac{A-D}{3},\ \dfrac{B-A}{3},\ \dfrac{B-D}{3},\) so an equilateral centroid triangle forces \(AB=BD=DA;\) that is, \(\triangle ABD\) is equilateral with side \(s=BD.\)

Splitting along \(BD,\) \[ \begin{gathered} [ABCD]=[ABD]+[BCD] \\ =\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}s^2 \\ {}+\dfrac12\cdot2\cdot6\sin C, \end{gathered} \] where \(C=\angle BCD.\) By the Law of Cosines \(s^2=40-24\cos C,\) so \[ \begin{gathered} [ABCD]=10\sqrt3 \\ {}-6\sqrt3\cos C+6\sin C. \end{gathered} \]

The expression \(6\sin C-6\sqrt3\cos C\) has maximum \(\sqrt{6^2+(6\sqrt3)^2}=12,\) so the greatest area is \(10\sqrt3+12=12+10\sqrt3.\) Equality occurs at \(C=150^\circ;\) constructing \(\triangle BCD\) with that angle and placing equilateral \(\triangle ABD\) on the opposite side of \(\overline{BD}\) produces a convex quadrilateral, so the maximum is attainable.

Thus, C is the correct answer.
