1

一个手机套餐每月费用为 \(\$20\)，外加每条短信 \(5\)¢，以及每分钟 \(10\)¢ 的超时通话费（超过 \(30\) 小时的部分）。Michelle 一月份发送了 \(100\) 条短信，并通话 \(30.5\) 小时。她需要支付多少钱？

A\(\$24.00\) B\(\$24.50\) C\(\$25.50\) D\(\$28.00\) E\(\$30.00\)

English

A cell phone plan costs \(\$20\) each month, plus \(5\)¢ per text message sent, plus \(10\)¢ for each minute used over \(30\) hours. In January Michelle sent \(100\) text messages and talked for \(30.5\) hours. How much did she have to pay?

(A) \(\$24.00\)(B) \(\$24.50\)(C) \(\$25.50\)(D) \(\$28.00\)(E) \(\$30.00\)

2

一个小洗发水瓶可装 \(35\) 毫升洗发水，而一个大瓶可装 \(500\) 毫升。Jasmine 想购买最少数量的小瓶，足以完全装满一个大瓶。她必须买多少瓶？

A\(11\) B\(12\) C\(13\) D\(14\) E\(15\)

English

A small bottle of shampoo can hold \(35\) milliliters of shampoo, whereas a large bottle can hold \(500\) milliliters of shampoo. Jasmine wants to buy the minimum number of small bottles necessary to completely fill a large bottle. How many bottles must she buy?

(A) \(11\)(B) \(12\)(C) \(13\)(D) \(14\)(E) \(15\)

3

假设 \([a\ b]\) 表示 \(a\) 和 \(b\) 的平均数，而 \(\{a\ b\ c\}\) 表示 \(a\)、\(b\) 和 \(c\) 的平均数。求下式的值：\[\{\{1 \ 1 \ 0\} \ [0 \ 1] \ 0\}\]

A\(\dfrac{2}{9}\) B\(\dfrac{5}{18}\) C\(\dfrac{1}{3}\) D\(\dfrac{7}{18}\) E\(\dfrac{2}{3}\)

English

Suppose \([a\ b]\) denotes the average of \(a\) and \(b,\) and \(\{a\ b\ c\}\) denotes the average of \(a,\) \(b,\) and \(c.\) What is the value of the following expression? \[\{\{1 \ 1 \ 0\} \ [0 \ 1] \ 0\}\]

(A) \(\dfrac{2}{9}\)(B) \(\dfrac{5}{18}\)(C) \(\dfrac{1}{3}\)(D) \(\dfrac{7}{18}\)(E) \(\dfrac{2}{3}\)

4

令 \(X\) 和 \(Y\) 为下列等差数列的和：\[\begin{aligned} X &= 10+12+14+\cdots+100,\\ Y &= 12+14+16+\cdots+102 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]\(Y - X\) 的值是多少？

A\(92\) B\(98\) C\(100\) D\(102\) E\(112\)

English

Let \(X\) and \(Y\) be the following sums of arithmetic sequences: \[\begin{aligned} X &= 10+12+14+\cdots+100,\\ Y &= 12+14+16+\cdots+102. \end{aligned}\] What is the value of \(Y - X?\)

(A) \(92\)(B) \(98\)(C) \(100\)(D) \(102\)(E) \(112\)

5

在一所小学，三年级、四年级、五年级学生每天平均分别跑 \(12\)、\(15\)、\(10\) 分钟。三年级学生人数是四年级的两倍，四年级学生人数是五年级的两倍。这些学生每天平均跑多少分钟？

A\(12\) B\(\dfrac{37}{3}\) C\(\dfrac{88}{7}\) D\(13\) E\(14\)

English

At an elementary school, the students in third grade, fourth grade, and fifth grade run an average of \(12,\) \(15,\) and \(10\) minutes per day, respectively. There are twice as many third graders as fourth graders, and twice as many fourth graders as fifth graders. What is the average number of minutes run per day by these students?

(A) \(12\)(B) \(\dfrac{37}{3}\)(C) \(\dfrac{88}{7}\)(D) \(13\)(E) \(14\)

6

集合 \(A\) 有 \(20\) 个元素，集合 \(B\) 有 \(15\) 个元素。\(A \cup B\)，即 \(A\) 和 \(B\) 的并集，最少可能有多少个元素？

A\(5\) B\(15\) C\(20\) D\(35\) E\(300\)

English

Set \(A\) has \(20\) elements, and set \(B\) has \(15\) elements. What is the smallest possible number of elements in \(A \cup B,\) the union of \(A\) and \(B?\)

(A) \(5\)(B) \(15\)(C) \(20\)(D) \(35\)(E) \(300\)

7

下列哪个方程<u>没有</u>解？

A\((x + 7)^2 = 0\) B\(|-3x| + 5 = 0\) C\(\sqrt{-x} - 2 = 0\) D\(\sqrt{x} - 8 = 0\) E\(|-3x| - 4 = 0\)

English

Which of the following equations does <u>not</u> have a solution?

(A) \((x + 7)^2 = 0\)(B) \(|-3x| + 5 = 0\)(C) \(\sqrt{-x} - 2 = 0\)(D) \(\sqrt{x} - 8 = 0\)(E) \(|-3x| - 4 = 0\)

8

去年夏天，Town Lake 上的鸟中 \(30 \%\) 是鹅，\(25 \%\) 是天鹅，\(10 \%\) 是鹭，\(35 \%\) 是鸭。不是天鹅的鸟中，百分之多少是鹅？

A\(20\) B\(30\) C\(40\) D\(50\) E\(60\)

English

Last summer \(30 \%\) of the birds living on Town Lake were geese, \(25 \%\) were swans, \(10 \%\) were herons, and \(35 \%\) were ducks. What percent of the birds that were not swans were geese?

(A) \(20\)(B) \(30\)(C) \(40\)(D) \(50\)(E) \(60\)

9

一个长方形区域由直线 \(y=a\)，\(y=-b\)，\(x=-c\) 和 \(x=d\) 围成，其中 \(a\)，\(b\)，\(c\) 和 \(d\) 都是正数。下列哪一项表示该区域的面积？

A\(ac+ad+bc+bd\) B\(ac-ad+bc-bd\) C\(ac+ad-bc-bd\) D\(-ac-ad+bc+bd\) E\(ac-ad-bc+bd\)

English

A rectangular region is bounded by the graphs of the equations \(y=a,\) \(y=-b,\) \(x=-c,\) and \(x=d,\) where \(a,\) \(b,\) \(c,\) and \(d\) are all positive numbers. Which of the following represents the area of this region?

(A) \(ac+ad+bc+bd\)(B) \(ac-ad+bc-bd\)(C) \(ac+ad-bc-bd\)(D) \(-ac-ad+bc+bd\)(E) \(ac-ad-bc+bd\)

10

Demeanor 老师班上 \(30\) 名学生中的大多数人在学校书店买了铅笔。这些学生每人买了相同数量的铅笔，且这个数量大于 \(1\)。每支铅笔的价格（美分）大于每人购买的铅笔数。所有铅笔的总价为 \(\$17.71\)。每支铅笔多少美分？

A\(7\) B\(11\) C\(17\) D\(23\) E\(77\)

English

A majority of the \(30\) students in Ms. Demeanor’s class bought pencils at the school bookstore. Each of these students bought the same number of pencils, and this number was greater than \(1.\) The cost of a pencil in cents was greater than the number of pencils each student bought, and the total cost of all the pencils was \(\$17.71.\) What was the cost of a pencil in cents?

(A) \(7\)(B) \(11\)(C) \(17\)(D) \(23\)(E) \(77\)

11

正方形 \(EFGH\) 的每个顶点分别位于正方形 \(ABCD\) 的一条边上。点 \(E\) 在 \(\overline{AB}\) 上，且 \(AE=7\cdot EB\)。\(EFGH\) 的面积与 \(ABCD\) 的面积之比是多少？

English

Square \(EFGH\) has one vertex on each side of square \(ABCD.\) Point \(E\) is on \(\overline{AB}\) with \(AE=7\cdot EB.\) What is the ratio of the area of \(EFGH\) to the area of \(ABCD?\)

(A) \(\dfrac{49}{64}\)(B) \(\dfrac{25}{32}\)(C) \(\dfrac78\)(D) \(\dfrac{5\sqrt{2}}{8}\)(E) \(\dfrac{\sqrt{14}}{4}\)

12

一支篮球队投进了一些三分球、两分球和一分罚球。他们通过两分球得到的分数与通过三分球得到的分数相同。他们投进的罚球数比投进的两分球数多一个。该队总得分为 \(61\) 分。他们投进了多少个罚球？

A\(13\) B\(14\) C\(15\) D\(16\) E\(17\)

English

The players on a basketball team made some three-point shots, some two-point shots, and some one-point free throws. They scored as many points with two-point shots as with three-point shots. Their number of successful free throws was one more than their number of successful two-point shots. The team’s total score was \(61\) points. How many free throws did they make?

(A) \(13\)(B) \(14\)(C) \(15\)(D) \(16\)(E) \(17\)

13

在 \(200\) 到 \(700\) 之间，有多少个偶整数的各位数字互不相同，且都取自集合 \(\{1,2,5,7,8,9\}\)？

A\(12\) B\(20\) C\(72\) D\(120\) E\(200\)

English

How many even integers are there between \(200\) and \(700\) whose digits are all different and come from the set \(\{1,2,5,7,8,9\}?\)

(A) \(12\)(B) \(20\)(C) \(72\)(D) \(120\)(E) \(200\)

14

一对标准的 \(6\)-面均匀骰子掷一次。掷出的点数和决定一个圆的直径。圆的面积数值小于圆周长数值的概率是多少？

A\(\dfrac{1}{36}\) B\(\dfrac{1}{12}\) C\(\dfrac{1}{6}\) D\(\dfrac{1}{4}\) E\(\dfrac{5}{18}\)

English

A pair of standard \(6\)-sided fair dice is rolled once. The sum of the numbers rolled determines the diameter of a circle. What is the probability that the numerical value of the area of the circle is less than the numerical value of the circle’s circumference?

(A) \(\dfrac{1}{36}\)(B) \(\dfrac{1}{12}\)(C) \(\dfrac{1}{6}\)(D) \(\dfrac{1}{4}\)(E) \(\dfrac{5}{18}\)

15

Roy 买了一辆新的电池汽油混合动力汽车。一次旅行中，汽车前 \(40\) 英里只使用电池，之后的路程只使用汽油，每英里耗油 \(0.02\) 加仑。整趟旅行平均每加仑行驶 \(55\) 英里。这趟旅行总长多少英里？

A\(140\) B\(240\) C\(440\) D\(640\) E\(840\)

English

Roy bought a new battery-gasoline hybrid car. On a trip the car ran exclusively on its battery for the first \(40\) miles, then ran exclusively on gasoline for the rest of the trip, using gasoline at a rate of \(0.02\) gallons per mile. On the whole trip he averaged \(55\) miles per gallon. How long was the trip in miles?

(A) \(140\)(B) \(240\)(C) \(440\)(D) \(640\)(E) \(840\)

17

在八项数列 \(A\)、\(B\)、\(C\)、\(D\)、\(E\)、\(F\)、\(G\)、\(H\) 中，\(C\) 的值为 \(5\)，并且任意三个连续项之和为 \(30\)。\(A+H\) 是多少？

18

圆 \(A\)、\(B\) 和 \(C\) 的半径都为 \(1\)。圆 \(A\) 和圆 \(B\) 有一个切点。圆 \(C\) 与 \(\overline{AB}\) 的中点相切。圆 \(C\) 内但圆 \(A\) 和圆 \(B\) 外的面积是多少？

English

Circles \(A,\) \(B,\) and \(C\) each have radius \(1.\) Circles \(A\) and \(B\) share one point of tangency. Circle \(C\) has a point of tangency with the midpoint of \(\overline{AB}.\) What is the area inside circle \(C\) but outside circle \(A\) and circle \(B?\)

(A) \(3 - \dfrac{\pi}{2}\)(B) \(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\)(C) \(2\)(D) \(\dfrac{3\pi}{4}\)(E) \(1+\dfrac{\pi}{2}\)

19

In \(1991\) the population of a town was a perfect square. Ten years later, after an increase of \(150\) people, the population was \(9\) more than a perfect square. Now, in \(2011,\) with an increase of another \(150\) people, the population is once again a perfect square. Which of the following is closest to the percent growth of the town’s population during this twenty-year period?

(A) \(42\)(B) \(47\)(C) \(52\)(D) \(57\)(E) \(62\)

20

21

(A) \(\dfrac{7}{11}\)(B) \(\dfrac{9}{13}\)(C) \(\dfrac{11}{15}\)(D) \(\dfrac{15}{19}\)(E) \(\dfrac{15}{16}\)

22

23

七名学生按如下方式从 \(1\) 数到 \(1000\)：

• Alice 说出所有数字，但跳过每连续三个数字中的中间那个。也就是说，Alice 说 \(1\)、\(3\)、\(4\)、\(6\)、\(7\)、\(9\)、\(\ldots\)、\(997\)、\(999\)、\(1000\)。

• Barbara 说出所有 Alice 没说的数字，但她也跳过自己连续三个数字中的中间那个。

• Candice 说出 Alice 和 Barbara 都没说的数字，但她也跳过自己连续三个数字中的中间那个。

• Debbie、Eliza 和 Fatima 也依次说出所有名字字母顺序在她们之前的学生都没说的数字，但各自也跳过自己连续三个数字中的中间那个。

• 最后，George 说出唯一没有别人说过的数字。

George 说的数字是多少？

A\(37\) B\(242\) C\(365\) D\(728\) E\(998\)

English

Seven students count from \(1\) to \(1000\) as follows:

• Alice says all the numbers, except she skips the middle number in each consecutive group of three numbers. That is, Alice says \(1,\) \(3,\) \(4,\) \(6,\) \(7,\) \(9,\) \(\ldots,\) \(997,\) \(999,\) \(1000.\)

• Barbara says all of the numbers that Alice doesn’t say, except she also skips the middle number in each consecutive group of three numbers.

• Candice says all of the numbers that neither Alice nor Barbara says, except she also skips the middle number in each consecutive group of three numbers.

• Debbie, Eliza, and Fatima say all of the numbers that none of the students with the first names beginning before theirs in the alphabet say, except each also skips the middle number in each of her consecutive groups of three numbers.

• Finally, George says the only number that no one else says.

What number does George say?

(A) \(37\)(B) \(242\)(C) \(365\)(D) \(728\)(E) \(998\)

逐题解析

1 答案 D

月费为 \(\$20\)。短信费用为 \[100\cdot 5=500\] 美分，即 \(\$5\)。她还多通话 \(0.5\) 小时，即 \(30\) 分钟，超出了套餐包含的 \(30\) 小时。

因此超时通话费用为 \[30\cdot10=300\] 美分，即 \(\$3\)。

总费用为 \[ \$20 + \$5 + \$3 = \$28\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Michelle has to pay \(\$20\) for the monthly fee. Her text messages cost \[100\cdot 5=500\] cents, or \(\$5.\) Finally she talked for \(0.5\) hours, \(30\) minutes, over \(30\) hours.

This means her extra charge is \[30\cdot10=300\] cents, or \(\$3.\)

Her total cost is \[ \$20 + \$5 + \$3 = \$28. \]

Thus, D is the correct answer.

2 答案 E

所需瓶数为 \[ \dfrac{500}{35} = \dfrac{100}{7} = 14 \dfrac{2}{7}\text{。}\]

因此最少必须购买 \(15\) 个小瓶。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The desired amount is \[ \dfrac{500}{35} = \dfrac{100}{7} = 14 \dfrac{2}{7}. \]

This means that the smallest number of small bottles she must buy is \(15.\)

Thus, E is the correct answer.

3 答案 D

先计算得 \[ \{1 \ 1 \ 0\} = \dfrac{1 + 1 + 0}{3} = \dfrac{2}{3}\text{。}\]

另一个内层平均数为 \[ [0 \ 1] = \dfrac{1 + 0}{2} = \dfrac{1}{2}\text{。}\]

最后得 \[ \left\{\dfrac{2}{3} \ \dfrac{1}{2} \ 0\right\} = \dfrac{\dfrac{2}{3} + \dfrac{1}{2} + 0}{3} = \dfrac{7}{18}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

We have that \[ \{1 \ 1 \ 0\} = \dfrac{1 + 1 + 0}{3} = \dfrac{2}{3}. \]

We also get that \[ [0 \ 1] = \dfrac{1 + 0}{2} = \dfrac{1}{2}. \]

Finally, \[ \left\{\dfrac{2}{3} \ \dfrac{1}{2} \ 0\right\} = \dfrac{\dfrac{2}{3} + \dfrac{1}{2} + 0}{3} = \dfrac{7}{18}.\]

Thus, D is the correct answer.

4 答案 A

从 \(12\) 到 \(100\) 的所有偶数项在两个和中都出现，作差时会全部抵消。

因此 \[ Y - X = 102 - 10 = 92\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Note that the terms \(12\) through \(100\) are common to both sums. When we subtract, all these terms cancel out.

This means that \[ Y - X = 102 - 10 = 92. \]

Applying the same argument to all four sides shows that \[\begin{gathered} \dfrac{nu}{2},\quad\dfrac{n(1-u)}{2},\\ \dfrac{nv}{2},\quad\dfrac{n(1-v)}{2} \end{gathered}\] are positive integers. Conversely, whenever these four numbers are integers, subdividing each side into the indicated number of equal bases and joining the division points to \(X\) produces the required triangles.

For \(n=100,\) this says \(u=\frac{i}{50}\) and \(v=\frac{j}{50}\) for \(i,j\in\{1,2,\ldots,49\}.\) Hence the \(100\)-ray points form a \(49\times49\) grid. Similarly, the \(60\)-ray points have coordinates \(u=\frac{i}{30}\) and \(v=\frac{j}{30}\) with \(i,j\in\{1,2,\ldots,29\}.\)

A coordinate belongs to both grids exactly when \(\frac{i}{50}=\frac{j}{30},\) or \(3i=5j.\) Thus the common coordinates are \(\frac{1}{10},\frac{2}{10},\ldots,\frac{9}{10},\) giving a \(9\times9\) overlap. The requested number is \[49^2-9^2=2401-81=2320.\]

Thus, C is the correct answer.
