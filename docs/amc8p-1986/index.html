逐题解析

1 答案 A

平均每小时降雨量等于总降雨量除以总小时数。七月有 \(31\) 天，即 \(31 \times 24\) 小时，所以平均值是 \(\dfrac{366}{31 \times 24} = \dfrac{61}{124}\) 英寸每小时。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The average rainfall per hour equals the total rainfall divided by the total number of hours. July has \(31\) days, or \(31 \times 24\) hours, so the average is \(\dfrac{366}{31 \times 24} = \dfrac{61}{124}\) inches per hour.

Thus, the correct answer is A .

2 答案 A

较大的正数倒数较小，较小的正数倒数较大。列出的数中最小的是 \(\dfrac13\)，它的倒数 \(3\) 最大。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

A large positive number has a small reciprocal, and a small positive number has a large reciprocal. The smallest number listed is \(\dfrac13,\) whose reciprocal \(3\) is the largest.

Thus, the correct answer is A .

3 答案 C

集合中三个最小的数是 \(-3\)、\(-1\) 和 \(7\)。它们的和是 \(-3 + (-1) + 7 = 3\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The three smallest numbers in the set are \(-3,\) \(-1,\) and \(7.\) Their sum is \(-3 + (-1) + 7 = 3.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C .

4 答案 C

第二个因数 \(40.3 + 0.07 = 40.37\) 约为 \(40\)，而 \(1.8\) 约为 \(2\)。快速估算为 \(2(40) - 0.2(40) = 80 - 8 = 72\)，所以乘积最接近 \(74\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The second factor \(40.3 + 0.07 = 40.37\) is about \(40,\) and \(1.8\) is about \(2.\) A quick estimate is \(2(40) - 0.2(40) = 80 - 8 = 72,\) so the product is closest to \(74.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C .

5 答案 D

因为 \(1000\) 分钟 \(= \dfrac{1000}{60}\) 小时 \(= 16\dfrac23\) 小时 \(= 16\) 小时 \(40\) 分钟，所以比赛在中午后 \(16\) 小时 \(40\) 分钟结束，即凌晨 \(4\!:\!40\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Since \(1000\) minutes \(= \dfrac{1000}{60}\) hours \(= 16\dfrac23\) hours \(= 16\) hours \(40\) minutes, the contest ended \(16\) hours \(40\) minutes past noon, which is \(4\!:\!40\) a.m.

Thus, the correct answer is D .

6 答案 E

分母是 \(1 - \dfrac23 = \dfrac13\)，所以表达式是 \(\dfrac{2}{\frac{1}{3}} = 2 \times 3 = 6\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The denominator is \(1 - \dfrac23 = \dfrac13,\) so the expression is \(\dfrac{2}{\frac{1}{3}} = 2 \times 3 = 6.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E .

7 答案 B

因为 \(2 \lt \sqrt{8} \lt 3\)，且 \(8 \lt \sqrt{80} \lt 9\)，所以严格在 \(\sqrt{8}\) 和 \(\sqrt{80}\) 之间的整数是 \(3\)、\(4\)、\(5\)、\(6\)、\(7\)、\(8\)。共有六个。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Since \(2 \lt \sqrt{8} \lt 3\) and \(8 \lt \sqrt{80} \lt 9,\) the whole numbers strictly between \(\sqrt{8}\) and \(\sqrt{80}\) are \(3,\) \(4,\) \(5,\) \(6,\) \(7,\) \(8.\) There are six of them.

Thus, the correct answer is B .

8 答案 E

乘积的个位来自 \(B \times 2\)，只有当 \(B = 3\) 或 \(B = 8\) 时才以 \(6\) 结尾。由于乘积 \(6396\) 大于 \(6000\)，\(B\) 必须是 \(8\)：确实 \(82 \times 78 = 6396\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The units digit of the product comes from \(B \times 2,\) which ends in \(6\) only when \(B = 3\) or \(B = 8.\) Since the product \(6396\) exceeds \(6000,\) \(B\) must be \(8:\) indeed \(82 \times 78 = 6396.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E .

11 答案 A

12 答案 D

学生两次测验成绩相同，正对应主对角线上的格子。这些数是 \(2 + 4 + 5 + 1 + 0 = 12\)。

所以比例是 \(\dfrac{12}{30} = \dfrac{4}{10} = 40\%\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

A student received the same grade on both tests exactly when counted on the main diagonal. Those entries are \(2 + 4 + 5 + 1 + 0 = 12.\)

So the fraction is \(\dfrac{12}{30} = \dfrac{4}{10} = 40\%.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D .

13 答案 C

因为每个角都是直角，把边平移重组可见，水平边总共走过宽度两次，竖直边走过高度两次。因此周长等于完整 \(8\) 乘 \(6\) 矩形的周长，即 \(2(8 + 6) = 28\)。

答案不取决于缺口具体切在哪里。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Because every angle is a right angle, sliding the edges shows that the horizontal edges together traverse the width twice and the vertical edges traverse the height twice. So the perimeter equals that of the full \(8\) by \(6\) rectangle, \(2(8 + 6) = 28.\)

The answer does not depend on exactly where the notch is cut.

Thus, the correct answer is C .

English

The sale price is \(50\%\) of \(\$180,\) or \(\$90.\) The Saturday price takes another \(20\%\) off, leaving \(80\%\) of \(\$90,\) which is \(\$72.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B .

16 答案 A

如果秋季 \(4\) 百万个是全年总量的 \(25\%\)，那么全年总量是 \(16\) 百万个。

冬季销量是 \(16 - (4.5 + 5 + 4) = 2.5\) 百万个。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

If the fall sales of \(4\) million are \(25\%\) of the yearly total, then the yearly total is \(16\) million.

The winter sales are \(16 - (4.5 + 5 + 4) = 2.5\) million.

Thus, the correct answer is A .

17 答案 E

因式分解得 \(o^2 + no = o(o + n)\)。因为 \(o\) 是奇数，这个乘积为奇数当且仅当 \(o + n\) 为奇数，而这只在 \(n\) 为偶数时发生。当 \(n\) 为奇数时，\(o + n\) 为偶数，乘积为偶数。

所以这个数只有当 \(n\) 为偶数时才是奇数。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Factor \(o^2 + no = o(o + n).\) Because \(o\) is odd, the product is odd exactly when \(o + n\) is odd, which happens only when \(n\) is even. When \(n\) is odd, \(o + n\) is even and the product is even.

So the number is odd only if \(n\) is even.

Thus, the correct answer is E .

18 答案 B

当石墙作为较长的 \(60\) 米边时，用的柱子最少；此时栅栏覆盖两条 \(36\) 米边和一条 \(60\) 米边，总路径长 \(36 + 60 + 36 = 132\) 米。

每隔 \(12\) 米放一根柱子并包括两端，共用 \(\dfrac{132}{12} + 1 = 12\) 根柱子。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The fewest posts are used when the wall serves as the longer \(60\) meter side, so the fence covers two \(36\) meter sides and one \(60\) meter side, a path of \(36 + 60 + 36 = 132\) meters.

Placing a post every \(12\) meters, including both ends, uses \(\dfrac{132}{12} + 1 = 12\) posts.

Thus, the correct answer is B .

19 答案 D

旅行距离是 \(57{,}060 - 56{,}200 = 860\) 英里。最初的 \(6\) 加仑只是出发前加满油箱；旅途中真正用掉的是后来为补回油量所加的 \(12 + 20 = 32\) 加仑。

所以平均值是 \(\dfrac{860}{32} \approx 26.9\) 英里每加仑。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The trip was \(57{,}060 - 56{,}200 = 860\) miles. The initial \(6\) gallons only topped off the tank before the trip; the gas actually used during the trip is the \(12 + 20 = 32\) gallons later added to replace it.

So the average is \(\dfrac{860}{32} \approx 26.9\) miles per gallon.

Thus, the correct answer is D .

20 答案 D

估算：

\[ \begin{aligned} \dfrac{300^5}{30 \cdot 400^4} &= 10 \left(\dfrac{300}{400}\right)^4 \\ &= 10 \left(\dfrac34\right)^4 \\ &= 10 \cdot \dfrac{81}{256} \approx 3 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Estimating,

\[ \begin{aligned} \dfrac{300^5}{30 \cdot 400^4} &= 10 \left(\dfrac{300}{400}\right)^4 \\ &= 10 \left(\dfrac34\right)^4 \\ &= 10 \cdot \dfrac{81}{256} \approx 3. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is D .

21 答案 E

四个阴影正方形折成开口盒子的四个面，新增正方形提供第五个面。想象折叠过程，正方形 \(A\)、\(E\)、\(H\)、\(B\)、\(D\) 和 \(F\) 都能补成一个有效的无顶盒。

正方形 \(C\) 和 \(G\) 不行，因为折叠后会迫使四个面在同一个角相遇。因此有 \(6\) 个有效图形。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The four shaded squares fold into four faces of an open box, and the added square supplies the fifth face. Picturing the folds, the squares \(A,\) \(E,\) \(H,\) \(B,\) \(D,\) and \(F\) each complete a valid topless box.

The squares \(C\) and \(G\) do not work, because folding would force four faces to meet at a single corner. That leaves \(6\) valid figures.

Thus, the correct answer is E .

22 答案 C

这些陈述形成一条链：艾伦得 A 会推出贝丝得 A，贝丝得 A 会推出卡洛斯得 A，卡洛斯得 A 会推出戴安娜得 A。如果艾伦得 A，则四人都得 A；如果贝丝得 A，则三人得 A。

正好有两个 A 的唯一方式是链条最后两人得 A，即卡洛斯和戴安娜。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The statements form a chain: Alan’s A forces Beth’s, Beth’s forces Carlos’s, and Carlos’s forces Diana’s. If Alan got an A, all four would; if Beth got an A, three would.

The only way to have exactly two A’s is for them to be the last two in the chain, Carlos and Diana.

Thus, the correct answer is C .

23 答案 B

大圆半径为 \(2\)，所以面积是 \(\pi(2)^2 = 4\pi\)，每个小圆面积是 \(\pi\)。

由对称性，阴影区域是面积差的一半：\(\dfrac12(4\pi - 2\pi) = \pi\)。它与一个小圆面积 \(\pi\) 的比值是 \(1\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The large circle has radius \(2,\) so its area is \(\pi(2)^2 = 4\pi,\) and each small circle has area \(\pi.\)

By symmetry the shaded region is half the difference of the areas: \(\dfrac12(4\pi - 2\pi) = \pi.\) The ratio of this to one small circle’s area \(\pi\) is \(1.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B .

24 答案 B

不论艾尔在哪一组，鲍勃进入同一组的概率为 \(\dfrac{199}{599}\)。如果鲍勃进入该组，卡罗尔也进入该组的概率为 \(\dfrac{198}{598}\)。

所以三人在同一组的概率约为 \(\dfrac{199}{599} \cdot \dfrac{198}{598} \approx 0.110\)，最接近 \(\dfrac19\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Whatever group Al is in, Bob joins that same group with probability \(\dfrac{199}{599}.\) If Bob does, Carol joins them with probability \(\dfrac{198}{598}.\)

Thus all three share a group with probability \(\dfrac{199}{599} \cdot \dfrac{198}{598} \approx 0.110,\) closest to \(\dfrac19.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B .

25 答案 D

对一组等间隔整数，平均数等于最小数与最大数的平均数。各组平均数为：A：\(\dfrac{2 + 100}{2} = 51\)，B：\(\dfrac{3 + 99}{2} = 51\)，C：\(\dfrac{4 + 100}{2} = 52\)，D：\(\dfrac{5 + 100}{2} = 52.5\)，E：\(\dfrac{6 + 96}{2} = 51\)。

最大平均数是 \(52.5\)，来自 \(5\) 的倍数。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

For a set of evenly spaced whole numbers, the average is the average of the smallest and largest. The averages are: A: \(\dfrac{2 + 100}{2} = 51,\) B: \(\dfrac{3 + 99}{2} = 51,\) C: \(\dfrac{4 + 100}{2} = 52,\) D: \(\dfrac{5 + 100}{2} = 52.5,\) E: \(\dfrac{6 + 96}{2} = 51.\)

The largest average is \(52.5,\) from the multiples of \(5.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D .
