1

English

(A) \(1\!:\!50\) pm(B) \(3\!:\!00\) pm(C) \(3\!:\!30\) pm(D) \(4\!:\!30\) pm(E) \(5\!:\!50\) pm

6

(A) \(750\)(B) \(900\)(C) \(1000\)(D) \(1050\)(E) \(1500\)

7

Steve 和 LeRoy 在离岸 \(1\) 英里的地方钓鱼时，船漏水了，水以每分钟 \(10\) 加仑的恒定速度进入船内。若船中进水超过 \(30\) 加仑，船就会沉。Steve 以每小时 \(4\) 英里的恒定速度划向岸边，同时 LeRoy 把水舀出船。为了在船不沉的情况下到岸，LeRoy 最慢需要以每分钟多少加仑的速度舀水？

A\(2\) B\(4\) C\(6\) D\(8\) E\(10\)

English

While Steve and LeRoy are fishing \(1\) mile from shore, their boat springs a leak, and water comes in at a constant rate of \(10\) gallons per minute. The boat will sink if it takes in more than \(30\) gallons of water. Steve starts rowing toward the shore at a constant rate of \(4\) miles per hour while LeRoy bails water out of the boat. What is the slowest rate, in gallons per minute, at which LeRoy can bail if they are to reach the shore without sinking?

(A) \(2\)(B) \(4\)(C) \(6\)(D) \(8\)(E) \(10\)

逐题解析

1 答案 D

从上午 \(8\!:\!30\) 到 \(11\!:\!10\) 是 \(2\) 小时 \(40\) 分，也就是 \(160\) 分钟，完成了三分之一。

整项工作需要 \(3 \cdot 160 = 480\) 分钟，即 \(8\) 小时。从 \(8\!:\!30\) 再过 \(8\) 小时，得到 \(4\!:\!30\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

From \(8\!:\!30\) am to \(11\!:\!10\) am is \(2\) hours \(40\) minutes, or \(160\) minutes, to complete one third of the job.

The whole job then takes \(3 \cdot 160 = 480\) minutes, or \(8\) hours. Adding \(8\) hours to \(8\!:\!30\) am gives \(4\!:\!30\) pm.

Thus, D is the correct answer.

2 答案 A

先通分相加，得到 \[ \dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{3}{6} + \dfrac{4}{6} = \dfrac{7}{6}\text{。}\]

\(\dfrac{7}{6}\) 的倒数是 \(\dfrac{6}{7}\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Using a common denominator, \[ \dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{3}{6} + \dfrac{4}{6} = \dfrac{7}{6}. \]

The reciprocal of \(\dfrac{7}{6}\) is \(\dfrac{6}{7}.\)

Thus, A is the correct answer.

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Adding \(1 + i\) to both sides, the left side becomes \[ \begin{aligned} &z^4 + 4z^3 i - 6z^2 - 4zi + 1 \\ &= (z + i)^4, \end{aligned} \] so \((z + i)^4 = 1 + i.\)

The four solutions for \(w = z + i\) are equally spaced on a circle of radius \(|1 + i|^{\frac{1}{4}} = (2^{\frac{1}{2}})^{\frac{1}{4}} = 2^{\frac{1}{8}},\) and they form a square. Subtracting \(i\) merely translates it.

A square inscribed in a circle of radius \(2^{\frac{1}{8}}\) has diagonal \(2 \cdot 2^{\frac{1}{8}} = 2^{\frac{9}{8}},\) so its side is \(\tfrac{2^{\frac{9}{8}}}{\sqrt{2}} = 2^{\frac{5}{8}}.\)

The area is \[ \left(2^{\frac{5}{8}}\right)^2 = 2^{\frac{5}{4}}. \]

Thus, D is the correct answer.

24 答案 D

取 \(C = (0, 0)\)，\(B = (2, 2\sqrt{3})\)，使 \(\angle C = 60^\circ\) 且 \(BC = 4\)，令 \(A = (x, 0)\)，其中 \(x \gt 0\)。则 \(D = (1, \sqrt{3})\) 是 \(BC\) 的中点。

向量 \(\overrightarrow{AB} = (2-x, 2\sqrt{3})\) 和 \(\overrightarrow{AD} = (1-x, \sqrt{3})\) 的叉积的模为 \(\sqrt{3}\,x\)，点积为 \(x^2 - 3x + 8\)，后者恒为正。因此 \[ \tan(\angle BAD) = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}\,x}{x^2 - 3x + 8}\text{。}\]

导数的符号与 \(8-x^2\) 相同，所以唯一的最大值在 \(x = 2\sqrt{2}\) 处取得。代入得 \[ \begin{aligned} \tan(\angle BAD) &= \dfrac{2\sqrt{6}}{16 - 6\sqrt{2}} \\ &= \dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{8 - 3\sqrt{2}} \\ &= \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4\sqrt{2} - 3} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Place \(C = (0, 0),\) \(B = (2, 2\sqrt{3})\) so that \(\angle C = 60^\circ\) and \(BC = 4,\) and let \(A = (x, 0)\) with \(x \gt 0.\) Then \(D = (1, \sqrt{3})\) is the midpoint of \(BC.\)

The vectors \(\overrightarrow{AB} = (2-x, 2\sqrt{3})\) and \(\overrightarrow{AD} = (1-x, \sqrt{3})\) have cross-product magnitude \(\sqrt{3}\,x\) and dot product \(x^2 - 3x + 8,\) which is always positive. Hence \[ \tan(\angle BAD) = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}\,x}{x^2 - 3x + 8}. \]

The derivative has the sign of \(8-x^2,\) so the unique maximum occurs at \(x = 2\sqrt{2}.\) Substituting, \[ \begin{aligned} \tan(\angle BAD) &= \dfrac{2\sqrt{6}}{16 - 6\sqrt{2}} \\ &= \dfrac{\sqrt{6}}{8 - 3\sqrt{2}} \\ &= \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4\sqrt{2} - 3}. \end{aligned} \]

25 答案 D

令 \(z_n = a_n + b_n i\)，则 \[ \begin{aligned} z_{n+1} &= (\sqrt{3}\,a_n - b_n) \\ &\quad {}+ (\sqrt{3}\,b_n + a_n)i \\ &= (a_n + b_n i)(\sqrt{3} + i)\text{，} \end{aligned} \] 所以 \(z_{n+1} = z_n(\sqrt{3} + i)\)，且 \(z_{100} = z_1(\sqrt{3} + i)^{99}\)。

因为 \(\sqrt{3} + i = 2(\cos 30^\circ + i\sin 30^\circ)\)，棣莫弗定理给出 \((\sqrt{3} + i)^{99}\) \(= 2^{99}(\cos 2970^\circ + i\sin 2970^\circ)\)。而 \(2970^\circ\) 与 \(90^\circ\) 同终边，所以它等于 \(2^{99} i\)。

于是 \(2 + 4i = z_1 \cdot 2^{99} i\)，所以 \[ z_1 = \dfrac{2 + 4i}{2^{99} i} = \dfrac{4 - 2i}{2^{99}}\text{。} \]

因此 \(a_1 = \tfrac{4}{2^{99}}\)，\(b_1 = -\tfrac{2}{2^{99}}\)，所以 \[ a_1 + b_1 = \dfrac{2}{2^{99}} = \dfrac{1}{2^{98}}\text{。} \]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Let \(z_n = a_n + b_n i.\) Then \[ \begin{aligned} z_{n+1} &= (\sqrt{3}\,a_n - b_n) \\ &\quad {}+ (\sqrt{3}\,b_n + a_n)i \\ &= (a_n + b_n i)(\sqrt{3} + i), \end{aligned} \] so \(z_{n+1} = z_n(\sqrt{3} + i)\) and \(z_{100} = z_1(\sqrt{3} + i)^{99}.\)

Since \(\sqrt{3} + i = 2(\cos 30^\circ + i\sin 30^\circ),\) De Moivre’s theorem gives \((\sqrt{3} + i)^{99}\) \(= 2^{99}(\cos 2970^\circ + i\sin 2970^\circ).\) As \(2970^\circ\) is coterminal with \(90^\circ,\) this equals \(2^{99} i.\)

Thus \(2 + 4i = z_1 \cdot 2^{99} i,\) so \[ z_1 = \dfrac{2 + 4i}{2^{99} i} = \dfrac{4 - 2i}{2^{99}}. \]

Then \(a_1 = \tfrac{4}{2^{99}}\) and \(b_1 = -\tfrac{2}{2^{99}},\) so \[ a_1 + b_1 = \dfrac{2}{2^{99}} = \dfrac{1}{2^{98}}. \]

Thus, D is the correct answer.
