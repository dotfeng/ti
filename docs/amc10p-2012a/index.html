1

Cagney 每 \(20\) 秒给一个纸杯蛋糕涂糖霜，Lacey 每 \(30\) 秒给一个纸杯蛋糕涂糖霜。两人一起工作，\(5\) 分钟内可以给多少个纸杯蛋糕涂糖霜？

A\(10\) B\(15\) C\(20\) D\(25\) E\(30\)

English

Cagney can frost a cupcake every \(20\) seconds and Lacey can frost a cupcake every \(30\) seconds. Working together, how many cupcakes can they frost in \(5\) minutes?

(A) \(10\)(B) \(15\)(C) \(20\)(D) \(25\)(E) \(30\)

2

一个边长为 \(8\) 的正方形被切成两块全等的长方形。每个长方形的边长是多少？

A\(2 \text{ 乘 } 4\) B\(2 \text{ 乘 } 6\) C\(2 \text{ 乘 } 8\) D\(4 \text{ 乘 } 4\) E\(4 \text{ 乘 } 8\)

English

A square with side length \(8\) is cut in half, creating two congruent rectangles. What are the dimensions of one of these rectangles?

(A) \(2 \text{ by } 4\)(B) \(2 \text{ by } 6\)(C) \(2 \text{ by } 8\)(D) \(4 \text{ by } 4\)(E) \(4 \text{ by } 8\)

3

一只小虫在数轴上爬行，起点是 \(-2\)。它先爬到 \(-6\)，然后掉头爬到 \(5\)。这只小虫一共爬了多少个单位？

A\(9\) B\(11\) C\(13\) D\(14\) E\(15\)

English

A bug crawls along a number line, starting at \(-2.\) It crawls to \(-6,\) then turns around and crawls to \(5.\) How many units does the bug crawl altogether?

(A) \(9\)(B) \(11\)(C) \(13\)(D) \(14\)(E) \(15\)

4

已知 \(\angle ABC = 24^\circ\)，\(\angle ABD = 20^\circ \)。\(\angle CBD\) 的最小可能度数是多少？

A\(0\) B\(2\) C\(4\) D\(6\) E\(12\)

English

Let \(\angle ABC = 24^\circ\) and \(\angle ABD = 20^\circ .\) What is the smallest possible degree measure for \(\angle CBD?\)

(A) \(0\)(B) \(2\)(C) \(4\)(D) \(6\)(E) \(12\)

5

去年有 \(100\) 只成年猫被送到 Smallville 动物收容所，其中一半是母猫。成年母猫中有一半各带着一窝小猫，每窝平均有 \(4\) 只小猫。去年这个收容所一共收到多少只成年猫和小猫？

A\(150\) B\(200\) C\(250\) D\(300\) E\(400\)

English

Last year \(100\) adult cats, half of whom were female, were brought into the Smallville Animal Shelter. Half of the adult female cats were accompanied by a litter of kittens. The average number of kittens per litter was \(4.\) What was the total number of cats and kittens received by the shelter last year?

(A) \(150\)(B) \(200\)(C) \(250\)(D) \(300\)(E) \(400\)

6

两个正数的乘积为 \(9\)。其中一个数的倒数是另一个数倒数的 \(4\) 倍。这两个数的和是多少？

A\(\dfrac{10}{3}\) B\(\dfrac{20}{3}\) C\(7\) D\(\dfrac{15}{2}\) E\(8\)

English

The product of two positive numbers is \(9.\) The reciprocal of one of these numbers is \(4\) times the reciprocal of the other number. What is the sum of the two numbers?

(A) \(\dfrac{10}{3}\)(B) \(\dfrac{20}{3}\)(C) \(7\)(D) \(\dfrac{15}{2}\)(E) \(8\)

7

一个袋子里有若干弹珠，其中 \(\dfrac{3}{5}\) 是蓝色的，其余是红色的。现在把红色弹珠的数量加倍，而蓝色弹珠数量不变。此时红色弹珠占全部弹珠的几分之几？

A\(\dfrac{2}{5}\) B\(\dfrac{3}{7}\) C\(\dfrac{4}{7}\) D\(\dfrac{3}{5}\) E\(\dfrac{4}{5}\)

English

In a bag of marbles, \(\dfrac{3}{5}\) of the marbles are blue and the rest are red. If the number of red marbles is doubled and the number of blue marbles stays the same, what fraction of the marbles will be red?

(A) \(\dfrac{2}{5}\)(B) \(\dfrac{3}{7}\)(C) \(\dfrac{4}{7}\)(D) \(\dfrac{3}{5}\)(E) \(\dfrac{4}{5}\)

8

三个非负整数两两相加得到的和分别为 \(12\)，\(17\) 和 \(19\)。这三个数中的中间那个数是多少？

A\(4\) B\(5\) C\(6\) D\(7\) E\(8\)

English

The sums of three whole numbers taken in pairs are \(12,\) \(17,\) and \(19.\) What is the middle number?

(A) \(4\)(B) \(5\)(C) \(6\)(D) \(7\)(E) \(8\)

9

A\(\dfrac{1}{6}\) B\(\dfrac{1}{5}\) C\(\dfrac{1}{4}\) D\(\dfrac{1}{3}\) E\(\dfrac{1}{2}\)

English

A pair of six-sided fair dice are labeled so that one die has only even numbers (two each of \(2,\) \(4,\) and \(6\)), and the other die has only odd numbers (two each of \(1,\) \(3,\) and \(5\)). The pair of dice is rolled. What is the probability that the sum of the numbers on the tops of the two dice is \(7?\)

(A) \(\dfrac{1}{6}\)(B) \(\dfrac{1}{5}\)(C) \(\dfrac{1}{4}\)(D) \(\dfrac{1}{3}\)(E) \(\dfrac{1}{2}\)

10

Mary 把一个圆分成 \(12\) 个扇形。这些扇形的圆心角度数都是整数，并且构成一个等差数列。最小的扇形角的度数最小可能是多少？

A\(5\) B\(6\) C\(8\) D\(10\) E\(12\)

English

Mary divides a circle into \(12\) sectors. The central angles of these sectors, measured in degrees, are all integers and they form an arithmetic sequence. What is the degree measure of the smallest possible sector angle?

(A) \(5\)(B) \(6\)(C) \(8\)(D) \(10\)(E) \(12\)

11

两个圆外切，圆心分别为 \(A\) 和 \(B\)，半径分别为 \(5\) 和 \(3\)。一条公共外切线与射线 \(AB\) 相交于点 \(C\)。求 \(BC\) 的长度。

A\(4\) B\(4.8\) C\(10.2\) D\(12\) E\(14.4\)

English

Externally tangent circles with centers at points \(A\) and \(B\) have radii of lengths \(5\) and \(3,\) respectively. A line externally tangent to both circles intersects ray \(AB\) at point \(C.\) What is \(BC?\)

(A) \(4\)(B) \(4.8\)(C) \(10.2\)(D) \(12\)(E) \(14.4\)

12

根据闰年规则，年份能被 \(400\) 整除就是闰年，例如 \(2000\) 年；或者能被 \(4\) 整除但不能被 \(100\) 整除也是闰年，例如 \(2012\) 年。Charles Dickens 诞辰 \(200\) 周年的庆祝日是 \(2012\) 年二月 \(7\) 日，星期二。他出生那天是星期几？

A星期五 B星期六 C星期日 D星期一 E星期二

English

A year is a leap year if and only if the year number is divisible by \(400\) (such as \(2000\)) or is divisible by \(4\) but not \(100\) (such as \(2012\)). The \(200\)th anniversary of the birth of novelist Charles Dickens was celebrated on February \(7,\) \(2012,\) a Tuesday. On what day of the week was Dickens born?

(A) Friday(B) Saturday(C) Sunday(D) Monday(E) Tuesday

13

数 \(1\)、\(2\)、\(3\)、\(4\) 和 \(5\) 的迭代平均数按如下方式计算。把这五个数按某种顺序排列。先求前两个数的平均数，再求它与第三个数的平均数，然后求所得结果与第四个数的平均数，最后求所得结果与第五个数的平均数。用这个过程能得到的最大可能值与最小可能值之差是多少？

A\(\dfrac{31}{16}\) B\(2\) C\(\dfrac{17}{8}\) D\(3\) E\(\dfrac{65}{16}\)

English

An iterative average of the numbers \(1,\) \(2,\) \(3,\) \(4,\) and \(5\) is computed the following way. Arrange the five numbers in some order. Find the mean of the first two numbers, then find the mean of that with the third number, then the mean of that with the fourth number, and finally the mean of that with the fifth number. What is the difference between the largest and smallest possible values that can be obtained using this procedure?

(A) \(\dfrac{31}{16}\)(B) \(2\)(C) \(\dfrac{17}{8}\)(D) \(3\)(E) \(\dfrac{65}{16}\)

14

Chubby 制作每边有 \(31\) 个方格的非标准棋盘。棋盘的四个角都是黑格，并且每一行、每一列都红黑相间。这样的棋盘上有多少个黑格？

English

Chubby makes nonstandard checkerboards that have \(31\) squares on each side. The checkerboards have a black square in every corner and alternate red and black squares along every row and column. How many black squares are there on such a checkerboard?

(A) \(480\)(B) \(481\)(C) \(482\)(D) \(483\)(E) \(484\)

16

三名跑步者同时从一条 \(500\) 米环形跑道的同一点出发，沿顺时针方向跑。他们的速度分别为每秒 \(4.4\)，\(4.8\) 和 \(5.0\) 米。三人第一次再次同时相遇时，他们已经跑了多少秒？

Three runners start running simultaneously from the same point on a \(500\)-meter circular track. 17

整数 \(a\) 和 \(b\) 互质，满足 \(a > b > 0\)，且 \[\dfrac{a^3-b^3}{(a-b)^3} = \dfrac{73}{3}\text{。}\] 求 \(a-b\)。

18

19

油漆工 Paula 和她的两名助手各自以固定但不同的速度刷漆。他们总是上午 \(8:00\) 开始工作，并且三人每天午餐休息的时间相同。

星期一，三人一起刷完了一栋房子的 \(50\%\)，并在下午 \(4:00\) 停工。星期二，Paula 不在，两名助手只刷完了房子的 \(24\%\)，并在下午 \(2:12\) 停工。星期三，Paula 独自工作，到晚上 \(7:12\) 完成整栋房子。

每天的午餐休息是多少分钟？

A\(30\) B\(36\) C\(42\) D\(48\) E\(60\)

English

Paula the painter and her two helpers each paint at constant, but different, rates. They always start at \(8:00\) AM, and all three always take the same amount of time to eat lunch.

On Monday the three of them painted \(50\%\) of a house, quitting at \(4:00\) PM. On Tuesday, when Paula wasn’t there, the two helpers painted only \(24\%\) of the house and quit at \(2:12\) PM. On Wednesday Paula worked by herself and finished the house by working until \(7:12\) P.M.

How long, in minutes, was each day’s lunch break?

(A) \(30\)(B) \(36\)(C) \(42\)(D) \(48\)(E) \(60\)

20

然后把整个正方形绕中心顺时针旋转 \(90^{\circ}\)，并把每个位于原来黑格位置上的白格染成黑色，其余方格的颜色都保持不变。最终整个网格全为黑色的概率是多少？

The square is then rotated \(90^{\circ}\) clockwise about its center, and every white square in a position formerly occupied by a black square is painted black. The colors of all other squares are left unchanged. What is the probability the grid is now entirely black?

(A) \(\dfrac{49}{512}\)(B) \(\dfrac{7}{64}\)(C) \(\dfrac{121}{1024}\)(D) \(\dfrac{81}{512}\)(E) \(\dfrac{9}{32}\)

逐题解析

1 答案 D

Cagney 每分钟完成 \(60 \div 20 = 3\) 个，Lacey 每分钟完成 \(60 \div 30 = 2\) 个。

\(5\) 分钟内，他们能完成 \[ 5(2 + 3) = 5 \cdot 5 = 25\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Cagney can make \(60 \div 20 = 3\) cupcakes per minute, and Lacey can make \(60 \div 30 = 2.\)

In \(5\) minutes, they can make \[ 5(2 + 3) = 5 \cdot 5 = 25. \]

Thus, D is the correct answer.

2 答案 E

注意其中一条边仍与原正方形的边长相同。

因此一个尺寸是 \(8\)，另一个尺寸是原边长的一半，即 \(4\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Note that the one of the sides remains the same as the original square.

This means that one dimension is \(8.\) The other dimension is the original side cut in half, which is \(4.\)

Thus, E is the correct answer.

3 答案 E

它爬行的距离为 \[ |-2 - (-6)| = |4| = 4 \] 即从 \(-2\) 移动到 \(-6\)。接着它爬行的距离为 \[ |-6 - 5| = |-11| = 11 \] 即从 \(-6\) 移动到 \(5\)。

总距离为 \[ 4 + 11 = 15\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 E。

English

It crawls a distance of \[ |-2 - (-6)| = |4| = 4 \] when it moves from \(-2\) to \(-6.\) It then travels a distance of \[ |-6 - 5| = |-11| = 11 \] as it moves from \(-6\) to \(5.\)

The total distance is then \[ 4 + 11 = 15. \]

Thus, E is the correct answer.

4 答案 C

注意两个角都共用射线 \(AB\)。为了使所求角最小，让 \(D\) 位于 \(A\) 和 \(C\) 之间。

这样就有 \[ \begin{aligned} \angle CBD &= \angle ABC - \angle ABD \\ &= 4^{\circ} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Note that both of the angles share the ray \(AB.\) To minimize the desired degree, we want \(D\) to be between \(A\) and \(C.\)

This would make \[ \begin{aligned} \angle CBD &= \angle ABC - \angle ABD \\ &= 4^{\circ}. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, C is the correct answer.

5 答案 B

成年母猫有 \(100 \div 2 = 50\) 只，其中带小猫的有 \(50 \div 2 = 25\) 只。

因为平均每窝有 \(4\) 只小猫，所以小猫总数为 \[ 4 \cdot 25 = 100\text{。}\]

因此成年猫和小猫的总数为 \[ 100 + 100 = 200\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

We have that there are \(100 \div 2 = 50\) female cats. We then have that \(50 \div 2 = 25\) cats that have kittens.

Since the average number of kittens per litter is \(4,\) the total number of kittens is \[ 4 \cdot 25 = 100. \]

The total number of cats and kittens is then \[ 100 + 100 = 200. \]

Thus, B is the correct answer.

6 答案 D

设两个数为 \(x\) 和 \(y\)，满足 \[ xy = 9 \text{ 和 } \dfrac{1}{x} = \dfrac{4}{y}\text{。}\]

由乘积条件与倒数条件得 \[ y = \dfrac{9}{x} \]\[ \dfrac{1}{x} = \dfrac{4x}{9}\text{。}\]\[ x = \dfrac{3}{2}\text{，}\] 其中 \(x\) 为正。

于是另一个数为 \[ y = 9 \div \dfrac{3}{2} = 6\text{。}\]

两数之和为 \[ 6 + \dfrac{3}{2} = \dfrac{15}{2}\text{。}\]

把这些值代入第一个方程，得到 \[ a^2 - (a - 1)^2 - (a - 3)^2 \]\[ + a(a - 1) = 2011\text{。}\] 化简得 \(7a = 2021\)。由于 \(2021\) 不能被 \(7\) 整除，所以应有 \(a - b = 2\)、\(b - c = 1\)。

在另一种情形下，\(b=a-2\)，\(c=a-3\)。第一个方程变为 \[\begin{aligned} a^2-(a-2)^2-(a-3)^2\\ {}+a(a-2)&=2011 \end{aligned}\text{，}\] 即 \(8a-13=2011\)。因此 \(a=253\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Adding together the equations gives us \[ 2(a^2 + b^2 + c^2) - 2(ab + ac + bc)\]\[ = 14. \]

We can group terms and factor this to get \[ (a - b)^2 + (a - c)^2 + (b - c)^2\]\[ = 14. \]

Note that every term on the left hand side is a nonnegative square integer. The only triple of squares that add to \(14\) is \(9, 4,\) and \(1.\)

We have that \(a - c\) is the biggest difference among the three pairs. Therefore, \(a - c = 3.\)

We cannot discern which of the other terms we can match with the other squares. Let us try \(a - b = 1\) and \(b - c = 2.\)

Plugging in these values into the first equation gives us \[ a^2 - (a - 1)^2 - (a - 3)^2 \] \[ + a(a - 1) = 2011. \] Simplifying yields \(7a = 2021.\) Since \(2021\) is not divisible by \(7,\) we have that \(a - b = 2\) and \(b - c = 1.\)

In the other case, \(b=a-2\) and \(c=a-3.\) The first equation becomes \[\begin{aligned} a^2-(a-2)^2-(a-3)^2\\ {}+a(a-2)&=2011, \end{aligned}\] or \(8a-13=2011.\) Hence \(a=253.\)

Thus, E is the correct answer.

25 答案 D

这个问题可以看作几何概率问题，把区间看成坐标轴上的范围。

不妨先考虑 \[ n \geq x \geq y \geq z \geq 0\text{。}\]

满足这个顺序限制的点 \((x, y, z)\) 构成一个四面体。

这个四面体的高为 \(n\)，底面积为 \(\dfrac{n^2}{2}\)，所以体积为 \[ \dfrac{1}{3} \cdot \dfrac{n^2}{2} \cdot n = \dfrac{n^3}{6}\text{。}\]

现在加入题目中的限制。需要找出满足下面条件的区域：\[ |x - y|, |x - z|, |y - z| \geq 1\text{。}\]

根据已经规定的顺序，这些不等式可化为 \[ x - y \geq 1 \text{ 和 } y - z \geq 1\text{。}\]

这两个限制形成如下图所示的另一个四面体。

注意，在新的四面体中，所有线性尺寸都减少了 \(2\)。因此高为 \(n - 2\)，底面积为 \(\dfrac{(n - 2)^2}{2}\)。

因此体积为 \[ \dfrac{1}{3} \cdot \dfrac{(n - 2)^2}{2} \cdot (n - 2)\] \[ = \dfrac{(n - 2)^3}{6}\text{。}\]

所求概率为 \[ \dfrac{(n - 2)^3}{6} \div \dfrac{n^3}{6} = \dfrac{(n - 2)^3}{n^3}\text{。}\]

逐一检查选项可知，使这个比例大于 \(\dfrac{1}{2}\) 的最小值是 \(10\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

This problem lends itself to geometric probability since we can view the interval as a range on an axis.

WLOG, let \[ n \geq x \geq y \geq z \geq 0. \]

Then we have that the points \((x, y, z)\) which satisfy this restriction form a tetrahedron.

The height of this tetrahedron is \(n,\) and the base has an area of \(\dfrac{n^2}{2}.\) This makes the volume \[ \dfrac{1}{3} \cdot \dfrac{n^2}{2} \cdot n = \dfrac{n^3}{6}. \]

Now we have to apply the restrictions from the problem statement. We need to find the region where \[ |x - y|, |x - z|, |y - z| \geq 1. \]

From our ordering condition that we imposed, these inequalities reduce to \[ x - y \geq 1 \text{ and } y - z \geq 1. \]

These two restrictions form another tetrahedron as shown below.

Note that in the new tetrahedron, all the dimensions have been reduced by \(2.\) This makes the height \(n - 2\) and the base \(\dfrac{(n - 2)^2}{2}.\)

The volume is then \[ \dfrac{1}{3} \cdot \dfrac{(n - 2)^2}{2} \cdot (n - 2)\]\[ = \dfrac{(n - 2)^3}{6}. \]

The desired probability is then \[ \dfrac{(n - 2)^3}{6} \div \dfrac{n^3}{6} = \dfrac{(n - 2)^3}{n^3}. \]

Plugging in all the answer choices, we get that the smallest value such that this fraction is greater than \(\dfrac{1}{2}\) is \(10.\)

Thus, D is the correct answer.
