令 \(n=x-9\)。则 \[ 2^y=n^2-1=(n-1)(n+1)\text{。}\]由于乘积是 \(2\) 的幂，两个因式都不能含有奇素因子。相差 \(2\) 且均为带符号的 \(2\) 的幂的相邻偶数，只有 \((-4,-2)\) 和 \((2,4)\)。因此 \(n=\pm3\) 且 \(y=3\)，给出 \(x=6\) 或 \(12\)。共有 \(2\) 个有序数对 \((x,y)\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Put \(n=x-9.\) Then \[ 2^y=n^2-1=(n-1)(n+1). \] Since the product is a power of \(2,\) both factors must have no odd prime divisor. The only consecutive even integers differing by \(2\) that are both signed powers of \(2\) are \((-4,-2)\) and \((2,4).\) Thus \(n=\pm3\) and \(y=3,\) giving \(x=6\) or \(12.\) There are \(2\) ordered pairs \((x,y).\)

Therefore, the correct answer is C.

37 答案 D

令 \(A=(0,0)\)、\(B=(1,0)\)、\(C=(1,1)\) 且 \(D=(0,1)\)。则 \(E=(t,0)\)，且 \(F=(0,t)\)。由鞋带公式得 \[ \begin{aligned} [CDFE]&=\frac{1+t-t^2}{2}\\ &=\frac58-\frac12 \left(t-\frac12\right)^2 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]其最大值为 \(\frac{5}{8}\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Set \(A=(0,0),\) \(B=(1,0),\) \(C=(1,1),\) and \(D=(0,1).\) Then \(E=(t,0)\) and \(F=(0,t).\) The shoelace formula gives \[ \begin{aligned} [CDFE]&=\frac{1+t-t^2}{2}\\ &=\frac58-\frac12 \left(t-\frac12\right)^2. \end{aligned} \] Its maximum is \(\frac{5}{8}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

38 答案 B

设原来的人口为 \(a^2\)。则 \[ \begin{aligned} b^2-a^2&=99,\\ c^2-a^2&=200\text{。} \end{aligned} \] 由 \((b-a)(b+a)=99\)，\(a\) 的正数可能值为 \(49\)、\(15\) 和 \(1\)。检验第二个条件，只有 \(a=49\) 可行，因为 \[ 49^2+200=2601=51^2\text{。}\]人口为 \(49^2=7^4\)，是 \(7\) 的倍数。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Let the original population be \(a^2.\) Then \[ \begin{aligned} b^2-a^2&=99,\\ c^2-a^2&=200. \end{aligned} \] From \((b-a)(b+a)=99,\) the positive possibilities for \(a\) are \(49,\) \(15,\) and \(1.\) Checking the second condition, only \(a=49\) works, since \[ 49^2+200=2601=51^2. \] The population is \(49^2=7^4,\) a multiple of \(7.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

39 答案 C

以三条中线为边所组成的三角形，其面积为 \[ \frac34\left(3\sqrt{15}\right)=\frac{9\sqrt{15}}4\text{。}\]若 \(\theta\) 是边长 \(3\) 与 \(6\) 的夹角，则 \[ 9\sin\theta=\frac{9\sqrt{15}}4\text{，}\]所以 \(\cos\theta=\pm\frac{1}{4}\)。由余弦定理，第三条中线 \(m\) 满足 \[ m^2=3^2+6^2-2(3)(6)\cos\theta\text{，}\]得到 \(m^2=36\) 或 \(54\)。若 \(m=6\)，则两条中线相等，从而两条边也相等。由于原三角形各边不等，故 \(m=\sqrt{54}=3\sqrt6\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The triangle whose sides are the three medians has area \[ \frac34\left(3\sqrt{15}\right)=\frac{9\sqrt{15}}4. \] If \(\theta\) is the included angle between its sides \(3\) and \(6,\) then \[ 9\sin\theta=\frac{9\sqrt{15}}4, \] so \(\cos\theta=\pm\frac{1}{4}.\) By the law of cosines, the third median \(m\) satisfies \[ m^2=3^2+6^2-2(3)(6)\cos\theta, \] giving \(m^2=36\) or \(54.\) The value \(m=6\) would make two medians, and hence two sides, equal. Because the triangle has unequal sides, \(m=\sqrt{54}=3\sqrt6.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is C.

40 答案 B

当 \(|x|\lt1\) 时， \[ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}nx^n=\frac{x}{(1-x)^2}\text{。}\]取 \(x=\frac{1}{10}\)，得到 \[ \frac{\frac{1}{10}}{(\frac{9}{10})^2}=\frac{10}{81}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

For \(|x|\lt1,\) \[ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}nx^n=\frac{x}{(1-x)^2}. \] Taking \(x=\frac{1}{10}\) gives \[ \frac{\frac{1}{10}}{(\frac{9}{10})^2}=\frac{10}{81}. \]

Thus, the correct answer is B.
