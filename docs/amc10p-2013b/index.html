1

求下式的值： \[\frac{2+4+6}{1+3+5} - \frac{1+3+5}{2+4+6}\]

A\(-1\) B\(\dfrac{5}{36}\) C\(\dfrac{7}{12}\) D\(\dfrac{49}{20}\) E\(\dfrac{43}{3}\)

English

What is the value of the following expression? \[\frac{2+4+6}{1+3+5} - \frac{1+3+5}{2+4+6}\]

(A) \( -1 \)(B) \( \dfrac{5}{36} \)(C) \( \dfrac{7}{12} \)(D) \( \dfrac{49}{20} \)(E) \( \dfrac{43}{3} \)

2

Green 先生通过走过矩形花园的两条边来测量花园，发现它是 \(15\) 步乘 \(20\) 步。Green 先生每一步长 \(2\) 英尺。他预计花园每平方英尺能收获半磅土豆。Green 先生预计能收获多少磅土豆？

A\(600\) B\(800\) C\(1000\) D\(1200\) E\(1400\)

English

Mr. Green measures his rectangular garden by walking two of the sides and finds that it is \(15\) steps by \(20\) steps. Each of Mr. Green’s steps is \(2\) feet long. Mr. Green expects a half a pound of potatoes per square foot from his garden. How many pounds of potatoes does Mr. Green expect from his garden?

(A) \( 600 \)(B) \( 800 \)(C) \( 1000 \)(D) \( 1200 \)(E) \( 1400 \)

3

一月的某一天，内布拉斯加州 Lincoln 的最高气温比最低气温高 \(16\) 度，且最高气温和最低气温的平均值为 \(3\) 度。那天 Lincoln 的最低气温是多少度？

A\(-13\) B\(-8\) C\(-5\) D\(-3\) E\(11\)

English

On a particular January day, the high temperature in Lincoln, Nebraska, was \(16\) degrees higher than the low temperature, and the average of the high and low temperatures was \(3\) degrees. What was the low temperature in Lincoln that day (in degrees)?

(A) \( -13 \)(B) \( -8 \)(C) \( -5 \)(D) \( -3 \)(E) \( 11 \)

4

从 \(3\) 数到 \(201\) 时，\(53\) 位于第 \(51\) 位。从 \(201\) 倒数到 \(3\) 时，\(53\) 位于第 \(n\) 位。求 \(n\)。

A\(146\) B\(147\) C\(148\) D\(149\) E\(150\)

English

When counting from \(3\) to \(201,\) the number \(53\) is in position \(51.\) When counting backward from \(201\) to \(3,\) the number \(53\) is in position \(n.\) What is \(n?\)

(A) \( 146 \)(B) \( 147 \)(C) \( 148 \)(D) \( 149 \)(E) \( 150 \)

5

A\(-20\) B\(-15\) C\(-10\) D\(0\) E\(2\)

English

Positive integers \(a\) and \(b\) are each less than \(6.\) What is the smallest possible value of the following expression? \[2 \cdot a - a \cdot b\]

(A) \( -20 \)(B) \( -15 \)(C) \( -10 \)(D) \( 0 \)(E) \( 2 \)

6

\(33\) 名五年级学生的平均年龄为 \(11\) 岁。他们的 \(55\) 位家长的平均年龄为 \(33\) 岁。所有这些家长和五年级学生的平均年龄是多少？

A\(22\) B\(23.25\) C\(24.75\) D\(26.25\) E\(28\)

English

The average age of \(33\) fifth-graders is \(11.\) The average age of \(55\) of their parents is \(33.\) What is the average age of all of these parents and fifth-graders?

(A) \( 22 \)(B) \( 23.25 \)(C) \( 24.75 \)(D) \( 26.25 \)(E) \( 28 \)

7

半径为 \(1\) 的圆周上有六个等间距点。从中取三个点作为一个既不是等边也不是等腰的三角形的顶点。这个三角形的面积是多少？

English

8

English

(A) \( 10 \)(B) \( 16 \)(C) \( 25 \)(D) \( 30 \)(E) \( 40 \)

10

(A) \( 10 \)(B) \( 15 \)(C) \( 20 \)(D) \( 25 \)(E) \( 30 \)

12

13

(A) \( 2 \)(B) \( 3 \)(C) \( 5 \)(D) \( 6 \)(E) \( 8 \)

15

17

Alex 有 \(75\) 个红色代币和 \(75\) 个蓝色代币。有一个摊位可以用两个红色代币换一个银色代币和一个蓝色代币；另一个摊位可以用三个蓝色代币换一个银色代币和一个红色代币。Alex 一直交换，直到不能再交换为止。最后 Alex 有多少个银色代币？

Alex continues to exchange tokens until no more exchanges are possible. How many silver tokens will Alex have at the end?

(A) \( 62 \)(B) \( 82 \)(C) \( 83 \)(D) \( 102 \)(E) \( 103 \)

(A) \( 1 \)(B) \( 2 \)(C) \( 3 \)(D) \( 4 \)(E) \( 5 \)

21

24

若存在一个正整数 \(m\)，它恰好有四个正因数（包括 \(1\) 和 \(m\)），且这四个因数之和等于正整数 \(n\)，则称 \(n\) 为“好数”。集合 \[\{ 2010,2011,2012,\dotsc,2019 \}\] 中有多少个好数？

25

例如，若 \(N = 749\)，Bernardo 写下 \(10\)，\(\!444\) 和 \(3\)，\(\!245\)，LeRoy 得到 \(S = 13\)，\(\!689\)。有多少个 \(N\) 的选择，使得 \(S\) 的最右两位数字按顺序与 \(2N\) 的最右两位数字相同？

A\(5\) B\(10\) C\(15\) D\(20\) E\(25\)

English

Bernardo chooses a three-digit positive integer \(N\) and writes both its base-\(5\) and base-\(6\) representations on a blackboard. Later LeRoy sees the two numbers Bernardo has written. Treating the two numbers as base-\(10\) integers, he adds them to obtain an integer \(S.\)

(A) \( 5 \)(B) \( 10 \)(C) \( 15 \)(D) \( 20 \)(E) \( 25 \)

2 答案 A

花园尺寸为 \(2\cdot 15 = 30\) 英尺和 \(2\cdot 20=40\) 英尺，面积为 \(30\cdot 40 = 1200\) 平方英尺。

因此，预计收获 \[1200\cdot \dfrac 12 =600\] 磅土豆。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The dimensions of the garden are \( 2\cdot 15 = 30\) feet by \(2\cdot 20=40\) feet. Thus, the square footage is \(30\cdot 40 = 1200.\)

Therefore, there are \[1200\cdot \dfrac 12 =600\] pounds of potatoes.

Thus, the correct answer is A .

3 答案 C

设最低气温为 \(l\)。则最高气温为 \(l+16\)。

平均值满足 \[ \dfrac{l+l+16}2 = l+8 = 3\text{。}\] 因此 \(l = -5\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Let \(l\) represent the low temperature. Then the high temperature is \(l+16.\)

The average satisfies \[ \dfrac{l+l+16}2 = l+8 = 3.\] Therefore, \(l = -5.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

4 答案 D

倒数时，\(201\) 是第一个数，\(200\) 是第二个数；一般地，数 \(x\) 是第 \(202-x\) 个数。

因此 \(53\) 是第 \(202-53=149\) 个数。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

When counting backward, \(201\) is the first number, \(200\) is the second, and in general \(x\) is the \(202-x\)th number.

Thus \(53\) is the \(202-53=149\)th number counted.

Thus, the correct answer is D .

5 答案 B

\(2a-ab=a(2-b)\)。

为了使它最小，取 \(b\) 尽可能大，使 \(2-b\) 最负，同时取 \(a\) 尽可能大。因为 \(a,b<6\)，可取 \(a=b=5\)。

此时 \(5(2-5)=-15\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The expression is \(2a-ab=a(2-b)\).

To make it as small as possible, choose \(b\) as large as possible so that \(2-b\) is most negative, and choose \(a\) as large as possible. Since \(a,b<6\), take \(a=b=5\).

The minimum value is \(5(2-5)=-15\).

Thus, the correct answer is B .

6 答案 C

所有五年级学生和家长的年龄总和为 \[33\cdot 11+55\cdot 33 = 66\cdot 33\text{。}\]

平均年龄等于总年龄除以总人数，所以 \[\dfrac{66\cdot 33}{88} = \dfrac 34 \cdot 33 = 24.75\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The sum of the ages of all the fifth-graders and their parents is: \[33\cdot 11+55\cdot 33 = 66\cdot 33.\]

Then, as the average is the sum divided by the number of people, the average age must be: \[\dfrac{66\cdot 33}{88} = \dfrac 34 \cdot 33 = 24.75 .\]

Thus, the correct answer is C .

7 答案 B

六个等间距点形成正六边形。只有角为 \(30^\circ,60^\circ,90^\circ\) 的三角形既不是等边也不是等腰三角形。

斜边是单位圆的直径，长为 \(2\)。这个 \(30\)-\(60\)-\(90\) 三角形的两条直角边为 \(1\) 和 \(\sqrt3\)。

面积为 \(\frac12\cdot1\cdot\sqrt3=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Six equally spaced points on the circle form a regular hexagon. A triangle using three of them is not equilateral or isosceles only when its angles are \(30^\circ,60^\circ,90^\circ\).

The hypotenuse is a diameter of the unit circle, so it has length \(2\). The legs of the \(30\)-\(60\)-\(90\) triangle are \(1\) and \(\sqrt3\).

The area is \(\frac12\cdot1\cdot\sqrt3=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\).

Thus, the correct answer is B .

给定前三位数字时，如果它们的和不超过 \(9\)，就能确定一个满足条件的数。

设公差为 \(d\)，则 \(c=b-d\)、\(a=b+d\)。

English

25 答案 E

只需在模 \(900=\operatorname{lcm}(25,36,100)\) 下分析，因为 \(5\) 进制、\(6\) 进制和以十为底表示时的末两位都以此为周期。

设 \(5\) 进制末两位为 \(a_1a_0\)，\(6\) 进制末两位为 \(b_1b_0\)。个位条件给出 \(a_0+b_0\equiv2N\equiv2a_0\pmod{10}\)，所以 \(a_0=b_0\)。

写作 \(N=150N_3+30a_1+a_0\)。\(6\) 进制十位条件给出 \(N_3\equiv a_1+b_1\pmod6\)，所以 \(N=900N_4+180a_1\) \(+150b_1+a_0\)。

比较 \(S\) 和 \(2N\) 的十位条件，化简得 \(5a_1\equiv b_1\pmod{10}\)。在 \(0\le a_1\le4\)、\(0\le b_1\le5\) 下，可行对为 \((0,0),(2,0),(4,0),(1,5),(3,5)\)。

又 \(a_0=b_0\) 有 \(5\) 种选择，所以共有 \(5\cdot5=25\) 个 \(N\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

It is enough to work modulo \(900=\operatorname{lcm}(25,36,100)\), because the last two base-\(5\), base-\(6\), and decimal digits repeat with that period.

Let the last two base-\(5\) digits be \(a_1a_0\) and the last two base-\(6\) digits be \(b_1b_0\). The units digit condition gives \(a_0+b_0\equiv2N\equiv2a_0\pmod{10}\), so \(a_0=b_0\).

Writing \(N=150N_3+30a_1+a_0\), the base-\(6\) tens digit condition gives \(N_3\equiv a_1+b_1\pmod6\), so \(N=900N_4+180a_1\) \(+150b_1+a_0\).

The tens digit condition for \(S\) and \(2N\) reduces to \(5a_1\equiv b_1\pmod{10}\). With \(0\le a_1\le4\) and \(0\le b_1\le5\), the valid pairs are \((0,0),(2,0),(4,0),(1,5),(3,5)\).

There are \(5\) choices for \(a_0=b_0\), so there are \(5\cdot5=25\) choices of \(N\).

Thus, the correct answer is E .
