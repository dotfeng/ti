2

A\(\$7\) B\(\$14\) C\(\$19\) D\(\$20\) E\(\$39\)

English

逐题解析

1 答案 C

从分子提出 \(2\)，从分母提出 \(3\) 得到 \[\frac{2(1 - 2 + 3 - 4 + 5 - 6 + 7)}{3(1 - 2 + 3 - 4 + 5 - 6 + 7)}\text{。}\]

相同的括号和可以约去，剩下 \(\dfrac{2}{3}\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Factor \(2\) from the numerator and \(3\) from the denominator: \[\frac{2(1 - 2 + 3 - 4 + 5 - 6 + 7)}{3(1 - 2 + 3 - 4 + 5 - 6 + 7)}.\]

The equal parenthesized sums cancel, leaving \(\dfrac{2}{3}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

2 答案 D

共 \(14\) 天，两粒药丸的每日费用为 \[ \frac{546}{14} = 39\text{。} \]

设一粒绿色药丸价格为 \(g\)。粉色药丸价格为 \(g - 1\)，所以 \[ g + (g - 1) = 39\text{，} \] 解得 \(g = 20\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

Over \(14\) days the daily cost of the two pills is \[ \frac{546}{14} = 39. \]

Let \(g\) be the cost of a green pill. The pink pill costs \(g - 1,\) so \[ g + (g - 1) = 39, \] giving \(g = 20.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

3 答案 A

五个区域的面积分别为 \(4\)、\(6\)、\(15\)、\(20\) 和 \(21\) 平方英尺。

为了使费用最小，应把越贵的花种在越小的区域。最小可能费用为 \[ \begin{aligned} &3(4) + 2.5(6) + 2(15) \\ &\quad {}+ 1.5(20) + 1(21) = 108 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

The five regions have areas \(4,\) \(6,\) \(15,\) \(20,\) and \(21\) square feet.

To minimize the cost, plant the most expensive flowers in the smallest regions. The least possible cost is \[ \begin{aligned} &3(4) + 2.5(6) + 2(15) \\ &\quad {}+ 1.5(20) + 1(21) = 108. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is A.

4 答案 C

因为重叠，每次新增的草带宽度为 \(28 - 4 = 24\) 英寸 \(= 2\) 英尺。所以 Moe 每走一英尺就割 \(2\) 平方英尺，也就是每小时割 \(2 \cdot 5000 = 10000\) 平方英尺。

草坪面积为 \(90 \cdot 150 = 13500\) 平方英尺，所以所需时间为 \[ \frac{13500}{10000} = 1.35 \] 小时。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Because of the overlap, each pass adds a strip \(28 - 4 = 24\) inches \(= 2\) feet wide. So each foot Moe walks mows \(2\) square feet, that is, \(2 \cdot 5000 = 10000\) square feet per hour.

The lawn has area \(90 \cdot 150 = 13500\) square feet, so the time is \[ \frac{13500}{10000} = 1.35 \] hours.

Thus, the correct answer is C.

5 答案 D

边长比为 \(4 : 3\) 的长方形，其高度、水平长度和对角线之比为 \(3 : 4 : 5\)。对角线为 \(27\)，所以水平长度为 \[ \frac{4}{5}(27) = 21.6\text{，} \] 最接近 \(21.5\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

A rectangle with side ratio \(4 : 3\) has height, length, and diagonal in ratio \(3 : 4 : 5.\) With diagonal \(27,\) the horizontal length is \[ \frac{4}{5}(27) = 21.6, \] which is closest to \(21.5.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

6 答案 B

设第一项为 \(a\)，公比为 \(r\)，则 \(ar = 2\)，且 \(ar^3 = 6\)，因此 \(r^2 = 3\)，\(r = \pm\sqrt{3}\)。

第一项为 \[ a = \frac{2}{r} = \pm\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}} = \pm\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}\text{。} \] 选项中出现的是 \(-\dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}\)。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

函数 \(y = |x-a| + |x-b| + |x-c|\) 是分段线性的，斜率依次为 \(-3, -1, 1, 3\)，折点在 \(x = a, b, c\)。直线 \(2x + y = 2003\) 的斜率为 \(-2\)。

English

25 答案 D

一条弦的长度小于半径，当且仅当它所对的弧小于 \(60^\circ\)，因为 \(60^\circ\) 弧对应的弦长正好等于半径。

三条两两弦都短于半径，恰好等价于三个点都位于某个 \(60^\circ\) 弧内。

在任何成功的情形中，恰好有一个点是这样一段包含弧的逆时针端点（概率为零的边界情形除外）。这个端点有 \(3\) 种选法；另外两个点各自独立地以概率 \(\dfrac{1}{6}\) 落在其后的 \(60^\circ\) 弧内。因此所求概率为 \[ 3\left(\frac{1}{6}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{12}\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

A chord has length less than the radius exactly when the arc it subtends is less than \(60^\circ,\) since a chord of a \(60^\circ\) arc equals the radius.

All three pairwise chords are shorter than the radius precisely when the three points all lie within some arc of \(60^\circ.\)

For any successful configuration, exactly one of the three points is the counterclockwise endpoint of such a containing arc (apart from probability-zero boundary cases). Choose that endpoint in \(3\) ways; each of the other two points independently has probability \(\dfrac{1}{6}\) of lying in the next \(60^\circ.\) Hence the probability is \[ 3\left(\frac{1}{6}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{12}. \]

Thus, the correct answer is D.
