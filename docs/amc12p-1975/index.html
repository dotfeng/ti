令 \(w=\cos36^\circ\)、\(y=\cos72^\circ\)。由二倍角公式，\[ y=2w^2-1,\qquad w=1-2y^2\text{。} \] 两式相加得 \[ \begin{aligned} w+y&=2(w^2-y^2)\\ &=2(w-y)(w+y)\text{。} \end{aligned} \] 因为 \(w+y\ne0\)，两边相除得 \(w-y=\frac{1}{2}\)。因此 \(x=\frac{1}{2}\)。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Set \(w=\cos36^\circ\) and \(y=\cos72^\circ.\) The double-angle identities give \[ y=2w^2-1,\qquad w=1-2y^2. \] Adding yields \[ \begin{aligned} w+y&=2(w^2-y^2)\\ &=2(w-y)(w+y). \end{aligned} \] Since \(w+y\ne0,\) division gives \(w-y=\frac{1}{2}.\) Thus \(x=\frac{1}{2}.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is B.
