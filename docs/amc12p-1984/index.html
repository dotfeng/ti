逐题解析

1 答案 C

分解分母得 \[ \begin{aligned} 252^2-248^2 &=(252+248)\\ &\quad{}\cdot(252-248)\\ &=500\cdot4=2000 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]因此原商为 \(\frac{1000^2}{2000}=500\)。所以正确答案是 C。

English

Factoring the denominator gives \[ \begin{aligned} 252^2-248^2 &=(252+248)\\ &\quad{}\cdot(252-248)\\ &=500\cdot4=2000. \end{aligned} \] Thus the given quotient is \(\frac{1000^2}{2000}=500.\) Therefore, the correct answer is C.

2 答案 B

有 \(x-\frac1y=\frac{xy-1}{y}\) 和 \(y-\frac1x=\frac{xy-1}{x}\)。已知条件保证所需各量不为零，所以它们的商为 \[ \frac{\frac{xy-1}{y}}{\frac{xy-1}{x}}=\frac{x}{y}\text{。}\]所以正确答案是 B。

English

We have \(x-\frac1y=\frac{xy-1}{y}\) and \(y-\frac1x=\frac{xy-1}{x}.\) The hypotheses make the needed quantities nonzero, so their quotient is \[ \frac{\frac{xy-1}{y}}{\frac{xy-1}{x}}=\frac{x}{y}. \] Therefore, the correct answer is B.

3 答案 C

\(n\) 的每个素因数都至少为 \(11\)。满足这一性质的最小合数是 \(11\cdot11=121\)，它位于 \(120\lt n\le130\) 内。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Every prime factor of \(n\) is at least \(11.\) The smallest composite with that property is \(11\cdot11=121,\) which lies in \(120\lt n\le130.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is C.

4 答案 B

经过圆心并垂直于平行弦 \(BC\) 和 \(EF\) 的直线平分这两条弦。从长方形左边起测量，\(BC\) 的中点位置为 \(4+\frac52\)，而 \(EF\) 的中点位置为 \(3+\frac{EF}{2}\)。因此 \[ 4+\frac52=3+\frac{EF}{2}\text{，}\]得到 \(EF=7\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The line through the circle’s center perpendicular to the parallel chords \(BC\) and \(EF\) bisects both. Measured from the rectangle’s left side, the midpoint of \(BC\) is \(4+\frac52,\) while the midpoint of \(EF\) is \(3+\frac{EF}{2}.\) Thus \[ 4+\frac52=3+\frac{EF}{2}, \] giving \(EF=7.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is B.

5 答案 D

对两边取正的一百分之一次方，得到 \(n^2\lt5^3=125\)。由于 \(11^2=121\lt125\)，而 \(12^2=144\gt125\)，所以最大可能整数为 \(11\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Taking positive one-hundredth roots gives \(n^2\lt5^3=125.\) Since \(11^2=121\lt125\) but \(12^2=144\gt125,\) the largest possible integer is \(11.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is D.

6 答案 B

因为 \(b=3g\) 且 \(g=9t\)，所以 \(g=\frac{b}{3}\)，且 \(t=\frac{b}{27}\)。因此 \[ b+g+t=b+\frac b3+\frac b{27}=\frac{37}{27}b\text{。}\]所以正确答案是 B。

English

Because \(b=3g\) and \(g=9t,\) we have \(g=\frac{b}{3}\) and \(t=\frac{b}{27}.\) Hence \[ b+g+t=b+\frac b3+\frac b{27}=\frac{37}{27}b. \] Therefore, the correct answer is B.

7 答案 C

路线长为 \(16\cdot90\cdot75\) 厘米。杰克每分钟走 \(100\cdot60\) 厘米，所以所需时间为 \[ \frac{16\cdot90\cdot75}{100\cdot60}=18 \] 分钟。所以正确答案是 C。

English
