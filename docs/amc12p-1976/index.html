\(\mathrm{II}.\) “If the pink elephant on planet alpha does not have purple eyes, then the wild pig on planet beta does not have a long nose.”

Let the internal tangent touch \(O\) and \(O'\) at \(R\) and \(S.\) Let the external tangent through \(P\) touch them at \(X\) and \(Y,\) and let the one through \(Q\) touch them at \(V\) and \(W.\) Equal tangent segments from a point give \[ \begin{gathered} PR=PX,\\ PS=PY,\\ QR=QV,\\ QS=QW. \end{gathered} \] Along the internal tangent, \(PR+QR=PQ\) and \(PS+QS=PQ.\) Adding, \[ \begin{aligned} PR+QR+PS+QS=2PQ. \end{aligned} \] Along the external tangents, \(PX+PY=XY\) and \(QV+QW=VW.\) Therefore \(2PQ=XY+VW.\) The two external common tangent segments have equal length, so \(XY=VW\) and hence \(PQ=XY=VW.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is C.

27 答案 A

设 \(T\) 为第一个分数。它的分子平方为 \[ \begin{aligned} &(\sqrt5+2)+(\sqrt5-2)\\ &\quad+2\sqrt{(\sqrt5+2)(\sqrt5-2)}\\ &=2\sqrt5+2 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]因此 \(T^2=\frac{2\sqrt5+2}{\sqrt5+1}=2\)，且 \(T=\sqrt2\)。另外，\(\sqrt{3-2\sqrt2}=\sqrt2-1\)。所以 \(N=\sqrt2-(\sqrt2-1)=1\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Let \(T\) be the first fraction. Its numerator squared is \[ \begin{aligned} &(\sqrt5+2)+(\sqrt5-2)\\ &\quad+2\sqrt{(\sqrt5+2)(\sqrt5-2)}\\ &=2\sqrt5+2. \end{aligned} \] Thus \(T^2=\frac{2\sqrt5+2}{\sqrt5+1}=2,\) and \(T=\sqrt2.\) Also \(\sqrt{3-2\sqrt2}=\sqrt2-1.\) Hence \(N=\sqrt2-(\sqrt2-1)=1.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is A.

28 答案 B

能被 \(4\) 整除的下标有 \(25\) 个，与 \(1\pmod4\) 同余的下标也有 \(25\) 个。从 \(\binom{100}{2}=4950\) 个交点出发，平行线组不产生交点，因此减去 \(\binom{25}{2}=300\)。共点线组的 \(300\) 对直线只产生一个点，而不是 \(300\) 个不同的点，因此再减去 \(299\)。其余交点都可以选成互不相同，所以最大值为 \[ 4950-300-299=4351\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

There are \(25\) indices divisible by \(4\) and \(25\) congruent to \(1\pmod4.\) Starting from \(\binom{100}{2}=4950,\) the parallel group contributes no intersections, removing \(\binom{25}{2}=300.\) The concurrent group’s \(300\) pairs all give one point rather than \(300\) distinct points, removing another \(299.\) All remaining intersections can be chosen distinct, so the maximum is \[ 4950-300-299=4351. \]

Therefore, the correct answer is B.

29 答案 B

设安和芭芭拉现在的年龄分别为 \(x\) 和 \(y\)，并令她们恒定的年龄差为 \(d=x-y\)。在第一个所述时刻，安的年龄为 \(y\)，所以芭芭拉的年龄为 \(y-d\)。在更早的所述时刻，安的年龄为 \(y-d\)，所以芭芭拉的年龄为 \(y-2d\)。最后一句说明这个年龄等于 \(\frac{x}{2}\)。因此 \[ y-2(x-y)=\frac{x}{2}\text{，}\]从而 \(6y=5x\)。再结合 \(x+y=44\)，得 \(x=24\)、\(y=20\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Let Ann’s and Barbara’s present ages be \(x\) and \(y,\) and let their constant age difference be \(d=x-y.\) At the first referenced time Ann was \(y,\) so Barbara was \(y-d.\) At the earlier referenced time Ann was that age \(y-d,\) so Barbara was \(y-2d.\) The final clause says this last age was \(\frac{x}{2}.\) Hence \[ y-2(x-y)=\frac{x}{2}, \] which gives \(6y=5x.\) Together with \(x+y=44,\) this yields \(x=24\) and \(y=20.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is B.

30 答案 E

令 \(x=2u\)、\(y=v\) 和 \(z=\frac{w}{2}\)。前两个方程都除以 \(2\)，得 \[ \begin{aligned} u+v+w&=6,\\ uv+vw+uw&=11 \end{aligned}\text{，}\]而 \(uvw=6\)。因此 \(u\)、\(v\)、\(w\) 是多项式 \(p(t)=t^3-6t^2+11t-6\) 的三个根。该多项式分解为 \[ p(t)=(t-1)(t-2)(t-3)\text{，}\]所以这三个数是按任意顺序排列的 \(1\)、\(2\)、\(3\)。它们的 \(3!=6\) 种排列产生 \(6\) 个不同的有序三元组 \((x,y,z)\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Set \(x=2u,\) \(y=v,\) and \(z=\frac{w}{2}.\) Dividing the first two equations by \(2\) gives \[ \begin{aligned} u+v+w&=6,\\ uv+vw+uw&=11, \end{aligned} \] while \(uvw=6.\) Thus \(u,\) \(v,\) and \(w\) are the three roots of \(p(t)=t^3-6t^2+11t-6.\) This polynomial factors as \[ p(t)=(t-1)(t-2)(t-3), \] so they are \(1,\) \(2,\) and \(3\) in any order. Their \(3!=6\) permutations produce \(6\) distinct ordered triples \((x,y,z).\)

Therefore, the correct answer is E.
