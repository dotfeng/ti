2 答案 D

五支装盒子最划算，\(\$3\) 可以买五支。

用六美元买两盒，可得 \(5 \cdot 2 = 10\) 支，还剩 \(\$8 - \$6 = \$2\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Recall that a number is divisible by \(9\) if and only if the sum of its digits is also divisible by \(9.\)

This means that looking at \(S(n)\) mod \(9\) would also give us \(n\) mod \(9.\)

Let us prove this. If we add \(x\) to \(n\) without carrying, it is clear that the sum of the digits increases by \(x\) and that \(n\) itself increases by \(x.\)

This would increase both their values mod \(9\) by \(x.\)

Now, if it does carry, we would be subtracting \(10\) from some digit and adding on \(1\) to the next digit.

This would keep the value mod \(9\) constant. We did, however, add \(x\) in there, so the value mod \(9\) still increased by \(x.\)

These are the only two cases, and in both we have shown that the value mod \(9\) for both \(n\) and \(S(n)\) increased by \(x.\)

Therefore, we have that \[ n \equiv S(n) \equiv 5 \pmod 9. \]

From this, we can see that \[ \begin{aligned} n + 1 &\equiv 6 \\ &\equiv S(n + 1) \pmod 9. \end{aligned} \]

The only answer choice that leaves a remainder of \(6\) when divided by \(9\) is \(1239.\)

This value is attainable. Take \(n\) to consist of \(137\) digits \(9,\) followed by a digit \(5,\) followed by four more digits \(9.\) Then \[\begin{aligned}S(n)&=137\cdot9+5+4\cdot9\\&=1274.\end{aligned}\] Adding \(1\) changes the final four \(9\)s to \(0\)s and changes the \(5\) to \(6,\) so \(S(n+1)=137\cdot9+6=1239.\)

Thus, D is the correct answer.

22 答案 E

设圆的半径为 \(r\)。

要求三角形在圆外的面积，可以先求三角形在圆内的面积，再从三角形总面积中减去。

因为 \(\angle ABO\) 和 \(\angle ACO\) 都是直角，所以 \(\angle BOC = 120^{\circ}\)。

因此扇形 \(OBC\) 的面积为 \[ \dfrac{120}{360} \cdot \pi r^2 = \dfrac{\pi r^2}{3}\text{。}\]

接下来求 \(\triangle BOC\) 的面积。利用三角形面积的正弦公式，可得 \[ \dfrac{1}{2} \sin (120^{\circ}) \cdot r^2 = \dfrac{r^2\sqrt{3}}{4}\text{。}\]

所以三角形在圆内的面积为 \[ \dfrac{\pi r^2}{3} - \dfrac{r^2\sqrt{3}}{4} = \dfrac{r^2(4\pi - 3\sqrt{3})}{12}\text{。}\]

\(\triangle ABC\) 的面积为 \[ \dfrac{(r\sqrt{3})^2\sqrt{3}}{4} = \dfrac{3r^2\sqrt{3}}{4}\text{。}\]

三角形位于圆内的面积占比为 \[\dfrac{r^2(4\pi-3\sqrt3)}{12}\cdot \dfrac{4}{3r^2\sqrt3}\text{，}\]化简得 \[\dfrac{4\pi-3\sqrt3}{9\sqrt3}\text{。}\]

因此所求占比为 \[\begin{aligned} &1-\dfrac{4\pi-3\sqrt3}{9\sqrt3}\\ &=1-\dfrac{4\pi\sqrt3}{27}+\dfrac13\\ &=\dfrac43-\dfrac{4\pi\sqrt3}{27} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Let the radius of the circle be \(r.\)

To find the area of the triangle outside of the circle, we can find the area of the triangle inside the circle and subtract it.

We get that \(\angle BOC = 120^{\circ}\) since \(\angle ABO\) and \(\angle ACO\) are right angles.

This means that the area of sector \(OBC\) is \[ \dfrac{120}{360} \cdot \pi r^2 = \dfrac{\pi r^2}{3}. \]

Now, we need to find the area of \(\triangle BOC.\) Using the formula for the area of a triangle with sine, we get the area to be \[ \dfrac{1}{2} \sin (120^{\circ}) \cdot r^2 = \dfrac{r^2\sqrt{3}}{4}. \]

Then the area of the triangle inside the circle is \[ \dfrac{\pi r^2}{3} - \dfrac{r^2\sqrt{3}}{4} = \dfrac{r^2(4\pi - 3\sqrt{3})}{12}. \]

The area of \(\triangle ABC\) is \[ \dfrac{(r\sqrt{3})^2\sqrt{3}}{4} = \dfrac{3r^2\sqrt{3}}{4}. \]

The fraction of the triangle lying inside the circle is \[\dfrac{r^2(4\pi-3\sqrt3)}{12}\cdot \dfrac{4}{3r^2\sqrt3},\] which simplifies to \[\dfrac{4\pi-3\sqrt3}{9\sqrt3}.\]

Hence the desired fraction is \[\begin{aligned} &1-\dfrac{4\pi-3\sqrt3}{9\sqrt3}\\ &=1-\dfrac{4\pi\sqrt3}{27}+\dfrac13\\ &=\dfrac43-\dfrac{4\pi\sqrt3}{27}. \end{aligned}\]

Thus, E is the correct answer.

23 答案 B

可以使用补集计数：先求三角形总数，再减去不能形成三角形的选法。

共有 \(5^2 = 25\) 个格点，因此任取三点有 \(\binom{25}{3} = 2300\) 种可能的三角形。

注意，不能形成三角形的唯一情形是所有 \(3\) 个点共线。

有 \(5\) 行、\(5\) 列和 \(2\) 条长对角线。这样的 \(12\) 条直线各有 \(5\) 个点，所以它们贡献 \[ 12 \cdot \binom{5}{3} = 12 \cdot 10 = 120 \] 个退化三角形。

另外还有含 \(4\) 个点的对角线，例如从 \((1,2)\) 到 \((4,5)\)。这样的直线有 \(4\) 条，所以它们贡献 \[ 4 \cdot \binom{4}{3} = 4 \cdot 4 = 16 \] 个退化三角形。

类似地，还有 \(4\) 条含 \(3\) 个点的对角线。它们额外给出 \(4 \cdot 1 = 4\) 个不能形成三角形的选法。

现在还要看斜率为 \(\dfrac{1}{2}, 2, -\dfrac{1}{2}\) 和 \(-2\) 的直线。

每种斜率有 \(3\) 条这样的直线，并且每条都有 \(3\) 个点。因此还要扣除 \[ 4 \cdot 3 \cdot 1 = 12 \] 个退化三角形。

面积为正的三角形总数为 \[ 2300 - 120 - 16 - 4 - 12 \] \[= 2148\text{。}\]所以正确答案是 B。

English

We can use complementary counting to find the total number of triangles and subtract out the ones that don’t work.

There are a total of \(5^2 = 25\) points, so there are \(\binom{25}{3} = 2300\) possible triangles.

Note that the only way a triangle doesn’t work is if all the \(3\) points are in a straight line.

There are \(5\) rows, \(5\) columns, and \(2\) long diagonals. Each of these \(12\) lines have \(5\) points, which means they contribute \[ 12 \cdot \binom{5}{3} = 12 \cdot 10 = 120 \] degenerate triangles.

There are also the diagonal lines with \(4\) points, such as the line from \((1,2)\) to \((4,5).\) There are \(4\) of these lines, so they have \[ 4 \cdot \binom{4}{3} = 4 \cdot 4 = 16 \] degenerate triangles.

Similarly, there are \(4\) diagonal lines with \(3\) points. These give us \(4 \cdot 1 = 4\) extra triangles that don’t work.

Now, we have to look at the lines with slopes of \(\dfrac{1}{2}, 2, -\dfrac{1}{2},\) and \(-2.\)

There are \(3\) such lines for each slope, and they all have \(3\) points on them. Therefore, they contribute \[ 4 \cdot 3 \cdot 1 = 12 \] more triangles to discount.

The total number of working triangles is then \[ 2300 - 120 - 16 - 4 - 12 \]\[= 2148. \] Thus, B is the correct answer.

The coefficient of \(x\) in this product is \(10-r,\) so \(10-r=100\) and \(r=-90.\) The coefficient of \(x^3\) is \(a-r,\) so \(a-r=1\) and \(a=-89.\)

25 答案 A

可以分析所有 \(11\) 的倍数，并看它们各自贡献多少排列。我们可以按数字中不同数字的个数分类。

情形 \(1\) 三个数字全相同

这不可能。由 \(11\) 的整除规则可知，首位与末位之和减去中间位必须能被 \(11\) 整除。

若三个数字全相同，上述表达式就等于那个数字，不可能被 \(11\) 整除。

情形 \(2\) 两个数字相同

可以把它分为含有数字 \(0\) 的数和不含数字零的数。

不含数字 \(0\) 的 \(11\) 的倍数有 \(8\) 个： \[ 121, 242, 363, 484, 616, 737, 858 \] 以及 \(979\)。

这些数各贡献 \(3\) 个排列，所以这一情形有 \(8 \cdot 3 = 24\) 个数。

含数字 \(0\) 的 \(11\) 的倍数有 \(9\) 个： \[ 110, 220, 330, 440, 550, 660, 770 \] \(880\) 和 \(990\)。

对这些数，\(0\) 不能作为百位，所以每个只贡献 \(2\) 个排列，总共 \(9 \cdot 2 = 18\)。

情形 \(3\) 三个数字都不同

\(100\) 到 \(999\) 之间共有 \(81\) 个 \(11\) 的倍数。三位都不同的个数为 \[ 81 - 8 - 9 = 64\text{。}\]与情形 \(2\) 一样，还要特别处理含数字 \(0\) 的数。这样的数有 \(8\) 个： \[ 209, 308, 407, 506, 605, 704, 803 \] 以及 \(902\)。

每个这样的数给出 \(2 \cdot 2 = 4\) 个排列，但首末位交换会得到集合中已有的另一个数，所以要除以 \(2\)。

因此这些数总共提供 \[ 8 \cdot 4 \div 2 = 16 \] 个不同的排列。

现在还剩 \(64 - 8 = 56\) 个需要计入的 \(11\) 的倍数。

每个这样的数有 \(3! = 6\) 个排列。不过和上面一样，任意一个数首末位交换后仍会得到这个集合中的另一个数。

这可以用 \(11\) 的整除规则看出。若 \(ABC\) 能被 \(11\) 整除，则 \(A + C - B\) 能被 \(11\) 整除。

这意味着 \(C + A - B\) 能被 \(11\) 整除，也就意味着 \(CBA\) 也能被 \(11\) 整除。

因此这些数还贡献 \[ 56 \cdot 6 \div 2 = 168 \] 个排列。

所有情形合计共有 \[ 24 + 18 + 16 + 168 = 226 \] 个数。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

We can analyze all the multiples of \(11\) and see how many permutations each of them contribute. We can do this by casing on the number of unique digits in the number.

Case \(1:\) all the digits are the same

This cannot happen. We can see this by the divisibility rule for \(11,\) which says that the sum of the first and last digit minus the middle digit must be divisible by \(11.\)

If all the digits are the same, then the above expression evaluates to that digit, which cannot be divisible by \(11.\)

Case \(2:\) two of the digits are the same

We can split this up into the numbers that have the digit \(0\) and those that don’t.

There are \(8\) multiples of \(11\) that do not have the digit \(0:\) \[ 121, 242, 363, 484, 616, 737, 858, \] and \(979.\)

Each of these numbers contributes \(3\) permutations, so this scenario has \(8 \cdot 3 = 24\) numbers.

There are \(9\) multiples of \(11\) that have the digit \(0:\)\[ 110, 220, 330, 440, 550, 660, 770, \] \(880,\) and \(990.\)

For these numbers, \(0\) cannot be the hundreds digit, so each of them only contributes \(2\) permutations, for a total of \(9 \cdot 2 = 18.\)

Case \(3:\) all the digits are different

There are a total of \(81\) multiples of \(11\) between \(100\) and \(999.\) The number of these with all different digits is \[ 81 - 8 - 9 = 64. \] As in case \(2,\) we have to specially account for the numbers with \(0\) as a digit. There are \(8:\) \[ 209, 308, 407, 506, 605, 704, 803, \] and \(902.\)

Each of these gives us \(2 \cdot 2 = 4\) permutations, but we overcount by a factor of \(2\) since flipping the first and last digits creates another number already in the set.

Therefore, these numbers provide a total of \[ 8 \cdot 4 \div 2 = 16 \] unique permutations.

There are now \(64 - 8 = 56\) multiples of \(11\) that we need to account for.

We know that each of these provides \(3! = 6\) permutations. As above, however, note that flipping the first and last digit of any number in this set produces another number in this set.

We can see this by using the divisibility rule for \(11.\) If \(ABC\) is divisible by \(11,\) then we have that \(A + C - B\) is divisible by \(11.\)

This means that \(C + A - B\) is divisible by \(11,\) which means that \(CBA\) is also divisible by \(11.\)

Therefore, these numbers contribute \[ 56 \cdot 6 \div 2 = 168 \] more permutations.

Over all the cases, we have a total of \[ 24 + 18 + 16 + 168 = 226 \] numbers.

Thus, A is the correct answer.
