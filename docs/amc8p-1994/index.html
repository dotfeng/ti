1 答案 D

通分到分母 \(24\)，这些分数分别是 \(\dfrac{8}{24}\)、\(\dfrac{6}{24}\)、\(\dfrac{9}{24}\)、\(\dfrac{10}{24}\) 和 \(\dfrac{7}{24}\)。

最大的分子是 \(10\)，所以 \(\dfrac{5}{12} = \dfrac{10}{24}\) 最大。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Over the common denominator \(24\), the fractions are \(\dfrac{8}{24},\) \(\dfrac{6}{24},\) \(\dfrac{9}{24},\) \(\dfrac{10}{24},\) and \(\dfrac{7}{24}.\)

The largest numerator is \(10\), so \(\dfrac{5}{12} = \dfrac{10}{24}\) is the largest.

Thus, the correct answer is D .

2 答案 D

分子之和为 \(1 + 2 + \cdots + 9 + 55\) \(= 45 + 55\) \(= 100\)。

所以总和是 \(\dfrac{100}{10} = 10\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The numerators sum to \(1 + 2 + \cdots + 9 + 55\) \(= 45 + 55\) \(= 100.\)

So the total is \(\dfrac{100}{10} = 10.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D .

3 答案 C

上午 \(7{:}25\) 之后八小时是下午 \(3{:}25\)。

再加上 \(45\) 分钟午餐时间，结束时间为下午 \(4{:}10\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

4 答案 B

顺时针旋转 \(120°\) 后，每个图形移动到顺时针方向的下一个位置：上方的三角形移到右下，右下的正方形移到左下，左下的圆移到上方。

因此结果应为圆在上方，正方形在左下，三角形在右下，且每个图形形状保持不变。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

A clockwise turn of \(120°\) sends each shape to the next position clockwise: the triangle at the top moves to the lower right, the square at the lower right moves to the lower left, and the circle at the lower left moves to the top.

The result therefore has a circle on top, a square at the lower left, and a triangle at the lower right, with each shape keeping its original form.

Thus, the correct answer is B .

6 答案 A

任意六个连续正整数中至少包含一个 \(5\) 的倍数和一个偶数，所以它们的乘积是 \(5 \times 2 = 10\) 的倍数。

\(10\) 的倍数个位数字总是 \(0\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Any six consecutive whole numbers include at least one multiple of \(5\) and at least one even number, so their product is a multiple of \(5 \times 2 = 10.\)

A multiple of \(10\) always ends in \(0\).

Thus, the correct answer is A .

7 答案 B

在三角形 \(ABE\) 中，\(\angle ABE = 180°\) \(- (60° + 40°)\) \(= 80°\)。

因为 \(A, B, C\) 共线且 \(D\) 在 \(EB\) 上，所以 \(\angle DBC = 180° - 80° = 100°\)。

在三角形 \(BDC\) 中，\(\angle BDC = 180°\) \(- (100° + 30°)\) \(= 50°\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

In triangle \(ABE,\) \(\angle ABE = 180°\) \(- (60° + 40°)\) \(= 80°.\)

Since \(A, B, C\) are collinear and \(D\) lies on segment \(EB,\) the angle \(\angle DBC = 180° - 80° = 100°.\)

In triangle \(BDC,\) \(\angle BDC = 180°\) \(- (100° + 30°)\) \(= 50°.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B .

8 答案 C

数位和为 \(25\)，而最大可能和是 \(27\)，所以至少有一个数字是 \(9\)。可能的数字组是 \(\{9, 9, 7\}\) 和 \(\{9, 8, 8\}\)。

每组都有 \(3\) 种不同排列：\(997\)、\(979\)、\(799\) 以及 \(988\)、\(898\)、\(889\)，共 \(6\) 个数。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Since the digits sum to \(25\) and the maximum is \(27\), at least one digit is \(9\). The possible digit sets are \(\{9, 9, 7\}\) and \(\{9, 8, 8\}.\)

Each set has \(3\) distinct arrangements (\(997,\) \(979,\) \(799\) and \(988,\) \(898,\) \(889\)), giving \(6\) numbers in all.

Thus, the correct answer is C .

9 答案 A

加上 \(8\%\) 的税后，总价为 \(1.08 \times \$75 = \$81.00\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The \(20\%\) discount lowers the price to \(\$80,\) and the \(\$5\) coupon reduces it to \(\$75.\)

Adding \(8\%\) tax gives \(1.08 \times \$75 = \$81.00.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A .

10 答案 A

表达式为整数时，\(N+2\) 必须整除 \(36\)。\(36\) 的正因数是 \(1\)、\(2\)、\(3\)、\(4\)、\(6\)、\(9\)、\(12\)、\(18\)、\(36\)。

因为 \(N \gt 0\)，所以 \(N + 2 \gt 2\)，可用的因数是 \(3\)、\(4\)、\(6\)、\(9\)、\(12\)、\(18\)、\(36\)，共有 \(7\) 个值。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The expression is an integer when \(N+2\) divides \(36.\) The divisors of \(36\) are \(1,\) \(2,\) \(3,\) \(4,\) \(6,\) \(9,\) \(12,\) \(18,\) \(36.\)

Because \(N \gt 0,\) we need \(N + 2 \gt 2,\) leaving the divisors \(3,\) \(4,\) \(6,\) \(9,\) \(12,\) \(18,\) \(36\): that is \(7\) values.

Thus, the correct answer is A .

11 答案 B

共有 \(48\) 名女生，其中 \(20\) 名来自 Jones，所以来自 Clay 的女生有 \(48 - 20 = 28\) 名。

Clay 总共有 \(60\) 名学生，所以来自 Clay 的男生人数是 \(60 - 28 = 32\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Since \(48\) girls attended and \(20\) were from Jones, \(48 - 20 = 28\) girls were from Clay.

Clay had \(60\) students total, so the number of Clay boys is \(60 - 28 = 32.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B .

12 答案 A

在正方形 \(II\) 中，阴影占 \(1\) 个小格，总共有 \(4\) 个相等小格，所以阴影面积是 \(\tfrac14\)。

正方形 \(I\) 可分成 \(8\) 个相等三角形，其中 \(2\) 个涂色；正方形 \(III\) 可分成 \(16\) 个相等三角形，其中 \(4\) 个涂色。这些比例都是 \(\tfrac{2}{8} = \tfrac{4}{16} = \tfrac14\)。

因为三个图形的阴影面积都占 \(\tfrac14\)，所以阴影面积全都相等。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

In square \(II,\) \(1\) of the \(4\) equal cells is shaded, so \(\tfrac14\) of it is shaded.

Square \(I\) breaks into \(8\) equal triangles with \(2\) shaded, and square \(III\) breaks into \(16\) equal triangles with \(4\) shaded; each of these equals \(\tfrac{2}{8} = \tfrac{4}{16} = \tfrac14.\)

Since every figure has exactly \(\tfrac14\) shaded, the shaded areas are all equal.

Thus, the correct answer is A .

13 答案 C

两个数正中间的数是它们的平均数：\(\dfrac{\frac16 + \frac14}{2}\)。

因为 \(\dfrac16 + \dfrac14 = \dfrac{2}{12} + \dfrac{3}{12} = \dfrac{5}{12}\)，所以平均数是 \(\dfrac{5}{12} \div 2 = \dfrac{5}{24}\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The number halfway between two values is their average: \(\dfrac{\frac16 + \frac14}{2}.\)

Since \(\dfrac16 + \dfrac14 = \dfrac{2}{12} + \dfrac{3}{12} = \dfrac{5}{12},\) the average is \(\dfrac{5}{12} \div 2 = \dfrac{5}{24}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C .

14 答案 E

任意时刻都有两个孩子在玩，共 \(90\) 分钟，所以总游戏时间是 \(2 \times 90 = 180\) 个孩子分钟。

平均分给 \(5\) 个孩子，每个孩子玩 \(\dfrac{180}{5} = 36\) 分钟。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Two children play at every moment for \(90\) minutes, so the total playing time is \(2 \times 90 = 180\) child-minutes.

Split equally among \(5\) children, each plays \(\dfrac{180}{5} = 36\) minutes.

Thus, the correct answer is E .

15 答案 A

这个模式每 \(4\) 个点重复一次，所以从某点出发的箭头只取决于它除以 \(4\) 的余数。

因为 \(425 = 4(106) + 1\)，且 \(427 = 4(106) + 3\)，所以从 \(425\) 到 \(427\) 的路径与从点 \(1\) 到点 \(3\) 相同：从 \(1\) 向上到 \(2\)，再向右到 \(3\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The pattern repeats every \(4\) points, so the arrows leaving a point depend only on its remainder upon division by \(4.\)

Because \(425 = 4(106) + 1\) and \(427 = 4(106) + 3,\) the path from \(425\) to \(427\) looks just like the path from point \(1\) to point \(3\): from \(1\) it goes up to \(2,\) then right to \(3.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A .

16 答案 E

正方形的周长与边长成正比，所以较大正方形的边长是较小正方形的 \(3\) 倍。

面积等于边长的平方，所以较大面积是较小面积的 \(3^2 = 9\) 倍。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

A square’s perimeter is proportional to its side, so the larger square has side length \(3\) times the smaller one.

Area is the side squared, so the larger area is \(3^2 = 9\) times the smaller.

Thus, the correct answer is E .

17 答案 D

雪的体积是 \(4 \times 10 \times 3 = 120\) 立方码。

累计铲雪量是 \(20 + 19 + 18 + 17\) \(+ 16 + 15 + 14 = 119\) 立方码，也就是 \(7\) 小时的工作量。第八小时的铲雪速度是每小时 \(13\) 立方码，因此剩下的一立方码需要 \(\dfrac{1}{13}\) 小时。准确时间是 \(7\dfrac{1}{13}\) 小时，最接近 \(7\) 小时。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The volume of snow is \(4 \times 10 \times 3 = 120\) cubic yards.

The amounts shoveled add up to \(20 + 19 + 18 + 17\) \(+ 16 + 15 + 14 = 119\) after \(7\) hours. In the eighth hour her rate is \(13\) cubic yards per hour, so the last cubic yard takes \(\dfrac{1}{13}\) hour. The exact time is therefore \(7\dfrac{1}{13}\) hours, which is closest to \(7\).

Thus, the correct answer is D .

18 答案 B

去程中 Mike 先慢速行驶，对应平缓上升；再快速行驶，对应较陡上升。购物一小时期间离家距离不变，对应水平线段。

回程中他先快速行驶，对应较陡下降；再慢速行驶，对应较平缓下降。只有先慢后快上升、顶部水平、再先快后慢下降的图符合。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

On the way out Mike drives slowly (a shallow slope) then rapidly (a steep slope), so the distance rises slowly and then more steeply. During the hour at the mall the distance stays the same, a flat segment.

On the return he drives rapidly (steep) then slowly (shallow), so the distance falls steeply and then more gently. Only the graph with a slow-then-fast rise, a flat top, and a fast-then-slow fall fits.

Thus, the correct answer is B .

19 答案 E

每个半圆建在长度为 \(4\) 的边上，所以半径为 \(2\)。每个半圆从原正方形的一边向外突出一个半径。

\(ABCD\) 的每条边等于原边长加两个半径：\(4 + 2(2) = 8\)。所以 \(ABCD\) 的面积是 \(8^2 = 64\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Each semicircle is built on a side of length \(4,\) so its radius is \(2.\) A semicircle bulges out from the middle of each side by that radius.

Each side of \(ABCD\) is the original side plus two radii: \(4 + 2(2) = 8.\) So the area of \(ABCD\) is \(8^2 = 64.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E .

20 答案 D

小分子和大分母会产生较小分数，所以用 \(1\) 和 \(2\) 作分子，用 \(8\) 和 \(9\) 作分母。

把较大的分子配给较大的分母，得到 \(\dfrac18 + \dfrac29 = \dfrac{9 + 16}{72} = \dfrac{25}{72}\)，它小于 \(\dfrac19 + \dfrac28 = \dfrac{26}{72}\)。所以最小和是 \(\dfrac{25}{72}\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Small numerators and large denominators make small fractions, so use \(1\) and \(2\) as numerators and \(8\) and \(9\) as denominators.

Pairing the larger numerator with the larger denominator gives \(\dfrac18 + \dfrac29 = \dfrac{9 + 16}{72} = \dfrac{25}{72},\) which is smaller than \(\dfrac19 + \dfrac28 = \dfrac{26}{72}.\) So the minimum sum is \(\dfrac{25}{72}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D .

21 答案 C

最坏情况下，一个人可以先抽到 \(3\) 个红球、\(3\) 个白球和 \(3\) 个蓝球，共 \(9\) 个球，还没有四个同色。

第十个球必须与其中某一种颜色相同，从而得到四个同色。因此需要 \(10\) 个球。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

In the worst case, a person could draw \(3\) red, \(3\) white, and \(3\) blue, which is \(9\) gumballs, without yet having four of any color.

The next (tenth) gumball must match one of these colors, giving four of that color. So \(10\) gumballs are needed.

Thus, the correct answer is C .

22 答案 D

第一个转盘中，\(P(1) = \dfrac14\)，\(P(2) = \dfrac14\)，\(P(3) = \dfrac12\)。第二个转盘中，\(4\)、\(5\)、\(6\) 各自的概率为 \(\dfrac13\)。

和为偶数需要两个数同奇偶。两数都为奇数时，第一个数是 \(1\) 或 \(3\)，概率为 \(\left(\dfrac34\right)\)；第二个数是 \(5\)，概率为 \(\left(\dfrac13\right)\)，所以概率为 \(\dfrac34 \cdot \dfrac13 = \dfrac14\)。两数都为偶数时，第一个数是 \(2\)，概率为 \(\left(\dfrac14\right)\)；第二个数是 \(4\) 或 \(6\)，概率为 \(\left(\dfrac23\right)\)，所以概率为 \(\dfrac14 \cdot \dfrac23 = \dfrac16\)。

总概率是 \(\dfrac14 + \dfrac16 = \dfrac{5}{12}\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

On the first wheel, \(P(1) = \dfrac14,\) \(P(2) = \dfrac14,\) and \(P(3) = \dfrac12.\) On the second wheel, each of \(4,\) \(5,\) \(6\) has probability \(\dfrac13.\)

The sum is even when both numbers are odd or both are even. Both odd: the first is \(1\) or \(3\) \(\left(\dfrac34\right)\) and the second is \(5\) \(\left(\dfrac13\right),\) giving \(\dfrac34 \cdot \dfrac13 = \dfrac14.\) Both even: the first is \(2\) \(\left(\dfrac14\right)\) and the second is \(4\) or \(6\) \(\left(\dfrac23\right),\) giving \(\dfrac14 \cdot \dfrac23 = \dfrac16.\)

The total probability is \(\dfrac14 + \dfrac16 = \dfrac{5}{12}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D .

23 答案 D

和的百位来自 \(X\) 加上可能的进位，所以若 \(X = 9\)，和会变成四位数。允许的最大值是 \(X = 8\)。

为了使和最大，取 \(Y = 9\)：此时 \(888 + 98 + 8 = 994\)。它的数字是 \(9\)、\(9\)、\(4\)；因为 \(9 = Y\)，而 \(4\) 是新数字 \(Z\)，所以和的形式是 \(YYZ\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The hundreds digit of the sum comes from \(X\) plus any carry, so if \(X = 9\) the sum would spill over into four digits. The largest allowed value is \(X = 8.\)

To make the sum as large as possible, take \(Y = 9:\) then \(888 + 98 + 8 = 994.\) Its digits are \(9,\) \(9,\) \(4;\) since \(9 = Y\) and \(4\) is a new digit \(Z,\) the sum has the form \(YYZ.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D .

24 答案 B

条件表示绿色方格不能在上方或右方紧邻红色方格，因此任何绿色方格都会迫使它上方和右方的方格也为绿色。绿色方格必须向右上角聚集。

合法涂法为：全红；只有右上角为绿；整行上排为绿；整列右排为绿；除左下角外全绿；全绿。共 \(6\) 种。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The rule says a green square cannot have a red square on its top or right side, so any green square forces the squares above and to its right to be green as well. The green squares must therefore cluster toward the top-right corner.

The valid colorings are: all four red; only the top-right green; the whole top row green; the whole right column green; all green except the bottom-left; and all four green. That is \(6\) colorings.

Thus, the correct answer is B .

25 答案 A

小例子显示规律：\(99 \times 44 = 4356\) 和 \(999 \times 444 = 443556\)。一般地，由 \(n\) 个九组成的数乘以由 \(n\) 个四组成的数，结果为 \((n-1)\) 个四，然后一个 \(3\)，再 \((n-1)\) 个五，最后一个 \(6\)。

当 \(n = 94\) 时，乘积含有 \(93\) 个四、一个 \(3\)、\(93\) 个五和一个 \(6\)。数字和为 \(93(4) + 3\) \(+ 93(5) + 6\) \(= 93(9) + 9\) \(= 94(9)\) \(= 846\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Small cases show the pattern: \(99 \times 44 = 4356\) and \(999 \times 444 = 443556.\) In general, a string of \(n\) nines times a string of \(n\) fours gives \((n-1)\) fours, then a \(3,\) then \((n-1)\) fives, then a \(6.\)

For \(n = 94,\) the product has \(93\) fours, one \(3,\) \(93\) fives, and one \(6.\) The digit sum is \(93(4) + 3\) \(+ 93(5) + 6\) \(= 93(9) + 9\) \(= 94(9)\) \(= 846.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A .
