1 答案 D

因为 \(3\pi \approx 9.42\)，不等式 \(|x| \lt 3\pi\) 表示 \(-9.42 \lt x \lt 9.42\)。

English

Since \(3\pi \approx 9.42,\) the inequality \(|x| \lt 3\pi\) means \(-9.42 \lt x \lt 9.42.\)

The integers in this range run from \(-9\) to \(9,\) giving \(19\) values.

Thus, the correct answer is D.

2 答案 B

\(23\) 对全蓝组合用了 \(46\) 名蓝衬衫学生，还剩 \(57 - 46 = 11\) 名蓝衬衫学生。

这 \(11\) 名蓝衬衫学生每人都必须和一名黄衬衫学生配对，所以有 \(11\) 对混合组合，用掉 \(11\) 名黄衬衫学生。

剩下的 \(75 - 11 = 64\) 名黄衬衫学生组成 \(64 \div 2 = 32\) 对全黄组合。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The \(23\) all-blue pairs account for \(46\) blue students, leaving \(57 - 46 = 11\) blue students.

Each of those \(11\) blue students must be paired with a yellow student, so there are \(11\) mixed pairs, using \(11\) yellow students.

The remaining \(75 - 11 = 64\) yellow students form \(64 \div 2 = 32\) all-yellow pairs.

Having \(8\) outcomes for a sum of \(10\) requires \(a\ge8\) and \(b\ge9,\) so necessarily \(a+b\ge17.\) For \((a,b)=(8,9),\) a sum of \(10\) has \(8\) outcomes, while a sum of \(12\) has the \(6\) outcomes with the first die showing \(3,4,\ldots,8.\) Since \(\frac{6}{8\cdot9}=\frac{1}{12},\) both conditions hold and the lower bound \(17\) is attained.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

20 答案 A

因为 \(z^3\equiv 1\pmod{z^2+z+1}\) 且 \(2021=3\cdot 673+2\)，所以 \(z^{2021}\equiv z^2\)。

因此 \(z^{2021}+1\equiv z^2+1\)。再用 \(z^2\equiv -z-1\)，化简，得到 \(-z-1+1=-z\)。

所以 \(R(z)=-z\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Since \(z^3\equiv 1\pmod{z^2+z+1}\) and \(2021=3\cdot 673+2,\) we have \(z^{2021}\equiv z^2.\)

So \(z^{2021}+1\equiv z^2+1.\) Reducing further with \(z^2\equiv -z-1,\) this is \(-z-1+1=-z.\)

Therefore \(R(z)=-z.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

21 答案 D

两边取 \(\log_2\)，方程变为 \(2^{\sqrt2}\log_2 x=2^{x-1}\)。代入 \(x=\sqrt2\) 得 \(2^{\sqrt2}\cdot\tfrac12=2^{\sqrt2-1}\) 成立，所以 \(x=\sqrt2\) 是一个解。

令 \(f(x)=2^{x-1}-2^{\sqrt2}\log_2 x\)。则 \(f(1)\gt 0\)、\(f(\sqrt2)=0\)、\(f(2)\lt 0\)，且 \(f(4)\gt 0\)，所以在 \(2\) 与 \(4\) 之间有第二个根 \(x_0\)。

为证明没有其他解，将方程除以 \(2^x\)，并在 \(x\gt1\) 时考察 \(h(x)=\frac{\ln x}{2^x}\)。它的导数符号与 \(\frac{1}{x}-(\ln2)(\ln x)\) 相同，而后者严格递减。因此，\(h\) 先增后减一次，任意水平线与其图像至多相交两次。前面找到的两个根就是全部解。因为 \(2\lt x_0\lt4\)，所以 \(2\lt S=\sqrt2+x_0\lt6\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Taking \(\log_2,\) the equation becomes \(2^{\sqrt2}\log_2 x=2^{x-1}.\) Substituting \(x=\sqrt2\) gives \(2^{\sqrt2}\cdot\tfrac12=2^{\sqrt2-1},\) which holds, so \(x=\sqrt2\) is a solution.

Let \(f(x)=2^{x-1}-2^{\sqrt2}\log_2 x.\) Then \(f(1)\gt 0,\) \(f(\sqrt2)=0,\) \(f(2)\lt 0,\) and \(f(4)\gt 0,\) so there is a second root \(x_0\) between \(2\) and \(4.\)

To prove there are no others, divide the equation by \(2^x\) and consider \(h(x)=\frac{\ln x}{2^x}\) for \(x\gt1.\) Its derivative has the sign of \(\frac{1}{x}-(\ln2)(\ln x),\) a strictly decreasing expression. Thus \(h\) increases once and then decreases, so a horizontal line meets its graph at most twice. The two roots already found are all the solutions. Since \(2\lt x_0\lt4,\) we have \(2\lt S=\sqrt2+x_0\lt6.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

22 答案 B

把每堵砖墙看作一个类似 Nim 的堆，并赋予 Grundy 值。一步移走 \(1\) 块或 \(2\) 块相邻砖，可能把一堵墙分成长度为 \(a,b\) 的两堵。因此，\(g(n)\) 是所有满足 \(a+b=n-1\) 或 \(a+b=n-2\) 的 \(g(a)\oplus g(b)\) 的最小未出现非负整数。

从 \(g(0)=0\) 开始，这个递推给出 \(g(1),g(2),\ldots,g(6)\) 依次为 \(1,2,3,1,4,3\)。

后手 Beth 获胜当且仅当各墙 Grundy 值的异或为 \(0\)。检查每个选项，只有 \((6,2,1)\) 给出 \(g(6)\oplus g(2)\oplus g(1)\) \(=3\oplus 2\oplus 1=0\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Treat each wall as a Nim-like heap with a Grundy value. A move removes \(1\) or \(2\) adjacent bricks, possibly splitting a wall into lengths \(a,b.\) Thus \(g(n)\) is the mex of \(g(a)\oplus g(b)\) over \(a+b=n-1\) or \(a+b=n-2.\)

Starting with \(g(0)=0,\) this recurrence gives \(g(1),g(2),\ldots,g(6)\) equal to \(1,2,3,1,4,3,\) respectively.

The second player Beth wins exactly when the XOR of the walls’ Grundy values is \(0.\) Checking each option, only \((6,2,1)\) gives \(g(6)\oplus g(2)\oplus g(1)\) \(=3\oplus 2\oplus 1=0.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

23 答案 A

不同且等间距的箱子形成等差数列 \(n,n+d,n+2d\)，其中 \(n,d\ge 1\)。三个编号之和为 \(3(n+d)\)，因此把球固定分配到这三个箱子的概率为 \(2^{-3(n+d)}\)。

三个球可以按 \(3!=6\) 种方式对应到这些箱子，所以总概率为 \(6\sum_{n\ge 1}\sum_{d\ge 1}2^{-3(n+d)}\) \(=6\left(\sum_{n\ge 1}\tfrac{1}{8^n}\right)^2\) \(=6\cdot\tfrac17\cdot\tfrac17=\tfrac{6}{49}\)。

因为 \(\gcd(6,49)=1\)，得 \(p+q=6+49=55\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Evenly spaced distinct bins form an arithmetic progression \(n,n+d,n+2d\) with \(n,d\ge 1.\) The three labels sum to \(3(n+d),\) so a fixed assignment of balls to these bins has probability \(2^{-3(n+d)}.\)

The three balls can be ordered in \(3!=6\) ways, so the total probability is \(6\sum_{n\ge 1}\sum_{d\ge 1}2^{-3(n+d)}\) \(=6\left(\sum_{n\ge 1}\tfrac{1}{8^n}\right)^2\) \(=6\cdot\tfrac17\cdot\tfrac17=\tfrac{6}{49}.\)

Since \(\gcd(6,49)=1,\) we get \(p+q=6+49=55.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

24 答案 A

设两条对角线交于 \(O\)，夹角为 \(\theta\)。从 \(A\) 和 \(C\) 到 \(BD\) 的垂足关于 \(O\) 对称，所以 \(PQ=AC\cos\theta=6\)；同理 \(RS=BD\cos\theta=8\)。

平行四边形面积为 \(\tfrac12\cdot AC\cdot BD\sin\theta=15\)，所以 \(AC\cdot BD\sin\theta=30\)。于是 \(\dfrac{48\sin\theta}{\cos^2\theta}=30\)，得 \(\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos^2\theta}=\dfrac58\)。

令 \(s=\sin\theta\)，则 \(8s=5(1-s^2)\)，解得 \(s=\dfrac{-4+\sqrt{41}}{5}\)，所以 \(\cos^2\theta=1-s^2=\dfrac{8(\sqrt{41}-4)}{25}\)。

因此 \(d^2=BD^2\) \(=\dfrac{64}{\cos^2\theta}\) \(=8(\sqrt{41}+4)\) \(=32+8\sqrt{41}\)，所以 \(m+n+p=32+8+41=81\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Let the diagonals meet at \(O\) at angle \(\theta.\) The feet of the perpendiculars from \(A\) and \(C\) to \(BD\) are symmetric about \(O,\) so \(PQ=AC\cos\theta=6;\) likewise \(RS=BD\cos\theta=8.\)

The parallelogram’s area is \(\tfrac12\cdot AC\cdot BD\sin\theta=15,\) so \(AC\cdot BD\sin\theta=30.\) Then \(\dfrac{48\sin\theta}{\cos^2\theta}=30,\) giving \(\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos^2\theta}=\dfrac58.\)

Writing \(s=\sin\theta,\) \(8s=5(1-s^2)\) gives \(s=\dfrac{-4+\sqrt{41}}{5},\) so \(\cos^2\theta=1-s^2=\dfrac{8(\sqrt{41}-4)}{25}.\)

Then \(d^2=BD^2\) \(=\dfrac{64}{\cos^2\theta}\) \(=8(\sqrt{41}+4)\) \(=32+8\sqrt{41},\) so \(m+n+p=32+8+41=81.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

25 答案 E

对斜率 \(m\)，第 \(x\) 列（其中 \(1\le x\le 30\)）贡献 \(\min(30,\lfloor mx\rfloor)\) 个在直线 \(y=mx\) 上或其下方的点，我们需要总数等于 \(300\)。

在 \(m=\tfrac23\) 时，\(30\) 的上限尚未起作用，并且 \(\sum_{x=1}^{30}\lfloor \frac{2x}{3}\rfloor=300\)。计数会保持不变，直到遇到下一个更大的斜率 \(\frac{y}{x}\)，其中 \(x\le30\)。

若 \(\frac{y}{x}>\frac{2}{3}\)，则 \(3y-2x\) 是正整数。最接近的情形满足 \(3y-2x=1\)；在约束 \(x\le30\) 下取满足这个同余式的最大横坐标，得到 \((y,x)=(19,28)\)。若这个正整数至少为 \(2\)，斜率差会更大。因此，所求区间是 \([\tfrac23,\tfrac{19}{28})\)，长度为 \(\tfrac{19}{28}-\tfrac23=\tfrac1{84}\)。

因为 \(\gcd(1,84)=1\)，所以 \(a+b=1+84=85\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

For slope \(m,\) column \(x\) (with \(1\le x\le 30\)) contributes \(\min(30,\lfloor mx\rfloor)\) points on or below \(y=mx,\) and we need the total to equal \(300.\)

At \(m=\tfrac23,\) the cap at \(30\) is inactive and \(\sum_{x=1}^{30}\lfloor \frac{2x}{3}\rfloor=300.\) The count remains fixed until the next larger slope \(\frac{y}{x}\) with \(x\le30.\)

If \(\frac{y}{x}>\frac{2}{3},\) then \(3y-2x\) is a positive integer. The closest possibility has \(3y-2x=1;\) maximizing \(x\le30\) in this congruence gives \((y,x)=(19,28).\) Any numerator at least \(2\) gives a larger gap. Hence the interval is \([\tfrac23,\tfrac{19}{28}),\) whose length is \(\tfrac{19}{28}-\tfrac23=\tfrac1{84}.\)

Since \(\gcd(1,84)=1,\) \(a+b=1+84=85.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.
