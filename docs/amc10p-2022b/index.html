第一个余式条件给出 \[\begin{aligned}P(x)&=(x^2+x+1)Q(x)\\&\quad+x+2\end{aligned}\text{。}\]其中 \(Q\) 是某个多项式。模 \(x^2+1\) 时有 \(x^2\equiv-1\)，所以 \[P(x)\equiv xQ(x)+x+2\text{。}\]

若 \(Q(x)=c\) 是常数，则这个余式为 \((c+1)x+2\)，不可能等于 \(2x+1\)。所以 \(Q\) 的次数至少为 \(1\)。

现在令 \(Q(x)=ax+b\)。模 \(x^2+1\) 化简可得 \[P(x)\equiv(b+1)x+(2-a)\text{。}\]与 \(2x+1\) 比较系数，得到 \(a=b=1\)。这构造出了一个 \(3\) 次多项式，而常数情形不成立又说明最低次数为 \(3\)。

因此 \[\begin{aligned}P(x)&=(x+1)(x^2+x+1)\\&\quad+x+2\\&=x^3+2x^2+3x+3\end{aligned}\text{。}\]它的系数平方和为 \(1^2+2^2+3^2+3^2=23\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The first remainder condition gives \[\begin{aligned}P(x)&=(x^2+x+1)Q(x)\\&\quad+x+2.\end{aligned}\] for some polynomial \(Q.\) Modulo \(x^2+1,\) we have \(x^2\equiv-1,\) so \[P(x)\equiv xQ(x)+x+2.\]

If \(Q(x)=c\) is constant, this remainder is \((c+1)x+2,\) which cannot equal \(2x+1.\) Thus \(Q\) must have degree at least \(1.\)

Now let \(Q(x)=ax+b.\) Reducing modulo \(x^2+1\) gives \[P(x)\equiv(b+1)x+(2-a).\] Matching this with \(2x+1\) yields \(a=b=1.\) This constructs a degree-\(3\) polynomial, and the failed constant case proves that degree \(3\) is minimal.

Therefore, \[\begin{aligned}P(x)&=(x+1)(x^2+x+1)\\&\quad+x+2\\&=x^3+2x^2+3x+3.\end{aligned}\] The sum of the squares of its coefficients is \(1^2+2^2+3^2+3^2=23.\)

Thus, the answer is E .

22 答案 E

将半径为 \(2\) 和 \(8\) 的两个同心圆分别称为内圆和外圆。设所求圆的半径为 \(r\)，圆心到原点的距离为 \(d\)。所求圆必与外圆内切，因此 \(d+r=8\)。

若所求圆与内圆外切，则 \(d-r=2\)，从而 \((r,d)=(3,5)\)。若所求圆包含内圆，则 \(r-d=2\)，从而 \((r,d)=(5,3)\)。因此，所求圆的半径必为 \(3\) 或 \(5\)。

第三个已知圆的半径为 \(\sqrt3\)，圆心为 \((5,0)\)。对 \(r\) 的两个可能值，所求圆都可以与第三个圆内切或外切，所以两圆心之间的距离为 \(r-\sqrt3\) 或 \(r+\sqrt3\)。在这四种情形中，若该距离为 \(q\)，则都有 \(|d-q|<5<d+q\)。因此，以原点为圆心、\(d\) 为半径的圆与所求圆圆心的轨迹交于关于 \(x\) 轴对称的两点，如图所示。于是，每一种半径都有 \(4\) 个所求圆。

所以总面积为 \[4(5^2\pi+3^2\pi)=136\pi\text{。}\]因此，正确答案是 E。

English

Call the concentric circles of radii \(2\) and \(8\) the inner and outer circles. Let a desired circle have radius \(r\) and let its center be distance \(d\) from the origin. It must be internally tangent to the outer circle, so \(d+r=8.\)

If it is externally tangent to the inner circle, then \(d-r=2,\) giving \((r,d)=(3,5).\) If it contains the inner circle, then \(r-d=2,\) giving \((r,d)=(5,3).\) Thus every desired circle has radius \(3\) or \(5.\)

The third given circle has radius \(\sqrt3\) and center \((5,0).\) For either value of \(r,\) a desired circle may be internally or externally tangent to it, so the distance between their centers is \(r-\sqrt3\) or \(r+\sqrt3.\) In all four cases, if this distance is \(q,\) then \(|d-q|<5<d+q,\) so the circle of possible centers intersects the circle of radius \(d\) about the origin in two points, symmetric across the \(x\)-axis, as shown. Hence there are \(4\) desired circles of each radius.

The total area is therefore \[4(5^2\pi+3^2\pi)=136\pi.\] Thus, E is the correct answer.

23 答案 C

停止时间只依赖时间变量，而最终位置只依赖距离变量，所以相应概率可以相乘。

两个独立 \((0,1)\) 数之和小于 \(1\) 的概率是单位正方形中一个直角三角形的面积，即 \(\frac12\)。

三个独立 \((0,1)\) 数之和小于 \(1\) 的概率是截距为 \(1\) 的四面体体积，即 \(\frac16\)。

若 \(t_1+t_2>1\)，Amelia 走两步后停止。其概率为 \(\frac12\)，且独立地有 \(x_1+x_2>1\) 的概率为 \(\frac12\)，贡献 \(\frac14\)。

若 \(t_1+t_2<1\)，Amelia 会走第三步。其概率为 \(\frac12\)，且独立地有 \(x_1+x_2+x_3>1\) 的概率为 \(1-\frac16=\frac56\)，贡献 \(\frac5{12}\)。

总概率为 \(\frac14+\frac5{12}=\frac23\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The stopping time depends only on the time variables, while the final position depends only on the distance variables, so the corresponding probabilities multiply.

For two independent numbers in \((0,1),\) the probability that their sum is less than \(1\) is the area of a right triangle, namely \(\frac12.\)

For three independent numbers in \((0,1),\) the probability that their sum is less than \(1\) is the volume of a tetrahedron with side intercepts \(1,\) namely \(\frac16.\)

If \(t_1+t_2>1,\) Amelia stops after two steps. This has probability \(\frac12,\) and independently \(x_1+x_2>1\) has probability \(\frac12,\) contributing \(\frac14.\)

If \(t_1+t_2<1,\) Amelia takes the third step. This has probability \(\frac12,\) and independently \(x_1+x_2+x_3>1\) has probability \(1-\frac16=\frac56,\) contributing \(\frac5{12}.\)

The total probability is \(\frac14+\frac5{12}=\frac23.\)

Thus, the answer is C .

24 答案 B

连续使用两次收缩不等式可得 \[ \begin{aligned} &|f(f(400))-f(f(300))|\\ &\quad\le\frac12|f(400)-f(300)|\\ &\quad\le25 \end{aligned}\text{，}\]同理，\[|f(f(800))-f(f(900))|\le25\text{。}\]

设 \(M=f(f(300))=f(f(900))\)。由三角不等式，\[ \begin{aligned} &|f(f(800))-f(f(400))|\\ &\quad\le|f(f(800))-M|\\ &\qquad+|M-f(f(400))|\\ &\quad\le50 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

为取到这个上界，定义 \(f\) 为依次经过下列各点的分段线性函数：\[\begin{gathered}(300,600),(400,550),(550,575),\\ (650,625),(800,650),(900,600)\end{gathered}\text{。}\]并规定当 \(x\leq300\) 或 \(x\geq900\) 时 \(f(x)=600\)。每一段斜率的绝对值都不超过 \(\dfrac12\)，因此满足收缩条件。特别地，\(f(300)=f(900)=600\)、\(f(400)=550\)、\(f(800)=650\)。所以 \(f(f(400))=f(550)=575\)，而 \(f(f(800))=f(650)=625\)，两者之差为 \(50\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Applying the contraction inequality twice gives \[ \begin{aligned} &|f(f(400))-f(f(300))|\\ &\quad\le\frac12|f(400)-f(300)|\\ &\quad\le25, \end{aligned} \] and similarly \[|f(f(800))-f(f(900))|\le25.\]

Set \(M=f(f(300))=f(f(900)).\) The triangle inequality now yields \[ \begin{aligned} &|f(f(800))-f(f(400))|\\ &\quad\le|f(f(800))-M|\\ &\qquad+|M-f(f(400))|\\ &\quad\le50. \end{aligned} \]

To attain the bound, define \(f\) by linear interpolation through the points \[\begin{gathered}(300,600),(400,550),(550,575),\\ (650,625),(800,650),(900,600).\end{gathered}\] and set \(f(x)=600\) for \(x\leq300\) or \(x\geq900.\) Every segment has slope with absolute value at most \(\dfrac12,\) so the contraction condition holds. In particular, \(f(300)=f(900)=600,\) \(f(400)=550,\) and \(f(800)=650.\) Hence \(f(f(400))=f(550)=575,\) while \(f(f(800))=f(650)=625,\) giving the difference \(50.\)

Thus, the answer is B .

25 答案 A

所求的和为 \[\frac{S_{2023}-S_{2019}}{2^{2019}}\text{。}\]此外，\(0\le S_n<2^n\)。

因此，存在唯一的 \(m_n\in\{0,1,\ldots,6\}\)，使得 \[7S_n=m_n2^n+1\text{。}\]模 \(7\) 化简得到 \(m_n2^n\equiv-1\pmod7\)。

因为 \(2^3\equiv1\pmod7\)，且 \(2019\equiv0\pmod3\)，所以 \(m_{2019}=6\)。又因为 \(2023\equiv1\pmod3\)，所以 \(2m_{2023}\equiv-1\pmod7\)，从而 \(m_{2023}=3\)。因此 \[\begin{gathered}S_{2019}=\frac{6\cdot2^{2019}+1}{7},\\ S_{2023}=\frac{3\cdot2^{2023}+1}{7}\end{gathered}\text{。}\]

最后，\[ \frac{S_{2023}-S_{2019}}{2^{2019}} =\frac{3\cdot2^4-6}{7}=6\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The desired sum is \[\frac{S_{2023}-S_{2019}}{2^{2019}}.\] Also, \(0\le S_n<2^n.\)

Therefore, for a unique \(m_n\in\{0,1,\ldots,6\},\) \[7S_n=m_n2^n+1.\] Reducing modulo \(7\) gives \(m_n2^n\equiv-1\pmod7.\)

Since \(2^3\equiv1\pmod7\) and \(2019\equiv0\pmod3,\) we get \(m_{2019}=6.\) Since \(2023\equiv1\pmod3,\) we have \(2m_{2023}\equiv-1\pmod7,\) so \(m_{2023}=3.\) Hence \[\begin{gathered}S_{2019}=\frac{6\cdot2^{2019}+1}{7},\\ S_{2023}=\frac{3\cdot2^{2023}+1}{7}.\end{gathered}\]

Finally, \[ \frac{S_{2023}-S_{2019}}{2^{2019}} =\frac{3\cdot2^4-6}{7}=6. \]

Thus, the correct answer is A .
