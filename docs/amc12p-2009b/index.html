1

Jane 在五天工作周的每天早上都会买一个 \(50\) 美分的松饼或一个 \(75\) 美分的贝果。她这一周的总花费正好是整数美元。她买了多少个贝果？

A\(1\) B\(2\) C\(3\) D\(4\) E\(5\)

English

Each morning of her five-day workweek, Jane bought either a \(50\)-cent muffin or a \(75\)-cent bagel. Her total cost for the week was a whole number of dollars. How many bagels did she buy?

(A) \(1\)(B) \(2\)(C) \(3\)(D) \(4\)(E) \(5\)

2

油漆工 Paula 原本有刚好够刷 \(30\) 间同样大小房间的油漆。不幸的是，在去工作的路上，有三罐油漆从卡车上掉了下来，所以她只剩下够刷 \(25\) 间房间的油漆。她刷这 \(25\) 间房间用了多少罐油漆？

A\(10\) B\(12\) C\(15\) D\(18\) E\(25\)

English

Paula the painter had just enough paint for \(30\) identically sized rooms. Unfortunately, on the way to work, three cans of paint fell off her truck, so she had only enough paint for \(25\) rooms. How many cans of paint did she use for the \(25\) rooms?

(A) \(10\)(B) \(12\)(C) \(15\)(D) \(18\)(E) \(25\)

4

A\(\dfrac{1}{8}\) B\(\dfrac{1}{6}\) C\(\dfrac{1}{5}\) D\(\dfrac{1}{4}\) E\(\dfrac{1}{3}\)

English

A rectangular yard contains two flower beds in the shape of congruent isosceles right triangles. The remainder of the yard has a trapezoidal shape, as shown. The parallel sides of the trapezoid have lengths \(15\) and \(25\) meters. What fraction of the yard is occupied by the flower beds?

(A) \(\dfrac{1}{8}\)(B) \(\dfrac{1}{6}\)(C) \(\dfrac{1}{5}\)(D) \(\dfrac{1}{4}\)(E) \(\dfrac{1}{3}\)

7

某一年中，汽油价格在一月上涨 \(20\%\)，二月下跌 \(20\%\)，三月上涨 \(25\%\)，四月下跌 \(x\%\)。四月底的汽油价格与一月初相同。四舍五入到最接近的整数，\(x\) 是多少？

English

The price of gasoline at the end of April was the same as it had been at the beginning of January. To the nearest integer, what is \(x?\)

(A) \(12\)(B) \(17\)(C) \(20\)(D) \(25\)(E) \(35\)

8

A\(\dfrac{2}{3}a + \dfrac{1}{3}b\) B\(\dfrac{3}{2}a - \dfrac{1}{2}b\) C\(\dfrac{3}{2}a + b\) D\(\dfrac{3}{2}a + 2b\) E\(3a - 2b\)

English

逐题解析

1 答案 B

若她买了 \(b\) 个贝果，则买了 \(5 - b\) 个松饼，总价为 \(50(5-b) + 75b = 250 + 25b\) 美分。要使总价为整数美元，它必须是 \(100\) 的倍数，于是 \(25b\equiv50\pmod{100}\)，即 \(b\equiv2\pmod4\)。因为 \(0\le b\le5\)，只有 \(b=2\) 可行，此时总价为 \(300\) 美分，即 \(= \$3\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

With \(b\) bagels she buys \(5 - b\) muffins, costing \(50(5-b) + 75b = 250 + 25b\) cents. For a whole number of dollars this must be a multiple of \(100.\) Thus \(25b\equiv50\pmod{100},\) or \(b\equiv2\pmod4.\) Since \(0\le b\le5,\) only \(b=2\) works, giving \(300\) cents \(= \$3.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

2 答案 C

丢失 \(3\) 罐使她少刷 \(5\) 间房间，所以 \(3\) 罐油漆能刷 \(5\) 间房间，每间房需要 \(\dfrac{3}{5}\) 罐。

刷 \(25\) 间房间需要 \(25 \cdot \dfrac{3}{5} = 15\) 罐。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Losing \(3\) cans cost her \(5\) rooms, so \(3\) cans paint \(5\) rooms and each room needs \(\dfrac{3}{5}\) of a can.

For \(25\) rooms she used \(25 \cdot \dfrac{3}{5} = 15\) cans.

Thus, the correct answer is C.

3 答案 D

比 \(60\) 少百分之二十是 \(0.8 \cdot 60 = 48\)。

比 \(n\) 多三分之一是 \(\dfrac{4}{3}n\)，所以 \(\dfrac{4}{3}n = 48\)，得到 \(n = 36\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Twenty percent less than \(60\) is \(0.8 \cdot 60 = 48.\)

One-third more than \(n\) is \(\dfrac{4}{3}n,\) so \(\dfrac{4}{3}n = 48\) gives \(n = 36.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

4 答案 C

两条平行边相差 \(25 - 15 = 10\)，所以每个三角形的直角边为 \(\dfrac{10}{2} = 5\)，面积为 \(\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 5^2 = \dfrac{25}{2}\)。两个花坛总面积为 \(25\)。

长方形尺寸为 \(25\) 乘 \(5\)，面积为 \(125\)，花坛所占比例为 \(\dfrac{25}{125} = \dfrac{1}{5}\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The parallel sides differ by \(25 - 15 = 10,\) so each triangle has legs \(\dfrac{10}{2} = 5\) and area \(\dfrac{1}{2} \cdot 5^2 = \dfrac{25}{2}.\) The two beds total \(25.\)

The rectangle measures \(25\) by \(5,\) so its area is \(125,\) and the fraction occupied is \(\dfrac{25}{125} = \dfrac{1}{5}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

5 答案 D

因为 \(128 = 2^7\)，每个年龄都是 \(2\) 的幂。双胞胎年龄相同，设为 \(t\)，则 Kiana 的年龄为 \(\dfrac{128}{t^2}\)。

取 \(t = 8\) 时，Kiana 的年龄是 \(\dfrac{128}{64} = 2\)，比双胞胎小。（若双胞胎年龄更小，Kiana 就会更大，这是不允许的。）年龄和为 \(8 + 8 + 2 = 18\)。

\(G\) consists of four \(5 \times 5\) blocks \(G_1, \ldots, G_4,\) one in each quadrant. Any square of side \(\ge 6\) uses exactly one vertex in each block, since two points in one block are less than \(6\) apart while points in different blocks are at least \(6\) apart.

Sliding each block inward by \((\pm 5, \pm 5)\) superimposes them on one \(5 \times 5\) grid \(G'\) (points with \(|x|, |y| \le 2\)). Each such square maps to either a single point of \(G'\) or a square in \(G'.\) So the count equals the number of points of \(G'\) plus \(4\) times the number of squares with vertices in \(G'.\)

The grid has \(4^2+3^2+2^2+1^2=30\) axis-parallel squares. For a tilted square, let one side move \(a\) units horizontally and \(b\) units vertically, where \(a,b\gt0\) and \(a+b\le4.\) For each ordered pair \((a,b),\) there are \((5-a-b)^2\) placements. The totals for \(a+b=2,3,4\) are \(9,8,3,\) respectively, giving \(20\) tilted squares and \(50\) squares altogether. Therefore the required count is \(25+4\cdot50=225.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.
