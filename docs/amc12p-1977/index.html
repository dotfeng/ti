Therefore, the correct answer is B.

若外接圆半径为 \(r\)，则三条弦所对的圆心角分别为 \(40^\circ\)、\(80^\circ\)、\(160^\circ\)。因此 \[ \begin{aligned} a&=2r\sin20^\circ,\\ b&=2r\sin40^\circ,\\ d&=2r\sin80^\circ \end{aligned}\text{。}\]由和差化积公式，\[ \begin{gathered} \sin20^\circ+\sin40^\circ\\ =2\sin30^\circ\cos10^\circ\\ =\cos10^\circ=\sin80^\circ \end{gathered}\text{。}\]两边乘以 \(2r\)，得到 \(a+b=d\)。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

If the circumradius is \(r,\) the three chords subtend central angles \(40^\circ,\) \(80^\circ,\) \(160^\circ,\) respectively. Thus \[ \begin{aligned} a&=2r\sin20^\circ,\\ b&=2r\sin40^\circ,\\ d&=2r\sin80^\circ. \end{aligned} \] The sum-to-product identity gives \[ \begin{gathered} \sin20^\circ+\sin40^\circ\\ =2\sin30^\circ\cos10^\circ\\ =\cos10^\circ=\sin80^\circ. \end{gathered} \] Multiplying by \(2r\) yields \(a+b=d.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is A.
