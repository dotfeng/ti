Thus, the correct answer is E.

22 答案 B

每轮有 \(4 \cdot 3 = 12\) 个等可能的（给出者，接收者）有序对，所以共有 \(12^4\) 个结果序列。每个人最后都有四枚硬币，当且仅当四次转移相互抵消。有利模式为：一次 \(4\)-循环赠送（\(24 \cdot 6 = 144\) 种），两组不相交的相互交换（\(24 \cdot 3 = 72\) 种），同一对玩家互相交换两次（\(6 \cdot 6 = 36\) 种），以及某个玩家既给另外两人各一枚又从这两人各收一枚（\(4 \cdot 3 \cdot 24 = 288\) 种）。总数为 \(144 + 72 + 36 + 288 = 540\)，概率为 \(\dfrac{540}{12^4} = \dfrac{540}{20736} = \dfrac{5}{192}\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Each round has \(4 \cdot 3 = 12\) equally likely (giver, receiver) pairs, so there are \(12^4\) outcome sequences. Everyone ends with four coins exactly when the four transfers cancel. The favorable patterns are: a \(4\)-cycle of gifts (\(24 \cdot 6 = 144\) ways), two disjoint mutual exchanges (\(24 \cdot 3 = 72\)), one pair exchanging twice (\(6 \cdot 6 = 36\)), and one player both giving to and receiving from each of two others (\(4 \cdot 3 \cdot 24 = 288\)). These total \(144 + 72 + 36 + 288 = 540.\) The probability is \(\dfrac{540}{12^4} = \dfrac{540}{20736} = \dfrac{5}{192}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

23 答案 D

点 \(A, B, C\) 都在 \(y = x^2\) 上，所以 \(g(x) = f(x) - x^2\) 以 \(2, 3, 4\) 为根，即 \(g(x) = a(x-2)(x-3)(x-4)\)，其中 \(a \ne 0\)。\(x^3\) 和 \(x^2\) 在 \(f\) 中的系数分别为 \(a\) 和 \(1 - 9a\)，因此由韦达定理，\(f(x) - L(x)\)（对任意一次函数 \(L\)）的三个根之和为 \(9 - \tfrac1a\)。直线 \(AB, AC, BC\) 与三次曲线相交的三个横坐标集合分别为 \(\{2, 3, x_D\}\)、\(\{2, 4, x_E\}\)、\(\{3, 4, x_F\}\)，所以 \[ \begin{aligned} &x_D + x_E + x_F \\ &\quad {}= 3\left(9 - \tfrac1a\right) \\ &\quad {}- 2(2 + 3 + 4) \\ &\quad {}= 9 - \tfrac3a = 24\text{，} \end{aligned} \] 解得 \(a = -\tfrac15\)。因此 \(f(x) = x^2\) \(- \tfrac15(x-2)(x-3)(x-4)\)，所以 \(f(0) = 0 - \tfrac15(-2)(-3)(-4)\) \(= \tfrac{24}{5}\)。

English

The points \(A, B, C\) lie on \(y = x^2,\) so \(g(x) = f(x) - x^2\) has roots \(2, 3, 4:\) \(g(x) = a(x-2)(x-3)(x-4)\) for some \(a \ne 0.\) The coefficients of \(x^3\) and \(x^2\) in \(f\) are \(a\) and \(1 - 9a,\) so by Vieta the three roots of \(f(x) - L(x)\) (for any linear \(L\)) sum to \(9 - \tfrac1a.\) The lines \(AB, AC, BC\) meet the cubic in triples \(\{2, 3, x_D\},\) \(\{2, 4, x_E\},\) \(\{3, 4, x_F\},\) so \[ \begin{aligned} &x_D + x_E + x_F \\ &\quad {}= 3\left(9 - \tfrac1a\right) \\ &\quad {}- 2(2 + 3 + 4) \\ &\quad {}= 9 - \tfrac3a = 24, \end{aligned} \] giving \(a = -\tfrac15.\) Then \(f(x) = x^2\) \(- \tfrac15(x-2)(x-3)(x-4),\) so \(f(0) = 0 - \tfrac15(-2)(-3)(-4)\) \(= \tfrac{24}{5}.\)

24 答案 D

令 \(BC = 1\)，\(AB = r \gt 1\)。由 \(\triangle ABC \sim \triangle BCD\) 以及直角位置，可取 \(C = (0,0)\)，\(B = (0,1)\)，\(A = (r,1)\)，\(D = \bigl(\tfrac1r, 0\bigr)\)。设 \(E = (x, y)\)，其中 \(x, y \gt 0\)。由 \(\triangle ABC \sim \triangle CEB\) 得 \(\dfrac{x}{y} = \tan(\angle ECB)\) \(= \tan(\angle BAC)\) \(= \dfrac1r\) 且 \(x^2 + y^2 = \dfrac{r^2}{1 + r^2}\)，所以 \(x = \dfrac{r}{1+r^2}\)，\(y = \dfrac{r^2}{1+r^2}\)。这两个相关的面积为 \[ \begin{aligned} [\triangle CEB]&=\dfrac{r}{2(1+r^2)},\\ [\triangle AED]&=\dfrac{r^4-r^2+1}{2r(1+r^2)} \end{aligned}\text{。}\] 令后者等于前者的 \(17\) 倍，得到 \(r^4 - 18r^2 + 1 = 0\)。于是 \(r^2 = 9 + 4\sqrt5 = (2 + \sqrt5)^2\)，所以 \(r = 2 + \sqrt5\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Set \(BC = 1\) and \(AB = r \gt 1.\) The similarity \(\triangle ABC \sim \triangle BCD\) with the right angles places the figure at \(C = (0,0),\) \(B = (0,1),\) \(A = (r,1),\) \(D = \bigl(\tfrac1r, 0\bigr).\) Let \(E = (x, y)\) with \(x, y \gt 0.\) From \(\triangle ABC \sim \triangle CEB\) we get \(\dfrac{x}{y} = \tan(\angle ECB)\) \(= \tan(\angle BAC)\) \(= \dfrac1r\) and \(x^2 + y^2 = \dfrac{r^2}{1 + r^2},\) so \(x = \dfrac{r}{1+r^2},\) \(y = \dfrac{r^2}{1+r^2}.\) The two relevant areas are \[ \begin{aligned} [\triangle CEB]&=\dfrac{r}{2(1+r^2)},\\ [\triangle AED]&=\dfrac{r^4-r^2+1}{2r(1+r^2)}. \end{aligned} \] Setting the second equal to \(17\) times the first gives \(r^4 - 18r^2 + 1 = 0.\) Then \(r^2 = 9 + 4\sqrt5 = (2 + \sqrt5)^2,\) so \(r = 2 + \sqrt5.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

25 答案 D

设球队数为 \(T\)。对 \(9\) 人子集求和时，每支球队被计数 \(\binom{n-5}{4}\) 次；对 \(8\) 人子集求和时，每支球队被计数 \(\binom{n-5}{3}\) 次。两个平均值分别为 \(\dfrac{\binom{n-5}{4}T}{\binom n9}\) 和 \(\dfrac{\binom{n-5}{3}T}{\binom n8}\)。令第一个等于第二个的倒数并化简，得到 \[ \begin{aligned} &T \\ &\quad {}= \scriptsize \frac{n(n-1)(n-2)(n-3)(n-4)}{2^5 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5 \cdot 7} \end{aligned}\text{。}\] 我们需要它在 \(n \ge 9\) 时为正整数。令 \(N =\) \(n(n-1)(n-2)(n-3)(n-4)\)；作为五个连续整数的乘积，\(N\) 总能被 \(5\) 整除。\(N\) 是 \(7\) 的倍数这一条件对模 \(7\) 的 \(5\) 个余数成立；是 \(9\) 的倍数对模 \(9\) 的 \(7\) 个余数成立；是 \(32\) 的倍数对模 \(16\) 的 \(8\) 个余数成立。因此由中国剩余定理，模 \(\operatorname{lcm}(7,9,16)=1008\) 共有 \(5\cdot7\cdot8=280\) 个解。于是 \(1 \le n \le 2016\) 中有 \(560\) 个值；去掉 \(n = 1, 2, 3, 4\)（它们小于 \(9\)），再加上 \(n = 2017\)（因为 \(2017 \equiv 1 \pmod{1008}\)），得到 \(560 - 4 + 1 = 557\) 个有效值。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Let \(T\) be the number of teams. Summing over size-\(9\) subsets counts each team \(\binom{n-5}{4}\) times and over size-\(8\) subsets \(\binom{n-5}{3}\) times. The averages are \(\dfrac{\binom{n-5}{4}T}{\binom n9}\) and \(\dfrac{\binom{n-5}{3}T}{\binom n8};\) setting the first equal to the reciprocal of the second and simplifying gives \[ \begin{aligned} &T \\ &\quad {}= \scriptsize \frac{n(n-1)(n-2)(n-3)(n-4)}{2^5 \cdot 3^2 \cdot 5 \cdot 7}. \end{aligned} \] We need this to be a positive integer with \(n \ge 9.\) Let \(N =\) \(n(n-1)(n-2)(n-3)(n-4);\) as a product of five consecutive integers, \(N\) is always divisible by \(5.\) The condition that \(N\) is divisible by \(7\) holds for \(5\) residues modulo \(7;\) divisibility by \(9\) holds for \(7\) residues modulo \(9;\) and divisibility by \(32\) holds for \(8\) residues modulo \(16.\) The Chinese Remainder Theorem therefore gives \(5\cdot7\cdot8=280\) solutions modulo \(\operatorname{lcm}(7,9,16)=1008.\) So there are \(560\) values in \(1 \le n \le 2016;\) removing \(n = 1, 2, 3, 4\) (which are below \(9\)) and adding \(n = 2017\) (since \(2017 \equiv 1 \pmod{1008}\)) gives \(560 - 4 + 1 = 557\) valid values.

Thus, the correct answer is D.
