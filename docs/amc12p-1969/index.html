所以正确答案是 D。

English

Take \(A=(-a,0),\) \(B=(a,0),\) \(C=(0,a),\) and \(P=(p,0).\) This describes every point on the hypotenuse line. Then \[ \begin{aligned} s&=(p+a)^2+(p-a)^2\\ &=2p^2+2a^2,\\ 2CP^2&=2(p^2+a^2)\\ &=2p^2+2a^2. \end{aligned} \] Thus \(s=2CP^2\) for every position of \(P.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is D.

31 答案 D

各顶点的像为 \[ \begin{aligned} O&\mapsto(0,0),\\ A&\mapsto(1,0),\\ B&\mapsto(0,2),\\ C&\mapsto(-1,0) \end{aligned}\text{。}\]边 \(OA\) 映为从 \((0,0)\) 到 \((1,0)\) 的线段，\(CO\) 映为从 \((-1,0)\) 到 \((0,0)\) 的线段。在 \(AB\) 上，\(x=1\)，所以 \(u=1-\frac{v^2}{4}\)；在 \(BC\) 上，\(y=1\)，所以 \(u=\frac{v^2}{4}-1\)。它们是连接 \((\pm1,0)\) 与 \((0,2)\) 的两段上方抛物线弧。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The vertices map as \[ \begin{aligned} O&\mapsto(0,0),\\ A&\mapsto(1,0),\\ B&\mapsto(0,2),\\ C&\mapsto(-1,0). \end{aligned} \] Side \(OA\) maps to the segment from \((0,0)\) to \((1,0),\) and \(CO\) maps to the segment from \((-1,0)\) to \((0,0).\) On \(AB,\) \(x=1,\) so \(u=1-\frac{v^2}{4};\) on \(BC,\) \(y=1,\) so \(u=\frac{v^2}{4}-1.\) These are the two upper parabolic arcs joining \((\pm1,0)\) to \((0,2).\)

Therefore, the correct answer is D.

32 答案 C

对任意多项式 \(p(n)\)，其系数和为 \(p(1)\)。这里该多项式表示 \(u_n\)，而初始条件给出 \(u_1=5\)。因此系数和为 \(5\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

For any polynomial \(p(n),\) the sum of its coefficients is \(p(1).\) Here the polynomial represents \(u_n,\) and the initial condition gives \(u_1=5.\) Therefore the coefficient sum is \(5.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is C.

33 答案 A

对等差数列而言，第十一项是前 \(21\) 项的平均数。因此两个数列的第十一项分别为 \(\frac{S_{21}}{21}\) 与 \(\frac{T_{21}}{21}\)。它们的比为 \[ \frac{S_{21}}{T_{21}} =\frac{7(21)+1}{4(21)+27} =\frac{148}{111} =\frac43\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 A。

English

For an arithmetic sequence, the eleventh term is the average of the first \(21\) terms. Thus the respective eleventh terms are \(\frac{S_{21}}{21}\) and \(\frac{T_{21}}{21}.\) Their ratio is \[ \frac{S_{21}}{T_{21}} =\frac{7(21)+1}{4(21)+27} =\frac{148}{111} =\frac43. \]

Therefore, the correct answer is A.

34 答案 B

写成 \(R(x)=ax+b\)。由于除式为 \((x-1)(x-2)\)，在它的两个根处代入多项式除法恒等式，得到 \[ R(1)=1,\qquad R(2)=2^{100}\text{。}\]因此 \[ \begin{aligned} R(x)&=1+(2^{100}-1)(x-1)\\ &=2^{100}(x-1)-(x-2) \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Write \(R(x)=ax+b.\) Since the divisor is \((x-1)(x-2),\) evaluating the division identity at its roots gives \[ R(1)=1,\qquad R(2)=2^{100}. \] Therefore \[ \begin{aligned} R(x)&=1+(2^{100}-1)(x-1)\\ &=2^{100}(x-1)-(x-2). \end{aligned} \]

Therefore, the correct answer is B.

35 答案 B

左侧交点满足 \[ L(m)=-\sqrt{6+m}\text{。}\]因此 \[ \begin{aligned} r&=\frac{-\sqrt{6-m}+\sqrt{6+m}}{m}\\ &=\frac{2}{\sqrt{6+m}+\sqrt{6-m}} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]当 \(m\) 趋近 \(0\) 时，该式趋近于 \(\frac{2}{2\sqrt6}=\frac{1}{\sqrt6}\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The left intersection satisfies \[ L(m)=-\sqrt{6+m}. \] Hence \[ \begin{aligned} r&=\frac{-\sqrt{6-m}+\sqrt{6+m}}{m}\\ &=\frac{2}{\sqrt{6+m}+\sqrt{6-m}}. \end{aligned} \] As \(m\) approaches \(0,\) this approaches \(\frac{2}{2\sqrt6}=\frac{1}{\sqrt6}.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is B.
