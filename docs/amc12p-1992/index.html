取 \(A=(-46,0)\)、\(B=(46,0)\)、\(D=(-\frac{19}{2},h)\)，且 \(C=(\frac{19}{2},h)\)。由对称性，圆心必为 \(O=(0,0)\)。每条腰的水平位移为 \(\frac{73}{2}\)，所以 \[ x^2=h^2+\left(\frac{73}{2}\right)^2\text{。}\]从 \(O\) 向腰作垂线，当垂足到达上端点时，它才首次落在线段上；这个临界条件为 \(OD\perp AD\)。因此 \[ \begin{aligned} (-\frac{19}{2},h)\cdot(\frac{73}{2},h)&=0,\\ h^2&=\frac{1387}{4} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]所以 \[ m^2=\frac{1387+5329}{4}=1679\text{。}\]

因此正确答案是 B。

English

Place \(A=(-46,0),\) \(B=(46,0),\) \(D=(-\frac{19}{2},h),\) and \(C=(\frac{19}{2},h).\) Symmetry forces the circle’s center to be \(O=(0,0).\) The horizontal offset along each leg is \(\frac{73}{2},\) so \[ x^2=h^2+\left(\frac{73}{2}\right)^2. \] The perpendicular from \(O\) to a leg first has its foot on the segment when the foot reaches the upper endpoint; this boundary condition is \(OD\perp AD.\) Thus \[ \begin{aligned} (-\frac{19}{2},h)\cdot(\frac{73}{2},h)&=0,\\ h^2&=\frac{1387}{4}. \end{aligned} \] Therefore \[ m^2=\frac{1387+5329}{4}=1679. \]

Thus the correct answer is B.
