1

Mary 想了一个正的两位数。她把它乘以 \(3\)，再加上 \(11\)。然后她交换所得结果的两个数字，得到一个介于 \(71\) 和 \(75\) 之间（含端点）的数。Mary 原来的数是多少？

A\(11\) B\(12\) C\(13\) D\(14\) E\(15\)

English

Mary thought of a positive two-digit number. She multiplied it by \(3\) and added \(11.\) Then she switched the digits of the result, obtaining a number between \(71\) and \(75,\) inclusive. What was Mary’s number?

(A) \(11 \)(B) \(12 \)(C) \(13 \)(D) \(14 \)(E) \(15 \)

2

Sofia 在学校的 \(400\) 米跑道上跑了 \(5\) 圈。每一圈中，她前 \(100\) 米的平均速度为每秒 \(4\) 米，剩下 \(300\) 米的平均速度为每秒 \(5\) 米。Sofia 跑完 \(5\) 圈共花了多少时间？

A\(5\) 分 \(35\) 秒 B\(6\) 分 \(40\) 秒 C\(7\) 分 \(5\) 秒 D\(7\) 分 \(25\) 秒 E\(8\) 分 \(10\) 秒

English

Sofia ran \(5\) laps around the \(400\)-meter track at her school. For each lap, she ran the first \(100\) meters at an average speed of \(4\) meters per second and the remaining \(300\) meters at an average speed of \(5\) meters per second. How much time did Sofia take running the \(5\) laps?

(A) \(5\) minutes and \(35\) seconds(B) \(6\) minutes and \(40\) seconds(C) \(7\) minutes and \(5\) seconds(D) \(7\) minutes and \(25\) seconds(E) \(8\) minutes and \(10\) seconds

3

实数 \(x\)、\(y\)、\(z\) 满足 \(0 < x < 1\)、\(-1 < y < 0\)、\(1 < z < 2\)。

下面哪个数一定为正？

A\(y+x^2\) B\(y+xz\) C\(y+y^2\) D\(y+2y^2\) E\(y+z\)

English

Real numbers \(x,\) \(y,\) and \(z\) satisfy the inequalities \(0 < x < 1,\) \(-1 < y < 0,\) and \(1 < z < 2.\)

Which of the following numbers is necessarily positive?

(A) \(y+x^2 \)(B) \(y+xz \)(C) \(y+y^2 \)(D) \(y+2y^2 \)(E) \(y+z \)

4

设 \(x\) 和 \(y\) 是非零实数，且满足 \[\frac{3x+y}{x-3y}=-2\text{。}\] 求下式的值： \[\frac{x+3y}{3x-y}\text{。}\]

A\(-3\) B\(-1\) C\(1\) D\(2\) E\(3\)

English

Suppose that \(x\) and \(y\) are nonzero real numbers such that \[\frac{3x+y}{x-3y}=-2.\] What is the value of \[\frac{x+3y}{3x-y}?\]

(A) \(-3 \)(B) \(-1 \)(C) \(1 \)(D) \(2 \)(E) \(3 \)

5

Camilla 原来拥有的蓝莓味软糖数量是樱桃味软糖数量的两倍。她每种各吃了 \(10\) 颗后，蓝莓味软糖数量变成樱桃味软糖数量的三倍。她原来有多少颗蓝莓味软糖？

A\(10\) B\(20\) C\(30\) D\(40\) E\(50\)

English

Camilla had twice as many blueberry jelly beans as cherry jelly beans. After eating \(10\) pieces of each kind, she now has three times as many blueberry jelly beans as cherry jelly beans. How many blueberry jelly beans did she originally have?

(A) \(10 \)(B) \(20 \)(C) \(30 \)(D) \(40 \)(E) \(50 \)

6

一个 \(3\) 英寸 \(\times\) \(2\) 英寸 \(\times\) \(3\) 英寸的盒子中，最多能放入多少个实心的 \(2\) 英寸 \(\times\) \(2\) 英寸 \(\times\) \(1\) 英寸长方体？

A\(3\) B\(4\) C\(5\) D\(6\) E\(7\)

English

What is the largest number of solid \(2\)-in \(\times\) \(2\)-in \(\times\) \(1\)-in blocks that can fit in a \(3\)-in \(\times\) \(2\)-in \(\times\) \(3\)-in box?

(A) \(3 \)(B) \(4 \)(C) \(5 \)(D) \(6 \)(E) \(7 \)

7

她总共花了 \(44\) 分钟到达朋友家。四舍五入到最接近的十分之一千米，Samia 走了多远？

A\(2.0\) B\(2.2\) C\(2.8\) D\(3.4\) E\(4.4\)

English

Samia set off on her bicycle to visit her friend, traveling at an average speed of \(17\) kilometers per hour. When she had gone half the distance to her friend’s house, a tire went flat, and she walked the rest of the way at \(5\) kilometers per hour.

In all, it took her \(44\) minutes to reach her friend’s house. In kilometers rounded to the nearest tenth, how far did Samia walk?

(A) \(2.0 \)(B) \(2.2 \)(C) \(2.8 \)(D) \(3.4 \)(E) \(4.4 \)

逐题解析

1 答案 B

从可能的结果倒推。把 \(71,72,73,74,75\) 的两个数字换回去，分别得到 \(17,27,37,47,57\)。再减去 \(11\)，依次得到 \(6,16,26,36,46\)。其中只有 \(6\) 和 \(36\) 能被 \(3\) 整除，而其中只有 \(36\div3=12\) 是两位数。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Work backward from the possible results. Reversing \(71,72,73,74,75\) gives \(17,27,37,47,57,\) respectively. Subtracting \(11\) gives \(6,16,26,36,46.\) Only \(6\) and \(36\) are divisible by \(3,\) and only \(36\div3=12\) is a two-digit number.

Thus, the correct answer is B .

2 答案 C

Sofia 总共以每秒 \(4\) 米跑了 \(5\cdot 100=500\) 米，以每秒 \(5\) 米跑了 \(5\cdot 300=1500\) 米。

因此总时间为 \[\frac{500}4 + \frac{1500}5 = 425\] 秒。

这个时间等于 \(7\) 分 \(5\) 秒。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

She ran a total of \(5\cdot 100=500\) meters at \(4\) meters per second and \(5\cdot 300=1500\) meters at \(5\) meters per second.

Therefore, her time is \[\frac{500}4 + \frac{1500}5 = 425\] seconds.

This is equal to a total of \(7\) minutes and \(5\) seconds.

Thus, the correct answer is C .

3 答案 E

因为 \(-1 < y\) 且 \(1 < z\)，所以 \(0 < y+z\)，因此选项 E 一定正确。

此外，用下面这组取值可以排除其余每个选项：\[x=0.1\text{，}\]\[y=-0.25\text{，}\]\[z=1.25\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Since \(-1 < y\) and \(1 < z,\) we can add the inequalities to see that \(0 < y+z.\) This naturally proves choice E correct.

Furthermore, we can eliminate every other choice with the following values: \[x=0.1,\]\[y=-0.25,\]\[z=1.25.\]

Thus, the correct answer is E .

4 答案 D

由 \[\frac{3x+y}{x-3y}=-2\text{，}\] 两边同乘分母，得到 \[3x+y = 6y-2x\text{。}\] 整理可得 \(x=y\)。

因此 \[\frac{x+3y}{3x-y} = \frac{x+3x}{3x-x}=2\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Given that \[\frac{3x+y}{x-3y}=-2,\] we can multiply by the denominator to get \[3x+y = 6y-2x.\] Solving, we can see that \(x=y.\)

Therefore, \[\frac{x+3y}{3x-y} = \frac{x+3x}{3x-x}=2.\]

Thus, the correct answer is D .

5 答案 D

设原来樱桃味有 \(c\) 颗，蓝莓味有 \(b\) 颗。

由第一句和第二句分别可得 \[b = 2c\]\[b-10=3(c-10)\] 这两个方程。

因此 \[2c-10 = 3c-30\]\[c=20\text{。}\] 这说明 \[b = 2\cdot 20=40\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

由对称性，不妨设重心是双曲线顶点 \(G=(1,1)\)。三角形至少有两个顶点位于双曲线的同一支上。它们不能都位于负支：如果其中两个顶点的 \(x\) 坐标为负，第三个顶点的横坐标就会大于 \(3\)，而三个 \(y\) 坐标之和会小于 \(\frac{1}{3}\)，这与重心是 \((1,1)\) 矛盾。因此两个顶点位于正支。

把这两个顶点写成 \(P=(a,\frac{1}{a})\) 和 \(Q=(b,\frac{1}{b})\)，其中 \(a,b>0\)。等边三角形的重心也是外心，所以 \(P\) 和 \(Q\) 到 \(G\) 的距离相等。对 \(t>0\)，点 \((t,\frac{1}{t})\) 到 \(G\) 的距离平方为 \[\left(t+\dfrac1t\right)\left(t+\dfrac1t-2\right)\text{。}\]当 \(t+\frac{1}{t}\) 从 \(2\) 开始增大时，此式严格递增，所以两个不同的点必须满足 \(b=\frac{1}{a}\)。因此 \(P\) 和 \(Q\) 关于 \(y=x\) 对称。

第三个顶点位于垂直平分线 \(y=x\) 上。它的坐标还满足 \(xy=1\)，所以它是 \((1,1)\) 或 \((-1,-1)\)。它不能与重心重合，所以是 \((-1,-1)\)。因此外接圆半径就是从 \((1,1)\) 到 \((-1,-1)\) 的距离，即 \(2\sqrt2\)。

从中心把等边三角形分成三个三角形，其面积为 \[3\cdot\dfrac12(2\sqrt2)^2\sin120^\circ=6\sqrt3\text{。}\]面积的平方为 \((6\sqrt3)^2=108\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

By symmetry, assume that the centroid is the hyperbola vertex \(G=(1,1).\) At least two triangle vertices lie on the same branch of the hyperbola. They cannot both lie on the negative branch: if two of their \(x\)-coordinates were negative, the third would exceed \(3,\) while the sum of the three \(y\)-coordinates would be less than \(\frac{1}{3},\) contradicting that their centroid is \((1,1).\) Thus two vertices lie on the positive branch.

Write these vertices as \(P=(a,\frac{1}{a})\) and \(Q=(b,\frac{1}{b}),\) where \(a,b>0.\) The centroid of an equilateral triangle is also its circumcenter, so \(P\) and \(Q\) are equidistant from \(G.\) For \(t>0,\) the squared distance from \((t,\frac{1}{t})\) to \(G\) is \[\left(t+\dfrac1t\right)\left(t+\dfrac1t-2\right).\] This is strictly increasing as \(t+\frac{1}{t}\) increases from \(2,\) so the distinct points must satisfy \(b=\frac{1}{a}.\) Hence \(P\) and \(Q\) are reflections across \(y=x.\)

The third vertex lies on the perpendicular bisector \(y=x.\) Its coordinates also satisfy \(xy=1,\) so it is either \((1,1)\) or \((-1,-1).\) It cannot equal the centroid, so it is \((-1,-1).\) Therefore, the circumradius is the distance from \((1,1)\) to \((-1,-1),\) namely \(2\sqrt2.\)

Dividing the equilateral triangle into three triangles at its center gives its area as \[3\cdot\dfrac12(2\sqrt2)^2\sin120^\circ=6\sqrt3.\] The square of the area is \((6\sqrt3)^2=108.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C .

25 答案 E

设七次总分为 \(S\)。因为七次后的平均数是整数，\(S\) 可被 \(7\) 整除。七个不同分数都在 \(91\) 到 \(100\) 之间，所以 \(91+92+\cdots+97\) \(\le S\le\) \(94+95+\cdots+100\)。

因此 \(658\le S\le 679\)，可能的 \(7\) 的倍数为 \(658,665,672,679\)。由于第七次分数是 \(95\)，前六次总分为 \(S-95\)，它必须能被 \(6\) 整除。这迫使 \(S=665\)。

因此前六次总分为 \(570\)。前五次平均数也是整数，所以前五次总分能被 \(5\) 整除。因此第六次分数也必须能被 \(5\) 整除。由于第七次已经是 \(95\)，且所有分数不同，第六次分数只能是 \(100\)。所以正确答案是 E。

English

Let \(S\) be the sum of all seven scores. Since all seven averages were integers, \(S\) is divisible by \(7\). Also the seven distinct scores are between \(91\) and \(100\), so \(91+92+\cdots+97\) \(\le S\le\) \(94+95+\cdots+100\).

Thus \(658\le S\le 679\), and the possible multiples of \(7\) are \(658,665,672,679\). Since the seventh score is \(95\), the first six scores sum to \(S-95\), which must be divisible by \(6\). This forces \(S=665\).

The first six scores sum to \(570\). The first five-score average was also an integer, so the sum of the first five scores is divisible by \(5\). Therefore the sixth score is divisible by \(5\). Since the seventh score is already \(95\) and all scores are distinct, the sixth score is \(100\). Thus, E is the correct answer.
