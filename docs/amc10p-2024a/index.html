一个用于估计沿小路登上山顶所需时间的模型形如 \(T = aL + bG\)，其中 \(a\) 和 \(b\) 为常数，\(T\) 是分钟数，\(L\) 是小路长度，单位为英里，\(G\) 是海拔上升高度，单位为英尺。模型估计，若小路长 \(1.5\) 英里并上升 \(800\) 英尺，或小路长 \(1.2\) 英里并上升 \(1100\) 英尺，到达山顶都需要 \(69\) 分钟。若小路长 \(4.2\) 英里并上升 \(4000\) 英尺，模型估计需要多少分钟？

A\(240\) B\(246\) C\(252\) D\(258\) E\(264\)

English

A model used to estimate the time it will take to hike to the top of the mountain on a trail is of the form \(T = aL + bG,\) where \(a\) and \(b\) are constants, \(T\) is the time in minutes, \(L\) is the length of the trail in miles, and \(G\) is the altitude gain in feet. The model estimates that it will take \(69\) minutes to hike to the top if a trail is \(1.5\) miles long and ascends \(800\) feet, as well as if a trail is \(1.2\) miles long and ascends \(1100\) feet. How many minutes does the model estimate it will take to hike to the top if the trail is \(4.2\) miles long and ascends \(4000\) feet?

(A) \(240\)(B) \(246\)(C) \(252\)(D) \(258\)(E) \(264\)

• 向右平移 \(2\) 个单位；

• 绕原点逆时针旋转 \(90^\circ\)；

• 关于 \(x\) 轴反射；

• 以原点为中心、比例因子为 \(2\) 的伸缩。

A\(1\) B\(2\) C\(3\) D\(4\) E\(5\)

English

Two transformations are said to commute if applying the first followed by the second gives the same result as applying the second followed by the first. Consider these four transformations of the coordinate plane:

• a translation \(2\) units to the right,

• a \(90^\circ\) rotation counterclockwise about the origin,

• a reflection across the \(x\)-axis, and

• a dilation centered at the origin with scale factor \(2.\)

Of the \(6\) pairs of distinct transformations from this list, how many commute?

(A) \(1\)(B) \(2\)(C) \(3\)(D) \(4\)(E) \(5\)

直接计算：\(9901 \cdot 101 = 990100 + 9901\) \(= 1000001\)，而 \(99 \cdot 10101 = 999999\)。因此差为 \(1000001 - 999999 = 2\)，正确答案是 A。

English

Just compute each piece. We have \(9901 \cdot 101 = 990100 + 9901\) \(= 1000001,\) and \(99 \cdot 10101 = 999999.\) Subtracting, \(1000001 - 999999 = 2.\) Thus, A is the correct answer.

2 答案 B

两个方程为 \(1.5a + 800b = 69\) 和 \(1.2a + 1100b = 69\)。相减消去 \(69\)，得到 \(0.3a - 300b = 0\)，所以 \(a = 1000b\)。代入第一式得 \(1500b + 800b = 2300b = 69\)，因此 \(b = 0.03\)、\(a = 30\)。所求时间为 \(T = 30(4.2) + 0.03(4000)\) \(= 126 + 120\) \(= 246\)。所以正确答案是 B。

English

Subtract the second equation from the first: \((a-c)(b-1)=13.\) Put \(u=a-c\) and \(v=b-1.\) The four possibilities \((u,v)=(1,13),\) \((13,1),\) \((-1,-13),\) and \((-13,-1)\) give, after substitution into \(bc+a=87,\) respectively \(c=\frac{86}{15},\) \(c=\frac{74}{3},\) \(c=-8,\) and \(c=100.\) Only the third is an integer solution of all three original equations: \((a,b,c)=(-9,-12,-8).\) (The last candidate is \((87,0,100),\) which fails \(ca+b=60.\)) Adding the original equations gives \(ab+bc+ca\) \({}+(a+b+c)=247.\) Since \(a+b+c=-29,\) we obtain \(ab+bc+ca\) \(=247-(-29)=276.\) Thus, D is the correct answer.

24 答案 B

每次掷骰都会让蜜蜂沿 \(\pm x,\pm y\) 或 \(\pm z\) 方向移动一个单位，所以共有 \(6^4=1296\) 个等可能移动序列。有效路径分成两类。第一类走过某个正方形面的四条边：坐标方向对有 \(3\) 种选择，两个方向的正负号有 \(4\) 种选择，第一个方向有 \(2\) 种选择，共 \(24\) 条路径。第二类用到三个坐标方向，其中一个方向使用两次。重复方向有 \(3\) 种选择；它在四步方向序列中的两个位置不能相邻，这两个位置有 \(3\) 种选法，其余两个方向的顺序有 \(2\) 种。对每个这样的方向序列，恰有 \(2^3=8\) 种正负号选择能使四条不同的边位于同一个立方体上。因此第二类有 \(3\cdot3\cdot2\cdot8=144\) 条路径。总共有 \(24+144=168\) 个有利序列，所以概率为 \(\frac{168}{1296}=\frac{7}{54}\)，正确答案是 B。

English

Every roll moves the bee one unit along \(\pm x,\pm y,\) or \(\pm z,\) so there are \(6^4=1296\) equally likely sequences. There are two types of valid paths. A path around one square face has \(3\) choices of coordinate plane, \(4\) choices for the signs of its two axes, and \(2\) choices for which axis is used first, giving \(24.\) Otherwise all three coordinate directions are used, with one repeated: choose that axis in \(3\) ways, choose its two nonadjacent positions in \(3\) ways, order the other two axes in \(2\) ways, and choose their three initial signs in \(2^3=8\) ways. (The second step on the repeated axis must have the opposite sign.) This gives \(3\cdot3\cdot2\cdot8=144.\) Hence there are \(24+144=168\) favorable sequences, and the probability is \(\frac{168}{1296}=\frac{7}{54}.\) Therefore, the answer is B.

25 答案 C

中间一行的每个方格必须恰好接触一根牙签。先考虑从中间条带一侧穿到另一侧的回路。回路可以跨越全部 \(8\) 列、前 \(7\) 列、后 \(7\) 列，或中间 \(6\) 列；更窄的跨度会使外侧的一个中间方格没有接触牙签。固定两个端点后，每个内部的中间方格都可以独立选择把唯一一根牙签放在上边或下边，其余不自交回路随即唯一确定。因此四种情况分别给出 \(2^6,2^5,2^5\)，和 \(2^4\) 条回路。另有恰好两条不穿过中间条带的回路：完全沿顶部或完全沿底部延伸的水平长方形。所以总数为 \(2^6+2^5+2^5+2^4+2\) \(=64+32+32+16+2\) \(=146\)。所以正确答案是 C。

English

Each middle-row cell must touch exactly one toothpick. First consider loops that pass from one side of the middle strip to the other. The loop can span all \(8\) columns, the first \(7,\) the last \(7,\) or the middle \(6;\) a narrower span would leave an outer middle cell untouched. Once the two ends are fixed, each interior middle cell independently has its one toothpick on its top or bottom side, and the rest of the non-self-intersecting loop is forced. The four cases therefore contribute \(2^6,2^5,2^5,\) and \(2^4\) loops. There are also exactly two loops that do not cross the middle strip: the horizontal rectangle running entirely along the top or entirely along the bottom. Hence the total is \(2^6+2^5+2^5+2^4+2\) \(=64+32+32+16+2\) \(=146.\) Thus, C is the correct answer.
