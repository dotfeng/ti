1

一个大瓮里有 \(100\) 个球，其中 \(36\%\) 是红球，其余是蓝球。必须取出多少个蓝球，才能使瓮中红球的百分比变为 \(72\%\)？（不取出红球。）

A\(28\) B\(32\) C\(36\) D\(50\) E\(64\)

English

A large urn contains \(100\) balls, of which \(36\%\) are red and the rest are blue. How many of the blue balls must be removed so that the percentage of red balls in the urn will be \(72\%?\) (No red balls are to be removed.)

(A) \(28\)(B) \(32\)(C) \(36\)(D) \(50\)(E) \(64\)

2

Carl 在探索洞穴时发现一批石头：\(5\) 磅重的石头每块价值 $\(14\)，\(4\) 磅重的石头每块价值 $\(11\)，\(1\) 磅重的石头每块价值 $\(2\)。每种大小至少有 \(20\) 块。他最多能携带 \(18\) 磅。按美元计，他最多能从洞穴中带出价值多少的石头？

A\(48\) B\(49\) C\(50\) D\(51\) E\(52\)

English

While exploring a cave, Carl comes across a collection of \(5\)-pound rocks worth $\(14\) each, \(4\)-pound rocks worth $\(11\) each, and \(1\)-pound rocks worth $\(2\) each. There are at least \(20\) of each size. He can carry at most \(18\) pounds. What is the maximum value, in dollars, of the rocks he can carry out of the cave?

(A) \(48\)(B) \(49\)(C) \(50\)(D) \(51\)(E) \(52\)

3

English

(A) \(3\)(B) \(6\)(C) \(12\)(D) \(18\)(E) \(24\)

4

A\((0, 4)\) B\((4, 5)\) C\((4, 6)\) D\((5, 6)\) E\((5, \infty)\)

English

Alice, Bob, and Charlie were on a hike and were wondering how far away the nearest town was. When Alice said, “We are at least \(6\) miles away,” Bob replied, “We are at most \(5\) miles away.” Charlie then remarked, “Actually the nearest town is at most \(4\) miles away.” It turned out that none of the three statements was true. Let \(d\) be the distance in miles to the nearest town. Which of the following intervals is the set of all possible values of \(d?\)

(A) \((0, 4)\)(B) \((4, 5)\)(C) \((4, 6)\)(D) \((5, 6)\)(E) \((5, \infty)\)

选项 A-D 中每个多项式都没有正根，且恰有一个负根，位于 \((-1, 0)\)（在 \(0\) 处为正，在 \(-1\) 处为负），并且在该区间递增。在区间 \((-1, 0)\) 上，\(x^{19} \gt x^{17}\) 且 \(x^{13} \gt x^{11}\)。因此在这个区间的每一点上，A、C、D 的取值都大于 B，于是它们与 \(0\) 的交点都比 B 更靠左。所以在 A 到 D 中，B 的根最大。

一次的选项 E 的根为 \(e=-\tfrac{2018}{2019}\)。因为 \(\tfrac{2018}{2019}\gt\tfrac9{10}\)，所以 \(e^{17}+2018e^{11}+1\) \(\lt-2018\left(\tfrac9{10}\right)^{11}+1\) \(\lt0\)。由于选项 B 的多项式单调递增，且在 \(e\) 处取负值，它的根在 E 的根的右侧。因此 B 的实根最大。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Each polynomial in choices A–D has no positive root and exactly one negative root, which lies in \((-1, 0)\) (it is positive at \(0\) and negative at \(-1\)) and is increasing there. On the interval \((-1, 0),\) \(x^{19} \gt x^{17}\) and \(x^{13} \gt x^{11}.\) Thus each of A, C, and D has a larger value than B at every point of this interval, so each crosses \(0\) to the left of B. Therefore B has the greatest root among A–D.

The linear choice E has root \(e=-\tfrac{2018}{2019}.\) Since \(\tfrac{2018}{2019}\gt\tfrac9{10},\) we have \(e^{17}+2018e^{11}+1\) \(\lt-2018\left(\tfrac9{10}\right)^{11}+1\) \(\lt0.\) Because B is increasing and is negative at \(e,\) its root lies to the right of E’s. Hence B has the greatest real root.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

22 答案 A

写 \(z = a + bi\)，由 \((a + bi)^2 = 4 + 4\sqrt{15}\,i\) 得 \(a^2 - b^2 = 4\) 和 \(2ab = 4\sqrt{15}\)。因此 \(a^4 - 4a^2 - 60 = 0\)，即 \((a^2 - 10)(a^2 + 6) = 0\)，得 \(a = \pm\sqrt{10}\)，\(b = \pm\sqrt{6}\)。第一个方程给出的顶点为 \(\pm(\sqrt{10} + \sqrt{6}\,i)\)。对 \(z^2 = 2 + 2\sqrt{3}\,i\) 用同样方法得到 \(\pm(\sqrt{3} + i)\)。

对 \((\sqrt{10}, \sqrt{6})\)，\((\sqrt{3}, 1)\)，\((-\sqrt{10}, -\sqrt{6})\)，\((-\sqrt{3}, -1)\) 使用鞋带公式，面积为 \(6\sqrt{2} - 2\sqrt{10}\)。因此 \(p + q + r + s\) \(= 6 + 2 + 2 + 10\) \(= 20\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Writing \(z = a + bi\) with \((a + bi)^2 = 4 + 4\sqrt{15}\,i\) gives \(a^2 - b^2 = 4\) and \(2ab = 4\sqrt{15}.\) Then \(a^4 - 4a^2 - 60 = 0,\) so \((a^2 - 10)(a^2 + 6) = 0,\) yielding \(a = \pm\sqrt{10},\) \(b = \pm\sqrt{6}.\) The vertices from the first equation are \(\pm(\sqrt{10} + \sqrt{6}\,i).\) The same method on \(z^2 = 2 + 2\sqrt{3}\,i\) gives \(\pm(\sqrt{3} + i).\)

Applying the shoelace formula to \((\sqrt{10}, \sqrt{6}),\) \((\sqrt{3}, 1),\) \((-\sqrt{10}, -\sqrt{6}),\) \((-\sqrt{3}, -1)\) gives area \(6\sqrt{2} - 2\sqrt{10}.\) Thus \(p + q + r + s\) \(= 6 + 2 + 2 + 10\) \(= 20.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

23 答案 E

将 \(PN\) 经过 \(N\) 延长到 \(Q\)，使 \(PN = NQ\)。 因为 \(N\) 是 \(UG\) 和 \(PQ\) 的中点，四边形 \(UPGQ\) 是平行四边形，所以 \(GQ \parallel PT\) 且 \(GQ = PU = 1 = AG\)。 因此 \(\angle QGA\) \(= 180^\circ - \angle T\) \(= \angle P + \angle A\) \(= 36^\circ + 56^\circ = 92^\circ\)， 等腰三角形 \(QGA\) 给出 \(\angle QAG = \tfrac12(180^\circ - 92^\circ) = 44^\circ\)。

因为 \(M, N\) 是中点，\(MN\) 是 \(\triangle QPA\) 的中位线，所以 \(MN \parallel AQ\)，并且 \[ \begin{aligned} \angle NMP &= \angle QAP \\ &= \angle QAG + \angle GAP \\ &= 44^\circ + 56^\circ = 100^\circ\text{。} \end{aligned} \] 因此直线 \(MN\) 和 \(PA\) 所成锐角为 \(180^\circ - 100^\circ = 80^\circ\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Extend \(PN\) through \(N\) to \(Q\) with \(PN = NQ.\) Since \(N\) is the midpoint of \(UG\) and of \(PQ,\) the quadrilateral \(UPGQ\) is a parallelogram, so \(GQ \parallel PT\) and \(GQ = PU = 1 = AG.\) Then \(\angle QGA\) \(= 180^\circ - \angle T\) \(= \angle P + \angle A\) \(= 36^\circ + 56^\circ = 92^\circ,\) and the isosceles triangle \(QGA\) gives \(\angle QAG = \tfrac12(180^\circ - 92^\circ) = 44^\circ.\)

Because \(M, N\) are midpoints, \(MN\) is a midline of \(\triangle QPA,\) so \(MN \parallel AQ\) and \[ \begin{aligned} \angle NMP &= \angle QAP \\ &= \angle QAG + \angle GAP \\ &= 44^\circ + 56^\circ = 100^\circ. \end{aligned} \] The acute angle between line \(MN\) and \(PA\) is therefore \(180^\circ - 100^\circ = 80^\circ.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

24 答案 B

若 \(c \le \tfrac12\)，Carol 一定小于 Bob，所以她只有在 Alice 小于 \(c\) 时获胜，概率为 \(c \le \tfrac12\)。若 \(c \ge \tfrac23\)，她获胜的概率为 \(1 - c \le \tfrac13\)。这两种情况都不超过 \(\tfrac12\)。

对于 \(\tfrac12 \lt c \lt \tfrac23\)，Bob 的数大于 \(c\) 的概率是 \(\frac{\frac{2}{3} - c}{\frac{2}{3} - \frac{1}{2}} = 4 - 6c\)，所以 Carol 大于 Alice 且小于 Bob 的概率是 \(c(4 - 6c)\)；反向排序的概率是 \((1 - c)(6c - 3)\)。相加得\[ \begin{aligned} &c(4 - 6c) + (1 - c)(6c - 3) \\ &= -12c^2 + 13c - 3 \end{aligned}\text{。}\] 这个开口向下的抛物线在 \(c = \tfrac{13}{24}\) 处取到最大值，该点位于 \(\left(\tfrac12, \tfrac23\right)\) 内，且最大值超过 \(\tfrac12\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

If \(c \le \tfrac12,\) Carol beats Bob automatically, so she wins only if Alice is below \(c,\) probability \(c \le \tfrac12.\) If \(c \ge \tfrac23,\) she wins with probability \(1 - c \le \tfrac13.\) Neither case exceeds \(\tfrac12.\)

For \(\tfrac12 \lt c \lt \tfrac23,\) the chance Bob’s number exceeds \(c\) is \(\frac{\frac{2}{3} - c}{\frac{2}{3} - \frac{1}{2}} = 4 - 6c,\) so the probability Carol is above Alice and below Bob is \(c(4 - 6c);\) the reverse ordering has probability \((1 - c)(6c - 3).\) Adding, \[ \begin{aligned} &c(4 - 6c) + (1 - c)(6c - 3) \\ &= -12c^2 + 13c - 3. \end{aligned} \] This downward parabola is maximized at \(c = \tfrac{13}{24},\) which lies in \(\left(\tfrac12, \tfrac23\right),\) and its value exceeds \(\tfrac12.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

25 答案 D

利用 \(A_n = a \cdot \tfrac{10^n - 1}{9}\)、\(B_n = b \cdot \tfrac{10^n - 1}{9}\) 以及 \(C_n = c \cdot \tfrac{10^{2n} - 1}{9}\)，方程 \(C_n - B_n = A_n^2\) 在除以 \(10^n - 1\) 并清分母后变为 \[ (9c - a^2) \cdot 10^n = 9b - 9c - a^2\text{。} \] 若它要对两个不同的 \(n\) 成立，则 \(10^n\) 的系数必须为零，所以 \(9c = a^2\)，进而 \(9b - 9c - a^2 = 0\)。

于是 \(c = \tfrac{a^2}{9}\) 且 \(b = 2c\)。 因此 \(a \in \{3, 6, 9\}\)，对应 \(c \in \{1, 4, 9\}\) 和 \(b \in \{2, 8, 18\}\)； 情况 \(b = 18\) 不是一个数字。有效三元组为 \((a, b, c) = (3, 2, 1)\) 和 \((6, 8, 4)\)， 而且确实有 \(4444 - 88 = 4356 = 66^2\)。 较大的数字和为 \(6 + 8 + 4 = 18\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Using \(A_n = a \cdot \tfrac{10^n - 1}{9},\) \(B_n = b \cdot \tfrac{10^n - 1}{9},\) and \(C_n = c \cdot \tfrac{10^{2n} - 1}{9},\) the equation \(C_n - B_n = A_n^2\) becomes, after dividing by \(10^n - 1\) and clearing fractions, \[ (9c - a^2) \cdot 10^n = 9b - 9c - a^2. \] For this to hold at two different \(n,\) the coefficient of \(10^n\) must be zero, so \(9c = a^2\) and hence \(9b - 9c - a^2 = 0.\)

Then \(c = \tfrac{a^2}{9}\) and \(b = 2c.\) So \(a \in \{3, 6, 9\}\) with \(c \in \{1, 4, 9\}\) and \(b \in \{2, 8, 18\};\) the case \(b = 18\) is not a digit. The valid triples are \((a, b, c) = (3, 2, 1)\) and \((6, 8, 4),\) and indeed \(4444 - 88 = 4356 = 66^2.\) The greater digit sum is \(6 + 8 + 4 = 18.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.
