对整数 \(a\)、\(b\)、\(c\)，定义 \(\boxed{a,b,c}\) 表示 \(a^b-b^c+c^a\)。那么 \(\boxed{1,-1,2}\) 等于

A\(-4\) B\(-2\) C\(0\) D\(2\) E\(4\)

English

For integers \(a,\) \(b,\) and \(c,\) define \(\boxed{a,b,c}\) to mean \(a^b-b^c+c^a.\) Then \(\boxed{1,-1,2}\) equals

(A) \(-4\)(B) \(-2\)(C) \(0\)(D) \(2\)(E) \(4\)

A\(50^\circ\) B\(55^\circ\) C\(60^\circ\) D\(65^\circ\) E\(70^\circ\)

English

In \(\triangle ABC,\) \(\angle A=55^\circ,\) \(\angle C=75^\circ,\) \(D\) is on side \(\overline{AB}\) and \(E\) is on side \(\overline{BC}.\) If \(DB=BE,\) then \(\angle BED=\)

(A) \(50^\circ\)(B) \(55^\circ\)(C) \(60^\circ\)(D) \(65^\circ\)(E) \(70^\circ\)

4

定义运算“\(\circ\)”为 \(x\circ y=4x-3y+xy\)，其中 \(x\) 和 \(y\) 为任意实数。有多少个实数 \(y\) 满足 \(3\circ y=12\)？

A\(0\) B\(1\) C\(3\) D\(4\) E多于 \(4\) 个

English

Define the operation “\(\circ\)” by \(x\circ y=4x-3y+xy,\) for all real numbers \(x\) and \(y.\) For how many real numbers \(y\) does \(3\circ y=12?\)

(A) \(0\)(B) \(1\)(C) \(3\)(D) \(4\)(E) more than \(4\)

5

去年，一辆自行车售价 \(\$160\)，一个自行车头盔售价 \(\$40\)。今年，自行车价格上涨了 \(5\%\)，头盔价格上涨了 \(10\%\)。自行车与头盔的总价上涨了百分之多少？

A\(6\%\) B\(7\%\) C\(7.5\%\) D\(8\%\) E\(15\%\)

English

Last year a bicycle cost \(\$160\) and a cycling helmet cost \(\$40.\) This year the cost of the bicycle increased by \(5\%,\) and the cost of the helmet increased by \(10\%.\) The percent increase in the combined cost of the bicycle and the helmet is

(A) \(6\%\)(B) \(7\%\)(C) \(7.5\%\)(D) \(8\%\)(E) \(15\%\)

7

符号 \(R_k\) 表示一个十进制整数，它由连续 \(k\) 个数字一组成。例如，\(R_3=111\)、\(R_5=11111\) 等。用 \(R_{24}\) 除以 \(R_4\)，所得商 \(Q=\frac{R_{24}}{R_4}\) 是一个十进制表示中只含一和零的整数。\(Q\) 中零的个数是

A\(10\) B\(11\) C\(12\) D\(13\) E\(15\)

English

The symbol \(R_k\) stands for an integer whose base-ten representation is a sequence of \(k\) ones. For example, \(R_3=111,\) \(R_5=11111,\) etc. When \(R_{24}\) is divided by \(R_4,\) the quotient \(Q=\frac{R_{24}}{R_4}\) is an integer whose base-ten representation is a sequence containing only ones and zeros. The number of zeros in \(Q\) is

(A) \(10\)(B) \(11\)(C) \(12\)(D) \(13\)(E) \(15\)

8

设 \(C_1\) 和 \(C_2\) 是同一平面内两个半径为 \(1\) 且互相相切的圆。该平面内有多少个半径为 \(3\) 且同时与 \(C_1\) 和 \(C_2\) 相切的圆？

A\(2\) B\(4\) C\(5\) D\(6\) E\(8\)

English

Let \(C_1\) and \(C_2\) be circles of radius \(1\) that are in the same plane and tangent to each other. How many circles of radius \(3\) are in this plane and tangent to both \(C_1\) and \(C_2?\)

(A) \(2\)(B) \(4\)(C) \(5\)(D) \(6\)(E) \(8\)

9

国家 \(A\) 的人口占世界人口的 \(c\%\)，拥有世界财富的 \(d\%\)。国家 \(B\) 的人口占世界人口的 \(e\%\)，拥有世界财富的 \(f\%\)。假设 \(A\) 国公民平均分享 \(A\) 国财富，\(B\) 国公民也平均分享 \(B\) 国财富。求 \(A\) 国一名公民的财富与 \(B\) 国一名公民的财富之比。

A\(\frac{cd}{ef}\) B\(\frac{ce}{df}\) C\(\frac{cf}{de}\) D\(\frac{de}{cf}\) E\(\frac{df}{ce}\)

English

Country \(A\) has \(c\%\) of the world’s population and owns \(d\%\) of the world’s wealth. Country \(B\) has \(e\%\) of the world’s population and \(f\%\) of its wealth. Assume that the citizens of \(A\) share the wealth of \(A\) equally, and assume that those of \(B\) share the wealth of \(B\) equally. Find the ratio of the wealth of a citizen of \(A\) to the wealth of a citizen of \(B.\)

(A) \(\frac{cd}{ef}\)(B) \(\frac{ce}{df}\)(C) \(\frac{cf}{de}\)(D) \(\frac{de}{cf}\)(E) \(\frac{df}{ce}\)

10

设 \(r\) 为将 \(a^b\) 的底数和指数都变为原来的三倍后所得的数，其中 \(a\)、\(b\gt0\)。若 \(r\) 等于 \(a^b\) 与 \(x^b\) 的乘积，其中 \(x\gt0\)，则 \(x=\)

A\(3\) B\(3a^2\) C\(27a^2\) D\(2a^{3b}\) E\(3a^{2b}\)

English

Let \(r\) be the number that results when both the base and the exponent of \(a^b\) are tripled, where \(a,\) \(b\gt0.\) If \(r\) equals the product of \(a^b\) and \(x^b\) where \(x\gt0,\) then \(x=\)

(A) \(3\)(B) \(3a^2\)(C) \(27a^2\)(D) \(2a^{3b}\)(E) \(3a^{2b}\)

13

一个周长为 \(20\) 的正方形内接于一个周长为 \(28\) 的正方形。内正方形的一个顶点与外正方形的一个顶点之间的最大距离是多少？

14

16

(A) \(0\)(B) \(1\)(C) \(2\)(D) \(3\)(E) \(4\)

17

艾米在一个正方形钟面上画了飞镖盘，并以各整点方向为分界线，如图所示。设 \(t\) 为八个三角形区域之一的面积，例如 \(12\) 点方向与 \(1\) 点方向之间的区域；设 \(q\) 为四个角部四边形区域之一的面积，例如 \(1\) 点方向与 \(2\) 点方向之间的区域。则 \(\frac qt=\)

A\(2\sqrt3-2\) B\(\frac32\) C\(\frac{\sqrt5+1}{2}\) D\(\sqrt3\) E\(2\)

Amy painted a dart board over a square clock face using the “hour positions” as boundaries. [See figure.] If \(t\) is the area of one of the eight triangular regions such as that between \(12\) o’clock and \(1\) o’clock, and \(q\) is the area of one of the four corner quadrilaterals such as that between \(1\) o’clock and \(2\) o’clock, then \(\frac qt=\)

(A) \(2\sqrt3-2\)(B) \(\frac32\)(C) \(\frac{\sqrt5+1}{2}\)(D) \(\sqrt3\)(E) \(2\)

18

阿尔和芭布在同一天开始新工作。阿尔的工作安排是工作 \(3\) 天后休息 \(1\) 天。芭布的安排是工作 \(7\) 天后休息 \(3\) 天。在最初的 \(1000\) 天中，有多少天两人同时休息？

A\(48\) B\(50\) C\(72\) D\(75\) E\(100\)

English

Al and Barb start their new jobs on the same day. Al’s schedule is \(3\) work-days followed by \(1\) rest-day. Barb’s schedule is \(7\) work-days followed by \(3\) rest-days. On how many of their first \(1000\) days do both have rest-days on the same day?

(A) \(48\)(B) \(50\)(C) \(72\)(D) \(75\)(E) \(100\)

19

20

考虑方程 \(10z^2-3iz-k=0\)，其中 \(z\) 是复变量且 \(i^2=-1\)。下列哪个说法正确？

A对所有正实数 \(k\)，两个根都是纯虚数。 B对所有负实数 \(k\)，两个根都是纯虚数。 C对所有纯虚数 \(k\)，两个根都是实有理数。 D对所有纯虚数 \(k\)，两个根都是实无理数。 E对所有复数 \(k\)，两个根都不是实数。

English

Consider the equation \(10z^2-3iz-k=0,\) where \(z\) is a complex variable and \(i^2=-1.\) Which of the following statements is true?

(A) For all positive real numbers \(k,\) both roots are pure imaginary.(B) For all negative real numbers \(k,\) both roots are pure imaginary.(C) For all pure imaginary numbers \(k,\) both roots are real and rational.(D) For all pure imaginary numbers \(k,\) both roots are real and irrational.(E) For all complex numbers \(k,\) neither root is real.

22

如图所示排列二十个正方体积木。首先将 \(10\) 个积木排成三角形；再将由 \(6\) 个积木排成的三角形层居中放在这 \(10\) 个积木上；然后将由 \(3\) 个积木排成的三角形层居中放在这 \(6\) 个积木上；最后在第三层顶端中央放一个积木。底层积木以某种顺序编号为 \(1\) 到 \(10\)。第 \(2\)、\(3\)、\(4\) 层的每个积木所标的数，等于支撑它的三个积木所标数之和。求顶层积木可能标上的最小数。

A\(55\) B\(83\) C\(114\) D\(137\) E\(144\)

English

Twenty cubical blocks are arranged as shown. First, \(10\) are arranged in a triangular pattern; then a layer of \(6,\) arranged in a triangular pattern, is centered on the \(10;\) then a layer of \(3,\) arranged in a triangular pattern, is centered on the \(6;\) and finally one block is centered on top of the third layer. The blocks in the bottom layer are numbered \(1\) through \(10\) in some order. Each block in layers \(2,\) \(3\) and \(4\) is assigned the number which is the sum of the numbers assigned to the three blocks on which it rests. Find the smallest possible number which could be assigned to the top block.

(A) \(55\)(B) \(83\)(C) \(114\)(D) \(137\)(E) \(144\)

24

一个盒子里有 \(3\) 枚有光泽的便士和 \(4\) 枚无光泽的便士。从盒中逐枚随机抽取便士，抽出后不放回。直到第三枚有光泽的便士出现需要抽取超过四次的概率为 \(\frac{a}{b}\)。若 \(\frac{a}{b}\) 是最简分数，则 \(a+b=\)

25

There are

(A) exactly \(2\) such triangles.(B) exactly \(3\) such triangles.(C) exactly \(7\) such triangles.(D) exactly \(15\) such triangles.(E) more than \(15\) such triangles.

若棱长为 \(a\)、\(b\)、\(c\)，则三条表面对角线长度的平方为 \(a^2+b^2\)、\(a^2+c^2\)、\(b^2+c^2\)。对按大小排列的对角线 \(p\le q\le r\)，有 \[ a^2=\frac{p^2+q^2-r^2}{2}\text{，}\]因此必须满足 \(r^2\lt p^2+q^2\)；类似公式也说明这个条件充分。只有 \(\{4,5,7\}\) 不满足，因为 \(7^2=49\gt16+25=41\)。所以正确答案是 B。

English

If the edge lengths are \(a,\) \(b,\) \(c,\) then the three face-diagonal squares are \(a^2+b^2,\) \(a^2+c^2,\) \(b^2+c^2.\) For sorted diagonals \(p\le q\le r,\) \[ a^2=\frac{p^2+q^2-r^2}{2}, \] so necessarily \(r^2\lt p^2+q^2;\) the analogous formulas show this is also sufficient. Only \(\{4,5,7\}\) fails, since \(7^2=49\gt16+25=41.\) Thus the correct answer is B.

30 答案 D

递推关系为 \(x_n=\{2x_{n-1}\}\)，所以 \(x_5=\{32x_0\}\)。等式 \(x_5=x_0\) 要求 \[ 32x_0-x_0=\lfloor32x_0\rfloor\text{，}\]因而 \(31x_0\) 是整数。值 \(x_0=\frac{k}{31}\) 在 \(k=0\)、\(1\)、\(\ldots\)、\(30\) 时都满足递推关系并位于 \([0,1)\) 内。共有 \(31\) 个值。因此正确答案是 D。

English

The recurrence is \(x_n=\{2x_{n-1}\},\) so \(x_5=\{32x_0\}.\) The equality \(x_5=x_0\) requires \[ 32x_0-x_0=\lfloor32x_0\rfloor, \] hence \(31x_0\) is an integer. The values \(x_0=\frac{k}{31}\) for \(k=0,\) \(1,\) \(\ldots,\) \(30\) all satisfy the recurrence and lie in \([0,1).\) There are \(31\) values. Thus the correct answer is D.
