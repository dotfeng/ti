逐题解析

1 答案 B

只需考虑各数的个位。模 \(10\) 计算，原式为 \[ \begin{gathered} 2-2-2-2-2-2=-8,\\ -8\equiv2\pmod{10} \end{gathered}\text{。}\]因此原式的个位数字是 \(2\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Only the ones digits matter. Modulo \(10,\) the expression is \[ \begin{gathered} 2-2-2-2-2-2=-8,\\ -8\equiv2\pmod{10}. \end{gathered} \] Therefore its ones digit is \(2.\)

Thus, B is the correct answer.

2 答案 C

提出公因数 \(11\) 后，\(\dfrac{44}{11}\) 化为 \(4\)，\(\dfrac{110}{44}\) 化为 \(\dfrac{10}{4} = \dfrac{5}{2} = 2.5\)，而 \(\dfrac{44}{1100}\) 化为 \(\dfrac{4}{100} = 0.04\)。因此 \(4 + 2.5 + 0.04 = 6.54\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

We can simplify the fractions by taking out the common factor \(11\): \( \dfrac{44}{11} \) simplifies to \( 4 \), \( \dfrac{110}{44} \) simplifies to \( \dfrac{10}{4} = \dfrac{5}{2} = 2.5 \), and \( \dfrac{44}{1100} \) simplifies to \( \dfrac{4}{100} = 0.04 \). Therefore, we have \( 4 + 2.5 + 0.04 = 6.54.\)

Thus, C is the correct answer.

3 答案 E

可见阴影部分由 \(10\) 乘 \(10\) 的正方形中位于 \(9\) 乘 \(9\) 的正方形之外的部分，以及 \(7\) 乘 \(7\) 的正方形中位于 \(4\) 乘 \(4\) 的正方形之外的部分组成：\[ \begin{gathered} 10^2-9^2+7^2-4^2 \\ =100-81+49-16 \\ =52 \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The visible shaded region is the part inside the \(10\) by \(10\) square but outside the \(9\) by \(9\) square, together with the part inside the \(7\) by \(7\) square but outside the \(4\) by \(4\) square. Its area is \[ \begin{gathered} 10^2-9^2+7^2-4^2 \\ =100-81+49-16 \\ =52. \end{gathered} \]

Thus, E is the correct answer.

4 答案 E

要求 Yunji 漏掉的数，先求从 \(1\) 到 \(9\) 的总和，再求这个总和与比它小的最大完全平方数之差。从 \(1\) 到 \(9\) 的整数和为 \[ 1 + \ldots + 9 = \dfrac{9(9+1)}{2} = 45 \] 小于 \(45\) 的最大完全平方数是 \(36\)，所以漏掉的数是 \(45 - 36 = 9\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

To find the number that Yunji left out, we need to find the sum of the integers from \(1\) to \(9\) and find its difference with the largest perfect square below the sum. We can calculate the sum of the integers from \(1\) to \(9\) as follows: \[ 1 + \ldots + 9 = \dfrac{9(9+1)}{2} = 45 \] The largest perfect square less than \( 45 \) would be \( 36 \) and \( 45 - 36 = 9 \).

前 \(8\) 名乘客坐下后，还有 \(4\) 个空座分布在 \(12\) 个座位中，所以空座集合共有 \(\binom{12}{4}=495\) 种等可能情况。

用补集计数，即没有任何一排有两个相邻空座。在一排三个座位中，不含相邻空座的方案所含空座数可以是 \(0\)、\(1\) 或 \(2\)，对应的方案数分别是 \(1\)、\(3\) 和 \(1\)。因此，我们需要求下式中 \(x^4\) 的系数：\[ (1+3x+x^2)^4\text{。}\]这个系数是 \[ \begin{gathered} 3^4+4\cdot3\cdot3^2+\binom42 \\ =81+108+6 \\ =195 \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

因此至少有一排存在相邻空座的概率为 \[ \frac{495-195}{495}=\frac{20}{33}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

After the first \(8\) passengers sit, there are \(4\) empty seats among the \(12\) seats, so there are \(\binom{12}{4}=495\) equally likely sets of empty seats.

Count the complement, where no row has two adjacent empty seats. In a row of three seats, the possible empty-seat patterns with no adjacent empty seats have sizes \(0,\) \(1,\) and \(2,\) with \(1,\) \(3,\) and \(1\) choices respectively. So we need the coefficient of \(x^4\) in \[ (1+3x+x^2)^4. \] This coefficient is \[ \begin{gathered} 3^4+4\cdot3\cdot3^2+\binom42 \\ =81+108+6 \\ =195. \end{gathered} \]

Therefore the probability that at least one row has adjacent empty seats is \[ \frac{495-195}{495}=\frac{20}{33}. \]

Thus, C is the correct answer.
