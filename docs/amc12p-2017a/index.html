1

Pablo 给他的朋友们买冰棒。商店出售单支冰棒，每支 \(\$1\)；\(3\) 支装盒，每盒 \(\$2\)；以及 \(5\) 支装盒，每盒 \(\$3\)。Pablo 用 \(\$8\) 最多可以买多少支冰棒？

A\(8\) B\(11\) C\(12\) D\(13\) E\(15\)

English

Pablo buys popsicles for his friends. The store sells single popsicles for \(\$1\) each, \(3\)-popsicle boxes for \(\$2,\) and \(5\)-popsicle boxes for \(\$3.\) What is the greatest number of popsicles that Pablo can buy with \(\$8?\)

(A) \(8\)(B) \(11\)(C) \(12\)(D) \(13\)(E) \(15\)

2

A\(1\) B\(2\) C\(4\) D\(8\) E\(12\)

English

The sum of two nonzero real numbers is \(4\) times their product. What is the sum of the reciprocals of the two numbers?

(A) \(1\)(B) \(2\)(C) \(4\)(D) \(8\)(E) \(12\)

3

Carroll 老师承诺：即将到来的考试中，凡是把所有选择题都答对的人，都会在这次考试中得到 A。以下哪一句一定能由此在逻辑上推出？

A如果 Lewis 没有得到 A，那么他把所有选择题都答错了。 B如果 Lewis 没有得到 A，那么他至少答错了一道选择题。 C如果 Lewis 至少答错了一道选择题，那么他没有得到 A。 D如果 Lewis 得到了 A，那么他把所有选择题都答对了。 E如果 Lewis 得到了 A，那么他至少答对了一道选择题。

English

Ms. Carroll promised that anyone who got all the multiple choice questions right on the upcoming exam would receive an A on the exam. Which one of these statements necessarily follows logically?

(A) If Lewis did not receive an A, then he got all of the multiple choice questions wrong.(B) If Lewis did not receive an A, then he got at least one of the multiple choice questions wrong.(C) If Lewis got at least one of the multiple choice questions wrong, then he did not receive an A.(D) If Lewis received an A, then he got all of the multiple choice questions right.(E) If Lewis received an A, then he got at least one of the multiple choice questions right.

4

English

Jerry and Silvia wanted to go from the southwest corner of a square field to the northeast corner. Jerry walked due east and then due north to reach the goal, but Silvia headed northeast and reached the goal walking in a straight line. Which of the following is closest to how much shorter Silvia’s trip was, compared to Jerry’s trip?

(A) \(30\%\)(B) \(40\%\)(C) \(50\%\)(D) \(60\%\)(E) \(70\%\)

5

6

A\(16\) B\(17\) C\(18\) D\(19\) E\(20\)

She then wants to choose a fourth rod that she can put with these three to form a quadrilateral with positive area. How many of the remaining rods can she choose as the fourth rod?

(A) \(16\)(B) \(17\)(C) \(18\)(D) \(19\)(E) \(20\)

7

在正整数上递归定义函数：\(f(1)=2\)，当 \(n\) 为偶数时 \(f(n)=f(n-1)+1\)，当 \(n\) 为大于 \(1\) 的奇数时 \(f(n)=f(n-2)+2\)。\(f(2017)\) 是多少？

A\(2017\) B\(2018\) C\(4034\) D\(4035\) E\(4036\)

English

Define a function on the positive integers recursively by \(f(1)=2,\) \(f(n)=f(n-1)+1\) if \(n\) is even, and \(f(n)=f(n-2)+2\) if \(n\) is odd and greater than \(1.\) What is \(f(2017)?\)

(A) \(2017\)(B) \(2018\)(C) \(4034\)(D) \(4035\)(E) \(4036\)

8

9

12

有 \(10\) 匹马，名字分别为 Horse \(1\)、Horse \(2\)、\(\ldots\)、Horse \(10\)。它们的名字来自它们绕圆形跑道跑一圈所需的分钟数：Horse \(k\) 恰好用 \(k\) 分钟跑一圈。在时刻 \(0\)，所有马都在跑道起点。它们沿同一方向开始奔跑，并以各自恒定速度一直绕跑道跑。所有 \(10\) 匹马再次同时到达起点的最小正时间（分钟）为 \(S\gt0\)，且 \(S=2520\)。设 \(T\gt0\) 是至少 \(5\) 匹马再次同时在起点的最小正时间（分钟）。\(T\) 的各位数字之和是多少？

A\(2\) B\(3\) C\(4\) D\(5\) E\(6\)

English

There are \(10\) horses, named Horse \(1,\) Horse \(2,\) \(\ldots,\) Horse \(10.\) They get their names from how many minutes it takes them to run one lap around a circular race track: Horse \(k\) runs one lap in exactly \(k\) minutes. At time \(0\) all the horses are together at the starting point on the track. The horses start running in the same direction, and they keep running around the circular track at their constant speeds. The least time \(S\gt0,\) in minutes, at which all \(10\) horses will again simultaneously be at the starting point is \(S=2520.\) Let \(T\gt0\) be the least time, in minutes, such that at least \(5\) of the horses are again at the starting point. What is the sum of the digits of \(T?\)

(A) \(2\)(B) \(3\)(C) \(4\)(D) \(5\)(E) \(6\)

13

Sharon 通常以恒定速度从她家开车到她母亲家需要 \(180\) 分钟。有一天，Sharon 以通常速度出发，但开了全程的 \(\dfrac{1}{3}\) 后遇到严重暴风雪，于是把速度降低了每小时 \(20\) 英里。这次行程总共用了 \(276\) 分钟。从 Sharon 家到她母亲家有多少英里？

A\(132\) B\(135\) C\(138\) D\(141\) E\(144\)

English

Driving at a constant speed, Sharon usually takes \(180\) minutes to drive from her house to her mother’s house. One day Sharon begins the drive at her usual speed, but after driving \(\dfrac{1}{3}\) of the way, she hits a bad snowstorm and reduces her speed by \(20\) miles per hour. This time the trip takes her a total of \(276\) minutes. How many miles is the drive from Sharon’s house to her mother’s house?

(A) \(132\)(B) \(135\)(C) \(138\)(D) \(141\)(E) \(144\)

16

19

一个边长为 \(x\) 的正方形内接于边长为 \(3\)，\(4\)，\(5\) 的直角三角形中，使得正方形的一个顶点与三角形的直角顶点重合。另一个边长为 \(y\) 的正方形内接于另一个边长为 \(3\)，\(4\)，\(5\) 的直角三角形中，使得正方形的一条边落在三角形的斜边上。\(\dfrac{x}{y}\) 是多少？

21

集合 \(S\) 按如下方式构造。开始时，\(S=\{0,10\}\)。之后尽可能重复下面的操作：如果 \(x\) 是某个多项式 \(a_nx^n+a_{n-1}x^{n-1}\) \(+\cdots+a_1x+a_0\) 的整数根，其中 \(n\ge1\) 且所有系数 \(a_i\) 都是 \(S\) 中的元素，那么把 \(x\) 加入 \(S\)。当不能再向 \(S\) 加入新元素时，\(S\) 有多少个元素？

English

A set \(S\) is constructed as follows. To begin, \(S=\{0,10\}.\) Repeatedly, as long as possible, if \(x\) is an integer root of some polynomial \(a_nx^n+a_{n-1}x^{n-1}\) \(+\cdots+a_1x+a_0\) for some \(n\ge1,\) all of whose coefficients \(a_i\) are elements of \(S,\) then \(x\) is put into \(S.\) When no more elements can be added to \(S,\) how many elements does \(S\) have?

(A) \(4\)(B) \(5\)(C) \(7\)(D) \(9\)(E) \(11\)

22

在笛卡尔坐标平面中画一个正方形，顶点为 \((2,2)\)、\((-2,2)\)、\((-2,-2)\) 和 \((2,-2)\)。一个粒子从 \((0,0)\) 出发。每一秒，它等概率地移动到离当前位置最近的八个格点（坐标均为整数的点）之一，且与之前的移动相互独立。换句话说，粒子从 \((x,y)\) 移动到 \((x,y+1)\)、\((x+1,y+1)\)、\((x+1,y)\)、\((x+1,y-1)\)、\((x,y-1)\)、\((x-1,y-1)\)、\((x-1,y)\) 或 \((x-1,y+1)\) 中每一点的概率都是 \(\dfrac{1}{8}\)。粒子最终会第一次碰到这个正方形，碰到的位置要么是正方形的 \(4\) 个顶点之一，要么是某条边内部的 \(12\) 个格点之一。它碰到顶点而不是边内部点的概率为 \(\dfrac{m}{n}\)，其中 \(m\) 和 \(n\) 是互质正整数。\(m+n\) 是多少？

A\(4\) B\(5\) C\(7\) D\(15\) E\(39\)

English

A square is drawn in the Cartesian coordinate plane with vertices at \((2,2),\) \((-2,2),\) \((-2,-2),\) and \((2,-2).\) A particle starts at \((0,0).\) Every second it moves with equal probability to one of the eight lattice points (points with integer coordinates) closest to its current position, independently of its previous moves. In other words, the probability is \(\dfrac{1}{8}\) that the particle will move from \((x,y)\) to each of \((x,y+1),\) \((x+1,y+1),\) \((x+1,y),\) \((x+1,y-1),\) \((x,y-1),\) \((x-1,y-1),\) \((x-1,y),\) or \((x-1,y+1).\) The particle will eventually hit the square for the first time, either at one of the \(4\) corners of the square or at one of the \(12\) lattice points in the interior of one of the sides of the square. The probability that it will hit at a corner rather than at an interior point of a side is \(\dfrac{m}{n},\) where \(m\) and \(n\) are relatively prime positive integers. What is \(m+n?\)

(A) \(4\)(B) \(5\)(C) \(7\)(D) \(15\)(E) \(39\)

23

(A) \(-9009\)(B) \(-8008\)(C) \(-7007\)(D) \(-6006\)(E) \(-5005\)

24

English

Quadrilateral \(ABCD\) is inscribed in circle \(O\) and has sides \(AB=3,\) \(BC=2,\) \(CD=6,\) and \(DA=8.\) Let \(X\) and \(Y\) be points on \(BD\) such that \(\dfrac{DX}{BD}=\dfrac{1}{4}\) and \(\dfrac{BY}{BD}=\dfrac{11}{36}.\) Let \(E\) be the intersection of line \(AX\) and the line through \(Y\) parallel to \(AD.\) Let \(F\) be the intersection of line \(CX\) and the line through \(E\) parallel to \(AC.\) Let \(G\) be the point on circle \(O\) other than \(C\) that lies on line \(CX.\) What is \(XF\cdot XG?\)

(A) \(17\)(B) \(\dfrac{59-5\sqrt2}{3}\)(C) \(\dfrac{91-12\sqrt3}{4}\)(D) \(\dfrac{67-10\sqrt2}{3}\)(E) \(18\)

Let \(u=\log_b a.\) Since \(\log_b(a^{2017})=2017\log_b a,\) the equation is \(u^{2017}=2017u,\) so \(u=0\) or \(u^{2016}=2017.\)

If \(u=0,\) then \(a=1,\) valid for every one of the \(199\) bases. If \(u^{2016}=2017,\) then \(u=\pm2017^{\frac{1}{2016}},\) giving \(2\) values of \(a\) for each base, i.e. \(2\cdot199=398\) pairs.

In total there are \(199+398=597\) ordered pairs.

Thus, the correct answer is E.

21 答案 D

使用多项式 \(10x+10\)，根 \(-1\) 进入 \(S\)。然后 \(1\) 作为 \(-x^{10}-x^9-\cdots-x+10\) 的根进入，而 \(-10\) 由 \(x+10\) 得到。

现在 \(x^3+x-10\) 有根 \(2\)，而 \(x+2\) 给出 \(-2\)；接着 \(2x-10\) 和 \(2x+10\) 给出 \(\pm5\)。此时 \(S=\{0,\pm1,\pm2,\pm5,\pm10\}\)。

不会再出现其他整数。用归纳法，\(S\) 中每个非零元素都整除 \(10\)。如果规则中使用的多项式常数项为 \(0\)，先提出最高可能次幂的 \(x\)；任何非零根就成为一个新多项式的根，而其常数项是原多项式的第一个非零系数。由有理根定理，该根整除这个系数；归纳假设说明该系数整除 \(10\)。因此 \(S\) 有 \(9\) 个元素。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

Using \(10x+10,\) the root \(-1\) enters \(S.\) Then \(1\) enters as a root of \(-x^{10}-x^9-\cdots-x+10,\) and \(-10\) enters from \(x+10.\)

Now \(x^3+x-10\) has root \(2,\) and \(x+2\) gives \(-2;\) then \(2x-10\) and \(2x+10\) give \(\pm5.\) At this point \(S=\{0,\pm1,\pm2,\pm5,\pm10\}.\)

No further integer can appear. Inductively, every nonzero member of \(S\) divides \(10.\) If a polynomial used in the rule has constant term \(0,\) factor out the largest possible power of \(x;\) any nonzero root is then a root of a polynomial whose constant term is the first nonzero original coefficient. The Rational Root Theorem shows that the root divides this coefficient, which by the inductive hypothesis divides \(10.\) So \(S\) has \(9\) elements.

Thus, the correct answer is D.

22 答案 E

由对称性，把相关内部点分成三类：\(C=\{(0,0)\}\)、“轴向”点 \(A=\{(\pm1,0),(0,\pm1)\}\) 和“对角”点 \(I=\{(\pm1,\pm1)\}\)。设从 \(A,C,I\) 三类点出发最终碰到顶点的概率分别为 \(a,c,i\)。

读出转移概率（例如，\(A\) 中一点以概率 \(\tfrac28\) 到 \(A\)，以 \(\tfrac18\) 到 \(C\)，以 \(\tfrac28\) 到 \(I\)，并以 \(\tfrac38\) 到边内部，等等），得到 \[ \begin{aligned} &a=\tfrac28 a+\tfrac18 c+\tfrac28 i,\quad \\ &c=\tfrac48 a+\tfrac48 i,\quad \\ &i=\tfrac28 a+\tfrac18 c+\tfrac18 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

解得 \(a=\dfrac{1}{14}\)，\(c=\dfrac{4}{35}\)，\(i=\dfrac{11}{70}\)。所求概率是 \(c=\dfrac{4}{35}\)，所以 \(m+n=4+35=39\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

By symmetry, group the relevant interior points into three types: \(C=\{(0,0)\},\) the “axis” points \(A=\{(\pm1,0),(0,\pm1)\},\) and the “diagonal” points \(I=\{(\pm1,\pm1)\}.\) Let \(a,c,i\) be the probabilities of eventually hitting a corner starting from a point of type \(A,C,I.\)

Reading off the transition probabilities (a point in \(A\) goes to \(A\) with prob \(\tfrac28,\) to \(C\) with \(\tfrac18,\) to \(I\) with \(\tfrac28,\) and to a side interior with \(\tfrac38,\) etc.) gives \[ \begin{aligned} &a=\tfrac28 a+\tfrac18 c+\tfrac28 i,\quad \\ &c=\tfrac48 a+\tfrac48 i,\quad \\ &i=\tfrac28 a+\tfrac18 c+\tfrac18. \end{aligned} \]

Solving yields \(a=\dfrac{1}{14},\) \(c=\dfrac{4}{35},\) \(i=\dfrac{11}{70}.\) The required probability is \(c=\dfrac{4}{35},\) so \(m+n=4+35=39.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

23 答案 C

因为 \(g\) 有三个不同的根，且都被四次多项式 \(f\) 共享，所以可写成 \(f(x)=(x-q)g(x)\)，其中 \(q\) 是剩下的根。展开得 \[ \begin{aligned} &f(x)=x^4+(a-q)x^3 \\ &\quad {}+(1-qa)x^2 \\ &\quad {}+(10-q)x-10q \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

比较 \(x\) 的系数，\(10-q=100\)，所以 \(q=-90\)。比较 \(x^3\) 的系数，\(a-q=1\)，所以 \(a=-89\)。

因此 \(g(1)=1+a+1+10\) \(=12-89=-77\)，且 \(1-q=91\)，所以 \[ \begin{aligned} &f(1)=(1-q)g(1) \\ &=91\cdot(-77)=-7007 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Since \(g\) has three distinct roots all shared by the quartic \(f,\) we can write \(f(x)=(x-q)g(x)\) for some remaining root \(q.\) Expanding, \[ \begin{aligned} &f(x)=x^4+(a-q)x^3 \\ &\quad {}+(1-qa)x^2 \\ &\quad {}+(10-q)x-10q. \end{aligned} \]

Matching the \(x\) coefficient, \(10-q=100,\) so \(q=-90.\) Matching the \(x^3\) coefficient, \(a-q=1,\) so \(a=-89.\)

Then \(g(1)=1+a+1+10\) \(=12-89=-77\) and \(1-q=91,\) so \[ \begin{aligned} &f(1)=(1-q)g(1) \\ &=91\cdot(-77)=-7007. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is C.

24 答案 A

因为 \(YE\parallel AD\) 且 \(EF\parallel AC\)，可得到 \(\triangle XEY\sim\triangle XAD\) 和 \(\triangle XEF\sim\triangle XAC\)，所以 \(\dfrac{XY}{XE}=\dfrac{XD}{XA}\) 且 \(\dfrac{XF}{XE}=\dfrac{XC}{XA}\)。因而 \(\dfrac{XC}{XD}=\dfrac{XF}{XY}\)，所以 \(XF\cdot XD=XC\cdot XY\)。

在点 \(X\) 使用点的幂，\(XC\cdot XG=XD\cdot XB\)，合并可得 \(XF\cdot XG=XB\cdot XY\)。令 \(d=BD\)，则 \(DX=\dfrac14 d\) 且 \(BY=\dfrac{11}{36}d\)，所以 \[ \begin{aligned} XF\cdot XG &=\left(d-\tfrac14 d\right) \\ &\quad {}\cdot\left(d-\tfrac14 d-\tfrac{11}{36}d\right) \\ &=\dfrac34 d\cdot\dfrac49 d=\dfrac{d^2}{3} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

由于 \(ABCD\) 是圆内接四边形，\(\angle BAD\) 与 \(\angle BCD\) 互补。在 \(\triangle ABD\) 和 \(\triangle CBD\) 中使用余弦定理，得到 \(\dfrac{73-d^2}{48}=\dfrac{d^2-40}{24}\)，所以 \(d^2=51\)。因此 \(XF\cdot XG=\dfrac{51}{3}=17\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Because \(YE\parallel AD\) and \(EF\parallel AC,\) we get \(\triangle XEY\sim\triangle XAD\) and \(\triangle XEF\sim\triangle XAC,\) giving \(\dfrac{XY}{XE}=\dfrac{XD}{XA}\) and \(\dfrac{XF}{XE}=\dfrac{XC}{XA}.\) Hence \(\dfrac{XC}{XD}=\dfrac{XF}{XY},\) so \(XF\cdot XD=XC\cdot XY.\)

Power of a Point at \(X\) gives \(XC\cdot XG=XD\cdot XB,\) and combining yields \(XF\cdot XG=XB\cdot XY.\) With \(d=BD,\) \(DX=\dfrac14 d\) and \(BY=\dfrac{11}{36}d,\) so \[ \begin{aligned} XF\cdot XG &=\left(d-\tfrac14 d\right) \\ &\quad {}\cdot\left(d-\tfrac14 d-\tfrac{11}{36}d\right) \\ &=\dfrac34 d\cdot\dfrac49 d=\dfrac{d^2}{3}. \end{aligned} \]

Since \(ABCD\) is cyclic, \(\angle BAD\) and \(\angle BCD\) are supplementary. The Law of Cosines on \(\triangle ABD\) and \(\triangle CBD\) gives \(\dfrac{73-d^2}{48}=\dfrac{d^2-40}{24},\) so \(d^2=51.\) Therefore \(XF\cdot XG=\dfrac{51}{3}=17.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

25 答案 E

令 \(A=\{\sqrt2 i,-\sqrt2 i\}\)（每个模为 \(\sqrt2\)），令 \(B\) 为另外四个元素 （每个模为 \(\dfrac12\)）。由于 \(|P|=(\sqrt2)^{\#A}\left(\tfrac12\right)^{\#B}=1\) 强制 \(\#A=8\) 且 \(\#B=4\)，必须恰有 \(8\) 个因子来自 \(A\)，\(4\) 个来自 \(B\)。

\(8\) 个 \(A\) 中元素的乘积等于 \(\pm16\)（实数），而 \(4\) 个 \(B\) 中元素的乘积等于 \(\pm\tfrac{1}{16},\pm\tfrac{i}{16}\) 中的一个。它们的乘积是 \(\pm1,\pm i\) 中的一个，且四种结果等可能，所以这些配置中恰有 \(\tfrac14\) 给出 \(P=-1\)。

出现 \(8\) 个来自 \(A\)、\(4\) 个来自 \(B\) 这种模式的概率为 \(\binom{12}{4}\left(\tfrac13\right)^8\left(\tfrac23\right)^4=\dfrac{880}{3^{10}}\)。再乘以 \(\tfrac14\) 得到 \[ \begin{aligned} P&=\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{880}{3^{10}} \\ &=\dfrac{220}{3^{10}}=\dfrac{2^2\cdot5\cdot11}{3^{10}} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Let \(A=\{\sqrt2 i,-\sqrt2 i\}\) (each of magnitude \(\sqrt2\)) and \(B\) be the other four elements (each of magnitude \(\dfrac12\)). Since \(|P|=(\sqrt2)^{\#A}\left(\tfrac12\right)^{\#B}=1\) forces \(\#A=8\) and \(\#B=4,\) exactly \(8\) factors must come from \(A\) and \(4\) from \(B.\)

A product of \(8\) elements of \(A\) equals \(\pm16\) (real), and a product of \(4\) elements of \(B\) equals one of \(\pm\tfrac{1}{16},\pm\tfrac{i}{16}.\) Their product is one of \(\pm1,\pm i,\) each equally likely, so exactly \(\tfrac14\) of these configurations give \(P=-1.\)

The chance of landing in the \(8\)-from-\(A\), \(4\)-from-\(B\) pattern is \(\binom{12}{4}\left(\tfrac13\right)^8\left(\tfrac23\right)^4=\dfrac{880}{3^{10}}.\) Multiplying by \(\tfrac14\) gives \[ \begin{aligned} P&=\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot\dfrac{880}{3^{10}} \\ &=\dfrac{220}{3^{10}}=\dfrac{2^2\cdot5\cdot11}{3^{10}}. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is E.
