1

Kim 的航班上午 \(10{:}34\) 从 Newark 起飞，下午 \(1{:}18\) 在 Miami 降落。两个城市在同一时区。如果她的飞行时间为 \(h\) 小时 \(m\) 分钟，其中 \(0 \le m \lt 60\)，那么 \(h + m\) 是多少？

A\(46\) B\(47\) C\(50\) D\(53\) E\(54\)

English

Kim’s flight took off from Newark at \(10{:}34\) am and landed in Miami at \(1{:}18\) pm. Both cities are in the same time zone. If her flight took \(h\) hours and \(m\) minutes, with \(0 \le m \lt 60,\) what is \(h + m?\)

(A) \(46\)(B) \(47\)(C) \(50\)(D) \(53\)(E) \(54\)

逐题解析

1 答案 A

从上午 \(10{:}34\) 到上午 \(11{:}00\) 是 \(26\) 分钟，从上午 \(11{:}00\) 到下午 \(1{:}00\) 是 \(2\) 小时，从下午 \(1{:}00\) 到下午 \(1{:}18\) 是 \(18\) 分钟。

所以飞行时间为 \(2\) 小时 \(26 + 18 = 44\) 分钟。因此 \(h = 2\)，\(m = 44\)，且 \(h + m = 46\)。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

From \(10{:}34\) am to \(11{:}00\) am is \(26\) minutes, from \(11{:}00\) am to \(1{:}00\) pm is \(2\) hours, and from \(1{:}00\) pm to \(1{:}18\) pm is \(18\) minutes.

So the flight lasted \(2\) hours and \(26 + 18 = 44\) minutes. Thus \(h = 2,\) \(m = 44,\) and \(h + m = 46.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

2 答案 C

从里面开始，\(1 + 1 = 2\)，所以 \(1 + \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{3}{2}\)。然后 \(\dfrac{1}{\frac{3}{2}} = \dfrac{2}{3}\)，且 \(1 + \dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{5}{3}\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Starting inside, \(1 + 1 = 2,\) so \(1 + \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{3}{2}.\) Then \(\dfrac{1}{\frac{3}{2}} = \dfrac{2}{3},\) and \(1 + \dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{5}{3}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

3 答案 B

间距是 \(\dfrac{3}{4} - \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{1}{2}\)。走三分之一处就是加上 \(\dfrac{1}{3} \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{1}{6}\)。

所以这个数是 \(\dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{1}{6} = \dfrac{3}{12} + \dfrac{2}{12} = \dfrac{5}{12}\)。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

The gap is \(\dfrac{3}{4} - \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{1}{2}.\) One third of the way adds \(\dfrac{1}{3} \cdot \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{1}{6}.\)

So the number is \(\dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{1}{6} = \dfrac{3}{12} + \dfrac{2}{12} = \dfrac{5}{12}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

4 答案 A

如果四枚硬币中有一分硬币，总额不是 \(5\) 的倍数，所以不可能等于列出的五个 \(5\) 的倍数。如果没有一分硬币，每枚硬币至少值 \(5\) 分，所以总额至少是 \(20\) 分。无论哪种情况，\(15\) 都不可能。

其他数额都能达到：\(25 = 10 + 5 + 5 + 5\)，\(35 = 10 + 10 + 10 + 5\)，\(45 = 25 + 10 + 5 + 5\)，且 \(55 = 25 + 10 + 10 + 10\)。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

If the four coins include a penny, the total is not a multiple of \(5,\) so it cannot equal any of the five listed multiples of \(5.\) If there is no penny, every coin is worth at least \(5\) cents, so the total is at least \(20\) cents. Either way, \(15\) is impossible.

The other amounts are attainable: \(25 = 10 + 5 + 5 + 5,\) \(35 = 10 + 10 + 10 + 5,\) \(45 = 25 + 10 + 5 + 5,\) and \(55 = 25 + 10 + 10 + 10.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

5 答案 D

设立方体边长为 \(x\)。新长方体的三个维度是 \(x + 1\)，\(x - 1\)，和 \(x\)，因此体积为 \(x(x+1)(x-1) = x^3 - x\)。

写成 \(a_n = \tan\dfrac{\pi c_n}{12}\)，其中 \(c_1 = 3\)、\(c_2 = 2\)，且 \(c_{n+2} \equiv c_n + c_{n+1} \pmod{12}\)。序列 \(c_n\) 为 \[ \begin{gathered} 3, 2, 5, 7, 0, 7, 7, 2, \\ 9, 11, 8, 7, 3, 10, 1, 11, \\ 0, 11, 11, 10, 9, 7, 4, 11, \ldots \end{gathered} \] 其周期为 \(24\)。

因为 \(2009 = 24\cdot 83 + 17\)，所以 \(c_{2009} = c_{17} = 0\)，从而 \(a_{2009} = \tan 0 = 0\)，\(|a_{2009}| = 0\)。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

The recursion is exactly the tangent addition formula, and \(a_1 = \tan\dfrac{\pi}{4},\) \(a_2 = \tan\dfrac{\pi}{6}.\)

Writing \(a_n = \tan\dfrac{\pi c_n}{12}\) with \(c_1 = 3,\) \(c_2 = 2,\) and \(c_{n+2} \equiv c_n + c_{n+1} \pmod{12},\) the sequence \(c_n\) is \[ \begin{gathered} 3, 2, 5, 7, 0, 7, 7, 2, \\ 9, 11, 8, 7, 3, 10, 1, 11, \\ 0, 11, 11, 10, 9, 7, 4, 11, \ldots \end{gathered} \] which is periodic with period \(24.\)

Since \(2009 = 24\cdot 83 + 17,\) \(c_{2009} = c_{17} = 0,\) so \(a_{2009} = \tan 0 = 0\) and \(|a_{2009}| = 0.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.
