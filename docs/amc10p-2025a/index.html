We can reach \(10.\) The odds \(\{1, 3, 5, \ldots, 19\}\) are sum-free, since two odds sum to an even. So is \(\{11, 12, \ldots, 20\},\) since any two of those sum past \(20.\) Each has \(10\) elements. Now let \(m\) be the largest element of any sum-free subset. For \(1\le i<\frac{m}{2},\) at most one member of \(\{i,m-i\}\) can be chosen, because the two sum to \(m.\) If \(m\) is even, \(\frac{m}{2}\) cannot be chosen either, since it can be used twice and \(\frac{m}{2}+\frac{m}{2}=m.\) Thus besides \(m\) there are at most \(\lfloor\frac{m-1}{2}\rfloor\) chosen elements, for a total of at most \(\lfloor\frac{m-1}{2}\rfloor+1\le10.\) Therefore the greatest possible size is \(10.\) Thus, C is the correct answer.

22 答案 B

三个外圆圆心 \(A, B, C\) 的两两距离分别为 \(AB = 1 + 2 = 3\)，\(AC = 1 + 3 = 4\)，\(BC = 2 + 3 = 5\)，构成 \(3\)-\(4\)-\(5\) 直角三角形。现在对四个相互相切的圆应用笛卡尔圆定理，曲率分别为 \(1, \tfrac12, \tfrac13\)，以及 \(\tfrac1r\)：\(\frac1r = 1 + \tfrac12 + \tfrac13\) \(+ 2\sqrt{\tfrac12 + \tfrac16 + \tfrac13}\) \(= \tfrac{11}{6} + 2\sqrt{1}\) \(= \tfrac{23}{6}\)。取倒数得 \(r = \tfrac{6}{23}\)。因此正确答案是 B。

English

The three outer centers \(A, B, C\) are pairwise \(AB = 1 + 2 = 3,\) \(AC = 1 + 3 = 4,\) and \(BC = 2 + 3 = 5\) apart, a \(3\)-\(4\)-\(5\) right triangle. Now apply Descartes’ Circle Theorem with curvatures \(1, \tfrac12, \tfrac13,\) and \(\tfrac1r,\) all mutually tangent: \(\frac1r = 1 + \tfrac12 + \tfrac13\) \(+ 2\sqrt{\tfrac12 + \tfrac16 + \tfrac13}\) \(= \tfrac{11}{6} + 2\sqrt{1}\) \(= \tfrac{23}{6}.\) Inverting, \(r = \tfrac{6}{23}.\) Therefore, the answer is B.

23 答案 D

设 \(\angle B\) 的角平分线交 \(AC\) 于 \(D\)。由角平分线定理，\(\frac{AD}{DC} = \frac{AB}{BC} = \frac{80}{45}\)，又因为 \(AC = 75\)，得到 \(AD = 48\)、\(CD = 27\)。三角形 \(BCD\) 与 \(ACB\) 共有 \(\angle C\)，夹角两边的比例都为 \(\tfrac{45}{75}=\tfrac{27}{45}=\tfrac35\)，所以由边角边相似。于是 \(BD=\tfrac35\cdot80=48=AD\)，故 \(\triangle ADB\) 是等腰三角形。设 \(\angle DAB=\angle DBA=\theta\)。因为过 \(C\) 的高垂直于 \(AB\)，所以 \(\angle DPC\) 和 \(\angle DCP\) 都等于 \(90^\circ-\theta\)。因此 \(\triangle CDP\) 是等腰三角形，故 \(PD=CD=27\)。由于 \(P\) 位于 \(BD\) 上，所以 \(BP=BD-PD\) \(=48-27=21\)。所以正确答案是 D。

English

Let the bisector of \(\angle B\) hit \(AC\) at \(D.\) By the Angle Bisector Theorem, \(\frac{AD}{DC} = \frac{AB}{BC} = \frac{80}{45},\) and since \(AC = 75,\) we get \(AD = 48\) and \(CD = 27.\) Triangles \(BCD\) and \(ACB\) share \(\angle C,\) with adjacent sides in the common ratio \(\tfrac{45}{75}=\tfrac{27}{45}=\tfrac35,\) so they are similar by SAS. Hence \(BD=\tfrac35\cdot80=48=AD,\) making \(\triangle ADB\) isosceles. Put \(\angle DAB=\angle DBA=\theta.\) Because the altitude through \(C\) is perpendicular to \(AB,\) both \(\angle DPC\) and \(\angle DCP\) equal \(90^\circ-\theta.\) Thus \(\triangle CDP\) is isosceles, so \(PD=CD=27.\) Since \(P\) lies on \(BD,\) we get \(BP=BD-PD\) \(=48-27=21.\) Thus, D is the correct answer.

24 答案 C

在一个公平数中，数字必须先上升到最大数字 \(m\)，再下降。事实上，任何一次下降之后的第一次上升，都会起始于一个比左右两个邻居都小的数字。对于 \(k\) 位数，从 \(1\) 到 \(9\) 中选择数字集合，有 \(\binom{9}{k}\) 种。比 \(m\) 小的那 \(k-1\) 个数字各自独立地放在递增的左侧或递减的右侧，之后它们的位置就被唯一确定。因此总数为 \(\sum_{k=1}^{9}\binom{9}{k}2^{k-1}\) \(= \tfrac12\big((1+2)^9-1\big)\) \(= \tfrac{3^9-1}{2}\) \(=9841\)。因此正确答案是 C。

English

A fair number’s digits must increase up to its largest digit \(m\) and then decrease. Indeed, the first ascent after any descent would begin at a digit smaller than both of its neighbors. For \(k\) digits, choose the digit set from \(1\) to \(9\) in \(\binom{9}{k}\) ways. Each of the \(k-1\) digits below \(m\) independently goes on the increasing left side or the decreasing right side, after which its position is forced. Hence the total is \(\sum_{k=1}^{9}\binom{9}{k}2^{k-1}\) \(= \tfrac12\big((1+2)^9-1\big)\) \(= \tfrac{3^9-1}{2}\) \(=9841.\) Therefore, the answer is C.

25 答案 A

将 \(A = (0,0)\)、\(B = (1,0)\) 放在单位正方形中。\(AP\) 是中间长度，当且仅当 \(BP \lt AP \lt AB\) 或 \(AB \lt AP \lt BP\)。这两个条件给出的区域由以 \(A\) 为圆心、半径为 \(1\) 的圆（其中 \(AP = AB\)）和垂直平分线 \(x = \tfrac12\)（其中 \(AP = BP\)）围出。设 \(S\) 是该圆与 \(x = \tfrac12\) 的交点，则 \(\triangle ABS\) 为等边三角形，所以相关扇形角为 \(\angle BAS = 60^\circ\)。计算这些区域面积，较大区域为 \(\frac{4\pi - 3\sqrt3}{24}\)，较小区域为 \(\frac{12 - 2\pi - 3\sqrt3}{24}\)。相加得概率 \(\frac{6 + \pi - 3\sqrt3}{12}\)。因此 \(a + b + c + d + e\) \(= 6 + 1 + 3 + 3 + 12\) \(= 25\)，所以正确答案是 A。

English

Place \(A = (0,0)\) and \(B = (1,0)\) on the unit square. \(AP\) is the middle length when \(BP \lt AP \lt AB\) or \(AB \lt AP \lt BP.\) These regions are bounded by the circle centered at \(A\) with radius \(1\) (where \(AP = AB\)) and the line \(x = \tfrac12\) (where \(AP = BP\)). Let \(S\) be where the circle meets \(x = \tfrac12.\) Then \(\triangle ABS\) is equilateral, so \(\angle BAS = 60^\circ.\) The larger region has area \(\frac{4\pi - 3\sqrt3}{24}\) and the smaller has area \(\frac{12 - 2\pi - 3\sqrt3}{24}.\) They add to \(\frac{6 + \pi - 3\sqrt3}{12}.\) Thus \(a + b + c + d + e\) \(= 6 + 1 + 3 + 3 + 12\) \(= 25.\) Thus, A is the correct answer.
