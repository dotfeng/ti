Vieta’s formulas give \(a+b+c+d=0.\) Thus, for example, \[ \frac{a+b+c}{d^2}=-\frac1d, \] and similarly the new roots are \(-\frac{1}{a},\) \(-\frac{1}{b},\) \(-\frac{1}{c},\) \(-\frac{1}{d}.\) Put \(x=-\frac{1}{r}\) in \(r^4-br-3=0.\) Multiplying by \(x^4\) gives \(1+bx^3-3x^4=0,\) or \[ 3x^4-bx^3-1=0. \]

Therefore, the correct answer is D.
