\[\dfrac{2-4+6-8+10-12+14}{3-6+9-12+15-18+21}?\]

2

阿尔患上了“代数病”（algebritis），每天必须服用一颗绿色药丸和一颗粉色药丸，持续两周。绿色药丸比粉色药丸贵 \(\$1\)，两周的药丸总费用为 \(\$546\)。一颗绿色药丸多少钱？

A\(\$7\) B\(\$14\) C\(\$19\) D\(\$20\) E\(\$39\)

English

Al gets the disease algebritis and must take one green pill and one pink pill each day for two weeks. A green pill costs \(\$1\) more than a pink pill, and Al’s pills cost a total of \(\$546\) for the two weeks. How much does one green pill cost?

(A) \(\$7\)(B) \(\$14\)(C) \(\$19\)(D) \(\$20\)(E) \(\$39\)

3

\(5\) 个连续偶数的和比前 \(8\) 个连续正奇数的和少 \(4\)。这些偶数中最小的是多少？

A\(6\) B\(8\) C\(10\) D\(12\) E\(14\)

English

The sum of \(5\) consecutive even integers is \(4\) less than the sum of the first \(8\) consecutive odd counting numbers. What is the smallest of the even integers?

(A) \(6\)(B) \(8\)(C) \(10\)(D) \(12\)(E) \(14\)

4

罗斯用不同种类的花填满她的长方形花坛中的每个长方形区域。图中给出了这些长方形区域的边长，单位为英尺。她在每个区域每平方英尺种一朵花。紫菀每朵 \(\$1\)，秋海棠每朵 \(\$1.50\)，美人蕉每朵 \(\$2\)，大丽花每朵 \(\$2.50\)，复活节百合每朵 \(\$3\)。她的花园最少可能花多少钱？

A\(108\) B\(115\) C\(132\) D\(144\) E\(156\)

English

Rose fills each of the rectangular regions of her rectangular flower bed with a different type of flower. The lengths, in feet, of the rectangular regions in her flower bed are as shown in the figure. She plants one flower per square foot in each region. Asters cost \(\$1\) each, begonias \(\$1.50\) each, cannas \(\$2\) each, dahlias \(\$2.50\) each, and Easter lilies \(\$3\) each. What is the least possible cost, in dollars, for her garden?

(A) \(108\)(B) \(115\)(C) \(132\)(D) \(144\)(E) \(156\)

5

莫用割草机修剪一个 \(90\) 英尺乘 \(150\) 英尺的长方形草坪。割草机每次割出的宽度为 \(28\) 英寸，但为了不漏草，他每次重叠 \(4\) 英寸。他推割草机时的速度为每小时 \(5000\) 英尺。下列哪个数最接近他割完整个草坪所需的小时数？

A\(0.75\) B\(0.8\) C\(1.35\) D\(1.5\) E\(3\)

English

Moe uses a mower to cut his rectangular \(90\)-foot by \(150\)-foot lawn. The swath he cuts is \(28\) inches wide, but he overlaps each cut by \(4\) inches to make sure that no grass is missed. He walks at the rate of \(5000\) feet per hour while pushing the mower. Which of the following is closest to the number of hours it will take Moe to mow his lawn?

(A) \(0.75\)(B) \(0.8\)(C) \(1.35\)(D) \(1.5\)(E) \(3\)

逐题解析

1 答案 C

因式分解得 \[\dfrac{2(1-2+3-4+5-6+7)}{3(1-2+3-4+5-6+7)}\text{。}\]分子、分母中相同的交错和约去后，剩下 \(\dfrac{2}{3}\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Factoring gives \[\dfrac{2(1-2+3-4+5-6+7)}{3(1-2+3-4+5-6+7)}.\] The identical alternating sums cancel, leaving \(\dfrac{2}{3}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

2 答案 D

每天药丸费用为 \(546 \div 14 = 39\) 美元。设绿色药丸价格为 \(x\)，则粉色药丸价格为 \(x-1\)，所以 \(x+(x-1)=39\)，解得 \(x=20\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Each day’s pills cost \(546 \div 14 = 39\) dollars. If \(x\) is the cost of a green pill, then the pink pill costs \(x-1,\) so \(x+(x-1)=39.\) Solving gives \(x=20.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

3 答案 B

前 \(8\) 个正奇数的和是 \(1+3+\cdots+15=64\)。

设最小偶数为 \(n\)，则 \[ \begin{gathered} n+(n+2)+(n+4) \\ {}+(n+6)+(n+8) \\ = 5n+20 = 60 \end{gathered}\text{，}\]因此 \(n=8\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The first \(8\) odd counting numbers sum to \(1+3+\cdots+15=64.\)

Letting \(n\) be the smallest even integer, \[ \begin{gathered} n+(n+2)+(n+4) \\ {}+(n+6)+(n+8) \\ = 5n+20 = 60, \end{gathered} \] so \(n=8.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

4 答案 A

五个区域的面积分别为 \(4\)、\(6\)、\(15\)、\(20\) 和 \(21\) 平方英尺。

要使费用最小，应把最贵的花种在最小的区域，所以总费用为 \[ \begin{gathered} (3)(4)+(2.5)(6)+(2)(15) \\ {}+(1.5)(20)+(1)(21) \\ = 108 \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The five regions have areas \(4,\) \(6,\) \(15,\) \(20,\) and \(21\) square feet.

Cost is minimized by placing the most expensive flower in the smallest region, so the total is \[ \begin{gathered} (3)(4)+(2.5)(6)+(2)(15) \\ {}+(1.5)(20)+(1)(21) \\ = 108. \end{gathered} \]

Thus, the correct answer is A.

5 答案 C

草坪面积为 \(90 \cdot 150 = 13{,}500\) 平方英尺。

莫每走一英尺，有效割草宽度为 \(2\) 英尺，所以每小时割草面积为 \(2 \cdot 5000 = 10{,}000\) 平方英尺。因此所需时间为 \[\dfrac{13{,}500}{10{,}000}=1.35\] 小时。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The lawn has area \(90 \cdot 150 = 13{,}500\) square feet.

Each foot Moe walks mows an effective strip \(2\) feet wide, so he mows \(2 \cdot 5000 = 10{,}000\) square feet per hour. The time needed is \[\dfrac{13{,}500}{10{,}000}=1.35\] hours.

Thus, the correct answer is C.

6 答案 D

18 答案 D

当 \(n\) 为偶数时，这五个因数是连续五个奇数。其中至少有一个能被 \(3\) 整除，且恰有一个能被 \(5\) 整除，所以乘积总能被 \(15\) 整除。

要证明不存在更大的固定公因数，比较以下三种情形：\[ \begin{aligned} n=2 &: 3\cdot5\cdot7\cdot9\cdot11,\\ n=10 &: 11\cdot13\cdot15\cdot17\cdot19,\\ n=12 &: 13\cdot15\cdot17\cdot19\cdot21 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]这三个乘积的最大公因数恰为 \(15\)，所以能整除所有情形的整数不可能更大。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

When \(n\) is even, the factors are five consecutive odd numbers. Among any five consecutive odd numbers, at least one is divisible by \(3\) and exactly one by \(5,\) so the product is always divisible by \(15.\)

To prove that no larger fixed divisor is forced, compare three cases: \[ \begin{aligned} n=2 &: 3\cdot5\cdot7\cdot9\cdot11,\\ n=10 &: 11\cdot13\cdot15\cdot17\cdot19,\\ n=12 &: 13\cdot15\cdot17\cdot19\cdot21. \end{aligned} \] The greatest common divisor of these three products is exactly \(15,\) so a divisor common to every case cannot be any larger.

Thus, the correct answer is D.

19 答案 E

大半圆面积为 \(\dfrac12 \pi (2)^2 = 2\pi\)。

删去的部分等于五个半径为 \(1\) 的 \(60^\circ\) 扇形和两个边长为 \(1\) 的等边三角形；每个扇形面积为 \(\dfrac{\pi}{6}\)，每个等边三角形面积为 \(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}\)。

阴影面积为 \[ \begin{gathered} 2\pi - 5 \cdot \dfrac{\pi}{6} - 2 \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt3}{4} \\ = \dfrac{7}{6}\pi - \dfrac{\sqrt3}{2} \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The large semicircle has area \(\dfrac12 \pi (2)^2 = 2\pi.\)

Removing the small semicircles deletes a region equal to five congruent \(60^\circ\) sectors of radius \(1\) plus two equilateral triangles of side \(1.\) Each sector has area \(\dfrac{\pi}{6}\) and each triangle has area \(\dfrac{\sqrt3}{4}.\)

The shaded area is \[ \begin{gathered} 2\pi - 5 \cdot \dfrac{\pi}{6} - 2 \cdot \dfrac{\sqrt3}{4} \\ = \dfrac{7}{6}\pi - \dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}. \end{gathered} \]

Thus, the correct answer is E.

20 答案 D

先计算 \(FG = CD - DF - GC\) \(= 5 - 1 - 2\) \(= 2\)。设 \(E\) 到直线 \(CD\) 的距离为 \(h\)。由于 \(\triangle FEG \sim \triangle AEB\)，相似比为 \(\dfrac{FG}{AB}=\dfrac25\)，所以 \[\dfrac{h}{h+3}=\dfrac25\text{，}\]解得 \(h=2\)。

21 答案 C

袋中始终有 \(4\) 颗珠子。

最终全为红色，正好表示两颗绿珠都被抽到。先绿后绿的概率为 \(\dfrac24 \cdot \dfrac14 = \dfrac18\)。绿、红、绿的概率为 \(\dfrac24 \cdot \dfrac34 \cdot \dfrac14 = \dfrac{3}{32}\)。红、绿、绿的概率为 \(\dfrac24 \cdot \dfrac24 \cdot \dfrac14 = \dfrac{1}{16}\)。

把三种情况相加，得到 \[\dfrac18 + \dfrac{3}{32} + \dfrac{1}{16} = \dfrac{9}{32}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The bag always holds \(4\) beads. All are red at the end precisely when both greens are drawn.

Drawing green then green has probability \(\dfrac24 \cdot \dfrac14 = \dfrac18.\) Green, red, green has probability \(\dfrac24 \cdot \dfrac34 \cdot \dfrac14 = \dfrac{3}{32}.\) Red, green, green has probability \(\dfrac24 \cdot \dfrac24 \cdot \dfrac14 = \dfrac{1}{16}.\)

The total is \[\dfrac18 + \dfrac{3}{32} + \dfrac{1}{16} = \dfrac{9}{32}.\]

Thus, the correct answer is C.
