1

Mary 书架最上层有五本书，它们的宽度分别为 \(6\)、\(\dfrac{1}{2}\)、\(1\)、\(2.5\) 和 \(10\) 厘米。

这些书的平均宽度是多少厘米？

A\(1\) B\(2\) C\(3\) D\(4\) E\(5\)

English

Mary’s top book shelf holds five books with the following widths, in centimeters: \(6,\) \(\dfrac{1}{2},\) \(1,\) \(2.5,\) and \(10.\)

What is the average book width, in centimeters?

(A) \(1\)(B) \(2\)(C) \(3\)(D) \(4\)(E) \(5\)

2

四个相同的正方形和一个长方形拼在一起，形成如图所示的一个大正方形。这个长方形的长是宽的多少倍？

A\(\dfrac{5}{4}\) B\(\dfrac{4}{3}\) C\(\dfrac{3}{2}\) D\(2\) E\(3\)

English

Four identical squares and one rectangle are placed together to form one large square as shown. The length of the rectangle is how many times as large as its width?

(A) \(\dfrac{5}{4}\)(B) \(\dfrac{4}{3}\)(C) \(\dfrac{3}{2}\)(D) \(2\)(E) \(3\)

3

Tyrone 有 \(97\) 颗弹珠，Eric 有 \(11\) 颗弹珠。之后 Tyrone 给了 Eric 一些弹珠，使得最后 Tyrone 的弹珠数是 Eric 的两倍。Tyrone 给了 Eric 多少颗弹珠？

A\(3\) B\(13\) C\(18\) D\(25\) E\(29\)

English

Tyrone had \(97\) marbles and Eric had \(11\) marbles. Tyrone then gave some of his marbles to Eric so that Tyrone ended with twice as many marbles as Eric. How many marbles did Tyrone give to Eric?

(A) \(3\)(B) \(13\)(C) \(18\)(D) \(25\)(E) \(29\)

4

一本要录成光盘的书，朗读需要 \(412\) 分钟。每张光盘最多能容纳 \(56\) 分钟的朗读。假设使用尽可能少的光盘，并且每张光盘包含相同长度的朗读内容。每张光盘包含多少分钟朗读？

A\(50.2\) B\(51.5\) C\(52.4\) D\(53.8\) E\(55.2\)

English

A book that is to be recorded onto compact discs takes \(412\) minutes to read aloud. Each disc can hold up to \(56\) minutes of reading. Assume that the smallest possible number of discs is used and that each disc contains the same length of reading. How many minutes of reading will each disc contain?

(A) \(50.2\)(B) \(51.5\)(C) \(52.4\)(D) \(53.8\)(E) \(55.2\)

5

A\(6\) B\(12\) C\(24\) D\(36\) E\(144\)

English

The area of a circle whose circumference is \(24\pi\) is \(k\pi.\) What is the value of \(k?\)

(A) \(6\)(B) \(12\)(C) \(24\)(D) \(36\)(E) \(144\)

6

对正数 \(x\) 和 \(y\)，运算 \(\spadesuit (x,y)\) 定义为 \[\spadesuit (x,y) = x-\dfrac{1}{y}\] 求 \(\spadesuit (2,\spadesuit (2,2))\)。

A\(\dfrac{2}{3}\) B\(1\) C\(\dfrac{4}{3}\) D\(\dfrac{5}{3}\) E\(2\)

English

For positive numbers \(x\) and \(y\) the operation \(\spadesuit (x,y)\) is defined as \[\spadesuit (x,y) = x-\dfrac{1}{y}\] What is \(\spadesuit (2,\spadesuit (2,2))?\)

(A) \(\dfrac{2}{3}\)(B) \(1\)(C) \(\dfrac{4}{3}\)(D) \(\dfrac{5}{3}\)(E) \(2\)

7

Crystal 为每日跑步规划了一条路线。她先向正北跑一英里，然后向东北跑一英里，再向东南跑一英里。最后一段她沿直线跑回起点。最后一段长多少英里？

A\(1\) B\(\sqrt{2}\) C\(\sqrt{3}\) D\(2\) E\(2\sqrt{2}\)

English

Crystal has a running course marked out for her daily run. She starts this run by heading due north for one mile. She then runs northeast for one mile, then southeast for one mile. The last portion of her run takes her on a straight line back to where she started. How far, in miles, is this last portion of her run?

(A) \(1\)(B) \(\sqrt{2}\)(C) \(\sqrt{3}\)(D) \(2\)(E) \(2\sqrt{2}\)

8

English

(A) \(9\)(B) \(11\)(C) \(12\)(D) \(13\)(E) \(14\)

9

(A) \(20\)(B) \(21\)(C) \(22\)(D) \(23\)(E) \(24\)

12

13

Angelina 先以平均 \(80\) 千米/小时的速度驾驶，然后停车加油 \(20\) 分钟。停车后，她以平均 \(100\) 千米/小时的速度驾驶。包括停车时间在内，她总共用 \(3\) 小时行驶了 \(250\) 千米。下列哪个方程可用来求她停车前驾驶的时间 \(t\)，单位为小时？

A\(80t + 100\left(\dfrac83 - t\right) = 250\) B\(80t = 250\) C\(100t = 250\) D\(90t = 250\) E\(80\left(\dfrac83 - t\right) + 100t = 250\)

English

Angelina drove at an average rate of \(80\) kph and then stopped \(20\) minutes for gas. After the stop, she drove at an average rate of \(100\) kph. Altogether she drove \(250\) km in a total trip time of \(3\) hours including the stop. Which equation could be used to solve for the time \(t\) in hours that she drove before her stop?

(A) \(80t + 100\left(\dfrac83 - t\right) = 250\)(B) \(80t = 250\)(C) \(100t = 250\)(D) \(90t = 250\)(E) \(80\left(\dfrac83 - t\right) + 100t = 250\)

14

Triangle \(ABC\) has \(AB=2 \cdot AC.\) Let \(D\) and \(E\) be on \(\overline{AB}\) and \(\overline{BC},\) respectively, such that \(\angle BAE = \angle ACD.\) Let \(F\) be the intersection of segments \(AE\) and \(CD,\) and suppose that \(\triangle CFE\) is equilateral. What is \(\angle ACB?\)

(A) \(60^\circ\)(B) \(75^\circ\)(C) \(90^\circ\)(D) \(105^\circ\)(E) \(120^\circ\)

15

在一个神奇沼泽中，有两种会说话的两栖动物：蟾蜍说的话总是真的，青蛙说的话总是假的。Brian、Chris、LeRoy 和 Mike 四只两栖动物住在这个沼泽里，并作出如下陈述。

Brian：“Mike 和我是不同物种。”

Chris：“LeRoy 是青蛙。”

LeRoy：“Chris 是青蛙。”

Mike：“我们四个中至少有两个是蟾蜍。”

这四只两栖动物中有多少只是青蛙？

A\(0\) B\(1\) C\(2\) D\(3\) E\(4\)

English

In a magical swamp there are two species of talking amphibians: toads, whose statements are always true, and frogs, whose statements are always false. Four amphibians, Brian, Chris, LeRoy, and Mike live together in this swamp, and they make the following statements.

Brian: “Mike and I are different species.”

Chris: “LeRoy is a frog.”

LeRoy: “Chris is a frog.”

Mike: “Of the four of us, at least two are toads.”

How many of these four amphibians are frogs?

(A) \(0\)(B) \(1\)(C) \(2\)(D) \(3\)(E) \(4\)

25

Jim 从一个正整数 \(n\) 开始构造一个数列。每个后续数都由当前数减去不超过当前数的最大整数平方得到，直到达到零为止。例如，如果 Jim 从 \(n = 55\) 开始，则他的数列包含 \(5\) 个数：\[\begin{array}{ccccc} {}&{}&{}&{}&55\\ 55&-&7^2&=&6\\ 6&-&2^2&=&2\\ 2&-&1^2&=&1\\ 1&-&1^2&=&0\\ \end{array}\] 设 \(N\) 为使 Jim 的数列有 \(8\) 个数的最小数。\(N\) 的个位数字是多少？

A\(1\) B\(3\) C\(5\) D\(7\) E\(9\)

English

Jim starts with a positive integer \(n\) and creates a sequence of numbers. Each successive number is obtained by subtracting the largest possible integer square less than or equal to the current number until zero is reached. For example, if Jim starts with \(n = 55,\) then his sequence contains \(5\) numbers: \[\begin{array}{ccccc} {}&{}&{}&{}&55\\ 55&-&7^2&=&6\\ 6&-&2^2&=&2\\ 2&-&1^2&=&1\\ 1&-&1^2&=&0\\ \end{array}\] Let \(N\) be the smallest number for which Jim’s sequence has \(8\) numbers. What is the units digit of \(N?\)

(A) \(1\)(B) \(3\)(C) \(5\)(D) \(7\)(E) \(9\)

1 答案 D

五个宽度之和为 \(6+\dfrac12+1+2.5+10=20\)。平均宽度为 \(20\div5=4\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Adding the five widths gives \(6+\dfrac12+1+2.5+10=20.\) The average is \(20\div5=4.\)

Thus, D is the correct answer.

2 答案 B

不妨设每个小正方形的边长为 \(1\)。

这样长方形的长为 \(4\)，宽为 \(4 - 1 = 3\)。

所求长宽比为 \(\dfrac{4}{3}\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

WLOG, let the side lengths of the squares be \(1.\)

This means that the length of the rectangle is \(4.\) We also have that the width must be \(4 - 1 = 3.\)

The desired ratio is then \(\dfrac{4}{3}.\)

Thus, B is the correct answer.

3 答案 D

设 Eric 最后有 \(x\) 颗弹珠，则 Tyrone 最后有 \(2x\) 颗。

总弹珠数为 \(97 + 11 = 108\)，所以 \[ 3x = 108 \] \[x = 36\text{。}\]

因此 Tyrone 最后有 \(36 \cdot 2 = 72\) 颗弹珠，他给出了 \(97 - 72 = 25\) 颗。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Let \(x\) be the number of marbles that Eric ends up with. Then Tyrone ends up with \(2x.\)

The total number of marbles is \(97 + 11 = 108,\) so \[ 3x = 108 \]\[x = 36. \]

Then, Tyrone ends up with \(36 \cdot 2 = 72\) marbles. This means he has to give away \(97 - 72 = 25\) marbles.

Thus, D is the correct answer.

4 答案 B

注意 \(7 \cdot 56 = 392\)，而 \(8 \cdot 56 = 448\)，所以最少需要 \(8\) 张光盘。

每张光盘包含的朗读时间为 \[ 412 \div 8 = 51.5\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Note that \(7 \cdot 56 = 392\) and \(8 \cdot 56 = 448,\) which means that the minimum number of discs needed is \(8.\)

Then the minutes of reading that each disc contains is \[ 412 \div 8 = 51.5. \]

Thus, B is the correct answer.

5 答案 E

圆的周长公式为 \(2 \pi r\)，所以 \[ 24\pi = 2\pi r \] \[ r = 12\text{。}\]

圆面积为 \(\pi r^2\)，因此 \[ k \pi = \pi 12^2 \] \[ k = 144\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Recall that the formula for the circumference of a circle is \(2 \pi r.\) We then have that \[ 24\pi = 2\pi r \]\[ r = 12. \]

The area of a circle is \(\pi r^2,\) so we have that \[ k \pi = \pi 12^2 \]\[ k = 144. \]

Thus, E is the correct answer.

6 答案 C

先计算内层，得到 \[ \spadesuit (2, 2) = 2 - \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{3}{2}\text{。}\]于是 \[ \spadesuit \left(2, \dfrac{3}{2}\right) = 2 - \dfrac{1}{\frac{3}{2}} = \dfrac{4}{3}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Evaluating the inner expression, we get \[ \spadesuit (2, 2) = 2 - \dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{3}{2}. \] Then we have \[ \spadesuit \left(2, \dfrac{3}{2}\right) = 2 - \dfrac{1}{\frac{3}{2}} = \dfrac{4}{3}. \]

Thus, C is the correct answer.

从图中可以看出，最后一段是一个直角三角形的斜边。

8 答案 D

10 答案 E

普通年有 \[ 365 = 52 \cdot 7 + 1\text{，}\]所以同一日期在下一年会向后移一天。

对于闰年中位于二月二十九日之后的日期，星期会向后移两天。

因此 \(2009\) 年这一天是星期三，\(2010\) 年是星期四。

\(2011\) 年是星期五；由于 \(2012\) 年是闰年，这一天变为星期日。

接下来三年每年向后移一天。到 \(2016\) 年又因闰年向后移两天，落在星期五。

最后，\(2017\) 年这一天是星期六。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Note that on a normal year, we have that \[ 365 = 52 \cdot 7 + 1, \] which means that for a specific day, it moves to the day after the next year.

On a leap year, the day of the week moves forward two since there is an extra day.

Then in \(2009,\) this day falls on a Wednesday. In \(2010,\) it falls on a Thursday.

Similarly, in \(2011,\) it falls on a Friday. In \(2012,\) however, since it is a leap year, it falls on a Sunday.

Now, for the next three years, the day moves forward one. Then in \(2016,\) it moves forward two, landing on a Friday.

Finally, in \(2017,\) the day of the week is a Saturday.

Thus, E is the correct answer.

14 答案 C

设 \(\angle BAE = \angle ACD = x\)。因为 \(\triangle CFE\) 是等边三角形，所以 \(\angle CFE = 60^{\circ}\)。

因此 \[ \angle AFC = 180^{\circ} - \angle CFE = 120^{\circ}\text{。}\]

因此 \[\begin{aligned} \angle FAC &= 180^{\circ} - 120^{\circ} - x\\ &=60^{\circ} - x \\ &= \angle EAC\end{aligned}\text{。}\]

于是 \[ \begin{aligned} \angle BAC &= \angle BAE + \angle EAC\\ &= x + 60^{\circ} - x \\&= 60^{\circ} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

由于 \(AB = 2 \cdot AC\) 且 \(\angle BAC = 60^{\circ}\)，\(\triangle ABC\) 是 \(30-60-90\) 三角形。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Let \(\angle BAE = \angle ACD = x.\) Note that \(\angle CFE = 60^{\circ}\) since \(\triangle CFE\) is equilateral.

We then have that \[ \angle AFC = 180^{\circ} - \angle CFE = 120^{\circ}. \]

Then: \[\begin{aligned} \angle FAC &= 180^{\circ} - 120^{\circ} - x\\ &=60^{\circ} - x \\ &= \angle EAC.\end{aligned} \]

We then get that \[ \begin{aligned} \angle BAC &= \angle BAE + \angle EAC\\ &= x + 60^{\circ} - x \\&= 60^{\circ}. \end{aligned}\]

Since \(AB = 2 \cdot AC\) and \(\angle BAC = 60^{\circ},\) we have that \(\triangle ABC\) is a \(30-60-90\) triangle.

Thus, C is the correct answer.

15 答案 D

Chris 和 LeRoy 不可能都是青蛙，否则他们的陈述都会是真的；也不可能都是蟾蜍，否则他们的陈述都会是假的。因此两者中恰有一只是蟾蜍。

如果 Brian 是蟾蜍，他的陈述会说明 Mike 是青蛙。这样 Brian 与 Chris、LeRoy 中的那只蟾蜍就使 Mike 的陈述为真，这与青蛙总说假话矛盾。因此 Brian 是青蛙。他的陈述为假，所以 Mike 也是青蛙。再加上 Chris、LeRoy 中的那只青蛙，共有 \(3\) 只青蛙。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Chris and LeRoy cannot both be frogs, because then both of their statements would be true. They cannot both be toads either, because then both statements would be false. Thus exactly one of them is a toad.

If Brian were a toad, his statement would make Mike a frog. Brian and the one toad among Chris and LeRoy would then make Mike’s statement true, which is impossible for a frog. Therefore Brian is a frog. His statement is false, so Mike is also a frog. Along with the one frog among Chris and LeRoy, there are \(3\) frogs.

Thus, D is the correct answer.

16 答案 B

由角平分线定理，\[ \dfrac{AB}{3} = \dfrac{BC}{8} \] \[ AB = \dfrac{3}{8} BC\text{。}\]

为使 \(AB\) 和 \(BC\) 都是整数，\(BC\) 必须是 \(8\) 的倍数。

若为了最小化周长取 \(BC = 8\)、\(AB = 3\)，三角形会退化。

因此 \(BC\) 必须取 \(16\)，此时 \(AB = 6\)。又因为 \(AC = AD + DC = 11\)，所以周长为 \[ 16 + 6 + 11 = 33\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Using the Angle Bisector Theorem, we have that \[ \dfrac{AB}{3} = \dfrac{BC}{8} \]\[ AB = \dfrac{3}{8} BC. \]

For \(AB\) and \(BC\) to be integers, we must have that \(BC\) is a multiple of \(8.\)

To minimize the perimeter, we can set \(BC = 8\) and \(AB = 3.\) This, however, makes the triangle degenerate.

\(BC\) must then be \(16\) and \(AB = 6.\) Since \(AC = AD + DC = 11,\) the perimeter is \[ 16 + 6 + 11 = 33. \]

Thus, B is the correct answer.

17 答案 A

三个被切出的长方体都在立方体中心相交。

交集是边长为 \(2\) 的立方体。因此被切除区域的体积为 \[ \begin{aligned}3 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 - 2 \cdot 2^3 &= 36 - 16 \\&= 20\end{aligned}\text{。}\]

中心区域被所有 \(3\) 个孔都包含，所以要减去重复计入的两次。

剩余体积为 \[ 3^3 - 20 = 27 - 20 = 7\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Note that all the cut out solids intersect in the middle of the cube.

This region of intersection is a cube with side length \(2.\) Then the volume of the cutout region is \[ \begin{aligned}3 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 3 - 2 \cdot 2^3 &= 36 - 16 \\&= 20.\end{aligned} \]

We have to subtract out the center region twice since it is included in all \(3\) regions.

The remaining volume is then \[ 3^3 - 20 = 27 - 20 = 7. \]

Thus, A is the correct answer.

18 答案 B

分两种情况：Bernardo 选到 \(9\)，或没有选到这个数。

情况 \(1\)：Bernardo 选到 \(9\)。

由于一个数字已经固定，另外两个数有 \(\binom{8}{2} = 28\) 种选法。

总选法为 \(\binom{9}{3} = 84\)，概率为 \[ \dfrac{28}{84} = \dfrac{1}{3}\text{。}\]

注意，如果 Bernardo 选到 \(9\)，他一定比 Silvia 的数大。

因此 Bernardo 在这种情况下总是获胜。

情况 \(2\)：Bernardo 没有选到 \(9\)。

这种情况发生的概率为 \(1 - \frac{1}{3} = \frac{2}{3}\)。由于两人此时从相同的数字中选择，除了平局以外，双方获胜的概率相同。

Silvia 与 Bernardo 选到同一组数字的概率为 \[ \dfrac{1}{\binom{8}{3}} = \dfrac{1}{56}\text{。}\]所以在这种情况下，Bernardo 的数较大的概率为 \[ \dfrac{1 - \frac{1}{56}}{2} = \dfrac{55}{112}\text{。}\]

因此总概率为 \[ \dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{2}{3} \cdot \dfrac{55}{112} = \dfrac{37}{56}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

There are two cases: Bernardo picks a \(9\) or he doesn’t.

Case \(1:\) Bernardo picks a \(9\)

Since a number is fixed, there are \(\binom{8}{2} = 28\) ways to choose the other two numbers.

There are a total of \(\binom{9}{3} = 84\) ways to pick all three numbers. The probability is then \[ \dfrac{28}{84} = \dfrac{1}{3}. \]

Note that if Bernardo picks a \(9,\) he automatically has a greater number than Silvia.

This means that Bernardo always wins in this case.

Case \(2:\) Bernardo doesn’t pick a \(9\)

There is a \(1 - \frac{1}{3} = \frac{2}{3}\) chance of this happening. Since both people are choosing from the same numbers, they have an equal chance of winning.

We still need to find the probability that the numbers are the same. There is a \[ \dfrac{1}{\binom{8}{3}} = \dfrac{1}{56} \] chance that Silvia chooses the same numbers as Bernardo. The probability that Bernardo gets a higher number is then \[ \dfrac{1 - \frac{1}{56}}{2} = \dfrac{55}{112}. \]

The total probability of Bernardo getting a higher number is then \[ \dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{2}{3} \cdot \dfrac{55}{112} = \dfrac{37}{56}. \]

Thus, B is the correct answer.

22 答案 A

一个内部三角形由三条在圆内部两两相交的弦形成。这样的三角形使用圆上的六个不同端点。

反过来，对任意选出的六个圆上点，按圆周顺序看，只有一种方法把相对端点配对成三条弦，使这三条弦在圆内两两相交。

因此三角形的数量为 \(\binom{8}{6}=\binom{8}{2}=28\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

An interior triangle is formed by three chords that pairwise intersect inside the circle. Such a triangle uses six distinct endpoints on the circle.

Conversely, for any six chosen points in circular order, exactly one set of three chords pairs opposite endpoints so that the three chords intersect pairwise inside the circle.

Therefore the number of triangles is \(\binom{8}{6}=\binom{8}{2}=28.\)

Thus, A is the correct answer.

23 答案 A

第 \(k\) 个盒子共有 \(k + 1\) 颗弹珠，所以从中抽到白弹珠的概率为 \(\dfrac{k}{k + 1}\)。

抽到红弹珠的概率为 \(\dfrac{1}{k + 1}\)。要在第 \(n\) 次抽取后停止，前 \(n - 1\) 次必须抽到白弹珠，最后一次抽到红弹珠。

概率为 \[ \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \dfrac{2}{3} \cdot \ldots \cdot \dfrac{n - 1}{n} \cdot \dfrac{1}{n + 1}\text{。} \]

分子与相邻分母相消，得到概率为 \(\dfrac{1}{n(n + 1)}\)。

我们需要找出使下式成立的最小 \(n\)： \[ \dfrac{1}{n(n + 1)} \lt \dfrac{1}{2010} \] \[ n(n + 1) \gt 2010\text{。} \]

逐一试算可知，满足条件的最小 \(n\) 为 \(45\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Since there are \(k + 1\) marbles in the \(k\) th box, there is a \(\dfrac{k}{k + 1}\) chance Isabella draws a white marble from it.

The probability of drawing a red marble is then \(\dfrac{1}{k + 1}.\) To stop after drawing the \(n\) th marble, the first \(n - 1\) marbles must have been white.

This happens with a probability of \[ \dfrac{1}{2} \cdot \dfrac{2}{3} \cdot \ldots \cdot \dfrac{n - 1}{n} \cdot \dfrac{1}{n + 1}. \]

Note that all the numerators cancel with the adjacent denominator, which means that this expression reduces to \(\dfrac{1}{n(n + 1)}.\)

We have to find the smallest \(n\) such that \[ \dfrac{1}{n(n + 1)} \lt \dfrac{1}{2010} \] \[ n(n + 1) \gt 2010. \]

Guessing and checking gives us that the smallest \(n\) that works is \(45.\)

24 答案 A

\(90!\) 末尾零的个数为 \(\left\lfloor\dfrac{90}{5}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{90}{25}\right\rfloor=21\)。令 \(N=\dfrac{90!}{10^{21}}\)。

去掉 \(10^{21}\) 后仍剩下超过两个因数 \(2\)，所以 \(N\equiv0 \pmod4\)。

设 \(A\) 为 \(90!\) 中不被 \(5\) 整除的因数之积，\(B\) 为被 \(5\) 整除的因数之积。按模 \(25\) 的余数分组，得到 \(A\equiv1\pmod{25}\) 且 \(\dfrac{B}{5^{21}}\equiv-1\pmod{25}\)。

因此 \(\dfrac{90!}{5^{21}}\equiv-1\pmod{25}\)。又因为 \(2^{21}\equiv2\pmod{25}\)，所以 \(N=\dfrac{90!}{5^{21}\cdot2^{21}}\) \(\equiv-13\) \(\equiv12\pmod{25}\)。

同时满足 \(0\pmod4\) 和 \(12\pmod{25}\) 的数为 \(12\pmod{100}\)，所以最后两个非零数字组成 \(12\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The number of trailing zeroes in \(90!\) is \(\left\lfloor\dfrac{90}{5}\right\rfloor+\left\lfloor\dfrac{90}{25}\right\rfloor=21.\) Let \(N=\dfrac{90!}{10^{21}}.\)

There are still more than two factors of \(2\) left after removing \(10^{21},\) so \(N\equiv0 \pmod4.\)

Let \(A\) be the product of factors of \(90!\) not divisible by \(5,\) and let \(B\) be the product of the factors divisible by \(5.\) Grouping residues modulo \(25\) gives \(A\equiv1\pmod{25}\) and \(\dfrac{B}{5^{21}}\equiv-1\pmod{25}.\)

Therefore \(\dfrac{90!}{5^{21}}\equiv-1\pmod{25}.\) Since \(2^{21}\equiv2\pmod{25},\) \(N=\dfrac{90!}{5^{21}\cdot2^{21}}\) \(\equiv-13\) \(\equiv12\pmod{25}.\)

The number congruent to \(0\pmod4\) and \(12\pmod{25}\) is \(12\pmod{100},\) so the last two nonzero digits form \(12.\)

Thus, A is the correct answer.

25 答案 B

从 \(0\) 开始反向工作。加上 \(1^2\) 得到 \(1\)。

再次加上 \(1^2\) 得到 \(2\)，再加 \(1^2\) 得到 \(3\)。

若此时再加 \(1^2\)，会得到 \(4\)，但对于 \(4\)，\(1^2\) 不是不超过它的最大平方。

因此下一步加 \(2^2\) 得到 \(7\)。继续反向构造，直到正向数列共有八项，得到 \(7223\)。

\[\begin{array}{ccccc} {}&{}&{}&{}&7223\\ 7223&-&84^2&=&167\\ 167&-&12^2&=&23\\ 23&-&4^2&=&7\\ 7&-&2^2&=&3\\ 3&-&1^2&=&2\\2&-&1^2&=&1\\1&-&1^2&=&0\end{array}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

We can work backwards starting with \(0.\) From this, we can add on \(1^2\) to get \(1.\)

We can again add on \(1^2\) to get \(2.\) Again, adding on \(1^2\) gives us \(3.\)

If we add on \(1^2\) now, we get \(4,\) but then \(1^2\) is not the greatest square less than or equal to \(4.\)

Then adding on \(2^2\) gives us \(7.\) Continuing until there are eight terms in the forward sequence gives \(7223.\)

\[\begin{array}{ccccc} {}&{}&{}&{}&7223\\ 7223&-&84^2&=&167\\ 167&-&12^2&=&23\\ 23&-&4^2&=&7\\ 7&-&2^2&=&3\\ 3&-&1^2&=&2\\2&-&1^2&=&1\\1&-&1^2&=&0\end{array}\]

Thus, B is the correct answer.
