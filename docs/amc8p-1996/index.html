何塞、翠和卡里姆都从数 \(10\) 开始。何塞把数 \(10\) 减去 \(1\)，把答案加倍，然后加 \(2\)。翠把数 \(10\) 加倍，把答案减去 \(1\)，然后加 \(2\)。卡里姆把数 \(10\) 减去 \(1\)，给答案加 \(2\)，然后把结果加倍。谁得到的最终答案最大？

A何塞 B翠 C卡里姆 D何塞和翠 E翠和卡里姆

English

José, Thuy, and Kareem each start with the number \(10.\) José subtracts \(1\) from the number \(10,\) doubles his answer, and then adds \(2.\) Thuy doubles the number \(10,\) subtracts \(1\) from her answer, and then adds \(2.\) Kareem subtracts \(1\) from the number \(10,\) adds \(2\) to his answer, and then doubles the result. Who gets the largest final answer?

(A) José(B) Thuy(C) Kareem(D) José and Thuy(E) Thuy and Kareem

1 答案 B

\(36\) 的正因数是 \(1\)、\(2\)、\(3\)、\(4\)、\(6\)、\(9\)、\(12\)、\(18\)、\(36\)。其中只有 \(4\)、\(12\) 和 \(36\) 是 \(4\) 的倍数。

English

The positive factors of \(36\) are \(1,\) \(2,\) \(3,\) \(4,\) \(6,\) \(9,\) \(12,\) \(18,\) and \(36.\) Of these, only \(4,\) \(12,\) and \(36\) are multiples of \(4.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B .

2 答案 C

从 \(10\) 开始，何塞依次得到 \(9\)、\(18\)、\(20\)；翠依次得到 \(20\)、\(19\)、\(21\)；卡里姆依次得到 \(9\)、\(11\)、\(22\)。

卡里姆最后加倍，所以他加上的 \(2\) 也被加倍，得到最大结果。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Starting from \(10,\) José computes \(9,\) \(18,\) \(20;\) Thuy computes \(20,\) \(19,\) \(21;\) and Kareem computes \(9,\) \(11,\) \(22.\)

Kareem doubles last, so the \(2\) he adds is doubled too, giving the largest result.

Thus, the correct answer is C .

English

Thus, the correct answer is B .

5 答案 A

从数轴上看，\(P\) 和 \(Q\) 是负数，而 \(R\)、\(S\)、\(T\) 是正数。

\(P \cdot Q\) 为正；\(\dfrac{S}{Q} \cdot P\) 有两个负因子，所以为正；\(\dfrac{R}{P \cdot Q}\) 为正；\(\dfrac{S + T}{R}\) 也为正。因为 \(P\) 在 \(Q\) 左边，所以 \(P - Q\) 为负。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

From the number line, \(P\) and \(Q\) are negative and \(R,\) \(S,\) and \(T\) are positive.

Then \(P \cdot Q\) is positive; \(\dfrac{S}{Q} \cdot P\) has two negative factors, so it is positive; \(\dfrac{R}{P \cdot Q}\) is positive; and \(\dfrac{S + T}{R}\) is positive. Since \(P\) is to the left of \(Q,\) \(P - Q\) is negative.

Thus, the correct answer is A .

6 答案 C

使用三个最小的数 \(3\)、\(5\)、\(7\)。可能结果为 \(3(5 + 7) = 36\)、\(5(3 + 7) = 50\)、\(7(3 + 5) = 56\)。

让最小的数作为乘数得到最小结果 \(36\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Use the three smallest numbers \(3,\) \(5,\) and \(7.\) The choices are \(3(5 + 7) = 36,\) \(5(3 + 7) = 50,\) and \(7(3 + 5) = 56.\)

Making the smallest number the multiplier gives the least result, \(36.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C .

Thus, the correct answer is B .

8 答案 B

距离最小时，四点共线并且 \(C\)、\(D\) 朝 \(A\) 的方向排列：取 \(A = 0\)、\(B = 10\)、\(C = 6\)、\(D = 3\)。

因此 \(AD = 10 - 4 - 3 = 3\)。

English

The distance is smallest when the points are collinear with \(C\) and \(D\) toward \(A:\) take \(A = 0,\) \(B = 10,\) \(C = 6,\) \(D = 3.\)

Then \(AD = 10 - 4 - 3 = 3.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B .

10 答案 D

增加量是 \(\dfrac58 - \dfrac18 = \dfrac12\) 个油箱，对应 \(7.5\) 加仑。

因此满油箱容量为 \(2 \cdot 7.5 = 15\) 加仑。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

So a full tank holds \(2 \cdot 7.5 = 15\) gallons.

Thus, the correct answer is D .

11 答案 D

因为 \(x\) 是很小的正数，\(3 + x\) 和 \(3 - x\) 都接近 \(3\)，而 \(3 \cdot x\) 和 \(\frac{x}{3}\) 都接近 \(0\)。

但 \(\frac{3}{x}\) 是 \(3\) 后面跟着 \(1997\) 个零，远大于其他选项。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Since \(x\) is a very small positive number, \(3 + x\) and \(3 - x\) are near \(3,\) while \(3 \cdot x\) and \(\frac{x}{3}\) are near \(0.\)

But \(\frac{3}{x}\) is \(3\) followed by \(1997\) zeros, far larger than any other choice.

Thus, the correct answer is D .

12 答案 B

\(1\) 到 \(11\) 的和是 \(66\)。若剩下十个数的平均数是 \(6.1\)，它们的和必须是 \(10 \cdot 6.1 = 61\)。

所以被去掉的数是 \(66 - 61 = 5\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The sum of \(1\) through \(11\) is \(66.\) For ten numbers to average \(6.1,\) their sum must be \(10 \cdot 6.1 = 61.\)

So the removed number is \(66 - 61 = 5.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B .

13 答案 E

每一年人数乘以 \(1.5\)：\(800 \to 1200 \to 1800 \to 2700\)。

所以 \(800 \cdot 1.5^3 = 2700\)，这是 \(1999\) 年的预计人数。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Each year multiplies the count by \(1.5:\) \(800 \to 1200 \to 1800 \to 2700.\)

So \(800 \cdot 1.5^3 = 2700\) participants are expected in \(1999.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E .

14 答案 B

从 \(1\) 到 \(9\) 中选三个不同数字和为 \(23\)，只能是 \(6\)、\(8\)、\(9\)。水平行另外三个数字最小为 \(1 + 2 + 3 = 6\)，所以共享方格至多是 \(12 - 6 = 6\)。因此共享数字是 \(6\)。

六个数字是 \(6\)、\(8\)、\(9\)、\(1\)、\(2\)、\(3\)，总和为 \(29\)。等价地，\(23 + 12 - 6 = 29\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Three distinct digits from \(1\) through \(9\) summing to \(23\) must be \(6,\) \(8,\) and \(9.\) The row’s other three digits are at least \(1 + 2 + 3 = 6,\) so the shared square (belonging to both the column and the row) is at most \(12 - 6 = 6.\) Hence the shared digit is \(6.\)

The six digits are then \(6,\) \(8,\) \(9,\) \(1,\) \(2,\) and \(3,\) whose sum is \(29.\) Equivalently, \(23 + 12 - 6 = 29.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B .

15 答案 E

乘积的个位数字等于 \(2 \cdot 6 \cdot 2 \cdot 6 = 144\) 的个位数字，也就是 \(4\)。

一个以 \(4\) 结尾的数除以 \(5\) 时余 \(4\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The units digit of the product equals the units digit of \(2 \cdot 6 \cdot 2 \cdot 6 = 144,\) which is \(4.\)

A number ending in \(4,\) when divided by \(5,\) leaves remainder \(4.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E .

16 答案 C

每四项分组：\(1 - 2 - 3 + 4 = 0\)，\(5 - 6 - 7 + 8 = 0\)，依此类推。

共有 \(\frac{1996}{4} = 499\) 组，每组都等于 \(0\)，所以总和是 \(0\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Grouping in blocks of four gives \(1 - 2 - 3 + 4 = 0,\) \(5 - 6 - 7 + 8 = 0,\) and so on.

There are \(\frac{1996}{4} = 499\) such blocks, each equal to \(0,\) so the total is \(0.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C .

17 答案 C

因为 \(OPQR\) 是正方形，且 \(O = (0, 0)\)、\(Q = (2, 2)\)，所以 \(P = (2, 0)\)、\(R = (0, 2)\)，正方形面积为 \(2^2 = 4\)。

三角形 \(PQT\) 的竖直底边 \(PQ\) 长为 \(2\)。若 \(T = (t, 0)\) 位于 \(x\) 轴上，它的面积是 \(\tfrac12 \cdot 2 \cdot \lvert 2 - t\rvert = \lvert 2 - t\rvert\)。令 \(\lvert 2 - t\rvert = 4\)，得到 \(t = -2\) 或 \(t = 6\)。选项中只有 \(t = -2\)，所以 \(T = (-2, 0)\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Since \(OPQR\) is a square with \(O = (0, 0)\) and \(Q = (2, 2),\) we have \(P = (2, 0)\) and \(R = (0, 2),\) so the area is \(2^2 = 4.\)

Triangle \(PQT\) has vertical base \(PQ\) of length \(2,\) and \(T = (t, 0)\) lies on the \(x\)-axis. Its area is \(\tfrac12 \cdot 2 \cdot \lvert 2 - t\rvert = \lvert 2 - t\rvert.\) Setting \(\lvert 2 - t\rvert = 4\) gives \(t = -2\) or \(t = 6.\) Only \(t = -2\) appears among the choices, so \(T = (-2, 0).\)

Thus, the correct answer is C .

18 答案 A

加薪后，工资为 \(2000 \cdot 1.2 = 2400\)。

降薪后，工资为 \(2400 \cdot 0.8 = 1920\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

After the raise, the salary is \(2000 \cdot 1.2 = 2400.\)

After the cut, it is \(2400 \cdot 0.8 = 1920.\)

19 答案 C

East 喜欢网球的人数为 \(0.22 \cdot 2000 = 440\) 人；West 喜欢网球的人数为 \(0.40 \cdot 2500 = 1000\) 人。

两校合计 \(1440\) 名学生喜欢网球，总人数为 \(4500\)，比例为 \(\dfrac{1440}{4500} = 32\%\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

East has \(0.22 \cdot 2000 = 440\) tennis fans, and West has \(0.40 \cdot 2500 = 1000.\)

Together \(1440\) of the \(4500\) students prefer tennis, which is \(\dfrac{1440}{4500} = 32\%.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C .

20 答案 A

从 \(5\) 开始：\(\frac{1}{1 - 5} = -0.25\)，再按得到 \(\frac{1}{1 + 0.25} = 0.8\)，再按得到 \(\frac{1}{1 - 0.8} = 5\)。数值按周期 \(3\) 循环。

因为 \(100 = 3 \cdot 33 + 1\)，第 \(100\) 次按键与第一次按键结果相同，为 \(-0.25\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Starting from \(5:\) \(\frac{1}{1 - 5} = -0.25,\) then \(\frac{1}{1 + 0.25} = 0.8,\) then \(\frac{1}{1 - 0.8} = 5.\) The values repeat with period \(3.\)

Since \(100 = 3 \cdot 33 + 1,\) the \(100\)th press gives the same result as the first press, \(-0.25.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A .

21 答案 D

集合中有 \(4\) 个奇数：\(89\)、\(95\)、\(99\)、\(173\)，以及 \(2\) 个偶数：\(132\)、\(166\)。三个数和为偶数需要两个奇数和一个偶数，因为只有两个偶数，无法选三个偶数。

数量为 \(\binom{4}{2} \cdot \binom{2}{1} = 6 \cdot 2 = 12\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The set has \(4\) odd numbers—\(89,\) \(95,\) \(99,\) and \(173\)—and \(2\) even numbers—\(132\) and \(166.\) A sum of three is even only with two odds and one even, since three evens is impossible with just two available.

The count is \(\binom{4}{2} \cdot \binom{2}{1} = 6 \cdot 2 = 12.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D .

22 答案 B

取 \(A = (0, 0)\)、\(B = (3, 2)\)、\(C = (4, 3)\)，由行列式面积公式可得 \[\frac12\left\lvert 3\cdot3-2\cdot4\right\rvert=\frac12\text{。}\]

等价地，用 Pick 定理，内部格点数为零，边界格点数为 \(3\)，面积为 \(0 + \tfrac32 - 1 = \tfrac12\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Taking \(A = (0, 0),\) \(B = (3, 2),\) and \(C = (4, 3),\) the determinant formula gives \[\frac12\left\lvert 3\cdot3-2\cdot4\right\rvert=\frac12.\]

Equivalently, by Pick’s theorem with no interior lattice points and \(3\) boundary points, the area is \(0 + \tfrac32 - 1 = \tfrac12.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B .

23 答案 E

设员工人数为 \(n\)。基金数为 \(50n - 5\)（比每人 \(50\) 美元少五美元），也等于 \(45n + 95\)。

令 \(50n - 5 = 45n + 95\)，得 \(5n = 100\)，所以 \(n = 20\)。基金数为 \(45 \cdot 20 + 95 = 995\) 美元。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Let \(n\) be the number of employees. The fund is \(50n - 5\) (five dollars short of \(50\) each) and also \(45n + 95.\)

Setting \(50n - 5 = 45n + 95\) gives \(5n = 100,\) so \(n = 20.\) The fund is \(45 \cdot 20 + 95 = 995.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E .

24 答案 C

在三角形 \(ABC\) 中，\[\begin{aligned} \angle BAC + \angle BCA &= 180^\circ - 50^\circ \\ &= 130^\circ \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

角平分线给出 \(\angle DAC + \angle DCA = \tfrac{130^\circ}{2} = 65^\circ\)。在三角形 \(ADC\) 中，\(\angle ADC = 180^\circ - 65^\circ = 115^\circ\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

In triangle \(ABC,\) \[ \begin{aligned} \angle BAC + \angle BCA &= 180^\circ - 50^\circ \\ &= 130^\circ. \end{aligned} \]

The bisectors give \(\angle DAC + \angle DCA = \tfrac{130^\circ}{2} = 65^\circ.\) In triangle \(ADC,\) \(\angle ADC = 180^\circ - 65^\circ = 115^\circ.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C .

25 答案 A

设圆半径为 \(1\)。距圆心 \(r\) 的点到圆心比到边界更近，当且仅当 \(r \lt 1 - r\)，即 \(r \lt \tfrac12\)。

有利区域是半径为 \(\tfrac12\) 的圆，面积为 \(\pi(\tfrac12)^2 = \tfrac{\pi}{4}\)，整个圆面积为 \(\pi\)。概率为 \(\dfrac{\frac{\pi}{4}}{\pi} = \dfrac14\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Take the radius to be \(1.\) A point at distance \(r\) from the center is closer to the center than to the boundary when \(r \lt 1 - r,\) i.e. \(r \lt \tfrac12.\)

The favorable region is a circle of radius \(\tfrac12,\) with area \(\pi(\tfrac12)^2 = \tfrac{\pi}{4},\) out of the total area \(\pi.\) The probability is \(\dfrac{\frac{\pi}{4}}{\pi} = \dfrac14.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A .
