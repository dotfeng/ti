1

你和五位朋友需要为慈善机构筹集 \(1500\) 美元的捐款，并且平均分担筹款任务。你们每个人需要筹集多少美元？

A\(250\) B\(300\) C\(1500\) D\(7500\) E\(9000\)

English

You and five friends need to raise $\(1500\) in donations for a charity, dividing the fundraising equally. How many dollars will each of you need to raise?

(A) \(250\)(B) \(300\)(C) \(1500\)(D) \(7500\)(E) \(9000\)

2

对任意三个实数 \(a\)、\(b\)、\(c\)，其中 \(b \neq c\)，定义运算 \(\diamond\) 为 \[\diamond(a, b, c) = \dfrac{a}{b - c}\text{。}\]求 \(\diamond(\diamond(1, 2, 3), \diamond(2, 3, 1), \diamond(3, 1, 2))\) 的值。

A\(-\dfrac{1}{2}\) B\(-\dfrac{1}{4}\) C\(0\) D\(\dfrac{1}{4}\) E\(\dfrac{1}{2}\)

English

For any three real numbers \(a,\) \(b,\) and \(c,\) with \(b \neq c,\) the operation \(\diamond\) is defined by \[\diamond(a, b, c) = \dfrac{a}{b - c}.\] What is \(\diamond(\diamond(1, 2, 3), \diamond(2, 3, 1), \diamond(3, 1, 2))?\)

(A) \(-\dfrac{1}{2}\)(B) \(-\dfrac{1}{4}\)(C) \(0\)(D) \(\dfrac{1}{4}\)(E) \(\dfrac{1}{2}\)

3

Alicia 每小时赚 \(20\) 美元，其中 \(1.45\%\) 会被扣除用于缴纳地方税。她每小时工资中有多少美分用于缴纳地方税？

A\(0.0029\) B\(0.029\) C\(0.29\) D\(2.9\) E\(29\)

English

Alicia earns $\(20\) per hour, of which \(1.45\%\) is deducted to pay local taxes. How many cents per hour of Alicia’s wages are used to pay local taxes?

(A) \(0.0029\)(B) \(0.029\)(C) \(0.29\)(D) \(2.9\)(E) \(29\)

4

若 \(|x - 1| = |x - 2|\)，则 \(x\) 的值是多少？

A\(-\dfrac{1}{2}\) B\(\dfrac{1}{2}\) C\(1\) D\(\dfrac{3}{2}\) E\(2\)

English

What is the value of \(x\) if \(|x - 1| = |x - 2|?\)

(A) \(-\dfrac{1}{2}\)(B) \(\dfrac{1}{2}\)(C) \(1\)(D) \(\dfrac{3}{2}\)(E) \(2\)

5

从图中网格点随机选取三个点，每组三点被选中的概率相同。这三个点在同一直线上的概率是多少？

A\(\dfrac{1}{21}\) B\(\dfrac{1}{14}\) C\(\dfrac{2}{21}\) D\(\dfrac{1}{7}\) E\(\dfrac{2}{7}\)

English

A set of three points is chosen randomly from the grid shown. Each three-point set has the same probability of being chosen. What is the probability that the points lie on the same straight line?

(A) \(\dfrac{1}{21}\)(B) \(\dfrac{1}{14}\)(C) \(\dfrac{2}{21}\)(D) \(\dfrac{1}{7}\)(E) \(\dfrac{2}{7}\)

6

Bertha 有 \(6\) 个女儿，没有儿子。她的一些女儿各有 \(6\) 个女儿，其余女儿没有女儿。Bertha 的女儿和外孙女总共有 \(30\) 人，并且没有曾外孙女。Bertha 的女儿和外孙女中，有多少人没有女儿？

A\(22\) B\(23\) C\(24\) D\(25\) E\(26\)

English

Bertha has \(6\) daughters and no sons. Some of her daughters have \(6\) daughters, and the rest have none. Bertha has a total of \(30\) daughters and granddaughters, and no great-granddaughters. How many of Bertha’s daughters and granddaughters have no daughters?

(A) \(22\)(B) \(23\)(C) \(24\)(D) \(25\)(E) \(26\)

7

一位杂货商把橙子堆成类似金字塔的形状，长方形底层为 \(5\) 个橙子乘 \(8\) 个橙子。第一层以上的每个橙子都放在下一层四个橙子形成的凹处。最上层是一排橙子。这个橙子堆共有多少个橙子？

A\(96\) B\(98\) C\(100\) D\(101\) E\(134\)

English

A grocer stacks oranges in a pyramid-like stack whose rectangular base is \(5\) oranges by \(8\) oranges. Each orange above the first level rests in a pocket formed by four oranges in the level below. The stack is completed by a single row of oranges. How many oranges are in the stack?

(A) \(96\)(B) \(98\)(C) \(100\)(D) \(101\)(E) \(134\)

8

A\(36\) B\(37\) C\(38\) D\(39\) E\(40\)

English

A game is played with tokens according to the following rule. In each round, the player with the most tokens gives one token to each of the other players and also places one token into a discard pile. The game ends when some player runs out of tokens. Players \(A,\) \(B,\) and \(C\) start with \(15,\) \(14,\) and \(13\) tokens, respectively. How many rounds will there be in the game?

(A) \(36\)(B) \(37\)(C) \(38\)(D) \(39\)(E) \(40\)

9

11

(A) \(10\)(B) \(25\)(C) \(36\)(D) \(50\)(E) \(60\)

12

Henry’s Hamburger Heaven 提供以下汉堡配料：番茄酱、芥末酱、蛋黄酱、番茄、生菜、腌黄瓜、奶酪和洋葱。顾客可以选择一块、两块或三块肉饼，并可选择任意一组配料。共有多少种不同的汉堡可以点？

(A) \(24\)(B) \(256\)(C) \(768\)(D) \(40{,}320\)(E) \(120{,}960\)

13

English

(A) \(8\)(B) \(12\)(C) \(16\)(D) \(18\)(E) \(24\)

14

Paula 钱包中所有一分、五分、十分和二十五分硬币的平均面值是 \(20\) 美分。如果她再多一枚二十五分硬币，平均面值会变为 \(21\) 美分。她钱包中有多少枚十分硬币？

A\(0\) B\(1\) C\(2\) D\(3\) E\(4\)

English

The average value of all the pennies, nickels, dimes, and quarters in Paula’s purse is \(20\) cents. If she had one more quarter, the average value would be \(21\) cents. How many dimes does she have in her purse?

(A) \(0\)(B) \(1\)(C) \(2\)(D) \(3\)(E) \(4\)

15

A\(-1\) B\(-\dfrac{1}{2}\) C\(0\) D\(\dfrac{1}{2}\) E\(1\)

English

16

17

Brenda 和 Sally 从圆形跑道上一对直径相对的点出发，沿相反方向跑。她们第一次相遇时 Brenda 跑了 \(100\) 米。下一次相遇时，Sally 已经从第一次相遇点又跑了 \(150\) 米。两人都以恒定速度跑。跑道长多少米？

A\(250\) B\(300\) C\(350\) D\(400\) E\(500\)

English

Brenda and Sally run in opposite directions on a circular track, starting at diametrically opposite points. They first meet after Brenda has run \(100\) meters. They next meet after Sally has run \(150\) meters past their first meeting point. Each girl runs at a constant speed. What is the length of the track in meters?

(A) \(250\)(B) \(300\)(C) \(350\)(D) \(400\)(E) \(500\)

18

三个实数组成一个等差数列，第一项为 \(9\)。若第二项加 \(2\)，第三项加 \(20\)，所得三个数形成等比数列。该等比数列第三项的最小可能值是多少？

A sequence of three real numbers forms an arithmetic progression with a first term of \(9.\) If \(2\) is added to the second term and \(20\) is added to the third term, the three resulting numbers form a geometric progression. What is the smallest possible value for the third term of the geometric progression?

(A) \(1\)(B) \(4\)(C) \(36\)(D) \(49\)(E) \(81\)

19

20

22

逐题解析

1 答案 A

包括你在内共有 \(6\) 个人平均分担筹款。每人需要筹集 \[ \dfrac{1500}{6} = 250 \] 美元。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Including you, there are \(6\) people sharing the fundraising equally. Each must raise \[ \dfrac{1500}{6} = 250 \] dollars.

Thus, the correct answer is A.

2 答案 B

三个内层表达式的值为 \[ \begin{aligned} \diamond(1,2,3) &= \dfrac{1}{2-3} = -1, \\ \diamond(2,3,1) &= \dfrac{2}{3-1} = 1, \\ \diamond(3,1,2) &= \dfrac{3}{1-2} = -3 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因此 \[ \begin{aligned} \diamond(-1, 1, -3) &= \dfrac{-1}{1 - (-3)} \\ &= -\dfrac{1}{4} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The inner values are \[ \begin{aligned} \diamond(1,2,3) &= \dfrac{1}{2-3} = -1, \\ \diamond(2,3,1) &= \dfrac{2}{3-1} = 1, \\ \diamond(3,1,2) &= \dfrac{3}{1-2} = -3. \end{aligned} \]

Therefore \[ \begin{aligned} \diamond(-1, 1, -3) &= \dfrac{-1}{1 - (-3)} \\ &= -\dfrac{1}{4}. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is B.

3 答案 E

\(20\) 美元等于 \(2000\) 美分，地方税为每小时 \[ 0.0145 \times 2000 = 29 \] 美分。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Since $\(20\) equals \(2000\) cents, the local tax is \[ 0.0145 \times 2000 = 29 \] cents per hour.

Thus, the correct answer is E.

4 答案 D

由于 \(|x - 1|\) 和 \(|x - 2|\) 分别表示 \(x\) 到 \(1\) 和 \(2\) 的距离，所以点 \(x\) 到 \(1\) 和 \(2\) 的距离相等。

到一和二距离相等的点是它们的中点：\[ x = \dfrac{1 + 2}{2} = \dfrac{3}{2}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Since \(|x - 1|\) and \(|x - 2|\) are the distances from \(x\) to \(1\) and \(2,\) the point \(x\) is equidistant from \(1\) and \(2.\)

That midpoint is \[ x = \dfrac{1 + 2}{2} = \dfrac{3}{2}. \]

Thus, the correct answer is D.

5 答案 C

三点集合总数为 \[ \binom{9}{3} = 84\text{。}\]

共线三点包括 \(3\) 行、\(3\) 列和 \(2\) 条主对角线，共 \(8\) 组。

因此所求概率为 \[ \dfrac{8}{84} = \dfrac{2}{21}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The number of three-point sets is \[ \binom{9}{3} = 84. \]

The collinear triples are the \(3\) rows, the \(3\) columns, and the \(2\) main diagonals, for a total of \(8.\)

The probability is therefore \[ \dfrac{8}{84} = \dfrac{2}{21}. \]

Thus, the correct answer is C.

6 答案 E

Bertha 有 \(30 - 6 = 24\) 个外孙女，这些外孙女都没有女儿。

她们来自 \(\frac{24}{6} = 4\) 个 Bertha 的女儿，所以正好 \(4\) 人有女儿，没有女儿的人数为 \[ 30 - 4 = 26\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Bertha has \(30 - 6 = 24\) granddaughters, none of whom have daughters.

These granddaughters belong to \(\frac{24}{6} = 4\) of Bertha’s daughters. So exactly \(4\) women have daughters, and the number with no daughters is \[ 30 - 4 = 26. \]

Thus, the correct answer is E.

7 答案 C

共有五层，每层都比下面一层少一行且少一列。橙子总数为 \[ \begin{aligned} &5\cdot 8 + 4\cdot 7 + 3\cdot 6 \\ &\quad {}+ 2\cdot 5 + 1\cdot 4 \\ &= 40 + 28 + 18 + 10 + 4 = 100 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

There are five layers, each one shorter and narrower than the one below. The total number of oranges is \[ \begin{aligned} &5\cdot 8 + 4\cdot 7 + 3\cdot 6 \\ &\quad {}+ 2\cdot 5 + 1\cdot 4 \\ &= 40 + 28 + 18 + 10 + 4 = 100. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is C.

8 答案 B

前三轮后，筹码数从 \((15, 14, 13)\) 变为 \((14, 13, 12)\)。

一般地，每三轮后每位玩家都减少一枚筹码。\(36\) 轮后筹码数为 \((3, 2, 1)\)。第 \(37\) 轮中，领先者给出三枚筹码后降到 \(0\)，游戏结束。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

14 答案 A

若有 \(n\) 枚硬币，总面值为 \(20n\) 美分。加一枚二十五分硬币后，方程为 \[ 20n + 25 = 21(n + 1)\text{，}\]解得 \(n = 4\)。

如果二十五分硬币至多有两枚，其余硬币每枚最多值 \(10\) 美分，那么四枚硬币的总值至多为 \(2(25)+2(10)=70\) 美分。因此必须有三枚二十五分硬币，第四枚硬币的面值为 \(5\) 美分。钱包中有三枚二十五分硬币和一枚五分硬币，所以十分硬币有 \(0\) 枚。

21 答案 B

设锐角为 \(\theta\)。阴影区域分三部分：单位圆中的两个锐角扇形总面积为 \(\theta\)；半径 \(1\) 到 \(2\) 的圆环中两个钝角扇形总面积为 \(3(\pi - \theta)\)；半径 \(2\) 到 \(3\) 的圆环中两个锐角扇形总面积为 \(5\theta\)。

相加得到阴影面积 \[ \theta + 3(\pi - \theta) + 5\theta = 3\pi + 3\theta\text{。}\]

阴影面积是非阴影面积的 \(\dfrac{8}{13}\)，因此是总面积 \(9\pi\) 的 \(\dfrac{8}{21}\)。于是 \[ 3\pi + 3\theta = \dfrac{8}{21}(9\pi) = \dfrac{24\pi}{7}\text{，}\]解得 \(\theta = \dfrac{\pi}{7}\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Let \(\theta\) be the acute angle. The shaded region has three parts: two acute sectors of the unit disk with total area \(\theta,\) two obtuse sectors of the ring between radii \(1\) and \(2\) with total area \(3(\pi - \theta),\) and two acute sectors of the ring between radii \(2\) and \(3\) with total area \(5\theta.\)

Adding these gives a shaded area of \[ \theta + 3(\pi - \theta) + 5\theta = 3\pi + 3\theta. \]

The shaded region is \(\dfrac{8}{13}\) of the unshaded region, so it is \(\dfrac{8}{21}\) of the total area \(9\pi.\) Then \[ 3\pi + 3\theta = \dfrac{8}{21}(9\pi) = \dfrac{24\pi}{7}, \] which gives \(\theta = \dfrac{\pi}{7}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

25 答案 B

三个小球球心形成边长为 \(2\) 的等边三角形，每个球心距平面 \(1\)。设其重心为 \(D\)，则它到每个顶点的距离为 \(\dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}\)。

大球球心 \(E\) 位于 \(D\) 正上方，且 \(E\) 到每个小球球心的距离为 \(1 + 2 = 3\)，所以 \[ \begin{aligned} DE &= \sqrt{3^2 - \left(\dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}\right)^2} \\ &= \sqrt{9 - \dfrac{4}{3}} = \dfrac{\sqrt{69}}{3} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

再加上平面到 \(D\) 的 \(1\) 个单位，以及从 \(E\) 到大球顶部的 \(2\) 个单位，得到 \[ 1 + \dfrac{\sqrt{69}}{3} + 2 = 3 + \dfrac{\sqrt{69}}{3}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The three small centers form an equilateral triangle of side \(2,\) each \(1\) unit above the plane. Its centroid \(D\) is at distance \(\dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}\) from each vertex.

The large sphere’s center \(E\) sits directly above \(D,\) and the distance between \(E\) and a small center is \(1 + 2 = 3.\) Thus \[ \begin{aligned} DE &= \sqrt{3^2 - \left(\dfrac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}\right)^2} \\ &= \sqrt{9 - \dfrac{4}{3}} = \dfrac{\sqrt{69}}{3}. \end{aligned} \]

Adding the \(1\) unit from the plane to \(D\) and the \(2\) units from \(E\) to the top of the large sphere gives \[ 1 + \dfrac{\sqrt{69}}{3} + 2 = 3 + \dfrac{\sqrt{69}}{3}. \]

Thus, the correct answer is B.
