1 答案 B

展开两个方括号中的式子，得到 \[ \begin{aligned} &[x-(y-x)]-[(x-y)-x]\\ &\qquad=(2x-y)-(-y)=2x\text{。} \end{aligned} \]

English

Expanding the two bracketed expressions gives \[ \begin{aligned} &[x-(y-x)]-[(x-y)-x]\\ &\qquad=(2x-y)-(-y)=2x. \end{aligned} \]

Thus the correct answer is B.

2 答案 A

原直线的斜率为 \(\frac{2}{3}\)，\(y\) 轴截距为 \(4\)。因此，\(L\) 的斜率为 \(\frac{1}{3}\)，\(y\) 轴截距为 \(8\)，所以它的方程是 \(y=\frac13x+8\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

By AM-GM, every variable is at least \(\sqrt{17}.\) For \(t\gt\sqrt{17},\) \[ \frac{t+\frac{17}{t}}{2}\lt t. \] If any variable exceeded \(\sqrt{17},\) the equations would give the impossible strict cycle \(x\gt y\gt z\gt w\gt x.\) Hence the only positive solution is \[ x=y=z=w=\sqrt{17}. \] Negating all four variables preserves the system, giving exactly one negative solution, with all variables equal to \(-\sqrt{17}.\) Thus there are two real solutions.

Therefore the correct answer is B.
