4

(A) \(\dfrac{x}{100lm}\)(B) \(\dfrac{xlm}{100}\)(C) \(\dfrac{lm}{100x}\)(D) \(\dfrac{100}{xlm}\)(E) \(\dfrac{100lm}{x}\)

12

万圣节时，\(31\) 个孩子走进校长办公室要糖果。他们可分为三类：有些总是说谎，有些总是说真话，还有些真话和谎话交替说。交替者可以任意选择第一次回答是真话还是谎话，但之后每次回答的真假都与前一次相反。校长按顺序问每个人同样的三个问题。

“你是说真话者吗？”校长给每个回答“是”的孩子一颗糖，共有 \(22\) 人回答“是”。

“你是交替者吗？”校长给每个回答“是”的孩子一颗糖，共有 \(15\) 人回答“是”。

“你是说谎者吗？”校长给每个回答“是”的孩子一颗糖，共有 \(9\) 人回答“是”。

校长总共给那些总是说真话的孩子多少颗糖？

A\(7\) B\(12\) C\(21\) D\(27\) E\(31\)

English

On Halloween \(31\) children walked into the principal’s office asking for candy. They can be classified into three types: Some always lie; some always tell the truth; and some alternately lie and tell the truth. The alternaters arbitrarily choose their first response, either a lie or the truth, but each subsequent statement has the opposite truth value from its predecessor. The principal asked everyone the same three questions in this order.

“Are you a truth-teller?” The principal gave a piece of candy to each of the \(22\) children who answered yes.

“Are you an alternater?” The principal gave a piece of candy to each of the \(15\) children who answered yes.

“Are you a liar?” The principal gave a piece of candy to each of the \(9\) children who answered yes.

How many pieces of candy in all did the principal give to the children who always tell the truth?

(A) \(7\)(B) \(12\)(C) \(21\)(D) \(27\)(E) \(31\)

(A) \(8\)(B) \(9\)(C) \(10\)(D) \(11\)(E) \(12\)

After \(T_4,\) we get \((1, -2^{\circ}).\)

\[\vdots\]

After \(T_{2m - 1},\) we get \((1, 180^{\circ} - m^{\circ}).\)

After \(T_{2m},\) we get \((1, -m^{\circ}).\)

From this, we can see that the first time the angle is back to \(0^{\circ}\) is after \(T_{2(180)-1}=T_{359}.\) Therefore \(n=359.\)

Thus, A is the correct answer.

19 答案 C

将调和和乘以 \(L_{17}\)，得到 \(h=\sum_{i=1}^{17}\frac{L_{17}}{i}\)。

对 \(1\le i\le16\)，项 \(\frac{L_{17}}{i}\) 仍能被 \(17\) 整除，所以这些项贡献 \(0\pmod{17}\)。

因此 \(h\equiv \frac{L_{17}}{17}\pmod{17}\)。最小公倍数 \(L_{17}\) 含有素数幂因子 \(16,9,5,7,11,13,17\)，所以 \[ \begin{aligned} \frac{L_{17}}{17} &\equiv 16\cdot9\cdot5\cdot7\cdot11\cdot13 \\ &\pmod{17} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

模 \(17\) 化简后，这就是 \((-1)\cdot9\cdot5\cdot7\cdot11\cdot13\) \(\equiv5\pmod{17}\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Multiplying the harmonic sum by \(L_{17},\) we get \(h=\sum_{i=1}^{17}\frac{L_{17}}{i}.\)

For \(1\le i\le16,\) the term \(\frac{L_{17}}{i}\) is still divisible by \(17,\) so these terms contribute \(0\pmod{17}.\)

Thus \(h\equiv \frac{L_{17}}{17}\pmod{17}.\) The least common multiple \(L_{17}\) contains the prime-power factors \(16,9,5,7,11,13,17,\) so \[ \begin{aligned} \frac{L_{17}}{17} &\equiv 16\cdot9\cdot5\cdot7\cdot11\cdot13 \\ &\pmod{17}. \end{aligned} \]

Reducing modulo \(17,\) this is \((-1)\cdot9\cdot5\cdot7\cdot11\cdot13\) \(\equiv5\pmod{17}.\)

Thus, C is the correct answer.

20 答案 E

设等差数列为 \[ a, a + d, a + 2d, a + 3d\text{，}\]等比数列为 \[ b, br, br^2, br^3\text{。}\]

则 \[ a + b = 57 \tag*{(1)}\text{，}\]\[ a + d + br = 60 \tag*{(2)}\text{，}\]且 \[ a + 2d + br^2 = 91 \tag*{(3)}\text{。}\]

用 \((2)\) 减去 \((1)\)，再用 \((3)\) 减去 \((2)\)，得到 \[ d + b(r - 1) = 3 \] 和 \[ d + br(r - 1) = 31\text{。}\]

两式相减，得 \[ b(r - 1)^2 = 28\text{。}\]

令 \(t=b(r-1)=br-b\)，它是整数。则 \(t^2=28b\)，所以对某个非零整数 \(u\)，有 \(t=14u\)、\(b=7u^2\)。因为 \(a=57-b>0\)，必须有 \(u\in\{-2,-1,1,2\}\)。

现在 \(r=1+\frac{t}{b}=1+\frac{2}{u}\)。当 \(u=-1\) 或 \(u=-2\) 时，分别得到 \(r=-1\) 和 \(r=0\)，不能形成正数等比数列。若 \(u=2\)，则 \(b=28, r=2, a=29\)，且 \(d=3-t=-25\)，使等差数列第三项为负。

因此 \(u=1\)，所以 \(b = 7, r = 3, a = 50\)，且 \(d = -11\)。等差数列为 \[ 50,39,28,17\text{，}\]等比数列为 \[ 7,21,63,189\text{。}\]

所求答案为 \(17 + 189 = 206\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Let the arithmetic sequence be \[ a, a + d, a + 2d, a + 3d \] and the geometric sequence be \[ b, br, br^2, br^3. \]

Then \[ a + b = 57 \tag*{(1)}, \] \[ a + d + br = 60 \tag*{(2)}, \] and \[ a + 2d + br^2 = 91 \tag*{(3)}. \]

Subtracting \((1)\) from \((2)\) and \((2)\) from \((3),\) we get \[ d + b(r - 1) = 3 \] and \[ d + br(r - 1) = 31. \]

Subtracting these, we get \[ b(r - 1)^2 = 28. \]

Let \(t=b(r-1)=br-b,\) which is an integer. Then \(t^2=28b,\) so \(t=14u\) and \(b=7u^2\) for some nonzero integer \(u.\) Because \(a=57-b>0,\) we must have \(u\in\{-2,-1,1,2\}.\)

Now \(r=1+\frac{t}{b}=1+\frac{2}{u}.\) The cases \(u=-1\) and \(u=-2\) give \(r=-1\) and \(r=0,\) respectively, so they cannot produce a positive geometric sequence. If \(u=2,\) then \(b=28, r=2, a=29,\) and \(d=3-t=-25,\) making the third arithmetic term negative.

Therefore \(u=1,\) so \(b = 7, r = 3, a = 50,\) and \(d = -11.\) The arithmetic sequence is \[ 50,39,28,17, \] and the geometric sequence is \[ 7,21,63,189. \]

The desired answer is \(17 + 189 = 206.\)

Thus, E is the correct answer.

21 答案 B

从正上方观察碗口。把底部正方形放在 \((\pm\tfrac12,\pm\tfrac12,0)\)。正六边形中，与所接边相邻的一条边，其平行于该边和垂直于该边的分量分别为 \(\tfrac12\) 与 \(\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\)。

在底部正方形的一个顶点处，相邻两个六边形的对应边重合。比较它们的水平分量可知，在六边形平面内，垂直于所接边的单位向量的水平分量为 \(\frac{1}{\sqrt3}\)。正六边形的对边与所接边相距 \(\sqrt3\)，所以水平向外位移为 \(1\)。

因此，碗口的四条单位长度边都位于底部正方形对应边之外一个单位处。其俯视图是一个 \(3\) 乘 \(3\) 的正方形，去掉四个直角边为 \(1\) 的等腰直角三角形：

面积为 \[3^2-4\left(\frac12\cdot1^2\right)=7\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

View the rim from directly above. Place the bottom square at \((\pm\tfrac12,\pm\tfrac12,0).\) In a regular hexagon, an edge adjacent to the attached side has components \(\tfrac12\) parallel and \(\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\) perpendicular to that side.

At a corner of the bottom square, the corresponding edges of two adjacent hexagons coincide. Comparing their horizontal components shows that the horizontal component of a unit vector perpendicular to an attached side within its hexagon is \(\frac{1}{\sqrt3}.\) The opposite side of a regular hexagon is \(\sqrt3\) units from the attached side, so its horizontal outward displacement is \(1.\)

Consequently, the four unit-length sides of the rim lie one unit beyond the four sides of the bottom square. Its top view is therefore a \(3\)-by-\(3\) square with four isosceles right corner triangles of leg \(1\) removed:

Its area is \[3^2-4\left(\frac12\cdot1^2\right)=7.\]

Thus, B is the correct answer.

22 答案 D

设第一次扫描拿到 \(n\) 张卡片，其中 \(1 \leq n \leq 12\)。

选择这 \(n\) 张卡片所占的位置后，剩余卡片的位置也被确定，因为两组卡片都必须按递增顺序排列。

共有 \(\binom{13}{n}\) 种方法选择这 \(n\) 张卡片的位置；但若这 \(n\) 张卡片恰好位于最前面，则所有卡片都会在第一次扫描中拿完。

因此对固定的 \(n\)，共有 \(\binom{13}{n} - 1\) 种排列。

对 \(1\le n\le12\) 求和，得到 \[ \begin{gathered} \sum_{n=1}^{12}\left(\binom{13}{n}-1\right) \\ = (2^{13}-2)-12 \\ = 8178 \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Let \(n\) be the number of cards picked up on the first pass, where \(1 \leq n \leq 12.\)

If we choose the spaces that the \(n\) cards occupy, the positions of the remaining cards are determined since they must be placed in order.

There are \(\binom{13}{n}\) ways to choose where the \(n\) cards go, but if the \(n\) cards are placed at the very beginning, then all the cards will be picked up on the first pass.

Therefore, for a given \(n\) there are \(\binom{13}{n} - 1\) ways to arrange the cards.

Summing over \(1\le n\le12,\) we get \[ \begin{gathered} \sum_{n=1}^{12}\left(\binom{13}{n}-1\right) \\ = (2^{13}-2)-12 \\ = 8178. \end{gathered} \]

Thus, D is the correct answer.

23 答案 B

令 \(P'\) 为 \(P\) 关于 \(\overline{BC}\) 的垂直平分线的反射点。

这样得到两个等腰梯形 \(CBPP'\) 和 \(DAPP'\)。

由反射对称可得 \[\begin{gathered} P'A = PD = 4 \\ P'D = PA = 1 \\ P'C = PB = 2 \\ P'B = PC = 3 \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

对两个圆内接梯形使用托勒密定理，知道对角线乘积等于两组对边乘积之和。因此 \[\begin{gathered} PP' \cdot AD + 1 = 16 \\ PP' \cdot BC + 4 = 9 \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

于是 \(PP' \cdot AD = 15\)，且 \(PP' \cdot BC = 5\)。两式相除得到 \(\dfrac{BC}{AD} = \dfrac{1}{3}\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Let \(P'\) be the reflection of \(P\) across the perpendicular bisector of \(\overline{BC}.\)

This forms two new isosceles trapezoids: \(CBPP'\) and \(DAPP'.\)

Therefore, we get \[\begin{gathered} P'A = PD = 4 \\ P'D = PA = 1 \\ P'C = PB = 2 \\ P'B = PC = 3. \end{gathered}\]

Using Ptolemy’s theorem, we know that the product of the diagonals is equal to the sum of the products of the opposite sides. Therefore: \[\begin{gathered} PP' \cdot AD + 1 = 16 \\ PP' \cdot BC + 4 = 9. \end{gathered}\]

This gets us \(PP' \cdot AD = 15\) and \(PP' \cdot BC = 5.\) Dividing these two equations yields \(\dfrac{BC}{AD} = \dfrac{1}{3}.\)

Thus, B is the correct answer.

24 答案 E

把这五个数字依次看作五辆车的首选车位。车位编号为 \(0,1,2,3,4\)，每辆车若首选车位空着就停在那里，否则停在右边第一个空位。若把首选车位按非递减顺序排列为 \(b_1\le b_2\le\cdots\le b_5\)，则所有车都能停下，当且仅当 \[b_i\le i-1\qquad(1\le i\le5)\text{。}\]这些不等式正是题目中的条件。

为了计数，加入第六个车位，把 \(0,1,\ldots,5\) 号车位排列在一个圆上。对任意一个 \(6^5\) 种首选字符串，五辆车都能停下，且恰好留下一个空位。把所有首选位置都旋转一格，空位也随之旋转。因此每个由六个字符串组成的轨道中，每一种可能的空位恰好出现一次。

所以恰有 \(\frac{6^5}{6}=6^4=1296\) 个圆形首选字符串留下 \(5\) 号车位为空。这样的字符串中没有车首选 \(5\) 号车位；紧接着这个空位把圆切开，就恰好得到车位 \(0\) 到 \(4\) 上的成功停车序列。因此所求字符串数为 \(1296\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Regard the five digits, in order, as the preferred parking spaces of five cars. Spaces are numbered \(0,1,2,3,4,\) and each car takes its preferred space if possible, or else the first empty space to its right. If the preferences sorted into nondecreasing order are \(b_1\le b_2\le\cdots\le b_5,\) all cars park exactly when \[b_i\le i-1\qquad(1\le i\le5).\] These inequalities are precisely the conditions in the problem.

To count such preference strings, add a sixth space and arrange spaces \(0,1,\ldots,5\) in a circle. For any of the \(6^5\) preference strings, all five cars park and exactly one space remains empty. Rotating every preference by one position rotates the empty space as well. Thus each orbit of six preference strings has each possible empty space exactly once.

Therefore exactly \(\frac{6^5}{6}=6^4=1296\) circular preference strings leave space \(5\) empty. No car in such a string prefers space \(5,\) and cutting the circle immediately after that empty space gives exactly a successful parking sequence on spaces \(0\) through \(4.\) Hence the desired number of strings is \(1296.\)

Thus, E is the correct answer.

25 答案 B

令 \(r\) 为 \(R\) 的每条边上的格点数；类似地，令 \(s\) 对应 \(S\)，令 \(t\) 对应 \(T\)。注意，一个矩形中的格点数等于沿其宽方向的格点数与沿其长方向的格点数之积。

第一个条件给出 \[ r^2 = \dfrac{9}{4} \cdot s^2 \]\[r = \dfrac{3}{2} \cdot s \tag*{(1)} \]

\(R \cup S\) 中的格点数等于两个区域各自格点数之和，但在 \(y\) 轴上有重叠，也就是正方形 \(S\) 与该轴相接之处。

因此第二个条件给出 \[ t^2 = \dfrac{1}{4}(r^2 + s^2 - s) \] \[ t^2 = \dfrac{1}{4}(\dfrac{9}{4} \cdot s^2 + s^2 - s) \] \[ t^2 = \dfrac{1}{4} \cdot \dfrac{13s^2 - 4s}{4} \] \[ 16t^2 = s(13s - 4)\text{。}\] 由 \((1)\) 可知 \(s\) 是 \(2\) 的倍数。把 \(s\) 换成 \(2j\)，得到 \[ 16t^2 = 2j(26j - 4) \] \[ 4t^2 = j(13j - 2)\text{。}\] 为了使乘积能被 \(4\) 整除，\(j\) 必须被 \(2\) 整除。再把 \(j\) 换成 \(2k\)，得到 \[ 4t^2 = 2k(26k - 2) \] \[ t^2 = k(13k - 1) \tag*{(2)} \]

令 \(x\) 为矩形 \(S \cap T\) 底边上的格点数，令 \(y\) 为矩形 \(R \cap T\) 底边上的格点数。

这样，\(S \cap T\) 中的格点数为 \(xt\)，而 \(R \cap T\) 中的格点数为 \(yt\)。

第三个条件给出 \[ \dfrac{xt}{s^2} = 27 \cdot \dfrac{yt}{r^2} \] \[ \dfrac{x}{s^2} = 27 \cdot \dfrac{y}{\dfrac{9}{4} s^2} \] \[ x = 12y\text{。}\]

又已知 \(t = x + y - 1\)（其中已计入重叠部分），于是 \[ t = 13y - 1 \tag*{(3)} \]

由 \((3)\) 可得 \[ \begin{gathered} t \equiv -1 \pmod{13}, \\ t^2 \equiv 1 \pmod{13} \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

然而由 \((2)\) 可得 \[ t^2 \equiv -k \pmod{13} \] \[ k \equiv -1 \pmod{13}\text{。}\]

同时由 \((2)\) 可知 \(k\) 是平方数，因为它与 \(13k - 1\) 互质，而它们的乘积是平方数。

因此 \(k\) 必须是满足 \(k\equiv-1\pmod{13}\) 的完全平方数。较小的正平方数 \(1,4,9,16\) 都不满足这一同余条件，而 \(k=25\) 满足，所以 \(25\) 是最小可能值。

由这个 \(k\) 值可得 \(j = 2 \cdot 25 = 50\)、\(s = 2 \cdot 50 = 100\)，以及 \(r = \dfrac{3}{2} \cdot 100 = 150\)。还可算得 \[ t^2 = 25(13 \cdot 25 - 1) = 25 \cdot 324 \] \[ t = 5 \cdot 18 = 90\text{。}\]因此 \[ r + s + t = 340\text{。}\]不过题目问的是边长之和。每个正方形的边长都比每边的格点数少 \(1\)，三个正方形共要减去 \(3\)。

这个值可以达到：由方程 \((3)\) 得 \(y=7\)，从而 \(x=84\)。因为 \(x\le s=100\)、\(y\le r=150\)，每边有 \(90\) 个格点的正方形 \(T\) 可以跨过 \(y\) 轴形成所需的重叠，而且它的上方两个顶点位于 \(R\cup S\) 中。

因此所求答案为 \(340 - 3 = 337\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Let \(r\) be the number of lattice points on the side length of \(R.\) Similarly define \(s\) for \(S\) and \(t\) for \(T.\) Note that the number of lattice points in a rectangle is the product of the number of lattice points along its width and the number of lattice points along its length.

The first condition gives us that \[ r^2 = \dfrac{9}{4} \cdot s^2 \]\[r = \dfrac{3}{2} \cdot s \tag*{(1)} \]

The number of lattice points in \(R \cup S\) is the sum of the lattice points in each of the regions, but there is overlap along the \(y\)-axis where \(S\) touches it.

The second condition, therefore, yields \[ t^2 = \dfrac{1}{4}(r^2 + s^2 - s) \] \[ t^2 = \dfrac{1}{4}(\dfrac{9}{4} \cdot s^2 + s^2 - s) \] \[ t^2 = \dfrac{1}{4} \cdot \dfrac{13s^2 - 4s}{4} \] \[ 16t^2 = s(13s - 4). \] From \((1),\) we get that \(s\) is a multiple of \(2.\) We can substitute \(s\) with \(2j\) to get \[ 16t^2 = 2j(26j - 4) \] \[ 4t^2 = j(13j - 2). \] For the product to be divisible by \(4,\) \(j\) must be divisible by \(2.\) We can again substitute \(j\) with \(2k\) to get \[ 4t^2 = 2k(26k - 2) \] \[ t^2 = k(13k - 1) \tag*{(2)} \]

Let \(x\) be the number of lattice points along the bottom of the rectangle formed by \(S \cap T\) and \(y\) be the number of lattice points along the bottom of the rectangle formed by \(R \cap T.\)

Using these variables, we get that the number of lattice points in \(S \cap T\) is \(xt\) and in \(R \cap T\) is \(yt.\)

The third condition gives us that \[ \dfrac{xt}{s^2} = 27 \cdot \dfrac{yt}{r^2} \] \[ \dfrac{x}{s^2} = 27 \cdot \dfrac{y}{\dfrac{9}{4} s^2} \] \[ x = 12y. \]

We also know that \(t = x + y - 1\) (accounting for overlap), and this yields \[ t = 13y - 1 \tag*{(3)} \]

\((3)\) gives us that \[ \begin{gathered} t \equiv -1 \pmod{13}, \\ t^2 \equiv 1 \pmod{13}. \end{gathered} \]

However, by \((2),\) we get that \[ t^2 \equiv -k \pmod{13} \] \[ k \equiv -1 \pmod{13}. \]

By \((2),\) we also get that \(k\) is a perfect square since it is relatively prime to \(13k - 1,\) and they must multiply to a perfect square.

Thus \(k\) must be a perfect square satisfying \(k\equiv-1\pmod{13}.\) The smaller positive squares \(1,4,9,16\) do not satisfy this congruence, while \(k=25\) does, so \(25\) is the least possible value.

From this value of \(k,\) we get that \(j = 2 \cdot 25 = 50,\) \(s = 2 \cdot 50 = 100,\) and \(r = \dfrac{3}{2} \cdot 100 = 150.\) We can also find that \[ t^2 = 25(13 \cdot 25 - 1) = 25 \cdot 324 \] \[ t = 5 \cdot 18 = 90. \] Therefore, \[ r + s + t = 340. \] The question, however, asked for the sum of the side lengths. The side lengths of the squares are \(1\) less than the number of lattice points on the side, so we have to subtract \(3.\)

This value is attainable: equation \((3)\) gives \(y=7\) and hence \(x=84.\) Since \(x\le s=100\) and \(y\le r=150,\) a square \(T\) with \(90\) lattice points per side can straddle the \(y\)-axis with the required overlaps, and its top vertices lie in \(R\cup S.\)

Therefore, the desired answer is \(340 - 3 = 337.\)

Thus, B is the correct answer.
