Thus, the correct answer is E.

格点 \(X=(x,y)\) 位于某条路径上，当且仅当 \[ \begin{aligned} d&=|x-3|+|x+3| \\ &\quad {}+|y-2|+|y+2|\le20 \end{aligned}\text{。}\] 该式在 \(x\to-x\) 或 \(y\to-y\) 时不变，所以先数 \(x\ge0\)、\(y\ge0\) 的点，再乘以 \(4\)，并校正坐标轴上的重复计数。

若 \(0\le x\le3\) 且 \(0\le y\le2\)，则这 \(4\cdot3=12\) 个点全都满足条件。若 \(0\le x\le3\) 且 \(y\ge3\)，则 \(y\le7\)，共给出 \(4\cdot5=20\) 个点。若 \(x\ge4\) 且 \(0\le y\le2\)，则 \(x\le8\)，共给出 \(5\cdot3=15\) 个点。最后，当 \(x\ge4\) 且 \(y\ge3\) 时，条件化为 \(x+y\le10\)，共给出 \(4+3+2+1=10\) 个点。于是第一象限中共有 \(57\) 个点，其中 \(15\) 个位于非负坐标轴上。由对称性，总数为 \[ 4\cdot57-2\cdot15-3=195\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

A lattice point \(X=(x,y)\) lies on some path exactly when \[ \begin{aligned} d&=|x-3|+|x+3| \\ &\quad {}+|y-2|+|y+2|\le20. \end{aligned} \] This expression is unchanged when \(x\to-x\) or \(y\to-y,\) so we count points with \(x\ge0,\) \(y\ge0,\) multiply by \(4,\) and correct for the axes.

If \(0\le x\le3\) and \(0\le y\le2,\) all \(4\cdot3=12\) points work. If \(0\le x\le3\) and \(y\ge3,\) then \(y\le7,\) giving \(4\cdot5=20\) points. If \(x\ge4\) and \(0\le y\le2,\) then \(x\le8,\) giving \(5\cdot3=15\) points. Finally, for \(x\ge4\) and \(y\ge3,\) the condition is \(x+y\le10,\) giving \(4+3+2+1=10\) points. Thus there are \(57\) in the first quadrant, including \(15\) on the nonnegative axes. By symmetry the total is \[ 4\cdot57-2\cdot15-3=195. \]

Thus, the correct answer is C.

24 答案 B

按 \(z^4\) 分解： \[ \begin{aligned} P(z) &=(z^4-1) \\ &\quad {}\cdot\big(z^4+(4\sqrt3+7)\big)\text{。} \end{aligned} \] 第一因子给出根 \(1,-1,i,-i\)。又因为 \(4\sqrt3+7=(\sqrt3+2)^2\)，且 \(2(\sqrt3+2)=(\sqrt3+1)^2\)，令 \(w=\tfrac12(\sqrt3+1)\)，另四个根为 \(w(\pm1\pm i)\)。

这八个根关于原点对称，并具有 \(4\) 重旋转对称性；任意两个根之间的线段长度至少为 \(\sqrt2\)。因此任何这样的八边形周长至少为 \(8\sqrt2\)，而依次取顶点 \(1\)、\(w(1+i)\)、\(i\)、\(w(-1+i)\)、\(-1\)、\(w(-1-i)\)、\(-i\)、\(w(1-i)\) 时可达到该下界。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Factoring in \(z^4,\) \[ \begin{aligned} P(z) &=(z^4-1) \\ &\quad {}\cdot\big(z^4+(4\sqrt3+7)\big). \end{aligned} \] The first factor gives the roots \(1,-1,i,-i.\) Since \(4\sqrt3+7=(\sqrt3+2)^2\) and \(2(\sqrt3+2)=(\sqrt3+1)^2,\) writing \(w=\tfrac12(\sqrt3+1)\) the other four roots are \(w(\pm1\pm i).\)

The eight roots are symmetric about the origin with \(4\)-fold symmetry, and every segment joining two of them has length at least \(\sqrt2.\) Thus any such polygon has perimeter at least \(8\sqrt2,\) and the polygon with vertices \(1,\) \(w(1+i),\) \(i,\) \(w(-1+i),\) \(-1,\) \(w(-1-i),\) \(-i,\) \(w(1-i)\) achieves it.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

25 答案 D

因为 \(\left[\dfrac{n+mk}{k}\right]=\left[\dfrac{n}{k}\right]+m\)，所以 \(n\) 是否满足恒等式只取决于 \(n\bmod k\)。由于 \(99!\) 能被 \(k\) 整除对 \(1\le k\le99\) 成立，每个剩余类等可能。

写 \(100=qk+r\) 和 \(n=q_1k+r_1\)，其中两个余数都取自 \([-\frac{(k-1)}{2},\frac{k-1}{2}]\)。若 \(r\ge0\)，则不发生进位当且仅当 \(r-\frac{k-1}{2}\le r_1\le\frac{k-1}{2}\)；这给出 \(k-r\) 个剩余类。\(r<0\) 的情形同样给出 \(k+r\) 个剩余类。因此两种情形下都有 \[ P(k)=1-\dfrac{|r|}{k}\text{。}\]

要最小化 \(P(k)\)，就要最大化 \(|r|/k\)。若 \(r=\frac{k-1}{2}\)，则 \(201=k(2q+1)\)，而满足 \(k\le99\) 的最大取值是 \(201\) 的因数 \(67\)。若 \(r=-\frac{(k-1)}{2}\)，则 \(199=k(2q-1)\)；因为 \(199\) 是质数，只有 \(k=1\) 可能。在其余所有情形中都有 \(|r|\le\frac{k-3}{2}\)，所以 \[ \begin{aligned} P(k)&\ge\dfrac12+\dfrac{3}{2k} \\ &\ge\dfrac12+\dfrac{3}{198} \\ &>\dfrac{34}{67} \end{aligned}\text{。}\] 而当 \(k=67\) 时， \[ P(67)=\dfrac12+\dfrac{1}{2\cdot67}=\dfrac{34}{67}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Because \(\left[\dfrac{n+mk}{k}\right]=\left[\dfrac{n}{k}\right]+m,\) whether \(n\) satisfies the identity depends only on \(n\bmod k.\) Since \(99!\) is divisible by \(k\) for \(1\le k\le99,\) every residue class is equally likely.

Write \(100=qk+r\) and \(n=q_1k+r_1,\) choosing both remainders in \([-\frac{(k-1)}{2},\frac{k-1}{2}].\) If \(r\ge0,\) no carry occurs precisely when \(r-\frac{k-1}{2}\le r_1\le\frac{k-1}{2};\) this gives \(k-r\) residue classes. The case \(r<0\) similarly gives \(k+r\) classes. Hence in both cases \[ P(k)=1-\dfrac{|r|}{k}. \]

To minimize \(P(k)\) we maximize \(|r|/k.\) If \(r=\frac{k-1}{2},\) then \(201=k(2q+1),\) and the largest possible \(k\le99\) is the divisor \(67\) of \(201.\) If \(r=-\frac{(k-1)}{2},\) then \(199=k(2q-1);\) because \(199\) is prime, only \(k=1\) is possible. In every remaining case \(|r|\le\frac{k-3}{2},\) so \[ \begin{aligned} P(k)&\ge\dfrac12+\dfrac{3}{2k} \\ &\ge\dfrac12+\dfrac{3}{198} \\ &>\dfrac{34}{67}. \end{aligned} \] For \(k=67,\) \[ P(67)=\dfrac12+\dfrac{1}{2\cdot67}=\dfrac{34}{67}. \]

Thus, the correct answer is D.
