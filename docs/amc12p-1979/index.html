令 \(t=x+\frac{1}{x}\)，\(u=x^3+x^{-3}=t^3-3t\)。因为 \(x^6+x^{-6}=u^2-2\)，所以分子为 \[ \begin{aligned} t^6-u^2&=(t^3-u)(t^3+u)\\ &=3t(t^3+u) \end{aligned}\text{。}\]分母为 \(t^3+u\)，所以 \(f(x)=3t=3(x+\frac{1}{x})\)。当 \(x\gt0\) 时，\(x+\frac{1}{x}\ge2\)，且在 \(x=1\) 时取等号。因此最小值为 \(6\)。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

Let \(t=x+\frac{1}{x}\) and \(u=x^3+x^{-3}=t^3-3t.\) Since \(x^6+x^{-6}=u^2-2,\) the numerator is \[ \begin{aligned} t^6-u^2&=(t^3-u)(t^3+u)\\ &=3t(t^3+u). \end{aligned} \] The denominator is \(t^3+u,\) so \(f(x)=3t=3(x+\frac{1}{x}).\) For \(x\gt0,\) \(x+\frac{1}{x}\ge2,\) with equality at \(x=1.\) Thus the minimum is \(6.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is E.

30 答案 B

将 \(AB\) 经过 \(B\) 延长至 \(F\)，使 \(AF=AC=1\)。则 \(\triangle ACF\) 为等边三角形。在 \(BF\) 上取点 \(G\)，使 \(\angle BCG=20^\circ\)。由角度条件可得 \(\triangle FGC\cong\triangle ABC\)，且 \(\triangle BCG\sim\triangle DCE\)。因为 \(E\) 是 \(BC\) 的中点，所以相似比为 \(2\)，从而 \[ [BCG]=4[CDE]\text{。}\]分割这个等边三角形可得 \[ \frac{\sqrt3}{4} =2[ABC]+4[CDE]\text{。}\]两边除以 \(2\)，得到 \([ABC]+2[CDE]=\frac{\sqrt3}{8}\)。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Extend \(AB\) through \(B\) to \(F\) with \(AF=AC=1.\) Then \(\triangle ACF\) is equilateral. Choose \(G\) on \(BF\) so that \(\angle BCG=20^\circ.\) The angle conditions give \(\triangle FGC\cong\triangle ABC,\) while \(\triangle BCG\sim\triangle DCE.\) Since \(E\) is the midpoint of \(BC,\) the similarity scale is \(2,\) so \[ [BCG]=4[CDE]. \] Decomposing the equilateral triangle gives \[ \frac{\sqrt3}{4} =2[ABC]+4[CDE]. \] Dividing by \(2\) yields \([ABC]+2[CDE]=\frac{\sqrt3}{8}.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is B.
