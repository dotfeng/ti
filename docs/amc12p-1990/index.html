English

Taking the reciprocal gives \(4^{\frac{1}{4}}=(2^2)^{\frac{1}{4}}=2^{\frac{1}{2}}=\sqrt2.\)

3 答案 C

四个角的和为 \(360^\circ\)，因此 \[ \begin{aligned} &75+(75+d)\\ &\quad +(75+2d)\\ &\quad +(75+3d)=360 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]所以 \(d=10\)，最大角为 \(75+3(10)=105^\circ\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The four angles sum to \(360^\circ,\) so \[ \begin{aligned} &75+(75+d)\\ &\quad +(75+2d)\\ &\quad +(75+3d)=360. \end{aligned} \] Thus \(d=10,\) and the largest angle is \(75+3(10)=105^\circ.\)

Thus the correct answer is C.

4 答案 B

由于 \(AB\parallel ED\)，三角形 \(ABF\) 与 \(DEF\) 相似。因此 \[ \frac{AF}{FD}=\frac{AB}{DE}=\frac{16}{4}=4\text{。}\]又因 \(AF+FD=AD=BC=10\)，所以 \(5FD=10\)，从而 \(FD=2\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Because \(AB\parallel ED,\) triangles \(ABF\) and \(DEF\) are similar. Hence \[ \frac{AF}{FD}=\frac{AB}{DE}=\frac{16}{4}=4. \] Since \(AF+FD=AD=BC=10,\) we get \(5FD=10,\) so \(FD=2.\)

Thus the correct answer is B.

5 答案 B

对五个正数分别取六次方不改变它们的大小顺序，依次得到 \[ \begin{aligned} 30,\qquad &6^3\cdot5=1080,\\ 5^3\cdot6&=750,\\ 5^2\cdot6&=150,\qquad 6^2\cdot5=180 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]其中第二个最大。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Raising the five positive choices to the sixth power preserves their order and gives, respectively, \[ \begin{aligned} 30,\qquad &6^3\cdot5=1080,\\ 5^3\cdot6&=750,\\ 5^2\cdot6&=150,\qquad 6^2\cdot5=180. \end{aligned} \] The largest is the second.

Thus the correct answer is B.

符合条件的直线，是以 \(A\) 为圆心、半径为 \(2\) 的圆与以 \(B\) 为圆心、半径为 \(3\) 的圆的公切线。两圆的圆心距等于半径之和，所以两圆外切。它们有两条外公切线，并在切点处另有一条公切线，共有 \(3\) 条。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

A qualifying line is a common tangent to the circle centered at \(A\) with radius \(2\) and the circle centered at \(B\) with radius \(3.\) Their center distance equals the sum of their radii, so they are externally tangent. They have two external common tangents and one common tangent at their point of contact, for a total of \(3.\)

Thus the correct answer is D.

17 答案 C

递增的三位数不能使用 \(0\)，所以递增数共有 \(\binom93=84\) 个。从 \(0\) 到 \(9\) 中任取三个数字，都能组成一个有效的递减三位数，因为最大的数字位于首位；这样的数有 \(\binom{10}{3}=120\) 个。总数为 \(84+120=204\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

An increasing three-digit number cannot use \(0,\) so there are \(\binom93=84\) increasing numbers. Any three digits chosen from \(0\) through \(9\) form a valid decreasing three-digit number, because the largest digit comes first; this gives \(\binom{10}{3}=120.\) The total is \(84+120=204.\)

Thus the correct answer is C.

18 答案 C

\(3^a\) 在 \(a\equiv1,2,3,0\pmod4\) 时的个位数字依次为 \(3,9,7,1\)；而 \(7^b\) 的个位数字依次为 \(7,9,3,1\)。和的个位数字为 \(8\) 时，余数对为 \[ \begin{aligned} (a,b)\equiv{}&(2,2),(3,0),\\ &(0,1)\pmod4 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]每种余数都出现 \(25\) 次（在 \(1,\ldots,100\) 中），所以所求概率为 \(\frac{3}{16}\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

The units digits of \(3^a\) for \(a\equiv1,2,3,0\pmod4\) are \(3,9,7,1,\) while those of \(7^b\) are \(7,9,3,1.\) A sum ending in \(8\) occurs for the residue pairs \[ \begin{aligned} (a,b)\equiv{}&(2,2),(3,0),\\ &(0,1)\pmod4. \end{aligned} \] Each residue occurs \(25\) times among \(1,\ldots,100,\) so the probability is \(\frac{3}{16}.\)

Thus the correct answer is C.

19 答案 B

对 \(N+4\) 取模，有 \(N\equiv-4\)，所以 \[ N^2+7\equiv16+7=23\text{。}\]因此，该分数可约分，当且仅当 \(N+4\) 能被 \(23\) 整除，即 \(N\equiv19\pmod{23}\)。这些值为 \(19,42,\ldots,1974\)，一共有 \(86\) 个。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Modulo \(N+4,\) we have \(N\equiv-4,\) so \[ N^2+7\equiv16+7=23. \] Thus the fraction is reducible exactly when \(N+4\) is divisible by \(23,\) or \(N\equiv19\pmod{23}.\) The values are \(19,42,\ldots,1974,\) a total of \(86.\)

Thus the correct answer is B.

20 答案 C

令 \(A=(0,0)\)、\(E=(3,0)\)、\(C=(10,0)\)、\(D=(3,-5)\)、\(B=(f,h)\)，其中 \(h=BF\)。因为 \(AB\perp AD\)，\[ (f,h)\cdot(3,-5)=0\text{，}\]所以 \(3f=5h\)。因为 \(BC\perp DC\)，\[ (f-10,h)\cdot(-7,-5)=0\text{，}\]所以 \(7f+5h=70\)。代入可得 \(f=7\) 和 \(h=\frac{21}{5}=4.2\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Set \(A=(0,0),\) \(E=(3,0),\) \(C=(10,0),\) \(D=(3,-5),\) and \(B=(f,h),\) where \(h=BF.\) Since \(AB\perp AD,\) \[ (f,h)\cdot(3,-5)=0, \] so \(3f=5h.\) Since \(BC\perp DC,\) \[ (f-10,h)\cdot(-7,-5)=0, \] so \(7f+5h=70.\) Substitution gives \(f=7\) and \(h=\frac{21}{5}=4.2.\)

21 答案 E

令 \(R=PA=PB\)。在等腰三角形 \(APB\) 中，\[ 1=AB=2R\sin\theta\text{，}\]所以 \(R=\frac{1}{2\sin\theta}\)。若 \(h\) 为棱锥的高，则正方形中心到 \(A\) 的水平距离为 \(\frac{1}{\sqrt2}\)，因此 \[ \begin{aligned} h^2 &=R^2-\frac12\\ &=\frac{\cos2\theta}{4\sin^2\theta} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]所以 \(h=\frac{\sqrt{\cos2\theta}}{2\sin\theta}\)，体积为 \(\frac13(1)h=\frac{\sqrt{\cos2\theta}}{6\sin\theta}\)。

English

Thus the correct answer is E.

24 答案 D

29 答案 D

将这些整数分成形如 \(m,3m,9m,\ldots\) 的链，其中 \(m\) 不是 \(3\) 的倍数。在每条链中，两个相邻项不能同时选取，所以最大选择方案是从 \(m\) 开始隔项选取。等价地，选择质因数 \(3\) 的指数为偶数的整数。这样的整数有 \[ \begin{aligned} &\left(100-\left\lfloor\frac{100}{3}\right\rfloor\right)\\ &\quad+\left(\left\lfloor\frac{100}{9}\right\rfloor -\left\lfloor\frac{100}{27}\right\rfloor\right)\\ &\quad+\left\lfloor\frac{100}{81}\right\rfloor\\ &=67+8+1=76 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]上述链的论证也证明了不可能选取更多元素。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Partition the integers into chains \(m,3m,9m,\ldots,\) with \(m\) not a multiple of \(3.\) In each chain, no two adjacent terms may both be selected, so a maximum selection takes alternating terms starting with \(m.\) Equivalently, select the integers whose exponent of \(3\) is even. Thus the correct answer is D.
