1

城市 \(A\) 和 \(B\) 相距 \(45\) 英里。Alicia 住在 \(A\)，Beth 住在 \(B\)。Alicia 以每小时 \(18\) 英里的速度骑向 \(B\)。Beth 同时出发，以每小时 \(12\) 英里的速度骑向 \(A\)。她们相遇时距离城市 \(A\) 多少英里？

A\(20\) B\(24\) C\(25\) D\(26\) E\(27\)

English

Cities \(A\) and \(B\) are \(45\) miles apart. Alicia lives in \(A\) and Beth lives in \(B.\) Alicia bikes towards \(B\) at \(18\) miles per hour. Leaving at the same time, Beth bikes toward \(A\) at \(12\) miles per hour. How many miles from City \(A\) will they be when they meet?

(A) \(20\)(B) \(24\)(C) \(25\)(D) \(26\)(E) \(27\)

2

一个大披萨的 \(\frac{1}{3}\) 加上 \(3\frac{1}{2}\) 杯橙子片的重量，等于同一个大披萨的 \(\frac{3}{4}\) 加上 \(\frac{1}{2}\) 杯橙子片的重量。一杯橙子片重 \(\frac{1}{4}\) 磅。一个大披萨重多少磅？

A\(1\frac{4}{5}\) B\(2\) C\(2\frac{2}{5}\) D\(3\) E\(3\frac{3}{5}\)

English

The weight of \(\frac{1}{3}\) of a large pizza together with \(3\frac{1}{2}\) cups of orange slices is the same as the weight of \(\frac{3}{4}\) of a large pizza together with \(\frac{1}{2}\) cup of orange slices. A cup of orange slices weighs \(\frac{1}{4}\) of a pound. What is the weight, in pounds, of a large pizza?

(A) \(1\frac{4}{5}\)(B) \(2\)(C) \(2\frac{2}{5}\)(D) \(3\)(E) \(3\frac{3}{5}\)

(A) \(8\)(B) \(9\)(C) \(10\)(D) \(11\)(E) \(12\)

4

一个四边形的所有边长都是整数，周长为 \(26\)，其中一条边长为 \(4\)。这个四边形的一条边的最大可能长度是多少？

A\(9\) B\(10\) C\(11\) D\(12\) E\(13\)

English

A quadrilateral has all integer side lengths, a perimeter of \(26,\) and one side of length \(4.\) What is the greatest possible length of one side of this quadrilateral?

(A) \(9\)(B) \(10\)(C) \(11\)(D) \(12\)(E) \(13\)

6

一个整数被分配给立方体的每个顶点。一条边的值定义为它所连接的两个顶点的值之和，一个面的值定义为围成该面的四条边的值之和。立方体的值定义为六个面的值之和。若分配给各顶点的整数之和为 \(21\)，这个立方体的值是多少？

A\(42\) B\(63\) C\(84\) D\(126\) E\(252\)

English

The value of the cube is defined as the sum of the values of its six faces. Suppose the sum of the integers assigned to the vertices is \(21.\) What is the value of the cube?

(A) \(42\)(B) \(63\)(C) \(84\)(D) \(126\)(E) \(252\)

7

(A) \(33\)(B) \(34.5\)(C) \(36\)(D) \(37.5\)(E) \(39\)

逐题解析

1 答案 E

她们相向骑行，所以速度相加。两人以每小时 \(18 + 12 = 30\) 英里的速度缩短这 \(45\) 英里的距离，因此经过 \(\frac{45}{30} = 1.5\) 小时相遇。Alice 从 \(A\) 出发，所以这时她骑了 \(18 \cdot 1.5 = 27\) 英里。因此，正确答案是 E。

English

They ride toward each other, so their speeds add. That closes the \(45\)-mile gap at \(18 + 12 = 30\) mph, and they meet after \(\frac{45}{30} = 1.5\) hours. Alice starts at \(A,\) so by then she’s gone \(18 \cdot 1.5 = 27\) miles. Thus, E is the correct answer.

2 答案 A

设披萨重量为 \(p\)。一杯橙子片重 \(\frac14\) 磅，所以天平两边满足 \(\frac13 p + \frac72 \cdot \frac14 = \frac34 p + \frac12 \cdot \frac14\)，也就是 \(\frac13 p + \frac78 = \frac34 p + \frac18\)。整理披萨项得到 \(\frac68 = \left(\frac34 - \frac13\right)p = \frac{5}{12}p\)。因此 \(p = \frac34 \cdot \frac{12}{5} = \frac95 = 1\frac45\)。所以答案是 A。

Put the centers of \(C_1, C_2\) at \(\left(\pm\frac14, 0\right).\) By symmetry the largest circle inside both sits at the origin with radius \(r_3,\) where \(1 - r_3 = \frac14,\) so \(r_3 = \frac34.\) Let \(C_4\) be centered at \((0, y)\) with radius \(r.\) Internal tangency to \(C_1\) gives \(\sqrt{\frac1{16} + y^2} = 1 - r,\) and external tangency to \(C_3\) gives \(y = \frac34 + r.\) Substitute the second into the first: \(\frac1{16} + \left(\frac34 + r\right)^2 = (1 - r)^2.\) This collapses to \(\frac72 r = \frac38,\) so \(r = \frac{3}{28}.\) Therefore, the answer is D.

23 答案 C

相差 \(20\) 的互补因数为 \(b\) 和 \(b + 20\)，乘积为 \(N\)，所以 \(N = b^2 + 20b\)，且 \(N + 100 = (b + 10)^2\)。相差 \(23\) 的一对给出 \(4N + 529 = (2d + 23)^2\)。设 \(N + 100 = k^2\)。则 \(4k^2 + 129 = m^2\)，所以 \((m - 2k)(m + 2k)\) \(= 129\) \(= 3 \cdot 43\)。正因数对 \(3,43\) 给出 \(k=10\)，从而得到不合要求的 \(N=0\)。因数对 \(1,129\) 给出 \(m = 65\)、\(k = 32\)，于是 \(N = 32^2 - 100 = 924\)。检验：\(924 = 22 \cdot 42 = 21 \cdot 44\)，数字和为 \(9 + 2 + 4 = 15\)。因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Complementary divisors differing by \(20\) are \(b\) and \(b + 20\) with product \(N\), so \(N = b^2 + 20b\) and \(N + 100 = (b + 10)^2\). A pair differing by \(23\) gives \(4N + 529 = (2d + 23)^2\). Set \(N + 100 = k^2\). Then \(4k^2 + 129 = m^2\), so \((m - 2k)(m + 2k)\) \(= 129\) \(= 3 \cdot 43\). The positive factor pair \(3,43\) gives \(k=10\), hence the inadmissible value \(N=0\). The pair \(1,129\) gives \(m = 65\), \(k = 32\), hence \(N = 32^2 - 100 = 924\). Check it: \(924 = 22 \cdot 42 = 21 \cdot 44\), and the digit sum is \(9 + 2 + 4 = 15\). Thus, C is the correct answer.

24 答案 C

设 \(d=\tfrac37\)。把与框架某条固定边相接的那些积木的斜边延长。由于所有相关的角都是 \(60^\circ\)，这些延长线在一端围成一个边长为 \(1\) 的正三角形，在另一端围成一个边长为 \(1-d\) 的正三角形。于是这条框架边被分成长度依次为 \(d,1,1\) 和 \(1-d\) 的四段，所以它的长度是 \(d+1+1+(1-d)=3\)。边长为 \(t\) 的正六边形面积为 \(\tfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}t^2\)。因此未被占据的面积等于边长为 \(3\) 的框架面积减去六个单位积木的面积：\(\tfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}\cdot 3^2\) \(- 6\cdot\tfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}\) \(= \tfrac{27\sqrt3}{2} - 9\sqrt3\) \(= \tfrac{9\sqrt3}{2}\)。因此，答案是 C。

English

Let \(d=\tfrac37.\) Extend the slanted edges of the blocks that meet a fixed side of the frame. Because all the relevant angles are \(60^\circ,\) the extensions form an equilateral triangle of side \(1\) at one end and an equilateral triangle of side \(1-d\) at the other. Thus that frame side is partitioned into lengths \(d,1,1,\) and \(1-d,\) so its length is \(d+1+1+(1-d)=3.\) A regular hexagon of side \(t\) has area \(\tfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}t^2.\) Therefore the uncovered area is the area of the side-\(3\) frame minus the areas of the six unit blocks: \(\tfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}\cdot 3^2\) \(- 6\cdot\tfrac{3\sqrt3}{2}\) \(= \tfrac{27\sqrt3}{2} - 9\sqrt3\) \(= \tfrac{9\sqrt3}{2}.\) Therefore, the answer is C.

25 答案 A

固定 \(R\)。在其他 \(11\) 个顶点中，有 \(5\) 个到该固定顶点的距离为 \(1\)，\(5\) 个距离为 \(2\)，还有 \(1\) 个（对顶点）距离为 \(3\)。从这 \(11\) 个顶点中有序选出不同的 \(Q, S\)，共有 \(11 \cdot 10 = 110\) 对。满足 \(d(R,Q) = d(R,S)\) 的对数为 \(5\cdot4 + 5\cdot4 + 1\cdot0 = 40\)，所以 \(P(\text{相等}) = \frac{40}{110} = \frac{4}{11}\)。由 \(Q\) 与 \(S\) 的对称性，\(\gt\) 和 \(\lt\) 的情况平分剩余概率，所以 \(P(d(Q,R) \gt d(R,S))\) \(= \frac{1 - \frac4{11}}{2}\) \(= \frac{7}{22}\)。因此，正确答案是 A。

English

Fix \(R.\) Of the other \(11\) vertices, \(5\) sit at distance \(1,\) \(5\) at distance \(2,\) and \(1\) (the opposite vertex) at distance \(3.\) Pick ordered distinct \(Q, S\) from these \(11:\) that’s \(11 \cdot 10 = 110\) pairs. The ones with \(d(R,Q) = d(R,S)\) number \(5\cdot4 + 5\cdot4 + 1\cdot0 = 40,\) so \(P(\text{equal}) = \frac{40}{110} = \frac{4}{11}.\) By the symmetry between \(Q\) and \(S,\) the \(\gt\) and \(\lt\) cases split the rest evenly, so \(P(d(Q,R) \gt d(R,S))\) \(= \frac{1 - \frac4{11}}{2}\) \(= \frac{7}{22}.\) Thus, A is the correct answer.
