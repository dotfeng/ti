两场会议用了 \(45+90=135\) 分钟，而工作日共有 \(9\cdot60=540\) 分钟。

English

The two meetings lasted \(45+90=135\) minutes, and the work day is \(9\cdot60=540\) minutes.

The fraction of the day spent in meetings is \[ \frac{135}{540}=\frac14=25\%. \]

Thus, the correct answer is C.

这个区域可以分成一个 \(8\times2\) 的竖直长方形和一个 \(2\times3\) 的水平底部，其中底部的宽为 \(5-2=3\)。

The total area is \[ 8\cdot2+3\cdot2=16+6=22. \]

Thus, the correct answer is A.

3 答案 E

票价 \(x\) 必须同时整除两个总价，所以 \(x\) 是 \(48\) 和 \(64\) 的公因数。

因为 \(\gcd(48,64)=16\)，公因数为 \(1, 2, 4, 8\) 和 \(16\)。因此有 \(5\) 个可能的取值。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

The price \(x\) must divide both totals, so \(x\) is a common divisor of \(48\) and \(64.\)

Since \(\gcd(48,64)=16,\) the common divisors are \(1, 2, 4, 8,\) and \(16.\) There are \(5\) possible values.

Thus, the correct answer is E.

4 答案 B

因为 \(31=4\cdot7+3\)，这个月的前三天各出现五次，其余四天各出现四次。

星期一和星期三次数相同，恰好发生在它们都属于出现五次的一组，或都属于出现四次的一组时。

如果第一天是星期一，出现五次的是星期一、星期二、星期三。若第一天是星期四或星期五，出现五次的三天都不包含星期一和星期三。其他起始日都会只包含星期一和星期三中的一个。

所以第一天可以是星期一、星期四或星期五，共 \(3\) 种可能。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Since \(31=4\cdot7+3,\) the first three days of the month each occur five times, and the other four days occur four times.

Mondays and Wednesdays are equal in number exactly when both fall in the five-time group or both fall in the four-time group.

If the first day is Monday, the five-time days are Mon, Tue, Wed (both appear five times). If the first day is Thursday or Friday, the five-time days miss both Monday and Wednesday (both appear four times). Every other starting day includes exactly one of Monday or Wednesday.

So the first day can be Monday, Thursday, or Friday, giving \(3\) possibilities.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

English

Larry dropped the parentheses and computed \(1-2-3-4+e=-8+e.\)

Setting \(-2-e=-8+e\) gives \(2e=6,\) so \(e=3.\)

So \(15, 9,\) and \(105\) all divide \(2a,\) hence \(\operatorname{lcm}(15,9,105)=315\) divides \(2a.\) Since \(315\) is odd, \(a\) is divisible by \(315,\) so \(a\ge315.\)

To attain the bound, let \[ \begin{aligned} F(x)&=(x-1)(x-3) \\ &\quad\cdot(x-5)(x-7), \\ Q(x)&=42+(x-2)(x-6) \\ &\quad\cdot(60-8x), \\ P(x)&=315+F(x)Q(x). \end{aligned} \] This integer polynomial equals \(315\) at \(1,3,5,7.\) At \(2,4,\) the pairs \((F(x),Q(x))\) are \((-15,42),(9,-70),\) and at \(6,8\) they are \((-15,42),(105,-6).\) Thus \(F(x)Q(x)=-630\) each time and \(P(x)=-315.\) Hence the bound is attainable.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

22 答案 D

令 \(a=AB\)，\(b=BC\)，\(c=CD\)，\(d=DA\)，并令 \(k=bc=ad\)。用外接圆半径表示各三角形的面积，再利用 \([ABC]+[CDA]=\) \([BCD]+[ABD]\)，可得 \((ab+cd)\cdot AC=2k\cdot BD\)。

托勒密定理给出 \(AC\cdot BD=ac+bd\)。消去 \(AC\)，得 \[ \begin{aligned} BD^2 &=\frac{(ac+bd)(ab+cd)}{2k} \\ &=\frac12\left(a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2\right) \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

四条边是小于 \(15\) 的互异整数，并满足 \(bc=ad\)，所以 \(11\) 和 \(13\) 都不能出现（它们都是质数，需要等式另一侧有相应因子）。

如果最大边至多为 \(12\)，因为不能使用 \(11\)，四边平方和至多为 \(12^2+10^2+9^2+8^2=389\)。

现在设最大边为 \(14\)，其余三边记为 \(s_1\gt s_2\gt s_3\)。乘积条件必须把 \(14\) 与 \(s_3\) 配对，所以 \(14s_3=s_1s_2\)。因而 \(s_1,s_2\) 中一个等于 \(7\)。若 \(s_1=7\)，平方和小于 \(14^2+7^2+6^2+5^2=306\)。若 \(s_2=7\)，则 \(s_1=2s_3\)，最大的可能是 \((s_1,s_2,s_3)=(12,7,6)\)。因此 \[ \begin{aligned} 2BD^2 &=14^2+12^2+7^2+6^2 \\ &=425 \end{aligned}\text{，}\]所以 \(BD\le\sqrt{\dfrac{425}{2}}\)。边依次为 \((a,b,c,d)=(14,12,7,6)\) 的圆内接四边形达到等号，此时 \(bc=ad=84\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

Let \(a=AB,\) \(b=BC,\) \(c=CD,\) \(d=DA\) and \(k=bc=ad.\) Writing each triangle’s area in terms of the circumradius and using \([ABC]+[CDA]=\) \([BCD]+[ABD]\) gives \((ab+cd)\cdot AC=2k\cdot BD.\)

Ptolemy’s theorem gives \(AC\cdot BD=ac+bd.\) Eliminating \(AC,\) \[ \begin{aligned} BD^2 &=\frac{(ac+bd)(ab+cd)}{2k} \\ &=\frac12\left(a^2+b^2+c^2+d^2\right). \end{aligned} \]

The sides are distinct integers below \(15\) with \(bc=ad,\) so neither \(11\) nor \(13\) can appear (each is prime and would need a matching factor on the other side).

If the largest side is at most \(12,\) the four squares sum to at most \(12^2+10^2+9^2+8^2=389,\) because \(11\) is unavailable.

Now suppose the largest side is \(14,\) and write the others as \(s_1\gt s_2\gt s_3.\) The product condition must pair \(14\) with \(s_3,\) so \(14s_3=s_1s_2.\) Hence one of \(s_1,s_2\) equals \(7.\) If \(s_1=7,\) the sum of squares is less than \(14^2+7^2+6^2+5^2=306.\) If \(s_2=7,\) then \(s_1=2s_3,\) so the largest possibility is \((s_1,s_2,s_3)=(12,7,6).\) Thus \[ \begin{aligned} 2BD^2 &=14^2+12^2+7^2+6^2 \\ &=425, \end{aligned} \] so \(BD\le\sqrt{\dfrac{425}{2}}.\) Equality is attained by the cyclic quadrilateral with side order \((a,b,c,d)=(14,12,7,6),\) for which \(bc=ad=84.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

23 答案 A

如果 \(P\) 只有一个实根，那么 \(P(Q(x))=0\) 至多有两个实数解，而不是四个。因此 \(P\) 有两个不同的实根；同理，\(Q\) 也有两个不同的实根。写成 \(P(x)=(x-h_1)^2-k_1^2\) 和 \(Q(x)=(x-h_2)^2-k_2^2\)，其中 \(k_1,k_2\gt0\)，最小值分别为 \(-k_1^2\) 和 \(-k_2^2\)。

方程 \(P(Q(x))\) 的零点满足 \(Q(x)=h_1\pm k_1\)；四个解关于 \(h_2\) 对称，所以 \(h_2\) 是平均数 \(\tfrac{-23-21-17-15}{4}=-19\)。于是 \(Q(-15)-Q(-17)\) \(=(16-k_2^2)-(4-k_2^2)\) \(=12\)，而这个差等于 \(2k_1\)，所以 \(k_1=6\)。

对称地，\(h_1=\tfrac{-59-57-51-49}{4}=-54\)，且 \(P(-49)-P(-51)\) \(=(25-k_1^2)-(9-k_1^2)\) \(=16=2k_2\)，所以 \(k_2=8\)。

两个最小值之和为 \(-k_1^2-k_2^2=-36-64=-100\)。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

If \(P\) had only one real root, then \(P(Q(x))=0\) would have at most two real solutions, not four. Thus \(P\) has two distinct real roots, and the same argument applies to \(Q.\) Write \(P(x)=(x-h_1)^2-k_1^2\) and \(Q(x)=(x-h_2)^2-k_2^2,\) with \(k_1,k_2\gt0\) and minimum values \(-k_1^2\) and \(-k_2^2.\)

The zeros of \(P(Q(x))\) occur where \(Q(x)=h_1\pm k_1;\) their four solutions are symmetric about \(h_2,\) so \(h_2\) is the average \(\tfrac{-23-21-17-15}{4}=-19.\) Then \(Q(-15)-Q(-17)\) \(=(16-k_2^2)-(4-k_2^2)\) \(=12,\) and this difference equals \(2k_1,\) so \(k_1=6.\)

Symmetrically, \(h_1=\tfrac{-59-57-51-49}{4}=-54,\) and \(P(-49)-P(-51)\) \(=(25-k_1^2)-(9-k_1^2)\) \(=16=2k_2,\) so \(k_2=8.\)

The sum of the minimum values is \(-k_1^2-k_2^2=-36-64=-100.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

24 答案 C

设 \(f(x)\) 为不等式的左边。在相邻的竖直渐近线 \(2009, 2010, 2011\) 之间的每个区间上，函数 \(f\) 都递减；而且对所有 \(x\lt2009\)，都有 \(f\lt1\)。

在 \((2009,2010)\)、\((2010,2011)\) 和 \((2011,\infty)\) 上，解都是从左侧的渐近线开始，直到某个满足 \(f(x_i)=1\) 的 \(x_i\) 为止。因此解集由三个区间组成，左端点为 \(2009, 2010, 2011\)，右端点为 \(x_1, x_2, x_3\)。

总长度是 \((x_1-2009)\) \(+(x_2-2010)\) \(+(x_3-2011)\) \(=x_1+x_2+x_3-6030\)。

在 \(f(x)=1\) 中清除分母，得到 \[ \begin{aligned} &x^3 \\ &\quad \small{}-(2009+2010+2011+3)x^2 \\ &\quad {}+\cdots=0\text{，} \end{aligned} \] 它的三个根是 \(x_1, x_2, x_3\)。由韦达定理，\(x_1+x_2+x_3=6033\)，所以长度之和为 \(6033-6030=3\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Let \(f(x)\) be the left-hand side. On each interval between consecutive asymptotes \(2009, 2010, 2011,\) the function \(f\) is decreasing, and \(f\lt1\) for all \(x\lt2009.\)

On each of \((2009,2010),\) \((2010,2011),\) and \((2011,\infty),\) the solution is the part from the left asymptote up to a value \(x_i\) where \(f(x_i)=1.\) So the solution set consists of three intervals with left endpoints \(2009, 2010, 2011\) and right endpoints \(x_1, x_2, x_3.\)

The total length is \((x_1-2009)\) \(+(x_2-2010)\) \(+(x_3-2011)\) \(=x_1+x_2+x_3-6030.\)

Clearing denominators in \(f(x)=1\) gives \[ \begin{aligned} &x^3 \\ &\quad \small{}-(2009+2010+2011+3)x^2 \\ &\quad {}+\cdots=0, \end{aligned} \] whose roots are \(x_1, x_2, x_3.\) By Vieta, \(x_1+x_2+x_3=6033,\) so the sum of lengths is \(6033-6030=3.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

25 答案 D

因为 \(2010=2\cdot3\cdot5\cdot67\)，将乘积写成 \(2^A3^B5^C67^D\) 乘以一个与这四个质数都互质的因子；于是 \(m=\min(A,B,C,D)\)。

质数 \(2\)： 只有当 \(n=2^k\) 时，\(\operatorname{pow}(n)\) 才是 \(2\) 的幂。因为 \(2^{12}=4096\lt5300\lt2^{13}\)，所以 \(k=1,\ldots,12\) 贡献 \(A=1+2+\cdots+12=78\)。

质数 \(67\)： 当 \(67\) 是最大质因数时，\(\operatorname{pow}(n)=67\)，即 \(n=67j\)，其中 \(1\le j\le79\)，且 \(j\) 的每个质因数都至多为 \(67\)；排除 \(j=67, 71, 73, 79\) 后剩下 \(75\) 个值。唯一满足 \(\operatorname{pow}(n)=67^2\) 的 \(n\) 是 \(n=67^2\lt5300\)，再贡献 \(2\)。所以 \(D=75+2=77\)。

质数 \(3\)： 对于 \(n=2^a3^b\)，当 \(b=1,2,3,4\) 时，允许的指数 \(a\) 的个数分别为 \(11,10,8,7\)，仅这些项就贡献 \[ \begin{aligned} B&\ge11+2\cdot10 \\ &\quad+3\cdot8+4\cdot7=83 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

质数 \(5\)： 写成 \(n=2^a3^c5^b\)。当 \(b=1\) 时，对 \(c=0,\ldots,6\) 的计数总和为 \(11+9+7+6+4+3+1=41\)。当 \(b=2\) 时，计数总和为 \(8+7+5+3+2=25\)，每个贡献两个因数 \(5\)。因此 \(C\ge41+2\cdot25=91\)。

所以 \(m=\min(78,B,C,77)=77\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

Since \(2010=2\cdot3\cdot5\cdot67,\) write the product as \(2^A3^B5^C67^D\) times a factor coprime to all four primes; then \(m=\min(A,B,C,D).\)

Prime \(2:\) \(\operatorname{pow}(n)\) is a power of \(2\) only when \(n=2^k.\) Since \(2^{12}=4096\lt5300\lt2^{13},\) the values \(k=1,\ldots,12\) contribute \(A=1+2+\cdots+12=78.\)

Prime \(67:\) \(\operatorname{pow}(n)=67\) when \(67\) is the largest prime factor, i.e. \(n=67j\) with \(1\le j\le79\) and every prime factor of \(j\) at most \(67;\) excluding \(j=67, 71, 73, 79\) leaves \(75\) values. The one \(n\) with \(\operatorname{pow}(n)=67^2\) is \(n=67^2\lt5300,\) adding \(2.\) So \(D=75+2=77.\)

Prime \(3:\) For \(n=2^a3^b\) with \(b=1,2,3,4,\) the numbers of allowable exponents \(a\) are \(11,10,8,7,\) respectively. These terms alone contribute \[ \begin{aligned} B&\ge11+2\cdot10 \\ &\quad+3\cdot8+4\cdot7=83. \end{aligned} \]

Prime \(5:\) Write \(n=2^a3^c5^b.\) For \(b=1,\) the counts over \(c=0,\ldots,6\) total \(11+9+7+6+4+3+1=41.\) For \(b=2,\) the counts total \(8+7+5+3+2=25,\) each contributing two factors of \(5.\) Hence \(C\ge41+2\cdot25=91.\)

Therefore \(m=\min(78,B,C,77)=77.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.
