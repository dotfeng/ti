1

一个童子军小队以每五条 \(\$2\) 的价格买入 \(1000\) 条糖果棒。他们再以每两条 \(\$1\) 的价格全部卖出。他们的利润是多少美元？

A\(100\) B\(200\) C\(300\) D\(400\) E\(500\)

English

A scout troop buys \(1000\) candy bars at a price of five for \(\$2.\) They sell all the candy bars at a price of two for \(\$1.\) What was their profit, in dollars?

(A) \(100\)(B) \(200\)(C) \(300\)(D) \(400\)(E) \(500\)

2

A\(2\) B\(4\) C\(10\) D\(20\) E\(40\)

English

(A) \(2\)(B) \(4\)(C) \(10\)(D) \(20\)(E) \(40\)

3

A\(\dfrac{1}{5}\) B\(\dfrac{1}{3}\) C\(\dfrac{2}{5}\) D\(\dfrac{2}{3}\) E\(\dfrac{4}{5}\)

English

Brianna is using part of the money she earned on her weekend job to buy several equally-priced CDs. She used one fifth of her money to buy one third of the CDs. What fraction of her money will she have left after she buys all the CDs?

(A) \(\dfrac{1}{5}\)(B) \(\dfrac{1}{3}\)(C) \(\dfrac{2}{5}\)(D) \(\dfrac{2}{3}\)(E) \(\dfrac{4}{5}\)

4

A\(1\) B\(2\) C\(3\) D\(4\) E\(5\)

English

At the beginning of the school year, Lisa’s goal was to earn an A on at least \(80\%\) of her \(50\) quizzes for the year. She earned an A on \(22\) of the first \(30\) quizzes. If she is to achieve her goal, on at most how many of the remaining quizzes can she earn a grade lower than an A?

(A) \(1\)(B) \(2\)(C) \(3\)(D) \(4\)(E) \(5\)

5

A\(80 - 20\pi\) B\(60 - 10\pi\) C\(80 - 10\pi\) D\(60 + 10\pi\) E\(80 + 10\pi\)

English

Each tile has a pattern consisting of four white quarter circles of radius \(\dfrac12\) foot centered at each corner of the tile. The remaining portion of the tile is shaded. How many square feet of the floor are shaded?

(A) \(80 - 20\pi\)(B) \(60 - 10\pi\)(C) \(80 - 10\pi\)(D) \(60 + 10\pi\)(E) \(80 + 10\pi\)

逐题解析

1 答案 A

小队买入 \(1000 \div 5 = 200\) 组五条装糖果棒，成本为 \(200 \cdot 2 = 400\) 美元。

他们卖出 \(1000 \div 2 = 500\) 对糖果棒，收入为 \(500 \cdot 1 = 500\) 美元。

利润为 \(500 - 400 = 100\) 美元。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The troop buys \(1000 \div 5 = 200\) groups of five bars, costing \(200 \cdot 2 = 400\) dollars.

They sell \(1000 \div 2 = 500\) pairs of bars, earning \(500 \cdot 1 = 500\) dollars.

The profit is \(500 - 400 = 100\) dollars.

Thus, the correct answer is A.

2 答案 D

题意可化为 \[ \dfrac{x}{100}\cdot x = 4\text{，} \] 所以 \(x^2 = 400\)。

因为 \(x\) 为正，所以 \(x = 20\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The statement translates to \[ \dfrac{x}{100}\cdot x = 4, \] so \(x^2 = 400.\)

Since \(x\) is positive, \(x = 20.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

3 答案 C

全部 CD 的费用是三分之一 CD 费用的三倍，即她的钱的 \(3\cdot\dfrac15 = \dfrac35\)。

她还剩下自己钱的 \(1 - \dfrac35 = \dfrac25\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Buying all the CDs costs three times what one third of them cost, namely \(3\cdot\dfrac15 = \dfrac35\) of her money.

She has \(1 - \dfrac35 = \dfrac25\) of her money left.

Thus, the correct answer is C.

4 答案 B

Lisa 至少需要 \(0.8 \cdot 50 = 40\) 次 A。

她已经有 \(22\) 次，所以剩下 \(20\) 次中还需要 \(40 - 22 = 18\) 次 A。

因此最多可以有 \(20 - 18 = 2\) 次低于 A。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Lisa needs an A on at least \(0.8 \cdot 50 = 40\) quizzes.

She has \(22\) already, so she needs \(40 - 22 = 18\) more of the remaining \(20\) quizzes.

She can earn a lower grade on at most \(20 - 18 = 2\) of them.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

5 答案 A

每块瓷砖的四个四分之一圆合成一个半径为 \(\dfrac12\) 的圆，面积为 \(\pi\left(\dfrac12\right)^2 = \dfrac{\pi}{4}\)。

每块瓷砖阴影面积为 \(1 - \dfrac{\pi}{4}\)。

共有 \(8 \cdot 10 = 80\) 块瓷砖，所以总阴影面积为 \[ 80\left(1 - \dfrac{\pi}{4}\right) = 80 - 20\pi\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The four quarter circles in a tile together form one full circle of radius \(\dfrac12,\) with area \(\pi\left(\dfrac12\right)^2 = \dfrac{\pi}{4}.\)

So each tile has shaded area \(1 - \dfrac{\pi}{4}\) square feet.

There are \(8 \cdot 10 = 80\) tiles, so the total shaded area is \[ 80\left(1 - \dfrac{\pi}{4}\right) = 80 - 20\pi. \]

Thus, the correct answer is A.

6 答案 A

设 \(H\) 为从 \(C\) 到直线 \(AB\) 的垂足。由于 \(\triangle ABC\) 中 \(AC = BC\)，所以 \(H\) 是 \(AB\) 的中点，从而 \(AH = HB = 1\)。

于是 \(CH^2 = 7^2 - 1^2 = 48\)。对 \(\triangle CHD\) 应用勾股定理，其中 \(HD = HB + BD = 1 + BD\)，得 \[ 8^2 = 48 + (1 + BD)^2\text{，} \] 从而 \((1 + BD)^2 = 16\)。

于是由上式得 \(1 + BD = 4\)，所以 \(BD = 3\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Let \(H\) be the foot of the altitude from \(C\) to line \(AB.\) Since \(\triangle ABC\) is isosceles with \(AC = BC,\) \(H\) is the midpoint of \(AB,\) so \(AH = HB = 1.\)

Then \(CH^2 = 7^2 - 1^2 = 48.\) Applying the Pythagorean Theorem to \(\triangle CHD\) with \(HD = HB + BD = 1 + BD\) gives \[ 8^2 = 48 + (1 + BD)^2, \] so \((1 + BD)^2 = 16.\)

Therefore \(1 + BD = 4,\) which means \(BD = 3.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

7 答案 D

令 \(y = 0\)，得 \(|3x| = 12\)，所以 \(x = \pm 4\)。令 \(x = 0\)，得 \(|4y| = 12\)，所以 \(y = \pm 3\)。

该图形是一个菱形，顶点为 \((\pm 4, 0)\) 和 \((0, \pm 3)\)，所以两条对角线的长度分别为 \(8\) 和 \(6\)。

它的面积为 \(\dfrac12 \cdot 8 \cdot 6 = 24\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Setting \(y = 0\) gives \(|3x| = 12,\) so \(x = \pm 4.\) Setting \(x = 0\) gives \(|4y| = 12,\) so \(y = \pm 3.\)

The graph is a rhombus with vertices \((\pm 4, 0)\) and \((0, \pm 3),\) so its diagonals have lengths \(8\) and \(6.\)

Its area is \(\dfrac12 \cdot 8 \cdot 6 = 24.\)

共有 \(4^6\) 种等可能移动。把顶点标为 \(A, B, C, A', B', C'\)，其中带撇号的是对应对顶点。有效结果是一个排列 \(f\)，且 \(f(A) \notin \{A, A'\}\)，其他顶点同理。

有序对 \((f(A), f(A'))\) 有 \(4 \cdot 3 = 12\) 个选择。其中 \(f(A)\)、\(f(A')\) 互为对顶点的有 \(4\) 种，相邻的有 \(8\) 种。

若 \(f(A), f(A')\) 互为对顶点，例如 \(B, B'\)，则 \(\{f(C), f(C')\} = \{A, A'\}\)，且 \(\{f(B), f(B')\} = \{C, C'\}\)，给出 \(4 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 16\) 种。

若 \(f(A), f(A')\) 相邻，例如 \(B, C\)，则 \(f(B), f(B')\) 中必须有一个是 \(C'\)，有 \(4\) 个有序选择 \((f(B), f(B'))\)，每个给 \((f(C), f(C'))\) 留下 \(2\) 种，共 \(8 \cdot 4 \cdot 2 = 64\) 种。

概率为 \[ \dfrac{16 + 64}{4^6} = \dfrac{80}{4096} = \dfrac{5}{256}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 A。

English

There are \(4^6\) equally likely combinations of moves. Label the vertices \(A, B, C, A', B', C',\) where primed vertices are opposite the corresponding unprimed ones. An ant cannot move to its own vertex or the opposite one, so a valid outcome is a permutation \(f\) with \(f(A) \notin \{A, A'\},\) and similarly for each pair.

There are \(4 \cdot 3 = 12\) ordered choices for \((f(A), f(A')).\) Of these, \(f(A)\) and \(f(A')\) are opposite in \(4\) cases and adjacent in \(8.\)

If \(f(A), f(A')\) are opposite, say \(B, B',\) then \(\{f(C), f(C')\} = \{A, A'\}\) and \(\{f(B), f(B')\} = \{C, C'\},\) giving \(4 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 = 16\) valid combinations.

If \(f(A), f(A')\) are adjacent, say \(B, C,\) then one of \(f(B), f(B')\) must be \(C'\) and there are \(4\) ordered choices for \((f(B), f(B')),\) each leaving \(2\) for \((f(C), f(C')):\) that is \(8 \cdot 4 \cdot 2 = 64\) valid combinations.

Hence the probability is \[ \dfrac{16 + 64}{4^6} = \dfrac{80}{4096} = \dfrac{5}{256}. \]

Thus, the correct answer is A.
