3

在一个面向低年级和高年级学生的课后项目中，有一支辩论队，队中来自两个年级的学生人数相同。这个项目共有 \(28\) 名学生，其中 \(25\%\) 的低年级学生和 \(10\%\) 的高年级学生在辩论队中。这个项目中有多少名低年级学生？

A\(5\) B\(6\) C\(8\) D\(11\) E\(20\)

English

In an after-school program for juniors and seniors, there is a debate team with an equal number of students from each class on the team. Among the \(28\) students in the program, \(25\%\) of the juniors and \(10\%\) of the seniors are on the debate team. How many juniors are in the program?

(A) \( 5 \)(B) \( 6 \)(C) \( 8 \)(D) \( 11 \)(E) \( 20 \)

4

在一场数学竞赛中，\(57\) 名学生穿蓝色衬衫，另外 \(75\) 名学生穿黄色衬衫。这 \(132\) 名学生被分成 \(66\) 对。其中恰有 \(23\) 对中两名学生都穿蓝色衬衫。有多少对中两名学生都穿黄色衬衫？

A\(23\) B\(32\) C\(37\) D\(41\) E\(64\)

English

At a math contest, \(57\) students are wearing blue shirts, and another \(75\) students are wearing yellow shirts. The \(132\) students are assigned into \(66\) pairs. In exactly \(23\) of these pairs, both students are wearing blue shirts. In how many pairs are both students wearing yellow shirts?

(A) \( 23 \)(B) \( 32 \)(C) \( 37 \)(D) \( 41 \)(E) \( 64 \)

5

A\(21\) B\(22\) C\(23\) D\(24\) E\(25\)

English

The ages of Jonie’s four cousins are distinct single-digit positive integers. Two of the cousins’ ages multiplied together give \(24,\) while the other two multiply to \(30.\) What is the sum of the ages of Jonie’s four cousins?

(A) \( 21 \)(B) \( 22 \)(C) \( 23 \)(D) \( 24 \)(E) \( 25 \)

(A) \( 74 \)(B) \( 75 \)(C) \( 76 \)(D) \( 77 \)(E) \( 78 \)

What is the maximum possible area of region \(S?\)

(A) \( 24\pi \)(B) \( 32\pi \)(C) \( 64\pi \)(D) \( 65\pi \)(E) \( 84\pi \)

19

设 \(S\) 是一个有限的正整数集合。

如果从 \(S\) 中移除 \(S\) 中最大的整数，剩余整数的平均值为 \(32\)。如果再从 \(S\) 中移除最小的整数，剩余整数的平均值为 \(35\)。如果随后把最大的整数放回 \(S\)，平均值升高到 \(40\)。原集合 \(S\) 中最大的整数比最小的整数大 \(72\)。

集合 \(S\) 中所有整数的平均值是多少？

A\(36.2\) B\(36.4\) C\(36.6\) D\(36.8\) E\(37\)

English

Suppose that \(S\) is a finite set of positive integers.

If the greatest integer in \(S\) is removed from \(S,\) then the average value (arithmetic mean) of the integers remaining is \(32.\) If the least integer in \(S\) is also removed, then the average value of the integers remaining is \(35.\) If the greatest integer is then returned to the set, the average value of the integers rises to \(40.\) The greatest integer in the original set \(S\) is \(72\) greater than the least integer in \(S.\)

What is the average value of all the integers in the set \(S?\)

(A) \( 36.2 \)(B) \( 36.4 \)(C) \( 36.6 \)(D) \( 36.8 \)(E) \( 37 \)

2 答案 D

因为 \(\sqrt{u^2}=|u|\)，所以原式等于 \[|3-2\sqrt3|+|3+2\sqrt3|\text{。}\] 又因为 \(2\sqrt3>3\)，所以它化为 \[(2\sqrt3-3)+(3+2\sqrt3)=4\sqrt3\text{。}\]

Thus, the correct answer is D .

23 答案 C

硬币半径为 \(\frac12\)，所以它的圆心均匀分布在一个 \(7\times7\) 的正方形内，该区域面积为 \(49\)。

对每个角上的阴影三角形，能覆盖到它的圆心位置是在允许区域内距离该三角形不超过 \(\frac12\) 的点集。每个角对应一个高为 \(\frac{1+\sqrt2}{2}\) 的等腰直角三角形，所以面积为

\[\left(\frac{1+\sqrt2}{2}\right)^2=\frac{3+2\sqrt2}{4}\text{。}\]

四个角合计贡献 \(3+2\sqrt2\)。

中心阴影菱形是一个边长为 \(2\sqrt2\) 的正方形。把它向外扩张距离 \(\frac12\)，除了菱形本身面积 \(8\)，还增加总面积 \(4\sqrt2\) 的四个矩形，以及合起来面积为 \(\frac\pi4\) 的四个四分之一圆。因此中心部分贡献

\[8+4\sqrt2+\frac\pi4\text{。}\]

有利面积为

\[ \begin{aligned} &3+2\sqrt2+8+4\sqrt2+\frac\pi4 \\ &=11+6\sqrt2+\frac\pi4 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

概率为

\[ \begin{aligned} &\frac{11+6\sqrt2+\frac\pi4}{49} \\ &=\frac{44+24\sqrt2+\pi}{196} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以 \(a+b=44+24=68\)。

所以答案是 C。

English

The coin has radius \(\frac12,\) so its center is uniformly distributed over a \(7\times7\) square of area \(49.\)

A shaded corner triangle contributes the set of center positions within distance \(\frac12\) of that triangle, inside the allowed center square. For each corner this is a right isosceles triangle whose altitude is \(\frac{1+\sqrt2}{2},\) so its area is

\[\left(\frac{1+\sqrt2}{2}\right)^2=\frac{3+2\sqrt2}{4}.\]

All four corners contribute \(3+2\sqrt2.\)

The center shaded diamond is a square of side \(2\sqrt2.\) Expanding it by distance \(\frac12\) adds four rectangles of total area \(4\sqrt2\) and four quarter-circles of total area \(\frac\pi4,\) in addition to the diamond’s area \(8.\) Thus the center contribution is

\[8+4\sqrt2+\frac\pi4.\]

The favorable area is

\[ \begin{aligned} &3+2\sqrt2+8+4\sqrt2+\frac\pi4 \\ &=11+6\sqrt2+\frac\pi4. \end{aligned} \]

The probability is

\[ \begin{aligned} &\frac{11+6\sqrt2+\frac\pi4}{49} \\ &=\frac{44+24\sqrt2+\pi}{196}. \end{aligned} \]

So \(a+b=44+24=68.\)

Thus, the answer is C .

24 答案 B

对长度为 \(n\) 的单独一堵墙，根据所有可能操作计算它的 Sprague-Grundy 值。这里用到的各个墙长的值为

\[ \begin{aligned} &g(1)=1,\quad g(2)=2, \\ &g(3)=3,\quad g(4)=1, \\ &g(5)=4,\quad g(6)=3 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

多堵墙的局面在这些值按位异或为 \(0\) 时，正好是轮到行动者的必败局面。逐一计算选项：

\[(6,1,1): 3\oplus1\oplus1=3\text{，}\]

\[(6,2,1): 3\oplus2\oplus1=0\text{，}\]

\[(6,2,2): 3\oplus2\oplus2=3\text{，}\]

\[(6,3,1): 3\oplus3\oplus1=1\text{，}\]

\[(6,3,2): 3\oplus3\oplus2=2\text{。}\]

只有 \((6,2,1)\) 是轮到行动者的必败局面，所以 Beth 恰好在这个初始配置下有必胜策略。

所以答案是 B。

English

For a single wall of length \(n,\) compute its Sprague-Grundy value from the possible moves. For the wall lengths needed here, the values are

\[ \begin{aligned} &g(1)=1,\quad g(2)=2, \\ &g(3)=3,\quad g(4)=1, \\ &g(5)=4,\quad g(6)=3. \end{aligned} \]

For several walls, the position is losing for the player to move exactly when the xor of the wall values is \(0.\) Evaluating the choices gives

\[(6,1,1): 3\oplus1\oplus1=3,\]

\[(6,2,1): 3\oplus2\oplus1=0,\]

\[(6,2,2): 3\oplus2\oplus2=3,\]

\[(6,3,1): 3\oplus3\oplus1=1,\]

\[(6,3,2): 3\oplus3\oplus2=2.\]

Only \((6,2,1)\) is losing for the player to move, so Beth has a guaranteed win exactly for that starting configuration.

Thus, the answer is B .

25 答案 E

对固定斜率 \(m\)，集合 \(S\) 中位于 \(y=mx\) 上或下方的点数为

\[\sum_{x=1}^{30}\lfloor mx\rfloor\text{，}\]

这适用于答案附近的斜率。

当 \(m=\frac23\) 时，把 \(x=3k+1,3k+2,3k+3\) 分组，其中 \(k=0,1,\ldots,9\)。于是

\[\lfloor \frac{2x}{3}\rfloor=2k,\ 2k+1, \ 2k+2\text{，}\]

每个区块上的和为 \(6k+3\)。因此总数为

\[\sum_{k=0}^9(6k+3)=270+30=300\text{。}\]

若 \(m<\frac23\)，十个比值 \(\frac{y}{x}=\frac{2}{3}\) 的点不再被计入，所以点数小于 \(300\)。因此区间下端为 \(\frac23\)。

下一个大于 \(\frac23\) 的可能比值 \(\frac{y}{x}\)，在 \(1\le x,y\le30\) 下可按 \(x\) 模 \(3\) 检查。最佳候选为

\[\frac{19}{28},\qquad \frac{20}{29},\qquad \frac{21}{30}=\frac{7}{10}\text{，}\]

其中最小的是 \(\frac{19}{28}\)。因此区间长度为

\[\frac{19}{28}-\frac23=\frac1{84}\text{。}\]

所以 \(a+b=1+84=85\)。

所以答案是 E。

English

For a fixed slope \(m,\) the number of points in \(S\) on or below \(y=mx\) is

\[\sum_{x=1}^{30}\lfloor mx\rfloor,\]

for the slopes near the answer.

At \(m=\frac23,\) grouping \(x=3k+1,3k+2,3k+3\) for \(k=0,1,\ldots,9\) gives

\[\lfloor \frac{2x}{3}\rfloor=2k,\ 2k+1, \ 2k+2,\]

whose sum over each block is \(6k+3.\) Thus the total is

\[\sum_{k=0}^9(6k+3)=270+30=300.\]

If \(m<\frac23,\) the ten points with ratios \(\frac{y}{x}=\frac{2}{3}\) are no longer counted, so the count is less than \(300.\) Therefore the lower end is \(\frac23.\)

The next possible ratio \(\frac{y}{x}\) greater than \(\frac23,\) with \(1\le x,y\le30,\) is minimized by checking \(x\) modulo \(3.\) The best candidates are

\[\frac{19}{28},\qquad \frac{20}{29},\qquad \frac{21}{30}=\frac{7}{10},\]

and the smallest is \(\frac{19}{28}.\) Hence the interval length is

\[\frac{19}{28}-\frac23=\frac1{84}.\]

Thus \(a+b=1+84=85.\)

Thus, the answer is E .
