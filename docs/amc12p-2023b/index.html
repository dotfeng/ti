English

Mrs. Jones is pouring orange juice into four identical glasses for her four sons. She fills the first three glasses completely but runs out of juice when the fourth glass is only \(\tfrac13\) full. What fraction of a glass must Mrs. Jones pour from each of the first three glasses into the fourth glass so that all four glasses will have the same amount of juice?

(A) \(\dfrac{1}{12}\)(B) \(\dfrac{1}{4}\)(C) \(\dfrac{1}{6}\)(D) \(\dfrac{1}{8}\)(E) \(\dfrac{2}{9}\)

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

The total juice is \(3+\tfrac13=\tfrac{10}{3}\) glasses. Split evenly, each glass should hold \(\tfrac{10}{3}\div 4=\tfrac{5}{6}\) of a glass. Each of the first three glasses must therefore give up \(1-\tfrac{5}{6}=\tfrac{1}{6}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

Setting \(a=b=0\) gives \(2f(0)=2f(0)^2,\) so \(f(0)=0\) or \(f(0)=1.\) If \(f(0)=0,\) then setting \(b=0\) forces \(f\equiv 0,\) giving \(f(1)=0.\) Otherwise \(f(0)=1,\) and setting \(a=b\) gives \(f(2a)=2f(a)^2-1\ge -1\) for every \(a.\) In particular, with \(a=\tfrac12,\) \(f(1)=2f\!\left(\tfrac12\right)^2-1\ge -1.\) So \(f(1)\ge -1,\) and indeed every value in \([-1,\infty)\) is attainable (e.g. \(f(x)=\cos(kx)\) or \(\cosh(kx)\)). Hence \(-2\) is impossible.

Thus, the correct answer is E.

23 答案 A

每个骰子在质数 \(2,3,5\) 上贡献一个指数向量（点数 \(1\to(0,0,0)\)，\(2\to(1,0,0)\)，\(3\to(0,1,0)\)，\(4\to(2,0,0)\)，\(5\to(0,0,1)\)，\(6\to(1,1,0)\)），而乘积由这些向量之和决定。

要求恰有 \(k\) 枚骰子显示 \(5\)，从而固定 \(5\) 的指数，并令 \(m=n-k\)。对 \(3\) 的指数 \(b\)，其中 \(0\le b\le m\)，\(2\) 的可能指数 \(a\) 恰好是 \(0,1,\ldots,2m-b\)。若 \(a\le b\)，就用 \(a\) 枚显示 \(6\) 的骰子和 \(b-a\) 枚显示 \(3\) 的骰子。若 \(a\gt b\)，就用 \(b\) 枚显示 \(6\) 的骰子，再用显示 \(4\) 的骰子（必要时加一枚显示 \(2\) 的骰子）凑出余下的 \(a-b\) 个因子 \(2\)。由 \(a\le 2m-b\) 可知这样至多用 \(m\) 枚骰子；其余骰子都显示 \(1\)。因此指数对的个数为 \[ \begin{gathered} \sum_{b=0}^{m}(2m-b+1)\\ {}=\frac{(m+1)(3m+2)}2 \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

对 \(k=0,1,\ldots,n\) 求和，也就是对 \(m=0,1,\ldots,n\) 求和，不同乘积的个数为 \[ \begin{gathered} \sum_{m=0}^{n}\frac{(m+1)(3m+2)}2\\ {}=\frac{(n+1)^2(n+2)}2 \end{gathered}\text{。}\] 当 \(n=11\) 时它等于 \(\dfrac{12^2\cdot13}{2}=936\)，所以 \(n=11\)。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

Each die contributes an exponent vector in the primes \(2,3,5\) (face \(1\to(0,0,0),\) \(2\to(1,0,0),\) \(3\to(0,1,0),\) \(4\to(2,0,0),\) \(5\to(0,0,1),\) \(6\to(1,1,0)\)), and a product is determined by the sum of these vectors.

Fix the exponent of \(5\) by requiring exactly \(k\) dice to show \(5,\) and put \(m=n-k.\) For an exponent \(b\) of \(3,\) where \(0\le b\le m,\) the possible exponents \(a\) of \(2\) are precisely \(0,1,\ldots,2m-b.\) If \(a\le b,\) use \(a\) faces showing \(6\) and \(b-a\) showing \(3.\) If \(a\gt b,\) use \(b\) faces showing \(6\) and make the remaining \(a-b\) factors of \(2\) with faces \(4\) and, if needed, one face \(2.\) The bound \(a\le 2m-b\) says this uses at most \(m\) dice; fill unused dice with \(1\)’s. Thus the number of exponent pairs is \[ \begin{gathered} \sum_{b=0}^{m}(2m-b+1)\\ {}=\frac{(m+1)(3m+2)}2. \end{gathered} \]

Summing over \(k=0,1,\ldots,n\), equivalently over \(m=0,1,\ldots,n,\) the number of distinct products is \[ \begin{gathered} \sum_{m=0}^{n}\frac{(m+1)(3m+2)}2\\ {}=\frac{(n+1)^2(n+2)}2. \end{gathered} \] For \(n=11\) this is \(\dfrac{12^2\cdot13}{2}=936,\) so \(n=11.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

24 答案 C

对每个质数分别处理 \(a,b,c,d\) 中的指数。

质数 \(2\)（总指数 \(6\)）：\(\max(b,c)=1\) 强制 \(a=3\)；然后 \(b+c+d=3\)，且 \(\max(b,d)=\max(c,d)=2\)，得 \(d=2\) 且 \(\{b,c\}=\{0,1\}\)，所以最小指数为 \(0\)。

质数 \(3\)（总指数 \(9\)）：\(\max(a,b)=2\)，而其他最小公倍数都等于 \(3\)，强制 \(c=d=3\)；然后 \(a+b=3\)，且 \(\max(a,b)=2\)，得 \(\{a,b\}=\{1,2\}\)，所以最小指数为 \(1\)。

质数 \(5\)（总指数 \(7\)）：\(\max(a,b)=3\)，且 \(\max(b,c)\)，\(\max(b,d),\max(c,d)=2\)，强制 \(a=3\)；然后 \(b+c+d=4\)，每个都 \(\le 2\)，且两两最大值为 \(2\)，所以其中两个等于 \(2\)，一个等于 \(0\)，最小指数为 \(0\)。

因此 \(\gcd(a,b,c,d)=2^0\cdot 3^1\cdot 5^0=3\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Handle each prime separately using the exponents of \(a,b,c,d.\)

Prime \(2\) (total \(6\)): \(\max(b,c)=1\) forces \(a=3;\) then \(b+c+d=3\) with \(\max(b,d)=\max(c,d)=2\) gives \(d=2\) and \(\{b,c\}=\{0,1\},\) so the minimum exponent is \(0.\)

Prime \(3\) (total \(9\)): \(\max(a,b)=2\) with the other lcms equal to \(3\) forces \(c=d=3;\) then \(a+b=3\) with \(\max(a,b)=2\) gives \(\{a,b\}=\{1,2\},\) so the minimum is \(1.\)

Prime \(5\) (total \(7\)): \(\max(a,b)=3\) with \(\max(b,c),\) \(\max(b,d),\max(c,d)=2\) forces \(a=3;\) then \(b+c+d=4\) with each \(\le 2\) and pairwise maxima \(2\) gives two of them equal to \(2\) and one equal to \(0,\) so the minimum is \(0.\)

Therefore \(\gcd(a,b,c,d)=2^0\cdot 3^1\cdot 5^0=3.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

25 答案 B

设原五边形的外接圆半径为 \(R\)。将一个顶点折到中心时，折痕是中心到该顶点线段的垂直平分线，即离中心距离为 \(\tfrac{R}{2}\) 的直线。五条折痕围成一个内切圆半径为 \(\tfrac{R}{2}\) 的正五边形，而原五边形的内切圆半径为 \(R\cos 36^\circ\)。面积按内切圆半径的平方成比例，所以比例为 \[ \frac{(\frac{R}{2})^2}{(R\cos 36^\circ)^2}=\frac{1}{4\cos^2 36^\circ}\text{。}\] 因为 \(\cos 36^\circ=\tfrac{1+\sqrt5}{4}\)，这个比例为 \(\tfrac{4}{(1+\sqrt5)^2}\) \(=\tfrac{4}{6+2\sqrt5}\) \(=\tfrac{2}{3+\sqrt5}\) \(=\tfrac{3-\sqrt5}{2}\)。乘以原面积 \(\sqrt5+1\) 得 \(\tfrac{(3-\sqrt5)(\sqrt5+1)}{2}\) \(=\tfrac{2\sqrt5-2}{2}\) \(=\sqrt5-1\)。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Let the original pentagon have circumradius \(R.\) Folding a vertex to the center creases along the perpendicular bisector of the segment from the center to that vertex, a line at distance \(\tfrac{R}{2}\) from the center. The five creases bound a regular pentagon with apothem \(\tfrac{R}{2},\) whereas the original has apothem \(R\cos 36^\circ.\) Areas scale as the square of the apothem, so the ratio is \[ \frac{(\frac{R}{2})^2}{(R\cos 36^\circ)^2}=\frac{1}{4\cos^2 36^\circ}. \] Since \(\cos 36^\circ=\tfrac{1+\sqrt5}{4},\) this ratio is \(\tfrac{4}{(1+\sqrt5)^2}\) \(=\tfrac{4}{6+2\sqrt5}\) \(=\tfrac{2}{3+\sqrt5}\) \(=\tfrac{3-\sqrt5}{2}.\) Multiplying by the original area \(\sqrt5+1\) gives \(\tfrac{(3-\sqrt5)(\sqrt5+1)}{2}\) \(=\tfrac{2\sqrt5-2}{2}\) \(=\sqrt5-1.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.
