1

一个手机套餐每月费用为 \(\$20\) 外加每条短信 \(5\)¢，以及超过 \(30\) 小时后每分钟 \(10\)¢。Michelle 一月份发送了 \(100\) 条短信，并通话 \(30.5\) 小时。她需要支付多少钱？

A\(\$24.00\) B\(\$24.50\) C\(\$25.50\) D\(\$28.00\) E\(\$30.00\)

English

A cell phone plan costs \(\$20\) each month, plus \(5\)¢ per text message sent, plus \(10\)¢ for each minute used over \(30\) hours. In January Michelle sent \(100\) text messages and talked for \(30.5\) hours. How much did she have to pay?

(A) \(\$24.00\)(B) \(\$24.50\)(C) \(\$25.50\)(D) \(\$28.00\)(E) \(\$30.00\)

2

有 \(5\) 枚硬币按图示平放在桌上。按从上到下的顺序，这些硬币应怎样排列？

A\((C, A, E, D, B)\) B\((C, A, D, E, B)\) C\((C, D, E, A, B)\) D\((C, E, A, D, B)\) E\((C, E, D, A, B)\)

English

There are \(5\) coins placed flat on a table according to the figure. What is the order of the coins from top to bottom?

(A) \((C, A, E, D, B)\)(B) \((C, A, D, E, B)\)(C) \((C, D, E, A, B)\)(D) \((C, E, A, D, B)\)(E) \((C, E, D, A, B)\)

3

一个小洗发水瓶可装 \(35\) 毫升洗发水，而一个大瓶可装 \(500\) 毫升洗发水。Jasmine 想购买最少数量的小瓶，足以完全装满一个大瓶。她必须买多少瓶？

A\(11\) B\(12\) C\(13\) D\(14\) E\(15\)

English

A small bottle of shampoo can hold \(35\) milliliters of shampoo, whereas a large bottle can hold \(500\) milliliters of shampoo. Jasmine wants to buy the minimum number of small bottles necessary to completely fill a large bottle. How many bottles must she buy?

(A) \(11\)(B) \(12\)(C) \(13\)(D) \(14\)(E) \(15\)

4

在一所小学，三年级、四年级、五年级学生每天平均分别跑 \(12\)，\(15\)，和 \(10\) 分钟。三年级学生人数是四年级的两倍，四年级学生人数是五年级的两倍。这些学生每天平均跑多少分钟？

A\(12\) B\(\dfrac{37}{3}\) C\(\dfrac{88}{7}\) D\(13\) E\(14\)

English

At an elementary school, the students in third grade, fourth grade, and fifth grade run an average of \(12,\) \(15,\) and \(10\) minutes per day, respectively. There are twice as many third graders as fourth graders, and twice as many fourth graders as fifth graders. What is the average number of minutes run per day by these students?

(A) \(12\)(B) \(\dfrac{37}{3}\)(C) \(\dfrac{88}{7}\)(D) \(13\)(E) \(14\)

5

去年夏天，Town Lake 上生活的鸟中 \(30\%\) 是鹅，\(25\%\) 是天鹅，\(10\%\) 是鹭，\(35\%\) 是鸭。在不是天鹅的鸟中，鹅占百分之几？

A\(20\) B\(30\) C\(40\) D\(50\) E\(60\)

English

Last summer \(30\%\) of the birds living on Town Lake were geese, \(25\%\) were swans, \(10\%\) were herons, and \(35\%\) were ducks. What percent of the birds that were not swans were geese?

(A) \(20\)(B) \(30\)(C) \(40\)(D) \(50\)(E) \(60\)

6

一个篮球队的队员投进了一些三分球、一些两分球和一些一分罚球。他们由两分球得到的分数与由三分球得到的分数一样多。他们罚中的次数比投中的两分球次数多一。球队总得分为 \(61\) 分。他们罚中了多少球？

A\(13\) B\(14\) C\(15\) D\(16\) E\(17\)

English

The players on a basketball team made some three-point shots, some two-point shots, and some one-point free throws. They scored as many points with two-point shots as with three-point shots. Their number of successful free throws was one more than their number of successful two-point shots. The team’s total score was \(61\) points. How many free throws did they make?

(A) \(13\)(B) \(14\)(C) \(15\)(D) \(16\)(E) \(17\)

7

Ms. Demeanor 班上 \(30\) 名学生中的多数人在学校书店买了铅笔。这些学生每人买了相同数量的铅笔，且这个数量大于 \(1\)。每支铅笔的价格（单位：美分）大于每名学生买的铅笔数量，所有铅笔总费用为 \(\$17.71\)。每支铅笔的价格是多少美分？

A\(7\) B\(11\) C\(17\) D\(23\) E\(77\)

English

A majority of the \(30\) students in Ms. Demeanor’s class bought pencils at the school bookstore. Each of these students bought the same number of pencils, and this number was greater than \(1.\) The cost of a pencil in cents was greater than the number of pencils each student bought, and the total cost of all the pencils was \(\$17.71.\) What was the cost of a pencil in cents?

(A) \(7\)(B) \(11\)(C) \(17\)(D) \(23\)(E) \(77\)

8

在八项数列 \(A\)，\(B\)，\(C\)，\(D\)，\(E\)，\(F\)，\(G\)，\(H\) 中，\(C\) 的值为 \(5\)，且任意三个连续项的和为 \(30\)。求 \(A + H\)？

A\(17\) B\(18\) C\(25\) D\(26\) E\(43\)

English

In the eight-term sequence \(A,\) \(B,\) \(C,\) \(D,\) \(E,\) \(F,\) \(G,\) \(H,\) the value of \(C\) is \(5\) and the sum of any three consecutive terms is \(30.\) What is \(A + H?\)

(A) \(17\)(B) \(18\)(C) \(25\)(D) \(26\)(E) \(43\)

9

在一个双胞胎和三胞胎大会上，有 \(9\) 组双胞胎和 \(6\) 组三胞胎，且都来自不同家庭。每个双胞胎成员都与除自己兄弟姐妹外的所有双胞胎成员握手，并与一半的三胞胎成员握手。每个三胞胎成员都与除自己兄弟姐妹外的所有三胞胎成员握手，并与一半的双胞胎成员握手。一共发生了多少次握手？

A\(324\) B\(441\) C\(630\) D\(648\) E\(882\)

English

At a twins and triplets convention, there were \(9\) sets of twins and \(6\) sets of triplets, all from different families. Each twin shook hands with all the twins except his/her sibling and with half the triplets. Each triplet shook hands with all the triplets except his/her siblings and with half the twins. How many handshakes took place?

(A) \(324\)(B) \(441\)(C) \(630\)(D) \(648\)(E) \(882\)

10

一对标准 \(6\) 面公平骰子掷一次。掷出的点数和决定一个圆的直径。圆的面积的数值小于该圆周长的数值的概率是多少？

A\(\dfrac{1}{36}\) B\(\dfrac{1}{12}\) C\(\dfrac{1}{6}\) D\(\dfrac{1}{4}\) E\(\dfrac{5}{18}\)

English

A pair of standard \(6\)-sided fair dice is rolled once. The sum of the numbers rolled determines the diameter of a circle. What is the probability that the numerical value of the area of the circle is less than the numerical value of the circle’s circumference?

(A) \(\dfrac{1}{36}\)(B) \(\dfrac{1}{12}\)(C) \(\dfrac{1}{6}\)(D) \(\dfrac{1}{4}\)(E) \(\dfrac{5}{18}\)

11

圆 \(A\)、\(B\) 和 \(C\) 的半径都为 \(1\)。圆 \(A\) 和圆 \(B\) 共有一个切点。圆 \(C\) 与 \(\overline{AB}\) 的中点相切。位于圆 \(C\) 内部、但在圆 \(A\) 和圆 \(B\) 外部的面积是多少？

A\(3 - \dfrac{\pi}{2}\) B\(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\) C\(2\) D\(\dfrac{3\pi}{4}\) E\(1 + \dfrac{\pi}{2}\)

English

Circles \(A,\) \(B,\) and \(C\) each have radius \(1.\) Circles \(A\) and \(B\) share one point of tangency. Circle \(C\) has a point of tangency with the midpoint of \(\overline{AB}.\) What is the area inside circle \(C\) but outside circle \(A\) and circle \(B?\)

(A) \(3 - \dfrac{\pi}{2}\)(B) \(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\)(C) \(2\)(D) \(\dfrac{3\pi}{4}\)(E) \(1 + \dfrac{\pi}{2}\)

12

一艘机动船和一只木筏都从河上的码头 \(A\) 出发，向下游前进。木筏以河水水流速度漂流。机动船相对于河水保持恒定速度。机动船到达下游码头 \(B\) 后，立即掉头向上游返回。离开码头 \(A\) \(9\) 小时后，它在河上与木筏相遇。机动船从 \(A\) 到 \(B\) 花了多少小时？

A\(3\) B\(3.5\) C\(4\) D\(4.5\) E\(5\)

English

A power boat and a raft both left dock \(A\) on a river and headed downstream. The raft drifted at the speed of the river current. The power boat maintained a constant speed with respect to the river. The power boat reached dock \(B\) downriver, then immediately turned and traveled back upriver. It eventually met the raft on the river \(9\) hours after leaving dock \(A.\) How many hours did it take the power boat to go from \(A\) to \(B?\)

(A) \(3\)(B) \(3.5\)(C) \(4\)(D) \(4.5\)(E) \(5\)

13

三角形 \(ABC\) 的边长为 \(AB = 12\)，\(BC = 24\)，且 \(AC = 18\)。过 \(\triangle ABC\) 内心且平行于 \(\overline{BC}\) 的直线分别交 \(\overline{AB}\) 于 \(M\)，交 \(\overline{AC}\) 于 \(N\)。\(\triangle AMN\) 的周长是多少？

A\(27\) B\(30\) C\(33\) D\(36\) E\(42\)

English

Triangle \(ABC\) has side-lengths \(AB = 12,\) \(BC = 24,\) and \(AC = 18.\) The line through the incenter of \(\triangle ABC\) parallel to \(\overline{BC}\) intersects \(\overline{AB}\) at \(M\) and \(\overline{AC}\) at \(N.\) What is the perimeter of \(\triangle AMN?\)

(A) \(27\)(B) \(30\)(C) \(33\)(D) \(36\)(E) \(42\)

14

假设 \(a\) 和 \(b\) 是独立随机选取的一位正整数。点 \((a, b)\) 位于抛物线 \(y = ax^2 - bx\) 上方的概率是多少？

A\(\dfrac{11}{81}\) B\(\dfrac{13}{81}\) C\(\dfrac{5}{27}\) D\(\dfrac{17}{81}\) E\(\dfrac{19}{81}\)

English

Suppose \(a\) and \(b\) are single-digit positive integers chosen independently and at random. What is the probability that the point \((a, b)\) lies above the parabola \(y = ax^2 - bx?\)

(A) \(\dfrac{11}{81}\)(B) \(\dfrac{13}{81}\)(C) \(\dfrac{5}{27}\)(D) \(\dfrac{17}{81}\)(E) \(\dfrac{19}{81}\)

15

一个半径为 \(2\) 的半球的圆形底面放在一个高为 \(6\) 的正方形棱锥的底面上。该半球与棱锥的另外四个面相切。棱锥底面边长是多少？

A\(3\sqrt{2}\) B\(\dfrac{13}{3}\) C\(4\sqrt{2}\) D\(6\) E\(\dfrac{13}{2}\)

English

The circular base of a hemisphere of radius \(2\) rests on the base of a square pyramid of height \(6.\) The hemisphere is tangent to the other four faces of the pyramid. What is the edge-length of the base of the pyramid?

(A) \(3\sqrt{2}\)(B) \(\dfrac{13}{3}\)(C) \(4\sqrt{2}\)(D) \(6\)(E) \(\dfrac{13}{2}\)

16

凸五边形 \(ABCDE\) 的每个顶点都要指定一种颜色。有 \(6\) 种颜色可选，并且每条对角线的两个端点必须颜色不同。共有多少种不同的涂色方法？

A\(2520\) B\(2880\) C\(3120\) D\(3250\) E\(3750\)

English

Each vertex of convex pentagon \(ABCDE\) is to be assigned a color. There are \(6\) colors to choose from, and the ends of each diagonal must have different colors. How many different colorings are possible?

(A) \(2520\)(B) \(2880\)(C) \(3120\)(D) \(3250\)(E) \(3750\)

17

半径为 \(1\)，\(2\)，和 \(3\) 的三个圆两两外切。由这些切点确定的三角形面积是多少？

A\(\dfrac{3}{5}\) B\(\dfrac{4}{5}\) C\(1\) D\(\dfrac{6}{5}\) E\(\dfrac{4}{3}\)

English

(A) \(\dfrac{3}{5}\)(B) \(\dfrac{4}{5}\)(C) \(1\)(D) \(\dfrac{6}{5}\)(E) \(\dfrac{4}{3}\)

18

假设 \(|x + y| + |x - y| = 2\)。\(x^2 - 6x + y^2\) 的最大可能值是多少？

A\(5\) B\(6\) C\(7\) D\(8\) E\(9\)

English

19

在一个有 \(N\) 名选手的比赛中，被授予精英身份的选手人数等于 \[ 2^{1 + \lfloor \log_2 (N - 1) \rfloor} - N\text{。} \] 假设有 \(19\) 名选手被授予精英身份。\(N\) 的两个最小可能值之和是多少？

注：\(\lfloor x \rfloor\) 是小于或等于 \(x\) 的最大整数。

A\(38\) B\(90\) C\(154\) D\(406\) E\(1024\)

English

At a competition with \(N\) players, the number of players given elite status is equal to \[ 2^{1 + \lfloor \log_2 (N - 1) \rfloor} - N. \] Suppose that \(19\) players are given elite status. What is the sum of the two smallest possible values of \(N?\)

Note: \(\lfloor x \rfloor\) is the greatest integer less than or equal to \(x.\)

(A) \(38\)(B) \(90\)(C) \(154\)(D) \(406\)(E) \(1024\)

20

令 \(f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c\)，其中 \(a\)、\(b\) 和 \(c\) 是整数。假设 \(f(1) = 0\)、\(50 \lt f(7) \lt 60\)、\(70 \lt f(8) \lt 80\)，并且对某个整数 \(k\)，有 \(5000k \lt f(100) \lt 5000(k+1)\)。求 \(k\)。

A\(1\) B\(2\) C\(3\) D\(4\) E\(5\)

English

Let \(f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c,\) where \(a,\) \(b,\) and \(c\) are integers. Suppose that \(f(1) = 0,\) \(50 \lt f(7) \lt 60,\) \(70 \lt f(8) \lt 80,\) and \(5000k \lt f(100) \lt 5000(k+1)\) for some integer \(k.\) What is \(k?\)

21

Let \(f_1(x) = \sqrt{1 - x},\) and for integers \(n \ge 2,\) let \(f_n(x) = f_{n-1}\!\left(\sqrt{n^2 - x}\right).\) If \(N\) is the largest value of \(n\) for which the domain of \(f_n\) is nonempty, the domain of \(f_N\) is \(\{c\}.\) What is \(N + c?\)

(A) \(-226\)(B) \(-144\)(C) \(-20\)(D) \(20\)(E) \(144\)

22

How many points are \(100\)-ray partitional but not \(60\)-ray partitional?

(A) \(1500\)(B) \(1560\)(C) \(2320\)(D) \(2480\)(E) \(2500\)

23

24

逐题解析

1 答案 D

短信费用为 \(100 \cdot 5 = 500\) 美分 \(= \$5\)。她比 \(30\) 小时套餐多通话了 \(30\) 分钟，因此超时费用为 \(30 \cdot 10 = 300\) 美分 \(= \$3\)。

总费用为 \(\$20 + \$5 + \$3 = \$28\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

The text charge is \(100 \cdot 5 = 500\) cents \(= \$5.\) She talked \(30\) minutes past the \(30\)-hour allowance, so the overage is \(30 \cdot 10 = 300\) cents \(= \$3.\)

The total is \(\$20 + \$5 + \$3 = \$28.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

2 答案 E

硬币 \(C\) 画成一个完整且没有中断的圆，所以没有东西盖住它，它在最上面。

继续观察其余重叠处，未被遮住的弧线显示每枚硬币在下一枚硬币上方：\(C\) 盖住 \(E\)，\(E\) 盖住 \(D\)，\(D\) 盖住 \(B\) 而 \(A\) 盖住 \(B\)，同时在其他硬币下方。因此从上到下的顺序是 \((C, E, D, A, B)\)。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

Coin \(C\) is drawn as a complete, unbroken circle, so nothing covers it and it lies on top.

Reading the remaining overlaps, each coin’s uncovered arc shows it sits above the next: \(C\) covers \(E,\) \(E\) covers \(D,\) \(D\) covers \(B,\) and \(A\) covers \(B\) while lying under the others. This gives the top-to-bottom order \((C, E, D, A, B).\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

3 答案 E

十四瓶可装 \(14 \cdot 35 = 490\) 毫升，这还不够。十五瓶可装 \(15 \cdot 35 = 525\) 毫升，已经足够。

所以 Jasmine 需要 \(15\) 瓶。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

Fourteen bottles hold \(14 \cdot 35 = 490\) milliliters, which is not enough. Fifteen bottles hold \(15 \cdot 35 = 525\) milliliters, which suffices.

So Jasmine needs \(15\) bottles.

Thus, the correct answer is E.

4 答案 C

三、四、五年级的人数比为 \(4 : 2 : 1\)。加权平均数为 \[ \begin{aligned} \dfrac{4\cdot12+2\cdot15+10}{7} \\ = \dfrac{48+30+10}{7} \\ = \dfrac{88}{7} \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

Direct composition gives \[ g(z)=\dfrac{Az+B}{Cz+D}, \] where \(A=1+a,\) \(B=a(1+b),\) \(C=1+b,\) and \(D=a+b^2.\)

The matrix \(\begin{pmatrix}A&B\\C&D\end{pmatrix}\) represents \(g.\) For \(g\circ g\) to be the identity, its square must be scalar. Comparing the off-diagonal entries and the two diagonal entries gives two possibilities: either \(B=C=0\) and \(A=D,\) or \(A+D=0.\) The first gives \(b=-1\) (with \(a\ne-1\)); the second gives \[ b^2=-(1+2a). \]

In the second case, \(|b|^2=|1+2a|.\) As \(a\) runs around the unit circle, \(|1+2a|\) ranges from \(1\) to \(3,\) so \(1\le|b|\le\sqrt3.\) Both endpoints occur: take \((a,b)=(-1,1)\) and \((a,b)=(1,i\sqrt3).\) The separate case \(b=-1\) also has \(|b|=1.\) Therefore the requested difference is \(\sqrt3-1.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

24 答案 C

设以 \(X\) 为圆心、半径为 \(r\) 的圆能放入其中一个四边形。如果 \(h_1,h_2,h_3,h_4\) 是 \(X\) 到四条边所在直线的距离，那么每个 \(h_i\ge r\)。将四边形分成四个三角形，得到 \[ \begin{aligned} K&=\dfrac12(14h_1+9h_2 \\ &\qquad+7h_3+12h_4) \\ &\ge21r \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

布雷特施奈德不等式表明，给定这些边长的四边形面积不超过圆内接情形：\[ \begin{aligned} K^2&\le(21-14)(21-9) \\ &\qquad\cdot(21-7)(21-12) \\ &=7\cdot12\cdot14\cdot9, \\ K&\le42\sqrt6 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因此 \(r\le \frac{K}{21}\le2\sqrt6\)。等号可以达到：具有这些边长的圆内接四边形也有内切圆，因为 \(14+7=9+12\)，其内切圆半径为 \(\frac{K}{21}=2\sqrt6\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

Suppose a circle of radius \(r\) centered at \(X\) fits in one of the quadrilaterals. If \(h_1,h_2,h_3,h_4\) are the distances from \(X\) to the four side lines, then each \(h_i\ge r.\) Splitting the quadrilateral into four triangles gives \[ \begin{aligned} K&=\dfrac12(14h_1+9h_2 \\ &\qquad+7h_3+12h_4) \\ &\ge21r. \end{aligned} \]

Bretschneider’s inequality bounds the area of any quadrilateral with these sides by the cyclic case: \[ \begin{aligned} K^2&\le(21-14)(21-9) \\ &\qquad\cdot(21-7)(21-12) \\ &=7\cdot12\cdot14\cdot9, \\ K&\le42\sqrt6. \end{aligned} \]

Therefore \(r\le \frac{K}{21}\le2\sqrt6.\) Equality is attainable: the cyclic quadrilateral with these sides is also tangential because \(14+7=9+12,\) and its incircle has radius \(\frac{K}{21}=2\sqrt6.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

25 答案 D

令 \(B=\angle CBA\)，并令 \(C=\angle BCA=120^\circ-B\)。因为 \(AC\ge AB\)，所以 \(B\ge C\)，从而 \(60^\circ\le B\le90^\circ\)。标准角度公式给出 \[ \begin{aligned} \angle BOC&=2\angle A=120^\circ, \\ \angle BHC&=180^\circ-\angle A=120^\circ, \\ \angle BIC&=90^\circ+\dfrac{\angle A}{2}=120^\circ \end{aligned}\text{。}\]因此 \(B,C,O,I,H\) 位于同一个圆上。

此外，\(BC=1\) 且 \(A=60^\circ\) 确定外接圆半径 \(OB=OC=\frac{1}{\sqrt3}\)，所以 \(\triangle BCO\) 以及经过 \(B,C,O\) 的圆都是固定的。在 \(C\) 处追角可得 \[ \begin{aligned} \angle OCI&=30^\circ-\dfrac C2, \\ \angle ICH&=30^\circ-\dfrac C2 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]因此相应的弦满足 \(OI=IH\)。

五边形面积等于固定面积 \([BCO]\) 加上 \([BOIH]\)。当两个点分割固定弧 \(OB\) 时，圆内接四边形在三段连续小弧相等时面积最大（等价地，使它们的正弦和最大）。因此在最大值处有 \(OI=IH=HB\)。

在 \(\triangle BOC\) 中，\(\angle OCB=30^\circ\)。等弦使 \(\angle OCI=\angle ICH\)，且 \(\angle ICH=\angle HCB\)，所以这些角都等于 \(10^\circ\)。因此 \(30^\circ-\tfrac C2=10^\circ\)，得 \(C=40^\circ\) 和 \(B=80^\circ\)。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

Write \(B=\angle CBA\) and \(C=\angle BCA=120^\circ-B.\) Since \(AC\ge AB,\) we have \(B\ge C,\) so \(60^\circ\le B\le90^\circ.\) The standard angle formulas give \[ \begin{aligned} \angle BOC&=2\angle A=120^\circ, \\ \angle BHC&=180^\circ-\angle A=120^\circ, \\ \angle BIC&=90^\circ+\dfrac{\angle A}{2}=120^\circ. \end{aligned} \] Hence \(B,C,O,I,H\) lie on one circle.

Also \(BC=1\) and \(A=60^\circ\) fix the circumradius \(OB=OC=\frac{1}{\sqrt3},\) so \(\triangle BCO\) and the circle through \(B,C,O\) are fixed. Angle chasing at \(C\) gives \[ \begin{aligned} \angle OCI&=30^\circ-\dfrac C2, \\ \angle ICH&=30^\circ-\dfrac C2. \end{aligned} \] Thus the corresponding chords satisfy \(OI=IH.\)

The pentagon’s area is the fixed area \([BCO]\) plus \([BOIH].\) For two points dividing a fixed arc \(OB,\) an inscribed quadrilateral has greatest area when its three consecutive subarcs are equal (equivalently, maximize the sum of their sines). Hence at the maximum \(OI=IH=HB.\)

In \(\triangle BOC,\) \(\angle OCB=30^\circ.\) Equal chords make \(\angle OCI=\angle ICH,\) \(\angle ICH=\angle HCB,\) and each of these angles is \(10^\circ.\) Therefore \(30^\circ-\tfrac C2=10^\circ,\) so \(C=40^\circ\) and \(B=80^\circ.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.
