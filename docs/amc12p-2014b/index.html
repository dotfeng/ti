1

Leah 有 \(13\) 枚硬币，全部是一美分硬币和五美分硬币。如果她比现在多一枚五美分硬币，那么她的一美分硬币和五美分硬币数量就相同。Leah 的硬币一共值多少美分？

A\(33\) B\(35\) C\(37\) D\(39\) E\(41\)

English

Leah has \(13\) coins, all of which are pennies and nickels. If she had one more nickel than she has now, then she would have the same number of pennies and nickels. In cents, how much are Leah’s coins worth?

(A) \(33\)(B) \(35\)(C) \(37\)(D) \(39\)(E) \(41\)

2

Orvin 去商店时带的钱刚好够买 \(30\) 个气球。到达后他发现商店正在特价：按原价买 \(1\) 个气球，第二个气球可减免原价的 \(\tfrac13\)。Orvin 最多能买多少个气球？

A\(33\) B\(34\) C\(36\) D\(38\) E\(39\)

English

Orvin went to the store with just enough money to buy \(30\) balloons. When he arrived he discovered that the store had a special sale on balloons: buy \(1\) balloon at the regular price and get a second at \(\tfrac13\) off the regular price. What is the greatest number of balloons Orvin could buy?

(A) \(33\)(B) \(34\)(C) \(36\)(D) \(38\)(E) \(39\)

3

Randy 旅程的前三分之一在碎石路上行驶，接着在铺装路上行驶 \(20\) 英里，剩下的五分之一在土路上行驶。Randy 的旅程总长多少英里？

A\(30\) B\(\dfrac{400}{11}\) C\(\dfrac{75}{2}\) D\(40\) E\(\dfrac{300}{7}\)

English

Randy drove the first third of his trip on a gravel road, the next \(20\) miles on pavement, and the remaining one-fifth on a dirt road. In miles, how long was Randy’s trip?

(A) \(30\)(B) \(\dfrac{400}{11}\)(C) \(\dfrac{75}{2}\)(D) \(40\)(E) \(\dfrac{300}{7}\)

4

When they finish their lemonades they realize that they both drank the same amount. How many ounces of lemonade did they drink together?

(A) \(30\)(B) \(32\)(C) \(36\)(D) \(40\)(E) \(50\)

1 答案 C

设 \(n\) 为五美分硬币的数量，则 Leah 有 \(13-n\) 枚一美分硬币。多一枚五美分硬币后，她会有 \(n+1\) 枚五美分硬币，这等于一美分硬币的数量：\[ n+1 = 13-n\text{。} \] 解得 \(n=6\)，所以她有 \(6\) 枚五美分硬币和 \(7\) 枚一美分硬币。

总价值为 \(6\cdot5 + 7 = 37\) 美分。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Let \(n\) be the number of nickels, so Leah has \(13-n\) pennies. One more nickel would give her \(n+1\) nickels, and this equals the number of pennies: \[ n+1 = 13-n. \] Solving gives \(n=6,\) so there are \(6\) nickels and \(7\) pennies.

The total value is \(6\cdot5 + 7 = 37\) cents.

Thus, the correct answer is C.

2 答案 C

在特价下，一组两个气球的费用是一个原价气球的 \(1 + \tfrac23 = \tfrac53\) 倍。

Orvin 的钱按原价可以买 \(30\) 个气球，而按特价可以买 \[ 30 \div \tfrac53 = 18 \] 组，也就是 \(36\) 个气球。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Under the sale, a pair of balloons costs \(1 + \tfrac23 = \tfrac53\) times the regular price of one balloon.

Orvin’s money buys \(30\) balloons at the regular price, so he can afford \[ 30 \div \tfrac53 = 18 \] pairs, which is \(36\) balloons.

Thus, the correct answer is C.

3 答案 E

铺装路占全程的比例为 \[ 1 - \tfrac13 - \tfrac15 = \tfrac{7}{15}\text{。} \]

因为这部分等于 \(20\) 英里，所以全程为 \[ 20 \div \tfrac{7}{15} = \tfrac{300}{7} \] 英里。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The fraction of the trip on pavement is \[ 1 - \tfrac13 - \tfrac15 = \tfrac{7}{15}. \]

Since this equals \(20\) miles, the whole trip is \[ 20 \div \tfrac{7}{15} = \tfrac{300}{7} \] miles.

Thus, the correct answer is E.

4 答案 B

设一个松饼的价格为 \(m\)，一根香蕉的价格为 \(b\)，则 \[ 2(4m+3b) = 2m+16b\text{。}\]

展开得 \(8m+6b = 2m+16b\)，所以 \(6m = 10b\)，即 \(m = \tfrac53 b\)。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Let a muffin cost \(m\) and a banana cost \(b.\) Then \[ 2(4m+3b) = 2m+16b. \]

Expanding gives \(8m+6b = 2m+16b,\) so \(6m = 10b\) and \(m = \tfrac53 b.\)

Thus, the correct answer is B.

5 答案 A

设每块玻璃片宽 \(2x\)，高 \(5x\)。窗户横向有 \(4\) 块玻璃片和 \(5\) 条竖向边框，所以宽为 \(4(2x) + 5\cdot2 = 8x+10\)。

窗户纵向有 \(2\) 块玻璃片和 \(3\) 条横向边框，所以高为 \(2(5x) + 3\cdot2 = 10x+6\)。

令宽等于高，得到 \(8x+10 = 10x+6\)，所以 \(x=2\)，边长为 \(10\cdot2+6 = 26\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

Let each pane have width \(2x\) and height \(5x.\) The window is \(4\) panes wide with \(5\) vertical borders, so its width is \(4(2x) + 5\cdot2 = 8x+10.\)

It is \(2\) panes tall with \(3\) horizontal borders, so its height is \(2(5x) + 3\cdot2 = 10x+6.\)

Setting width equal to height gives \(8x+10 = 10x+6,\) so \(x=2\) and the side length is \(10\cdot2+6 = 26.\)

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Working modulo \(2017,\) the identity \(\binom{2014}{k} \cdot k! \cdot (2014-k)! = 2014!\) together with \(2016 \cdot 2015 \cdots (2015-k)\) \(\equiv (-1)^k (k+2)!\) leads to \[ \begin{gathered} 2\binom{2014}{k} \equiv (-1)^k \\ {}\cdot (k+2)(k+1) \pmod{2017}, \end{gathered} \] so \(\binom{2014}{k} \equiv (-1)^k \binom{k+2}{2}.\)

Then \[ \begin{gathered} S \equiv \sum_{k=0}^{62} (-1)^k \binom{k+2}{2} \\ = 1 \\ {}+ \sum_{k=1}^{31}\left[\binom{2k+2}{2} - \binom{2k+1}{2}\right] \\ = 1 + \sum_{k=1}^{31}(2k+1). \end{gathered} \]

The remaining sum is \(3 + 5 + \cdots + 63 = 1023,\) so \(S \equiv 1 + 1023\) \(= 1024 \pmod{2017}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

24 答案 D

因为弧 \(AB, CD\) 相等，弧 \(BC, DE\) 相等，所以弦 \(AC, BD, CE\) 都相等；设 \(x = AC = BD = CE\)， \(y = AD\)， 且 \(z = BE\)。

对 \(ABCD\)、\(BCDE\) 和 \(ABDE\) 使用托勒密定理，得到 \[ \begin{gathered} 10y + 9 = x^2, \\ \quad 100 + 3z = x^2, \\ \quad 30 + 14x = yz\text{。} \end{gathered} \] 由前两个方程解出 \(y\) 和 \(z\)，再代入第三个方程，得到 \[ \begin{gathered} x^3 - 109x - 420 = 0 \\ = (x-12)(x+5)(x+7)\text{。} \end{gathered} \]

所以 \(x = 12\)， \(y = \tfrac{135}{10} = \tfrac{27}{2}\)， 且 \(z = \tfrac{44}{3}\)。 五条对角线为 \(AC, BD, CE, AD, BE\)， 它们的和为 \[ \begin{gathered} 3x + y + z = 36 \\ {}+ \tfrac{27}{2} + \tfrac{44}{3} \\ = \tfrac{385}{6}\text{。} \end{gathered} \]

因此 \(m + n = 385 + 6 = 391\)， 正确答案是 D。

English

Because arcs \(AB, CD\) are equal and arcs \(BC, DE\) are equal, the chords \(AC, BD, CE\) are all equal; let \(x = AC = BD = CE,\) \(y = AD,\) and \(z = BE.\)

Ptolemy’s theorem on \(ABCD,\) \(BCDE,\) and \(ABDE\) gives \[ \begin{gathered} 10y + 9 = x^2, \\ \quad 100 + 3z = x^2, \\ \quad 30 + 14x = yz. \end{gathered} \] Solving the first two for \(y\) and \(z\) and substituting into the third yields \[ \begin{gathered} x^3 - 109x - 420 = 0 \\ = (x-12)(x+5)(x+7). \end{gathered} \]

So \(x = 12,\) \(y = \tfrac{135}{10} = \tfrac{27}{2},\) and \(z = \tfrac{44}{3}.\) The five diagonals are \(AC, BD, CE, AD, BE,\) summing to \[ \begin{gathered} 3x + y + z = 36 \\ {}+ \tfrac{27}{2} + \tfrac{44}{3} \\ = \tfrac{385}{6}. \end{gathered} \]

Thus \(m + n = 385 + 6 = 391,\) and the correct answer is D.

25 答案 D

令 \(x = \tfrac{\pi y}{2}\)。两边除以 \(2\)，并使用 \(\tfrac12(1 - \cos(2\pi y)) = \sin^2(\pi y)\)，方程化简为 \[ \cos(\pi y)\cos\left(\dfrac{4028\pi}{y}\right) = 1\text{。}\]

两个余弦必须都等于 \(1\)，或都等于 \(-1\)，因此 \(y\) 和 \(\tfrac{4028}{y}\) 是同奇偶性的整数。因为 \(4028 = 2^2 \cdot 19 \cdot 53\) 是偶数，二者都必须为偶数，所以 \(y = 2a\)，其中 \(a\) 是 \(2014 = 2 \cdot 19 \cdot 53\) 的正奇因数，故 \(a \in \{1, 19, 53, 19 \cdot 53\}\)。

每个这样的 \(a\) 给出 \(x = \tfrac{\pi y}{2} = \pi a\)，所以解的和为 \[ \begin{gathered} \pi(1 + 19 + 53 + 19\cdot53) \\ = \pi(19+1)(53+1) \\ = 1080\pi \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Let \(x = \tfrac{\pi y}{2}.\) Dividing by \(2\) and using \(\tfrac12(1 - \cos(2\pi y)) = \sin^2(\pi y),\) the equation simplifies to \[ \cos(\pi y)\cos\left(\dfrac{4028\pi}{y}\right) = 1. \]

Both cosines must equal \(1\) or both equal \(-1,\) so \(y\) and \(\tfrac{4028}{y}\) are integers of the same parity. Since \(4028 = 2^2 \cdot 19 \cdot 53\) is even, both must be even, so \(y = 2a\) with \(a\) a positive odd divisor of \(2014 = 2 \cdot 19 \cdot 53,\) giving \(a \in \{1, 19, 53, 19 \cdot 53\}.\)

Each such \(a\) gives \(x = \tfrac{\pi y}{2} = \pi a,\) so the sum of solutions is \[ \begin{gathered} \pi(1 + 19 + 53 + 19\cdot53) \\ = \pi(19+1)(53+1) \\ = 1080\pi. \end{gathered} \]

Thus, the correct answer is D.
