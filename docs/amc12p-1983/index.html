逐题解析

1 答案 E

第二个方程给出 \(x=16y\)。代入第一个方程，得到 \(8y=y^2\)，所以 \(y(y-8)=0\)。条件 \(x\ne0\) 排除 \(y=0\)，于是 \(y=8\)，且 \(x=128\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The second equation gives \(x=16y.\) Substituting into the first gives \(8y=y^2,\) so \(y(y-8)=0.\) The condition \(x\ne0\) rules out \(y=0,\) leaving \(y=8\) and \(x=128.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is E.

2 答案 B

与 \(P\) 相距 \(3\) 厘米的点构成一个以 \(P\) 为圆心的圆。两个不同的圆至多相交于两点，而且确实可能有两个交点。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The points \(3\) cm from \(P\) form a circle centered at \(P.\) Two distinct circles intersect in at most two points, and two intersections are possible.

Therefore, the correct answer is B.

3 答案 A

若 \(p\) 和 \(q\) 都是奇数，则 \(r=p+q\) 是大于 \(2\) 的偶数，因而不是素数。所以其中一个加数是唯一的偶素数 \(2\)。由于 \(p\lt q\)，这个加数就是 \(p\)。

所以正确答案是 A。

English

If both \(p\) and \(q\) were odd, then \(r=p+q\) would be even and greater than \(2,\) so it would not be prime. Thus one addend is the only even prime, \(2.\) Since \(p\lt q,\) that addend is \(p.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is A.
