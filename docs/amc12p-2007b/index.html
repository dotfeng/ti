1

Isabella 的房子有 \(3\) 间卧室。每间卧室长 \(12\) 英尺、宽 \(10\) 英尺、高 \(8\) 英尺。Isabella 需要粉刷所有卧室的墙壁。每间卧室中门和窗占 \(60\) 平方英尺，这些部分不粉刷。需要粉刷多少平方英尺的墙壁？

A\(678\) B\(768\) C\(786\) D\(867\) E\(876\)

English

Isabella’s house has \(3\) bedrooms. Each bedroom is \(12\) feet long, \(10\) feet wide, and \(8\) feet high. Isabella must paint the walls of all the bedrooms. Doorways and windows, which will not be painted, occupy \(60\) square feet in each bedroom. How many square feet of walls must be painted?

(A) \(678\)(B) \(768\)(C) \(786\)(D) \(867\)(E) \(876\)

2

一名大学生开小型车回家过周末，路程 \(120\) 英里，平均每加仑行驶 \(30\) 英里。返程时他开父母的 SUV，平均每加仑只能行驶 \(20\) 英里。往返全程平均每加仑行驶多少英里？

A\(22\) B\(24\) C\(25\) D\(26\) E\(28\)

English

A college student drove his compact car \(120\) miles home for the weekend and averaged \(30\) miles per gallon. On the return trip the student drove his parents’ SUV and averaged only \(20\) miles per gallon. What was the average gas mileage, in miles per gallon, for the round trip?

(A) \(22\)(B) \(24\)(C) \(25\)(D) \(26\)(E) \(28\)

3

English

The point \(O\) is the center of the circle circumscribed about \(\triangle ABC,\) with \(\angle BOC=120^\circ\) and \(\angle AOB=140^\circ,\) as shown. What is the degree measure of \(\angle ABC?\)

(A) \(35\)(B) \(40\)(C) \(45\)(D) \(50\)(E) \(60\)

4

A\(6\) B\(8\) C\(9\) D\(12\) E\(18\)

English

(A) \(6\)(B) \(8\)(C) \(9\)(D) \(12\)(E) \(18\)

5

After looking over the \(25\) problems, Sarah has decided to attempt the first \(22\) and leave the last \(3\) unanswered. How many of the first \(22\) problems must she solve correctly in order to score at least \(100\) points?

(A) \(13\)(B) \(14\)(C) \(15\)(D) \(16\)(E) \(17\)

逐题解析

1 答案 E

每间卧室地面周长为 \(2(12+10)=44\) 英尺。

所以一间卧室的墙面粉刷面积为 \(44\cdot8-60=352-60=292\) 平方英尺。三间卧室共需粉刷 \(3\cdot292=876\) 平方英尺。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

The perimeter of each bedroom floor is \(2(12+10)=44\) feet.

So the wall area in one bedroom is \(44\cdot8-60=352-60=292\) square feet. Across all three bedrooms, Isabella paints \(3\cdot292=876\) square feet.

Thus, the correct answer is E.

2 答案 B

去程用 \(\frac{120}{30}=4\) 加仑，返程用 \(\frac{120}{20}=6\) 加仑。

往返共 \(240\) 英里，用油 \(10\) 加仑，所以平均每加仑行驶的英里数为 \[ \dfrac{240}{10}=24\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 B。

English

The trip home uses \(\frac{120}{30}=4\) gallons, and the return trip uses \(\frac{120}{20}=6\) gallons.

The round trip covers \(240\) miles on \(10\) gallons, so the average is \[ \dfrac{240}{10}=24. \]

Thus, the correct answer is B.

3 答案 D

点 \(O\) 周围角和为 \(360^\circ\)，所以 \[ \begin{aligned} \angle AOC&=360^\circ-140^\circ-120^\circ \\ &=100^\circ \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

由圆周角定理，\(\angle ABC\) 与圆心角 \(\angle AOC\) 截同一弧 \(AC\)，所以 \[ \angle ABC=\tfrac12\angle AOC=50^\circ\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The angles around \(O\) sum to \(360^\circ,\) so \[ \begin{aligned} \angle AOC&=360^\circ-140^\circ-120^\circ \\ &=100^\circ. \end{aligned} \]

By the inscribed angle theorem, \(\angle ABC\) subtends the same arc \(AC\) as the central angle \(\angle AOC,\) so \[ \angle ABC=\tfrac12\angle AOC=50^\circ. \]

Thus, the correct answer is D.

4 答案 B

因为 \(3\) 根香蕉价格等于 \(2\) 个苹果，\(18\) 根香蕉价格等于 \(12\) 个苹果。

又因为 \(6\) 个苹果价格等于 \(4\) 个橙子，\(12\) 个苹果价格等于 \(8\) 个橙子，所以 \(18\) 根香蕉等价于 \(8\) 个橙子。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Since \(3\) bananas cost as much as \(2\) apples, \(18\) bananas cost as much as \(12\) apples.

Since \(6\) apples cost as much as \(4\) oranges, \(12\) apples cost as much as \(8\) oranges. Therefore \(18\) bananas cost as much as \(8\) oranges.

Thus, the correct answer is B.

5 答案 D

三道空题给 \(3\cdot1.5=4.5\) 分。

所以 Sarah 需要从答对题中得到至少 \(100-4.5=95.5\) 分。因为 \[ 15\lt\dfrac{95.5}{6}\lt16\text{，} \] 她至少要答对 \(16\) 题，此时得分为 \(16\cdot6+4.5=100.5\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The three unanswered problems give \(3\cdot1.5=4.5\) points.

So Sarah needs at least \(100-4.5=95.5\) points from correct answers. Since \[ 15\lt\dfrac{95.5}{6}\lt16, \] she must solve at least \(16\) problems correctly, which would give her \(16\cdot6+4.5=100.5\) points.

Thus, the correct answer is D.

6 答案 D

周长为 \(5+6+7=18\)，因此每只虫子在相遇前走了 \(9\) 个长度单位。

沿 \(A\to B\to C\) 方向的虫子到达 \(D\) 时走了 \(AB+BD=9\)，该点在 \(BC\) 上，且 \(AB=5\)，所以 \(BD=4\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The perimeter is \(5+6+7=18,\) so each bug crawls \(9\) before they meet.

The bug going \(A\to B\to C\) reaches \(D\) on side \(BC,\) having traveled \(AB+BD=9.\) Since \(AB=5,\) we get \(BD=4.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

7 答案 E

因为 \(AB=BC=EA\) 且 \(\angle A=\angle B=90^\circ\)，四边形 \(ABCE\) 是正方形，所以 \(\angle AEC=90^\circ\)，且 \(EC\) 等于公共边长。

又 \(CD=DE=EC\)，所以 \(\triangle CDE\) 是等边三角形，\(\angle CED=60^\circ\)。因此 \[ \begin{aligned} \angle E&=\angle AEC+\angle CED \\ &=90^\circ+60^\circ \\ &=150^\circ \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Because \(AB=BC=EA\) and \(\angle A=\angle B=90^\circ,\) quadrilateral \(ABCE\) is a square, so \(\angle AEC=90^\circ\) and \(EC\) equals the common side length.

Then \(CD=DE=EC,\) so \(\triangle CDE\) is equilateral and \(\angle CED=60^\circ.\) Therefore \[ \begin{aligned} \angle E&=\angle AEC+\angle CED \\ &=90^\circ+60^\circ \\ &=150^\circ. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, the correct answer is E.

15 答案 E

奇次幂项为 \(ar+ar^3+\cdots\) \(=r(a+ar^2+\cdots)\)，即偶次幂项之和的 \(r\) 倍。偶次幂项之和为 \(7-3=4\)。

Thus, the correct answer is E.

16 答案 A

四面体旋转群有 \(12\) 个元素：恒等、\(8\) 个绕顶点-对面轴的 \(3\) 阶旋转，以及 \(3\) 个绕对边中点轴的 \(2\) 阶旋转。

恒等固定全部 \(3^4=81\) 种涂色。每个顶点旋转固定一个面并轮换另外三个面，所以固定 \(3^2=9\) 种涂色；同样地，每个边旋转交换两对面，也固定 \(3^2=9\) 种。

由 Burnside 引理，可区分涂色数为 \[ \dfrac{81+8\cdot9+3\cdot9}{12}=\dfrac{180}{12}=15\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The rotation group of the tetrahedron has \(12\) elements: the identity, \(8\) rotations of order \(3\) about a vertex-face axis, and \(3\) rotations of order \(2\) about an edge-midpoint axis.

The identity fixes all \(3^4=81\) colorings. Each vertex rotation fixes one face and cycles the other three, so it fixes \(3^2=9\) colorings; likewise each edge rotation swaps two pairs of faces and fixes \(3^2=9.\)

By Burnside’s lemma the number of distinguishable colorings is \[ \dfrac{81+8\cdot9+3\cdot9}{12}=\dfrac{180}{12}=15. \]

Thus, the correct answer is A.

17 答案 D

对所有 \(b\gt0\) 都有 \(b\lt10^b\)，所以 \(\log_{10}b\lt b\)。若 \(b=1\)，则 \(a=0\)，不符合条件。若 \(b\gt1\)，则 \(0\lt\dfrac{\log_{10}b}{b^2}\lt1\)，因此 \(a\) 不可能是非零整数。

所以 \(0\lt b\lt1\)，从而 \(\log_{10}b\lt0\)，且 \(a=\dfrac{\log_{10}b}{b^2}\lt0\)。排序为 \(a\lt0\lt b\lt1\lt\dfrac1b\)，所以中位数是 \(b\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

For \(b\gt0,\) we have \(b\lt10^b,\) hence \(\log_{10}b\lt b.\) If \(b=1,\) then \(a=0.\) If \(b\gt1,\) then \(0\lt\dfrac{\log_{10}b}{b^2}\lt1,\) so \(a\) could not be an integer.

Hence \(0\lt b\lt1,\) so \(\log_{10}b\lt0\) and \(a=\dfrac{\log_{10}b}{b^2}\lt0.\) Thus \(a\lt0\lt b\lt1\lt\dfrac1b,\) and the middle value of the sorted set is \(b.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

18 答案 C

设较小平方数为 \(N^2\)，较大平方数为 \((N+1)^2\)，两者间隔为 \(2N+1\)。则 \[ \overline{abc}=N^2+\dfrac{2N+1}{3}\text{，}\]\[ \overline{acb}=N^2+\dfrac{2(2N+1)}{3}\text{。}\]

两式相减，\(\overline{acb}-\overline{abc}=9(c-b)\) \(=\dfrac{2N+1}{3}\)，所以 \(27(c-b)=2N+1\)。因为 \(\overline{acb}\) 在区间中位置更靠后，所以 \(c-b\) 为正；又因为右边是奇数，所以 \(c-b\) 是奇数。若 \(c-b\ge3\)，则 \(N\ge40\)，且 \(N^2\) 不再是三位数。

因此 \(c-b=1\)，得到 \(N=13\)。从 \(13^2=169\) 到 \(14^2=196\) 的三分之一与三分之二处分别为 \(178\) 和 \(187\)，所以 \(a+b+c=1+7+8=16\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Let the smaller square be \(N^2,\) so the larger is \((N+1)^2\) and the gap is \(2N+1.\) Then \[ \overline{abc}=N^2+\dfrac{2N+1}{3}, \] \[ \overline{acb}=N^2+\dfrac{2(2N+1)}{3}. \]

Subtracting, \(\overline{acb}-\overline{abc}=9(c-b)\) \(=\dfrac{2N+1}{3},\) so \(27(c-b)=2N+1.\) Since \(\overline{acb}\) is farther along the interval, \(c-b\) is positive; and because the right side is odd, \(c-b\) is odd. If \(c-b\ge3,\) then \(N\ge40\) and \(N^2\) is not three digits.

So \(c-b=1,\) giving \(N=13.\) The points one third and two thirds of the way from \(13^2=169\) to \(14^2=196\) are \(178\) and \(187,\) so \(a+b+c=1+7+8=16.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

20 答案 D

第一个平行四边形有两个顶点在 \((0,c)\) 和 \((0,d)\)，另外两个顶点的 \(x\)-坐标为 \(\pm\dfrac{c-d}{b-a}\)。因此第一个平行四边形面积为 \(\dfrac{(c-d)^2}{|b-a|}=18\)，第二个为 \(\dfrac{(c+d)^2}{|b-a|}=72\)。

于是 \((c-d)^2=18|b-a|\)，\((c+d)^2=72|b-a|\)。两式相减得 \(4cd=54|b-a|\)，即 \(2cd=27|b-a|\)。

因此 \(|b-a|\) 是偶数，取 \(\{a,b\}=\{1,3\}\) 时 \(a+b\) 最小；又 \(cd\) 是 \(27\) 的倍数，取 \(\{c,d\}=\{3,9\}\) 时 \(c+d\) 最小。这些取值满足全部条件，于是 \(a+b+c+d=1+3+3+9\) \(=16\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Two vertices of the first parallelogram lie at \((0,c)\) and \((0,d),\) and the other two have \(x\)-coordinates \(\pm\dfrac{c-d}{b-a}.\) Its area works out to \(\dfrac{(c-d)^2}{|b-a|}=18.\) The same computation for the second gives \(\dfrac{(c+d)^2}{|b-a|}=72.\)

So \((c-d)^2=18|b-a|\) and \((c+d)^2=72|b-a|.\) Subtracting, \(4cd=54|b-a|,\) i.e. \(2cd=27|b-a|.\)

Thus \(|b-a|\) is even, so \(a+b\) is smallest with \(\{a,b\}=\{1,3\};\) and \(cd\) is a multiple of \(27,\) so \(c+d\) is smallest with \(\{c,d\}=\{3,9\}.\) These satisfy all conditions, giving \(a+b+c+d=1+3+3+9\) \(=16.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

22 答案 A

设 \(D,E,F\) 分别为 \(BC,CA,AB\) 的中点，设 \(X,Y,Z\) 分别为 \(AD,BE,CF\) 的中点。追踪一个始终位于两个粒子正中间的第三点：当两个粒子位于 \(A,D\) 时，它位于 \(X\)；当它们位于 \(F,C\) 时，它位于 \(Z\)；当它们位于 \(B,E\) 时，它位于 \(Y\)。

在这些时刻之间，两个粒子的位置都线性变化，所以它们的中点依次描出线段 \(XZ,ZY,YX\)。因此围成的轨迹是等边三角形 \(XYZ\)，由对称性，它与 \(\triangle ABC\) 有同一个中心 \(O\)。因为 \(Z\) 是中线 \(CF\) 的中点，\[ \begin{aligned} OZ&=OC-ZC \\ &=\dfrac23 CF-\dfrac12 CF \\ &=\dfrac16 CF \end{aligned}\text{，}\]而 \(OC=\dfrac23 CF\)。

所以外接圆半径之比为 \(\dfrac{OZ}{OC}=\dfrac14\)，面积之比为 \(\left(\dfrac14\right)^2=\dfrac{1}{16}\)。

English

Let \(D,E,F\) be the midpoints of \(BC,CA,AB,\) respectively, and let \(X,Y,Z\) be the midpoints of \(AD,BE,CF,\) respectively. Track a third point halfway between the two particles. It is at \(X\) when the particles are at \(A,D;\) at \(Z\) when they are at \(F,C;\) and at \(Y\) when they are at \(B,E.\)

Between these instants both particle positions vary linearly, so their midpoint traces the segments \(XZ,ZY,YX.\) Thus the enclosed path is the equilateral triangle \(XYZ,\) which by symmetry shares the center \(O\) of \(\triangle ABC.\) Because \(Z\) is the midpoint of the median \(CF,\) \[ \begin{aligned} OZ&=OC-ZC \\ &=\dfrac23 CF-\dfrac12 CF \\ &=\dfrac16 CF, \end{aligned} \] while \(OC=\dfrac23 CF.\)

So the ratio of circumradii is \(\dfrac{OZ}{OC}=\dfrac14,\) and the area ratio is \(\left(\dfrac14\right)^2=\dfrac{1}{16}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

24 答案 A

设表达式等于整数 \(k\)。乘以 \(b\) 并减去 \(a\)，得 \(\dfrac{14b^2}{9a}=bk-a\) 为整数。由于 \(\gcd(a,b)=1\)，可得 \(14\) 是 \(a\) 的倍数。另一方面，乘以 \(9a\) 并减去 \(14b\)，得 \(\dfrac{9a^2}{b}=9ak-14b\)，所以 \(9\) 是 \(b\) 的倍数。

25 答案 C

取 \(D=(-1,0,0)\)、\(E=(1,0,0)\)，令 \(\triangle ABC\) 在平面 \(z=k\gt0\) 中。由于 \(\angle CDE\) 和 \(\angle DEA\) 为直角，\(A\) 和 \(C\) 分别位于以 \(E\) 和 \(D\) 为圆心、半径为 \(2\) 的圆上；这两个圆所在的平面分别为 \(x=1\) 和 \(x=-1\)。因此 \(A=(1,y_1,k),\ C=(-1,y_2,k)\)，其中 \(y_j=\pm\sqrt{4-k^2}\)。

因为 \(\angle ABC=90^\circ\)，所以 \(AC=2\sqrt2\)，这迫使 \(y_1=-y_2\)。取 \(y_1=1\)，\(y_2=-1\)，得 \(k=\sqrt3\)，于是 \(A=(1,1,\sqrt3)\)，\(C=(-1,-1,\sqrt3)\)，而 \(B\) 为 \((1,-1,\sqrt3)\) 或 \((-1,1,\sqrt3)\)。

第一种情况下 \(BE=2\) 且 \(BE\perp DE\)；第二种情况下 \(BD=2\) 且 \(BD\perp DE\)。无论哪种，\(\triangle BDE\) 的两条垂直边长度分别为 \(2\) 和 \(2\)，面积为 \(\tfrac12(2)(2)=2\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Set \(D=(-1,0,0)\) and \(E=(1,0,0),\) and let \(\triangle ABC\) lie in the plane \(z=k\gt0.\) Because \(\angle CDE\) and \(\angle DEA\) are right angles, \(A\) and \(C\) lie on radius-\(2\) circles centered at \(E\) and \(D\) in the planes \(x=1\) and \(x=-1,\) so \(A=(1,y_1,k),\ C=(-1,y_2,k)\) with \(y_j=\pm\sqrt{4-k^2}.\)

Since \(\angle ABC=90^\circ,\) \(AC=2\sqrt2,\) which forces \(y_1=-y_2.\) Taking \(y_1=1,\) \(y_2=-1,\) gives \(k=\sqrt3,\) so \(A=(1,1,\sqrt3),\) \(C=(-1,-1,\sqrt3),\) and \(B\) is \((1,-1,\sqrt3)\) or \((-1,1,\sqrt3).\)

In the first case \(BE=2\) with \(BE\perp DE;\) in the second \(BD=2\) with \(BD\perp DE.\) Either way \(\triangle BDE\) has legs \(2\) and \(2,\) so its area is \(\tfrac12(2)(2)=2.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.
