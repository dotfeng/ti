• 所有开心的蛇都会加法，

• 没有紫色的蛇会减法，并且

• 所有不会减法的蛇也不会加法。

关于 Tom 的蛇，可以推出下列哪个结论？

A紫色的蛇会加法。 B紫色的蛇是开心的。 C会加法的蛇是紫色的。 D开心的蛇不是紫色的。 E开心的蛇不会减法。

English

Tom has a collection of \(13\) snakes, \(4\) of which are purple and \(5\) of which are happy. He observes that

• all of his happy snakes can add,

• none of his purple snakes can subtract, and

• all of his snakes that can’t subtract also can’t add.

Which of these conclusions can be drawn about Tom’s snakes?

(A) Purple snakes can add.(B) Purple snakes are happy.(C) Snakes that can add are purple.(D) Happy snakes are not purple.(E) Happy snakes can’t subtract.

所以 \(PQ=16\sqrt3\)，\(YZ=36\)。为了说明这个周长关系，把六边形依次的边长记为 \(a,b,c,d,e,f\)。两条交替延长线截出的三角形的边长分别为 \(b+c+d\) 和 \(c+d+e\)，而六边形封闭又给出 \(a+f=c+d\)。因此它们的边长之和为 \[ \begin{aligned} &(b+c+d)\\ &\quad+(c+d+e)\\ &=b+e+2(c+d)\\ &=(a+b+c)+(d+e+f) \end{aligned}\text{，}\] 这正是六边形的周长。所以周长为

\[PQ+YZ=16\sqrt3+36\text{。}\]

因此 \(m+n+p=36+16+3=55\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Let the intersections of lines \(AB,CD,EF\) form triangle \(PQR,\) and let the intersections of lines \(BC,DE,FA\) form triangle \(XYZ.\) Because the hexagon is equiangular, all these outer triangles are equilateral.

For an equilateral triangle with side length \(s,\) the area is \(\frac{\sqrt3}{4}s^2.\) Hence

\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{\sqrt3}{4}PQ^2 &=192\sqrt3, \\ \frac{\sqrt3}{4}YZ^2 &=324\sqrt3. \end{aligned} \]

So \(PQ=16\sqrt3\) and \(YZ=36.\) To justify the perimeter relation, write the consecutive hexagon side lengths as \(a,b,c,d,e,f.\) The two alternating-line triangles have side lengths \(b+c+d\) and \(c+d+e,\) while closure of the hexagon gives \(a+f=c+d.\) Hence their side-length sum is \[ \begin{aligned} &(b+c+d)\\ &\quad+(c+d+e)\\ &=b+e+2(c+d)\\ &=(a+b+c)+(d+e+f), \end{aligned} \] the hexagon’s perimeter. Therefore the perimeter is

\[PQ+YZ=16\sqrt3+36.\]

Thus \(m+n+p=36+16+3=55.\)

Thus, C is the correct answer.

22 答案 B

设借走的是第 \(a\) 张到第 \(b\) 张纸，并设 \(s=b-a+1\)。被借走的页码从 \(2a-1\) 到 \(2b\)，所以共有 \(2s\) 页，页码之和为 \(s(2a+2b-1)\)。

所有页码之和为 \(\frac{50\cdot51}{2}=1275\)。若剩余页码的平均数为 \(19\)，则 \[ \begin{aligned} 1275&-s(2a+2b-1)\\ &=19(50-2s),\\ s(2a+2b-39)&=325 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

因为 \(b-a+1\) 是 \(325\) 的正因数，且不超过 \(25\)，所以它只能是 \(1,5,13,25\) 这四种可能。前两种会迫使 \(b>25\)，而 \(25\) 会借走所有纸张。因此唯一有效的可能是

\[ \begin{aligned} 2a+2b-39 &=25, \\ b-a+1 &=13 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以 \(a+b=32\)，且 \(b-a=12\)，得到 \(a=10\)、\(b=22\)。因此借走了 \(13\) 张纸。

所以正确答案是 B。

English

Suppose the borrowed sheets are sheets \(a\) through \(b,\) and let \(s=b-a+1.\) The borrowed pages run from \(2a-1\) through \(2b,\) so there are \(2s\) borrowed pages and their sum is \(s(2a+2b-1).\)

The total sum of all page numbers is \(\frac{50\cdot51}{2}=1275.\) If the remaining pages have mean \(19,\) then \[ \begin{aligned} 1275&-s(2a+2b-1)\\ &=19(50-2s),\\ s(2a+2b-39)&=325. \end{aligned} \]

Because \(b-a+1\) is a positive divisor of \(325\) and is at most \(25,\) its only possibilities are \(1,5,13,25.\) The first two would force \(b>25,\) and \(25\) would remove every sheet. Thus the only valid possibility is

\[ \begin{aligned} 2a+2b-39 &=25, \\ b-a+1 &=13. \end{aligned} \]

Thus \(a+b=32\) and \(b-a=12,\) so \(a=10\) and \(b=22.\) Therefore \(13\) sheets were borrowed.

Thus, B is the correct answer.

23 答案 D

用 \(M\) 表示中心格，\(E\) 表示非角的边格，\(C\) 表示角格。Frieda 从 \(M\) 出发，第一次跳一定到 \(E\)。

从一个边格出发，到 \(C,E,M\) 的概率分别为 \(\frac12,\frac14,\frac14\)。从 \(M\) 出发下一步一定到 \(E\)。

现在列出四次以内首次到达角格的状态模式及其概率。

\[EC:\quad 1\cdot\frac12=\frac12\text{，}\]

\[EEC:\quad 1\cdot\frac14\cdot\frac12=\frac18\text{，}\]

\[EEEC:\quad 1\cdot\frac14\cdot\frac14\cdot\frac12=\frac1{32}\text{，}\]

\[EMEC:\quad 1\cdot\frac14\cdot1\cdot\frac12=\frac18\text{。}\]

把这些概率相加：

\[\frac12+\frac18+\frac1{32}+\frac18=\frac{25}{32}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Classify a square as \(M\) for the center, \(E\) for a non-corner edge square, and \(C\) for a corner. Frieda starts at \(M,\) and the first hop always takes her to an \(E.\)

From an edge square, the probabilities of moving to \(C,E,M\) are \(\frac12,\frac14,\frac14,\) respectively. From \(M,\) the next hop always goes to an \(E.\)

Now count the possible first-hit patterns within four hops:

\[EC:\quad 1\cdot\frac12=\frac12,\]

\[EEC:\quad 1\cdot\frac14\cdot\frac12=\frac18,\]

\[EEEC:\quad 1\cdot\frac14\cdot\frac14\cdot\frac12=\frac1{32},\]

\[EMEC:\quad 1\cdot\frac14\cdot1\cdot\frac12=\frac18.\]

Adding gives

\[\frac12+\frac18+\frac1{32}+\frac18=\frac{25}{32}.\]

Thus, D is the correct answer.

24 答案 D

注意，每个方程都会给出两条平行直线。

\[ (x + ay)^2 = 4a^2 \] 给出两条直线 \[ x + ay - 2a = 0 \] 和 \[ x + ay + 2a = 0\text{。} \] 这两条直线的斜率都是 \(-\dfrac{1}{a}\)。

类似地，\[ (ax-y)^2 = a^2 \] 给出直线 \[ ax - y - a = 0 \] 和 \[ ax - y + a = 0\text{。} \] 这些直线的斜率为 \(a\)。

两组直线互相垂直，因此围成一个矩形。

回忆两条平行线 \[ \begin{cases} Ax+By+C_1=0 \\ Ax+By+C_2=0 \end{cases} \] 之间的距离 \(d\) 为 \[ d = \dfrac{\mid C_2 - C_1 \mid}{\sqrt{A^2 + B^2}}\text{。} \]

用这个公式，第一组平行线之间的距离为 \[ \dfrac{4a}{\sqrt{a^2 + 1}}\text{。} \] 类似地，第二组平行线之间的距离为 \[ \dfrac{2a}{\sqrt{a^2 + 1}}\text{。} \]

这两个距离就是矩形的边长，相乘得面积 \[ \dfrac{8a^2}{a^2 + 1}\text{。} \]

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Note that each of the equations yields two parallel lines.

\[ (x + ay)^2 = 4a^2 \] results in the two lines \[ x + ay - 2a = 0 \] and \[ x + ay + 2a = 0. \] Both of these lines have a slope of \(-\dfrac{1}{a}.\)

Similarly, \[ (ax-y)^2 = a^2 \] results in the lines \[ ax - y - a = 0 \] and \[ ax - y + a = 0. \] These lines have slope \(a.\)

Note that each pair of lines is perpendicular to the other pair of lines. This shows that the equations form a rectangle.

Recall that the formula for the distance \(d\) between two parallel lines \[ \begin{cases} Ax+By+C_1=0 \\ Ax+By+C_2=0 \end{cases} \] is \[ d = \dfrac{\mid C_2 - C_1 \mid}{\sqrt{A^2 + B^2}}. \]

Using this formula, we get that the distance between the first pair of lines is \[ \dfrac{4a}{\sqrt{a^2 + 1}}. \] Similarly, the distance between the second pair of lines is \[ \dfrac{2a}{\sqrt{a^2 + 1}}. \]

These are the side lengths of the rectangle. Multiplying yields the area \[ \dfrac{8a^2}{a^2 + 1}. \]

Thus, D is the correct answer.

25 答案 E

中心格的颜色有 \(3\) 种选法。这种颜色的另外两枚筹码都不能放在边中格，因为这些格子与中心格相邻。因此它们只能占据四个角格中的两个，共有 \(\binom42=6\) 种选法。

无论这两个角格是哪一种情形——处于对角，还是处于同一边的两端——相邻条件都会把剩下两种颜色的位置唯一确定，至多相差二者的互换。因此对中心颜色及其两个角格的每一种选择，都恰有 \(2\) 种补全方式。总数为 \[3\binom42\cdot2=36\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Choose the center color in \(3\) ways. Its other two chips cannot occupy any edge-middle square, because those squares are adjacent to the center. Thus they must occupy two of the four corners, which can be chosen in \(\binom42=6\) ways.

For either possible corner pattern—two opposite corners or two corners on the same side—the adjacency conditions force the remaining two colors up to interchanging them. Hence there are \(2\) completions for each choice of the center color and its two corners. The total is \[3\binom42\cdot2=36.\]

Thus, E is the correct answer.
