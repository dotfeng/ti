1

Andy 和 Betsy 都住在 Mathville。Andy 在 \(1{:}30\) 从 Mathville 骑自行车出发，以稳定的每小时 \(8\) 英里向正北行驶。Betsy 在 \(2{:}30\) 从同一点骑自行车出发，以稳定的每小时 \(12\) 英里向正东行驶。什么时候他们离共同出发点的距离恰好相同？

A\(3{:}30\) B\(3{:}45\) C\(4{:}00\) D\(4{:}15\) E\(4{:}30\)

English

Andy and Betsy both live in Mathville. Andy leaves Mathville on his bicycle at \(1{:}30,\) traveling due north at a steady \(8\) miles per hour. Betsy leaves on her bicycle from the same point at \(2{:}30,\) traveling due east at a steady \(12\) miles per hour. At what time will they be exactly the same distance from their common starting point?

(A) \(3{:}30\)(B) \(3{:}45\)(C) \(4{:}00\)(D) \(4{:}15\)(E) \(4{:}30\)

2

A\(3.5\) B\(4\) C\(4.5\) D\(5\) E\(6\)

English

A box contains \(10\) pounds of a nut mix that is \(50\) percent peanuts, \(20\) percent cashews, and \(30\) percent almonds. A second nut mix containing \(20\) percent peanuts, \(40\) percent cashews, and \(40\) percent almonds is added to the box resulting in a new nut mix that is \(40\) percent peanuts. How many pounds of cashews are now in the box?

(A) \(3.5\)(B) \(4\)(C) \(4.5\)(D) \(5\)(E) \(6\)

3

一队学生将和一队老师进行知识竞赛。学生和老师总共有 \(15\) 人。Ash 是其中一名学生的表亲，他想加入比赛。如果 Ash 加入学生队，该队的平均年龄会从 \(12\) 岁增加到 \(14\) 岁。如果 Ash 加入老师队，该队的平均年龄会从 \(55\) 岁降低到 \(52\) 岁。Ash 多少岁？

A\(28\) B\(29\) C\(30\) D\(32\) E\(33\)

English

A team of students is going to compete against a team of teachers in a trivia contest. The total number of students and teachers is \(15.\) Ash, a cousin of one of the students, wants to join the contest. If Ash plays with the students, the average age on that team will increase from \(12\) to \(14.\) If Ash plays with the teachers, the average age on that team will decrease from \(55\) to \(52.\) How old is Ash?

(A) \(28\)(B) \(29\)(C) \(30\)(D) \(32\)(E) \(33\)

English

In a population where all organisms have \(5\) toes, \(\log v = 4 + 2\log m;\) and in a population where all organisms have \(25\) eyes, \(\log v = 4 + 4\log n,\) where the logarithms are base \(10.\) What is \(k + a + b?\)

(A) \(20\)(B) \(21\)(C) \(22\)(D) \(23\)(E) \(24\)

12

一组数的调和平均数定义为这些数的倒数的算术平均数的倒数。例如，\(4\)，\(4\) 和 \(5\) 的调和平均数为 \[\frac{1}{\frac{1}{3}\left(\frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{5}\right)} = \frac{30}{7}\text{。}\] 求下面这个 \(4050\) 次多项式所有实根的调和平均数：\[ \begin{aligned} &\small \prod_{k=1}^{2025}(kx^2 - 4x - 3) \\ &= (x^2 - 4x - 3) \\ &\quad {}\cdot (2x^2 - 4x - 3) \\ &\quad {}\cdot (3x^2 - 4x - 3)\cdots \\ &\quad (2025x^2 - 4x - 3) \end{aligned}\text{？}\]

A\(-\dfrac{5}{3}\) B\(-\dfrac{3}{2}\) C\(-\dfrac{6}{5}\) D\(-\dfrac{5}{6}\) E\(-\dfrac{2}{3}\)

English

The harmonic mean of a collection of numbers is the reciprocal of the arithmetic mean of the reciprocals of the numbers in the collection. For example, the harmonic mean of \(4,\) \(4,\) and \(5\) is \[\frac{1}{\frac{1}{3}\left(\frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{5}\right)} = \frac{30}{7}.\] What is the harmonic mean of all the real roots of the \(4050\)th degree polynomial \[ \begin{aligned} &\small \prod_{k=1}^{2025}(kx^2 - 4x - 3) \\ &= (x^2 - 4x - 3) \\ &\quad {}\cdot (2x^2 - 4x - 3) \\ &\quad {}\cdot (3x^2 - 4x - 3)\cdots \\ &\quad (2025x^2 - 4x - 3)? \end{aligned} \]

(A) \(-\dfrac{5}{3}\)(B) \(-\dfrac{3}{2}\)(C) \(-\dfrac{6}{5}\)(D) \(-\dfrac{5}{6}\)(E) \(-\dfrac{2}{3}\)

Since \(\cos\dfrac{B}{2} = \sqrt{\dfrac{1 + \frac{7}{18}}{2}}\) \(= \sqrt{\dfrac{25}{36}}\) \(= \dfrac{5}{6},\) we get \(BP = \dfrac{17.5}{\frac{5}{6}} = 21.\)

Thus, the correct answer is D.

The sum of the roots is \(-6i,\) so the centroid is \(-2i.\) Substituting \(z = u - 2i,\) \[ \begin{gathered} (u - i)(u)(u + i) + 10 \\ = u(u^2 + 1) + 10 \\ = u^3 + u + 10. \end{gathered} \]

18 答案 C

如果某个变量为 \(0\)，例如 \(z = 0\)，那么 \(zx = 0 \gt y\) 不可能成立。因此 \(x, y, z \in \{1, \ldots, 8\}\) 是互不相同的正整数。

条件是对称的。对互异的数值 \(a \lt b \lt c\) 有 \(ac \gt b\) 和 \(bc \gt a\) 自动成立，所以唯一真正的限制是 \(ab \gt c\)。只要它成立，所有 \(6\) 种排列都可行。

对每个可能的最小值 \(a\)，满足 \(ab\gt c\) 的数对 \(a\lt b\lt c\le8\) 的个数为 \[ \begin{array}{c|rrrrrr} a&1&2&3&4&5&6\\ \hline \text{个数}&0&11&10&6&3&1 \end{array}\text{。}\] 对固定的 \(a,b\)，这个个数来自在 \(b+1\) 与 \(\min(8,ab-1)\) 之间选取 \(c\)。因此共有 \(31\) 个无序三元组，而且每个三元组的全部 \(6\) 种排序都可行。答案为 \(6\cdot31=186\)。

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

If any variable is \(0,\) say \(z = 0,\) then \(zx = 0 \gt y\) is impossible. So \(x, y, z \in \{1, \ldots, 8\}\) are distinct positive integers.

The conditions are symmetric. For distinct values \(a \lt b \lt c,\) we have \(ac \gt b\) and \(bc \gt a\) automatically, so the only real constraint is \(ab \gt c.\) When it holds, all \(6\) orderings work.

For each possible smallest value \(a,\) the numbers of pairs \(a\lt b\lt c\le8\) satisfying \(ab\gt c\) are \[ \begin{array}{c|rrrrrr} a&1&2&3&4&5&6\\ \hline \text{count}&0&11&10&6&3&1. \end{array} \] For fixed \(a,b,\) this count comes from choosing \(c\) between \(b+1\) and \(\min(8,ab-1).\) Thus there are \(31\) unordered triples, and all \(6\) orderings of each work. The answer is \(6\cdot31=186.\)

Thus, the correct answer is C.

19 答案 E

由韦达定理，\(a + b + c = 0\)， \(ab + bc + ca = k\)， 且 \(abc = -1\)。

将原和分组为 \[ \begin{aligned} &a^2b^2(a + b) + b^2c^2(b + c) \\ &\quad {}+ c^2a^2(c + a)\text{。} \end{aligned} \] 因为 \(a + b + c = 0\)，所以 \(a + b = -c\)、\(b + c = -a\)、\(c + a = -b\)。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

By Vieta’s formulas, \(a + b + c = 0,\) \(ab + bc + ca = k,\) and \(abc = -1.\)

Group the sum as \[ \begin{aligned} &a^2b^2(a + b) + b^2c^2(b + c) \\ &\quad {}+ c^2a^2(c + a). \end{aligned} \] Since \(a + b + c = 0,\) we have \(a + b = -c,\) \(b + c = -a,\) \(c + a = -b.\)

21 答案 A

如果 \(k=0\) 或 \(m=0\)，原比值就是 \(2^a\)，不可能等于 \(964\)。所以 \(k,m\gt0\)。

对等比级数求和，分子为 \(4^a\dfrac{4^{k(m+1)} - 1}{4^k - 1}\)，分母为 \(2^a\dfrac{2^{k(m+1)} - 1}{2^k - 1}\)。利用 \(4^N - 1 = (2^N - 1)(2^N + 1)\)，比值化简为 \[2^a \cdot \frac{2^{k(m+1)} + 1}{2^k + 1} = 964\text{。}\]

左边的分数是奇数除以奇数，所以它的 \(2\) 的指数为 \(0\)。因为 \(964=2^2\cdot241\)，必须有 \(a=2\)，并且 \[ 2^{k(m+1)}+1=241(2^k+1)\text{。}\]将此式模 \(2^k\) 化简，得到 \(240\) 是 \(2^k\) 的倍数，所以 \(k\le4\)。当 \(k=1,2,3\) 时，所需的 \(2\) 的幂将分别为 \(722,1204,2168\)，而它们都不是 \(2\) 的幂。然而当 \(k=4\) 时，\(241(2^4+1)=4097=2^{12}+1\)，所以 \(k(m+1)=12\) 且 \(m=2\)。这也证明了解的唯一性。

于是 \(a + k + m = 2 + 4 + 2 = 8\)。

因此，正确答案是 A。

English

If \(k=0\) or \(m=0,\) the original ratio is just \(2^a,\) which cannot equal \(964.\) Hence \(k,m\gt0.\)

Summing the geometric series, the numerator is \(4^a\dfrac{4^{k(m+1)} - 1}{4^k - 1}\) and the denominator is \(2^a\dfrac{2^{k(m+1)} - 1}{2^k - 1}.\) Using \(4^N - 1 = (2^N - 1)(2^N + 1),\) the ratio simplifies to \[2^a \cdot \frac{2^{k(m+1)} + 1}{2^k + 1} = 964.\]

The fraction on the left is odd over odd, so its power of \(2\) is \(0.\) Since \(964=2^2\cdot241,\) we must have \(a=2,\) and \[ 2^{k(m+1)}+1=241(2^k+1). \] Reducing this equation modulo \(2^k\) shows that \(240\) is divisible by \(2^k,\) so \(k\le4.\) For \(k=1,2,3,\) the required powers of \(2\) would be \(722,1204,2168,\) none of which is a power of \(2.\) For \(k=4,\) however, \(241(2^4+1)=4097=2^{12}+1,\) so \(k(m+1)=12\) and \(m=2.\) This also proves uniqueness.

Then \(a + k + m = 2 + 4 + 2 = 8.\)

Thus, the correct answer is A.

22 答案 E

将数按大小排列为 \(x_1 \gt x_2 \gt x_3\)；顺序统计量在这个区域上的联合密度为 \(6\)。所求事件为 \(x_1 \gt 2x_2\)。

对 \(x_3\) 从 \(0\) 到 \(x_2\) 积分，会贡献因子 \(x_2\)。于是 \[ \begin{aligned} P &= 6\int_0^{\frac{1}{2}} x_2\int_{2x_2}^{1} dx_1\, dx_2 \\ &= 6\int_0^{\frac{1}{2}} x_2(1 - 2x_2)\, dx_2 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

这等于 \(6\left(\dfrac{1}{8} - \dfrac{1}{12}\right) = 6 \cdot \dfrac{1}{24} = \dfrac{1}{4}\)。

因此，正确答案是 E。

English

Order the values as \(x_1 \gt x_2 \gt x_3\); the joint density of the order statistics is \(6\) on this region. The event is \(x_1 \gt 2x_2.\)

Integrating \(x_3\) from \(0\) to \(x_2\) contributes a factor of \(x_2.\) Then \[ \begin{aligned} P &= 6\int_0^{\frac{1}{2}} x_2\int_{2x_2}^{1} dx_1\, dx_2 \\ &= 6\int_0^{\frac{1}{2}} x_2(1 - 2x_2)\, dx_2. \end{aligned} \]

This equals \(6\left(\dfrac{1}{8} - \dfrac{1}{12}\right) = 6 \cdot \dfrac{1}{24} = \dfrac{1}{4}.\)

Thus, the correct answer is E.

23 答案 C

数字互不相同，并且来自 \(\{1, \ldots, 9\}\)，条件“没有数字同时与两个更大的数字相邻”等价于没有内部数字小于它的两个相邻数字。

对固定的 \(n\) 个数字，从大到小插入来构造排列；每个新的较小数字必须放在当前排列的两个端点之一，因此有 \(2^{n-1}\) 个有效排列。

对所有非空数字子集求和，\[ \begin{gathered} \sum_{n=1}^{9}\binom{9}{n}2^{n-1} \\ = \frac{1}{2}\sum_{n=1}^{9}\binom{9}{n}2^{n} \\ = \frac{3^9 - 1}{2} = \frac{19682}{2} = 9841 \end{gathered}\text{。}\]

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

The digits are distinct and drawn from \(\{1, \ldots, 9\},\) and “no digit adjacent to two greater digits” means no interior digit is smaller than both neighbors.

For a fixed set of \(n\) digits, build the arrangement by inserting digits from largest to smallest; each new (smaller) digit must go to one of the two ends, giving \(2^{n-1}\) valid arrangements.

Summing over all nonempty digit subsets, \[ \begin{gathered} \sum_{n=1}^{9}\binom{9}{n}2^{n-1} \\ = \frac{1}{2}\sum_{n=1}^{9}\binom{9}{n}2^{n} \\ = \frac{3^9 - 1}{2} = \frac{19682}{2} = 9841. \end{gathered} \]

Thus, the correct answer is C.

25 答案 E

本题已作废：按题面作答，正确答案不在选项中。下面给出计数。

对于 \(\{f \le 0\} = [a, b] \cup (c, d)\)，闭区间端点 \(a, b\) 必须是 \(P\) 的零点，且在这些点 \(Q\ne0\)，而 \(c,d\) 必须是极点。因此符号的变化必须与下面这个函数一致： \[ g(x)=\frac{(x-a)(x-b)}{(x-c)(x-d)}\text{。}\] \(P\) 中未使用的第三个因子必须与 \(Q\) 中未使用的第三个因子相同；否则会多出零点、极点或符号变化。

如果这个公共因子是 \(a,b,c,d\) 之外的第五个值，它不能在任一区间内部形成空点。在五个数从小到大的排列中，它只能位于 \(a\) 之前、\(b\) 与 \(c\) 之间，或 \(d\) 之后，因此给出 \(3\) 个函数。

另一种可能是公共因子等于 \(c\) 或 \(d\)。对 \(a,b,c,d\) 的每一种选择（共 \(\binom54=5\) 种），这两个多项式对化简为同一个公式 \(g\)，且定义域相同（都排除 \(c,d\)），所以只定义一个函数。这再给出 \(5\) 个函数，按题面字面理解总数为 \(3+5=8\)。

如果在后五种情形中把两个多项式对分别计数，就会得到 \(3+2\cdot5=13\)，即临时答案 (E)，但这没有回答题目所问的函数个数。因此印刷的选项都不正确。

English

This problem was voided: as written, its answer is not among the choices. Here is the count.

For \(\{f \le 0\} = [a, b] \cup (c, d),\) the endpoints \(a, b\) of the closed interval must be zeros of \(P\) at which \(Q\ne0,\) while \(c,d\) must be poles. The required sign pattern is therefore that of \[ g(x)=\frac{(x-a)(x-b)}{(x-c)(x-d)}. \] The unused third factor of \(P\) must match the unused third factor of \(Q;\) otherwise there would be an extra zero, pole, or sign change.

If the common factor is the fifth value not among \(a,b,c,d,\) it cannot make a hole inside either interval. In the ordered list of five values it may therefore occur before \(a,\) between \(b\) and \(c,\) or after \(d,\) giving \(3\) functions.

Alternatively, the common factor can equal \(c\) or \(d.\) For each of the \(\binom54=5\) choices of \(a,b,c,d,\) these two polynomial pairs simplify to the same formula \(g\) and have the same domain (both omit \(c,d\)), so they define only one function. This gives \(5\) more functions, for a literal total of \(3+5=8.\)

Counting the two polynomial pairs separately in each of the last five cases gives \(3+2\cdot5=13,\) the provisional answer (E), but that does not answer the stated question about functions. Thus none of the printed choices is correct.
