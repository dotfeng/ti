English

\[\dfrac{(2112-2021)^2}{169}\]

2

Menkara 有一张 \(4 \times 6\) 的索引卡片。若她把这张卡片的一边长度缩短 \(1\) 英寸，卡片面积会变成 \(18\) 平方英寸。如果她改为把另一边长度缩短 \(1\) 英寸，那么卡片面积是多少平方英寸？

A\(16\) B\(17\) C\(18\) D\(19\) E\(20\)

English

Menkara has a \(4 \times 6\) index card. If she shortens the length of one side of this card by \(1\) inch, the card would have area \(18\) square inches. What would the area of the card be in square inches if instead she shortens the length of the other side by \(1\) inch?

(A) \(16\)(B) \(17\)(C) \(18\)(D) \(19\)(E) \(20\)

3

半径为 \(2\) 的黏土球最多有多少个可以完全放进边长为 \(6\) 的立方体中？假设这些球在装入立方体之前可以重新塑形，但不能被压缩。

A\(3\) B\(4\) C\(5\) D\(6\) E\(7\)

English

What is the maximum number of balls of clay with radius \(2\) that can completely fit inside a cube of side length \(6\) assuming that the balls can be reshaped but not compressed before they are packed in the cube?

(A) \(3\)(B) \(4\)(C) \(5\)(D) \(6\)(E) \(7\)

4

Lopez 先生上班有两条路线可选。路线 A 长 \(6\) 英里，他在这条路线上的平均速度为每小时 \(30\) 英里。路线 B 长 \(5\) 英里，他在这条路线上的平均速度为每小时 \(40\) 英里，但其中有一段 \(\dfrac{1}{2}\) 英里的学校区域，平均速度为每小时 \(20\) 英里。路线 B 比路线 A 快多少分钟？

A\(2 \dfrac{3}{4}\) B\(3 \dfrac{3}{4}\) C\(4 \dfrac{1}{2}\) D\(5 \dfrac{1}{2}\) E\(6 \dfrac{3}{4}\)

English

Mr. Lopez has a choice of two routes to get to work. Route A is \(6\) miles long, and his average speed along this route is \(30\) miles per hour. Route B is \(5\) miles long, and his average speed along this route is \(40\) miles per hour, except for a \(\dfrac{1}{2}\)-mile stretch in a school zone where his average speed is \(20\) miles per hour. By how many minutes is Route B quicker than Route A?

(A) \(2 \dfrac{3}{4}\)(B) \(3 \dfrac{3}{4}\)(C) \(4 \dfrac{1}{2}\)(D) \(5 \dfrac{1}{2}\)(E) \(6 \dfrac{3}{4}\)

English

(A) \(1\)(B) \(3\)(C) \(5\)(D) \(7\)(E) \(9\)

6

A\(6\) B\(8\) C\(10\) D\(11\) E\(15\)

English

Elmer the emu takes \(44\) equal strides to walk between consecutive telephone poles on a rural road. Oscar the ostrich can cover the same distance in \(12\) equal leaps. The telephone poles are evenly spaced, and the \(41\)st pole along this road is exactly one mile (\(5280\) feet) from the first pole. How much longer, in feet, is Oscar’s leap than Elmer’s stride?

(A) \(6\)(B) \(8\)(C) \(10\)(D) \(11\)(E) \(15\)

10

A\(-18.5\) B\(-13.5\) C\(0\) D\(13.5\) E\(18.5\)

English

A school has \(100\) students and \(5\) teachers. In the first period, each student is taking one class, and each teacher is teaching one class. Let \(s\) be the average value obtained if a student was picked at random and the number of students in their class, including the student, is noted. What is \(t-s?\)

(A) \(-18.5\)(B) \(-13.5\)(C) \(0\)(D) \(13.5\)(E) \(18.5\)

In terms of Emily’s equal steps, what is the length of the ship?

(A) \(70\)(B) \(84\)(C) \(98\)(D) \(105\)(E) \(126\)

逐题解析

1 答案 C

可以如下化简这个表达式：\(2112-2021=91=7\cdot13\)，\[ \begin{aligned} \frac{(2112-2021)^2}{169} &=\frac{(7\cdot13)^2}{13^2}\\ &=7^2=49 \end{aligned}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Since \(2112-2021=91=7\cdot13,\) \[ \begin{aligned} \frac{(2112-2021)^2}{169} &=\frac{(7\cdot13)^2}{13^2}\\ &=7^2=49. \end{aligned} \]

Thus, C is the correct answer.

2 答案 E

若把 \(4\) 英寸的一边缩短 \(1\) 英寸，就得到 \(3\times 6\) 的卡片，面积为 \(18\) 平方英寸。

因此题中原先缩短的是 \(4\) 英寸的一边。若改为缩短另一边，则得到 \(4\times 5\) 的卡片，面积为 \(20\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

If she shortens the \(4\)-inch side by \(1,\) she has a \(3\times 6\) card, whose area is \(18\) square inches.

Therefore the given shortening was on the \(4\)-inch side. Shortening the other side instead gives a \(4\times 5\) card, with area \(20\) square inches.

Thus, E is the correct answer.

3 答案 D

立方体体积为 \(6^3=216\)。一个黏土球的体积为 \(\frac{4}{3}\pi\cdot 2^3=\frac{32\pi}{3}\)。

因为黏土可以重新塑形但不能压缩，最多的球数为 \[\left\lfloor \frac{216}{\frac{32\pi}{3}}\right\rfloor=\left\lfloor\frac{81}{4\pi}\right\rfloor\text{。}\]

由于 \(12\lt 4\pi\lt 13\)，可知 \(6\lt \frac{81}{4\pi}\lt \frac{81}{12}\lt 7\)，因此其整数部分为 \(6\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The cube has volume \(6^3=216.\) One ball of clay has volume \(\frac{4}{3}\pi\cdot 2^3=\frac{32\pi}{3}.\)

Because the clay may be reshaped but not compressed, the maximum number of balls is \[\left\lfloor \frac{216}{\frac{32\pi}{3}}\right\rfloor=\left\lfloor\frac{81}{4\pi}\right\rfloor.\]

Since \(12\lt 4\pi\lt 13,\) we have \(6\lt \frac{81}{4\pi}\lt \frac{81}{12}\lt 7.\) Therefore the floor is \(6.\)

Thus, D is the correct answer.

数值 \(12\) 是固定点，因为 \(12\) 有 \(6\) 个正因数，所以 \(f_1(12)=12\)。

先找所有满足 \(n\le50\) 且 \(f_1(n)=12\) 的 \(n\)，也就是有 \(6\) 个因数的数：\[12,18,20,28,32,44,45,50\text{。}\]

再检查 \(f_1(n)\) 在到达 \(12\) 之前能否取到上述其他值。由于 \(f_1(n)\) 是因数个数的两倍，列表中只有 \(18\) 和 \(20\) 有用，分别表示 \(n\) 有 \(9\) 或 \(10\) 个因数。

对 \(n\le50\)，额外的可能为 \(36\)（有 \(9\) 个因数）和 \(48\)（有 \(10\) 个因数）。因此共有 \(8+2=10\) 个 \(n\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

The value \(12\) is fixed by the function, since \(12\) has \(6\) positive divisors and therefore \(f_1(12)=12.\)

First find all \(n\le50\) with \(f_1(n)=12,\) meaning \(n\) has \(6\) divisors. These are \[12,18,20,28,32,44,45,50.\]

Now check whether \(f_1(n)\) can be one of these values before reaching \(12.\) Since \(f_1(n)\) is twice a divisor count, the only useful possibilities in that list are \(18\) and \(20,\) meaning \(n\) has \(9\) or \(10\) divisors.

For \(n\le50,\) the additional possibilities are \(36,\) which has \(9\) divisors, and \(48,\) which has \(10\) divisors. Therefore there are \(8+2=10\) values of \(n.\)

Thus, D is the correct answer.

24 答案 E

先标记一个面为 \(ABCD\)。每个面必须包含两个 \(0\) 和两个 \(1\)，所以先按 \(ABCD\) 四条棱的标签模式分类。

情形 \(1\)：\(ABCD\) 的相对棱标签相同。\(ABCD\) 上有 \(2\) 种这样的模式；任选一条竖直棱（如 \(AE\)）的标签后，其余标签都被确定。因此共有 \(2\cdot2=4\) 种标法。

情形 \(2\)：\(ABCD\) 的相对棱标签不同。\(ABCD\) 上有 \(4\) 种这样的模式；对每种模式，相邻两条竖直棱的标签有 \(4\) 种选择，其余标签随即确定。因此共有 \(4\cdot4=16\) 种标法。

总数为 \(4+16=20\)。

所以正确答案是 E。

English

Label one face \(ABCD.\) Each face must contain two \(0\)s and two \(1\)s, so first split by the pattern on the four edges of \(ABCD.\)

Case \(1:\) opposite edges of \(ABCD\) have the same label. There are \(2\) such patterns on \(ABCD.\) For either pattern, once the label of one vertical edge, say \(AE,\) is chosen, the face-sum conditions force all remaining labels. This gives \(2\cdot2=4\) labelings.

Case \(2:\) opposite edges of \(ABCD\) have different labels. There are \(4\) such patterns on \(ABCD.\) For each, the labels of two adjacent vertical edges may be chosen in \(4\) ways, and then the remaining labels are forced by the face-sum conditions. This gives \(4\cdot4=16\) labelings.

The total is \(4+16=20.\)

Thus, E is the correct answer.

25 答案 A

这个多项式必须有两个不同的实根：如果只有一个重实根，则 \(p(p(x))=0\) 至多有两个实数解；如果没有实根，则没有实数解。设它的两个根为 \(r\) 和 \(s\)，则 \[ \begin{aligned} p(x) &=(x-r)(x-s) \\ &=x^2-(r+s)x+rs \end{aligned}\text{。}\]方程 \(p(p(x))=0\) 等价于 \(p(x)=r\) 或 \(p(x)=s\)。

要恰好有三个实数解，其中一个二次方程必须有重根，另一个必须有两个不同实根。设 \(p(x)=r\) 有重根。它的判别式为 \[ \begin{aligned} &(r+s)^2-4(rs-r) \\ &=(r-s)^2+4r \end{aligned}\text{，}\]所以 \((r-s)^2=-4r\)，从而 \(r\le0\)。

另一个方程 \(p(x)=s\) 的判别式为 \((r-s)^2+4s\) \(=-4r+4s\) \(=4(s-r)\)，且必须为正。因此 \(s\gt r\)，所以 \(r-s=-2\sqrt{-r}\)，即 \(s=r+2\sqrt{-r}\)。

根之和为 \(r+s=2r+2\sqrt{-r}\)。令 \(u=\sqrt{-r}\)，则该和为 \(-2u^2+2u\)，在 \(u=\frac{1}{2}\) 时最大。因此 \(r=-\frac{1}{4}\)，\(s=\frac{3}{4}\)。

于是得到多项式 \(p(x)=x^2-\frac{1}{2}x-\frac{3}{16}\)，并且 \[p(1)=1-\frac{1}{2}-\frac{3}{16}=\frac{5}{16}\text{。}\]

所以正确答案是 A。

English

The polynomial must have two distinct real roots: a repeated real root produces at most two real solutions of \(p(p(x))=0,\) while nonreal roots produce none. Let its roots be \(r\) and \(s,\) so \[ \begin{aligned} p(x) &=(x-r)(x-s) \\ &=x^2-(r+s)x+rs. \end{aligned} \] The equation \(p(p(x))=0\) is equivalent to \(p(x)=r\) or \(p(x)=s.\)

For exactly three real solutions, one of these two quadratic equations must have a double root and the other must have two distinct real roots. Suppose \(p(x)=r\) has the double root. Its discriminant is \[ \begin{aligned} &(r+s)^2-4(rs-r) \\ &=(r-s)^2+4r, \end{aligned} \] so \((r-s)^2=-4r,\) forcing \(r\le0.\)

The other equation, \(p(x)=s,\) has discriminant \((r-s)^2+4s\) \(=-4r+4s\) \(=4(s-r),\) which must be positive. Hence \(s\gt r,\) so \(r-s=-2\sqrt{-r}\) and \(s=r+2\sqrt{-r}.\)

The sum of the roots is \(r+s=2r+2\sqrt{-r}.\) Let \(u=\sqrt{-r},\) so this is \(-2u^2+2u,\) maximized at \(u=\frac{1}{2}.\) Thus \(r=-\frac{1}{4}\) and \(s=\frac{3}{4}.\)

Therefore \(p(x)=x^2-\frac{1}{2}x-\frac{3}{16},\) and \[p(1)=1-\frac{1}{2}-\frac{3}{16}=\frac{5}{16}.\]

Thus, A is the correct answer.
