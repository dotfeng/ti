取 \(C=(0,0),\ A=(a,0)\)， 和 \(B=(0,b)\)。 \(AB\) 与以 \(BC\) 为直径的圆的另一个交点为 \[ D=\left(\frac{ab^2}{a^2+b^2}, \frac{a^2b}{a^2+b^2}\right)\text{。}\] 圆在 \(D\) 点的切线与 \(CA\) 交于 \(F=(\frac{a}{2},0)\)。 因此 \(DF\) 平分 \(CA\)； 又由从 \(F\) 引出的切线段相等，有 \(FC=FD=FA\)，从而得到选项 C 和 E；圆及直角三角形中的角关系又能得到选项 D。

若 \(DF\) 平分 \(\angle CDA\)， 则在三角形 \(CDA\) 中，由角平分线定理必须有 \(\frac{CF}{FA}=\frac{CD}{DA}\)。 左边为 \(1\)， 而 \[ \frac{CD}{DA}=\frac{ab}{a^2}=\frac ba\text{，}\] 它不一定等于 \(1\)。 因此选项 B 无法由已知条件证明。

因此，正确答案是 B。

English

Put \(C=(0,0),\ A=(a,0),\) and \(B=(0,b).\) The second intersection of \(AB\) with the circle of diameter \(BC\) is \[ D=\left(\frac{ab^2}{a^2+b^2}, \frac{a^2b}{a^2+b^2}\right). \] The tangent at \(D\) meets \(CA\) at \(F=(\frac{a}{2},0).\) Hence \(DF\) bisects \(CA;\) also \(FC=FD=FA\) by equal tangents from \(F,\) which supplies choices C and E, and the circle/right-triangle angles supply choice D.

If \(DF\) bisected \(\angle CDA,\) the angle-bisector theorem in triangle \(CDA\) would require \(\frac{CF}{FA}=\frac{CD}{DA}.\) The left side is \(1,\) while \[ \frac{CD}{DA}=\frac{ab}{a^2}=\frac ba, \] which need not be \(1.\) Thus choice B is not provable.

Therefore, the correct answer is B.

因此，正确答案是 C。

English

36 答案 E

每个新的直角三角形都有相同的锐角，所以垂线段长度构成等比数列。由于前两项为 \(a,b\)， 公比为 \(\frac{b}{a}\)。 收敛意味着 \(0\lt \frac{b}{a}\lt1\)， 因而总和为 \[\frac{a}{1-\frac{b}{a}}=\frac{a^2}{a-b}\text{。}\]

English

Each new right triangle has the same acute angle, so the perpendicular lengths form a geometric sequence. Since the first two lengths are \(a,b,\) the common ratio is \(\frac{b}{a}.\) Convergence implies \(0\lt \frac{b}{a}\lt1,\) and the sum is \[\frac{a}{1-\frac{b}{a}}=\frac{a^2}{a-b}.\]

将孔的圆心置于原点。直径为 \(2\) 英寸和 \(1\) 英寸的塞规圆心分别为 \((0,-\frac{1}{2})\) 和 \((0,1)\)。 设一个新量规的半径为 \(r\)，圆心为 \((u,v)\)。 与两个塞规相切给出 \[ \begin{gathered} u^2+(v+\frac{1}{2})^2=(1+r)^2,\\ u^2+(v-1)^2=(\frac{1}{2}+r)^2 \end{gathered}\text{，}\] 而与孔内切给出 \(u^2+v^2=(\frac{3}{2}-r)^2\)。 将方程两两相减得 \(v=\frac12+\frac r3\)，且 \(v=\frac32-2r\)。 因此 \(r=\frac{3}{7}\)， 所以 \(d=2r=\frac{6}{7}\approx0.86\)。

40 答案 D

令 \(N=x^2+3x+1\)。则 \[P=N^2-3(x-10)\text{。}\]当 \(x=10,\ P=N^2=131^2\) 时，有一个值符合条件。

因此，正确答案是 D。

English

Let \(N=x^2+3x+1.\) Then \[P=N^2-3(x-10).\] At \(x=10,\ P=N^2=131^2,\) so one value works.

If \(x\gt10\) and \(P=y^2,\) then \(N^2-y^2=3(x-10).\) Distinct integer squares differing from \(N^2\) differ by at least \(2|N|-1,\) which is already greater than \(3(x-10),\) a contradiction. Hence \(x=10\) is the unique solution and \(n=1.\)

Therefore, the correct answer is D.
