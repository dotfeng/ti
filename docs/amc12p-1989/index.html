逐题解析

1 答案 C

由于 \(5^2=25\) 是奇数，\((-1)^{5^2}=-1\)。又有 \(1^{2^5}=1\)，所以和为 \(0\)。

所以正确答案是 C。

English

Because \(5^2=25\) is odd, \((-1)^{5^2}=-1.\) Also \(1^{2^5}=1,\) so the sum is \(0.\)

Thus the correct answer is C.

2 答案 D

有 \(\frac19+\frac1{16}=\frac{16+9}{144}=\frac{25}{144}\)。其正平方根为 \(\frac5{12}\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

We have \(\frac19+\frac1{16}=\frac{16+9}{144}=\frac{25}{144}.\) Its positive square root is \(\frac5{12}.\)

Thus the correct answer is D.

3 答案 D

设正方形边长为 \(s\)。每个矩形的长均为 \(s\)，且周长相同，所以三个宽相等，均为 \(\frac{s}{3}\)。因此 \[ 2\left(s+\frac{s}{3}\right)=24\text{，}\]故 \(s=9\)，正方形面积为 \(81\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Let the square have side \(s.\) Since every rectangle has length \(s\) and the same perimeter, their three widths are equal and are each \(\frac{s}{3}.\) Thus \[ 2\left(s+\frac{s}{3}\right)=24, \] so \(s=9\) and the square’s area is \(81.\)

Thus the correct answer is D.

4 答案 D

作垂线 \(AG\) 和 \(BH\)，它们都垂直于 \(DF\)。在等腰梯形中，\(GH=AB=4\)，且 \[ DG=HC=\frac{DC-AB}{2}=3\text{。}\]由于 \(B\) 是 \(DE\) 的中点且 \(BH\parallel EF\)，中点定理说明 \(H\) 是 \(DF\) 的中点。因此 \(DH=DG+GH=7\)，故 \(DF=14\)，且 \(CF=14-10=4\)。

所以正确答案是 D。

English

Drop perpendiculars \(AG\) and \(BH\) to \(DF.\) In the isosceles trapezoid, \(GH=AB=4\) and \[ DG=HC=\frac{DC-AB}{2}=3. \] Since \(B\) is the midpoint of \(DE\) and \(BH\parallel EF,\) the midpoint theorem makes \(H\) the midpoint of \(DF.\) Hence \(DH=DG+GH=7,\) so \(DF=14\) and \(CF=14-10=4.\)

Thus the correct answer is D.
